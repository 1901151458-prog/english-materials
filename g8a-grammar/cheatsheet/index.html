一不定代词 some / any · many / much · few / little

词修饰倾向
some / any可数 + 不可数some 肯定，any 否/疑/条件
many / muchmany 可数复 / much 不可数「很多」
few / a few可数复数few 否定 / a few 肯定
little / a little不可数little 否定 / a little 肯定

例外：Would you like some tea?（希望对方肯定答复用 some）

复合不定代词：some/any/no/every + -body/-one/-thing，作主语用单数；定语后置（anything fun、nothing special）。

五一般将来时 will / shall + 动词原形

时间状语：tomorrow、next week、next year、in the future

缩写：will → 'll；will not → won't

shall：主语为 I / we 时可用于书面语（Shall we go?）

be going to + 动词原形：表示将要发生的动作或安排，或打算、计划、决定要做的事。

I'm going to make a sandwich. / What are you going to do tonight?

二比较级与最高级 · 构成

类型变化例
一般+ er / esttall → taller → tallest
e 结尾+ r / stlarge → larger → largest
重读闭音节双写 + er / estbig → bigger → biggest
辅音+yy→i + er / esteasy → easier → easiest
多音节more / mostbeautiful → more/most beautiful

不规则：good/well→better→best；bad/badly→worse→worst；many/much→more→most；little→less→least；far→farther/further→farthest/furthest

六句子种类 陈述 / 疑问 / 祈使 / 感叹

种类用途 / 例
陈述句说明事实或看法：Bamboo is a symbol of goodness.
疑问句提出问题：Did you do anything special?
祈使句请求/命令/建议：Don't be nervous. / Let's go.
感叹句强烈感情：How clever you are! / What wonderful memories!

感叹句：How + 形容词/副词；What + 名词短语。

三比较级与最高级 · 用法

比较级：两者比较，比较级 + than。than 后动词相同时可用助动词代替或省略。

I'm more outgoing than my sister. / You know more than I (do).

最高级：三者及以上比较；范围用 of / in / among；形容词前加 the，副词最高级 the 可省。

The blue whale is the largest animal in the world.

as + 原级 + as：两者相同；not as / so + 原级 + as：不及。修饰比较级用 much / a little。

七句子成分与五种基本句型

七大成分：主语、谓语、表语、宾语、宾语补足语、定语、状语

句型例
主谓 SVElla is dancing.
主（系）表 SPThey are friends.
主谓宾 SVOI like bananas.
主谓宾宾 SVOOShe gave me a pen.
主谓宾补 SVOCHe made us laugh.

四情态动词 can / could

功能例
能力He could sing well when he was young.
请求允许Can / Could I use your phone?
给予许可Yes, of course you can.
提出请求Could you please sweep the floor?
提出建议We could go fishing this weekend.

could 表示请求比 can 更委婉；表示能力时是 can 的过去式。
