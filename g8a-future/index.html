01

一般将来时：will / shall

助动词 + 动词原形

一般将来时通常由「助动词 + 动词原形」构成，常与 tomorrow、next week、next year、in the future 等时间状语连用。

We will come to see you next week.

next week 表将来。

You will see robots working everywhere in the future.

in the future 表将来。

缩写与否定

  • will → 'll
  • will not → won't

shall 的用法

  • 主语为 I / we 时可用 shall
  • Shall we go to see the sci-fi film?
  • 书面语更正式：I shall write you a letter.
02

一般将来时：be going to

打算、计划或将要发生

「be going to + 动词原形」表示将要发生的动作或安排，或打算、计划、决定要做的事。

I'm going to make a sandwich.

打算做（决定）。

What are you going to do tonight?

询问计划。

辨析：有迹象表明即将发生（Look at the clouds! It is going to rain.）或已计划好的安排用 be going to；单纯预测或带意愿色彩用 will。

03

情态动词 can

能力、许可、请求、建议

功能例句
能力Can you speak English?
请求允许Can I put some flowers on the table?
给予许可Yes, of course you can.
提出请求Can you help me hang up the photos?
提出建议We can eat in a restaurant this evening.
04

情态动词 could

过去的能力 + 更委婉的请求

can 的过去式（能力）

  • He could sing very well when he was young.
  • 表示过去具备的能力。

更委婉的请求 / 建议

  • Could I use your mobile phone?
  • Could you please sweep the floor?
  • We could go fishing this weekend.

要点：表示请求时 could 比 can 更客气；但回答时通常仍用 can（Yes, you can.）。
