\section{比较级用法}
比较级表示两者比较，用「比较级 + than」。当 than 前后动词相同时，通常用助动词代替或省略。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item I'm \underline{more outgoing than} my sister.
\item You know more about yourself \underline{than I (do)}.
\item He works much harder than me.（口语中一般用 me）
\end{itemize}
\textbf{教材注释：}比较级前可以用 much、a little 等修饰。\\
\quad I'm feeling \underline{much better} today. / My brother is \underline{a little taller} than me.

\section{最高级用法}
最高级表示三者或三者以上比较，其中一个在某一方面超过其他几个。范围用 of、in、among 短语说明。\textbf{形容词最高级前一般要加 the}，副词最高级前的 the 可以省去。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item The blue whale is \underline{the largest and heaviest} animal in the world.
\item In my country, July is \underline{the hottest} month of the year.
\item The cheetah runs \underline{(the) fastest} among all land animals.
\end{itemize}

\section{as ... as 句型}
（1）两者在某一方面\textbf{相同}：as + 形容词/副词原形 + as。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item I think science is \underline{as important as} maths.
\item Tom runs \underline{as fast as} Jack.
\end{itemize}
（2）一方\textbf{不及}另一方：not as / so + 原级 + as。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item It is \underline{not as / so warm} today as yesterday.
\item He did \underline{not come as / so early} as Wang Lin.
\end{itemize}
