\section{主动还是被动？先看主语}
英语动词有两种语态：\textbf{主动语态}（主语是动作的执行者）与\textbf{被动语态}（主语是动作的承受者）。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item Many people \underline{speak} English.（主动：many people 是 speak 的执行者）
\item English \underline{is spoken} by many people.（被动：English 是 speak 的承受者）
\item Alexander Bell \underline{invented} the telephone in 1876.（主动）
\item The telephone \underline{was invented} by Alexander Bell in 1876.（被动）
\end{itemize}
\textbf{判断口诀：}问自己“主语是动作的发起者还是接受者”——发起者用主动，接受者用被动。

\section{构成与时态：be + 过去分词}
被动语态由“助动词 be + 及物动词的 -ed 形式”构成，be 有人称、数和时态的变化。
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{2.6cm}p{5.6cm}p{3.4cm}}
\hline
\textbf{时态} & \textbf{结构} & \textbf{例（ask）}\\
\hline
一般现在时 & am / is / are + 过去分词 & is asked\\
一般过去时 & was / were + 过去分词 & was asked\\
一般将来时 & will be + 过去分词 & will be asked\\
情态动词 & modal + be + 过去分词 & must be asked\\
现在完成时 & have / has been + 过去分词 & has been asked\\
过去完成时 & had been + 过去分词 & had been asked\\
现在进行时 & am / is / are being + 过去分词 & is being asked\\
不定式 & to be + 过去分词 & to be asked\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\textbf{三个高频错点：}情态动词后忘 be（× must finished）／完成时少 been（× has rebuilt）／短语动词丢介词（× are looked）。

\section{主动变被动：三步走}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{第一步}：找出主动句的宾语，提到句首作主语。
\item \textbf{第二步}：谓语动词变成 be + 过去分词，be 的时态随原句。
\item \textbf{第三步}：原主语变 by 短语；不必说明时可省略。
\end{itemize}
例：Alexander Bell invented the telephone in 1876.
$\rightarrow$ The telephone \underline{was invented} by Alexander Bell in 1876.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item 代词要变格：him $\rightarrow$ he，me $\rightarrow$ I。
\item be 要跟人称和数：The books \underline{were} sold.
\item 时态不能变：原句一般过去时，被动也用一般过去时。
\item 双宾语：They gave him a prize. $\rightarrow$ He \underline{was given} a prize.
\end{itemize}

\section{扩展与陷阱：It is said 句型}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{4.6cm}p{2.8cm}p{6.4cm}}
\hline
\textbf{句型} & \textbf{含义} & \textbf{例句}\\
\hline
It is said that \ldots & 据说 & It is said that he is a genius.\\
It is reported that \ldots & 据报道 & It is reported that the team won.\\
It is believed that \ldots & 人们相信 & It is believed that the story is true.\\
It is well known that \ldots & 众所周知 & It is well known that the earth goes around the sun.\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\textbf{可转换：}It is said that he is a genius. $\rightarrow$ He is said to be a genius.（过去时用 to have done）\\
\textbf{无被动动词：}happen、take place、break out、belong to、come true；× An accident was happened.
