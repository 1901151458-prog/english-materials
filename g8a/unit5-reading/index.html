Section A 2a · Making Stir-fried Eggs and Tomatoes

Section A 2a 主题：学做中国菜 · 顺序副词与感叹句（教材 P43 为听力填空，完整原文见 Listening Scripts P98）

Peter: Hey Teng Fei, can you teach me how to cook a popular Chinese dish?

嘿，腾飞，你能教我怎么做一道受欢迎的中国菜吗？

词汇hey 嘿；teach 教；popular 受欢迎的；dish 菜肴 语法can you...? 表示请求；teach sb. how to do sth. 教某人如何做某事，how to cook 是“疑问词 + 不定式”结构，作 teach 的宾语。

Teng Fei: Sure. How about stir-fried eggs and tomatoes?

当然可以。西红柿炒鸡蛋怎么样？

词汇sure 当然；stir-fried 炒的；egg 鸡蛋；tomato 西红柿 语法How about + 名词/动名词？用于提出建议；stir-fried 是复合形容词，表示“炒的”。

Peter: Great idea. How do we make it?

好主意。我们怎么做呢？

词汇idea 主意；make 制作 语法How do we make it? 用 how 询问方式；it 指代上文的 stir-fried eggs and tomatoes。

Teng Fei: We need eggs, tomatoes, salt, and sugar.

我们需要鸡蛋、西红柿、盐和糖。

词汇need 需要；salt 盐；sugar 糖 语法need 是实义动词，后接宾语；并列列举多项时，最后一项前用 and 连接。

Peter: OK. How many tomatoes and eggs do we need?

好的。我们需要多少西红柿和鸡蛋？

词汇how many 多少（用于可数名词）；need 需要 语法How many + 可数名词复数 询问数量；特殊疑问句语序为“疑问词 + 助动词 do + 主语 + 动词原形”。

Teng Fei: Four tomatoes and three eggs.

四个西红柿和三个鸡蛋。

词汇four 四；three 三 语法用“数词 + 可数名词复数”回答数量；tomato 的复数是 tomatoes（加 -es）。

Peter: OK. What should we do with the tomatoes and eggs?

好的。我们应该怎么处理西红柿和鸡蛋？

词汇should 应该；do with 处理 语法What should we do with...? 用来询问如何处理某物；should + 动词原形。

Teng Fei: First, cut the tomatoes into small pieces.

首先，把西红柿切成小块。

词汇first 首先；cut 切；piece 小块 语法cut sth. into sth. 把某物切成……；first 是表示先后顺序的副词。

Teng Fei: Next, mix the eggs in a bowl.

接下来，在碗里把鸡蛋搅匀。

词汇next 接下来；mix 搅拌；bowl 碗 语法next 表示顺序；in a bowl 是介词短语作地点状语。

Peter: Like this?

像这样吗？

词汇like 像 语法省略句，完整意思是 Do I do it like this?；like this 表示“像这样”。

Teng Fei: Perfect. Then, heat some oil in a pan and add the eggs to it.

很好。然后，在平底锅里热一些油，把鸡蛋倒进去。

词汇perfect 极好的；then 然后；heat 加热；oil 油；pan 平底锅；add 加入 语法then 表示顺序；add sth. to sth. 把某物加到某物里；and 连接两个并列的祈使句。

Teng Fei: Stir-fry for one minute. Then take the eggs out of the pan.

翻炒一分钟。然后把鸡蛋从锅里盛出来。

词汇stir-fry 翻炒；minute 分钟；take... out of 把……从……中取出 语法祈使句用动词原形开头；for + 一段时间表示持续多久；take sth. out of sth. 把某物从某物中取出。

Peter: Got it.

明白了。

词汇got 明白了（get 的过去式） 语法Got it. 是口语中表示“明白了、懂了”的省略句。

Teng Fei: Next, cook the tomatoes in the pan until they're soft. Then, add some salt and sugar.

接下来，在锅里炒西红柿，直到它们变软。然后加一些盐和糖。

词汇cook 烹饪；until 直到；soft 软的 语法until 引导时间状语从句；they're = they are；then 表示顺序。

Peter: What an amazing smell! What's next?

多香的味道啊！下一步是什么？

词汇amazing 令人惊叹的；smell 气味；next 下一步 语法What an amazing smell! 是 what 引导的感叹句：What + a/an + 形容词 + 可数名词单数 (+ 主语 + 谓语)!；What's = What is。

Teng Fei: Finally, put the eggs back into the pan and mix them with the tomatoes.

最后，把鸡蛋放回锅里，和西红柿一起翻炒均匀。

词汇finally 最后；back 回（原处）；mix... with 把……与……混合 语法finally 表示顺序的最后一个步骤；put sth. back into... 把某物放回……；mix sth. with sth. 把某物与某物混合。

Peter: Wow, it looks so good! How simple!

哇，看起来太好了！多简单啊！

词汇look 看起来；simple 简单的 语法look 作系动词，后接形容词；How simple! 是 how 引导的感叹句：How + 形容词/副词 (+ 主语 + 谓语)!。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在彼得和腾飞之间。彼得请腾飞教他做一道受欢迎的中国菜，腾飞建议做西红柿炒鸡蛋。彼得问需要什么食材、各需要多少，腾飞说要四个西红柿和三个鸡蛋，还有盐和糖。接着腾飞一步步讲解做法：先把西红柿切成小块，在碗里把鸡蛋搅匀；然后在平底锅里热油，倒入鸡蛋翻炒一分钟盛出；再把西红柿炒软，加盐和糖；最后把鸡蛋倒回锅里和西红柿翻炒均匀。彼得边学边问，最后感叹这道菜看起来太好吃了、做法也很简单。

English Summary

The conversation is between Peter and Teng Fei. Peter asks Teng Fei to teach him how to cook a popular Chinese dish, and Teng Fei suggests stir-fried eggs and tomatoes. Peter asks what ingredients they need and how many, and Teng Fei says they need four tomatoes, three eggs, salt and sugar. Teng Fei then gives step-by-step instructions: first cut the tomatoes into small pieces and mix the eggs in a bowl; then heat some oil in a pan, add the eggs and stir-fry for one minute before taking them out; next cook the tomatoes in the pan until they are soft and add some salt and sugar; finally put the eggs back into the pan and mix them with the tomatoes. Peter learns as he asks questions, and at last he exclaims that the dish looks so good and that it is so simple.

语法点 Grammar

can you teach me how to cook a popular Chinese dish?

teach sb. how to do sth. 教某人如何做某事；how to cook 是“疑问词 + 不定式”，作 teach 的宾语。

How about stir-fried eggs and tomatoes?

How about + 名词/动名词？用于提出建议。

How many tomatoes and eggs do we need?

How many + 可数名词复数 询问数量；特殊疑问句用“疑问词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词原形”。

Four tomatoes and three eggs.

用“数词 + 可数名词复数”回答数量；tomato 的复数加 -es。

What should we do with the tomatoes and eggs?

What should we do with...? 询问如何处理某物；should + 动词原形。

First, cut the tomatoes into small pieces.

cut sth. into sth. 把某物切成……；first 是表示顺序的副词。

Next, mix the eggs in a bowl.

next 表示顺序；in a bowl 作地点状语。

Then, heat some oil in a pan and add the eggs to it.

then 表示顺序；add sth. to sth. 把某物加到某物里；and 连接两个并列祈使句。

Stir-fry for one minute.

祈使句用动词原形开头；for + 一段时间表示持续多久。

Then take the eggs out of the pan.

take sth. out of sth. 把某物从某物中取出；then 表示先后顺序。

Next, cook the tomatoes in the pan until they're soft.

until 引导时间状语从句，表示“直到……”；they're = they are。

What an amazing smell!

What 引导的感叹句：What + a/an + 形容词 + 可数名词单数 (+ 主语 + 谓语)!。

What's next?

What's = What is；next 表示“下一步”。

Finally, put the eggs back into the pan and mix them with the tomatoes.

finally 表示顺序的最后一步；put sth. back into... 把某物放回……；mix sth. with sth. 把某物与某物混合。

How simple!

How 引导的感叹句：How + 形容词/副词 (+ 主语 + 谓语)!。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Hey Teng Fei, can you teach me how to cook a popular Chinese dish?

嘿，腾飞，你能教我怎么做一道受欢迎的中国菜吗？

can you...? 是委婉的请求；teach me how to cook 是“teach + 宾语 + 疑问词 + 不定式”结构，how to cook 作直接宾语。

What should we do with the tomatoes and eggs?

我们应该怎么处理西红柿和鸡蛋？

What... do with...? 是固定表达，意为“怎么处理/加工……”；with 在这里表示“对于、处理”。

What an amazing smell! What's next?

多香的味道啊！下一步是什么？

感叹句结构为 What + a/an + 形容词 + 可数名词单数 (+ it is)!；What's next? 是省略句，常用于询问下一步做什么。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看教材 P43 的 2a：课文是听力填空，对话并不完整；先听录音把空缺的食材和数字填出来，再对照本页的完整原文。
  • 先想一想：做西红柿炒鸡蛋需要哪些食材？用英语说出至少三种。

读中

  • 边读边按先后顺序找出表示步骤的词：First, Next, Then, Finally。
  • 画出对话里的疑问句，注意 How many / What should we do with...? 是怎样提问的。

读后

  • 看教材 P44 的 2b 食谱，用 First / Next / Then / Finally 到黑板前复述做菜步骤。
  • 和同伴分角色朗读、表演对话（2c / 2d），再介绍一道自己会做的菜。

Section B 1b · Food for Thought (with Pumpkin Pie Recipe)

Section B 1b 主题：烹饪与爱 · 回忆与食谱（教材 P46-47，短文跨页）

“Cooking is love made visible.” That is what my grandmother always told me.

“烹饪是看得见的爱。”这是我奶奶总是对我说的话。

词汇cooking 烹饪；love 爱；visible 看得见的；grandmother 祖母；外婆 语法引号内是直接引语；love made visible 意为“被看得见的爱”（过去分词短语作定语）；what 引导表语从句。

Thanks to her, I love cooking.

因为她，我喜欢上了做饭。

词汇thanks to 多亏；因为；love 喜爱 语法thanks to 表示原因，后接名词或代词；love doing sth. 喜欢做某事。

It's a great way for me to share my love with others.

这对我来说是与他人分享爱的一种很好的方式。

词汇way 方式；share 分享；others 他人 语法It's a great way for sb. to do sth. 结构，it 是形式主语；share sth. with sb. 与某人分享某物。

Every time I sit at the table, after cooking for my family, we create memories.

每次我为家人做完饭坐到餐桌旁，我们都在创造回忆。

词汇every time 每次；create 创造；memory 回忆 语法every time 引导时间状语从句，意为“每当……”；after cooking 是“介词 after + 动名词”结构。

The smells and the food's taste, along with our conversations, connect us to each other.

饭菜的香味与味道，连同我们的交谈，把我们彼此联系在一起。

词汇smell 气味；taste 味道；along with 连同；connect 连接 语法along with 连接并列成分；connect sb. to sb. 把某人同某人联系起来。

What wonderful memories I have!

我拥有多么美好的回忆啊！

词汇wonderful 美好的；memory 回忆 语法What 引导的感叹句：What + 形容词 + 复数名词 + 主语 + 谓语!（memories 是 memory 的复数）。

One of my favourite memories is my grandmother's pumpkin pie.

我最喜爱的回忆之一是奶奶做的南瓜派。

词汇one of ……之一；favourite 最喜爱的；pumpkin 南瓜；pie 派 语法one of + 形容词最高级 + 复数名词 作主语时，谓语用单数 is。

She always made it in autumn, when the weather got colder.

她总是在秋天做它，那时天气开始变冷。

词汇autumn 秋天；weather 天气；colder 更冷的 语法made 是 make 的过去式；when 引导非限制性定语从句，修饰 autumn；get + 比较级 表示“变得……”。

Her friendly kitchen was the perfect place to warm up and enjoy a taste of the changing seasons.

她那亲切的厨房是温暖身心、品味季节更替味道的绝佳去处。

词汇friendly 亲切的；kitchen 厨房；perfect 完美的；season 季节 语法the perfect place to do sth. 中不定式作定语修饰 place；warm up 取暖、暖和起来。

I still remember the days when the smell of cinnamon filled the room with sweetness.

我仍然记得那些日子，肉桂的香味让整个房间充满甜蜜。

词汇still 仍然；cinnamon 肉桂；fill 充满；sweetness 甜蜜 语法when 引导定语从句，修饰 the days；fill sth. with sth. 用某物充满某物。

Now, every time I smell cinnamon, I think of my grandmother's pumpkin pie.

现在，每当我闻到肉桂的味道，就会想起奶奶的南瓜派。

词汇smell 闻；think of 想起 语法every time 引导时间状语从句；think of 表示“想起、想到”。

My grandmother told me about her first pumpkin pie.

奶奶给我讲了她第一次做南瓜派的事。

词汇tell sb. about sth. 给某人讲某事；first 第一次的 语法tell sb. about sth. 结构；first 在此作定语，表示“第一次的”。

When she was a college student in the US, she stayed with a host family.

当她在美国读大学时，她住在一个寄宿家庭里。

词汇college 大学；host family 寄宿家庭；stay with 与……住在一起 语法when 引导时间状语从句；stay with 表示“与……住在一起”。

One year around Thanksgiving, the hostess taught her how to make a pumpkin pie.

有一年感恩节前后，女主人教她做南瓜派。

词汇Thanksgiving 感恩节；hostess 女主人；teach 教 语法teach sb. how to do sth. 教某人如何做某事；around 表示“前后、左右”。

Many years later, she taught me how to make it too.

许多年后，她也教了我怎么做。

词汇many years later 许多年后；too 也 语法teach sb. how to do sth. 结构；too 用于肯定句句末，表示“也”。

Ingredients: eggs, sugar, cinnamon, salt, pumpkin, cream, pie crust

食材：鸡蛋、糖、肉桂、盐、南瓜、奶油、派皮

词汇ingredient 食材；cream 奶油；pie crust 派皮 语法食谱中的 Ingredients 用名词列出所需食材，名词用复数或不可数形式，一般不加冠词。

First, mix the eggs.

首先，把鸡蛋打散。

词汇first 首先；mix 搅拌 语法祈使句用动词原形开头；first 表示做法的第一步。

Then, add the sugar, cinnamon, salt, pumpkin, and cream.

然后，加入糖、肉桂、盐、南瓜和奶油。

词汇then 然后；add 加入 语法add sth. 加入某物；并列列举多项时用逗号隔开、最后用 and 连接。

Next, pour the mixture into the pie crust.

接下来，把混合物倒进派皮里。

词汇next 接下来；pour 倒；mixture 混合物；pie crust 派皮 语法pour sth. into sth. 把某物倒入某物；next 表示顺序。

After that, place the pie in the oven and cook for 40 to 50 minutes.

之后，把派放进烤箱烤 40 到 50 分钟。

词汇after that 之后；place 放置；oven 烤箱 语法after that 表示顺序；place sth. in sth. 把某物放在某物里；for + 一段时间表示持续。

Finally, let the pie cool for at least an hour before serving.

最后，让派冷却至少一个小时再端上桌。

词汇finally 最后；cool 冷却；at least 至少；serve 端上（饭菜） 语法let sb./sth. + 动词原形 表示“让……做……”；before serving 中 serving 是动名词，表示“在端上桌之前”。

My grandmother loved learning how to make food from other cultures.

我奶奶喜欢学习做其他文化中的食物。

词汇learn 学习；culture 文化 语法love doing sth. 喜欢做某事；how to make... 是“疑问词 + 不定式”，作 learning 的宾语。

“It's my way of making friends from around the world,” she said.

“这是我和世界各地的人交朋友的方式，”她说。

词汇way 方式；make friends 交朋友；around the world 世界各地 语法my way of doing sth. 做某事的方式；she said 是引述句，引语在后面时用逗号隔开。

Grandmother taught me the secret to cooking: Cook with love.

奶奶教了我烹饪的秘诀：用爱去做。

词汇secret 秘诀；cooking 烹饪 语法the secret to doing sth. 做某事的秘诀；冒号后是对 secret 的具体说明；Cook with love. 是祈使句。

Thanks to my grandmother, I love to try new food and new recipes from other countries and cultures.

多亏了奶奶，我喜欢尝试来自其他国家和文化的新食物与新食谱。

词汇try 尝试；recipe 食谱；country 国家 语法thanks to 表示原因；try doing/to do sth. 尝试做某事；from other countries and cultures 作后置定语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇题为 Food for Thought 的短文讲的是烹饪与爱。作者的奶奶常说“烹饪是看得见的爱”，正是因为奶奶，作者爱上了做饭，并把做饭当作与家人分享爱、创造回忆的方式。作者最喜爱的回忆之一是奶奶做的南瓜派：奶奶总在秋天做南瓜派，她那亲切的厨房是温暖身心、品味季节变化的地方，肉桂的香味至今让作者想起奶奶。奶奶给作者讲了她第一次做南瓜派的经历：她在美国读大学时住在寄宿家庭，女主人感恩节前后教她做南瓜派，多年后她又教给了作者。短文还给出了南瓜派食谱：先打散鸡蛋，加入糖、肉桂、盐、南瓜和奶油，倒进派皮，放进烤箱烤 40 到 50 分钟，最后冷却至少一个小时再端上桌。奶奶还喜欢学习其他文化的食物，她说这是和世界各地的人交朋友的方式，她教给作者的烹饪秘诀就是“用爱去做”。多亏了奶奶，作者也喜欢尝试来自其他国家和文化的新食物和新食谱。

English Summary

The passage, titled Food for Thought, is about cooking and love. The writer's grandmother always said that cooking is love made visible, and thanks to her the writer loves cooking as a way to share love with family and create memories. One of the writer's favourite memories is the grandmother's pumpkin pie, which she always made in autumn: her friendly kitchen was the perfect place to warm up and enjoy the changing seasons, and the smell of cinnamon still reminds the writer of her. The grandmother told the writer about her first pumpkin pie — when she was a college student in the US, she stayed with a host family and the hostess taught her how to make one around Thanksgiving, and years later she taught the writer too. A pumpkin pie recipe is also given: mix the eggs, add sugar, cinnamon, salt, pumpkin and cream, pour the mixture into the pie crust, bake for 40 to 50 minutes and let it cool for at least an hour. The grandmother also loved learning to make food from other cultures, saying it was her way of making friends from around the world; the secret she taught was to cook with love. Thanks to her, the writer loves trying new food and new recipes from other countries and cultures.

中文大意

这篇题为 The History of Pizza 的说明文介绍了披萨的历史。披萨是世界上最受欢迎的快餐之一，全世界每年要吃掉 50 亿个披萨。几千年前，希腊人、意大利人和波斯人就开始把蔬菜、油、奶酪等放在扁面包上，这就是披萨的古老形式。现代披萨可以追溯到两千多年前的古罗马人；18 世纪，加番茄酱和奶酪的披萨在意大利那不勒斯流行起来；后来意大利人把披萨带到美国，使它成为风靡世界的美食。电影明星和著名运动员与披萨的合影让披萨越来越受欢迎，还催生了各种比赛，比如今天的世界披萨大赛和各种各样的世界纪录——2017 年就有一项新的世界最长披萨纪录，长达 1.9 公里多。如今披萨风靡全球，各地都有自己的地方风味；文章最后问读者是喜欢吃还是做披萨、最喜欢哪种风格的披萨。

English Summary

This explanatory text, The History of Pizza, introduces the history of pizza. Pizza is one of the most popular fast foods in the world, and people eat 5 billion pizzas every year. Thousands of years ago, the Greeks, Italians and Persians began putting vegetables, oil, cheese and other things on flat bread — the ancient form of pizza. Modern pizza goes back to the Romans more than 2,000 years ago; in the 1700s a pizza with tomato sauce and cheese became popular in Naples, Italy, and later Italians brought it to America, where it became popular all over the world. Photographs of movie stars and famous athletes with pizza helped it become more and more popular and led to competitions such as the World Pizza Games and all kinds of world records — in 2017 there was a new record for the world's longest pizza, over 1.9 kilometres long. Today pizza is popular everywhere and different places have their own local pizzas; the text ends by asking readers whether they like eating or cooking pizza and what their favourite pizza style is.
