Section A 3a · What Can We Do for the Environment?

Section A 3a 主题：环境问题与建议 · 对话

A: What do you think of our environment?

你觉得我们的环境怎么样？

词汇environment 环境；think of 认为 语法What do you think of...? 询问看法。

B: There are some problems. For example, there is too much rubbish everywhere.

有一些问题。比如，到处都有太多垃圾。

词汇problem 问题；for example 例如；too much 太多；rubbish 垃圾 语法There be 句型；too much + 不可数名词。

A: Yes, and air pollution is serious in some cities.

是的，而且一些城市空气污染很严重。

词汇air pollution 空气污染；serious 严重的 语法主系表结构；in some cities 作地点状语。

B: What should we do to help?

我们应该怎么做来帮忙？

词汇help 帮忙 语法to help 作目的状语。

A: We can use fewer plastic bags and recycle more.

我们可以少用塑料袋，多回收。

词汇plastic bag 塑料袋；recycle 回收 语法fewer + 可数名词复数；can + 动词原形。

B: That's a good idea. We should also save water and electricity.

这主意不错。我们还应该节约用水和电。

词汇save 节约；electricity 电 语法should + 动词原形；also 表示“还”。

A: I think so. Small actions can make a big difference.

我也这么认为。小小的行动也能带来巨大改变。

词汇action 行动；make a difference 带来改变 语法I think so. 表示同意；make a big difference。

B: What about planting more trees?

多种些树怎么样？

词汇plant 种植；tree 树 语法What about doing sth.? 提建议。

A: Good idea! Trees can make the air cleaner.

好主意！树能让空气更干净。

词汇cleaner 更干净的 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词比较级。

B: Everyone should take part in protecting the environment.

每个人都应该参与保护环境。

词汇take part in 参与；protect 保护 语法take part in + 动名词。

A: Yes. If we work together, our city will be better.

是的。如果我们齐心协力，我们的城市会更好。

词汇work together 共同努力；better 更好的 语法if 引导条件状语从句，主将从现。

B: I agree. Let's start today.

我同意。让我们从今天开始。

词汇start 开始 语法Let's do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕环境问题与解决方法展开。两人都认为环境有问题：到处是垃圾，一些城市空气污染严重。他们讨论应该怎么做：少用塑料袋、多回收、节约水电、多种树，并认为小小的行动也能带来很大改变。最后他们一致认为每个人都应参与保护环境，齐心协力城市会更好，并从今天就开始行动。

English Summary

The conversation is about environmental problems and solutions. Both agree there are problems: rubbish everywhere and serious air pollution in some cities. They discuss what to do—use fewer plastic bags, recycle more, save water and electricity, and plant more trees—and believe small actions can make a big difference. They conclude that everyone should take part in protecting the environment and that if we work together the city will be better, so they start today.

Section B 1b · The Earth in Danger

Section B 1b 主题：地球危机 · 说明文

The earth is our home, but it is in danger.

地球是我们的家，但它正处在危险中。

词汇earth 地球；be in danger 处于危险中 语法but 转折；be in danger。

Many problems are caused by human activities.

很多问题是由人类活动造成的。

词汇activities 活动；be caused by 由……造成 语法被动语态 are caused by。

One problem is global warming.

一个问题是全球变暖。

词汇global warming 全球变暖 语法主系表结构。

Because of too much carbon dioxide, the earth is getting warmer and warmer.

由于二氧化碳过多，地球正变得越来越暖。

词汇carbon dioxide 二氧化碳；warmer and warmer 越来越暖 语法because of + 名词；比较级 and 比较级 表示“越来越……”。

Another problem is plastic pollution.

另一个问题是塑料污染。

词汇plastic pollution 塑料污染 语法another 表示“另一个”。

Too much plastic is thrown into the sea, and it kills many sea animals.

太多塑料被扔进大海，害死了许多海洋动物。

词汇throw into 扔进；sea animal 海洋动物；kill 杀死 语法is thrown 被动语态；and 连接并列分句。

We must take action now.

我们必须现在就采取行动。

词汇take action 采取行动 语法must + 动词原形。

First, we should reduce the use of plastic.

首先，我们应该减少塑料的使用。

词汇reduce 减少；use 使用 语法should + 动词原形。

Second, we can plant more trees to make the air cleaner.

其次，我们可以多种树让空气更干净。

词汇plant 种植；cleaner 更干净 语法to make 作目的状语；make + 宾语 + 形容词比较级。

Third, we should save energy and water.

第三，我们应该节约能源和水。

词汇energy 能源；save 节约 语法and 连接并列宾语。

If everyone does a little, the world will become a better place.

如果每个人都尽一点力，世界会变得更好。

词汇better 更好的 语法if 引导条件状语从句，主将从现。

It is our duty to protect the earth.

保护地球是我们的责任。

词汇duty 责任；protect 保护 语法It is + 名词 + to do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

短文说明地球面临的危机。地球是我们的家，却处在危险中，许多问题由人类活动造成。一是全球变暖：二氧化碳过多让地球越来越暖；二是塑料污染：太多塑料被扔进海里，害死许多海洋动物。因此我们必须行动：减少塑料使用、多种树让空气更清洁、节约能源和水。如果每个人都尽一点力，世界会更好，保护地球是我们的责任。

English Summary

The passage explains the danger facing the earth. The earth is our home but it is in danger, and many problems are caused by human activities. One is global warming: too much carbon dioxide makes the earth warmer and warmer. Another is plastic pollution: too much plastic is thrown into the sea and kills many sea animals. We must take action now—reduce the use of plastic, plant more trees to make the air cleaner, and save energy and water. If everyone does a little, the world will become a better place, and it is our duty to protect the earth.

Section B 3a · Our Green Club

Section B 3a 主题：校园环保社团 · 记叙与倡议

Our school started a green club last term.

上学期我们学校成立了一个绿色俱乐部。

词汇green club 绿色俱乐部；last term 上学期 语法一般过去时；start a club。

Many students joined it, and I am one of them.

许多学生加入了，我就是其中之一。

词汇join 加入；one of them 其中之一 语法one of + 代词。

In the club, we learn how to protect the environment.

在俱乐部里，我们学习如何保护环境。

词汇protect the environment 保护环境 语法learn how to do sth.。

We collect waste paper and bottles, and send them to be recycled.

我们收集废纸和瓶子，送去回收利用。

词汇waste paper 废纸；bottle 瓶子；recycle 回收 语法and 连接并列谓语；send sth. to be recycled。

We also grow vegetables in a small garden.

我们还在小花园里种菜。

词汇grow vegetables 种菜；garden 花园 语法in a small garden 作地点状语。

Every week, we give talks about saving water and electricity.

每周我们都会做关于节约用水用电的演讲。

词汇give a talk 做演讲 语法about + 动名词。

Last month, we cleaned up the river near our school.

上个月我们把学校附近的河清理干净了。

词汇clean up 打扫干净；near our school 我们学校附近 语法一般过去时；clean up。

We picked up a lot of rubbish, and we were tired but happy.

我们捡了很多垃圾，虽然很累但很开心。

词汇pick up 捡起；tired 累的 语法but 表转折。

Because of our work, the river looks much cleaner now.

由于我们的努力，那条河现在看起来干净多了。

词汇because of 由于；much cleaner 干净得多 语法because of + 名词；much 修饰比较级。

Small actions really make a big difference.

小小的行动真的能带来很大改变。

词汇action 行动；make a difference 带来改变 语法make a difference。

I hope more students will join us and help the earth.

我希望更多学生加入我们，一起帮助地球。

词汇join 加入；help 帮助 语法hope 后接宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍学校的绿色俱乐部。上学期成立，很多学生加入，作者也是其中之一。在俱乐部里他们学习如何保护环境：收集废纸和瓶子送去回收、在小花园种菜、每周做节水节电的演讲。上个月他们清理了学校附近的河，捡了很多垃圾，很累但很开心；因为他们的努力，河现在干净多了。作者相信小小行动能带来很大改变，希望更多学生加入帮助地球。

English Summary

The passage introduces the school's green club, started last term with many students joining, including the writer. In the club they learn how to protect the environment: they collect waste paper and bottles to be recycled, grow vegetables in a small garden, and give weekly talks about saving water and electricity. Last month they cleaned up the river near the school and picked up lots of rubbish; it was tiring but they were happy, and because of their work the river looks much cleaner now. The writer believes small actions really make a big difference and hopes more students will join to help the earth.
