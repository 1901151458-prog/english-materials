Section A 2a · What Will the Future Be Like?

Section A 2a 主题：未来预测 · 一般将来时

Helen: Hi, Jennifer. What's up?

嗨，珍妮弗。怎么了？

词汇what's up 怎么了；最近怎样 语法What's up? 是英语口语中常用的问候语，表示“怎么了/最近怎样”。

Jennifer: Oh, hi, Helen. I'm watching a video about future predictions.

哦，嗨，海伦。我在看一个关于未来预测的视频。

词汇video 视频；prediction 预测 语法现在进行时 am watching 表示此刻正在进行的动作；about... 作后置定语修饰 video。

Helen: So, what will the future be like?

那么，未来会是什么样子？

词汇future 未来；be like 像……一样 语法What will...be like? 用于询问将来的状况。

Jennifer: Well, there will be more people in cities, but the transport system will be more efficient. It means that there will be fewer traffic accidents.

嗯，城市里会有更多人，但交通系统会更高效。这意味着交通事故会更少。

词汇transport 交通运输；efficient 高效的；traffic accident 交通事故 语法There will be 是一般将来时的 There be 结构；fewer 修饰可数名词复数；It means that... 引导宾语从句。

Helen: Sounds great! Any other predictions?

听起来很棒！还有别的预测吗？

词汇sound 听起来；any other 其他的 语法Sounds great! 是省略句，完整为 It sounds great!；any other 后接可数名词复数。

Jennifer: Students probably won't go to a school in the future. They will study at home or any place they want.

将来学生们可能不会去学校上学。他们会在家或任何他们想待的地方学习。

词汇probably 很可能；study 学习；any place 任何地方 语法won't = will not；probably 表示可能性；or 连接两个并列的地点；they want 是定语从句。

Helen: Oh, but I really like going to school! Anything else?

哦，但我真的很喜欢去学校！还有别的吗？

词汇really 真的；anything else 还有别的吗 语法like doing sth. 表示“喜欢做某事”；Anything else? 是省略形式的问句。

Jennifer: And people will live longer.

而且人们会活得更久。

词汇live 生活；longer 更久 语法will + 动词原形；longer 是 long 的比较级。

Helen: Really? Why?

真的吗？为什么？

词汇really 真的；why 为什么 语法省略句，用于追问原因。

Jennifer: Because we'll have better health care technology in our homes. Machines will tell us how to solve small health problems before they become bigger.

因为我们在家里会有更好的医疗保健技术。机器会告诉我们如何在小健康问题变大之前解决它们。

词汇health care 医疗保健；technology 技术；machine 机器；solve 解决 语法because 引导原因状语从句；tell sb. how to do sth. 结构；before 引导时间状语从句。

Helen: That'll be great. How long will most people live?

那太好了。大多数人能活多久？

词汇how long 多久；most 大多数 语法That'll = That will；How long 用于询问时间长度。

Jennifer: Well, more people will live to be over 100 years old. Some may even live to be 150.

嗯，更多人会活到 100 岁以上。有些人甚至可能活到 150 岁。

词汇over 超过；may 可能；even 甚至 语法live to be... 表示“活到……岁”；may 表示可能的推测。

Helen: Wow, that sounds wonderful!

哇，那听起来太棒了！

词汇wonderful 极好的 语法sound 作系动词，后接形容词 wonderful 作表语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在海伦和珍妮弗之间。珍妮弗正在看一个关于未来预测的视频，海伦问她未来会是什么样子。珍妮弗说，城市里会有更多人，但交通系统会更高效，这意味着交通事故会更少。她还说，将来学生们可能不会去学校上学，而会在家或任何他们想待的地方学习——海伦却很喜欢去学校。珍妮弗接着说人们会活得更久，因为家里会有更好的医疗保健技术，机器能告诉我们如何在小健康问题变大之前解决它们。海伦问大多数人能活多久，珍妮弗说更多人会活到 100 岁以上，有些人甚至可能活到 150 岁，海伦觉得这一切听起来太棒了。

English Summary

The conversation takes place between Helen and Jennifer. Jennifer is watching a video about future predictions, and Helen asks what the future will be like. Jennifer says there will be more people in cities but the transport system will be more efficient, meaning there will be fewer traffic accidents. She also says students probably won't go to a school in the future but will study at home or any place they want — though Helen really likes going to school. Jennifer adds that people will live longer because we'll have better health care technology in our homes, and machines will tell us how to solve small health problems before they become bigger. When Helen asks how long most people will live, Jennifer says more people will live to be over 100 years old, and some may even live to be 150 — which sounds wonderful to Helen.

语法点 Grammar

Hi, Jennifer. What's up?

What's up? 是口语问候，表示“怎么了/最近怎样”。

I'm watching a video about future predictions.

现在进行时表示此刻正在进行的动作；about... 作后置定语。

So, what will the future be like?

What will...be like? 用于询问将来的状况。

there will be more people in cities

There will be 是一般将来时的 There be 结构，表示“将会有……”。

the transport system will be more efficient

will + 动词原形；more + 形容词原级构成比较级。

It means that there will be fewer traffic accidents.

It means that... 引导宾语从句；fewer 修饰可数名词复数。

Sounds great! Any other predictions?

Sounds great! 是省略句（It sounds great!）；any other + 可数名词复数。

Students probably won't go to a school in the future.

won't = will not；probably 表示“很可能”，放在助动词后。

They will study at home or any place they want.

or 连接两个并列的地点；they want 是定语从句，修饰 any place。

but I really like going to school!

like doing sth. 表示“喜欢做某事”；but 连接转折分句。

And people will live longer.

will + 动词原形；longer 是 long 的比较级。

Because we'll have better health care technology in our homes.

because 引导原因状语从句；we'll = we will。

Machines will tell us how to solve small health problems before they become bigger.

tell sb. how to do sth.；before 引导时间状语从句。

How long will most people live?

How long 询问多长时间；most people 作主语，谓语用复数形式。

Some may even live to be 150.

may 表示可能的推测；live to be... 表示“活到……岁”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

there will be more people in cities, but the transport system will be more efficient

城市里会有更多人，但交通系统会更高效。

There will be 是一般将来时的 There be 结构；but 连接两个并列分句；more efficient 是 efficient 的比较级。

Students probably won't go to a school in the future.

将来学生们可能不会去学校上学。

won't 是 will not 的缩写；probably 是副词，表示可能性，通常放在 will 之后、实义动词之前。

Machines will tell us how to solve small health problems before they become bigger.

机器会告诉我们如何在小健康问题变大之前解决它们。

tell sb. how to do sth. 表示“告诉某人如何做某事”；before 引导时间状语从句，从句用一般现在时表示将来。

Section B 1b · Interview: Ready for Tomorrow?

Section B 1b 主题：访谈 · 机器人、工作与人工智能

Ready for Tomorrow?

为明天做好准备？

词汇ready 准备好的；tomorrow 明天 语法访谈标题用问句形式，引发读者思考“是否已为未来做好准备”。

Host: Good evening, everyone! Our guest today is Dr Pete Roberts, author of the best-selling book Ready for Tomorrow. So welcome to our show, Dr Roberts.

大家晚上好！今天的嘉宾是皮特·罗伯茨博士，畅销书《为明天做好准备》的作者。欢迎来到我们的节目，罗伯茨博士。

词汇host 主持人；guest 嘉宾；author 作者；best-selling 畅销的 语法Our guest today is... 用于介绍嘉宾；author of... 作同位语，补充说明嘉宾身份。

Guest: Good evening. Thank you, it's a pleasure to be here.

晚上好。谢谢，很高兴来到这里。

词汇pleasure 荣幸 语法It's a pleasure to do sth. 表示“很荣幸做某事”。

Host: Dr Roberts, thank you for joining us. So, as a futurist, can you tell us how life will be different in 50 years?

罗伯茨博士，感谢您参加我们的节目。那么，作为一名未来学家，您能告诉我们 50 年后生活会有哪些不同吗？

词汇futurist 未来学家；different 不同的 语法thank sb. for doing sth.；as a futurist 作状语；how life will be different 是宾语从句。

Guest: Well, you'll see robots working everywhere.

嗯，你会看到机器人到处工作。

词汇robot 机器人；everywhere 到处 语法see sb./sth. doing sth. 表示“看到某人/某物正在做某事”。

Host: Can you give us some examples?

您能给我们举些例子吗？

词汇example 例子 语法give sb. sth. 是双宾语结构；Can you...? 用于礼貌请求。

Guest: Well, we'll have service robots. They will do almost all our housework. They will also help save lives when disasters and emergencies happen.

嗯，我们会有服务机器人。它们会做几乎所有的家务。当灾难和紧急情况发生时，它们还会帮助挽救生命。

词汇service 服务；housework 家务；disaster 灾难；emergency 紧急情况 语法help do sth. 中 do 前省略 to；when 引导时间状语从句。

Host: So, what jobs will humans still do in the future?

那么，将来人类还会从事哪些工作？

词汇job 工作；human 人类 语法what jobs 作 do 的宾语；still 表示“仍然”。

Guest: Good question! Some jobs today will disappear in the coming years. However, there will be more challenging jobs in the future, such as space pilots and AI experts.

问得好！现在的一些工作在未来几年会消失。不过，将来会有更多有挑战性的工作，比如太空飞行员和人工智能专家。

词汇disappear 消失；challenging 有挑战性的；such as 例如；AI expert 人工智能专家 语法in the coming years 表示“在未来几年”；However 表示转折；such as 用于举例。

Host: Do you think robots will replace humans one day?

您认为机器人有一天会取代人类吗？

词汇replace 取代；one day 有一天 语法Do you think + 宾语从句，从句用陈述语序。

Guest: No, I don't. Our creativity and emotional intelligence are important for relationships. AI will not replace humans for these things.

不，我不这么认为。我们的创造力和情商对人际关系很重要。人工智能不会在这些方面取代人类。

词汇creativity 创造力；emotional intelligence 情商；relationship 关系 语法I don't 是省略回答；be important for... 表示“对……重要”。

Host: You mentioned AI. How will AI change our world?

您提到了人工智能。人工智能将如何改变我们的世界？

词汇mention 提到；change 改变 语法How will...? 用于询问方式；mention 后接宾语。

Guest: It will change the world in many ways. For example, we won't need to go to the supermarket. Smart refrigerators will understand our needs. When we are almost out of some food, they will order it for us.

它会在很多方面改变世界。例如，我们将不需要去超市。智能冰箱会了解我们的需求。当我们某种食物快吃完时，它们会替我们订购。

词汇in many ways 在很多方面；supermarket 超市；refrigerator 冰箱；order 订购 语法need to do sth.；when 引导时间状语从句；order sth. for sb. 表示“为某人订购某物”。

Host: Wow, that'll save us a lot of time! Now, my last question: How can we prepare for the future?

哇，那会为我们节省很多时间！现在，我的最后一个问题：我们怎样才能为未来做好准备？

词汇save 节省；prepare for 为……做准备 语法that'll = that will；How can we...? 用于询问方式。

Guest: It's simple. Just accept change. Keep learning and working hard. Today's choices will influence your future.

很简单。接受变化。坚持学习和努力工作。今天的选择会影响你的未来。

词汇simple 简单的；accept 接受；choice 选择；influence 影响 语法祈使句 Just accept change. / Keep learning and working hard.；keep doing sth. 表示“坚持做某事”。

Host: Thank you for your time, Dr Roberts.

感谢您抽出时间，罗伯茨博士。

词汇thank you for your time 感谢您抽出时间 语法Thank you for... 表示感谢；your time 指对方付出的时间。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇电视访谈。主持人欢迎嘉宾皮特·罗伯茨博士——畅销书《为明天做好准备》的作者、一位未来学家。罗伯茨博士说，50 年后你会看到机器人到处工作：服务机器人会做几乎所有的家务，还能在灾难和紧急情况发生时帮助挽救生命。现在的一些工作在未来几年会消失，但将来会有更多有挑战性的工作，比如太空飞行员和人工智能专家。他不认为机器人会取代人类，因为创造力和情商对人际关系很重要。他还说，人工智能会在很多方面改变世界，例如我们不再需要去超市，智能冰箱会了解我们的需求，在食物快吃完时替我们订购。最后，他给我们的建议很简单：接受变化，坚持学习和努力工作，因为今天的选择会影响未来。

English Summary

This is a TV interview. The host welcomes Dr Pete Roberts, author of the best-selling book Ready for Tomorrow and a futurist. Dr Roberts says that in 50 years you'll see robots working everywhere: service robots will do almost all our housework and will also help save lives when disasters and emergencies happen. Some jobs today will disappear in the coming years, but there will be more challenging jobs such as space pilots and AI experts. He does not think robots will replace humans one day, because our creativity and emotional intelligence are important for relationships. He also says AI will change the world in many ways — for example, we won't need to go to the supermarket, and smart refrigerators will understand our needs and order food for us when we are almost out of it. Finally, his advice is simple: just accept change, keep learning and working hard, because today's choices will influence your future.

Reading Plus · Looking into the Future

Reading Plus 主题：科幻小说与未来想象

Looking into the Future

展望未来

词汇look into 展望；future 未来 语法文章标题，动名词短语点明主题：展望未来。

Are you curious about the future?

你对未来好奇吗？

词汇curious 好奇的；future 未来 语法be curious about... 表示“对……好奇”。

What do you think our life will be like in 50 years or more?

你认为 50 年或更久以后我们的生活会是怎样的？

词汇in 50 years or more 50 年或更久以后 语法Do you think + 宾语从句；What will...be like? 用于询问将来的状况。

To answer this, you can read a sci-fi book.

为了回答这个问题，你可以读一本科幻小说。

词汇sci-fi 科幻的；answer 回答 语法To answer this 是不定式短语作目的状语。

Perhaps you will have some ideas then.

也许那时你会有些想法。

词汇perhaps 也许；idea 想法 语法perhaps 表示可能性；then 指“那时”。

Today, many young readers are interested in science fiction.

如今，许多年轻读者对科幻小说感兴趣。

词汇be interested in 对……感兴趣；science fiction 科幻小说 语法be interested in 表示“对……感兴趣”。

But what makes it so popular?

但它为什么如此受欢迎呢？

词汇make 使；popular 受欢迎的 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补；what 作从句主语。

For one thing, science fiction gives us a chance to imagine what our future will be like.

一方面，科幻小说给了我们一个想象未来的机会。

词汇for one thing 一方面；chance 机会；imagine 想象 语法give sb. a chance to do sth.；what our future will be like 是宾语从句。

Anything is possible.

一切皆有可能。

词汇anything 任何事；possible 可能的 语法不定代词 anything 作主语，谓语用单数形式 is。

We can dream big.

我们可以大胆梦想。

词汇dream 梦想；big 大的 语法dream big 是固定表达，意为“志存高远、大胆梦想”。

Some people think there will be flying cars and robot servants.

有些人认为会有飞行汽车和机器人仆人。

词汇flying car 飞行汽车；robot servant 机器人仆人 语法think 后接宾语从句；there will be 表示“将会有”。

Others believe we will learn more about strange aliens and other worlds.

另一些人相信我们会了解更多关于奇怪外星人和其它世界的事情。

词汇believe 相信；alien 外星人；world 世界 语法Others 作主语，谓语用复数；believe 后接宾语从句。

Another reason is that sci-fi writers and their work can inspire us to create the future.

另一个原因是科幻作家及其作品能激励我们创造未来。

词汇reason 原因；writer 作家；inspire 激励；create 创造 语法that 引导表语从句；inspire sb. to do sth. 表示“激励某人做某事”。

In 1945, a sci-fi author named Arthur C. Clarke wrote about a system of space stations.

1945 年，一位名叫阿瑟·C·克拉克的科幻作家写到了一个太空站系统。

词汇named 名叫；space station 太空站 语法named Arthur C. Clarke 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 author。

In the article, he predicted the earth of the future would have many TV stations and people would send TV programmes around the world.

在那篇文章中，他预言未来的地球会有许多电视台，人们会在世界各地发送电视节目。

词汇predict 预言；programme 节目；around the world 世界各地 语法predicted 后接宾语从句，从句用过去将来时 would have / would send。

However, this was before TV was a common item at home!

然而，那时电视还没有成为家庭中的常见物品！

词汇however 然而；common 常见的；item 物品 语法before 引导时间状语从句；however 表示转折。

In a short story by Clarke, he suggested people in the future would use a system to talk with others around the world.

在克拉克的一篇短篇小说中，他提出未来的人们会使用一种系统与世界各地的人交谈。

词汇short story 短篇小说；suggest 提出；talk with 与……交谈 语法suggest 后接宾语从句；would use 表示过去将来时。

Amazingly, the short story was actually the inspiration for the internet!

令人惊叹的是，这篇短篇小说实际上启发了互联网的诞生！

词汇amazingly 令人惊叹地；actually 实际上；inspiration 灵感；the internet 互联网 语法the inspiration for... 表示“……的灵感来源”；amazingly 是副词，修饰整句。

Science fiction is also popular because it can make us think about our world in new ways.

科幻小说受欢迎还因为它能让我们以新的方式思考我们的世界。

词汇in new ways 以新的方式；think about 思考 语法because 引导原因状语从句；make sb. do sth. 中 do 用动词原形。

Although many sci-fi stories are set in imaginary worlds or societies, they can help us better understand the real world.

虽然许多科幻故事设定在想象中的世界或社会，但它们能帮助我们更好地理解现实世界。

词汇although 虽然；be set in 以……为背景；imaginary 想象中的；society 社会 语法although 引导让步状语从句；help sb. do sth.；better 修饰 understand。

They can also make us excited when we feel like we are living in a boring world.

当我们觉得自己生活在一个无聊的世界里时，它们也能让我们兴奋起来。

词汇excited 兴奋的；feel like 感觉好像；boring 无聊的 语法make sb. + 形容词作宾补；when 引导时间状语从句；feel like + 从句。

What will the future be like?

未来会是什么样子？

词汇future 未来 语法What will...be like? 用于询问将来的状况。

Sci-fi inspires us and helps us to understand what it could be like.

科幻小说激励我们，并帮助我们理解未来可能是什么样子。

词汇inspire 激励；understand 理解 语法and 连接两个并列谓语；what it could be like 作 understand 的宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇说明文探讨科幻小说为什么如此受欢迎。文章先提问：你对未来好奇吗？50 年或更久以后我们的生活会是怎样的？读一本科幻小说也许能给你一些想法。作者认为科幻小说受欢迎有三个原因：第一，它给了我们一个想象未来的机会，一切皆有可能，我们可以大胆梦想，比如飞行汽车、机器人仆人、外星人和其他世界；第二，科幻作家及其作品能激励我们创造未来，1945 年阿瑟·C·克拉克就曾写到太空站系统，预言未来的地球会有许多电视台、人们会在世界各地发送电视节目，而当时电视还不是家庭常见物品，他的一篇短篇小说甚至启发了互联网；第三，科幻小说能让我们以新的方式思考世界，虽然故事设定在想象中的世界或社会，却能帮助我们更好地理解现实世界，也能让我们在无聊时兴奋起来。最后文章总结：科幻小说激励我们，并帮助我们理解未来可能是什么样子。

English Summary

This expository text discusses why science fiction is so popular. It first asks whether you are curious about the future and what our life will be like in 50 years or more, suggesting that reading a sci-fi book may give you some ideas. The author gives three reasons. First, science fiction gives us a chance to imagine what our future will be like — anything is possible, and we can dream big about flying cars, robot servants, strange aliens and other worlds. Second, sci-fi writers and their work can inspire us to create the future: in 1945 Arthur C. Clarke wrote about a system of space stations and predicted that the earth of the future would have many TV stations and people would send TV programmes around the world, even though TV was not yet a common item at home, and one of his short stories was actually the inspiration for the internet. Third, science fiction can make us think about our world in new ways — although many stories are set in imaginary worlds or societies, they help us better understand the real world and can make us excited when we feel like we are living in a boring world. The text concludes that sci-fi inspires us and helps us understand what the future could be like.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

For one thing, science fiction gives us a chance to imagine what our future will be like.

一方面，科幻小说给了我们一个想象未来的机会。

for one thing 表示“一方面”；give sb. a chance to do sth.；what our future will be like 是宾语从句，用陈述语序。

Although many sci-fi stories are set in imaginary worlds or societies, they can help us better understand the real world.

虽然许多科幻故事设定在想象中的世界或社会，但它们能帮助我们更好地理解现实世界。

although 引导让步状语从句；be set in 表示“以……为背景”；help sb. do sth. 中 better 修饰 understand。

In a short story by Clarke, he suggested people in the future would use a system to talk with others around the world.

在克拉克的一篇短篇小说中，他提出未来的人们会使用一种系统与世界各地的人交谈。

suggest 后接宾语从句，从句用 would use 表示过去将来时；talk with sb. 表示“与某人交谈”。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题 Looking into the Future 和插图，猜一猜文章会谈论什么。
  • 快速浏览全文，找出作者认为科幻小说受欢迎的几方面原因。

读中

  • 边读边用 for one thing / another reason / also 等信号词梳理文章结构。
  • 画出文中关于阿瑟·C·克拉克的例子，思考它与互联网的关系。

读后

  • 用思维导图整理科幻小说受欢迎的三个原因。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面 3 篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What will the transport system be like in the future according to Jennifer?

  1. There will be fewer people in cities.
  2. It will be more efficient.
  3. It will be more expensive.
  4. There will be no transport system.
答案与解析

B 原文 Jennifer: … but the transport system will be more efficient. 可知未来交通系统会更高效。

2. Why will people live longer in the future?

  1. Because there will be more people in cities.
  2. Because students will study at home.
  3. Because there will be fewer traffic accidents.
  4. Because we'll have better health care technology in our homes, and machines will help solve small health problems.
答案与解析

D 原文 Because we'll have better health care technology in our homes. Machines will tell us how to solve small health problems before they become bigger. 可知人们会更长寿是因为家里有更好的医疗保健技术。

3. What is Jennifer watching at the beginning of the conversation?

  1. A video about future predictions.
  2. A sci-fi film.
  3. A video about schools.
  4. A health care programme.
答案与解析

A 原文 Jennifer: I'm watching a video about future predictions. 可知珍妮弗在看一个关于未来预测的视频。

4. What is Dr Pete Roberts' job?

  1. A science teacher.
  2. A robot engineer.
  3. A futurist.
  4. A TV host.
答案与解析

C 原文 Host: … as a futurist, can you tell us how life will be different in 50 years? 可知罗伯茨博士是一名未来学家。

5. Why does Dr Roberts think robots will not replace humans?

  1. Because robots are too expensive.
  2. Because our creativity and emotional intelligence are important for relationships.
  3. Because robots cannot do housework.
  4. Because humans will not need robots.
答案与解析

B 原文 Guest: Our creativity and emotional intelligence are important for relationships. AI will not replace humans for these things. 可知他认为机器人不会取代人类，因为创造力和情商对人际关系很重要。

6. How will smart refrigerators help us according to Dr Roberts?

  1. They will order food for us when we are almost out of it.
  2. They will cook meals for us.
  3. They will go to the supermarket for us.
  4. They will tell us what to buy at the supermarket.
答案与解析

A 原文 When we are almost out of some food, they will order it for us. 可知智能冰箱会在食物快吃完时替我们订购。

7. Which of the following makes science fiction popular?

  1. It tells people exactly what will happen.
  2. It teaches people how to write stories.
  3. It gives us a chance to imagine what our future will be like.
  4. It helps people live longer.
答案与解析

C 原文 For one thing, science fiction gives us a chance to imagine what our future will be like. 可知科幻小说受欢迎的原因之一是它给了我们想象未来的机会。

8. According to the text, what was the short story by Arthur C. Clarke actually the inspiration for?

  1. The space station.
  2. The flying car.
  3. The robot servant.
  4. The internet.
答案与解析

D 原文 Amazingly, the short story was actually the inspiration for the internet! 可知克拉克的短篇小说实际上启发了互联网的诞生。
