Section A 2a · Asking for Advice on Communication

Section A 2a 主题：寻求建议 · if 条件句（教材 P73 为听力填空）

Jason: Professor, thanks for the helpful speech on communication. I have a question. Sometimes my friends and I argue but don't know how to make up. What's the best way to communicate in this situation?

教授，谢谢您关于沟通的有益演讲。我有个问题。有时我和朋友们会争吵，却不知道怎么和好。在这种情况下，最好的沟通方式是什么？

词汇professor 教授；speech 演讲；communication 沟通；argue 争吵；make up 和好 语法thanks for + 名词/动名词表示感谢；but 连接转折；how to make up 是“疑问词 + 不定式”，作 know 的宾语；What's the best way to do sth.? 询问最好的方式。

Professor: If you want to solve the problem, a face-to-face talk usually works the best.

如果你想解决问题，面对面的交谈通常效果最好。

词汇solve 解决；face-to-face 面对面的；usually 通常 语法if 引导条件状语从句，从句用一般现在时，主句用一般现在时；work 在此表示“奏效，起作用”。

Jason: I understand, but meeting in person is not always easy.

我明白，但当面见面并不总是容易做到。

词汇in person 亲自；当面 语法动名词短语 meeting in person 作主语；but 连接转折分句。

Professor: If you can't meet, just call your friend. Some of you may prefer texting, but it takes longer and can make things worse if you're not careful with your words.

如果你们没法见面，就给你的朋友打个电话吧。你们中有些人可能更喜欢发短信，但发短信更费时间，而且如果不注意措辞，可能会让事情变得更糟。

词汇prefer 更喜欢；texting 发短信；worse 更糟的 语法if 条件句 + 祈使句主句；may 表可能；it 指 texting；make things worse 表示“把事情弄得更糟”。

Jason: But what if we argue more over the phone?

但如果我们打电话时吵得更厉害怎么办？

词汇what if 如果……怎么办；over the phone 在电话里 语法What if...? 用来提出假设，表示“如果……怎么办”。

Professor: If you stay calm and say sorry, it will help. If it is still hard to talk, take a break. Meet when you're both ready.

如果你保持冷静并道歉，会有帮助。如果还是很难开口，就先休息一下。等你们双方都准备好再见面。

词汇stay calm 保持冷静；say sorry 道歉；take a break 休息一下 语法if 从句用一般现在时表将来（主将从现），主句用 will；if + 祈使句；when 引导时间状语从句。

Jason: I'll call and try to meet. Because friendship is important. Thanks for the advice!

我会打电话试着见面。因为友谊很重要。谢谢您的建议！

词汇friendship 友谊；advice 建议 语法will 表将来；and 连接两个动词；because 引导原因；Thanks for... 表示感谢。

Professor: You're welcome.

不客气。

词汇You're welcome. 不客气 语法口语中回应感谢的常用表达。

English Summary

The 2a exercise on page 73 is a listening task with blanks; its complete text is in the Listening Scripts on pages 100–101 (the 2a conversation is on page 101), and this page presents the full text with the blanks filled in. The conversation is between Jason and the professor. After the professor's helpful speech on communication, Jason says he has a question: he and his friends sometimes argue but do not know how to make up, so he asks for the best way to communicate in this situation. The professor says a face-to-face talk usually works the best; if they cannot meet, Jason should just call his friend. He also warns that texting takes longer and can make things worse if people are not careful with their words. When Jason worries that they may argue more over the phone, the professor advises staying calm and saying sorry; if talking is still hard, they should take a break and meet when both are ready. Jason decides to call and try to meet because friendship is important, and he thanks the professor for the advice.

Section B 1b · How to Make a Better Conversation

Section B 1b 主题：沟通技巧 · 四条建议（教材 P76）

Do you get nervous when you talk to someone?

你和别人说话时会紧张吗？

词汇nervous 紧张的；talk to 和……说话 语法when 引导时间状语从句；一般疑问句。

Do you find it hard to have a conversation with a stranger?

你觉得和陌生人交谈很难吗？

词汇find it hard 觉得难；conversation 交谈；stranger 陌生人 语法find it + 形容词 + to do sth. 结构，it 是形式宾语，不定式才是真正的宾语。

If you have such worries, it is time for you to learn some more conversation skills.

如果你有这样的担忧，是时候学一些更多交谈技巧了。

词汇worry 担忧；it is time for sb. to do sth. 是某人做某事的时候了；skill 技巧 语法if 引导条件状语从句；it is time for sb. to do sth. 是固定句型。

Here are some tips for making better conversations.

下面是一些让交谈更愉快的建议。

词汇tip 建议；提示；介词 for + 动名词 语法Here are... 是完全倒装；for + 动名词表示用途。

Listen carefully and show interest in others.

认真倾听，并对他人表现出兴趣。

词汇carefully 认真地；show interest in 对……表现出兴趣 语法祈使句；and 连接两个并列的祈使动作。

One of the most important things is to listen to the person you speak to.

最重要的事情之一，就是倾听和你说话的人。

词汇one of the most important things 最重要的事情之一；listen to 听 语法one of + 最高级 + 复数名词；不定式 to listen 作表语；you speak to 是定语从句。

If you are a good listener, you will find some good points to ask questions about.

如果你善于倾听，你就会找到一些可以提问的好话题。

词汇listener 倾听者；point 要点 语法if 引导条件状语从句，从句用一般现在时，主句用 will；不定式 to ask 作后置定语。

For example, if someone tells you their hobby and you can ask questions about it, they will surely be happy to continue the conversation.

例如，如果某人告诉你他的爱好，而你能就此提问，他们一定会乐于继续交谈。

词汇for example 例如；hobby 爱好；continue 继续 语法and 连接两个并列条件从句；be happy to do sth. 表示“乐于做某事”。

Choose the right topic.

选择合适的话题。

词汇topic 话题；right 合适的 语法祈使句；形容词 right 修饰名词 topic。

Some of the best topics might be the weather, sports, music, films, food, or travel.

最好的话题可能是天气、运动、音乐、电影、食物或旅行。

词汇the weather 天气；sports 运动；travel 旅行 语法might be 表示可能性；并列列举用逗号和 or。

However, it is impolite to ask someone's age, weight, or other personal information.

然而，询问别人的年龄、体重或其他个人信息是不礼貌的。

词汇however 然而；impolite 不礼貌的；weight 体重；personal 个人的 语法It is + 形容词 + to do sth. 结构，it 是形式主语；however 表转折。

Also, if you don't agree with others, don't argue with them.

另外，如果你不同意别人的看法，也不要和他们争吵。

词汇agree with 同意；argue with 和……争吵 语法if + 否定祈使句；don't argue 表示建议。

Just move on to another topic.

换到另一个话题就好。

词汇move on to 转到；another 另一个 语法祈使句；move on to 是固定搭配。

Be honest and sincere.

要诚实、真诚。

词汇honest 诚实的；sincere 真诚的 语法祈使句；and 连接两个并列的形容词。

If you have no idea of a subject, just say “I don't know much about it.”

如果你对某个话题不了解，就直接说“我对此了解不多”。

词汇have no idea of 对……不了解；subject 话题；主题 语法if + 祈使句；直接引语作 say 的宾语。

If you want to make the other person happy, mention his or her strong points.

如果你想让对方高兴，就提一提他或她的优点。

词汇make sb. happy 使某人高兴；mention 提到；strong points 优点 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补；if + 祈使句。

But don't just pretend to be nice, because people can find out you are not telling the truth.

但不要只是假装友善，因为人们会发现你并没有说实话。

词汇pretend 假装；find out 发现；tell the truth 说实话 语法pretend to be 表示“假装是”；because 引导原因状语从句；find out 后接宾语从句。

Pay attention to your body language.

注意你的肢体语言。

词汇pay attention to 注意；body language 肢体语言 语法祈使句；pay attention to 是固定搭配。

Sometimes it is just as important as your words.

有时候它和你的话语一样重要。

词汇sometimes 有时候；as important as 和……一样重要 语法as + 形容词 + as 表示同级比较。

Don't be nervous.

不要紧张。

词汇nervous 紧张的 语法否定祈使句。

Stand up straight, smile, and be yourself—you have a lot to offer.

站直、微笑、做你自己——你能给予的有很多。

词汇stand up straight 站直；be yourself 做你自己；offer 提供 语法三个祈使句并列；破折号后补充说明。

If you really work at it, you will find having conversations much easier.

如果你真的下功夫练习，你会发现交谈容易得多。

词汇work at 致力于；下功夫；much easier 容易得多 语法if 从句用一般现在时，主句用 will；find + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补；动名词 having 作宾语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

教材 P76 的 1b 是一篇题为 How to Make a Better Conversation 的短文，作者写给想提高交谈能力的人，给出四条建议。第一，认真倾听并对他人表现出兴趣：善于倾听，才能找到可以提问的好话题，让对方乐于继续交谈。第二，选择合适的话题：天气、运动、音乐、电影、食物、旅行都是好话题，而询问年龄、体重等个人信息是不礼貌的；如果不同意别人，也不要争吵，换一个话题就好。第三，要诚实、真诚：不了解的话题就说“我了解不多”，想让他人高兴可以提一提他/她的优点，但不要假装友善，因为人们会发现你并没有说实话。第四，注意肢体语言：站直、微笑、做你自己。最后作者鼓励读者：只要真正下功夫练习，交谈就会容易得多。

English Summary

The 1b text on page 76, How to Make a Better Conversation, is written for people who want to improve their conversation skills, and it gives four tips. First, listen carefully and show interest in others: only a good listener can find good points to ask questions about, and people will be happy to continue the conversation. Second, choose the right topic: the weather, sports, music, films, food and travel are good topics, while asking about age, weight or other personal information is impolite; if you do not agree with others, do not argue and just move on to another topic. Third, be honest and sincere: say “I don't know much about it” when you have no idea of a subject, mention the other person's strong points if you want to make them happy, but do not just pretend to be nice because people can find out you are not telling the truth. Fourth, pay attention to your body language: stand up straight, smile and be yourself. In the end the author encourages readers that if they really work at it, they will find having conversations much easier.

Reading Plus · How to Stay Safe Online

Reading Plus 主题：网络安全 · 社交媒体（教材 P91 底部 + P92）

No matter whether you like it or not, social media is becoming a big part of many teenagers' lives.

无论你是否喜欢，社交媒体正成为许多青少年生活的重要组成部分。

词汇no matter whether 无论是否；social media 社交媒体；teenager 青少年 语法no matter whether... or not 引导让步状语从句；现在进行时表示正在发生的变化。

The internet is popular among youth people in China.

互联网在中国年轻人中很受欢迎。

词汇the internet 互联网；popular 受欢迎的；among 在……之中 语法be popular among 表示“在……中受欢迎”；一般现在时陈述事实。

According to a survey by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League, over 90 per cent of all Chinese minors use the internet and 50 per cent of these young people have made new friends online.

根据共青团中央的一项调查，超过 90% 的中国未成年人使用互联网，其中 50% 的年轻人在网上交了新朋友。

词汇according to 根据；survey 调查；minor 未成年人；per cent 百分比 语法according to 作状语；and 连接两个并列分句；现在完成时 have made 表示结果。

Social media allows people to connect with friends, share experiences, and stay on trend.

社交媒体让人们能够联系朋友、分享经历、跟上潮流。

词汇allow 允许；connect with 与……联系；experience 经历；trend 潮流 语法allow sb. to do sth.；and 连接三个并列的不定式。

However, the internet also has risks.

然而，互联网也有风险。

词汇however 然而；risk 风险 语法however 表示转折；also 表示“也”。

It is important to stay safe while having fun online.

在网上娱乐的同时保持安全很重要。

词汇stay safe 保持安全；have fun 玩得开心 语法It is + 形容词 + to do sth.，it 是形式主语；while + 动名词作时间状语。

Here are some simple tips.

下面是一些简单的建议。

词汇simple 简单的；tip 建议 语法Here are... 是完全倒装。

First, if you have accounts on different apps, make sure you use strong passwords.

第一，如果你在不同的应用上都有账户，一定要使用强密码。

词汇account 账户；app 应用程序；make sure 确保；password 密码 语法if + 祈使句；make sure (that) 后接宾语从句。

Avoid using easy-to-guess information, like your birthday or your pet's name.

避免使用容易被猜到的信息，比如你的生日或宠物的名字。

词汇avoid 避免；easy-to-guess 容易猜到的 语法avoid doing sth. 避免做某事；like 作介词，表示“像……这样的”。

Second, be careful when you talk to strangers online.

第二，在网上和陌生人交谈时要小心。

词汇be careful 小心；stranger 陌生人 语法be careful 是系表结构；when 引导时间状语从句。

Not every person online is friendly or telling the truth about themselves.

并不是每个网上的人都是友善的，或者都在说关于自己的实话。

词汇not every 并非每一个；friendly 友善的；tell the truth 说实话 语法not every 表示部分否定；or 连接两个并列的表语。

Don't share personal details, like your address, school name, or phone number.

不要分享个人信息，比如你的地址、学校名称或电话号码。

词汇share 分享；personal 个人的；detail 细节；address 地址 语法否定祈使句；like 举例。

Third, if you want to post anything online, think it over.

第三，如果你想在网上发布任何内容，要仔细考虑。

词汇post 发布；think it over 仔细考虑 语法if + 祈使句；think it over 中 it 是宾语，over 是副词。

Once it is out there, it is hard

一旦它发布出去，就很难……（本句跨 P91–P92 两页）

词汇once 一旦；out there 发布出去 语法本句在教材 P91 页末被分页断开，P92 页首接 “to know who sees it.”；once 引导时间状语从句。

to know who sees it.

……知道谁会看到它。

词汇who sees it 谁会看到它 语法接上页 P91 页末“…it is hard”，本句在 P92 页首继续；who 引导宾语从句。

Don't talk about things you may regret.

不要谈论你可能会后悔的事情。

词汇regret 后悔 语法否定祈使句；you may regret 是定语从句，修饰 things。

Lastly, keep your social media profiles private so only real friends can see your posts and information.

最后，把你的社交媒体主页设为私密，这样只有真正的朋友才能看到你发布的内容和信息。

词汇lastly 最后；keep... private 使……保持私密；profile 个人资料；post 帖子 语法keep + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补；so 引导目的状语从句。

Remember, by following these tips, you can stay safe on social media and the internet.

记住，遵循这些建议，你就能在社交媒体和互联网上保持安全。

词汇remember 记住；follow 遵循；by 通过 语法祈使句 Remember；by + 动名词表示方式；can 表能力。

Your parents and teachers want the best for you.

你的父母和老师希望把最好的给你。

词汇want the best for sb. 希望某人得到最好的 语法want sth. for sb. 结构；一般现在时。

Be aware of the risks and protect yourself.

要意识到风险并保护自己。

词汇be aware of 意识到；protect 保护 语法祈使句；and 连接两个并列动作。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇 Reading Plus 短文题为 How to Stay Safe Online，谈青少年如何安全使用社交媒体和互联网。文章首先指出，无论你是否喜欢，社交媒体正成为许多青少年生活的重要部分；据共青团中央的调查，超过 90% 的中国未成年人使用互联网，其中 50% 在网上交了新朋友。社交媒体让人联系朋友、分享经历、跟上潮流，但互联网也有风险，因此上网娱乐时要保持安全。文章给出四条建议：第一，为不同应用的账户设置强密码，避免用生日、宠物名等容易被猜到的信息；第二，和网上陌生人交谈要小心，不要分享地址、学校名称、电话号码等个人信息；第三，发布内容前要仔细考虑，因为一旦发布就很难知道谁会看到，不要谈论可能让自己后悔的事；最后，把社交媒体主页设为私密，只让真正的朋友看到。文章最后提醒读者：父母和老师都希望你平安，要意识到风险并保护好自己。

English Summary

This Reading Plus text, How to Stay Safe Online, tells teenagers how to use social media and the internet safely. It first points out that, whether we like it or not, social media is becoming a big part of many teenagers' lives. According to a survey by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League, over 90 per cent of all Chinese minors use the internet, and 50 per cent of these young people have made new friends online. Social media allows people to connect with friends, share experiences and stay on trend, but the internet also has risks, so it is important to stay safe while having fun online. The text gives four tips: first, use strong passwords for accounts on different apps and avoid easy-to-guess information such as birthdays or pet names; second, be careful when talking to strangers online and do not share personal details like your address, school name or phone number; third, think it over before you post anything, because once it is out there it is hard to know who sees it, and do not talk about things you may regret; lastly, keep your social media profiles private so only real friends can see your posts and information. In the end it reminds readers that parents and teachers want the best for them, so they should be aware of the risks and protect themselves.
