Section A 3a · A Favourite Memory

Section A 3a 主题：回忆与友谊 · 对话

A: What's your favourite memory of primary school?

你小学最难忘的记忆是什么？

词汇memory 记忆；primary school 小学 语法What's your favourite...? 询问喜好。

B: I remember the day we planted trees together.

我记得我们一起种树的那天。

词汇remember 记得；plant trees 种树 语法remember + 名词/从句 描述回忆。

A: That sounds great. Who was with you?

听起来很棒。谁和你们在一起？

词汇sound great 听起来很棒 语法Who was with you? 询问人物。

B: My teacher and all my classmates were there.

我的老师和所有同学都在那里。

词汇classmate 同学 语法There be / be there 表示在场。

A: What did you do there?

你们在那里做了什么？

词汇do 做 语法一般过去时特殊疑问句。

B: We dug holes, put in the young trees and watered them.

我们挖坑、放入小树、给它们浇水。

词汇dig 挖；hole 坑；water 浇水 语法一般过去时并列谓语。

A: Why is it your favourite memory?

为什么这是你最喜欢的记忆？

词汇why 为什么 语法why 引导特殊疑问句。

B: Because we worked as a team and had great fun.

因为我们团队合作，玩得很开心。

词汇work as a team 团队合作；have fun 玩得开心 语法because 引导原因状语从句。

A: Do you still keep in touch with your classmates?

你和同学们还保持联系吗？

词汇keep in touch with 与……保持联系 语法Do you still...? 一般现在时疑问句。

B: Yes, we meet once a year and share our stories.

是的，我们每年见一次，分享彼此的故事。

词汇once a year 每年一次；share 分享 语法一般现在时表示习惯。

A: Memories are like treasures, aren't they?

记忆就像珍宝，不是吗？

词汇treasure 珍宝；be like 像 语法反义疑问句 aren't they?。

B: Yes. Good memories make us smile.

是的。美好的记忆让我们微笑。

词汇make sb. smile 使某人微笑 语法make + 宾语 + 动词原形。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕小学时最难忘的记忆展开。一个人说记得大家一起种树的那天：老师和所有同学都在，大家挖坑、放入小树、浇水。被问为什么这是最喜欢的记忆时，回答说因为团队合作、玩得很开心。两人还谈到是否和同学保持联系——他们每年见一次，分享彼此的故事，并认为记忆像珍宝，美好的记忆让人微笑。

English Summary

The conversation is about a favourite primary school memory. One speaker remembers the day they planted trees together, with the teacher and all classmates there; they dug holes, put in the young trees and watered them. Asked why it is the favourite memory, the answer is that they worked as a team and had great fun. They also discuss whether they keep in touch with classmates—they meet once a year and share stories—and agree that memories are like treasures and good memories make us smile.

Section B 1b · The Words That Changed Me

Section B 1b 主题：成长记忆 · 老师的鼓励

When I was in primary school, I was afraid of speaking in public.

上小学时，我害怕当众发言。

词汇be afraid of 害怕；in public 当众 语法when 引导时间状语从句；be afraid of + 动名词。

Every time the teacher asked me to answer, my face turned red.

每次老师让我回答，我的脸都会变红。

词汇every time 每次；turn red 变红 语法every time 引导时间状语从句。

One day, my teacher said to me, "Take your time. We all learn step by step."

有一天，老师对我说：“别急。我们都是循序渐进地学习。”

词汇take your time 慢慢来；step by step 一步一步地 语法直接引语；祈使句。

Those words changed me.

那些话改变了我。

词汇word 话语；change 改变 语法一般过去时。

I started to raise my hand in class.

我开始在课上举手。

词汇raise one's hand 举手 语法start to do sth.。

I made mistakes, but nobody laughed at me.

我犯过错，但没有人嘲笑我。

词汇make mistakes 犯错；laugh at 嘲笑 语法but 转折；nobody 作主语，谓语用单数。

Instead, my classmates encouraged me.

相反，同学们鼓励我。

词汇instead 相反；encourage 鼓励 语法instead 表示转折。

Now I am not afraid anymore, and I can speak in front of the whole school.

现在我一点也不害怕了，还能在全校师生面前发言。

词汇not...anymore 不再；in front of 在……前面 语法not...anymore 表示“不再”。

I will never forget my teacher's smile and those kind words.

我永远不会忘记老师的微笑和那些温暖的话语。

词汇never forget 永不忘记 语法will never forget。

That memory gave me courage and confidence.

那段记忆给了我勇气和信心。

词汇courage 勇气；confidence 信心 语法give sb. sth.。

It taught me that mistakes are part of growing up.

它教会我，犯错是成长的一部分。

词汇part of 一部分；grow up 成长 语法teach sb. that... 引导宾语从句。

Every time I feel nervous, I remember those words.

每当我紧张时，我就会想起那些话。

词汇nervous 紧张的 语法every time 引导时间状语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文回忆作者小学时的一段经历。那时他害怕当众发言，每次被老师提问脸都会变红。一天老师说“别急，我们都是循序渐进的”，那些话改变了他。他开始在课上举手，虽然会犯错，但没人嘲笑他，反而鼓励他。现在他已不再害怕，能在全校师生面前发言。他永远不会忘记老师的微笑和温暖的话语，那段记忆给了他勇气和信心，也让他明白犯错是成长的一部分。

English Summary

The passage recalls an experience from primary school. The writer was afraid of speaking in public and his face turned red whenever the teacher asked him to answer. One day the teacher said, "Take your time. We all learn step by step," and those words changed him. He began to raise his hand in class; he made mistakes, but nobody laughed at him—instead, classmates encouraged him. Now he is no longer afraid and can speak in front of the whole school. He will never forget the teacher's smile and kind words: that memory gave him courage and confidence and taught him that mistakes are part of growing up.

Section B 3a · Dear Diary

Section B 3a 主题：日记 · 珍惜当下

Dear Diary,

亲爱的日记，

词汇dear 亲爱的（日记称呼） 语法日记开头。

Today, I found my old photo album and looked through it.

今天，我找到了旧相册并翻看了一遍。

词汇photo album 相册；look through 浏览 语法一般过去时；and 连接并列谓语。

There were many pictures of my primary school days.

里面有很多我小学时光的照片。

词汇picture 照片 语法There be 句型。

I saw myself in a small uniform, smiling at the camera.

我看到小小的自己穿着校服，对着镜头微笑。

词汇uniform 校服；camera 相机；smile at 对……微笑 语法smiling 现在分词作伴随状语。

At that time, everything seemed simple and happy.

那时候，一切似乎都很简单快乐。

词汇at that time 那时；seem 似乎 语法seem + adj.。

Now I am in junior high school, and life is busier but more interesting.

现在我在上初中，生活更忙但也更有趣。

词汇junior high school 初中；busier 更忙的 语法比较级 busier / more interesting。

Sometimes I miss the days when we had no exams.

有时我想念那些没有考试的日子。

词汇miss 想念；exam 考试 语法when 引导定语从句。

But I also enjoy learning new things and meeting new friends.

但我也喜欢学习新事物、结识新朋友。

词汇enjoy doing 喜欢做；meet 结识 语法enjoy + 动名词并列。

Grandma often says, "Cherish every day, because it will become a memory."

奶奶常说：“珍惜每一天，因为它会成为回忆。”

词汇cherish 珍惜；memory 回忆 语法直接引语；because 引导原因状语从句。

I think she is right.

我觉得她说得对。

词汇right 正确的 语法I think 后接宾语从句。

So I will keep this diary and record my days.

所以我会坚持写日记，记录我的日子。

词汇keep doing 坚持做；record 记录 语法keep doing sth.。

Years later, these words will be my beautiful memories.

多年以后，这些文字将成为我美好的回忆。

词汇years later 多年以后 语法will be 一般将来时。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇日记。作者今天翻看旧相册，看到许多小学时的照片：自己穿着小校服对着镜头微笑，那时一切似乎都很简单快乐。现在他上了初中，生活更忙也更有趣；有时会想念没有考试的日子，但也喜欢学习新事物、结识新朋友。奶奶常说“珍惜每一天，因为它会成为回忆”，作者认同，因此决定坚持写日记记录日子，认为多年以后这些文字会成为美好的回忆。

English Summary

This is a diary entry. The writer looked through an old photo album and saw many pictures from primary school days—himself in a small uniform smiling at the camera, when everything seemed simple and happy. Now in junior high school, life is busier but more interesting; sometimes he misses the days without exams, but he also enjoys learning new things and meeting new friends. Grandma often says, "Cherish every day, because it will become a memory," and the writer agrees, so he will keep the diary and record his days—years later these words will be his beautiful memories.
