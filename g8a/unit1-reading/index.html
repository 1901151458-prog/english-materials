Section A 3a · Holiday Talk with Indefinite Pronouns

Section A 3a 主题：假期见闻 · 不定代词

A: Did you go anywhere interesting on your holiday?

你假期去了什么有趣的地方吗？

词汇anywhere 任何地方；interesting 有趣的 语法anywhere 用于疑问句；形容词修饰不定代词时后置。

B: Yes, I went to Yunnan.

去了，我去了云南。

词汇Yunnan 云南 语法一般过去时 went。

C: Did you do anything interesting?

你做了什么有趣的事吗？

词汇anything 任何事；interesting 有趣的 语法anything 用于疑问句；形容词后置。

D: No, nothing special. But I met someone interesting.

没有特别的，但我遇到了一个有趣的人。

词汇nothing 没有什么；special 特别的；someone 某人 语法nothing / someone 作宾语；形容词后置。

E: Did you go with anyone?

你和别人一起去的吗？

词汇anyone 任何人；go with 和……一起去 语法anyone 用于疑问句。

F: Yes, I went with my family.

是的，我和家人一起去的。

词汇family 家人 语法go with sb.。

G: Did you eat anything special?

你吃了什么特别的东西吗？

词汇eat 吃；special 特别的 语法anything 用于疑问句；形容词后置修饰。

H: Yes, we did! Everything tasted good, especially the steamed chicken soup.

吃了！每一样都很好吃，尤其是汽锅鸡。

词汇everything 每样东西；taste 尝起来；especially 尤其；steamed 蒸的 语法Everything 作主语，谓语用单数 tasted；especially 修饰名词。

I: Everyone loved it.

大家都很喜欢。

词汇everyone 每个人；love 喜欢 语法everyone 作主语，谓语用单数形式 loved。

J: I didn't go anywhere special, but I tried something new—I worked as a guide at a museum.

我没去什么特别的地方，但我尝试了新事物——我在博物馆当导游。

词汇anywhere 任何地方；try 尝试；guide 导游 语法didn't go anywhere 否定句中用 anywhere；worked as 表示“作为……工作”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这组句子围绕假期见闻展开，集中练习不定代词。有人问假期是否去了有趣的地方，回答说去了云南；有人问有没有做有趣的事，回答没有特别的，但遇到了一个有趣的人；还有人问是否与人同去、是否吃了特别的东西，回答说和家人一起，吃的东西都很好吃，尤其是汽锅鸡，大家都很喜欢。最后一句说自己没去特别的地方，但尝试了新事物——在博物馆当导游。

English Summary

These sentences are about holiday experiences and practise indefinite pronouns. One person asks whether the other went anywhere interesting and hears the answer Yunnan; another asks whether anything interesting was done, and the reply is nothing special but meeting someone interesting. The talk also covers going with anyone, eating anything special (everything tasted good, especially the steamed chicken soup, and everyone loved it), and finally trying something new—working as a guide at a museum.

Section B 1b · Vincent's Moscow Diary

Section B 1b 主题：俄罗斯旅行日记 · 一般过去时

17 July

7 月 17 日

词汇July 七月 语法日记日期。

Today was our first day to visit Moscow.

今天是我们游览莫斯科的第一天。

词汇Moscow 莫斯科 语法the first day to do sth.；一般过去时。

The weather was fine and everyone felt excited to visit some famous places in Russia.

天气很好，每个人都为能参观俄罗斯的名胜感到兴奋。

词汇fine 晴朗的；excited 兴奋的；famous 著名的；Russia 俄罗斯 语法everyone 作主语，谓语用单数；be excited to do sth.。

In the morning, we went to the Victory Museum.

上午我们去了胜利博物馆。

词汇the Victory Museum 胜利博物馆 语法一般过去时；in the morning 作时间状语。

The Russian people built this museum to remember their fight against the Nazis during World War II.

俄罗斯人民建立这座博物馆，是为了铭记二战期间与纳粹的战斗。

词汇build 建立；remember 铭记；fight 战斗；Nazi 纳粹 语法to remember 作目的状语；fight against 与……作战。

As we walked through the halls, the tour guide told us stories about the war.

当我们走过展厅时，导游给我们讲战争的故事。

词汇walk through 走过；tour guide 导游；war 战争 语法as 引导时间状语从句；tell sb. sth.。

In one hall, I saw an artwork with thousands of glass "tears" falling down.

在一个展厅里，我看到一件艺术品：成千上万的玻璃“眼泪”正落下来。

词汇artwork 艺术品；thousands of 成千上万的；tear 眼泪；fall down 落下 语法with + 名词 + doing 是 with 复合结构；现在分词作宾补。

It reminds us that war is terrible and peace does not come easily.

它提醒我们，战争是可怕的，和平来之不易。

词汇remind 提醒；terrible 可怕的；peace 和平；easily 容易地 语法remind sb. that...；that 引导宾语从句。

We should not forget that.

我们不应该忘记这一点。

词汇forget 忘记 语法should not do。

At noon, we had lunch at a restaurant near the park.

中午，我们在公园附近的一家餐厅吃午饭。

词汇at noon 中午；restaurant 餐厅 语法have lunch；near the park 作后置定语。

I liked the ice cream there, so I ate a lot of it.

我喜欢那里的冰淇淋，所以吃了很多。

词汇ice cream 冰淇淋；a lot of 很多 语法so 连接结果；一般过去时。

But, during dinner, I didn't feel like eating because I felt sick.

但晚饭时我不想吃东西，因为我觉得不舒服。

词汇during 在……期间；feel like doing 想要做；sick 生病的；不舒服的 语法feel like doing sth.；because 引导原因状语从句。

So now, I just want to get a good night's sleep.

所以现在，我只想好好睡一觉。

词汇get a good night's sleep 睡个好觉 语法want to do sth.。

18 July

7 月 18 日

词汇July 七月 语法日记日期。

It rained last night, so this morning the air was fresh and cool.

昨晚下雨了，所以今天早上空气清新凉爽。

词汇rain 下雨；fresh 清新的；cool 凉爽的 语法so 连接结果；一般过去时。

I felt much better today.

我今天感觉好多了。

词汇feel better 感觉好些 语法much 修饰比较级 better。

We took the Moscow Metro to travel around.

我们乘坐莫斯科地铁游览。

词汇take 乘坐；the Moscow Metro 莫斯科地铁；travel around 游览 语法to travel 作目的状语。

The metro stations are really beautiful.

地铁站真的很漂亮。

词汇metro station 地铁站 语法主系表结构。

Sometimes I felt like I was walking inside a palace.

有时我感觉自己仿佛走进了一座宫殿。

词汇feel like 感觉好像；inside 在里面；palace 宫殿 语法feel like + 从句；过去进行时 was walking。

After that, we went to Red Square.

然后我们去了红场。

词汇Red Square 红场 语法after that 表示顺序。

We saw some fantastic buildings there.

我们在那里看到一些很棒的建筑。

词汇fantastic 极好的；building 建筑 语法一般过去时。

In the afternoon, we arrived at a school and met a group of Russian students.

下午，我们到了一所学校，遇见了一群俄罗斯学生。

词汇arrive at 到达；a group of 一群 语法arrive at + 小地点。

We played games and soon became friends.

我们一起玩游戏，很快就成了朋友。

词汇play games 玩游戏；become friends 成为朋友 语法and 连接并列谓语。

Later, someone took out his accordion, and soon everyone got together to sing and dance.

后来，有人拿出了手风琴，很快大家都聚在一起唱歌跳舞。

词汇take out 拿出；accordion 手风琴；get together 聚在一起 语法someone 作主语，谓语用单数 took out。

We were so happy that we didn't notice the time.

我们太高兴了，都没注意到时间。

词汇notice 注意；time 时间 语法so...that... 引导结果状语从句。

It was such a good experience!

这真是一次非常棒的体验！

词汇such 如此；experience 经历 语法such a/an + adj. + 可数名词单数。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是文森特 7 月 17—18 日的两篇旅行日记。17 日他们在莫斯科：上午参观胜利博物馆，了解俄罗斯人民二战期间抗击纳粹的历史，看到一件由成千上万玻璃“眼泪”组成的艺术品，体会到战争可怕、和平来之不易；中午在餐厅吃了很多冰淇淋，晚上却因不舒服不想吃饭。18 日下雨后空气清新，他坐莫斯科地铁游览，觉得地铁站像宫殿；之后去了红场，看到很棒的建筑；下午到一所学校，和俄罗斯学生玩游戏、成为朋友，大家一起拉手风琴唱歌跳舞，开心得忘了时间。

English Summary

These are Vincent's diary entries for 17 and 18 July. On 17 July they visited Moscow: in the morning they went to the Victory Museum, learned how Russians fought against the Nazis in World War II and saw an artwork with thousands of glass "tears" falling down, reminding them that war is terrible and peace does not come easily; at noon they had lunch near the park and he ate a lot of ice cream, but during dinner he did not feel like eating because he felt sick. On 18 July, after rain, the air was fresh and cool; they travelled by the Moscow Metro, whose stations felt like palaces, then went to Red Square to see fantastic buildings; in the afternoon they arrived at a school, played games with Russian students and soon became friends, and everyone sang and danced to an accordion, so happy that they forgot the time.

Section B 3a · My Holiday in Scotland

Section B 3a 主题：旅行故事 · 一般过去时叙事

My Holiday in Scotland

我的苏格兰假期

词汇Scotland 苏格兰 语法标题。

Last autumn, my family took a train to Scotland for a holiday.

去年秋天，我们一家坐火车去苏格兰度假。

词汇take a train 坐火车；for a holiday 度假 语法一般过去时；take a train to...。

We stayed in a comfortable house in the countryside with beautiful scenery.

我们住在乡下的一栋舒适的房子里，风景很美。

词汇comfortable 舒适的；countryside 乡下；scenery 风景 语法stay in；with beautiful scenery 作后置定语。

I live in a big city, so everything seemed so different.

我住在大城市，所以一切看起来都很不同。

词汇seem 看起来；different 不同的 语法seem + adj.；so 连接结果。

For example, beside the house, there was a huge forest.

比如，房子旁边有一大片森林。

词汇for example 例如；huge 巨大的；forest 森林 语法There be 句型；beside 表示位置。

On the last day, early in the morning, I went to the forest to explore.

最后一天一大早，我去森林里探索。

词汇on the last day 在最后一天；early 早；explore 探索 语法to explore 作目的状语。

While there, something made a noise.

在那里的时候，有东西发出了响声。

词汇while 当……时；make a noise 发出响声 语法While there 是省略句（while I was there）。

I turned around and was surprised to see a deer.

我转过身，惊讶地看到一只鹿。

词汇turn around 转身；be surprised to do 惊讶地做；deer 鹿 语法be surprised to do sth.；and 连接并列谓语。

I ran home and told my parents what I saw.

我跑回家，把看到的事告诉了父母。

词汇run home 跑回家；what I saw 我看到的 语法what 引导宾语从句作 told 的宾语。

My mum smiled and said, "The deer is probably looking for food and hoping to get some from you."

妈妈笑了笑说：“这头鹿大概在找吃的，希望你能给它一些。”

词汇probably 大概；look for 寻找；hope to do 希望做 语法现在进行时表示此刻正在进行的推测；hope to do sth.。

What a wonderful experience I had!

我经历了一件多么美妙的事啊！

词汇wonderful 美妙的；experience 经历 语法What a/an + adj. + 可数名词单数 + 主谓 感叹句。

I really hope I can go back again.

我真希望我能再去一次。

词汇go back 回去 语法hope 后接宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是吉娜记录假期的一篇短文。去年秋天全家坐火车去苏格兰度假，住在乡下舒适的房子里，风景优美。她住在大城市，所以觉得一切都很不同——房子旁还有一大片森林。最后一天清晨她去森林探索，有东西发出响声，她转身惊讶地看到一头鹿。她跑回家告诉父母，妈妈笑着说这头鹿大概在找吃的，希望她给点食物。她觉得这是很棒的经历，希望还能再去。

English Summary

This is Gina's holiday story. Last autumn her family took a train to Scotland and stayed in a comfortable house in the countryside with beautiful scenery. She lives in a big city, so everything seemed different—there was even a huge forest beside the house. On the last day, early in the morning, she went to the forest to explore; something made a noise, and she turned around and was surprised to see a deer. She ran home and told her parents, and her mum smiled and said the deer was probably looking for food and hoping to get some from her. Gina thought it was a wonderful experience and hopes to go back.

语法点 Grammar

Last autumn, my family took a train to Scotland for a holiday.

一般过去时；take a train to。

We stayed in a comfortable house in the countryside with beautiful scenery.

stay in；with... 作后置定语。

everything seemed so different

seem + adj.。

beside the house, there was a huge forest

There be 句型；beside 表示位置。

On the last day, early in the morning, I went to the forest to explore.

to explore 作目的状语。

While there, something made a noise.

while 引导的省略句；something 作主语，谓语用单数。

I turned around and was surprised to see a deer.

be surprised to do sth.；and 连接并列谓语。

I ran home and told my parents what I saw.

what 引导宾语从句。

The deer is probably looking for food and hoping to get some from you.

现在进行时；look for；hope to do sth.。

What a wonderful experience I had!

What a/an + adj. + 可数名词单数 + 主谓 感叹句。

I really hope I can go back again.

hope 后接宾语从句。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

We stayed in a comfortable house in the countryside with beautiful scenery.

我们住在乡下的一栋舒适的房子里，风景很美。

with beautiful scenery 是介词短语作后置定语。

I ran home and told my parents what I saw.

我跑回家，把看到的事告诉了父母。

what 引导宾语从句，作 told 的宾语。

What a wonderful experience I had!

我经历了一件多么美妙的事啊！

What a/an + adj. + 可数名词单数 + 主语 + 谓语 感叹句。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题 My Holiday in Scotland，猜会写哪些经历。
  • 注意叙事顺序：时间—地点—事件—感受。

读中

  • 找出旅行的时间、地点和住宿。
  • 画出最特别的一次经历。

读后

  • 按“开头—经过—感受”复述故事。
  • 仿写一段自己的假期经历。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. Where did the speaker go on holiday in Section A?

  1. Yunnan.
  2. Shanghai.
  3. Beijing.
  4. Hainan.
答案与解析

A 原文：Yes, I went to Yunnan.

2. What did the speaker meet in Section A?

  1. Someone boring.
  2. Someone interesting.
  3. Nothing.
  4. A deer.
答案与解析

B 原文：But I met someone interesting.

3. Who did the speaker go with in Section A?

  1. Friends.
  2. His family.
  3. Teachers.
  4. Nobody.
答案与解析

B 原文：Yes, I went with my family.

4. What did Vincent do at noon on 17 July?

  1. He went to Red Square.
  2. He had lunch at a restaurant and ate a lot of ice cream.
  3. He visited a school.
  4. He took the metro.
答案与解析

B 原文：At noon, we had lunch at a restaurant near the park. I liked the ice cream there, so I ate a lot of it.

5. Why didn't Vincent feel like eating during dinner?

  1. He was too excited.
  2. He felt sick.
  3. He was not hungry.
  4. He had no time.
答案与解析

B 原文：during dinner, I didn't feel like eating because I felt sick.

6. What did Vincent do with Russian students?

  1. He taught them English.
  2. He played games and they sang and danced together.
  3. He took photos of them.
  4. He had lunch with them.
答案与解析

B 原文：We played games and soon became friends ... everyone got together to sing and dance.

7. Where did Gina go with her family last autumn?

  1. England.
  2. Scotland.
  3. Wales.
  4. Ireland.
答案与解析

B 原文：Last autumn, my family took a train to Scotland for a holiday.

8. What do the three texts have in common?

  1. They are all about holiday experiences.
  2. They are all about food.
  3. They are all about school life.
  4. They are all about sports.
答案与解析

A 三篇文章分别谈假期见闻、旅行日记和假期故事，主题都是假期经历。
