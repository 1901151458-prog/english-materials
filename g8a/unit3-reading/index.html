Section A 3a · Different but Close Friends

Section A 3a 主题：朋友差异 · 比较级

A: How are you different from your best friend?

你和最好的朋友有哪些不同？

词汇be different from 与……不同；best friend 最好的朋友 语法How are you different from...? 询问差异。

B: Well, I'm quieter than her, but she's more outgoing than me.

我比她安静，但她比我外向。

词汇quieter 更安静的；outgoing 外向的 语法比较级 quieter / more outgoing；than 表示比较。

A: Who is more hard-working, you or your friend?

谁更用功，你还是你朋友？

词汇hard-working 用功的；more 更 语法多音节形容词用 more 构成比较级；选择疑问句。

B: My friend is. She works much harder than me.

是我朋友。她比我用功得多。

词汇work hard 用功；much 得多 语法much 修饰比较级 harder。

A: Do you have the same hobbies?

你们有相同的爱好吗？

词汇the same 相同的；hobby 爱好 语法the same + 名词。

B: Not really. She likes reading, but I prefer sports.

不太一样。她喜欢阅读，我更喜欢运动。

词汇prefer 更喜欢；sports 运动 语法but 表示对比；prefer sth.。

A: My friend and I are quite different, but we get along well.

我和朋友很不一样，但我们相处得很好。

词汇quite 相当；get along well 相处得好 语法but 转折；get along well with sb. 的省略。

A: She is taller than me, and I am heavier than her.

她比我高，我比她重。

词汇taller 更高的；heavier 更重的 语法比较级 taller / heavier。

B: It's important to accept each other's differences.

接受彼此的不同很重要。

词汇accept 接受；difference 不同 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.；each other's 所有格。

B: Good friends don't have to be the same.

好朋友不必完全一样。

词汇have to 必须；the same 相同 语法don't have to 表示“不必”。

A: I agree. Differences make our friendship more interesting.

我同意。不同让我们的友谊更有趣。

词汇agree 同意；friendship 友谊；interesting 有趣的 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词；比较级 more interesting。

B: That's true.

确实如此。

词汇true 真实的 语法That's true. 表示认同。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这组句子围绕朋友之间的差异展开，集中使用比较级。一个更安静，另一个更外向、更用功；爱好也不完全相同，一个喜欢阅读，另一个更喜欢运动；身高体重也不同。但两人都认同：接受彼此的不同很重要，好朋友不必完全一样，正因不同友谊才更有趣。

English Summary

These sentences are about differences between friends and use comparatives: one friend is quieter, the other more outgoing and hard-working; their hobbies differ (reading vs. sports), and they differ in height and weight. Both agree that it is important to accept each other's differences, that good friends don't have to be the same, and that differences make the friendship more interesting.

I'm quieter than her, but she's more outgoing than me.

比较级 quieter；多音节用 more outgoing。

Who is more hard-working, you or your friend?

选择疑问句；more hard-working 构成比较级。

She works much harder than me.

much 修饰比较级 harder。

Do you have the same hobbies?

the same + 名词。

we get along well

get along well 是固定短语。

Good friends don't have to be the same.

don't have to 表示“不必”。

make + 宾语 + 形容词比较级。

比较级 quieter / more outgoing；than 后接比较对象。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题和图片，猜两个朋友有哪些不同。
  • 注意比较级信号词：than / more / much。

读中

  • 找出两人在性格、爱好、外形上的差异。
  • 圈出所有的比较级形式。

读后

  • 用比较级介绍自己和朋友。
  • 说说朋友之间的不同为什么是好事。

Section B 1b · Twin Brothers Tim and Tom

Section B 1b 主题：双胞胎兄弟 · 差异与互补

Twin brothers Tim and Tom look the same, but they are very different.

双胞胎兄弟蒂姆和汤姆看起来一样，但他们非常不同。

词汇twin 双胞胎的；look the same 看起来一样 语法but 转折；look the same。

Tim is more outgoing and loves making friends.

蒂姆更外向，喜欢交朋友。

词汇outgoing 外向的；make friends 交朋友 语法more outgoing 比较级；love doing sth.。

Tom is quieter and enjoys reading at home.

汤姆更安静，喜欢在家读书。

词汇quiet 安静的；enjoy 喜欢 语法quieter 比较级；enjoy + 动名词。

At school, Tim is better at sports, especially basketball.

在学校，蒂姆更擅长运动，尤其是篮球。

词汇be good at 擅长；especially 尤其 语法better 是 well 的比较级。

Tom is much better at maths and often helps others with their homework.

汤姆数学好得多，经常帮别人做作业。

词汇maths 数学；help sb. with sth. 帮某人做某事 语法much 修饰比较级 better；help sb. with sth.。

Although they are different, they are best friends.

尽管他们不同，他们是最好的朋友。

词汇although 尽管；best friends 最好的朋友 语法although 引导让步状语从句，不与 but 连用。

They often share their ideas and learn from each other.

他们经常分享想法、互相学习。

词汇share 分享；learn from each other 互相学习 语法share sth.；learn from each other。

Their parents are happy that they get along so well.

他们的父母很高兴他们相处得这么好。

词汇get along 相处 语法be happy that...；get along well。

Tim helps Tom with sports, and Tom helps Tim with his studies.

蒂姆在运动上帮汤姆，汤姆在学习上帮蒂姆。

词汇help sb. with sth. 帮某人某方面 语法and 连接并列分句。

This is why they are such good friends.

这就是他们成为这么好的朋友的原因。

词汇this is why 这就是……的原因 语法This is why... 引导表语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

短文介绍双胞胎兄弟蒂姆和汤姆。他们看起来一样，性格却大不相同：蒂姆更外向、爱交朋友，汤姆更安静、喜欢在家读书；蒂姆更擅长运动，尤其是篮球，汤姆数学好得多，常帮别人做作业。尽管不同，他们是最好的朋友，经常分享想法、互相学习，互相帮助。父母很高兴他们相处如此融洽，这也正是他们成为好朋友的原因。

English Summary

The passage introduces twin brothers Tim and Tom. They look the same but are very different: Tim is more outgoing and loves making friends, while Tom is quieter and enjoys reading at home. Tim is better at sports, especially basketball, while Tom is much better at maths and often helps others with homework. Although they are different, they are best friends; they share ideas, learn from and help each other, and their parents are happy that they get along so well—which is why they are such good friends.

Section B 3a · My Best Friend Anna

Section B 3a 主题：我的好朋友 · 比较与感悟

I have a good friend. Her name is Anna, and she is quite different from me.

我有一个好朋友。她叫安娜，和我很不一样。

词汇be different from 与……不同；quite 相当 语法be different from sb.。

Anna is taller and more outgoing than me.

安娜比我更高、更外向。

词汇taller 更高的；outgoing 外向的 语法比较级并列；more outgoing 用 more 构成比较级。

She loves talking to people and always makes me laugh.

她喜欢和人聊天，总是逗我笑。

词汇talk to 和……聊天；make sb. laugh 使某人笑 语法love doing sth.；make sb. do sth.。

I am quieter than her, but I am better at drawing.

我比她安静，但我更擅长画画。

词汇quiet 安静的；be good at 擅长；draw 画画 语法比较级 quieter / better。

Although we are different, we have a lot in common.

尽管我们不同，我们有很多共同之处。

词汇although 尽管；have a lot in common 有很多共同之处 语法although 引导让步状语从句；have a lot in common。

We both like music and travel, and we often help each other.

我们俩都喜欢音乐和旅行，而且经常互相帮助。

词汇both 两者都；help each other 互相帮助 语法We both... 作主语；and 连接并列分句。

When I have problems, Anna is always there for me.

我遇到问题时，安娜总在我身边。

词汇be there for sb. 支持某人 语法when 引导时间状语从句；be there for sb.。

She is not only my friend but also my best teacher.

她不仅是我的朋友，也是我最好的老师。

词汇not only...but also... 不仅……而且…… 语法not only...but also... 连接并列成分。

I am lucky to have a friend like her.

我很幸运有她这样的朋友。

词汇be lucky to do 幸运做 语法be lucky to do sth.；like her 作后置定语。

Good friends don't need to be the same—they just need to understand each other.

好朋友不必一样——他们只需要互相理解。

词汇understand 理解；each other 彼此 语法need to do sth.；破折号补充说明。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍作者的好朋友安娜。安娜比作者更高、更外向，喜欢和人聊天，总逗她笑；作者更安静，但更擅长画画。尽管不同，她们有很多共同之处：都喜欢音乐和旅行，经常互相帮助。作者遇到问题时安娜总在身边，她不仅是朋友，也是最好的老师。作者很幸运有这样的朋友，也认为好朋友不必一样，只要互相理解就好。

English Summary

The passage introduces the writer's best friend Anna. Anna is taller and more outgoing, loves talking to people and always makes the writer laugh; the writer is quieter but better at drawing. Although they differ, they have a lot in common: they both like music and travel and often help each other. Anna is always there for the writer when there are problems—she is not only a friend but also the best teacher. The writer feels lucky to have such a friend and believes good friends need not be the same; they just need to understand each other.
