Section A 2a · An Interview with the Twins

Section A 2a 主题：双胞胎的相同与不同 · 比较级

Han Lin: Congratulations on winning the prize at the school music festival!

恭喜你们在学校音乐节上获奖！

词汇congratulation 祝贺；prize 奖；奖品；festival 节日；音乐节 语法Congratulations on doing sth. 祝贺某人做成某事；at the school music festival 表示场合。

The twins: Thank you!

谢谢！

词汇twin 双胞胎之一 语法The twins 作主语，指艾玛和埃拉这对双胞胎；口语中简答致谢。

Han Lin: You attended the festival last year and this year.

你们去年和今年都参加了音乐节。

词汇attend 参加；出席；last year 去年 语法一般过去时；and 连接两个并列的时间状语。

Han Lin: How is this year different?

今年有什么不同？

词汇different 不同的 语法How is ... different? 询问“哪里不同”；be different 是系表结构。

Ella: There are more students and more activities this year.

今年有更多学生和更多活动。

词汇student 学生；activity 活动 语法There be 句型；more + 可数名词复数，表示“更多……”。

Ella: I think it is more colourful than last year.

我觉得它比去年更丰富多彩。

词汇colourful 色彩丰富的；丰富多彩的 语法I think 后接宾语从句；多音节形容词比较级用 more + 形容词 + than。

Han Lin: As twins, you look very similar.

作为双胞胎，你们看起来非常像。

词汇as 作为；similar 相似的 语法as twins 作状语，表示“作为双胞胎”；look 是系动词，后接形容词 similar。

Han Lin: How are you different?

你们有什么不同？

词汇different 不同的 语法How are you different? 询问“你们哪里不同”。

Emma: I'm a little quieter than Ella.

我比埃拉安静一点。

词汇a little 一点；quieter 更安静的 语法a little 修饰比较级，表示“稍微”；quiet → quieter 是单音节形容词比较级。

Emma: And she is funnier than me.

而她比我更滑稽。

词汇funnier 更滑稽的 语法funny 变 y 为 i 再加 -er；than 后接比较对象。

Ella: Emma always gets up earlier than me.

艾玛总是比我起得早。

词汇get up 起床；early 早 语法early → earlier，副词比较级修饰动词 gets up；always 表示“总是”。

Ella: Many people say I'm lazier than Emma, but that's not true!

很多人说我比艾玛懒，但那不是真的！

词汇lazy 懒惰的；true 真的 语法lazy → lazier；but 连接转折分句；that's not true 表示“那不是真的”。

Ella: I work as hard as her!

我和她一样努力！

词汇work hard 努力工作 语法as + 副词原级 + as，表示“和……一样”；hard 在此是副词。

Han Lin: That's really interesting.

那真有意思。

词汇interesting 有趣的 语法That's = That is；really 修饰形容词 interesting。

Han Lin: Besides singing, how do you both spend your spare time?

除了唱歌，你们俩的空闲时间都怎么度过？

词汇besides 除……之外（还）；spare 空闲的 语法besides + 动名词，表示“除……之外（还）”；both 作同位语；how 引导特殊疑问句。

Ella: I love playing sports more than anything.

我最爱做运动。

词汇play sports 做运动；anything 任何事物 语法love doing sth. 喜欢做某事；more than anything 表示“超过一切，最……”。

Emma: I like reading.

我喜欢阅读。

词汇reading 阅读 语法like doing sth. 表示“喜欢做某事”（长期爱好）。

Emma: You see, we're different in many ways, but together, we make a great team!

你看，我们在很多方面不同，但在一起我们就是一个很棒的团队！

词汇in many ways 在许多方面；together 一起 语法be different in ... 表示“在……方面不同”；but 表转折；make a great team 是固定搭配。

Han Lin: Thanks for talking with me.

谢谢和我谈话。

词汇talk with 和……交谈 语法Thanks for doing sth. 感谢做某事；talk with sb. 表示“和某人交谈”。

The twins: Our pleasure!

不客气！

词汇pleasure 荣幸；快乐 语法Our pleasure. 是回应感谢的礼貌用语，意为“不客气”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是校报记者韩林对双胞胎艾玛（Emma）和埃拉（Ella）的采访。双胞胎先感谢韩林祝贺她们在学校音乐节上获奖，并说今年的音乐节学生更多、活动更多，比去年更丰富多彩。韩林问她们，作为双胞胎虽然长得很像，但有哪些不同：艾玛说自己比埃拉安静一点，而埃拉比自己更滑稽；埃拉说艾玛总是起得比自己早，很多人说自己比艾玛懒，但那不是真的，自己和她一样努力。韩林又问她们的空闲时间怎么度过：埃拉最爱做运动，艾玛喜欢阅读。最后她们说，虽然在很多方面不同，但在一起就是一个很棒的团队。

English Summary

This is an interview by Han Lin, a school newspaper reporter, with the twins Emma and Ella. The twins thank Han Lin for congratulating them on winning the prize at the school music festival and say that this year's festival has more students and more activities, so it is more colourful than last year's. Han Lin asks how the twins are different although they look so similar. Emma says she is a little quieter than Ella and that Ella is funnier than her; Ella says Emma always gets up earlier than her, and although many people say she is lazier than Emma, that is not true because she works as hard as her. Han Lin then asks how they both spend their spare time: Ella loves playing sports more than anything and Emma likes reading. Finally they say that although they are different in many ways, together they make a great team.

语法点 Grammar

Congratulations on winning the prize at the school music festival!

Congratulations on doing sth. 祝贺某人做成某事。

You attended the festival last year and this year.

一般过去时；and 连接两个并列时间状语。

How is this year different?

How is ... different? 询问“哪里不同”。

There are more students and more activities this year.

There be 句型；more + 可数名词复数。

I think it is more colourful than last year.

宾语从句；多音节形容词比较级 more + adj. + than。

As twins, you look very similar.

as twins 作状语；look 是系动词，后接形容词。

I'm a little quieter than Ella.

a little 修饰比较级；quiet → quieter。

And she is funnier than me.

funny → funnier（变 y 为 i 加 -er）。

Emma always gets up earlier than me.

early → earlier，副词比较级修饰动词短语。

Many people say I'm lazier than Emma, but that's not true!

宾语从句；lazy → lazier；but 表转折。

I work as hard as her!

as + 副词原级 + as，表示“和……一样”。

Besides singing, how do you both spend your spare time?

besides + 动名词；both 作同位语。

We're different in many ways, but together, we make a great team!

be different in ...；but 表转折；make a great team。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

There are more students and more activities this year. I think it is more colourful than last year.

今年有更多学生和更多活动。我觉得它比去年更丰富多彩。

There be 句型中 more 修饰可数名词复数；colourful 是多音节形容词，比较级用 more colourful，后面用 than 引出比较对象。

Many people say I'm lazier than Emma, but that's not true!

很多人说我比艾玛懒，但那不是真的！

say 后接省略 that 的宾语从句；lazier 是 lazy 的比较级；that's not true 用来反驳别人的说法。

Besides singing, how do you both spend your spare time?

除了唱歌，你们俩的空闲时间都怎么度过？

besides 是介词，后接动名词 singing，表示“除……之外（还）”；both 放在实义动词 spend 之前；spend time 表示“花时间”，常用 spend time (in) doing sth.。

Section B 1b · Forum: Should Friends Be Similar or Different?

Section B 1b 主题：论坛讨论 · 朋友应该相似还是不同

Some people think that friends should be alike, but some other people think that friends should be different.

有些人认为朋友应该相似，而另一些人认为朋友应该不同。

词汇alike 相像的；similar 相似的 语法that 引导宾语从句；some ... some other ... 表示“一些……另一些……”。

What do you think?

你觉得呢？

词汇think 认为 语法What do you think? 询问对方的看法。

Should friends be similar or different?

朋友应该相似还是不同？

词汇should 应该；or 或者 语法should + 动词原形，用于征询意见；or 连接两个选择项。

I think a good friend is like a mirror.

我认为好朋友就像一面镜子。

词汇mirror 镜子；like 像 语法I think 后接宾语从句；like 在此作介词，表示“像”。

You can understand people by knowing their friends.

通过了解一个人交的朋友，你可以了解他。

词汇understand 理解；know 认识 语法by + 动名词，表示方式、手段；people 泛指“人们”。

My friend, Stephen, is a great example.

我的朋友斯蒂芬就是一个很好的例子。

词汇example 例子 语法My friend, Stephen, 是同位语；a great example 作表语。

We often hang out together because we have similar hobbies and interests.

我们经常一起出去玩，因为我们有相似的爱好和兴趣。

词汇hang out 闲逛；一起玩；hobby 爱好；interest 兴趣 语法because 引导原因状语从句；have ... in common 的意思由 similar 体现。

For example, we both love sports.

例如，我们都热爱运动。

词汇for example 例如；both 两者都 语法For example 作插入语；both 作同位语，位于实义动词 love 之前。

He plays badminton better than I do, so he always wins our matches.

他羽毛球打得比我好，所以他总是赢我们的比赛。

词汇badminton 羽毛球；match 比赛 语法better 是 well 的比较级，修饰动词 plays；so 表示结果；do 替代 play badminton。

Because of that, I try harder when I practise, and now I'm getting better at badminton.

正因如此，我练习时更加努力，现在我的羽毛球水平越来越高。

词汇because of 因为；practise 练习；get better at 在……方面变得更好 语法Because of + 名词短语；harder 修饰 try；when 引导时间状语从句；get better at 表示“越来越擅长”。

Stephen and I are similar in other ways.

斯蒂芬和我在其他方面也很相似。

词汇similar 相似的；in other ways 在其他方面 语法be similar in ... 表示“在……方面相似”。

He is as serious as me.

他和我一样严肃认真。

词汇serious 严肃的；认真的 语法as + 形容词原级 + as，表示“和……一样……”。

When we aren't playing sports, we enjoy reading together.

我们不运动的时候，喜欢一起读书。

词汇enjoy 喜欢；read 阅读 语法when 引导时间状语从句；enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事。

I read more history books than he does, and he reads a lot of novels.

我读的历史书比他多，而他读很多小说。

词汇history 历史；novel 小说 语法more + 名词 + than 表示比较；does 替代 reads history books。

We often share interesting books with each other.

我们经常互相分享有趣的书。

词汇share ... with ... 和……分享……；each other 互相 语法share sth. with sb. 固定搭配；each other 作宾语。

I don't agree.

我不同意。

词汇agree 同意 语法don't agree 表示“不同意”；一般现在时的否定式。

For me, friends who are different make me better.

对我来说，不同的朋友让我变得更好。

词汇make 使；变得 语法who are different 是定语从句，修饰 friends；make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补。

I think we should respect each other's differences and learn from each other.

我认为我们应该尊重彼此的差异，互相学习。

词汇respect 尊重；difference 差异；learn from 向……学习 语法we should ... 是宾语从句；and 连接两个并列的谓语动词。

For example, I am kind of shy, but my best friend, Yuan Lei, is outgoing.

例如，我有点害羞，但我最好的朋友袁蕾很外向。

词汇kind of 有点；shy 害羞的；outgoing 外向的 语法for example 举例；but 表转折；Yuan Lei 是同位语。

She has a great sense of humour too.

她也很有幽默感。

词汇sense of humour 幽默感；too 也 语法have a great sense of humour 表示“很有幽默感”；too 用于肯定句句末。

Thanks to her, I am becoming more outgoing.

多亏了她，我变得越来越外向。

词汇thanks to 多亏；becoming 变得 语法Thanks to + 名词/代词；现在进行时表示逐渐变化；more outgoing 是多音节形容词比较级。

Now I think meeting new people is really fun!

现在我觉得认识新朋友真的很有趣！

词汇meet 认识；fun 有趣的 语法meeting new people 是动名词短语作宾语从句的主语；is 用单数。

I have another friend, Zhang Yong.

我还有另一个朋友，张勇。

词汇another 另一个 语法another + 单数名词，表示“另一个”；Zhang Yong 是同位语。

He is not afraid to give his opinion, especially when he thinks I am making a mistake.

他不怕说出自己的看法，尤其是当他觉得我犯错的时候。

词汇be afraid to do sth. 害怕做某事；opinion 看法；especially 尤其；mistake 错误 语法be afraid to do sth.；when 引导时间状语从句；thinks 后接宾语从句。

I am less straightforward than he is, but I am glad to have an honest friend.

我不如他直率，但我很高兴有这样一个诚实的朋友。

词汇less 不如；straightforward 直率的；honest 诚实的；glad 高兴的 语法less + 形容词 + than 表示“不如……”；be glad to do sth. 高兴做某事。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是校园论坛里的一个讨论帖。Tom 发帖提问：有人认为朋友应该相似，有人认为朋友应该不同，你觉得朋友应该相似还是不同？Matt 回复说，好朋友就像一面镜子，通过一个人交的朋友可以了解他；他和朋友斯蒂芬（Stephen）有很多相似的爱好和兴趣，比如都热爱运动。斯蒂芬羽毛球打得比他好，总是赢球，因此 Matt 练习时更加努力，现在球技也越来越好；他们还有别的相似之处：斯蒂芬和他一样严肃认真，不运动时喜欢一起读书，Matt 读的历史书比斯蒂芬多，斯蒂芬读的小说多，两人常互相分享有趣的书。Diana 则不同意：她认为不同的朋友让她变得更好，我们应该尊重彼此的差异、互相学习。她举例说，自己有点害羞，而好朋友袁蕾（Yuan Lei）性格外向、很有幽默感，多亏了她，Diana 变得越来越外向，现在觉得认识新朋友很有趣；她还有另一个朋友张勇（Zhang Yong），他不怕直率地说出自己的看法，尤其在她犯错的时候，Diana 不如他直率，但很高兴有这样一个诚实的朋友。

English Summary

This is a discussion thread on a school forum. Tom asks whether friends should be alike or different. Matt replies that a good friend is like a mirror, because you can understand people by knowing their friends. His friend Stephen is a great example: they have similar hobbies and interests and both love sports. Stephen plays badminton better than Matt and always wins their matches, so Matt tries harder when he practises and is getting better at badminton. They are similar in other ways too: Stephen is as serious as Matt, and they enjoy reading together; Matt reads more history books while Stephen reads a lot of novels, and they often share interesting books with each other. Diana disagrees. For her, friends who are different make her better, and we should respect each other's differences and learn from each other. She is kind of shy while her best friend Yuan Lei is outgoing and has a great sense of humour; thanks to her, Diana is becoming more outgoing and now finds meeting new people really fun. She also has another friend, Zhang Yong, who is not afraid to give his opinion, especially when he thinks she is making a mistake. Diana is less straightforward than he is, but she is glad to have an honest friend.

语法点 Grammar

Some people think that friends should be alike, but some other people think that friends should be different.

that 引导宾语从句；some ... some other ... 表示“一些……另一些……”。

Should friends be similar or different?

should + 动词原形，征询意见；or 连接选择。

I think a good friend is like a mirror.

宾语从句；like 作介词，表示“像”。

You can understand people by knowing their friends.

by + 动名词，表示方式、手段。

We often hang out together because we have similar hobbies and interests.

because 引导原因状语从句。

He plays badminton better than I do, so he always wins our matches.

副词比较级 better；so 表示结果；do 替代前文动词。

Because of that, I try harder when I practise, and now I'm getting better at badminton.

Because of + 名词短语；harder 修饰 try；get better at 表示“越来越擅长”。

He is as serious as me.

as + 形容词原级 + as，表示“和……一样……”。

When we aren't playing sports, we enjoy reading together.

when 引导时间状语从句；enjoy doing sth.。

I read more history books than he does, and he reads a lot of novels.

more + 名词 + than；does 替代 reads history books。

For me, friends who are different make me better.

who 引导定语从句；make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补。

Thanks to her, I am becoming more outgoing.

Thanks to 表示“多亏”；现在进行时表示逐渐变化。

I am less straightforward than he is, but I am glad to have an honest friend.

less + 形容词 + than 表示“不如……”；be glad to do sth.。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

You can understand people by knowing their friends.

通过了解一个人交的朋友，你可以了解他。

by 是介词，后接动名词 knowing 表示方式，意为“通过……”；their friends 指“他们的朋友”，即一个人所交的朋友。

He plays badminton better than I do, so he always wins our matches.

他羽毛球打得比我好，所以他总是赢我们的比赛。

better 是 well 的比较级，作状语修饰 plays；than 后接 I do，do 替代前文的 play badminton；so 表示结果。

I am less straightforward than he is, but I am glad to have an honest friend.

我不如他直率，但我很高兴有这样一个诚实的朋友。

less + 形容词原级 + than 表示“不如……”；he is 后省略 straightforward；be glad to do sth. 表示“高兴做某事”。

Reading Plus · Four Brothers

Reading Plus 主题：童话故事 · 各有所长与团结合作

Once upon a time, there were four brothers.

从前有四兄弟。

词汇once upon a time 从前；brother 兄弟 语法Once upon a time 是童话故事开头的固定表达；there be 句型用一般过去时 there were。

“I can see farther than anyone,” the first brother said.

“我能比任何人看得更远，”大哥说。

词汇farther 更远；anyone 任何人 语法can + 动词原形；farther 是 far 的比较级；than anyone 表示比较范围。

“At the top of that tree, there are five eggs in a nest.”

“在那棵树的树顶，巢里有五个蛋。”

词汇at the top of 在……顶部；nest 鸟巢 语法方位介词短语 at the top of that tree 置于句首；there be 句型表示存在。

“I am faster than all of you—I move as quickly as a cat,” said the second brother.

“我比你们所有人都快——我移动起来像猫一样敏捷，”二哥说。

词汇fast 快的；quickly 快速地 语法faster 是比较级；as + 副词原级 + as 表示“和……一样快”。

He climbed up, took the eggs quietly, and then put them back.

他爬上去，悄悄地把蛋拿下来，然后又放了回去。

词汇climb up 爬上；quietly 安静地；put back 放回 语法三个并列谓语 climbed / took / put 用逗号和 and 连接；quietly 修饰动词。

The bird never noticed it.

那只鸟从来没有察觉到。

词汇notice 注意到 语法never 表示“从不”，位于实义动词前；it 指二哥取蛋又放回这件事。

“I can shoot better than all of you,” said the third.

“我射得比你们所有人都准，”老三说。

词汇shoot 射击；better 更好地 语法better 是 well 的比较级，修饰动词 shoot；the third 后省略 brother。

He then shot a leaf above the nest.

然后他射中了鸟巢上方的一片叶子。

词汇leaf 叶子；above 在……上方 语法一般过去时；above the nest 是方位介词短语。

The fourth brother said, “I can sew anything together, even something as soft as that leaf.”

老四说：“我能把任何东西缝在一起，甚至是像那片叶子一样柔软的东西。”

词汇sew 缝；together 在一起；soft 柔软的 语法sew ... together 表示“把……缝在一起”；as + 形容词原级 + as 表示“和……一样”。

In no time, the leaf was as good as new.

转眼间，那片叶子就完好如新了。

词汇in no time 立刻；很快；as good as new 完好如新 语法in no time 作时间状语；as good as new 是固定表达。

Not long after, the king's daughter was taken away by a dragon.

不久之后，国王的女儿被一条龙抓走了。

词汇not long after 不久之后；take away 带走；dragon 龙 语法一般过去时的被动语态 was taken away；by a dragon 表示动作执行者。

The king said whoever brought the princess back could marry her.

国王说，谁能把公主带回来，谁就能娶她。

词汇whoever 无论谁；princess 公主；marry 嫁；娶 语法whoever 引导宾语从句并在从句中作主语；主句 said 后从句时态用 could。

The first brother said, “I see her! She is far away, on a rock at sea, with the dragon.”

大哥说：“我看见她了！她很远，在海上的一块岩石上，和那条龙在一起。”

词汇far away 遥远；rock 岩石；at sea 在海上 语法see 表示“看见”的结果；on a rock at sea 是地点状语。

So the brothers sailed to the rock.

于是兄弟们航行到那块岩石那里。

词汇sail 航行 语法so 表示结果；sail to + 地点，表示“航行到……”。

The third brother said, “I may hurt the princess if I shoot the dragon.”

老三说：“如果我射那条龙，可能会伤到公主。”

词汇may 可能；hurt 伤害 语法if 引导条件状语从句，主句用 may + 动词原形。

“Leave it to me,” said the second brother.

“交给我吧，”二哥说。

词汇leave ... to ... 把……交给…… 语法祈使句 Leave it to me；said the second brother 是倒装句式。

He moved as quietly as a mouse and quickly freed the princess.

他像老鼠一样悄无声息地移动，迅速解救了公主。

词汇quietly 安静地；mouse 老鼠；free 释放；解救 语法as + 副词原级 + as 表示“和……一样”；and 连接并列谓语 moved / freed。

Suddenly, the dragon began to chase them.

突然，那条龙开始追赶他们。

词汇suddenly 突然；chase 追赶 语法begin to do sth. 开始做某事；them 指四兄弟。

The third brother shot it, but its fall broke their ship.

老三射中了它，但它坠落时砸坏了他们的船。

词汇shot 射击（shoot 的过去式）；fall 坠落；break 弄坏 语法but 表转折；its 是 it 的形容词性物主代词，指龙。

Luckily, the fourth brother took his needle and sewed the ship together.

幸运的是，老四拿出针，把船缝补好了。

词汇luckily 幸运地；needle 针；sew 缝 语法Luckily 作评注性状语；and 连接两个并列谓语 took / sewed。

Then they sailed home safely.

然后他们平安地航行回家。

词汇sail 航行；safely 安全地 语法sail home 中 home 是副词，前面不加 to；safely 修饰动词 sailed。

The happy king asked, “Which of you will marry my daughter?”

高兴的国王问：“你们中谁愿意娶我的女儿？”

词汇which of 哪一个；daughter 女儿 语法Which of you 作主语，谓语用 will marry；引号内是直接引语。

The brothers began to argue.

兄弟们开始争论起来。

词汇argue 争论 语法begin to do sth. 开始做某事。

Each brother believed that he did more than the others.

每个兄弟都认为自己比别人做得更多。

词汇believe 认为；each 每个 语法that 引导宾语从句；more than 表示比较。

Finally, the king said, “Each of you is as good as the others, but you are much stronger together. Instead, I give half of my kingdom to you as a reward.”

最后，国王说：“你们每个人都和其他人一样出色，但你们团结起来会强大得多。作为奖赏，我把半个王国给你们。”

词汇finally 最后；each of 每个；kingdom 王国；reward 奖赏 语法each of + 复数代词作主语时谓语用单数 is；as good as 同级比较；much 修饰比较级 stronger。

The brothers were pleased.

兄弟们很高兴。

词汇pleased 高兴的；满意的 语法be pleased 表示“高兴”；主语 the brothers 是复数，be 动词用 were。

It was much better to share than to fight.

分享比争斗好得多。

词汇share 分享；fight 争斗 语法It 作形式主语，真正的主语是 to share；much 修饰比较级 better；than to fight 作比较。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇改编自格林童话的故事。从前有四兄弟：老大看得比谁都远，看见树顶鸟巢里有五个蛋；老二跑得比谁都快，像猫一样敏捷，悄悄爬上去把蛋取下来又放回去；老三射得比谁都准，射中了鸟巢上方的一片叶子；老四能把任何东西缝在一起，连像那片叶子一样柔软的东西也能缝好，转眼叶子就完好如新。不久，国王的女儿被一条龙抓走了，国王说谁能把公主带回来，谁就能娶她。老大看见公主远在海上的岩石上，四兄弟便一起出海。老三担心射龙会伤到公主，老二便像老鼠一样悄无声息地行动，迅速救出公主。龙追了上来，老三射中了龙，但龙坠落时砸坏了他们的船，老四拿出针把船缝补好，大家平安回家。国王问谁愿意娶公主，兄弟们争论起来，人人都觉得自己做得最多。最后国王说，你们每个人都和其他人一样出色，但团结起来会强大得多，于是把半个王国赏给他们。兄弟们很高兴，觉得分享远比争斗好。

English Summary

This story is adapted from a Grimm's fairy tale. Once upon a time there were four brothers. The first could see farther than anyone and saw five eggs in a nest at the top of a tree; the second was faster than all of them and, as quick as a cat, quietly climbed up, took the eggs and put them back without the bird noticing; the third could shoot better than all of them and shot a leaf above the nest; the fourth could sew anything together, even something as soft as that leaf, and in no time the leaf was as good as new. Not long after, the king's daughter was taken away by a dragon, and the king said whoever brought the princess back could marry her. The first brother saw her far away on a rock at sea, so the brothers sailed there. Because the third brother was afraid of hurting the princess, the second brother moved as quietly as a mouse and quickly freed her. When the dragon chased them, the third brother shot it, but its fall broke their ship; luckily the fourth brother sewed the ship together with his needle, and they sailed home safely. The king then asked which of them would marry his daughter, and the brothers began to argue, each believing he had done more than the others. Finally the king said that each of them was as good as the others but that they were much stronger together, and gave them half of his kingdom as a reward. The brothers were pleased, for it was much better to share than to fight.

there be 句型的一般过去时；童话故事的典型开头。

At the top of that tree, there are five eggs in a nest.

方位介词短语置于句首；there be 表示存在。

形容词比较级 faster + than。

as + 副词原级 + as，表示“和……一样……”。

三个并列谓语用逗号和 and 连接。

as + 形容词原级 + as；even 加强语气。

in no time 作时间状语；as good as new 固定表达。

The king's daughter was taken away by a dragon.

一般过去时的被动语态 was taken away。

whoever 引导宾语从句并作从句主语；间接引语时态后移。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I can sew anything together, even something as soft as that leaf.

我能把任何东西缝在一起，甚至是像那片叶子一样柔软的东西。

as soft as that leaf 是同级比较，as ... as 中间用形容词原级；even 用来加强语气，表示“甚至”。

The king said whoever brought the princess back could marry her.

国王说，谁能把公主带回来，谁就能娶她。

whoever 引导宾语从句，并在从句中作主语，相当于 anyone who；主句是一般过去时，从句中用 could 表示过去将来。

Each of you is as good as the others, but you are much stronger together.

你们每个人都和其他人一样出色，但你们团结起来会强大得多。

each of + 复数代词作主语时，谓语动词用单数 is；as good as 是同级比较；much 修饰比较级 stronger，表示程度。

1. What did the twins win at the school music festival?

  1. The prize at the school music festival.
  2. The prize for the school newspaper.

2. According to Ella, how is this year's music festival different?

  1. There are more teachers.
  2. There are more students and more activities.
  3. There are more music clubs.
  4. There are more prizes.
答案与解析

B 原文：Ella: There are more students and more activities this year. 可知今年学生更多、活动更多。

3. How is Emma different from Ella?

  1. She is taller than Ella.
  2. She is funnier than Ella.
  3. She is a little quieter than Ella.
  4. She works harder than Ella.
答案与解析

C 原文：Emma: I'm a little quieter than Ella. And she is funnier than me. 可知艾玛比埃拉安静一点，更滑稽的是埃拉；埃拉还说 I work as hard as her，说明两人一样努力，所以 D 不对。

4. Why does Matt think that a good friend is like a mirror?

  1. Because friends always have the same hobbies.
  2. Because a mirror can make people look better.
  3. Because friends should be different from each other.
  4. Because you can understand people by knowing their friends.
答案与解析

D 原文：I think a good friend is like a mirror. You can understand people by knowing their friends. 可知 Matt 认为通过一个人交的朋友可以了解他，所以好朋友像镜子。

5. Why is Matt getting better at badminton?

  1. Because he tries harder when he practises.
  2. Because Stephen teaches him every day.
  3. Because he is taller than Stephen.
  4. Because he watches matches on TV.
答案与解析

A 原文：He plays badminton better than I do, so he always wins our matches. Because of that, I try harder when I practise, and now I'm getting better at badminton. 可知 Matt 球技提高是因为练习时更加努力。

6. Why is Diana glad to have Zhang Yong as a friend?

  1. Because he is very outgoing.
  2. Because he is an honest friend who gives his opinion directly.
  3. Because he has a great sense of humour.
  4. Because he is kind of shy.
答案与解析

B 原文：He is not afraid to give his opinion, especially when he thinks I am making a mistake. I am less straightforward than he is, but I am glad to have an honest friend. 可知 Diana 高兴是因为张勇诚实、敢于直率地给出看法；外向、有幽默感的是袁蕾。

7. Which brother saw the princess far away on a rock at sea?

  1. The second brother.
  2. The third brother.
  3. The first brother.
  4. The fourth brother.
答案与解析

C 原文：The first brother said, "I see her! She is far away, on a rock at sea, with the dragon." 可知是老大看见了远在海中岩石上的公主。

8. How did the king settle the brothers' argument?

  1. He asked them to fight for the princess.
  2. He chose the eldest brother to marry her.
  3. He told them to go back home.
  4. He gave them half of his kingdom as a reward.
答案与解析

D 原文：Finally, the king said, "Each of you is as good as the others, but you are much stronger together. Instead, I give half of my kingdom to you as a reward." 可知国王把半个王国赏给四兄弟，解决了争论。
