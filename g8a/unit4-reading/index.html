Section A 3a · Protecting the Pandas

Section A 3a 主题：濒危动物 · 保护建议

A: What's your favourite animal?

你最喜欢的动物是什么？

词汇favourite 最喜欢的；animal 动物 语法What's your favourite...? 询问喜好。

B: I like pandas best. They are cute and smart.

我最喜欢熊猫。它们可爱又聪明。

词汇like...best 最喜欢；cute 可爱的；smart 聪明的 语法like...best；形容词并列作表语。

A: Where do pandas live?

熊猫生活在哪里？

词汇live 生活；where 哪里 语法Where do...live? 特殊疑问句。

B: They live in the mountains of Sichuan. They mainly eat bamboo.

它们生活在四川的山区，主要吃竹子。

词汇mountain 山；mainly 主要地；bamboo 竹子 语法live in；mainly 修饰动词 eat。

A: Why are they in danger?

它们为什么处于危险中？

词汇in danger 处于危险中 语法why 引导特殊疑问句；be in danger。

B: Because people cut down many trees, so they are losing their homes.

因为人们砍了很多树，所以它们正在失去家园。

词汇cut down 砍倒；lose 失去；home 家园 语法because 引导原因状语从句；现在进行时 are losing。

A: What can we do to protect them?

我们能做些什么来保护它们？

词汇protect 保护 语法to protect 作目的状语。

B: We should stop buying things made of ivory.

我们应该停止购买象牙制品。

词汇stop doing 停止做；be made of 由……制成；ivory 象牙 语法stop doing sth.；made of 作后置定语。

B: We can also protect their homes.

我们也可以保护它们的家园。

词汇protect 保护 语法can + 动词原形。

A: I think animals are our friends, and we should take good care of them.

我认为动物是我们的朋友，我们应该好好照顾它们。

词汇take care of 照顾 语法I think 后接宾语从句；take good care of。

B: I agree. Every animal is important in nature.

我同意。每种动物在自然界中都很重要。

词汇nature 自然界；important 重要的 语法主系表结构。

A: Let's do something to help them.

我们做点事情来帮助它们吧。

词汇help 帮助 语法Let's do sth.；to help 作目的状语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕动物保护展开。一个人说最喜欢熊猫，觉得它们可爱聪明；熊猫生活在四川山区，主要吃竹子。被问到为什么熊猫处于危险中时，回答说因为人们砍了很多树，熊猫正在失去家园。两人讨论可以做些什么来保护它们：不要购买象牙制品，保护它们的家园。最后他们一致认为动物是人类的朋友，每种动物在自然界中都很重要，应该做些事情来帮助它们。

English Summary

The conversation is about animal protection. One speaker likes pandas best because they are cute and smart; pandas live in the mountains of Sichuan and mainly eat bamboo. Asked why they are in danger, the answer is that people cut down many trees, so pandas are losing their homes. They discuss what to do: stop buying things made of ivory, and protect their homes. Finally they agree that animals are our friends, every animal is important in nature, and we should do something to help them.

Section B 1b · The Strange Platypus

Section B 1b 主题：奇特动物鸭嘴兽 · 说明文

Some animals are very strange, and they surprise us in many ways.

有些动物非常奇特，在很多方面让我们惊奇。

词汇strange 奇特的；surprise 使惊奇；in many ways 在很多方面 语法主系表结构；surprise sb.。

The platypus is one of them.

鸭嘴兽就是其中之一。

词汇the platypus 鸭嘴兽 语法one of + 代词/复数名词。

It looks like a mix of a duck, a beaver, and an otter.

它看起来像鸭子、河狸和水獭的结合体。

词汇mix 混合；duck 鸭子；beaver 河狸；otter 水獭 语法look like；a mix of...。

It has a beak like a duck and a tail like a beaver.

它有着鸭子一样的嘴和河狸一样的尾巴。

词汇beak 嘴；tail 尾巴 语法have + 名词 + like...。

But it is a mammal.

但它是哺乳动物。

词汇mammal 哺乳动物 语法but 转折。

Most mammals give birth to their babies, but the platypus lays eggs.

大多数哺乳动物会直接生下宝宝，但鸭嘴兽却下蛋。

词汇give birth to 生下；lay eggs 下蛋 语法but 表示对比。

The platypus lives in Australia, and it is very good at swimming.

鸭嘴兽生活在澳大利亚，非常擅长游泳。

词汇be good at 擅长 语法and 连接并列分句；be good at + 动名词。

It uses its beak to find food in the water.

它用嘴在水里找食物。

词汇use...to do 用……做 语法use sth. to do sth.。

Its skin can feel small changes in the water.

它的皮肤能感知水中的细微变化。

词汇skin 皮肤；feel 感知；change 变化 语法情态动词 can + 动词原形。

This helps it find worms and insects even in the dark.

这让它即使在黑暗中也能找到虫子。

词汇help sb. do 帮某人做；worm 虫子；insect 昆虫 语法help sb. do sth.；even in the dark。

In fact, the platypus is also very difficult to see in nature.

事实上，鸭嘴兽在野外也很难见到。

词汇in fact 事实上；in nature 在自然界 语法in fact 作插入语。

It is a shy animal that sleeps during the day and looks for food at night.

它是一种害羞的动物，白天睡觉，晚上觅食。

词汇shy 害羞的；during the day 白天；look for 寻找 语法that 引导定语从句；and 连接并列谓语。

That's why the platypus is so special.

这就是鸭嘴兽如此特别的原因。

词汇special 特别的 语法That's why... 引导表语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

短文介绍奇特动物鸭嘴兽。它长得像鸭子、河狸和水獭的结合体：有鸭嘴、河狸尾巴，却是哺乳动物。大多数哺乳动物直接生宝宝，鸭嘴兽却下蛋。它生活在澳大利亚，很会游泳，用嘴在水里找食物，皮肤能感知水中的细微变化，帮它在黑暗中找到虫子和昆虫。鸭嘴兽在野外很难见到，是白天睡觉、夜里觅食的害羞动物，这就是它如此特别的原因。

English Summary

The passage introduces the platypus, a strange animal. It looks like a mix of a duck, a beaver and an otter—it has a duck's beak and a beaver's tail, but it is a mammal. Most mammals give birth to their babies, but the platypus lays eggs. It lives in Australia and is very good at swimming; it uses its beak to find food in the water, and its skin can feel small changes in the water, which helps it find worms and insects even in the dark. The platypus is hard to see in nature and is a shy animal that sleeps by day and looks for food at night—that is why it is so special.

Section B 3a · My Favourite Plant

Section B 3a 主题：荷花 · 植物与文化

My favourite plant is the lotus.

我最喜欢的植物是荷花。

词汇favourite 最喜欢的；plant 植物；lotus 荷花 语法名词性物主代词 My favourite 表示“我最喜欢的（植物）”。

I first saw it in a park near my home.

我第一次是在家附近的公园看到它的。

词汇first 第一次；near my home 我家附近 语法一般过去时；near my home 作后置定语。

In summer, the lotus flowers are pink or white, and they look very beautiful.

夏天，荷花是粉色或白色的，看起来非常美。

词汇pink 粉色的；white 白色的 语法and 连接并列分句；look + adj.。

They grow in water, and their leaves are big and green.

它们生长在水中，叶子又大又绿。

词汇grow 生长；leaf 叶子 语法in water 表示地点；and 连接并列分句。

The lotus stands for purity in Chinese culture.

在中国文化中，荷花代表纯洁。

词汇stand for 代表；purity 纯洁；culture 文化 语法stand for 是固定短语。

People often use it in Chinese paintings and poems.

人们常在国画和诗歌中使用它。

词汇painting 绘画；poem 诗 语法use sth. in...。

Although it grows in mud, it becomes a beautiful flower.

它虽然生长在淤泥中，却能开出美丽的花朵。

词汇mud 淤泥；although 尽管 语法although 引导让步状语从句。

This teaches us to keep clean and honest no matter where we are.

这教我们无论身处何处都要保持清白诚实。

词汇clean 清洁的；honest 诚实的；no matter where 无论哪里 语法no matter where 引导让步状语从句。

I like the lotus because it is both beautiful and meaningful.

我喜欢荷花，因为它既美丽又有意义。

词汇meanful 有意义的（meaningful） 语法because 引导原因状语从句；both...and...。

I hope more people will protect the lakes and rivers where the lotus grows.

我希望更多人能保护荷花所生长的湖泊和河流。

词汇protect 保护；lake 湖；river 河流 语法hope 后接宾语从句；where 引导定语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍作者最喜欢的植物荷花。她第一次是在家附近的公园看到莲花的；夏天荷花粉色或白色，非常美，生长在水中，叶子又大又绿。荷花在中国文化中代表纯洁，常出现在国画和诗歌里。它虽然长在淤泥中却能开出美丽的花，这教人无论身处何处都要保持清白诚实。作者喜欢荷花，因为它既美又有意义，并希望更多人保护荷花生长的湖泊和河流。

English Summary

The passage introduces the lotus, the writer's favourite plant. She first saw it in a park near her home. In summer the lotus flowers are pink or white and very beautiful; they grow in water and their leaves are big and green. In Chinese culture the lotus stands for purity and often appears in Chinese paintings and poems. Although it grows in mud, it becomes a beautiful flower, teaching us to keep clean and honest wherever we are. The writer likes it because it is both beautiful and meaningful, and hopes more people will protect the lakes and rivers where it grows.
