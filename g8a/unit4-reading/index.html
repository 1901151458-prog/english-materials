Section A 2a · A Folding Fan and Bamboo (Ella & Chen Jie)

Section A 2a 主题：竹文化 · 对话与最高级表达

Ella: Oh, what's that, Chen Jie?

哦，陈洁，那是什么？

词汇Oh 哦（表示惊讶）；what 什么；that 那个 语法What's = what is；特殊疑问句 What's that? 用来询问“那是什么”。

Ella: It's so beautiful!

它真漂亮！

词汇beautiful 美丽的；so 如此 语法It's = it is；so + 形容词表示“如此……”。

Chen Jie: Thanks. It's a folding fan.

谢谢。这是一把折扇。

词汇thanks 谢谢；folding fan 折扇 语法It's = it is；folding 是现在分词作定语，修饰 fan，表示“可折叠的”。

Chen Jie: I bought it last year.

这是我去年买的。

词汇bought 买（buy 的过去式）；last year 去年 语法一般过去时表示过去发生的动作；bought 是 buy 的过去式。

Ella: Oh! It has a painting of bamboo on it.

哦！上面有一幅竹子的画。

词汇painting 绘画；画作；bamboo 竹子 语法have ... on it 表示“上面有……”；a painting of bamboo 意为“一幅竹画”。

Chen Jie: Yeah. Bamboo is one of the most popular subjects in Chinese paintings.

是啊。竹子是中国画中最受欢迎的主题之一。

词汇popular 受欢迎的；subject 主题；题材 语法one of the most + 形容词最高级 + 可数名词复数，表示“最……的……之一”；最高级用于三者或三者以上的比较。

Chen Jie: It's a symbol of goodness in Chinese culture.

它是中国文化中善良的象征。

词汇symbol 象征；goodness 善良；美德；culture 文化 语法a symbol of ... 表示“……的象征”；in Chinese culture 作状语。

Ella: I didn't know that before.

我以前不知道这一点。

词汇before 以前；know 知道 语法didn't + 动词原形构成一般过去时的否定句；that 指代上一句的内容。

Ella: So, where did you get that fan?

那么，你是在哪儿买到那把扇子的？

词汇so 那么；get 得到；买到；fan 扇子 语法特殊疑问句：疑问词 + did + 主语 + 动词原形；get 在这里表示“买到”。

Chen Jie: At the Bamboo Sea in Sichuan, the largest and oldest bamboo forest in China.

在四川的竹海——中国最大、最古老的竹林。

词汇the Bamboo Sea 竹海（四川景区）；Sichuan 四川；largest 最大的；oldest 最古老的；forest 森林 语法the largest and oldest bamboo forest in China 作 the Bamboo Sea 的同位语；两个最高级用 and 连接。

Chen Jie: I learnt lots of interesting things when I was there.

我在那里学到了许多有趣的东西。

词汇learnt 学到（learn 的过去式）；lots of 许多 语法when 引导时间状语从句；learnt 是 learn 的过去式。

Ella: Really? Like what?

真的吗？比如什么呢？

词汇really 真的；like 像；例如 语法Really? 用升调表示疑问；Like what? 是省略句，用来请对方举例。

Chen Jie: Well, bamboo is one of the fastest-growing plants.

嗯，竹子是生长最快的植物之一。

词汇well 嗯（表示停顿）；fastest-growing 生长最快的；plant 植物 语法one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词；fastest-growing 是复合形容词的最高级形式。

Chen Jie: Some bamboo can grow almost one metre in one day.

有些竹子一天能长将近一米。

词汇some 一些；almost 几乎；metre 米 语法in one day 表示“在一天之内”；almost 修饰数量，表示“将近”。

Chen Jie: Also, it's one of the most useful plants in the world.

而且，它是世界上最有用的植物之一。

词汇also 而且；useful 有用的；in the world 在世界上 语法the most useful 是 useful 的最高级；最高级前要加定冠词 the。

Chen Jie: People can use it to build houses and make tools and instruments.

人们可以用它建造房屋、制作工具和乐器。

词汇use 使用；build 建造；tool 工具；instrument 乐器；仪器 语法use sth. to do sth. 表示“用某物做某事”；and 连接 build 和 make 两个并列的动词原形。

Ella: Oh, that's interesting.

哦，真有意思。

词汇that's = that is；interesting 有趣的 语法that 指代陈洁刚说的内容；that's = that is。

Ella: I remember pandas love to eat bamboo too, right?

我记得熊猫也爱吃竹子，对吧？

词汇remember 记得；panda 熊猫 语法remember 后接宾语从句；love to do sth. 表示“喜爱做某事”；right? 用于寻求对方的认同。

Chen Jie: Exactly! Actually we eat bamboo shoots too.

没错！其实我们也吃竹笋。

词汇exactly 正是；完全同意；actually 实际上；bamboo shoot 竹笋 语法Exactly! 表示完全同意对方的说法；actually 用于补充说明。

Chen Jie: They're really delicious.

它们真的很好吃。

词汇delicious 美味的；really 真的 语法They're = they are；really 修饰形容词 delicious，加强语气。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在埃拉和陈洁之间。埃拉看到陈洁手里有一把漂亮的折扇，问那是什么；陈洁说是一把折扇，是她去年买的，扇面上有一幅竹子的画。陈洁告诉埃拉，竹子是中国画中最受欢迎的主题之一，也是中国文化中善良的象征。埃拉以前不知道这一点，又问陈洁是在哪里买到这把扇子的；陈洁说是在四川的竹海——中国最大、最古老的竹林，她在那里学到了许多有趣的东西。埃拉请她举例，陈洁说竹子是生长最快的植物之一，有些竹子一天能长将近一米；竹子还是世界上最有用的植物之一，人们可以用它建房子、制作工具和乐器。埃拉想起熊猫也爱吃竹子，陈洁说没错，其实人们也吃竹笋，而且竹笋非常美味。

English Summary

The conversation takes place between Ella and Chen Jie. Ella sees a beautiful folding fan in Chen Jie's hand and asks what it is. Chen Jie says it is a folding fan she bought last year, and that there is a painting of bamboo on it. She tells Ella that bamboo is one of the most popular subjects in Chinese paintings and a symbol of goodness in Chinese culture. Ella did not know that before, and asks where Chen Jie got the fan. Chen Jie says she got it at the Bamboo Sea in Sichuan, the largest and oldest bamboo forest in China, where she learnt lots of interesting things. Ella asks her to give an example, and Chen Jie says bamboo is one of the fastest-growing plants and that some bamboo can grow almost one metre in one day. She adds that bamboo is also one of the most useful plants in the world: people can use it to build houses and make tools and instruments. Ella remembers that pandas love to eat bamboo too, and Chen Jie agrees, adding that people eat bamboo shoots as well and that they are really delicious.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Bamboo is one of the most popular subjects in Chinese paintings.

竹子是中国画中最受欢迎的主题之一。

one of the most popular subjects 是“最受欢迎的主题之一”；注意 one of 后接可数名词复数，本句的主语 Bamboo 是单数，所以谓语用 is。

At the Bamboo Sea in Sichuan, the largest and oldest bamboo forest in China.

在四川的竹海——中国最大、最古老的竹林。

这是一个省略句，完整意思是 It is at the Bamboo Sea in Sichuan, the largest and oldest bamboo forest in China；the largest and oldest bamboo forest 作同位语，补充说明竹海。

I remember pandas love to eat bamboo too, right?

我记得熊猫也爱吃竹子，对吧？

remember 后接宾语从句 pandas love to eat bamboo too，从句用陈述语序；right? 用升调，用来寻求对方的认同。

Section B 1b · We Can't Live Without Bees

Section B 1b 主题：蜜蜂与生态 · 说明文与最高级

Imagine you go to the supermarket in your area, but there are almost no fruits, vegetables, honey, milk, eggs, or chocolate.

想象一下，你走进当地的超市，但那里几乎没有水果、蔬菜、蜂蜜、牛奶、鸡蛋和巧克力。

词汇imagine 想象；area 地区；almost 几乎；honey 蜂蜜；chocolate 巧克力 语法imagine 后接宾语从句，从句用陈述语序；there be 句型的否定形式表示“几乎没有”；并列名词用逗号和 or 连接。

How would you feel?

你会有什么感觉？

词汇would 将会；feel 感觉 语法would 表示假设情况下的结果；How would you feel? 用来引导读者设身处地地思考。

Disappointed? Angry? Sad?

失望？生气？难过？

词汇disappointed 失望的；angry 生气的；sad 难过的 语法三个形容词单独成句，构成省略问句，表示连续追问，语气强烈。

At this moment you realize people, plants, and animals are all connected.

这时你会意识到，人、植物和动物都是相互联系的。

词汇moment 时刻；realize 意识到；connected 有联系的 语法realize 后接宾语从句；are connected 表示“相互联系”，all 加强语气。

We need each other to live, and bees are one of the best examples of this connection.

我们彼此依赖才能生存，而蜜蜂就是这种联系最好的例子之一。

词汇each other 彼此；example 例子；connection 联系；关联 语法need sb./sth. to do sth.；one of the best examples of ... 表示“最好的例子之一”；and 连接两个并列分句。

Bees are very hard-working animals, and their greatest work is pollination.

蜜蜂是非常勤劳的动物，它们最重要的工作是授粉。

词汇hard-working 勤劳的；greatest 最伟大的；work 工作；pollination 授粉 语法greatest 是 great 的最高级；and 连接两个并列分句。

On one trip, they often visit 50 to 100 flowers.

一次外出，它们常常要拜访 50 到 100 朵花。

词汇trip 出行；visit 拜访；flower 花 语法on one trip 作时间状语；50 to 100 表示数量范围。

As they fly from one flower to another, they take pollen with them.

当它们从一朵花飞到另一朵花时，身上就带上了花粉。

词汇as 当……时；fly 飞；pollen 花粉 语法as 引导时间状语从句，表示“当……时”；from one ... to another 表示“从一个……到另一个……”。

This action helps plants grow.

这一行为帮助植物生长。

词汇action 行为；help 帮助；grow 生长 语法help sb./sth. (to) do sth. 结构，grow 前省略了 to。

In fact, without their work, about 80 per cent of the plants in the world cannot grow.

事实上，没有它们的工作，世界上大约 80% 的植物都无法生长。

词汇in fact 事实上；without 没有；per cent 百分之…… 语法without their work 表示假设条件；80 per cent of + 名词作主语时，谓语与 of 后的名词保持一致。

For this reason, many scientists believe that bees are the most important animals on this planet.

因此，许多科学家认为蜜蜂是地球上最重要的动物。

词汇reason 原因；scientist 科学家；believe 认为；planet 行星；地球 语法for this reason 表示原因；believe 后接宾语从句，从句用陈述语序；the most important 是最高级。

Bees are also one of the most interesting animals in the world.

蜜蜂还是世界上最有意思的动物之一。

词汇also 也；interesting 有趣的 语法one of the most + 形容词最高级 + 复数名词结构；in the world 表示范围。

For example, they work hard together to make their homes.

例如，它们一起努力建造自己的家。

词汇for example 例如；together 一起；home 家 语法to make their homes 作目的状语；for example 用于举例。

In order to store honey, they create honeycombs.

为了储存蜂蜜，它们建造蜂巢。

词汇in order to 为了；store 储存；create 创建；honeycomb 蜂巢 语法in order to do sth. 引导目的状语，可以放在句首。

Another interesting thing about bees is how they communicate.

关于蜜蜂的另一件有趣的事是它们如何交流。

词汇another 另一个；communicate 交流 语法how they communicate 是表语从句，用陈述语序。

When a bee finds new food, it uses a dance to tell the others where and how far away the food is.

当一只蜜蜂发现新食物时，它会用舞蹈告诉其他蜜蜂食物在哪里、有多远。

词汇find 发现；dance 舞蹈；tell 告诉；far away 遥远的 语法when 引导时间状语从句；tell sb. sth.；where and how far away ... 是宾语从句，用陈述语序。

Bees play an important part in the ecosystem of our planet.

蜜蜂在我们星球的生态系统中起着重要作用。

词汇play a part 起作用；ecosystem 生态系统；planet 行星；地球 语法play an important part in ... 表示“在……中起重要作用”。

However, the number of bees is dropping, and scientists are worried.

然而，蜜蜂的数量正在下降，科学家们很担心。

词汇however 然而；number 数量；drop 下降；worried 担心的 语法the number of + 复数名词作主语，谓语用单数 is；and 连接两个并列分句。

They need our help, and there is a lot we can do.

它们需要我们的帮助，而我们可以做的还有很多。

词汇need 需要；a lot 很多 语法there is a lot we can do 中 we can do 是定语从句，修饰 a lot。

Think about what you can do to protect one of the most amazing animals on earth.

想一想你能做些什么来保护地球上最神奇的动物之一。

词汇think about 思考；protect 保护；amazing 令人惊奇的；on earth 在地球上 语法what you can do 是宾语从句；to protect ... 作目的状语；one of the most amazing animals。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇说明文介绍为什么人类离不开蜜蜂。文章先让读者想象：如果超市里几乎没有水果、蔬菜、蜂蜜、牛奶、鸡蛋和巧克力，你会有什么感觉？这时你会意识到人、植物和动物都是相互联系的，而蜜蜂就是这种联系最好的例子之一。蜜蜂非常勤劳，它们最重要的工作是授粉：一次外出常常要拜访 50 到 100 朵花，从一朵花飞到另一朵花时把花粉带在身上，帮助植物生长；没有它们的工作，世界上大约 80% 的植物都无法生长，所以许多科学家认为蜜蜂是地球上最重要的动物。蜜蜂也是世界上最有意思的动物之一：它们一起努力建造家园，为了储存蜂蜜而建造蜂巢，还会用舞蹈告诉同伴食物在哪里、有多远。蜜蜂在生态系统中起着重要作用，然而它们的数量正在下降，科学家很担心。文章最后呼吁大家想一想：我们能做些什么来保护这种最神奇的动物。

English Summary

This expository text explains why we cannot live without bees. It first asks readers to imagine a supermarket with almost no fruits, vegetables, honey, milk, eggs, or chocolate, and to think about how they would feel. At that moment we realize that people, plants, and animals are all connected, and bees are one of the best examples of this connection. Bees are very hard-working animals, and their greatest work is pollination: on one trip they often visit 50 to 100 flowers and carry pollen with them as they fly from one flower to another, which helps plants grow. Without their work, about 80 per cent of the plants in the world cannot grow, so many scientists believe that bees are the most important animals on the planet. Bees are also among the most interesting animals in the world: they work hard together to make their homes, create honeycombs to store honey, and use a dance to tell the others where and how far away the food is. Bees play an important part in the ecosystem, but their number is dropping and scientists are worried. The text ends by asking us to think about what we can do to protect one of the most amazing animals on earth.

Reading Plus 主题：信鸽送信 · 过去时叙事与最高级

Somewhere in the Atlantic Ocean, 1862.

1862 年，大西洋某处。

词汇somewhere 某处；the Atlantic Ocean 大西洋 语法故事开头的场景说明，用“地点 + 时间”交代背景；1862 读作 eighteen sixty-two。

The captain sighed.

船长叹了口气。

词汇captain 船长；sigh 叹气 语法一般过去时叙事；sighed 是 sigh 的过去式。

This was the thickest fog he had ever seen.

这是他见过的最浓的雾。

词汇thick 浓的；fog 雾；ever 曾经 语法the thickest 是最高级；he had ever seen 是定语从句，用过去完成时表示“在此之前见过的”。

They were over 330 kilometres from the closest land, so he didn't know where to go.

他们离最近的陆地有 330 多公里，所以他不知道该往哪儿走。

词汇over 超过；kilometre 公里；closest 最近的；land 陆地 语法over 表示“超过”；so 连接结果；where to go 是“疑问词 + 不定式”结构。

The ship was on its way to Europe.

这艘船正在前往欧洲的途中。

词汇ship 船；on one's way 在去……的路上；Europe 欧洲 语法on its way to + 地点 表示“在去某地的路上”。

Just ten hours ago they left New York, but now the engine stopped working.

就在十小时前他们离开纽约，但现在发动机停止了运转。

词汇just 就在；leave 离开；engine 发动机；stop 停止 语法ten hours ago 与一般过去时连用；stop doing sth. 表示“停止做某事”。

Clearly, they needed help!

显然，他们需要帮助！

词汇clearly 显然；need 需要；help 帮助 语法clearly 作句子状语；needed 是 need 的过去式。

Suddenly, the captain remembered the homing pigeons.

突然，船长想起了信鸽。

词汇suddenly 突然；remember 想起；homing pigeon 信鸽 语法remember 的过去式 remembered；homing pigeon 中 homing 作定语。

He ran to them quickly, wrote a message for help, and tied it to Big Blue's leg.

他飞快地跑向它们，写了一封求救信，把它系在大蓝的腿上。

词汇run 跑；message 消息；tie 系；leg 腿 语法ran、wrote、tied 三个并列谓语都用一般过去时；tie sth. to ... 表示“把某物系在……上”。

Big Blue flew up and then returned almost right away.

大蓝飞了起来，但几乎马上就回来了。

词汇fly 飞；return 返回；right away 立刻 语法flew 是 fly 的过去式；returned 是 return 的过去式；right away 表示“立刻”。

The captain became more worried.

船长更担心了。

词汇become 变得；more worried 更担心的 语法became 是 become 的过去式；more worried 是比较级。

"You are our last hope, little guy," said the captain as he tied a note to Arny, the smaller and younger of the two pigeons.

“你是我们最后的希望了，小家伙。”船长一边把纸条系到阿尔尼腿上，一边说。阿尔尼是两只鸽子中更小、更年轻的那只。

词汇hope 希望；note 纸条；smaller 更小的；younger 更年轻的 语法as 引导时间状语从句；said the captain 是倒装结构；the smaller and younger of the two pigeons 作 Arny 的同位语，用比较级。

The captain threw him into the air.

船长把他抛向空中。

词汇throw 扔；air 空中 语法threw 是 throw 的过去式；throw sb. into ... 表示“把某人抛入……”。

Arny flew around and around, higher and higher.

阿尔尼一圈一圈地盘旋，越飞越高。

词汇around and around 一圈又一圈；higher 更高的 语法fly around 表示“盘旋”；higher and higher 是“比较级 + and + 比较级”，表示“越来越……”。

Soon, the bird was away from the ship, and the captain couldn't see Arny anymore.

很快，这只鸟离开了船，船长再也看不见阿尔尼了。

词汇soon 很快；away from 离开；anymore 再也（不） 语法couldn't 是 could not 的缩写，表示过去的能力；not ... anymore 表示“不再”。

"That's a good sign," said the captain with more hope.

“这是个好兆头。”船长带着更多希望说。

词汇sign 迹象；hope 希望；with 带着 语法with more hope 作伴随状语；said the captain 是倒装结构。

Almost 340 kilometres away, Billy heard fast-moving wings come into his building and go straight to the water bowl.

在将近 340 公里外，比利听到快速扇动的翅膀飞进他的鸽舍，直奔水碗。

词汇almost 几乎；wing 翅膀；straight 径直；water bowl 水碗 语法hear sb./sth. do sth. 表示“听见某人/某物做某事”，用动词原形；fast-moving 是复合形容词。

"Hi there, little guy. Well, aren't you the thirstiest bird!" Billy smiled.

“嗨，小家伙。哎呀，你真是最渴的鸟了！”比利笑了。

词汇thirsty 口渴的；thirstiest 最渴的；smile 微笑 语法aren't you ...! 是感叹疑问句，表示惊讶；the thirstiest 是最高级。

Then he saw the note.

然后他看到了那张纸条。

词汇then 然后；note 纸条 语法then 表示时间顺序；saw 是 see 的过去式。

Billy knew what to do.

比利知道该怎么做。

词汇knew 知道（know 的过去式）；what to do 做什么 语法what to do 是“疑问词 + 不定式”结构，作 knew 的宾语。

He took the note, running as fast as he could to the company office.

他拿着纸条，以最快的速度跑向公司办公室。

词汇take 拿；as fast as one can 尽可能快；company 公司；office 办公室 语法running ... 是现在分词短语作伴随状语；as fast as he could 表示“尽可能快”。

There, he learnt Arny had flown in fog and over the sea, for more than four hours.

在那里，他得知阿尔尼在雾中飞行、飞越海面，飞了四个多小时。

词汇learnt 得知；fog 雾；over 越过；more than 超过 语法learnt 后接宾语从句，从句用过去完成时 had flown；for + 一段时间表示持续。

Thanks to Arny, people sent help to the ship.

多亏了阿尔尼，人们给那艘船派去了救援。

词汇thanks to 多亏；send 派遣；help 帮助 语法thanks to 是固定短语，表示“多亏、由于”；sent 是 send 的过去式。

The captain later explained, "Arny really is the most amazing bird!"

船长后来解释说：“阿尔尼真的是最了不起的鸟！”

词汇later 后来；explain 解释；amazing 令人惊叹的 语法explain 后接宾语从句；the most amazing 是最高级；really 修饰 is，加强语气。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇故事改编自欧内斯特·西顿的《动物英雄》。1862 年，大西洋某处，一艘开往欧洲的船遇到了浓雾：船长从没见过这么浓的雾，他们离最近的陆地有 330 多公里，而就在十小时前离开纽约后，发动机又停止了运转。显然，他们需要帮助！船长想起了船上的信鸽，赶紧跑过去写了一封求救信，系在大蓝的腿上。大蓝飞起来，却几乎马上又回来了，船长更担心了。于是他把纸条系在两只鸽子中更小、更年轻的阿尔尼腿上，把阿尔尼抛向空中。阿尔尼一圈一圈地飞，越飞越高，很快消失在船外。船长满怀希望地说：“这是个好兆头。”在将近 340 公里外，比利听到快速扇动的翅膀飞进鸽舍、直奔水碗；他看到纸条后，以最快的速度跑到公司办公室，得知阿尔尼在雾中飞越海面四个多小时。多亏了阿尔尼，人们给那艘船派去了救援。船长后来说：“阿尔尼真的是最了不起的鸟！”

English Summary

This story is adapted from Ernest Seton's Animal Heroes. Somewhere in the Atlantic Ocean in 1862, a ship on its way to Europe ran into the thickest fog the captain had ever seen. They were over 330 kilometres from the closest land, and just ten hours after leaving New York the engine stopped working. Clearly, they needed help! The captain remembered the homing pigeons, ran to them quickly, wrote a message for help, and tied it to Big Blue's leg. Big Blue flew up but returned almost right away, so the captain became more worried. He then tied a note to Arny, the smaller and younger of the two pigeons, and threw him into the air. Arny flew around and around, higher and higher, and was soon away from the ship. "That's a good sign," said the captain with more hope. Almost 340 kilometres away, Billy heard fast-moving wings come into his building and go straight to the water bowl. After he saw the note, he ran as fast as he could to the company office and learnt that Arny had flown in fog and over the sea for more than four hours. Thanks to Arny, people sent help to the ship. The captain later explained, "Arny really is the most amazing bird!"

C 原文 When a bee finds new food, it uses a dance to tell the others where and how far away the food is. 可知蜜蜂用舞蹈告诉同伴食物在哪里、有多远。

7. Why did the captain decide to send Arny with a note?

  1. Because Arny was bigger and stronger than Big Blue.
  2. Because Big Blue returned right away and the captain became more worried.
  3. Because Arny had flown across the Atlantic before.
  4. Because the captain wanted to test both pigeons.
答案与解析

B 原文 Big Blue flew up and then returned almost right away. The captain became more worried. 接着船长才把纸条系在阿尔尼身上，可知是因为大蓝马上返回、船长更担心了。

8. How long had Arny flown in fog and over the sea?

  1. For about ten hours.
  2. For almost a year.
  3. For 330 kilometres.
  4. For more than four hours.
答案与解析

D 原文 There, he learnt Arny had flown in fog and over the sea, for more than four hours. 可知阿尔尼在雾中、海上飞了四个多小时。
