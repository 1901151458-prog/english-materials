Section A 3a · Asking for Permission Politely

Section A 3a 主题：家务与请求 · 礼貌用语

Mum: Tom, could you please help me do some chores?

汤姆，你能帮我做些家务吗？

词汇chore 家务；help sb. do sth. 帮某人做某事 语法Could you please do sth.? 是礼貌请求；help sb. do sth.。

Tom: Sure, Mum. What should I do?

当然，妈妈。我该做什么？

词汇sure 当然；should 应该 语法should + 动词原形征求指示。

Mum: Could you please sweep the floor and take out the rubbish?

你能扫扫地、倒倒垃圾吗？

词汇sweep the floor 扫地；take out the rubbish 倒垃圾 语法Could you please do sth.?；and 连接并列动词。

Tom: No problem. I'll do it right away.

没问题。我马上做。

词汇right away 立刻 语法will do 表将来；right away 作状语。

Mum: Thank you. And don't forget to fold your clothes.

谢谢。别忘了把你的衣服叠好。

词汇forget to do 忘记做；fold 折叠 语法Don't forget to do sth. 别忘了做某事。

Tom: OK. But I'm busy with my homework now.

好的。但我现在忙着做作业。

词汇be busy with 忙于 语法be busy with sth.。

Mum: That's fine. You can help after you finish your homework.

没关系。你可以在写完作业之后帮忙。

词汇after 在……之后；finish 完成 语法after 引导时间状语从句。

Tom: Mum, could I use your computer for a while?

妈妈，我可以用一会儿你的电脑吗？

词汇for a while 一会儿 语法Could I...? 礼貌请求许可。

Mum: I'm afraid not. I need it for work.

恐怕不行。我工作要用。

词汇be afraid not 恐怕不行 语法I'm afraid not. 礼貌拒绝。

Tom: Would you mind if I played games later?

你介意我待会儿玩游戏吗？

词汇would you mind 你介意吗 语法Would you mind if + 过去式？表示委婉请求。

Mum: Not at all. But finish your homework first.

不介意。但先写完作业。

词汇not at all 一点也不 语法Not at all. 表示同意请求。

Tom: Great! I'll do my homework first and then help you.

太好了！我先写作业，然后帮你。

词汇then 然后 语法一般将来时；and then 表顺序。

Mum: That's a good boy.

真是个好孩子。

词汇good boy 好孩子 语法称赞用语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，妈妈请汤姆帮忙做家务。汤姆答应了，问该做什么；妈妈让他扫地、倒垃圾、叠衣服。汤姆说自己现在忙着写作业，妈妈说写完后帮忙也可以。汤姆又请求用一会儿电脑，妈妈说恐怕不行，工作要用；汤姆问待会儿玩游戏是否介意，妈妈说可以，但要先写完作业。最后汤姆答应先写完作业再帮忙。

English Summary

In the conversation, Tom's mum asks him to help with chores. Tom agrees and asks what to do; she tells him to sweep the floor, take out the rubbish and fold his clothes. Tom says he is busy with homework now, and his mum says he can help after finishing it. Tom then asks to use the computer for a while; his mum says no because she needs it for work. When he asks whether she would mind if he played games later, she says not at all but he must finish his homework first. Tom agrees to do his homework first and then help.

语法点 Grammar

Could you please help me do some chores?

Could you please do sth.? 是礼貌请求；help sb. do sth.。

What should I do?

should + 动词原形征求指示。

Could you please sweep the floor and take out the rubbish?

and 连接并列动词。

I'll do it right away.

一般将来时；right away 作状语。

Don't forget to fold your clothes.

Don't forget to do sth. 祈使句否定式。

I'm busy with my homework now.

be busy with sth.。

You can help after you finish your homework.

after 引导时间状语从句。

Could I use your computer for a while?

Could I...? 礼貌请求许可。

I'm afraid not.

礼貌拒绝的表达。

Would you mind if I played games later?

Would you mind if + 过去式 表示委婉请求。

Not at all.

表示不介意、同意请求。

I'll do my homework first and then help you.

一般将来时；and then 表顺序。

Section B 1b · My Family

Section B 1b 主题：介绍家人 · 书信

Dear Lingling,

亲爱的玲玲，

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

Thanks for your email. I'm glad to hear that you are coming to my home.

谢谢你的邮件。听说你要来我家，我很高兴。

词汇be glad to do 很高兴做；hear 听说 语法Thanks for doing sth.；be glad to do sth.。

I'd like to tell you about my family.

我想给你讲讲我的家人。

词汇would like to do 想要做 语法I'd like to do sth.。

There are four people in my family: my parents, my sister and I.

我家有四口人：父母、妹妹和我。

词汇there be 有 语法There be 句型。

My father is a kind and hard-working man.

我父亲是个善良又勤劳的人。

词汇kind 善良的；hard-working 勤劳的 语法主系表结构。

He likes helping others and never gets tired of it.

他喜欢帮助别人，而且从不觉得厌烦。

词汇help others 帮助别人；get tired of 对……厌烦 语法like doing sth.；never 表示否定。

My mother is good at cooking and always takes care of us.

我母亲擅长做饭，总是照顾我们。

词汇be good at 擅长；take care of 照顾 语法be good at；always 表示频率。

She makes our home warm and cosy.

她让我们的家温暖舒适。

词汇make sb./sth. + adj. 使…… 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词。

My sister is younger than me, but she is very independent.

我妹妹比我小，但她很独立。

词汇independent 独立的；younger 更小 语法比较级 younger；but 转折。

She helps with the housework on weekends.

周末她会帮忙做家务。

词汇help with 帮忙做；housework 家务 语法help with sth.；on weekends 表示频率。

We are a close family and love each other.

我们是一个亲密的家庭，彼此相爱。

词汇close 亲密的；love each other 互爱 语法主系表结构；each other 作宾语。

I hope you can come soon.

希望你能快点来。

词汇hope 希望；soon 很快 语法hope 后接宾语从句。

Yours,

你的，

词汇yours 你的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Lily

莉莉

词汇Lily 莉莉（人名） 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是莉莉写给玲玲的邮件，介绍自己的家庭。她家有四口人：父母、妹妹和她。父亲善良勤劳，喜欢帮助别人、从不厌烦；母亲擅长做饭，总是照顾家人，让家温暖舒适；妹妹虽然比她小，却很独立，周末会帮忙做家务。她们是一个亲密的家庭，彼此相爱。莉莉希望玲玲能快些来做客。

English Summary

This is Lily's email to Lingling introducing her family. There are four people in her family: her parents, her sister and her. Her father is kind and hard-working and likes helping others without getting tired of it. Her mother is good at cooking, always takes care of them and makes the home warm and cosy. Her younger sister is very independent and helps with the housework on weekends. They are a close family and love each other, and Lily hopes Lingling can come soon.

Section B 3a · A Happy Sunday

Section B 3a 主题：家庭时光 · 叙事

Today is Sunday, so my family decided to have a picnic in the park.

今天是星期天，所以我们一家决定去公园野餐。

词汇decide to do 决定做；picnic 野餐 语法so 连接结果；decide to do sth.。

We got up early and prepared some food.

我们早早就起来了，准备了一些食物。

词汇get up 起床；prepare 准备 语法一般过去时；and 连接并列谓语。

My father made sandwiches, and my mother made some juice.

爸爸做了三明治，妈妈做了一些果汁。

词汇sandwich 三明治；juice 果汁 语法make sth.；and 连接并列分句。

I helped them put the food into bags.

我帮他们把食物装进袋子里。

词汇put...into 把……放进；bag 袋子 语法help sb. do sth.；put sth. into sth.。

When we arrived at the park, there were many people there.

我们到达公园时，那里已经有很多人了。

词汇arrive at 到达；many people 许多人 语法when 引导时间状语从句；There be 句型。

Some were flying kites, and some were playing ball games.

有些人在放风筝，有些人在玩球类游戏。

词汇fly kites 放风筝；ball game 球类游戏 语法过去进行时并列；some...some...。

We found a quiet place under a big tree and sat down.

我们在一棵大树下找到了一处安静的地方坐下。

词汇quiet 安静的；sit down 坐下 语法find + 宾语 + 地点；and 连接并列谓语。

The food was delicious, and the weather was perfect.

食物很美味，天气也很完美。

词汇delicious 美味的；perfect 完美的 语法主系表结构并列。

After lunch, we played games together and took lots of photos.

午饭后，我们一起玩游戏，还拍了很多照片。

词汇take photos 拍照；after lunch 午饭后 语法after 表示时间顺序。

My parents looked very happy.

我父母看起来很开心。

词汇look + adj. 看起来 语法look + adj.。

They said they enjoyed the time with us.

他们说很享受和我们一起的时光。

词汇enjoy 享受 语法that 从句省略；enjoy sth.。

I love my family and I hope we can have more weekends like this.

我爱我的家人，希望我们能多过一些这样的周末。

词汇weekend 周末 语法and 连接并列分句；like this 作后置定语。

Family time is the best time!

家庭时光是最美好的时光！

词汇family time 家庭时光 语法最高级 the best。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文记录了一个愉快的星期天。一家人决定去公园野餐，早起准备食物：爸爸做三明治，妈妈做果汁，作者帮忙装袋。到公园时人很多，有的放风筝，有的玩球。他们在大树下找到安静的地方坐下，食物美味、天气完美。午饭后一起玩游戏、拍了很多照片，父母看起来很开心，说很享受和家人一起的时光。作者爱家人，希望多过这样美好的周末。

English Summary

The passage describes a happy Sunday. The family decided to have a picnic in the park, got up early and prepared food: Dad made sandwiches, Mum made juice, and the writer helped put the food into bags. When they arrived, the park was full of people flying kites and playing ball games. They found a quiet place under a big tree, and the food was delicious and the weather perfect. After lunch they played games together and took lots of photos. The parents looked happy and said they enjoyed the time with the family. The writer loves the family and hopes for more weekends like this.
