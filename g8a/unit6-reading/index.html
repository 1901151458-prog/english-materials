Section A 2a · Dream Jobs: A Talk Between Adam and Fu Xing

Section A 2a 主题：梦想职业 · be going to 谈计划

Adam: What are you reading, Fu Xing?

付星，你在读什么？

词汇read 阅读 语法现在进行时，询问正在做的事情；称呼语 Fu Xing 置于句末。

Fu Xing: It's a book of essays by a famous Chinese writer.

是一本著名中国作家的散文集。

词汇essay 散文；文章；famous 著名的；writer 作家 语法by a famous Chinese writer 中 by 表示作者“由……所写”。

Adam: Wow, now I know why you're so good at writing.

哇，现在我知道你为什么这么擅长写作了。

词汇be good at 擅长；writing 写作 语法why 引导宾语从句；be good at + 动名词，表示“擅长做某事”。

Fu Xing: Thanks!

谢谢！

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法口语中简答致谢，相当于 Thank you。

Fu Xing: You know, I want to be a writer when I'm older.

你知道吗，我长大后想当一名作家。

词汇want to be 想成为；older 更年长的 语法want to be + 职业；when 引导时间状语从句，从句用一般现在时表将来。

Adam: Really?

真的吗？

词汇really 真的 语法省略问句，用升调表示惊讶和追问。

Adam: How do you plan to do that?

你打算怎样做到呢？

词汇plan to do sth. 计划做某事 语法How 询问方式；plan to do sth. 表示“计划做某事”。

Fu Xing: I'm going to read more classics.

我打算多读一些经典作品。

词汇be going to 打算；classics 经典作品 语法be going to do sth. 表示计划、打算做某事。

Fu Xing: And also, I'm going to keep on writing stories and essays.

而且，我打算继续写故事和散文。

词汇keep on doing sth. 继续做某事；story 故事 语法and also 表示递进；keep on doing sth. 表示“继续做某事”。

Adam: Your plan sounds wonderful!

你的计划听起来棒极了！

词汇sound 听起来；wonderful 精彩的；极好的 语法sound 是系动词，后接形容词 wonderful 作表语。

Adam: I think you're going to be a great writer.

我觉得你会成为一名很棒的作家。

词汇great 很棒的 语法I think 后接宾语从句；be going to be 表示“将会成为”。

Fu Xing: I hope so!

希望如此！

词汇hope 希望 语法I hope so. 是口语中回应他人祝愿的常用表达。

Fu Xing: What about you, Adam?

你呢，亚当？

词汇what about ……怎么样 语法What about you? 用于反问对方的情况。

Fu Xing: What do you want to be when you're older?

你长大后想做什么？

词汇want to be 想成为 语法when 引导时间状语从句；询问理想职业。

Adam: My parents want me to be a doctor, but I'm not sure about that.

我父母想让我当医生，但我对此不太确定。

词汇parents 父母；doctor 医生；be sure about 对……有把握 语法want sb. to do sth. 想让某人做某事；be sure about sth. 表示“对……有把握”；but 表转折。

Fu Xing: Well, don't worry.

嗯，别担心。

词汇worry 担心 语法Well 是口语中的语气词；don't worry 是祈使句的否定式。

Fu Xing: Not everyone knows what they want to be.

并不是每个人都知道自己将来想做什么。

词汇not everyone 不是每个人 语法not everyone 是部分否定，表示“并非人人”；what they want to be 是宾语从句。

Fu Xing: Just make sure you try your best at school.

只要确保在学校尽自己最大的努力就行。

词汇make sure 确保；try one's best 尽某人最大的努力 语法make sure + 从句；try one's best 是固定搭配。

Fu Xing: Then you can do anything you want!

那样你就能做任何你想做的事了！

词汇anything 任何事物 语法anything you want 中 you want 是定语从句，修饰 anything。

Adam: Yeah, you're right.

是的，你说得对。

词汇yeah 是的 语法Yeah 是口语中的肯定回答；you're right 表示“你说得对”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在亚当（Adam）和付星（Fu Xing）之间。亚当问付星在读什么，付星说是一本著名中国作家的散文集；亚当说现在明白她为什么这么擅长写作了。付星说，她长大后想当一名作家，打算多读经典作品，并继续写故事和散文。亚当觉得她的计划很棒，相信她会成为优秀的作家。付星反问亚当长大后想做什么，亚当说父母想让他当医生，但他自己并不确定。付星安慰他说别担心，不是每个人都知道自己将来想做什么，只要确保在学校尽最大努力，将来就能做任何想做的事，亚当说她说得对。

English Summary

This conversation takes place between Adam and Fu Xing. Adam asks Fu Xing what she is reading, and she says it is a book of essays by a famous Chinese writer. Adam says now he understands why she is so good at writing. Fu Xing says she wants to be a writer when she is older: she is going to read more classics and keep on writing stories and essays. Adam thinks her plan sounds wonderful and believes she is going to be a great writer. Fu Xing then asks Adam what he wants to be when he is older. Adam says his parents want him to be a doctor, but he is not sure about that. Fu Xing comforts him, saying that not everyone knows what they want to be, and that as long as he tries his best at school, he can do anything he wants. Adam agrees.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Wow, now I know why you're so good at writing.

哇，现在我知道你为什么这么擅长写作了。

why 引导宾语从句，作 know 的宾语，从句用陈述语序；be good at 表示“擅长”，at 是介词，后接动名词 writing。

You know, I want to be a writer when I'm older.

你知道吗，我长大后想当一名作家。

You know 是口语中的插入语，用来引起对方注意；when 引导时间状语从句，从句用一般现在时表示将来。

Not everyone knows what they want to be.

并不是每个人都知道自己将来想做什么。

not everyone 是部分否定，意为“并非人人”，不是“人人都不”；what they want to be 是宾语从句，作 knows 的宾语。

Section B 1b · Making Resolutions

Section B 1b 主题：新年决心 · 说明文（含义—种类—原因—建议）

Do you know what a resolution is?

你知道决心是什么吗？

词汇resolution 决心；决定 语法what 引导宾语从句，作 know 的宾语，从句用陈述语序。

A resolution is a promise that you make to yourself.

决心是你对自己许下的承诺。

词汇promise 承诺；make a promise 许下承诺 语法that 引导定语从句，修饰 promise；make a promise to sb. 向某人承诺。

People make resolutions to improve their life.

人们下决心是为了改善生活。

词汇improve 改善；提高 语法动词不定式 to improve 作目的状语。

The most common kind is a New Year's resolution.

最常见的一种是新年决心。

词汇common 常见的；New Year's resolution 新年决心 语法the most common 是形容词最高级；kind 在此指“种类”。

After all, the start of the year is often a perfect time for making resolutions.

毕竟，一年的开始往往是下决心的好时机。

词汇after all 毕竟；perfect 完美的 语法after all 作插入语；the start of the year 作主语；a perfect time for doing sth. 表示“做某事的好时机”。

Resolutions can be about anything.

决心可以关于任何事情。

词汇anything 任何事物 语法can + be about，表示“可以是关于……的”。

Some are about physical health.

有些是关于身体健康的。

词汇physical 身体的；health 健康 语法some 作主语，指代上文提到的 resolutions。

For example, some people may promise to exercise more or eat more healthily.

例如，有些人可能会承诺多锻炼、吃得更健康。

词汇for example 例如；exercise 锻炼；healthily 健康地 语法may 表示可能性；promise to do sth. 承诺做某事；more healthily 是副词比较级。

Some resolutions have to do with learning new things.

有些决心与学习新事物有关。

词汇have to do with 与……有关 语法have to do with + 名词/动名词，表示“与……有关”。

People might decide they are going to take up a hobby, like painting or photography.

人们可能决定开始培养一项爱好，比如绘画或摄影。

词汇might 可能；decide 决定；take up 开始从事；photography 摄影 语法might 表示可能性；decide 后接宾语从句；take up a hobby 表示“开始培养爱好”。

And some resolutions have to do with self-improvement, like becoming more confident or organized.

还有些决心与自我提高有关，比如变得更自信、更有条理。

词汇self-improvement 自我提高；confident 自信的；organized 有条理的 语法have to do with；like 作介词“像……一样”；becoming 是动名词。

For example, a student may decide to start a new study plan.

例如，一个学生可能决定开始一个新的学习计划。

词汇study plan 学习计划 语法decide to do sth. 决定做某事；study 在此作定语修饰 plan。

Although there are differences, most resolutions have one thing in common: people hardly ever keep them!

尽管（决心）各有不同，但大多数决心有一个共同点：人们几乎从不坚持它们！

词汇although 尽管；hardly ever 几乎从不；keep 坚持；遵守 语法although 引导让步状语从句，不与 but 连用；have ... in common 表示“有共同之处”；hardly ever 是表示否定意义的频率副词。

But there are good reasons for this.

但这其中有很好的原因。

词汇reason 原因 语法there are ... reasons for sth. 表示“某事有……原因”；this 指代上文“很少坚持”这件事。

Sometimes the resolutions may be too difficult to keep.

有时决心可能太难坚持。

词汇sometimes 有时；difficult 困难的 语法too ... to do sth. 结构，表示“太……而不能做某事”。

And sometimes people just forget about them.

而有时人们只是把它们忘了。

词汇forget about 忘记 语法forget about sth. 忘记某事；them 指上文的 resolutions。

To make your resolutions work, you need to plan them wisely.

要让你的决心奏效，你需要明智地计划它们。

词汇work 起作用；奏效；wisely 明智地 语法动词不定式 to make ... work 作目的状语；work 在此是不及物动词，意为“奏效”。

Always make sure your resolution is possible to achieve.

一定要确保你的决心是可以实现的。

词汇make sure 确保；possible 可能的；achieve 实现 语法make sure + 宾语从句；be possible to achieve 表示“有可能实现”。

Don't set a goal that is too difficult or too much for you.

不要设定一个对你来说太难或超出能力的目标。

词汇set a goal 设定目标；goal 目标 语法祈使句的否定式；that 引导定语从句修饰 goal；too ... for sb. 表示“对某人来说太……”。

Try to set mini-goals to help you achieve your resolution.

尝试设定小目标来帮助你实现决心。

词汇mini-goal 小目标；help sb. do sth. 帮助某人做某事 语法try to do sth. 尝试做某事；不定式 to help 作目的状语；help sb. do sth.。

And if you don't want to forget your resolutions, write them down.

如果你不想忘记自己的决心，就把它们写下来。

词汇write down 写下 语法if 引导条件状语从句；write them down 中代词 them 放在动词与副词之间。

Also, talk about them with your family and friends.

另外，和家人朋友谈谈它们。

词汇talk about 谈论 语法祈使句；talk about sth. with sb. 和某人谈论某事。

Encourage them to remind you of your goals.

鼓励他们提醒你你的目标。

词汇encourage 鼓励；remind sb. of sth. 提醒某人某事 语法encourage sb. to do sth. 鼓励某人做某事；remind sb. of sth. 使某人想起某事。

So what resolutions are you going to make?

那么你打算下什么决心呢？

词汇be going to 打算 语法what 引导特殊疑问句；be going to make resolutions 表示“打算下决心”。

How are you going to keep them?

你打算怎样坚持它们呢？

词汇keep 坚持；遵守 语法How 询问方式；keep resolutions 表示“坚持决心”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇关于“决心（resolution）”的说明文，共四段。第一段说明决心的含义：决心是你对自己许下的承诺，人们下决心是为了改善生活，最常见的是新年决心，而一年的开始正是下决心的好时机。第二段说明决心可以关于任何事情：有的关于身体健康，比如多锻炼、吃得更健康；有的与学习新事物有关，比如培养绘画、摄影等爱好；有的与自我提高有关，比如变得更自信、更有条理，比如学生决定开始新的学习计划。第三段指出：尽管决心各不相同，但大多数决心有一个共同点——人们几乎从不坚持；原因是有时决心太难坚持，或者人们干脆忘了。第四段给出建议：明智地计划决心，确保目标可以实现，不要设定太难或超出自己能力的目标；可以设定小目标帮助实现决心，把决心写下来，并和家人朋友谈论，请他们提醒你。最后作者反问：你打算下什么决心？你打算怎样坚持？

English Summary

This is an expository text about resolutions in four paragraphs. Paragraph 1 explains what a resolution is: it is a promise that you make to yourself, and people make resolutions to improve their life. The most common kind is a New Year's resolution, and the start of the year is a perfect time for making resolutions. Paragraph 2 shows that resolutions can be about anything: some are about physical health, such as exercising more or eating more healthily; some have to do with learning new things, such as taking up a hobby like painting or photography; some are about self-improvement, like becoming more confident or organized, for example a student may decide to start a new study plan. Paragraph 3 points out that although resolutions differ, most of them have one thing in common: people hardly ever keep them, because some resolutions are too difficult to keep and sometimes people just forget about them. Paragraph 4 gives advice: plan your resolutions wisely, make sure they are possible to achieve, do not set a goal that is too difficult for you, try to set mini-goals, write your resolutions down, and talk about them with your family and friends so that they can remind you of your goals. Finally the writer asks what resolutions you are going to make and how you are going to keep them.

Don't set a goal that is too difficult or too much for you.

祈使句否定式；that 引导定语从句；too ... for sb.。

If you don't want to forget your resolutions, write them down.

if 引导条件状语从句；write sth. down 代词放中间。

Encourage them to remind you of your goals.

encourage sb. to do sth.；remind sb. of sth.。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Although there are differences, most resolutions have one thing in common: people hardly ever keep them!

尽管（决心）各有不同，但大多数决心有一个共同点：人们几乎从不坚持它们！

although 引导让步状语从句，英语中 although 不能与 but 连用；have ... in common 表示“有共同之处”；hardly ever 是表示否定意义的频率副词，意为“几乎从不”。

To make your resolutions work, you need to plan them wisely.

要让你的决心奏效，你需要明智地计划它们。

句首的不定式短语 to make your resolutions work 作目的状语；work 在此是不及物动词，意为“奏效、起作用”；wisely 是副词，修饰动词 plan。

Encourage them to remind you of your goals.

鼓励他们提醒你你的目标。

encourage sb. to do sth. 表示“鼓励某人做某事”；remind sb. of sth. 表示“使某人想起某事、提醒某人某事”，注意 of 不能换成 about。

Reading Plus · The Ant and the Grasshopper

Reading Plus 主题：寓言故事 · 为将来做准备

It was summertime.

那是夏天。

词汇summertime 夏天 语法一般过去时，交代故事发生的季节。

The ant and the grasshopper were in the farmer's field.

蚂蚁和蚱蜢在一块农田里。

词汇ant 蚂蚁；grasshopper 蚱蜢；farmer 农民；field 田地 语法一般过去时；in the farmer's field 是地点状语。

The ant was busy collecting corn.

蚂蚁正忙着收集玉米。

词汇be busy doing sth. 忙于做某事；collect 收集；corn 玉米 语法be busy doing sth. 表示“忙于做某事”；was busy 是过去进行时的系表结构。

The grasshopper was relaxing in the sun.

蚱蜢正在阳光下休息。

词汇relax 放松；in the sun 在阳光下 语法过去进行时，表示过去某时正在进行的动作。

“Hey, Ant, come sit with me,” said the grasshopper.

“嘿，蚂蚁，过来和我坐坐吧，”蚱蜢说。

词汇come 来；sit with 和……坐在一起 语法直接引语；come sit 是 come and sit 的省略。

“No, thanks,” said the ant.

“不了，谢谢，”蚂蚁说。

词汇no, thanks 不了，谢谢 语法直接引语；said the ant 是倒装句式。

“I have to take this corn home. I'm preparing for the winter.”

“我得把这些玉米搬回家。我在为冬天做准备。”

词汇have to 不得不；take ... home 把……带回家；prepare for 为……做准备 语法have to + 动词原形；现在进行时表示正在进行的准备。

A few weeks later, summer was almost at an end.

几周之后，夏天几乎要结束了。

词汇a few weeks later 几周之后；at an end 结束 语法一般过去时；at an end 是固定短语，表示“结束”。

The ant continued to take corn home every day, but the grasshopper just played his violin and danced around.

蚂蚁每天继续把玉米搬回家，而蚱蜢只是拉小提琴、到处跳舞。

词汇continue to do sth. 继续做某事；violin 小提琴；dance around 到处跳舞 语法continue to do sth.；but 表示前后对比；and 连接并列谓语。

“Come on, Ant!” said the grasshopper. “Dance with me!”

“来吧，蚂蚁！”蚱蜢说，“和我一起跳舞吧！”

词汇come on 来吧；dance with 和……跳舞 语法直接引语；祈使句表示邀请。

“No,” replied the ant.

“不，”蚂蚁回答道。

词汇reply 回答 语法直接引语；replied the ant 是倒装句式。

“I'm going to get more corn. Why don't you join me? You should plan for the winter.”

“我还要去弄更多玉米。你为什么不和我一起呢？你应该为冬天做计划。”

词汇be going to 打算；join 加入；plan for 为……做计划 语法be going to 表示打算；Why don't you ...? 提出建议；should + 动词原形表示建议。

“But the winter is so far away! Your work looks so boring! Come with me! Let's have a picnic!”

“可是冬天还那么远！你的工作看起来好无聊！跟我来吧！我们去野餐！”

词汇far away 遥远；boring 无聊的；picnic 野餐 语法but 表转折；感叹句；Let's do sth. 提出建议。

“No, thanks, Grasshopper. I'm going to continue with my boring work.”

“不了，谢谢，蚱蜢。我要继续做我无聊的工作了。”

词汇continue with 继续做…… 语法be going to continue with 表示“打算继续做……”；boring 修饰 work。

The grasshopper was silent for a while, but then he happily danced away.

蚱蜢沉默了一会儿，然后就高兴地跳着舞走了。

词汇silent 沉默的；for a while 一会儿；dance away 跳着舞离开 语法for a while 表示一段时间；but then 表示转折；happily 修饰动词 danced。

The ant looked tired, but she felt happy when she saw all the food in her kitchen.

蚂蚁看起来很累，但当她看到厨房里所有的食物时，她感到很开心。

词汇look 看起来；tired 疲倦的；kitchen 厨房 语法look 是系动词，后接形容词 tired；when 引导时间状语从句；but 表转折。

The autumn arrived and the weather became cool and windy.

秋天到了，天气变得凉爽又多风。

词汇autumn 秋天；weather 天气；windy 多风的 语法一般过去时；become 是系动词，后接并列形容词 cool and windy。

The grasshopper ran around the farm singing songs.

蚱蜢在农场里到处跑着唱歌。

词汇run around 到处跑；sing songs 唱歌 语法singing songs 是现在分词短语作伴随状语。

The poor ant had to fight her way through the wind.

可怜的蚂蚁不得不顶着风艰难前行。

词汇poor 可怜的；fight one's way through 奋力穿过 语法had to 是 have to 的过去式；fight one's way through 表示“奋力穿过……”。

She wanted to collect lots of wood.

她想收集很多木柴。

词汇wood 木柴；木头；lots of 许多 语法want to do sth. 想要做某事；lots of 修饰不可数名词 wood。

“Ant, it's too windy to work!” said the grasshopper. “Come and sing with me!”

“蚂蚁，风太大了，没法干活！”蚱蜢说，“来和我一起唱歌吧！”

词汇too ... to 太……而不能 语法too windy to work 是 too ... to 结构；come and sing 表示“来唱歌”。

“No, thanks, Grasshopper. There's just a little bit more work for me to do. I want to be warm in the winter.”

“不了，谢谢，蚱蜢。我还有一些活儿要干。我想在冬天暖和一点。”

词汇a little bit 一点；warm 温暖的 语法There's ... work for me to do 中不定式作定语；want to be warm 表示“想暖和”。

And then the cold winter came.

然后寒冷的冬天来了。

词汇cold 寒冷的 语法一般过去时，推进故事情节。

The ant sat at home by her fire, enjoying some lovely corn soup.

蚂蚁坐在家里的火边，享用着美味的玉米汤。

词汇by the fire 在火边；enjoy 享受；soup 汤 语法enjoying 是现在分词短语作伴随状语；lovely 修饰 corn soup。

Then she heard a knock at the door.

接着她听到有人敲门。

词汇hear 听到；knock 敲门声 语法hear sb./sth. do 或 hear a knock；a knock at the door 表示“敲门声”。

“Oh, dear Ant,” said the grasshopper, “you must help me! I have no food! And no fire to keep me warm!”

“哦，亲爱的蚂蚁，”蚱蜢说，“你必须帮帮我！我没有食物了！也没有火取暖！”

词汇must 必须；keep sb. warm 使某人暖和 语法must + 动词原形表示“必须”；keep me warm 中 warm 作宾补。

“Sorry, Grasshopper, you must learn to look after yourself. Keep dancing and singing through the winter, and work hard when spring comes!”

“对不起，蚱蜢，你必须学会照顾自己。冬天就继续跳舞、唱歌吧，等春天来了再努力工作！”

词汇look after 照顾；through 穿过；整个……期间；spring 春天 语法learn to do sth. 学会做某事；Keep doing sth. 祈使句表示“继续做某事”；when 引导时间状语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇改编自《伊索寓言》的故事。夏天，蚂蚁和蚱蜢在农田里：蚂蚁忙着收集玉米，为冬天做准备；蚱蜢却在阳光下休息、拉小提琴、到处跳舞，还嘲笑蚂蚁的工作无聊。几周后夏天快结束时，蚂蚁每天继续搬玉米回家，蚱蜢仍然只顾玩乐，邀请蚂蚁一起野餐，蚂蚁拒绝了。蚱蜢沉默了一会儿又高兴地跳着舞走了；蚂蚁虽然累，但看到厨房里堆满食物时很开心。秋天到了，天气变得凉爽多风，蚱蜢在农场里唱歌，可怜的蚂蚁不得不顶着风艰难前行，还要收集木柴。蚱蜢说风太大不能干活，约蚂蚁一起唱歌，蚂蚁仍然坚持把活干完，好让自己在冬天暖和。寒冷的冬天来了，蚂蚁坐在家里的火边喝着美味的玉米汤，这时听到敲门声：蚱蜢没有食物也没有火，来向蚂蚁求助。蚂蚁说：对不起，你必须学会照顾自己；冬天继续唱歌跳舞吧，等春天来了再努力工作！

English Summary

This story is adapted from Aesop's Fables. In summertime, the ant and the grasshopper were in the farmer's field. The ant was busy collecting corn and preparing for the winter, while the grasshopper relaxed in the sun, played his violin and danced around, and even laughed at the ant's boring work. A few weeks later, when summer was almost at an end, the ant kept taking corn home every day, but the grasshopper just played and invited the ant to have a picnic; the ant refused. The grasshopper was silent for a while and then happily danced away, while the ant, though tired, felt happy when she saw all the food in her kitchen. In autumn, the weather became cool and windy. The grasshopper ran around the farm singing songs, but the poor ant had to fight her way through the wind and collect lots of wood. The grasshopper said it was too windy to work and asked the ant to sing with him, but she refused and wanted to be warm in the winter. When the cold winter came, the ant sat at home by her fire, enjoying some lovely corn soup, and then she heard a knock at the door. The grasshopper had no food and no fire to keep him warm and asked the ant for help. The ant answered, "Sorry, Grasshopper, you must learn to look after yourself. Keep dancing and singing through the winter, and work hard when spring comes!"
