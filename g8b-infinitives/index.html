动词不定式专项

人教版（新课纲版）八年级下册 · 动词不定式（Infinitives）

01

构成与三种句法作用

to + 动词原形

动词不定式的基本形式是「to + 动词原形」，没有人称和数的变化，不能作谓语，但可以有自己的宾语和状语。

句法作用例句
作宾语Do you want to visit the Great Wall?
I like to play badminton in my free time.
作目的状语What do you do to pass the time?
Allen ran to the living room to get the fire extinguisher.
作宾语补足语My instructor encouraged me to get back up.
Doctor Cooper asked him to be more careful.
02

否定式：not + 不定式

否定词放在 to 前面

动词不定式的否定形式由「not + 动词不定式」构成，not 放在 to 之前。

I would prefer not to talk about it.

我宁愿不谈这件事。

× to not talk

"Try not to make any noise when you eat," his mother said.

他妈妈说：「吃饭时尽量不要发出声音。」

try not to do sth。

My parents told me not to push myself too hard.

父母告诉我不要逼自己太紧。

She told the children not to play in the street.

她告诉孩子们不要在街上玩。

03

疑问词 + 不定式

what / how / where / which + to do

动词不定式可以和 what、which、how、where、when 等疑问词连用，构成「疑问词 + 不定式」结构，相当于一个名词短语。

He didn't know what to say to Ella.

他不知道该对埃拉说什么。

She asked me how to use the washing machine.

她问我怎么用洗衣机。

The tour guide will tell you where to go next.

导游会告诉你接下来去哪里。

I have no idea which book to choose.

我不知道该选哪本书。

04

不带 to 的不定式

感官动词 + 使役动词

有一些动词后用作宾语补足语的不定式通常不带 to，主要有两类：

感官动词

  • see、hear、watch、feel、notice
  • The referee saw Matt push the other player.
  • He heard someone walk down the stairway.

使役动词

  • let、make、have
  • Please let me know.
  • Hobbies can make you feel good about yourself.

注意：这两类动词变被动语态时，to 要还原：He was seen to push the other player.

05

作宾语 vs 作宾补

看动词后面接的是谁

作宾语：动词 + to do

  • want / decide / hope / agree / promise / plan / refuse / fail
  • She decided to study harder.

作宾补：动词 + sb + to do

  • tell / ask / want / encourage / advise / teach
  • She told me to study harder.

区别：不定式前面有没有「人（宾语）」——有就是宾语补足语，没有就是宾语。
