01

现在进行时：构成

am / is / are + 动词-ing

现在进行时由助动词 be（am / is / are）加动词 -ing 形式构成。

The students are listening to the teacher.

主语复数用 are。

He is sleeping now.

主语单数用 is。

否定：am not / is not (isn't) / are not (aren't) + 动词-ing。

02

动词 -ing 的变化

直接加、去 e、双写、ie 变 y

类型变化例
一般直接加 -inggo–going、study–studying
不发音 e 结尾去 e 加 -ingwrite–writing、make–making
重读闭音节单辅音双写加 -ingget–getting、sit–sitting、begin–beginning
ie 结尾ie 变 y 加 -inglie–lying、die–dying
03

一般过去时：构成

was / were；动词 + -ed

表示过去某个时间或某一段时间内发生的动作或存在的状态，常和 yesterday、last night、in 1990、two days ago 等连用。

He got up early yesterday.

实义动词过去式。

We were at home yesterday.

be 动词用 were。

04

一般过去时：否定与疑问

didn't + 动词原形

否定

  • be：was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't)
  • 实义动词：did not (didn't) + 动词原形
  • × didn't worked → ✓ didn't work

疑问

  • be：Was he ...? / Were they ...?
  • 实义动词：Did you go ...?
  • 简答：Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.
