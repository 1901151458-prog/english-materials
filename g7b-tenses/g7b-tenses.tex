\section{现在进行时的构成}
现在进行时由助动词 be（am / is / are）加动词 -ing 形式构成。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item The students \underline{are listening} to the teacher.（主语复数用 are）
\item He \underline{is sleeping} now.（主语单数用 is）
\end{itemize}
\textbf{否定：}am not / is not (isn't) / are not (aren't) + 动词-ing。

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{4.8cm}p{3.4cm}p{6.0cm}}
\hline
\textbf{类型} & \textbf{变化} & \textbf{例}\\
\hline
一般 & 直接加 -ing & go--going、study--studying\\

重读闭音节单辅音 & 双写加 -ing & get--getting、sit--sitting、begin--beginning\\
ie 结尾 & ie 变 y 加 -ing & lie--lying、die--dying\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{一般过去时的构成}
表示过去某个时间或某一段时间内发生的动作或存在的状态，常和 yesterday、last night、in 1990、two days ago 等连用。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item He \underline{got up} early yesterday.
\item We \underline{were} at home yesterday.
\end{itemize}

\section{一般过去时的否定与疑问}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{be 动词}：was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't)
\item \textbf{实义动词否定}：did not (didn't) + \textbf{动词原形}（× didn't worked → ✓ didn't work）
\item \textbf{疑问}：Was he ...? / Were they ...? / \underline{Did} you go ...?
\item \textbf{简答}：Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.
\end{itemize}
