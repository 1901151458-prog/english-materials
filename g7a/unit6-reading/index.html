Section A 3a · What Time Do You Get Up?

Section A 3a 主题：日常作息与时间 · 对话

Jack: Hi, Anna! What time do you usually get up?

嗨，安娜！你通常几点起床？

词汇get up 起床；usually 通常 语法What time do you...? 询问具体时间；usually 表频率。

Anna: I usually get up at half past six in the morning.

我通常早上六点半起床。

词汇half past six 六点半；in the morning 在早上 语法at + 具体时刻；half past + 基数词表示“几点半”。

Jack: That's early! When do you have breakfast?

好早啊！你什么时候吃早饭？

词汇breakfast 早餐 语法When do you...? 询问时间。

Anna: At seven o'clock. I have some bread and a glass of milk.

七点。我吃一些面包、喝一杯牛奶。

词汇at seven o'clock 在七点 语法at + 整点 + o'clock。

Jack: How do you go to school?

你怎么去上学？

词汇how 如何 语法How do you...? 询问方式。

Anna: I usually walk, but sometimes I take the bus.

我通常步行，但有时坐公交车。

词汇walk 步行；sometimes 有时；take the bus 乘公交车 语法sometimes 表频率；but 表转折。

Jack: What time does your first class start?

你的第一节课几点开始？

词汇start 开始；first class 第一节课 语法第三人称单数用 does；What time does...start?。

Anna: It starts at eight o'clock, and I am never late.

八点开始，我从不迟到。

词汇never 从不；late 迟到的 语法never 表频率，位于 be 动词之后。

Jack: Do you do sports in the afternoon?

你下午做运动吗？

词汇do sports 做运动；in the afternoon 在下午 语法Do you...? 一般现在时疑问句。

Anna: Yes, I always play basketball for an hour after school.

是的，放学后我总是打一小时篮球。

词汇always 总是；for an hour 一小时 语法always 位于实义动词前；for + 一段时间。

Jack: When do you go to bed?

你什么时候睡觉？

词汇go to bed 上床睡觉 语法When do you...? 询问时间。

Anna: I go to bed at half past nine and read for a while.

我九点半睡觉，睡前会看一会儿书。

词汇for a while 一会儿 语法at + 具体时刻；and 连接并列谓语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话是杰克和安娜在聊一天的作息。杰克问安娜通常几点起床，安娜说早上六点半；杰克觉得好早，又问什么时候吃早饭，安娜说七点，吃一些面包、喝一杯牛奶。杰克问她怎么去上学，安娜说通常步行，有时坐公交车。她第一节 课八点开始，从不迟到。下午放学后安娜总是打一小时篮球，晚上九点半睡觉，睡前会看一会儿书。

English Summary

The conversation is between Jack and Anna about their daily routine. Jack asks Anna what time she usually gets up, and she says half past six in the morning. Jack is surprised and asks when she has breakfast; Anna says at seven o'clock and she has some bread and a glass of milk. Jack asks how she goes to school, and she says she usually walks but sometimes takes the bus. Her first class starts at eight o'clock and she is never late. After school she always plays basketball for an hour, and she goes to bed at half past nine and reads for a while.

语法点 Grammar

What time do you usually get up?

What time do you...? 询问具体时间；助动词 do 提前。

I usually get up at half past six.

at + 具体时刻；half past + 基数词表示“几点半”。

I get up at half past six in the morning.

in the morning 表示“在上午”，at 用于时刻。

When do you have breakfast?

When do you...? 询问时间。

At seven o'clock.

at + 整点 + o'clock。

I usually walk, but sometimes I take the bus.

频率副词 sometimes 位于实义动词前；but 表转折。

What time does your first class start?

第三人称单数用 does + 动词原形。

It starts at eight o'clock, and I am never late.

never 位于 be 动词之后，表频率。

Do you do sports in the afternoon?

Do you...? 一般现在时一般疑问句。

I always play basketball for an hour.

always 位于实义动词前；for + 一段时间。

When do you go to bed?

When do you...? 询问时间。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I usually get up at half past six in the morning.

我通常早上六点半起床。

时间表达：in the morning 表“在上午”，具体时刻前用 at；half past six 表示六点半。

I usually walk, but sometimes I take the bus.

我通常步行，但有时坐公交车。

usually 与 sometimes 频率不同，构成转折；take the bus 表乘公交。

I always play basketball for an hour after school.

放学后我总是打一小时篮球。

always 与 for an hour 分别表示频率与持续时间。

Section B 1b · My Busy Day

Section B 1b 主题：一天的作息 · 说明短文

I am a student at No. 5 Middle School, and I have a busy day.

我是第五中学的一名学生，我的一天很忙。

词汇busy 忙的 语法主系表结构；and 连接并列分句。

I always get up at six o'clock in the morning.

我总是早上六点起床。

词汇always 总是 语法always 位于实义动词前，表频率。

Then I brush my teeth and wash my face.

然后我刷牙洗脸。

词汇brush my teeth 刷牙；wash my face 洗脸 语法一般现在时表示日常习惯。

At half past six, I usually have breakfast with my family.

六点半，我通常和家人一起吃早饭。

词汇have breakfast 吃早饭；with 和……一起 语法at + 具体时间；usually 表频率。

I leave home at seven o'clock and take the bus to school.

我七点离开家，坐公交车去学校。

词汇leave home 离开家；take the bus to 坐公交车去 语法一般现在时表习惯；take + 交通工具。

Classes start at eight o'clock, and we have four lessons in the morning.

八点开始上课，上午我们有四节课。

词汇lesson 课；in the morning 在上午 语法一般现在时；start 第三人称单数加 s。

I often eat rice and vegetables for lunch in the school dining hall.

我经常在学校食堂吃米饭和蔬菜当午餐。

词汇often 经常；dining hall 食堂 语法often 位于实义动词前；have sth. for lunch。

After lunch, I sometimes play ping-pong with my friends.

午饭后，我有时和朋友打乒乓球。

词汇sometimes 有时；after lunch 午饭后 语法sometimes 表频率。

In the afternoon, we have two classes and then do some sports.

下午我们上两节课，然后做一些运动。

词汇then 然后 语法一般现在时；do some sports 做运动。

I go home at half past five and do my homework first.

我五点半回家，先做作业。

词汇go home 回家；do my homework 做作业 语法go home 中 home 是副词；first 表示“首先”。

I never watch TV before I finish my homework.

我从不在做完作业前看电视。

词汇never 从不；finish 完成 语法never 表频率；before 引导时间状语从句。

After dinner, I read books or listen to music for half an hour.

晚饭后，我读会儿书或听半小时音乐。

词汇after dinner 晚饭后；listen to music 听音乐 语法or 表选择。

I usually go to bed at half past nine because I need to get up early.

我通常九点半睡觉，因为我需要早起。

词汇because 因为；get up early 早起 语法because 引导原因状语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍作者忙碌的一天。他是第五中学的学生。早上六点总起床，然后刷牙洗脸；六点半和家人吃早饭；七点离家坐公交车上学；八点上课，上午四节课。午饭常在食堂吃米饭和蔬菜，午饭后有时和朋友打乒乓球。下午上两节课、做些运动，五点半回家先做作业；做完作业前从不看电视。晚饭后看会儿书或听半小时音乐，九点半睡觉，因为要早起。

English Summary

The passage describes the writer's busy day. He is a student at No. 5 Middle School. He always gets up at six, then brushes his teeth and washes his face; at half past six he usually has breakfast with his family; at seven he leaves home and takes the bus to school; classes start at eight and they have four lessons in the morning. At lunch he often eats rice and vegetables in the school dining hall, and after lunch he sometimes plays ping-pong with friends. In the afternoon they have two more classes and do some sports; he goes home at half past five and does his homework first, and he never watches TV before finishing it. After dinner he reads or listens to music for half an hour and goes to bed at half past nine because he needs to get up early.

语法点 Grammar

I have a busy day.

一般现在时表日常状态。

I always get up at six o'clock in the morning.

always 位于实义动词前；at + 具体时刻。

At half past six, I usually have breakfast with my family.

时间状语置于句首；usually 表频率。

I leave home at seven o'clock and take the bus to school.

一般现在时表习惯；and 连接并列谓语。

Classes start at eight o'clock.

start 第三人称复数不加 -s；at + 具体时刻。

We have four lessons in the morning.

I often eat rice and vegetables for lunch.

often 表频率；have sth. for lunch。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

At half past six, I usually have breakfast with my family.

六点半，我通常和家人一起吃早饭。

时间状语置于句首强调时间；half past six 表示六点半；with 表伴随。

I never watch TV before I finish my homework.

我从不在做完作业前看电视。

never 表否定频率；before 引导时间状语从句，从句用一般现在时表将来。

I usually go to bed at half past nine because I need to get up early.

我通常九点半睡觉，因为我需要早起。

because 引导原因状语从句；need to do sth. 表示“需要做某事”。

Section B 3a · My Diary for the Day

Section B 3a 主题：日常作息 · 学生日记

Monday, November 11th

11 月 11 日，星期一

词汇date 日期写法 语法日记常以日期开头。

It was a busy but happy day.

这是忙碌却快乐的一天。

词汇busy 忙的；happy 快乐的 语法but 连接两个相对的形容词。

I got up at six thirty and had breakfast at seven o'clock.

我六点半起床，七点吃早饭。

词汇get up 起床；have breakfast 吃早饭 语法一般过去时：got、had。

My mother made some noodles and eggs for me.

妈妈给我做了面条和鸡蛋。

词汇make 做；noodle 面条 语法make sth. for sb.；过去式 made。

At eight o'clock, our first class started.

八点，我们的第一节课开始了。

词汇start 开始 语法一般过去时：started。

We had an English class and a maths class in the morning.

上午我们上了一节英语课和一节数学课。

词汇in the morning 在上午 语法an + 元音音素开头的词；had 是 have 的过去式。

In the afternoon, I played basketball with my friends for an hour.

下午我和朋友们打了一小时篮球。

词汇play basketball 打篮球；for an hour 一小时 语法一般过去时：played；for + 一段时间。

Then I went to the library and read an interesting book.

然后我去图书馆读了一本有趣的书。

词汇library 图书馆；interesting 有趣的 语法go → went，read 的过去式拼写不变。

I went home at half past five and did my homework.

我五点半回家，做了作业。

词汇go home 回家；do homework 做作业 语法一般过去时：went、did。

After dinner, my family and I watched TV together.

晚饭后，我和家人一起看电视。

词汇after dinner 晚饭后；together 一起 语法一般过去时：watched。

I went to bed at half past nine because I was tired.

我九点半睡觉，因为我很累。

词汇because 因为；tired 疲倦的 语法because 引导原因状语从句；was 为 am/is 的过去式。

I think I will do better tomorrow.

我想我明天会做得更好。

词汇tomorrow 明天；do better 做得更好 语法will + 动词原形表将来。

I am an early bird, so I always feel full of energy.

我是个早起的人，所以我总是精力充沛。

词汇early bird 早起的人；full of energy 精力充沛 语法so 表结果；always 表频率。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇日记，记录了一天里的一些事情，开头是日期。作者说这一天忙碌却快乐：六点半起床、七点吃早饭，妈妈做了面条和鸡蛋；八点第一节课开始，上午上了英语课和数学课；下午和朋友打了一小时篮球，然后去图书馆读了一本有趣的书；五点半回家做作业，晚饭后和家人一起看电视；九点半因为累了就去睡觉。最后作者说明天要做得更好，并说自己是个早起的人，总是精力充沛。

English Summary

This is a diary about a day in the writer's life. It was a busy but happy day: he got up at six thirty, had breakfast at seven o'clock and his mother made noodles and eggs for him; the first class started at eight, and in the morning they had an English class and a maths class. In the afternoon he played basketball with his friends for an hour and then went to the library to read an interesting book. He went home at half past five and did his homework; after dinner he watched TV with his family, and he went to bed at half past nine because he was tired. At the end he says he will do better tomorrow and that he is an early bird full of energy.

语法点 Grammar

I got up at six thirty and had breakfast at seven o'clock.

一般过去时：get→got，have→had。

My mother made some noodles and eggs for me.

make sth. for sb.；过去式 made。

At eight o'clock, our first class started.

一般过去时：started；时间状语置于句首。

We had an English class and a maths class.

an + 元音音素开头的词；had 为 have 的过去式。

I played basketball with my friends for an hour.

一般过去时：played；for + 一段时间。

Then I went to the library and read an interesting book.

go→went；read 的过去式拼写不变。

I went home at half past five and did my homework.

go→went，do→did；一般过去时并列谓语。

After dinner, my family and I watched TV together.

一般过去时：watched。

I went to bed at half past nine because I was tired.

because 引导原因状语从句；was 为 am/is 的过去式。

I think I will do better tomorrow.

宾语从句；will + 动词原形表将来。

I am an early bird, so I always feel full of energy.

so 表结果；always 表频率。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

My mother made some noodles and eggs for me.

妈妈给我做了面条和鸡蛋。

make sth. for sb. 结构；make 的过去式为 made，some 修饰复数名词。

I went to bed at half past nine because I was tired.

我九点半睡觉，因为我很累。

because 引导原因状语从句；主句与从句都用一般过去时，was 为 am/is 的过去式。

I am an early bird, so I always feel full of energy.

我是个早起的人，所以我总是精力充沛。

so 表结果；always 位于实义动词 feel 前；full of energy 是固定搭配。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What time does Anna usually get up?

  1. At six o'clock.
  2. At seven o'clock.
  3. At half past six.
  4. At half past seven.
答案与解析

C 原文：I usually get up at half past six in the morning.

2. How does Anna usually go to school?

  1. She takes the bus.
  2. She rides a bike.
  3. She walks.
  4. Her father drives her.
答案与解析

C 原文：I usually walk, but sometimes I take the bus.（另有 sometimes 坐公交，但通常方式是 walk）

3. What does Anna always do after school?

  1. She reads books.
  2. She plays basketball for an hour.
  3. She watches TV.
  4. She does her homework.
答案与解析

B 原文：Yes, I always play basketball for an hour after school.

4. In Section B 1b, what time does the writer leave home?

  1. At six o'clock.
  2. At half past six.
  3. At seven o'clock.
  4. At eight o'clock.
答案与解析

C 原文：I leave home at seven o'clock and take the bus to school.

5. What does the writer of Section B 1b do after dinner?

  1. He plays ping-pong.
  2. He does sports.
  3. He watches TV.
  4. He reads books or listens to music.
答案与解析

D 原文：After dinner, I read books or listen to music for half an hour.

6. Why does the writer of Section B 1b go to bed at half past nine?

  1. Because he needs to get up early.
  2. Because he is tired after sports.
  3. Because he has homework.
  4. Because his family goes to bed.
答案与解析

A 原文：I usually go to bed at half past nine because I need to get up early.

7. What did the writer do in the afternoon in Section B 3a?

  1. He went home at half past five and did his homework.
  2. He played basketball and read a book in the library.
  3. He had an English and a maths class.
  4. He watched TV with his family.
答案与解析

B 原文：In the afternoon, I played basketball with my friends for an hour. Then I went to the library and read an interesting book.

8. What does the writer of Section B 3a say about himself?

  1. He is always late for class.
  2. He goes to bed after eleven.
  3. He never does his homework.
  4. He is an early bird and full of energy.
答案与解析

D 原文：I am an early bird, so I always feel full of energy.
