Section A 3a · What Club Do You Want to Join?

Section A 3a 主题：选择社团 · 对话

Alice: Lin Tao, there are so many clubs this year. Which one do you want to join?

林涛，今年社团可真多。你想加入哪一个？

词汇club 社团；join 加入；which one 哪一个 语法Which one do you want to join? 用 want to do 询问意愿。

Lin Tao: I want to join the chess club. I can play chess well.

我想加入象棋社。我下棋下得好。

词汇chess club 象棋社；play chess 下象棋 语法want to join + 社团；can + 动词原形表能力。

Alice: Really? I can't play chess at all. It looks hard.

真的吗？我一点也不会下棋。看起来很难。

词汇not...at all 一点也不；hard 难的 语法can't 表示“不能、不会”；look + adj.。

Lin Tao: It isn't hard if you enjoy thinking. Do you like games?

如果你喜欢思考，它并不难。你喜欢游戏吗？

词汇enjoy thinking 喜欢思考；like games 喜欢游戏 语法enjoy + 动名词；if 引导条件状语从句。

Alice: Yes, I do. But I love singing and dancing more.

是的，我喜欢。不过我更喜欢唱歌跳舞。

词汇singing 唱歌；dancing 跳舞 语法love + 动名词；more 是 much 的比较级。

Lin Tao: Then the music club is good for you.

那音乐社适合你。

词汇music club 音乐社；be good for 对……有好处 语法be good for sb. 表示“对某人合适/有益”。

Alice: Can you sing in the music club?

在音乐社能唱歌吗？

词汇sing 唱歌 语法Can you...? 询问能力或可能性。

Lin Tao: Of course you can. They also ask members to play the guitar.

当然可以。他们还要求成员弹吉他。

词汇of course 当然；member 成员；play the guitar 弹吉他 语法ask sb. to do sth. 要求某人做某事；can + 动词原形。

Alice: That sounds great, but I can't play any instrument.

听起来很棒，但我不会任何乐器。

词汇sound great 听起来很棒；instrument 乐器 语法any 用于否定句；sound + adj.。

Lin Tao: Don't worry. They teach you how to play it.

别担心。他们会教你怎样弹。

词汇don't worry 别担心；teach sb. how to do sth. 语法祈使句否定式；how to play 是“疑问词 + 不定式”。

Alice: That's cool. I enjoy learning new things.

太棒了。我喜欢学新东西。

词汇enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事；new things 新事物 语法enjoy + 动名词。

Lin Tao: What about the art club? You can draw, right?

那美术社呢？你会画画，对吧？

词汇art club 美术社；right 对吧 语法What about...? 提出另一个选项；附加疑问 right。

Alice: Yes, I can. Maybe I should join two clubs!

是的，我会。也许我应该加入两个社团！

词汇maybe 也许；should 应该 语法should + 动词原形表示建议。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，爱丽丝和林涛在社团招新现场选社团。林涛想加入象棋社，因为他棋下得好；爱丽丝说自己一点也不会，看起来很难，林涛说只要喜欢思考就不难。爱丽丝更喜欢唱歌跳舞，林涛建议她去音乐社，还说音乐社能唱歌，也会要求成员弹吉他；爱丽丝担心自己不会乐器，林涛说别担心，他们会教。爱丽丝喜欢学新东西，林涛又问起美术社，爱丽丝说自己会画画，最后说也许该加入两个社团。

English Summary

In this conversation Alice and Lin Tao choose clubs at the club fair. Lin Tao wants to join the chess club because he can play chess well. Alice says she can't play chess at all and it looks hard, but Lin Tao tells her it isn't hard if she enjoys thinking. Alice loves singing and dancing more, so Lin Tao suggests the music club, which also asks members to play the guitar. Alice worries that she can't play any instrument, and Lin Tao tells her not to worry because the club will teach her. Since Alice enjoys learning new things, Lin Tao mentions the art club too; she can draw, and in the end she says maybe she should join two clubs.

语法点 Grammar

Which one do you want to join?

Which one + do you want to do? 询问选择；want to do sth.。

I want to join the chess club.

want to + 动词原形；社团名前加 the。

I can play chess well.

can + 动词原形表能力；well 作状语放句末。

I can't play chess at all.

can't 表示“不会”；not...at all 表示“一点也不”。

It isn't hard if you enjoy thinking.

if 引导条件状语从句；enjoy + 动名词。

I love singing and dancing more.

love + 动名词；more 是比较级，表比较。

The music club is good for you.

be good for sb. 表示“对某人合适”。

Can you sing in the music club? Of course you can.

Can you...? 询问能力；简略回答 can 后省略动词。

They also ask members to play the guitar.

ask sb. to do sth.；play the + 乐器。

That sounds great, but I can't play any instrument.

sound + 形容词；否定句中用 any；but 表转折。

You can draw, right?

附加的 right 表示“对吧”，确认信息。

Maybe I should join two clubs!

should + 动词原形表示建议。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I can't play chess at all. It looks hard.

我一点也不会下棋。看起来很难。

not...at all 表示“一点也不”；look 在此为系动词，后接形容词 hard。

That sounds great, but I can't play any instrument.

听起来很棒，但我不会任何乐器。

sound 是系动词，后接形容词；否定句中表示“任何”用 any；play + the + 乐器。

They teach you how to play it.

他们会教你怎样弹。

teach sb. how to do sth. 是“疑问词 + 不定式”作宾语，it 指 the guitar。

读前

  • 回忆社团名称：chess / music / art / English club。

读中

  • 找出林涛和爱丽丝各自想加入的社团及原因。
  • 圈出所有 can / can't 的句子，标出它表示的能力。

读后

  • 两人一组，用 What club do you want to join? 问答。
  • 用三句话说明自己想加入哪个社团、为什么、自己会什么。

Section B 1b · Clubs in Our School

Section B 1b 主题：校园社团介绍 · 说明文

Our school has many clubs, and every student can find one they love.

我们学校有很多社团，每个学生都能找到自己喜欢的。

词汇many clubs 很多社团；every student 每个学生 语法every + 单数名词作主语用单数谓语；they love 是省略 that 的定语从句。

The art club meets on Monday afternoons.

美术社每周一下午活动。

词汇art club 美术社；meet 聚会 语法on + 星期 + 时间 表示活动时间；meets 为第三人称单数。

Members can draw, paint and make posters, and the teacher teaches them new skills.

成员们可以画画、涂色、做海报，老师教他们新技巧。

词汇paint 涂色；poster 海报；skill 技巧 语法can + 动词原形；teach sb. sth.。

The music club meets on Wednesday and Friday.

音乐社每周三和周五活动。

词汇music club 音乐社；Friday 星期五 语法on + 星期复数并列用 and。

Students there love singing and playing the piano, and they usually give a show at the end of the term.

那里的学生喜欢唱歌、弹钢琴，通常学期末会演出一场。

词汇sing 唱歌；give a show 演出；at the end of 在……结束时 语法love + 动名词；usually 位于实义动词前。

The sports club is very popular.

体育社非常受欢迎。

词汇sports club 体育社；popular 受欢迎的 语法be + 形容词作表语。

In the sports club, you can play basketball or run on the playground.

在体育社，你可以打篮球，也可以在操场上跑步。

词汇basketball 篮球；playground 操场 语法can + 动词原形；or 表示选择。

You can also join the English club and practise speaking English.

你也可以加入英语社，练习说英语。

词汇English club 英语社；practise doing sth. 练习做某事 语法practise + 动名词；also 表示“也”。

Some members enjoy reading English stories, and others like acting out short plays.

有些成员喜欢读英语故事，另一些喜欢表演短剧。

词汇act out 表演；play 短剧 语法some... others... 表示“一些……另一些……”；enjoy/like + 动名词。

Which club do you want to join?

你想加入哪个社团？

词汇which club 哪个社团 语法What/Which club do you want to join? 询问意愿。

If you can't decide, you can ask your teachers for help.

如果你无法决定，可以向老师求助。

词汇decide 决定；ask sb. for help 向某人求助 语法if 引导条件状语从句；can't + 动词原形。

Clubs make our school life colourful and fun.

社团让我们的学校生活多彩又有趣。

词汇colourful 多彩的；fun 有趣的 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍学校里的各种社团。学校有很多社团，每个学生都能找到自己喜欢的。美术社周一下午活动，成员可以画画、涂色、做海报，老师还会教新技巧；音乐社周三和周五活动，学生喜欢唱歌、弹钢琴，通常学期末演出一场。体育社非常受欢迎，可以打篮球或在操场上跑步；你也可以加入英语社练习说英语，有些人喜欢读英语故事，另一些人喜欢表演短剧。最后短文问你想加入哪个社团，如果无法决定可以向老师求助——社团让学校生活多彩又有趣。

English Summary

This passage introduces the clubs in our school. The school has many clubs, so every student can find one they love. The art club meets on Monday afternoons, where members can draw, paint and make posters and the teacher teaches them new skills. The music club meets on Wednesday and Friday; students love singing and playing the piano and usually give a show at the end of the term. The sports club is very popular—you can play basketball or run on the playground. You can also join the English club to practise speaking English: some members enjoy reading English stories and others like acting out short plays. Finally the passage asks which club you want to join and says you can ask your teachers for help if you can't decide, because clubs make school life colourful and fun.

语法点 Grammar

Our school has many clubs.

have 表示拥有；many + 复数名词。

every student can find one they love

every + 单数名词作主语用单数谓语；one 后省略 that 的定语从句。

The art club meets on Monday afternoons.

主语第三人称单数用 meets；on + 星期。

Members can draw, paint and make posters.

can + 动词原形；并列动作用逗号与 and 连接。

the teacher teaches them new skills

teaches 为第三人称单数；teach sb. sth. 双宾语。

Students there love singing and playing the piano.

love + 动名词；play the + 乐器。

they usually give a show at the end of the term

usually 位于实义动词前；at the end of 表示时间。

The sports club is very popular.

be + 形容词作表语。

you can play basketball or run on the playground

can + 动词原形；or 表示选择。

Some members enjoy reading English stories, and others like acting out short plays.

some... others... 并列；enjoy / like + 动名词。

If you can't decide, you can ask your teachers for help.

if 引导条件状语从句；can't + 动词原形；ask sb. for help。

Clubs make our school life colourful and fun.

make + 宾语 + 形容词。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Every student can find one they love.

每个学生都能找到自己喜欢的。

every + 单数名词作主语用单数谓语；one they love 是省略关系代词 that 的定语从句。

Some members enjoy reading English stories, and others like acting out short plays.

有些成员喜欢读英语故事，另一些喜欢表演短剧。

some ... others ... 表示“一些……另一些……”；enjoy/like 后接动名词。

If you can't decide, you can ask your teachers for help.

如果你无法决定，可以向老师求助。

if 引导条件状语从句，主句用 can + 动词原形；ask sb. for help 表示“向某人求助”。

Section B 3a · A Poster for the Fun English Club

Section B 3a 主题：社团招新 · 海报兼习作

Come and join the Fun English Club!

快来加入趣味英语社吧！

词汇come and join 快来加入 语法祈使句用于海报号召。

We meet every Thursday afternoon in Room 201, from 4:00 to 5:00.

我们每周四下午在 201 教室活动，时间是四点至五点。

词汇every Thursday 每周四；from...to... 从……到…… 语法every + 单数星期 表示“每周几”。

You can sing English songs, play English games and watch English films.

你可以唱英语歌、玩英语游戏、看英语电影。

词汇English games 英语游戏；film 电影 语法can + 动词原形；并列动作用逗号与 and 连接。

Our members love speaking English with each other, so no one feels afraid.

我们的成员喜欢彼此说英语，所以没有人会害怕。

词汇speak English 说英语；each other 彼此；afraid 害怕的 语法love + 动名词；so 表结果。

You don't need to be good at English.

你不需要英语特别好。

词汇be good at 擅长；need to do sth. 需要做某事 语法don't need to 表示“不必”。

We can teach you new words and help you with your speaking.

我们可以教你新单词，帮助你练习口语。

词汇new words 新单词；help sb. with sth. 在某方面帮助某人 语法can + 动词原形；help sb. with sth.。

Our teacher, Miss Li, is friendly and always kind to us.

我们的老师李老师很友好，对我们总是很和善。

词汇friendly 友好的；kind 和善的 语法主语为第三人称单数用 is；always 位于 be 动词后。

Last term, our club sang a song at the school party, and everyone clapped.

上个学期，我们社团在学校晚会上唱了一首歌，大家都鼓掌。

词汇last term 上学期；party 晚会；clap 鼓掌 语法last term 表示过去时间；sang / clapped 为一般过去时。

I enjoy acting in short plays, and I want to be a good speaker one day.

我喜欢演短剧，希望将来成为一名优秀的演讲者。

词汇enjoy acting 喜欢表演；speaker 演讲者；one day 将来某天 语法enjoy + 动名词；want to be + 名词。

What club do you want to join?

你想加入什么社团？

词汇what club 什么社团 语法What club do you want to join? 询问想加入的社团。

Come to Room 201 on Thursday and become one of us!

周四来 201 教室，成为我们的一员吧！

词汇on Thursday 周四；become 成为 语法祈使句；and 连接两个动作。

See you there!

到时候见！

词汇see you there 到时见 语法海报常见的结束语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一张为“趣味英语社”招新的海报。海报号召大家加入，说明社团每周四下午四点到五点在 201 教室活动；成员可以唱英语歌、玩英语游戏、看英语电影。大家喜欢彼此说英语，所以没人会害怕，也不必英语特别好——社团会教新单词、帮助练口语；指导老师李老师很友好、很和善。上个学期社团在学校晚会上唱了歌，大家鼓掌。作者说自己喜欢演短剧，希望将来成为优秀的演讲者，最后问你想加入什么社团，并邀请你周四来 201 教室。

English Summary

This is a poster inviting students to join the Fun English Club. The club meets every Thursday afternoon in Room 201 from 4:00 to 5:00, where members can sing English songs, play English games and watch English films. Members love speaking English with each other, so no one feels afraid, and you don't need to be good at English—the club will teach you new words and help you with your speaking. The teacher, Miss Li, is friendly and always kind. Last term the club sang a song at the school party and everyone clapped. The writer enjoys acting in short plays and wants to be a good speaker one day; the poster ends by asking what club you want to join and inviting you to Room 201 on Thursday.

语法点 Grammar

Come and join the Fun English Club!

祈使句 + and，用于海报号召。

We meet every Thursday afternoon in Room 201.

一般现在时表固定活动；every + 单数星期。

from 4:00 to 5:00

from... to... 表示时间或地点的范围。

You can sing English songs, play English games and watch English films.

can + 动词原形；三项并列用逗号与 and。

Our members love speaking English with each other.

love + 动名词；each other 作宾语的同位成分。

so no one feels afraid

so 表结果；no one 作主语用单数谓语 feels。

You don't need to be good at English.

don't need to + 动词原形表示“不必”；be good at + 名词。

We can teach you new words and help you with your speaking.

can + 动词原形；teach sb. sth.；help sb. with sth.。

Our teacher, Miss Li, is friendly and always kind to us.

同位语 Miss Li 补充说明；always 位于 be 动词后。

Last term, our club sang a song at the school party.

last term 表示过去时间，谓语用一般过去时 sang。

I enjoy acting in short plays.

enjoy + 动名词。

What club do you want to join?

What club + do you want to join? 询问想加入的社团。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Our members love speaking English with each other, so no one feels afraid.

我们的成员喜欢彼此说英语，所以没有人会害怕。

love + 动名词；so 引导结果的并列句；no one 作主语视为单数。

We can teach you new words and help you with your speaking.

我们可以教你新单词，帮助你练习口语。

teach sb. sth. 双宾语；help sb. with sth. 表示“在某方面帮助某人”。

Last term, our club sang a song at the school party, and everyone clapped.

上个学期，我们社团在学校晚会上唱了一首歌，大家都鼓掌。

last term 表示过去时间，sang / clapped 用一般过去时。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题和版式，判断这是一张社团招新海报。
  • 注意海报的要素：活动时间、地点、活动内容、联系方式。

读中

  • 找出社团活动的时间、地点和主要内容。
  • 圈出 can / love + doing 的句子，以及表示“不必担心”的表达。

读后

  • 为自己喜欢的社团写一张招新海报。
  • 用 4-5 句话口头介绍一个社团。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. Which club does Lin Tao want to join?

  1. The art club.
  2. The music club.
  3. The chess club.
  4. The English club.
答案与解析

C 原文：I want to join the chess club.

2. Why does Alice think she can't join the chess club?

  1. She has no time.
  2. She can't play chess at all.
  3. She doesn't like games.
  4. She can't think.
答案与解析

B 原文：I can't play chess at all. It looks hard.

3. What does Lin Tao suggest for Alice?

  1. The music club.
  2. The chess club.
  3. The sports club.
  4. The English club.
答案与解析

A 原文：Then the music club is good for you.

4. When does the art club meet?

  1. On Monday afternoons.
  2. On Wednesday and Friday.
  3. Every Thursday afternoon.
  4. On Sunday morning.
答案与解析

A 原文：The art club meets on Monday afternoons.

5. What do the music club members usually do at the end of the term?

  1. They make posters.
  2. They run on the playground.
  3. They give a show.
  4. They act out short plays.
答案与解析

C 原文：they usually give a show at the end of the term

6. What can members do in the English club according to Section B 1b?

  1. Play the piano.
  2. Play basketball.
  3. Draw and paint.
  4. Practise speaking English.
答案与解析

D 原文：You can also join the English club and practise speaking English.

7. Where and when does the Fun English Club meet?

  1. In Room 102 on Friday.
  2. In Room 201 on Thursday afternoon.
  3. On the playground on Monday.
  4. In Room 201 on Wednesday.
答案与解析

B 原文：We meet every Thursday afternoon in Room 201, from 4:00 to 5:00.

8. What does the writer of Section B 3a want to be one day?

  1. A good singer.
  2. A good speaker.
  3. A chess player.
  4. An art teacher.
答案与解析

B 原文：I enjoy acting in short plays, and I want to be a good speaker one day.
