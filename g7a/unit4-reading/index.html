Section A 3a · What's Your Favourite Subject?

Section A 3a 主题：学科喜好 · 同学对话

Lily: Hi, Tom. What's your favourite subject?

嗨，汤姆。你最喜欢哪个学科？

词汇favourite 最喜欢的；subject 学科 语法What's your favourite subject? 询问最喜欢的学科。

Tom: My favourite subject is science. I love it very much.

我最喜欢的学科是科学。我非常喜欢它。

词汇science 科学；love 热爱 语法love sth. very much 表示“非常喜欢”。

Lily: Why do you like it?

你为什么喜欢它？

词汇why 为什么 语法Why do you like...? 询问原因。

Tom: Because the lessons are interesting, and I enjoy doing experiments.

因为这些课很有意思，而且我喜欢做实验。

词汇because 因为；interesting 有趣的；experiment 实验 语法because 引导原因状语从句；enjoy + 动名词。

Lily: That sounds fun. Is science easy for you?

听起来很有意思。科学对你来说容易吗？

词汇easy 容易的；for sb. 对某人而言 语法be + 形容词 + for sb. 表示“对某人来说……”。

Tom: Not really. I am good at it, but I have to work hard.

也不容易。我擅长这门课，但我必须努力学习。

词汇work hard 努力学习；but 但是 语法be good at sth.；have to + 动词原形。

Tom: What about you, Lily?

你呢，莉莉？

词汇what about 那么……呢 语法What about you? 反问对方。

Lily: I like art best. I am good at drawing pictures.

我最喜欢美术。我擅长画画。

词汇art 美术；draw pictures 画画 语法like...best 表示“最喜欢”。

Tom: Really? Do you enjoy painting?

真的吗？你喜欢画画吗？

词汇painting 绘画 语法enjoy + 动名词；Do you...? 一般疑问句。

Lily: Yes, I do. I want to be an artist one day.

是的，我喜欢。我将来想成为一名画家。

词汇artist 艺术家；one day 将来某一天 语法want to be + 职业；one day 表示将来。

Tom: That's great. We both work hard on what we like.

太棒了。我们都在自己喜欢的事情上努力。

词汇work hard on 在……上努力 语法work hard on + 名词。

Lily: Yes. Let's help each other with our subjects.

是的。我们在学科上互相帮助吧。

词汇help each other 互相帮助 语法help sb. with sth.；Let's + 动词原形。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话是莉莉和汤姆在聊最喜欢的学科。汤姆最喜欢科学，因为科学课很有意思，而且他喜欢做实验。莉莉问他科学难不难，汤姆说也不容易，自己虽然擅长但还是得努力学习。随后莉莉说自己最喜欢美术，擅长画画，想将来当一名画家。最后两人说要在自己喜欢的事情上努力，并在学科上互相帮助。

English Summary

The conversation is about Lily's and Tom's favourite subjects. Tom's favourite subject is science because the lessons are interesting and he enjoys doing experiments. When Lily asks whether science is easy for him, Tom says not really—he is good at it but still has to work hard. Lily then says she likes art best, is good at drawing pictures and wants to be an artist one day. In the end they agree to work hard on what they like and to help each other with their subjects.

语法点 Grammar

What's your favourite subject?

Wh- 疑问句：What's + 形容词性物主代词 + favourite + 名词。

My favourite subject is science.

主系表结构，favourite 作定语。

I love it very much.

love sth. very much 表示程度。

Why do you like it?

Why do you...? 询问原因。

Because the lessons are interesting.

because 引导原因状语从句，回答 why。

I enjoy doing experiments.

enjoy + 动名词。

Is science easy for you?

be + 形容词 + for sb. 的疑问句。

I am good at it, but I have to work hard.

be good at + 名词/代词；have to + 动词原形。

I like art best.

like...best 表示“最喜欢”。

Do you enjoy painting?

enjoy + 动名词的一般疑问句。

I want to be an artist one day.

want to be + 职业；an + 元音音素开头的词。

Let's help each other with our subjects.

Let's + 动词原形；help sb. with sth.。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Because the lessons are interesting, and I enjoy doing experiments.

因为这些课很有意思，而且我喜欢做实验。

because 引导原因状语从句；enjoy 后接动名词 doing。

Not really. I am good at it, but I have to work hard.

也不容易。我擅长这门课，但我必须努力学习。

Not really 是委婉否定；be good at 后接名词或代词。

I want to be an artist one day.

我将来想成为一名画家。

want to be + 职业；artist 以元音音素开头，用 an；one day 表将来。

  • 回忆学科名称：science / art / PE / history 等。

  • 圈出 enjoy + doing 与 because 引出的句子。

读后

  • 用三句话说明自己最喜欢哪个学科及原因。

Section B 1b · My Favourite Subject

Section B 1b 主题：最爱的学科 · 说明短文

Every student has a favourite subject, and so do I.

每个学生都有最喜欢的学科，我也是。

词汇every 每个；so do I 我也一样 语法so + 助动词 + 主语，表示“……也是一样”。

My favourite subject is history.

我最喜欢的学科是历史。

词汇history 历史 语法主系表结构；favourite 作定语。

I like it because I enjoy reading stories about the past.

我喜欢它，因为我喜欢读过去的故事。

词汇enjoy + 动名词；the past 过去 语法because 引导原因状语从句；enjoy + 动名词。

In class, our teacher often tells us interesting stories.

上课时，老师常给我们讲有趣的故事。

词汇tell sb. sth. 给某人讲某事；often 经常 语法often 位于实义动词前；tell sb. sth.。

We also watch short videos about old buildings and famous people.

我们还会看关于古老建筑和名人的短视频。

词汇video 视频；famous 著名的 语法also 表示“也”；about 表示“关于”。

Some of my classmates don't like history, but I find it really fun.

有些同学不喜欢历史，但我觉得它真的很有趣。

词汇classmate 同学；find + 宾语 + 形容词 语法find it + 形容词，表示“觉得它……”。

My friend Li Ming likes maths best.

我的朋友李明最喜欢数学。

词汇maths 数学 语法like...best 表示“最喜欢”。

He is good at numbers and enjoys solving problems.

他擅长数字，喜欢解题。

词汇be good at 擅长；solve problems 解题 语法be good at sth.；enjoy + 动名词。

He wants to be an engineer when he grows up.

他长大后想成为一名工程师。

词汇engineer 工程师；grow up 长大 语法want to be；when 引导时间状语从句。

Different students like different subjects.

不同的学生喜欢不同的学科。

词汇different 不同的 语法different 修饰复数名词。

But we all agree that every subject is useful.

但我们都认为每个学科都有用。

词汇agree 同意；useful 有用的 语法that 引导宾语从句。

When we study hard, we can learn more and choose our future jobs.

当我们努力学习时，就能学到更多，并选择自己未来的职业。

词汇learn 学习；choose 选择；future 未来的 语法when 引导时间状语从句；情态动词 can + 动词原形。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍自己的学科喜好。和每个学生一样，“我”也有最喜欢的学科——历史。喜欢它是因为“我”爱读关于过去的故事；课上老师常讲有趣的故事，大家还会看关于古老建筑和名人的短视频。有些同学不喜欢历史，但“我”觉得它很有趣。“我”的朋友李明最喜欢数学，他擅长数字、喜欢解题，长大想当工程师。短文最后说：不同的学生喜欢不同的学科，但大家都认为每个学科都有用；努力学习才能学到更多、选择未来的职业。

English Summary

The passage is about favourite subjects. The writer, like every student, has a favourite subject—history—because he enjoys reading stories about the past; in class the teacher often tells interesting stories and they watch short videos about old buildings and famous people. Some classmates don't like history, but he finds it really fun. His friend Li Ming likes maths best: he is good at numbers, enjoys solving problems and wants to be an engineer when he grows up. The passage ends by saying that different students like different subjects, but everyone agrees that every subject is useful, and studying hard helps us learn more and choose our future jobs.

主系表结构。

Section B 3a · A Letter About Favourite Subjects

Section B 3a 主题：学科喜好 · 书信交流

Dear Mike,

亲爱的迈克，

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

How are you? I am happy to tell you about my school life.

你好吗？很高兴跟你讲讲我的学校生活。

词汇school life 学校生活 语法be happy to do sth.。

Our class has many subjects: maths, science, history, geography, art, music and PE.

我们班有很多学科：数学、科学、历史、地理、美术、音乐和体育。

词汇geography 地理；PE 体育 语法列举多项时用逗号隔开，最后用 and。

My favourite subject is geography.

我最喜欢的学科是地理。

词汇geography 地理 语法主系表结构。

I like it because I enjoy learning about rivers, mountains and cities.

我喜欢它，因为我喜欢了解河流、山脉和城市。

词汇enjoy + 动名词；mountain 山脉 语法because 引导原因状语从句；enjoy + 动名词。

I also love looking at maps, and I can find many places on them.

我也喜欢看地图，能在上面找到很多地方。

词汇map 地图；also 也 语法love + 动名词；can + 动词原形。

My friend Zhang Wei likes PE best.

我的朋友张伟最喜欢体育。

词汇PE 体育 语法like...best 表示“最喜欢”。

He is good at running, and he often plays football after school.

他擅长跑步，放学后常踢足球。

词汇be good at 擅长；after school 放学后 语法be good at + 动名词；often 表频率。

He says PE makes him happy and strong.

他说体育让他开心又强壮。

词汇make + 宾语 + 形容词；strong 强壮的 语法make sb. + 形容词。

We have different favourites, but we help each other with our homework.

我们最喜欢的学科不同，但我们会互相帮助做作业。

词汇help sb. with sth. 在某方面帮助某人 语法but 表转折；help sb. with sth.。

Please write back and tell me about your favourite subject.

请回信告诉我你最喜欢的学科。

词汇write back 回信 语法祈使句 + and。

Yours,

你的朋友，

词汇yours 你的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Wang Lin

王林

词汇— 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是王林给迈克写的一封信，介绍学校生活。他先问好，说很高兴介绍自己的学校生活；然后列举班上开设的学科：数学、科学、历史、地理、美术、音乐和体育。他最喜欢地理，因为喜欢了解河流、山脉和城市，也喜欢看地图找地方。接着他介绍朋友张伟最喜欢体育，擅长跑步，放学后常踢足球，说体育让他开心又强壮。最后他说两人喜好不同，但会互相帮忙做作业，并请迈克回信介绍自己最喜欢的学科。

English Summary

This is Wang Lin's letter to Mike about school life. He greets Mike, says he is happy to talk about his school life, and lists the subjects their class has—maths, science, history, geography, art, music and PE. His favourite subject is geography because he enjoys learning about rivers, mountains and cities, and he also loves looking at maps and finding many places on them. Then he introduces his friend Zhang Wei, who likes PE best, is good at running, often plays football after school and says PE makes him happy and strong. At the end Wang Lin says that although their favourites are different, they help each other with homework, and he asks Mike to write back about his own favourite subject.

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

  2. Science.
  3. History.
  4. PE.
答案与解析

B 原文：My favourite subject is science.

2. Why does Tom like science?

  1. Because it is easy for him.
  2. Because he can draw pictures.
  3. Because the lessons are interesting and he enjoys doing experiments.
