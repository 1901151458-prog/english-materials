Section A 2a · Our Favourite Subjects

Section A 2a 主题：最喜欢的科目 · 原因表达

Binbin: What's your next class?

你下一节课是什么？

词汇next 下一个；class 课 语法What's...? 询问“是什么”；next class 表示“下一节课”。

Emma: History. It's my favourite subject.

历史。这是我最喜欢的科目。

词汇history 历史；favourite 最喜欢的；subject 科目 语法It's = It is；my favourite subject 作表语。

Binbin: Why do you like it?

你为什么喜欢它？

词汇why 为什么 语法Why do you...? 用于询问原因。

Emma: It's interesting to learn about the past.

了解过去很有趣。

词汇interesting 有趣的；learn about 了解；past 过去 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；learn about 表示“了解”。

Binbin: What's your favourite subject, Meimei?

美美，你最喜欢的科目是什么？

词汇Meimei 美美（人名） 语法What's your favourite subject? 询问最喜欢的科目。

Meimei: My favourite subject is English. It's useful, and my English teacher is really nice. How about you, Binbin?

我最喜欢的科目是英语。它很有用，而且我的英语老师非常好。你呢，彬彬？

词汇useful 有用的；really 真的 语法and 连接并列分句；How about you? 反问对方的情况。

Binbin: I like all the subjects, but my favourite is maths.

我喜欢所有科目，但我最喜欢的是数学。

词汇all the subjects 所有科目；maths 数学 语法but 连接转折分句；my favourite 后省略 subject。

Meimei: Why?

为什么？

词汇why 为什么 语法省略问句，承接上文继续追问原因。

Binbin: Because I'm good with numbers.

因为我擅长数字。

词汇because 因为；be good with 善于应付…… 语法Because 引导原因；be good with 是固定搭配。

Emma: Oh, it's difficult for me. Can you help me with this subject?

哦，这对我来说很难。你能帮我学这门科目吗？

词汇difficult 困难的；help sb. with sth. 帮助某人做某事 语法It's + 形容词 + for sb. 表示“对某人来说……”；Can you...? 请求帮助。

Binbin: Sure!

当然！

词汇sure 当然 语法口语中表示同意、答应。

Meimei: Hey, look at the time. Let's go to class!

嘿，看看时间。我们去上课吧！

词汇look at 看；time 时间 语法祈使句 Look at...；Let's do sth. 提出建议。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在彬彬、艾玛和美美三人之间。彬彬问艾玛下一节课是什么，艾玛说是历史，也是她最喜欢的科目，因为了解过去很有趣。彬彬又问美美最喜欢的科目，美美说是英语，英语有用而且老师很好，并反问彬彬；彬彬说他喜欢所有科目，但最喜欢数学，因为他擅长数字。艾玛觉得数学对她来说很难，请彬彬帮忙，彬彬答应了。最后美美提醒大家看时间，该去上课了。

English Summary

The conversation takes place between Binbin, Emma and Meimei. Binbin asks Emma what her next class is; she says history, her favourite subject, because it is interesting to learn about the past. Binbin then asks Meimei, who says English is her favourite because it is useful and her teacher is nice; she asks Binbin in return. Binbin likes all the subjects but his favourite is maths because he is good with numbers. Emma finds maths difficult for her and asks Binbin for help, and he agrees. Finally Meimei reminds them of the time and they go to class.

语法点 Grammar

What's your next class?

What's...? 询问“是什么”。

It's my favourite subject.

It's = It is；my favourite subject 作表语。

Why do you like it?

Why do you...? 询问原因。

It's interesting to learn about the past.

It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型。

What's your favourite subject?

询问最喜欢的科目。

My favourite subject is English.

My favourite subject is + 名词，主系表结构。

It's useful, and my English teacher is really nice.

and 连接两个并列分句。

I like all the subjects, but my favourite is maths.

but 连接两个转折分句。

Because I'm good with numbers.

Because 引导原因；be good with 表示“善于应付……”。

it's difficult for me

It's + 形容词 + for sb. 表示“对某人来说……”。

Can you help me with this subject?

Can you...? 请求帮助；help sb. with sth.。

Let's go to class!

Let's do sth. 提出建议。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It's interesting to learn about the past.

了解过去很有趣。

It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to learn about the past；learn about 表示“了解”。

Can you help me with this subject?

你能帮我学这门科目吗？

help sb. with sth. 是固定搭配；Can you...? 用于礼貌请求。

Let's go to class!

我们去上课吧！

Let's do sth. 表示提出建议；go to class 表示“去上课”。

Section B 1b · Message Board: Our Favourite Subjects

Section B 1b 主题：留言板 · 最喜欢的科目（加拿大 / 中国）

I'm a student in Canada.

我是加拿大的一个学生。

词汇Canada 加拿大 语法I'm + 身份；in Canada 表示地点。

I'm very busy this year.

我今年很忙。

词汇busy 忙碌的 语法this year 作时间状语。

I study maths, music, French, history, English, IT, science, and have gym.

我学数学、音乐、法语、历史、英语、信息技术，还上体育课。

词汇study 学习；French 法语；science 科学；gym 体育课 语法并列多个科目用逗号和 and 连接；have gym 表示“上体育课”。

My favourite subject is music because the class is fun and we learn a lot of new songs.

我最喜欢的科目是音乐，因为这门课很有趣，我们学很多新歌。

词汇because 因为；song 歌曲 语法because 引导原因状语从句；a lot of 表示“许多”。

Music always makes me happy.

音乐总是让我快乐。

词汇always 总是；make 使 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词（happy）作宾补，表示“使某人……”。

My music teacher is excellent.

我的音乐老师非常优秀。

词汇excellent 优秀的；极好的 语法形容词 excellent 作表语。

He lets us play all kinds of interesting instruments.

他让我们演奏各种有趣的乐器。

词汇let sb. do sth. 让某人做某事；instrument 乐器 语法let + 宾语 + 动词原形；all kinds of 表示“各种”。

I want to be a singer in the future.

我将来想当一名歌手。

词汇singer 歌手；in the future 将来 语法want to be... 表示“想成为……”；in the future 作时间状语。

How about you?

你呢？

词汇How about...? ……怎么样 语法How about you? 用于反问对方。

What do you study?

你学什么科目？

词汇study 学习 语法What do you...? 询问学习的内容。

What's your favourite class and why?

你最喜欢哪门课？为什么？

词汇class 课程 语法and 连接两个并列的疑问内容。

I'm from China.

我来自中国。

词汇China 中国 语法be from + 国家，表示“来自”。

This term, I have subjects like Chinese, history, maths, English, PE, and IT.

这学期，我有语文、历史、数学、英语、体育和信息技术等科目。

词汇term 学期；PE 体育课 语法like 在此作介词，表示“像……这样的”；并列科目用逗号和 and。

I like all the subjects, but my favourite is maths.

我喜欢所有科目，但我最喜欢的是数学。

词汇all the subjects 所有科目 语法but 连接转折分句。

I like it because I'm good with numbers.

我喜欢它，因为我擅长数字。

词汇be good with 善于应付…… 语法because 引导原因状语从句。

We learn how to work out maths problems in class.

我们在课堂上学习如何解决数学问题。

词汇how to 如何；work out 计算出；problem 难题 语法how to do sth. 作 learn 的宾语；work out 是固定搭配。

It feels like magic and is really fun.

这感觉像魔法一样，而且真的很有趣。

词汇feel like 感觉像；magic 魔法 语法and 连接两个并列谓语 feels / is。

Maths is very useful in our life, and I want to be a scientist in the future.

数学在我们的生活中很有用，我将来想当一名科学家。

词汇useful 有用的；scientist 科学家 语法and 连接两个并列分句；in the future 作时间状语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是留言板上的两条帖子。加拿大的迈克·戴维斯说他今年很忙，学数学、音乐、法语、历史、英语、信息技术等，还上体育课；他最喜欢音乐，因为课堂有趣、能学新歌，音乐让他快乐，音乐老师很优秀，让他们演奏各种乐器，他将来想当歌手。中国的吴彬彬说他这学期有语文、历史、数学、英语、体育和信息技术等科目；他喜欢所有科目，但最喜欢数学，因为他擅长数字，课堂上学习解数学题，感觉像魔法一样有趣，他希望将来当科学家。

English Summary

These are two posts on a message board. Mike Davis from Canada is very busy this year: he studies maths, music, French, history, English, IT and science, and has gym. His favourite subject is music because the class is fun and they learn new songs; music makes him happy, his music teacher is excellent and lets them play all kinds of instruments, and he wants to be a singer in the future. Wu Binbin from China has subjects like Chinese, history, maths, English, PE and IT this term. He likes all the subjects but his favourite is maths because he is good with numbers; they learn how to work out maths problems, which feels like magic and is fun, and he wants to be a scientist in the future.

Reading Plus · Choose a Class

Reading Plus 主题：兴趣课程 · 选择与理由

Learn to sing and dance to fun songs.

学习跟着有趣的歌曲唱歌、跳舞。

词汇sing 唱歌；dance 跳舞 语法learn to do sth. 学习做某事。

Work as a team to put on a big show at the end of the term.

团队合作，在学期末举办一场大型演出。

词汇work as a team 团队合作；put on 举办；show 演出 语法work as a team 表示“团队合作”；at the end of 表示“在……结束时”。

It's a great way to make new friends too!

这也是交新朋友的好方法！

词汇way 方式；make friends 交朋友 语法a great way to do sth. 表示“做某事的好方法”。

Are you good with your hands?

你动手能力强吗？

Come and learn how to make a table, a chair, or maybe a birdhouse.

In this class, you learn to use different tools in a safe way.

在这门课上，你学习安全地使用不同的工具。

词汇tool 工具；safe 安全的 语法in a safe way 表示“以安全的方式”。

Podcasting is an excellent way to develop your speaking and computer skills.

播客是培养口语和电脑技能的极好方式。

Learn how to make short but interesting videos.

学习制作简短而有趣的视频。

You choose the topic!

主题由你来选！

词汇choose 选择；topic 话题 语法主谓宾结构，强调主动权在对方。

Do you want to relax but don't know how?

你想放松却不知道怎么做吗？

词汇relax 放松 语法Do you want to...? 询问意愿；but 表转折。

Then join us for a yoga class.

那就来参加我们的瑜伽课吧。

词汇join 参加；yoga 瑜伽 语法祈使句；join sb. for sth. 表示“和某人一起参加……”。

Learn to stand like a tree, a mountain, and more!

学习像树、山等一样站立！

词汇stand 站立；mountain 山 语法like 作介词，表示“像”；and more 表示“等等”。

Yoga helps you sleep better and build a good body.

瑜伽帮助你睡得更好、塑造好身体。

词汇help 帮助；build 塑造；body 身体 语法help sb. do sth. 帮助某人做某事；and 连接并列动词。

a great way to do sth.；too 放句末表“也”。

Are you good with your hands?

Are you...? 一般疑问句；be good with 表示“善于应付……”。

Come and learn how to make a table, a chair, or maybe a birdhouse.

祈使句；how to do sth. 作 learn 的宾语。

you learn to use different tools in a safe way

in a safe way 表示“以安全的方式”。

Podcasting is an excellent way to develop your speaking and computer skills.

an excellent way to do sth. 结构。

Learn how to make short but interesting videos.

short but interesting 用 but 连接两个形容词。

You choose the topic!

主谓宾结构，强调主动权。

Do you want to relax but don't know how?

Do you want to...? 询问意愿；but 表转折。

Then join us for a yoga class.

祈使句；join sb. for sth.。

Learn to stand like a tree, a mountain, and more!

like 作介词“像”；and more 表示“等等”。

Yoga helps you sleep better and build a good body.

help sb. do sth.；and 连接两个并列动词。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It's a great way to make new friends too!

这也是交新朋友的好方法！

a great way to do sth. 中不定式 to make 作后置定语，修饰 way。

Podcasting is an excellent way to develop your speaking and computer skills.

播客是培养口语和电脑技能的极好方式。

an excellent way to do sth.；develop 后接并列的 skills。

Yoga helps you sleep better and build a good body.

瑜伽帮助你睡得更好、塑造好身体。

help sb. do sth. 中 do 用动词原形；and 连接并列的 sleep 和 build。
