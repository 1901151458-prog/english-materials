Section A 3a · Is This Your Bottle?

Section A 3a 主题：整理物品与询问归属 · 对话

Tom: Excuse me, Anna. Is this your bottle?

打扰一下，安娜。这是你的瓶子吗？

词汇bottle 瓶子；excuse me 打扰一下 语法Is this your...? 一般疑问句确认物品归属。

Anna: Yes, it is. Thanks, Tom!

是的，是我的。谢谢，汤姆！

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法肯定回答 Yes, it is.，it 指代 the bottle。

Tom: You're welcome. What's this on your desk?

不用谢。你桌上的这个是什么？

词汇You're welcome. 不用谢；desk 课桌 语法You're welcome. 是 Thank you. 的标准答语。

Anna: It's an eraser. It's mine, not yours.

是一块橡皮。它是我的，不是你的。

词汇eraser 橡皮；mine 我的 语法mine 是名词性物主代词，相当于 my eraser。

Tom: Is that your key under the desk?

桌子下面的那把钥匙是你的吗？

词汇key 钥匙；under 在……下面 语法Is that...? 确认较远的物品。

Anna: No, it isn't. That key is Tom's.

不，不是。那把钥匙是汤姆的。

词汇key 钥匙 语法否定回答 No, it isn't.；Tom's 是名词所有格。

Tom: Oh, you're right. It's my key.

哦，你说得对。这是我的钥匙。

词汇right 正确的 语法It's my key. 描述归属。

Anna: Whose key is this? And whose bottle is that?

这是谁的钥匙？那是谁的瓶子？

词汇whose 谁的 语法Whose + 名词 + is this/that? 询问物主。

Tom: The key is mine, and the bottle is Anna's.

钥匙是我的，瓶子是安娜的。

词汇bottle 瓶子 语法名词所有格 Anna's 表示“安娜的”。

Anna: Your things are everywhere! You need to keep them tidy.

你的东西到处都是！你需要把它们收拾整齐。

词汇thing 东西；tidy 整齐的；need 需要 语法need to do sth.；keep + 宾语 + 形容词（tidy）。

Tom: You're right. Let's put the bottle in the box.

你说得对。我们把这个瓶子放进盒子里吧。

词汇put...in 把……放进；box 盒子 语法祈使句提建议；Let's do sth.。

Anna: Good. And put your eraser in your pencil box, too.

好。也把橡皮放进你的文具盒里。

词汇eraser 橡皮；pencil box 文具盒 语法祈使句 Put it in your...，表示整理物品。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是汤姆和安娜在教室里整理物品的对话。汤姆问桌上的水瓶是不是安娜的，安娜说是，并向他道谢，汤姆回答“不用谢”；汤姆又问桌上的是什么，安娜说是自己的橡皮，不是汤姆的。汤姆问桌子下面的钥匙是不是安娜的，安娜说那是汤姆的钥匙，汤姆才想起是自己的。安娜由此问他钥匙和瓶子分别是谁的，并提醒他东西到处乱放、需要收拾整齐，两人一起把瓶子放进盒子、把橡皮放进文具盒。

English Summary

This is a conversation between Tom and Anna as they tidy things in the classroom. Tom asks whether the bottle on the desk is Anna's; she says yes and thanks him, and he answers "You're welcome". Tom asks what the thing on the desk is, and Anna says it is her eraser, not his. Tom then asks whether the key under the desk is hers, and Anna says it is Tom's key, so Tom realizes it belongs to him. Anna asks whose key and bottle they are and reminds Tom that his things are everywhere and he needs to keep them tidy. Together they put the bottle in the box and the eraser in the pencil box.

语法点 Grammar

Is this your bottle?

be 动词一般疑问句，确认物品归属；答语 Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

Yes, it is. Thanks, Tom!

it 指代上文的 the bottle；Thanks 是口语感谢。

You're welcome.

回答 Thank you. 的固定用语，意为“不用谢”。

What's this on your desk?

What's this? 询问近处物品；on your desk 表位置。

It's an eraser.

不定冠词 a/an：元音音素前用 an。

It's mine, not yours.

名词性物主代词 mine = my eraser，yours = your eraser。

Is that your key under the desk?

Is that...? 确认较远物品；under the desk 作后置定语。

That key is Tom's.

名词所有格：人名后加 's 表示“……的”。

Whose key is this?

Whose + 名词 + is this/that? 询问物主。

You need to keep them tidy.

need to do sth.；keep + 宾语 + 形容词。

Let's put the bottle in the box.

Let's do sth. 提建议；put sth. in sth.。

Put your eraser in your pencil box, too.

祈使句给建议：Put...in...。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It's mine, not yours.

是我的，不是你的。

mine、yours 是名词性物主代词，后面不接名词；mine = my eraser，yours = your eraser。

Whose key is this? And whose bottle is that?

这是谁的钥匙？那是谁的瓶子？

whose 询问物主，后接名词；this 指近处，that 指远处。

You need to keep them tidy.

你需要把它们收拾整齐。

need to do sth. 后接动词原形；keep + 宾语 + 形容词表示“使……保持某种状态”。

Section B 1b · Keep Our Classroom Tidy

Section B 1b 主题：整理书包与课桌 · 短文

Hello! I am Lin Tao. Let's keep our classroom tidy.

大家好！我是林涛。我们让教室保持整洁吧。

词汇keep 保持；classroom 教室；tidy 整洁的 语法keep + 宾语 + 形容词；Let's do sth. 提建议。

In my schoolbag, I have a bottle, a pencil box and some books.

我的书包里有一个水杯、一个文具盒和一些书。

词汇schoolbag 书包；bottle 瓶子；pencil box 文具盒 语法have 表示拥有；some + 可数名词复数。

My bottle is blue, and my keys are on my desk.

我的水杯是蓝色的，我的钥匙在课桌上。

词汇key 钥匙；desk 课桌 语法多个形容词并列描述物品；on my desk 表位置。

An eraser is under my chair. Is it yours?

我的椅子下面有一块橡皮。是你的吗？

词汇eraser 橡皮；under 在……下面 语法yours 是名词性物主代词，相当于 your eraser。

Our teacher says, "Put your things in the right places."

我们老师说：“把你们的东西放在正确的地方。”

词汇thing 东西；right 正确的；place 地方 语法祈使句 Put...in...；引号内是直接引语。

So I put my books in my schoolbag every day.

所以我每天把书放进书包里。

词汇put...in 把……放进 语法So 表示结果；every day 表频率。

I put my key in my pocket and my eraser in my pencil box.

我把钥匙放进口袋，把橡皮放进文具盒。

词汇key 钥匙；pocket 口袋；eraser 橡皮 语法两个并列分句；put sth. in sth.。

Whose eraser is this? I ask my classmates.

这是谁的橡皮？我问同学们。

词汇whose 谁的；classmate 同学 语法Whose...is this? 询问物主；宾语从句语序不变。

If nobody needs it, I will put it in the lost and found box.

如果没有人需要它，我就把它放在失物招领箱里。

词汇need 需要；lost and found 失物招领 语法if 条件句主将从现；nobody 作主语谓语用单数。

A tidy classroom is nice for us all.

整洁的教室对我们大家来说都很好。

词汇tidy 整洁的；us all 我们大家 语法主系表结构；for us all 表对象。

If everyone needs help, we can help them find things.

如果每个人都需要帮助，我们可以帮他们找东西。

词汇need 需要；find 找到 语法if 条件句；can + 动词原形。

Please keep your things tidy every day. It's easy!

请每天把东西收拾整齐。这很容易！

词汇keep...tidy 保持整洁；easy 容易的 语法祈使句提建议；it 作形式主语指代上句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文是林涛介绍自己如何保持教室整洁。他的书包里有水杯、文具盒和一些书；水杯是蓝色的，钥匙在课桌上；椅子下面有一块不属于自己的橡皮，他会问：“这是谁的？”老师要求大家把东西放在正确的地方，于是他每天把书放进书包、把钥匙放进口袋、把橡皮放进文具盒。如果没人认领橡皮，他就把它放进失物招领箱。他还说整洁的教室对大家都有好处，并请大家每天把东西收拾整齐。

English Summary

This passage is Lin Tao's description of how he keeps the classroom tidy. In his schoolbag he has a bottle, a pencil box and some books; his bottle is blue and his keys are on his desk. There is an eraser under his chair that is not his, so he asks, "Whose eraser is this?" His teacher tells them to put their things in the right places, so every day he puts his books in his schoolbag, his key in his pocket and his eraser in his pencil box. If nobody needs the eraser, he puts it in the lost and found box. He says a tidy classroom is nice for them all and asks everyone to keep their things tidy every day.

语法点 Grammar

Let's keep our classroom tidy.

Let's do sth. 提建议；keep + 宾语 + 形容词。

I have a bottle, a pencil box and some books.

have 表示拥有；and 连接三个并列宾语。

My bottle is blue, and my keys are on my desk.

主语单复数与 be 动词一致：bottle is / keys are。

An eraser is under my chair.

under 表位置；a/an 依元音音素选择。

Is it yours?

名词性物主代词 yours = your eraser。

Put your things in the right places.

祈使句给建议；in the right places 表位置。

So I put my books in my schoolbag every day.

So 表结果；every day 表频率。

Whose eraser is this?

Whose + 名词 + is this? 询问物主。

If nobody needs it, I will put it in the lost and found box.

if 条件句主将从现；nobody 作主语谓语用单数。

A tidy classroom is nice for us all.

主系表结构；for us all 表对象。

If everyone needs help, we can help them find things.

if 条件句；can + 动词原形；help sb. (to) do sth.。

Please keep your things tidy every day.

祈使句提建议；keep sth. tidy。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Put your things in the right places.

把你们的东西放在正确的地方。

祈使句以动词原形开头；in the right places 表示在正确的位置。

If nobody needs it, I will put it in the lost and found box.

如果没有人需要它，我就把它放在失物招领箱里。

if 引导条件状语从句，从句用一般现在时 needs，主句用 will put（主将从现）。

An eraser is under my chair. Is it yours?

椅子下面有一块橡皮。是你的吗？

yours 是名词性物主代词，后面不能再跟名词；容易错写成 your。

  • 复习物品类单词：bottle, eraser, key, pencil box, schoolbag。

读中

  • 找出作者分别把书包、钥匙、橡皮放在哪里。
  • 标出 Whose...? 和 If... 的句子。

读后

  • 用“物品—位置”表格复述短文。
  • 说说你会怎样整理自己的书桌和书包。

Section B 3a · A Thank-you Note to Tom

Section B 3a 主题：借还物品与致谢 · 学生便条

Dear Tom,

亲爱的汤姆：

词汇dear 亲爱的（信开头） 语法便条开头的称呼语。

Thank you for your help today.

谢谢你今天帮我。

词汇thank you for 因……而感谢 语法Thank you for sth./doing sth. 表示感谢的原因。

This morning, I lost my key, and I was worried.

今天早上，我把钥匙弄丢了，我很着急。

词汇lose 丢失；worried 着急的 语法一般过去时叙述过去的事；and 连接并列分句。

You found it under the desk and gave it to me.

你在课桌下面找到了它，还给了我。

词汇find 找到；under 在……下面 语法give sth. to sb.；一般过去时。

You said, "Here you are," and I said, "Thank you."

你说：“给你。”我说：“谢谢。”

词汇Here you are. 给你。 语法Here you are. 是给对方东西时的常用语。

And you said, "You're welcome." That was kind of you.

你又说：“不用谢。”你真好。

词汇kind 友好的；You're welcome. 不用谢 语法That is/was kind of you. 表示感谢。

I need to keep my things tidy from now on.

从现在起我需要把东西收拾整齐。

词汇need 需要；tidy 整洁的 语法need to do sth.；from now on 从现在起。

My bottle is in my schoolbag, and my eraser is in my pencil box.

我的水杯在书包里，橡皮在文具盒里。

词汇bottle 瓶子；eraser 橡皮；pencil box 文具盒 语法in + 名词 表地点，说明物品放在哪里。

If I find your things, I will give them back to you soon.

如果我找到你的东西，我会很快还给你。

词汇find 找到；give back 归还 语法if 条件句主将从现；give sth. back to sb.。

Whose bottle is this? Mine is here, so it is not mine.

这是谁的水杯？我的在这儿，所以它不是我的。

词汇whose 谁的；bottle 瓶子 语法Whose...is this?；mine 是名词性物主代词。

Let's keep our classroom bright and tidy together.

让我们一起把教室保持明亮整洁。

词汇bright 明亮的；together 一起 语法Let's do sth.；keep + 宾语 + 形容词。

See you tomorrow, Tom. Thanks again!

明天见，汤姆。再次感谢！

词汇see you 再见；again 再次 语法See you tomorrow. 告别用语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是安娜写给汤姆的一张英文便条。她先感谢汤姆今天的帮助并回应“不用谢”。接着讲事情的经过：今天早上她把钥匙弄丢了，很着急，汤姆在课桌下面找到了它并还给她，还说了“给你”，她说“谢谢”，汤姆回答“不用谢”，她觉得汤姆真好。她表示从现在起要把自己的东西收拾整齐：水杯放书包，橡皮放文具盒。她还说如果找到汤姆的东西会马上还给他，并问这是谁的水杯，最后请汤姆一起保持教室整洁，约定明天见、再次感谢。

English Summary

This is a short English note from Anna to Tom. She first thanks Tom for his help today and replies with "You're welcome" too. Then she tells what happened: this morning she lost her key and was worried, and Tom found it under the desk, gave it to her, and said "Here you are"; she said "Thank you" and he answered "You're welcome", which was very kind. She says she needs to keep her things tidy from now on: her bottle is in her schoolbag and her eraser is in her pencil box. She promises to give Tom's things back to him if she finds them, asks whose bottle it is, and asks him to keep the classroom bright and tidy together. She ends by saying she will see him tomorrow and thanking him again.

if 条件句主将从现；give sth. back to sb.。

Whose bottle is this?

Whose + 名词 + is this? 询问物主；mine 是名词性物主代词。

Let's keep our classroom bright and tidy together.

Let's do sth. 提建议；keep + 宾语 + 形容词并列。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

You found it under the desk and gave it to me.

你在课桌下面找到了它，还给了我。

it 指代 the key；give sth. to sb. 中 to 不能省略，代词要放 to 前。

If I find your things, I will give them back to you soon.

如果我找到你的东西，我会很快还给你。

if 从句用一般现在时 find，主句用 will give（主将从现）；them 指代 things。

Whose bottle is this? Mine is here, so it is not mine.

这是谁的水杯？我的在这儿，所以它不是我的。

whose 问物主；mine 作主语，等于 my bottle，后面不接名词。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题，判断这是信件、便条还是日记。
  • 回忆借还物品的礼貌用语：Here you are. / Thank you. / You're welcome.

读中

  • 找出便条里事情的经过：丢失—找到—归还—道谢。
  • 圈出一般过去时动词和名词所有格。

读后

  • 模仿便条给同学写一张致谢小便条。
  • 整理书包后，用 5 句话英文说明每样东西放哪里。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What is on Tom's desk at the beginning?

  1. A key.
  2. A bottle.
  3. An eraser.
  4. A pencil box.
答案与解析

B 原文：Excuse me, Anna. Is this your bottle? What's this on your desk? 汤姆指着桌上的物品问，安娜回答 It's an eraser.，可知汤姆问的物件依次是水瓶与橡皮。

2. Whose key is under the desk?

  1. Anna's.
  2. Tom's.
  3. The teacher's.
  4. We don't know.
答案与解析

B 原文：No, it isn't. That key is Tom's. 接着汤姆说 It's my key.。

3. What does Anna tell Tom to do with his things?

  1. To give them away.
  2. To put them in a box.
  3. To keep them tidy.
  4. To buy new ones.
答案与解析

C 原文：Your things are everywhere! You need to keep them tidy. 后面还具体提示把瓶子和橡皮放好。

4. What does the passage say about a tidy classroom?

  1. It is hard for students.
  2. It is nice for us all.
  3. It is only for teachers.
  4. It is not important.
答案与解析

B 原文：A tidy classroom is nice for us all. 整洁的教室对我们大家来说都很好。

5. Where does Lin Tao put his books every day?

  1. In his schoolbag.
  2. In his pocket.
  3. Under his chair.
  4. On the teacher's desk.
答案与解析

A 原文：So I put my books in my schoolbag every day. 钥匙进的是口袋，书进的是书包。

6. What does Lin Tao do if nobody needs the eraser?

  1. He keeps it.
  2. He puts it in the lost and found box.
  3. He gives it to his teacher.
  4. He throws it away.
答案与解析

B 原文：If nobody needs it, I will put it in the lost and found box.

7. What did Tom do when Anna lost her key?

  1. He bought a new key for her.
  2. He found it under the desk and gave it to her.
  3. He said "Whose key is this?"
  4. He put it in the lost and found box.
答案与解析

B 原文：You found it under the desk and gave it to me. 还有 You said, "Here you are." 的细节。

8. What does Anna say about her own things in the note?

  1. Her bottle is in her pocket.
  2. Her bottle is in her schoolbag and her eraser is in her pencil box.
  3. She still needs to buy a bottle.
  4. Her eraser is under the desk.
答案与解析

B 原文：My bottle is in my schoolbag, and my eraser is in my pencil box.
