Section A 3a · Hello! Nice to Meet You

Section A 3a 主题：问候与拼写 · 初次见面对话

A: Good morning! I'm Helen. What's your name?

早上好！我是海伦。你叫什么名字？

词汇good morning 早上好；name 名字 语法Good morning. 早晨问候语；What's your name? 询问姓名。

B: Good morning, Helen. My name is Peter.

早上好，海伦。我叫彼得。

词汇my 我的 语法My name is... 介绍自己的名字。

A: Nice to meet you, Peter. How do you spell your name?

很高兴见到你，彼得。你的名字怎么拼？

词汇spell 用字母拼；拼写 语法How do you spell...? 询问拼写。

B: P-E-T-E-R, Peter.

P-E-T-E-R，彼得。

词汇letter 字母 语法用单个字母拼写，字母间用连字符。

A: Thank you. Are you a new student here?

谢谢。你是这里的新生吗？

词汇new 新的；student 学生 语法Are you...? 一般疑问句。

B: Yes, I am. I'm in Class One.

是的。我在一班。

词汇class 班级 语法I'm in Class One. 表示所在班级。

A: Me too! Let's greet each other every morning.

我也是！我们每天早上互相问候吧。

词汇greet 问候；each other 互相 语法Let's do sth. 提建议。

B: Good idea. The bell is ringing. Let's start!

好主意。铃响了。我们开始吧！

词汇bell 铃（声）；start 开始 语法现在进行时 is ringing。

A: Before class, let's have a short conversation.

上课前，我们简单聊几句吧。

词汇conversation 谈话；交谈 语法have a conversation 进行交谈。

B: OK. Everyone, listen and speak in English!

好的。大家用英语听和说！

词汇everyone 每人；所有人 语法祈使句；everyone 作呼语。

A: And don't forget to say hello to each other.

别忘了互相问好。

词汇forget 忘记；say hello 问好 语法Don't forget to do sth. 否定祈使句。

B: We will. Hello, everyone!

我们会的。大家好！

词汇hello 你好 语法简单问候语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话是两个新生初次见面的问候与自我介绍。海伦早上好先打招呼并说自己叫海伦，彼得回答自己叫彼得；海伦接着问对方名字怎么拼，彼得逐个字母拼了出来。海伦问他是不是新生、在哪个班，彼得说在一班，海伦说自己也是。两人约定每天早上互相问候，铃响就开始上课，并倡议大家用英语听和说、互相问好。

English Summary

The conversation is a greeting and self-introduction between two new students. Helen says good morning and gives her name; Peter replies with his. Helen asks how to spell his name and Peter spells it letter by letter. She asks whether he is a new student and which class he is in; Peter says Class One, and Helen says she is there too. They agree to greet each other every morning, start the lesson when the bell rings, listen and speak in English, and remember to say hello to each other.

语法点 Grammar

Good morning!

早晨问候语。

I'm Helen.

be 动词第一人称 am 的缩写。

What's your name?

What's...? 询问姓名。

My name is Peter.

My name is... 自我介绍。

How do you spell your name?

How do you spell...? 询问拼写。

Are you a new student here?

be 动词一般疑问句。

Yes, I am.

简略回答，肯定用 Yes, I am.。

I'm in Class One.

in + 班级 表示所在班级。

Let's greet each other every morning.

Let's do sth.；each other 作宾语。

The bell is ringing.

现在进行时表示正在发生。

Everyone, listen and speak in English!

祈使句；Everyone 作呼语。

Don't forget to say hello to each other.

否定祈使句 Don't + 动词原形。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

How do you spell your name?

你的名字怎么拼？

spell 后接名词；how 引导询问方式。

Let's greet each other every morning.

我们每天早上互相问候吧。

Let's do sth. 提建议；each other 是相互代词。

Don't forget to say hello to each other.

别忘了互相问好。

否定祈使句用 Don't；forget to do sth. 表示“别忘了要做”。

  • 用三句话向同学介绍自己。

Section B 1b · Hello, Everyone!

Section B 1b 主题：班级日常问候 · 短文

Hello, everyone! I am Ella, and this is my friend Emma.

大家好！我是埃拉，这是我的朋友埃玛。

词汇everyone 每人；friend 朋友 语法this is... 介绍他人。

We are new students at this school.

我们是这所学校的新生。

词汇new 新的；school 学校 语法主系表结构。

Every morning, we greet our teachers and classmates.

每天早上，我们向老师和同学问好。

词汇greet 问候；classmate 同学 语法every morning 表示频率。

We say "Good morning" to each other.

我们互相说“早上好”。

词汇each other 互相 语法say sth. to sb.。

In class, we start a conversation in English.

课上我们用英语开始交谈。

词汇start 开始；conversation 交谈 语法in class 作状语。

Our teacher helps us spell new words.

老师帮助我们拼写新单词。

词汇spell 拼写；word 单词 语法help sb. do sth.。

When the bell rings, we begin our lesson.

铃一响，我们就开始上课。

词汇bell 铃；begin 开始 语法when 引导时间状语从句。

Everyone in our class is friendly to each other.

我们班每个人彼此都很友好。

词汇friendly 友好的 语法be friendly to sb.。

We often say hello to other students.

我们常向其他同学问好。

词汇often 经常；other 其他的 语法often 表示频率。

Oh, the bell is ringing again.

哦，铃又响了。

词汇again 再次 语法现在进行时。

Let's start a new conversation together.

让我们一起开始新的交谈吧。

词汇together 一起 语法Let's do sth.。

See you later, everyone!

大家回头见！

词汇see you later 回头见 语法告别用语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文是埃拉介绍自己和朋友埃玛的班级日常。她们是新生，每天早上向老师和同学问好，互说“早上好”；课上用英语开始交谈，老师帮助她们拼写新单词；铃一响就开始上课。班上每个人彼此都很友好，常向其他同学问好。最后铃又响了，大家准备一起开始新的交谈。

English Summary

The passage is Ella's introduction to her and her friend Emma's school routine. They are new students. Every morning they greet their teachers and classmates and say "Good morning" to each other; in class they start a conversation in English, and their teacher helps them spell new words. When the bell rings, the lesson begins. Everyone in the class is friendly to each other, and they often say hello to other students. At the end the bell rings again, and they are ready to start a new conversation together.

  • 注意短文里的日常活动与时间。

读后

Section B 3a · This Is Me

Section B 3a 主题：自我介绍 · 短文习作

Hello! My name is Helen Brown, and I am from China.

你好！我叫海伦·布朗，来自中国。

词汇be from 来自；China 中国 语法be from 表示来自某地。

I am a student in Starter Unit 1 of our English book.

我是我们英语书过渡单元一的学生。

词汇starter unit 过渡单元 语法in + 单元名。

I want to greet everyone here.

我想向这里的每个人问好。

词汇greet 问候 语法want to do sth.。

Every student says hello to each other in the morning.

每天早上每个学生都互相问好。

词汇each other 互相 语法every + 单数名词，谓语用单数。

My teacher is Miss Miller. She is very kind.

我的老师是米勒小姐。她非常和善。

词汇kind 和善的 语法主系表结构。

She often helps me spell long words.

她常帮我拼写长单词。

词汇spell 拼写；long 长的 语法help sb. do sth.；often 表示频率。

We start each lesson with a short conversation.

我们每节课都以一段简短交谈开始。

词汇start with 以……开始 语法start...with...。

When the bell rings, everyone sits down quietly.

铃响时，大家安静地坐下。

词汇sit down 坐下；quietly 安静地 语法when 引导时间状语从句。

After class, we talk about the PRC, the UN and the WHO.

课后我们谈论中国、联合国和世界卫生组织。

词汇the PRC 中华人民共和国；the WHO 世界卫生组织 语法缩略语前加 the。

Our English class is full of fun.

我们的英语课充满乐趣。

词汇be full of 充满 语法be full of...。

This is my new start, and I like it very much.

这是我新的开始，我非常喜欢。

词汇start 开始 语法主系表结构。

Please say hello to me when you see me.

见到我时请向我问好。

词汇say hello to sb. 向某人问好 语法when 引导时间状语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇习作是海伦·布朗的自我介绍。她来自中国，是英语书过渡单元一的学习者，想向这里的每个人问好。她介绍每天早上同学们互相问好，她的老师米勒小姐很和善，常帮她拼写长单词；每节课都以简短的交谈开始，铃响时大家安静坐下。课后他们谈论中国、联合国和世界卫生组织，她很喜欢这样的英语课，并请大家见到她时向她问好。

English Summary

This writing is Helen Brown's self-introduction. She is from China and is a learner in Starter Unit 1 of the English book, and she wants to greet everyone. She describes how every morning the students say hello to each other, how her teacher Miss Miller is very kind and often helps her spell long words, how each lesson starts with a short conversation, and how everyone sits down quietly when the bell rings. After class they talk about the PRC, the UN and the WHO. She likes her English class very much and asks people to say hello to her when they see her.
