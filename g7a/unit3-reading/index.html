Section A 3a · Is There a Library in Our School?

Section A 3a 主题：校园场所与位置 · 对话

Amy: Excuse me, is there a library in our school?

打扰一下，我们学校里有图书馆吗？

词汇excuse me 打扰一下；library 图书馆 语法Is there...? 询问单数场所是否存在，肯定回答用 Yes, there is.

Li Ming: Yes, there is. It's next to the classroom building.

有。它就在教学楼旁边。

词汇next to 在……旁边；classroom building 教学楼 语法next to 表示“紧挨着”，后接名词。

Amy: Great. Are there any computers in the library?

太好了。图书馆里有电脑吗？

词汇computer 电脑；any 任何（疑问句） 语法Are there any...? 询问复数事物；any 用于疑问句。

Li Ming: Yes, there are twenty computers, and we can use them after class.

有，一共有二十台电脑，我们课后可以使用。

词汇use 使用；after class 课后 语法and 连接并列句；can + 动词原形。

Amy: What about the playground? Is it behind the school building?

操场呢？它在教学楼后面吗？

词汇playground 操场；behind 在……后面 语法What about...? 引出新话题。

Li Ming: No, it isn't. It's between the gym and the dining hall.

不，不是。它在体育馆和食堂之间。

词汇gym 体育馆；between...and... 在……之间；dining hall 食堂 语法between A and B 表示“在 A 和 B 之间”。

Amy: Is there a dining hall near the gym?

体育馆附近有食堂吗？

词汇near 在……附近 语法Is there...near...? 复合地点疑问。

Li Ming: Yes, there is. The food there is very nice.

有的。那里的饭菜很好吃。

词汇there 在那里（副词） 语法there 作后置定语修饰 the food。

Amy: Where is the teachers' office then?

那教师办公室在哪里？

词汇teachers' office 教师办公室 语法Where is...? 询问地点。

Li Ming: It's in front of the library, on the first floor.

它在图书馆前面，一楼。

词汇in front of 在……前面；on the first floor 在一楼 语法in front of 与 in the front of 不同：后者指内部的前部。

Amy: Thank you. You know our school very well!

谢谢你。你对学校很熟悉啊！

词汇know 了解；very well 很熟悉地 语法very well 修饰动词。

Li Ming: Sure. Let's go and see the library together.

不客气。我们一起去看图书馆吧。

词汇together 一起 语法Let's do sth. 表建议。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话是艾米向李明询问学校里的场所。艾米先问学校里有没有图书馆，李明说在，就在教学楼旁边，而且图书馆里有二十台电脑，课后可以使用。艾米又问操场在哪里，李明说操场不在教学楼后面，而在体育馆和食堂之间；食堂就在体育馆附近，饭菜很好吃。艾米接着问教师办公室，李明说在图书馆前面的二楼。最后艾米感谢李明，李明提议一起去看图书馆。

English Summary

The conversation is between Amy and Li Ming about places in their school. Amy first asks if there is a library; Li Ming says yes, next to the classroom building, and there are twenty computers we can use after class. Then Amy asks about the playground, and Li Ming explains that it is not behind the school building but between the gym and the dining hall, which is near the gym and serves nice food. Amy goes on to ask where the teachers' office is, and Li Ming says it is in front of the library, on the first floor. Amy thanks him, and he suggests going to see the library together.

语法点 Grammar

Is there a library in our school?

Is there + 单数名词...? 询问是否存在。

Yes, there is.

there be 一般疑问句的肯定回答。

It's next to the classroom building.

next to 表示“紧挨着”，后接名词。

Are there any computers in the library?

Are there any + 复数名词...? any 用于疑问句。

Yes, there are twenty computers.

there be 后接复数名词，肯定回答用 are。

we can use them after class

can + 动词原形；them 作宾语。

Is it behind the school building?

behind 表示“在……的后面”。

It's between the gym and the dining hall.

between A and B 表示“在 A 和 B 之间”。

Is there a dining hall near the gym?

Is there... near...? 询问某地附近是否存在某物。

Where is the teachers' office then?

Where is...? 询问地点。

It's in front of the library, on the first floor.

in front of 表示“在……前面”（外部）；on + the + 序数词 + floor。

Let's go and see the library together.

Let's do sth. 提建议。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It's between the gym and the dining hall.

它在体育馆和食堂之间。

between A and B 结构，A、B 是并列成分，and 不能省略。

Are there any computers in the library?

图书馆里有电脑吗？

there be 疑问句的复数形式，any 修饰复数名词。

It's in front of the library, on the first floor.

它在图书馆前面，一楼。

in front of 指“在……外部的前面”；楼层用 on the first floor。

Section B 1b · Welcome to Our School

Section B 1b 主题：校园介绍 · 说明文

Hello, everyone! Welcome to our school.

大家好！欢迎来到我们学校。

词汇welcome to 欢迎来到 语法Welcome to... 表示欢迎。

Our school is big and beautiful.

我们学校又大又漂亮。

词汇beautiful 漂亮的 语法主系表结构；and 连接并列表语。

There are three classroom buildings, and there is a big library in the middle of the school.

学校有三栋教学楼，学校中间有一个大图书馆。

词汇in the middle of 在……中间；library 图书馆 语法There are + 复数；There is + 单数，两种 there be 并列。

The library is next to the dining hall, so we often read books there after lunch.

图书馆紧挨着食堂，所以午饭后我们常在那里看书。

词汇after lunch 午饭后；read books 读书 语法so 表示结果。

Behind the library, there is a large playground.

图书馆后面有一个大操场。

词汇playground 操场；large 大 语法Behind... 置于句首，用完全倒装结构 there be。

We do morning exercises there every day, and after school we play football and basketball.

我们每天在那里做早操，放学后还踢足球、打篮球。

词汇morning exercises 早操；do 做 语法play + 球类运动，不加 the。

There isn't a swimming pool in our school, but there are two gyms.

我们学校没有游泳池，但有两个体育馆。

词汇swimming pool 游泳池；gym 体育馆 语法there isn't a... 否定单数；there are 肯定复数。

In front of the gym, there is a small garden with many flowers.

体育馆前面有一个种着很多花的小花园。

词汇garden 花园；flowers 花；with 带有 语法with 短语作后置定语。

The teachers' office is between the garden and our classroom.

教师办公室在花园和我们的教室之间。

词汇teachers' office 教师办公室 语法between A and B 表示“在……之间”。

Our classroom is on the second floor, and it is very bright.

我们的教室在二楼，非常明亮。

词汇on the second floor 在二楼；bright 明亮的 语法on + 序数词 + floor 表示在几楼。

Is there a school bus near the school gate?

学校门口附近有校车吗？

词汇school gate 校门；school bus 校车 语法Is there...? 一般疑问句。

Yes, there is. It takes us home every afternoon.

有。它每天下午送我们回家。

词汇take...home 送……回家 语法take sb. + 地点 表示带某人去某处；every afternoon 表频率。

I love my school, and I am happy to study here.

我爱我的学校，很高兴在这里学习。

词汇study 学习 语法be happy to do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍了一位同学的学校。学校又大又漂亮：有三栋教学楼，学校中间有一个大图书馆，图书馆紧挨着食堂，午饭后同学们常在那里看书。图书馆后面是一个大操场，大家每天在那里做早操，放学后踢足球、打篮球。学校里没有游泳池，但有两个体育馆；体育馆前面有一个种满花的小花园。教师办公室在花园和教室之间，教室在二楼，非常明亮。校门附近有校车，每天下午送同学回家。最后作者说自己很喜欢学校，很高兴在这里学习。

English Summary

The passage introduces the writer's school. It is big and beautiful: there are three classroom buildings and a big library in the middle of the school, next to the dining hall, where students often read after lunch. Behind the library is a large playground, where they do morning exercises every day and play football and basketball after school. There isn't a swimming pool, but there are two gyms, and in front of the gyms there is a small garden with many flowers. The teachers' office is between the garden and the classroom, which is on the second floor and very bright. There is a school bus near the school gate, and it takes students home every afternoon. In the end the writer says he loves the school and is happy to study there.

语法点 Grammar

Welcome to our school.

Welcome to... 表示欢迎。

Our school is big and beautiful.

主系表结构；and 连接并列表语。

There are three classroom buildings.

There are + 复数名词，表示“有”。

there is a big library in the middle of the school

in the middle of 表示“在……中间”。

The library is next to the dining hall.

next to 表示“紧挨着”。

Behind the library, there is a large playground.

方位短语置于句首，倒装 + there be。

We do morning exercises there every day.

一般现在时表习惯；every day 表频率。

There isn't a swimming pool in our school, but there are two gyms.

there isn't a... 与 there are... 否定与肯定对照。

there is a small garden with many flowers

with 短语作后置定语。

The teachers' office is between the garden and our classroom.

between A and B 表示“在……之间”。

Our classroom is on the second floor.

on + the + 序数词 + floor 表示楼层。

Is there a school bus near the school gate?

Is there...? 一般疑问句。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

There are three classroom buildings, and there is a big library in the middle of the school.

学校有三栋教学楼，学校中间有一个大图书馆。

并列的 there be 结构中，主语复数用 are、单数用 is，就近与各自主语一致。

There isn't a swimming pool in our school, but there are two gyms.

我们学校没有游泳池，但有两个体育馆。

there be 的否定形式为 there isn't / there aren't；but 表示转折。

The teachers' office is between the garden and our classroom.

教师办公室在花园和我们的教室之间。

between A and B 表示“在 A 和 B 之间”，and 不能省略。

Section B 3a · A Letter About My School

Section B 3a 主题：校园介绍 · 书信

Dear Peter,

亲爱的彼得，

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

How are you? Now let me tell you about my school.

你好吗？我来给你介绍一下我的学校吧。

词汇tell sb. about sth. 告诉某人关于某事 语法let sb. do sth. 让某人做某事。

There are two classroom buildings in my school.

我们学校有两栋教学楼。

词汇classroom building 教学楼 语法There be 句型，复数用 are。

My classroom is on the third floor, in front of the library.

我的教室在三楼，就在图书馆前面。

词汇third 第三；in front of 在……前面 语法on + the + 序数词 + floor；in front of 表示“在……前面”。

There is a big library next to my classroom building.

我们教学楼旁边有一个大图书馆。

词汇next to 在……旁边 语法There is + 单数名词。

There are many books in it, and there isn't any noise there.

里面有很多书，那里一点也不吵。

词汇noise 噪音；many books 很多书 语法there isn't any... 否定存在；any 用于否定句。

Behind the library there is a playground, and I often play basketball there.

图书馆后面有一个操场，我常在那里打篮球。

词汇often 经常 语法often 位于实义动词前。

The dining hall is between the playground and the gym, and we have lunch there at half past eleven.

食堂在操场和体育馆之间，我们十一点半在那里吃午饭。

词汇between...and... 在……之间；at half past eleven 十一点半 语法between A and B 表示“在……之间”；at + 具体时间。

Is there a garden in your school? What is it like?

你的学校里有花园吗？它是什么样的？

词汇What is it like? 它是什么样的？ 语法What is ... like? 询问情况。

I like my school very much because it is new and clean.

我很喜欢我的学校，因为它又新又干净。

词汇because 因为；clean 干净的 语法because 引导原因状语从句。

Please write soon and tell me about your school.

请尽快回信，告诉我你们学校的情况。

词汇write soon 尽快回信 语法祈使句 + and。

Yours,

你的，

词汇yours 你的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Liu Fang

刘芳

词汇Liu Fang 刘芳（人名） 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是刘芳写给彼得的一封信，介绍自己的学校。她先问候，然后说学校有两栋教学楼，她的教室在三楼、在图书馆前面；教学楼旁边有一个大图书馆，里面书很多，一点也不吵。图书馆后面有一个操场，她常在那里打篮球；食堂在操场和体育馆之间，中午十一点半吃午饭。她问彼得的学校里有没有花园、是什么样的，说自己很喜欢这所学校，因为它又新又干净。最后请彼得回信介绍自己的学校。

English Summary

This is Liu Fang's letter to Peter about her school. After greeting him, she says there are two classroom buildings; her classroom is on the third floor, in front of the library. Next to her classroom building is a big library with many books and no noise. Behind the library there is a playground where she often plays basketball, and the dining hall is between the playground and the gym; they have lunch there at half past eleven. She asks Peter whether there is a garden in his school and what it is like, says she likes her school very much because it is new and clean, and asks him to write soon about his school.

1. Where is the library in Section A 3a?

  1. Behind the gym.

  4. On the playground.

  1. Ten.
  2. Fifteen.
  3. Twenty.
  4. Thirty.

3. Where is the playground?
