Section A 3a · Nice to Meet You

Section A 3a 主题：初次见面 · 自我介绍对话

A: Hello! My name is Li Hua. What's your name?

你好！我叫李华。你叫什么名字？

词汇name 名字 语法What's your name? 询问姓名。

B: Hi, Li Hua. I'm Emma. Nice to meet you.

你好，李华。我是艾玛。很高兴见到你。

词汇nice 令人愉快的；meet 遇见 语法Nice to meet you. 初次见面的问候语。

A: Nice to meet you too. Are you a new student?

我也很高兴见到你。你是新生吗？

词汇new 新的；student 学生 语法Are you...? 一般疑问句。

B: Yes, I am. I'm in Class 3, Grade 7.

是的。我在七年级三班。

词汇class 班级；grade 年级 语法in Class 3, Grade 7 表示班级（注意大小写与顺序）。

A: Me too! What's your favourite subject?

我也是！你最喜欢的科目是什么？

词汇favourite 最喜欢的；subject 科目 语法Me too. 表示“我也是”。

B: I like English best. What about you?

我最喜欢英语。你呢？

词汇like...best 最喜欢 语法What about you? 反问对方。

A: I like music. I can play the piano.

我喜欢音乐。我会弹钢琴。

词汇music 音乐；play the piano 弹钢琴 语法can + 动词原形；play the + 乐器。

B: That's cool! Can you play it well?

太酷了！你弹得好吗？

词汇cool 酷的；well 好地 语法Can you...? 询问能力。

A: A little. I practise every day.

一点点。我每天都练习。

词汇a little 一点点；practise 练习 语法every day 表示频率。

B: Great! I hope we can be good friends.

太棒了！希望我们能成为好朋友。

词汇hope 希望 语法hope 后接宾语从句。

A: Sure! Let's help each other.

当然！我们互相帮助吧。

词汇help each other 互相帮助 语法Let's do sth.。

B: OK. See you later, Li Hua!

好的。回头见，李华！

词汇see you later 回头见 语法告别用语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话是两个新生初次见面的自我介绍。李华先打招呼并问对方名字，艾玛回答并说很高兴认识他；两人互相问候后，李华问艾玛是不是新生，艾玛说自己在七年级三班。他们聊到最喜欢的科目：艾玛最喜欢英语，李华喜欢音乐，还会弹钢琴，每天练习。最后两人希望成为好朋友，互相帮助。

English Summary

The conversation is a self-introduction between two new students. Li Hua greets Emma and asks her name; Emma replies and says nice to meet her. Li Hua asks whether she is a new student, and Emma says she is in Class 3, Grade 7. They talk about favourite subjects: Emma likes English best, while Li Hua likes music and can play the piano, which he practises every day. In the end they hope to be good friends and help each other.

studentn.学生
classn.班级
graden.年级
subjectn.科目
musicn.音乐
practisev.练习
hopev.希望

  • favourite subject 最喜欢的科目
  • like English best 最喜欢英语
  • what about you 你呢
  • play the piano 弹钢琴
  • play it well 弹得好
  • a little 一点点

语法点 Grammar

What's your name?

What's...? 询问姓名。

I'm Emma.

be 动词缩写 I'm。

Nice to meet you.

初次见面的固定问候语。

Are you a new student?

be 动词一般疑问句 Are you...?。

I'm in Class 3, Grade 7.

in + 班级、年级 表示“在几年级几班”。

What's your favourite subject?

询问最喜欢的科目。

I like English best.

like...best 表示“最喜欢”。

What about you?

后用名词或动名词。

I can play the piano.

can + 动词原形；play the + 乐器。

Can you play it well?

Can you...? 询问能力。

I practise every day.

every day 表示频率。

I hope we can be good friends.

hope 后接宾语从句。

Let's help each other.

Let's do sth. 提建议。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Are you a new student?

你是新生吗？

be 动词一般疑问句，主语为 you 时用 Are。

I can play the piano.

我会弹钢琴。

can + 动词原形；play the + 乐器（球类运动不加 the）。

I hope we can be good friends.

希望我们能成为好朋友。

hope 后接宾语从句，从句用 can be。

Section B 1b · This Is Me

Section B 1b 主题：自我介绍 · 家庭与爱好

Hello, everyone! My name is Zhang Ming, and I am twelve years old.

大家好！我叫张明，今年十二岁。

词汇twelve 十二；years old 岁 语法be + 数字 + years old 表示年龄。

I am a student at Sunshine Middle School.

我是阳光中学的一名学生。

词汇middle school 中学 语法at + 学校名 表示就读学校。

I am in Class Two, Grade Seven.

我在七年级二班。

词汇Class Two 二班；Grade Seven 七年级 语法班级在前、年级在后，注意大写。

I have a big family: my parents, my sister and I.

我有一个大家庭：我的父母、我姐姐和我。

词汇parents 父母 语法have 表示拥有。

My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher.

我爸爸是医生，我妈妈是老师。

词汇doctor 医生；teacher 老师 语法主系表结构 a doctor / a teacher。

My sister is fifteen, and she is in high school.

我姐姐十五岁，在上高中。

词汇high school 高中 语法and 连接并列分句。

I like sports. I often play basketball after school.

我喜欢运动。放学后我常打篮球。

词汇sports 运动；often 经常；after school 放学后 语法often 表示频率。

My favourite food is noodles, and I also like apples.

我最喜欢的食物是面条，我也喜欢苹果。

词汇noodle 面条；apple 苹果 语法also 表示“也”。

I am good at maths, but I am not good at drawing.

我擅长数学，但不擅长画画。

词汇be good at 擅长；drawing 画画 语法be good at + 名词/动名词。

I want to make friends with you all.

我想和大家交朋友。

词汇make friends with 与……交朋友 语法want to do sth.。

This is me. Thank you for listening!

这就是我。谢谢大家的聆听！

词汇listen 听 语法Thank you for + 动名词。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文是张明的自我介绍。他十二岁，是阳光中学的学生，在七年级二班。他有一个大家庭：父母、姐姐和他自己；爸爸是医生，妈妈是老师，十五岁的姐姐在上高中。他喜欢运动，放学后常打篮球；最喜欢的食物是面条，也喜欢苹果；擅长数学但不擅长画画。最后他说想和大家交朋友。

English Summary

The passage is Zhang Ming's self-introduction. He is twelve and a student at Sunshine Middle School, in Class Two, Grade Seven. He has a big family—his parents, his sister and himself. His father is a doctor, his mother is a teacher, and his fifteen-year-old sister is in high school. He likes sports and often plays basketball after school; his favourite food is noodles and he also likes apples. He is good at maths but not at drawing, and he wants to make friends with everyone.

词汇与含义 Vocabulary

词汇词性含义
twelvenum.十二
studentn.学生
middle schooln.中学
familyn.家庭
doctorn.医生
teachern.老师
sportsn.体育运动
favouriteadj.最喜欢的
friendlyadj.友好的

短语与固定搭配 Phrases

  • hello, everyone 大家好
  • twelve years old 十二岁
  • a student at Sunshine Middle School 阳光中学的学生
  • in Class Two, Grade Seven 在七年级二班
  • have a big family 有一个大家庭
  • play basketball 打篮球
  • after school 放学后
  • favourite food 最喜欢的食物
  • be good at maths 擅长数学
  • be not good at drawing 不擅长画画
  • want to make friends with 想和……交朋友
  • thank you for listening 谢谢聆听

语法点 Grammar

My name is Zhang Ming.

be 动词表身份。

I am twelve years old.

be + 数字 + years old 表示年龄。

I am a student at Sunshine Middle School.

at + 学校名。

I am in Class Two, Grade Seven.

in + 班级、年级。

I have a big family.

have 表示拥有。

My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher.

主系表结构；用 a 表示职业。

My sister is fifteen, and she is in high school.

and 连接并列分句。

I often play basketball after school.

often 表示频率，位于实义动词前。

My favourite food is noodles.

主系表；noodles 常用复数。

I am good at maths, but I am not good at drawing.

be good at + 名词/动名词；but 转折。

I want to make friends with you all.

want to do sth.。

Thank you for listening!

Thank you for + 动名词。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher.

我爸爸是医生，我妈妈是老师。

表示职业的单数名词前用 a/an。

I am good at maths, but I am not good at drawing.

我擅长数学，但不擅长画画。

be good at 后接名词或动名词，如 drawing。

Thank you for listening!

谢谢大家的聆听！

Thank you for + 动名词。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题 This Is Me，猜短文会介绍哪些内容。
  • 注意自我介绍的几个方面：姓名、年龄、家庭、爱好。

读中

  • 按“家庭—爱好—能力”列出关键信息。
  • 画出 be 动词与 have 的句子。

读后

  • 用表格整理张明的信息。
  • 仿写一段自我介绍。

Section B 3a · A Letter to a New Friend

Section B 3a 主题：交笔友 · 书信自我介绍

Dear Tom,

亲爱的汤姆，

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

How are you? I am very happy to write to you.

你好吗？很高兴给你写信。

词汇be happy to do sth. 高兴做某事 语法be happy to do sth.。

My name is Wang Mei. I am thirteen and I live in Chengdu.

我叫王梅，今年十三岁，住在成都。

词汇live in 住在 语法and 连接并列谓语。

There are four people in my family.

我家有四口人。

词汇four people 四口人 语法There be 句型。

My mum is a nurse and my dad works in a bank.

我妈妈是护士，我爸爸在银行工作。

词汇nurse 护士；bank 银行 语法works 第三人称单数。

I have a little brother. He is only five.

我有一个弟弟。他才五岁。

词汇little brother 弟弟 语法only 强调“才”。

At school, my favourite subject is English.

在学校里，我最喜欢的科目是英语。

词汇favourite 最喜欢的 语法主系表结构。

I like reading stories in English, and I want to be an English teacher.

我喜欢读英文故事，我想成为一名英语老师。

词汇reading 阅读；teacher 老师 语法want to be 想成为。

In my free time, I play ping-pong with my friends.

空闲时我和朋友打乒乓球。

词汇free time 空闲时间 语法play + 球类运动（不加 the）。

We also sing English songs together.

我们还一起唱英文歌。

词汇sing 唱歌；together 一起 语法and/also 表示补充。

Please write back and tell me about yourself.

请回信告诉我你的情况。

词汇write back 回信；yourself 你自己 语法祈使句 + and。

Yours,

你的朋友，

词汇yours 你的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Wang Mei

王梅

词汇— 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是王梅写给汤姆的一封信。她先问候并说自己很高兴给他写信，然后自我介绍：她十三岁，住在成都；家里有四口人，妈妈是护士，爸爸在银行工作，还有一个五岁的弟弟。在学校里她最喜欢英语，喜欢读英文故事，想成为英语老师；空闲时和朋友打乒乓球、一起唱英文歌。最后她请汤姆回信介绍自己。

English Summary

This is Wang Mei's letter to Tom. She greets him and says she is happy to write to him, then introduces herself: she is thirteen and lives in Chengdu; there are four people in her family—her mother is a nurse, her father works in a bank, and she has a five-year-old brother. At school her favourite subject is English; she likes reading English stories and wants to be an English teacher. In her free time she plays ping-pong with friends and they sing English songs together. She asks Tom to write back and tell her about himself.

livev.居住
nursen.护士
bankn.银行
subjectn.科目
free timen.空闲时间
togetheradv.一起

语法点 Grammar

How are you?

问候语。

I am very happy to write to you.

be happy to do sth.。

My name is Wang Mei.

be 动词表身份。

I am thirteen and I live in Chengdu.

and 连接并列谓语；live in 表示居住地。

There are four people in my family.

There be 句型，复数用 are。

My mum is a nurse and my dad works in a bank.

第三人称单数 works。

I have a little brother. He is only five.

have 表示拥有；only 强调“才”。

At school, my favourite subject is English.

at school 作地点状语。

I like reading stories in English.

like + 动名词。

I want to be an English teacher.

want to be 想成为；an + 元音音素开头的词。

In my free time, I play ping-pong with my friends.

play + 球类运动（不加 the）。

Please write back and tell me about yourself.

祈使句 + and；tell sb. about sth.。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

There are four people in my family.

我家有四口人。

There be 句型，主语为复数时用 are。

My mum is a nurse and my dad works in a bank.

我妈妈是护士，我爸爸在银行工作。

两个分句主语第三人称单数：is / works。

I want to be an English teacher.

我想成为一名英语老师。

want to be + 职业；English 以元音音素开头，用 an。
