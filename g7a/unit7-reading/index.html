Section A 2a · A Surprise Birthday Party

Section A 2a 主题：生日购物 · 价格与数量

Teng Fei: Hi, Peter!

嗨，彼得！

词汇hi 嗨；Peter 彼得（人名） 语法口语问候语，读时常用升调。

Teng Fei: It's Ella and Emma's birthday soon.

埃拉和埃玛的生日快到了。

词汇soon 不久；在即；birthday 生日 语法It's = It is；Ella and Emma's 是名词所有格，两人共有只在最后的名字后加 's。

Teng Fei: How about a surprise party for them?

给她们办一个惊喜派对怎么样？

词汇surprise 惊喜；surprise party 惊喜派对；how about ……怎么样 语法How about + 名词? 用于提出建议；for them 表示“为她们”。

Peter: Good idea!

好主意！

词汇idea 主意；想法 语法省略句：(That's a) good idea! 表示赞同对方的提议。

Peter: Let's go and buy something for the party.

我们去给派对买点东西吧。

词汇let's 让我们；buy 买；something 某物 语法Let's + 动词原形，表示“我们一起……吧”；go and buy 表示“去买”。

[At the shop.]

【在商店里】

词汇shop 商店；at 在…… 语法舞台说明，交代对话发生的地点，用方括号标出。

Woman: Good morning.

早上好。

词汇good morning 早上好 语法店员问候顾客的常用语。

Woman: Can I help you?

我能帮您吗？

词汇help 帮助；can 能 语法Can I help you? 是服务行业礼貌用语，意为“您需要点什么？”。

Teng Fei: Yes, please.

好的，麻烦了。

词汇yes, please 好的，麻烦了 语法接受对方帮助时的礼貌回答。

Teng Fei: How much is this birthday cake?

这个生日蛋糕多少钱？

词汇how much 多少钱；birthday cake 生日蛋糕 语法How much is + 单数名词? 询问价格。

Woman: We have a sale today.

我们今天有促销活动。

词汇sale 促销；减价出售；have a sale 举行促销 语法have a sale 是“促销、特价销售”的固定说法。

Woman: It's 85 yuan, and it comes with some candles.

它 85 元，还附带一些蜡烛。

词汇yuan 元；come with 随附；candle 蜡烛 语法It's + 价格，表示“多少钱”；come with 表示“附带、随赠”。

Peter: Good, we'll take it.

好的，我们买了。

词汇we'll = we will；take 买下 语法take it 在购物语境中表示“买下它”。

Peter: How much are those oranges?

那些橙子多少钱？

词汇orange 橙子；those 那些 语法How much are + 复数名词? 询问复数物品的价格。

Woman: Six yuan a kilo.

六元一公斤。

词汇kilo 公斤；千克；a kilo 每公斤 语法a + 计量单位，表示“每……”；如 a kilo、a bottle。

Woman: How many kilos do you want?

您要几公斤？

词汇how many 多少（后接可数名词复数）；kilo 公斤 语法How many + 复数名词 + do you want? 询问数量。

Peter: Five kilos, please.

请来五公斤。

词汇five 五；please 请 语法购物回答常用省略句：数量 + please。

Peter: Teng Fei, how about some yogurt?

腾飞，来点酸奶怎么样？

词汇yogurt 酸奶；some 一些 语法How about + 名词? 提出建议；some 用于提议买某物。

Teng Fei: Sure.

好啊。

词汇sure 当然；好的 语法Sure. 是口语中表示同意的简答。

Teng Fei: How much is the yogurt?

酸奶多少钱？

词汇yogurt 酸奶 语法How much is + 单数名词? 询问价格。

Woman: Five yuan a bottle.

五元一瓶。

词汇a bottle 每瓶；bottle 瓶子 语法a + 容器名词，表示“每……”。

Teng Fei: 10 bottles, please.

请来十瓶。

词汇ten 十；bottle 瓶子 语法数量 + please 的购物省略表达。

Teng Fei: What's the total?

总共多少钱？

词汇total 总数；总计；what's = what is 语法What's the total? 询问总价。

Woman: 165 yuan.

165 元。

词汇hundred 百；价格读作 one hundred and sixty-five yuan 语法价格表达：数字 + yuan；165 读作 one hundred and sixty-five。

Teng Fei: Here you are.

给您。

词汇here you are 给你；递东西时用语 语法Here you are. 是递东西给对方时的交际用语。

Woman: Thank you!

谢谢！

词汇thank 感谢 语法口语致谢的常用表达。

Woman: Have a nice day!

祝你今天愉快！

词汇have a nice day 祝你今天愉快 语法告别时的祝福语。

Teng Fei & Peter: Thanks.

谢谢。

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法口语致谢，比 thank you 更随意。

Teng Fei & Peter: You too.

你也是。

词汇you too 你也是 语法回应 Have a nice day! 的省略句：You too. = You have a nice day too.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在腾飞和彼得之间。埃拉和埃玛的生日快到了，腾飞提议给她们办一个惊喜派对，彼得说“好主意”，两人一起去商店为派对买东西。在商店里，他们先问生日蛋糕的价格：85 元，还附带蜡烛，于是决定买下；接着问橙子的价格，六元一公斤，买了五公斤；腾飞又提议买酸奶，五元一瓶，买了十瓶。最后问总价，一共 165 元，他们付了钱，店员祝他们今天愉快，他们道谢作别。

English Summary

This conversation takes place between Teng Fei and Peter. Ella and Emma's birthday is coming soon, and Teng Fei suggests a surprise party for them; Peter agrees, and they go to buy things for the party. At the shop they first ask about the birthday cake: it is 85 yuan and comes with some candles, so they take it. Then they ask about the oranges—six yuan a kilo—and buy five kilos. Teng Fei also suggests some yogurt, which is five yuan a bottle, and they buy ten bottles. They ask for the total, which is 165 yuan, pay for the things, and the shop assistant wishes them a nice day.

语法点 Grammar

It's Ella and Emma's birthday soon.

It's = It is；Ella and Emma's 是名词所有格，两人共有只在最后的名字后加 's。

How about a surprise party for them?

How about + 名词? 用于提出建议，后面也可接动词 -ing 形式。

Let's go and buy something for the party.

Let's + 动词原形，表示“我们一起……吧”；go and buy 表示“去买”。

Can I help you?

Can I ...? 用于主动提供帮助；help sb. 表示“帮助某人”。

How much is this birthday cake?

How much is + 单数名词? 询问价格，be 动词用 is。

How much are those oranges?

How much are + 复数名词? 询问价格，be 动词用 are；this/that 相应变为 these/those。

It's 85 yuan, and it comes with some candles.

It's + 价格表示“多少钱”；come with 表示“附带、随赠”。

How many kilos do you want?

How many + 可数名词复数 + do you want? 询问数量。

Six yuan a kilo.

a + 计量单位表示“每……”；如 a kilo、a bottle、a day。

We have a sale today.

一般现在时表示客观情况；have a sale 是固定搭配。

Good, we'll take it.

we'll = we will；take it 在购物时表示“买下它”。

What's the total?

What's = What is；total 在此作名词，表示“总价、总数”。

Here you are.

递东西给对方时的交际用语，也可说 Here it is。

Have a nice day!

祈使句表示祝福；回应常用 Thanks. You too.

10 bottles, please.

购物省略句：数量 + please，后面省略了 I want / I'd like。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It's Ella and Emma's birthday soon.

埃拉和埃玛的生日快到了。

soon 表示“不久、快要”；Ella and Emma's 是表示两人共同拥有的所有格形式，只在最后一个名字后加 's。

It's 85 yuan, and it comes with some candles.

它 85 元，还附带一些蜡烛。

It 指代 birthday cake；come with 意为“随附、附赠”，是购物场景中的常见表达。

What's the total?

总共多少钱？

total 在此作名词，表示“总额、总价”；询问总价也可以说 How much is that in total?

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 先看教材 P69 的 2a：它是听力填空，四处空格分别是 Good idea! / Yes, please. / Sure. / Here you are.
  • 完整对话原文见书后 Listening Scripts P90–91，按“提议办派对—进店—买蛋糕—买橙子—买酸奶—结账”的顺序读。

读中

  • 边读边圈出所有询问价格和数量的问句。
  • 注意 a kilo / a bottle 这类“每……”的表达，以及 Here you are. 等购物交际用语。

读后

  • 两人一组分角色朗读对话，注意店员与顾客的语气。
  • 用 How much is / are ...? 和 How many ...? 各造两个购物问句。

Section B 1b · Birthday Celebrations Online

Section B 1b 主题：生日庆祝方式 · 论坛帖

By: Yu Xiaoming on 08/06 5:07 PM

作者：于晓明，08/06 下午 5:07

词汇by 由（作者）；on 在（某日）；PM 下午 语法论坛发帖信息：By + 作者，on + 日期，PM 表示下午。

My birthday is on 13th June.

我的生日是 6 月 13 日。

词汇13th June 6 月 13 日；序数词 13th 读作 thirteenth 语法具体日期用介词 on；英文日期可写成“日 + 月”。

On that day, I always eat birthday noodles with eggs.

那天我总是吃加鸡蛋的生日面条。

词汇on that day 在那天；always 总是；birthday noodles 生日面条（长寿面）；with eggs 加鸡蛋 语法一般现在时 + always 表示习惯性动作；with 表示“带有、加上”。

Long noodles are a symbol of long life.

长长的面条是长寿的象征。

词汇long 长的；symbol 象征；life 生命 语法a symbol of ... 表示“……的象征”；主语 Long noodles 是复数，用 are。

Every year, my family and I take a photo together under the big tree in our village.

每年，我和家人在村里的大树下一起拍一张照片。

词汇every year 每年；take a photo 拍照；together 一起；village 村庄 语法Every year 作时间状语；my family and I 是并列主语，谓语用 take。

I like taking photos because they help me remember fun times with my family.

我喜欢拍照，因为它们帮我记住与家人一起的快乐时光。

词汇like doing sth. 喜欢做某事；remember 记得；fun times 快乐时光；family 家人 语法like doing sth. 表示“喜欢做某事”；because 引导原因状语从句；help sb. do sth. 中 do 前省略 to。

This year, I want to plant a small tree for my 14th birthday.

今年，我想为我的 14 岁生日种一棵小树。

词汇plant 种植；small 小的；14th birthday 14 岁生日 语法want to do sth. 想要做某事；for + 生日，表示“为……（生日）”。

I'd like to watch it grow with me.

我想看着它和我一起长大。

词汇I'd like to 我想要；grow 生长；with me 和我一起 语法I'd like to = I would like to；watch sth. do sth. 表示“看着某物做某事”。

When is your birthday?

你的生日是什么时候？

词汇when 什么时候 语法When 询问时间；birthday 前用形容词性物主代词 your。

How do you celebrate it?

你是怎么庆祝生日的？

词汇how 怎样；celebrate 庆祝 语法How do you + 动词原形? 询问方式；it 指代 birthday。

By: Judy Clark on 09/06 7:54 PM

作者：朱迪·克拉克，09/06 晚上 7:54

词汇Judy Clark 朱迪·克拉克（人名）；PM 下午/晚上 语法第二个帖子的发帖信息，格式与第一个相同。

It's a great idea to plant a tree for your birthday!

为你的生日种一棵树真是个好主意！

词汇great idea 好主意；plant a tree 种一棵树 语法It's + 名词 + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语，不定式是真正的主语。

My birthday is on 28th July.

我的生日是 7 月 28 日。

词汇28th July 7 月 28 日；序数词 28th 读作 twenty-eighth 语法具体日期用 on；英语日期可写成“日 + 月”。

I usually have a party with my friends to celebrate.

我通常和朋友们举办派对来庆祝。

词汇usually 通常；have a party 举办派对；to celebrate 为了庆祝 语法usually 放在实义动词前；to celebrate 作目的状语。

My mother always makes my favourite chocolate cake.

我妈妈总是做我最喜欢的巧克力蛋糕。

词汇always 总是；favourite 最喜欢的；chocolate cake 巧克力蛋糕 语法always 位于实义动词前；make sth. for sb. 为某人做某物。

Everyone sings the "Happy Birthday" song, and then I make a wish and blow out the candles.

大家唱《生日快乐》歌，然后我许愿并吹灭蜡烛。

词汇everyone 每个人；sing 唱；make a wish 许愿；blow out 吹灭；candle 蜡烛 语法everyone 作主语视为第三人称单数，谓语用 sings；and then 表示顺承。

Next, we enjoy the cake, and I open all my gifts.

接下来，我们享用蛋糕，我打开所有礼物。

词汇enjoy 享用；gift 礼物；next 接下来 语法next 作连接词引出下一步；and 连接两个并列分句。

My father marks my height on the door every year.

我爸爸每年在门上标我的身高。

词汇mark 标记；height 身高；on the door 在门上 语法marks 是第三人称单数形式；mark 在此作动词，意为“标记”。

It's fun to look at the marks later.

以后看看这些记号很有趣。

词汇fun 有趣的；look at 看；later 以后 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；later 表示“以后”。

Next time, I also want to do something different.

下次，我也想做一些不一样的事。

词汇next time 下次；also 也；something different 不一样的事 语法something different 中形容词 different 后置修饰 something。

Any good ideas?

有什么好主意吗？

词汇any 任何；idea 主意 语法省略问句：(Do you have) any good ideas? 用于征求意见。

语法点 Grammar

My birthday is on 13th June.

具体日期用介词 on；序数词 13th 读作 thirteenth。

On that day, I always eat birthday noodles with eggs.

一般现在时 + always 表示习惯；with eggs 表示“加鸡蛋”。

Long noodles are a symbol of long life.

a symbol of ... 表示“……的象征”；主语 Long noodles 是复数，用 are。

Every year, my family and I take a photo together under the big tree in our village.

Every year 作时间状语；my family and I 是并列主语，谓语用 take。

I like taking photos because they help me remember fun times with my family.

like doing sth. 喜欢做某事；because 引导原因状语从句；help sb. do sth.。

This year, I want to plant a small tree for my 14th birthday.

want to do sth. 想要做某事；for + 生日表示“为……（生日）”。

I'd like to watch it grow with me.

I'd like to = I would like to；watch sth. do sth. 表示“看着某物做某事”。

When is your birthday?

When 询问时间；birthday 前用形容词性物主代词 your。

It's a great idea to plant a tree for your birthday!

It's + 名词 + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语，不定式是真正的主语。

My birthday is on 28th July.

具体日期用 on；序数词 28th 读作 twenty-eighth。

I usually have a party with my friends to celebrate.

usually 放在实义动词前；to celebrate 作目的状语。

My mother always makes my favourite chocolate cake.

always 位于实义动词前；make sth. for sb. 为某人做某物。

Everyone sings the "Happy Birthday" song, and then I make a wish and blow out the candles.

everyone 作主语视为第三人称单数，谓语用 sings；and then 表示顺承。

My father marks my height on the door every year.

marks 是第三人称单数；on the door 表示位置；mark 作动词意为“标记”。

It's fun to look at the marks later.

It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；later 表示“以后”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I like taking photos because they help me remember fun times with my family.

我喜欢拍照，因为它们帮我记住与家人一起的快乐时光。

like doing sth. 表示“喜欢做某事”；they 指代 photos；help sb. do sth. 中 do 前省略 to。

It's a great idea to plant a tree for your birthday!

为你的生日种一棵树真是个好主意！

It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to plant a tree；也可理解为 a great idea to do sth.（做某事的好主意）。

Everyone sings the "Happy Birthday" song, and then I make a wish and blow out the candles.

大家唱《生日快乐》歌，然后我许愿并吹灭蜡烛。

everyone 视为单数，谓语用 sings；make a wish 和 blow out the candles 是生日庆祝的固定搭配。

Reading Plus · Three Birthday Cards

Reading Plus 主题：生日贺卡 · 亲情与友情

My dear son,

我亲爱的儿子：

词汇dear 亲爱的；son 儿子 语法贺卡的称呼语：My dear + 称呼，后面用逗号。

It's so wonderful to watch you grow up.

看着你长大真是太美好了。

词汇wonderful 精彩的；watch 观看；grow up 长大 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；watch sb. do sth. 表示“看着某人做某事”。

I like to see your smile every day!

我喜欢每天看到你的笑容！

词汇smile 微笑；every day 每天 语法like to do sth. 表示“喜欢做某事”；every day 作时间状语。

Thank you for bringing joy to the family.

谢谢你给这个家带来欢乐。

词汇thank you for 因……而感谢；joy 欢乐；family 家庭 语法Thank you for doing sth. 感谢对方做了某事；bring ... to ... 把……带给……。

Sometimes you do drive me up the wall with your loud music.

有时候你放的大声音乐真让我受不了。

词汇sometimes 有时；drive sb. up the wall 让某人受不了；loud 大声的 语法句中的 do 用于强调谓语动词；drive sb. up the wall 是习语。

But I wouldn't change a thing about you.

但我不会改变你身上的任何一点。

词汇wouldn't = would not；change 改变；a thing 一点 语法wouldn't 是 would not 的缩写；about you 作后置定语修饰 a thing。

I love you so much.

我非常爱你。

词汇love 爱；so much 非常 语法so much 表示程度，修饰动词 love。

Happy birthday!

生日快乐！

词汇happy 快乐的；birthday 生日 语法省略句：(I wish you a) happy birthday! 表示生日祝福。

Love you,

爱你的，

词汇love you 爱你 语法贺卡结束语，相当于“爱你的”，后面另起署名。

Dad

爸爸

词汇dad 爸爸 语法贺卡署名，前面结束语后用逗号，署名另起一行。

Dear Penny,

亲爱的彭妮：

词汇dear 亲爱的；Penny 彭妮（人名） 语法写给朋友的贺卡称呼语：Dear + 名字，后面用逗号。

You laugh all the time, and you have no worries—you're everything I want in a friend.

你总是笑，没有烦恼——你具备我想要的朋友应有的一切。

词汇laugh 笑；all the time 一直；worry 担心；烦恼；everything 一切 语法一般现在时描述一贯的性格；破折号后的 you're everything I want in a friend 进一步说明。

Don't ever change!

永远不要改变！

词汇don't = do not；ever 曾经；永远 语法祈使句的否定形式：Don't + 动词原形；ever 加强语气。

Stay as amazing as you are, my dear friend.

保持你现在的出色样子，我亲爱的朋友。

词汇stay 保持；amazing 令人惊叹的；as ... as 和……一样 语法as + 形容词 + as 表示“和……一样”；Stay + 形容词 表示“保持……”。

Always remember that if you fall, I'll pick you up.

永远记住：如果你摔倒了，我会扶你起来。

词汇always 永远；remember 记住；if 如果；fall 摔倒；pick sb. up 扶起某人 语法remember + that 宾语从句；if 引导条件状语从句，主句用一般将来时。

Happy birthday, and stay happy every day!

生日快乐，每天都开开心心！

词汇stay happy 保持快乐；every day 每天 语法and 连接两个祝福；Stay + 形容词 表示“保持……”。

Your best friend forever,

你永远的好朋友，

词汇best friend 最好的朋友；forever 永远 语法贺卡结束语：Your best friend forever, 后面另起署名。

Tina

蒂娜

词汇Tina 蒂娜（人名） 语法贺卡署名。

To my wonderful mum,

献给我了不起的妈妈：

词汇to 给；献给；wonderful 了不起的；mum 妈妈 语法贺卡开头用 To + 收卡人，表示“献给……”，后面用逗号。

I wish you a happy birthday!

祝你生日快乐！

词汇wish 祝愿；happy birthday 生日快乐 语法wish + 人 + 名词 表示祝愿；也可说 Wish you a happy birthday!

Thank you so much for taking care of me and loving me every day.

非常感谢你每天照顾我、爱我。

词汇thank you for 因……而感谢；take care of 照顾；every day 每天 语法Thank you for doing sth.；for 后接两个并列的动名词短语。

You're such a beautiful and kind woman.

你是这样一位美丽又善良的女性。

词汇such 这样的；beautiful 美丽的；kind 善良的；woman 女性 语法such + a/an + 形容词 + 名词；You're = You are。

I want to be just like you when I grow up.

我长大后想成为像你一样的人。

词汇want to be 想要成为；just like 就像；grow up 长大 语法want to be 想要成为；when 引导时间状语从句，主句用一般现在时表意愿。

Your loving daughter,

爱你的女儿，

词汇loving 充满爱的；daughter 女儿 语法贺卡结束语：Your loving daughter, 后面另起署名。

Amy

艾米

词汇Amy 艾米（人名） 语法贺卡署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P81 的三张生日贺卡，按版面顺序依次呈现。爸爸写给儿子的卡片说：看着他长大是一件美好的事，他每天的笑容给家庭带来欢乐；虽然有时儿子大声放音乐让他受不了，但他不会改变儿子身上的任何一点，他非常爱儿子，并祝他生日快乐。Tina 写给 Penny 的卡片说：Penny 总是笑，没有烦恼，是 Tina 心目中朋友应有的一切；希望她永远不要改变，保持现在的出色；并告诉她，如果她摔倒了，自己会扶她起来，自己永远是她最好的朋友。Amy 写给妈妈的卡片说：祝妈妈生日快乐，感谢妈妈每天照顾她、爱她；妈妈美丽善良，她长大后想像妈妈一样，落款是“爱你的女儿 Amy”。

English Summary

These are three birthday cards on page 81, presented in the order they appear on the page. In the card from Dad to his son, the father says it is wonderful to watch his son grow up and that he likes to see his smile every day. He thanks his son for bringing joy to the family; sometimes the loud music drives him up the wall, but he wouldn't change a thing about his son, and he loves him so much. In the card from Tina to Penny, Tina says Penny laughs all the time and has no worries, and she is everything Tina wants in a friend. She asks Penny never to change and to stay as amazing as she is, promises to pick her up if she falls, and says she is her best friend forever. In the card from Amy to her mum, Amy wishes her mum a happy birthday and thanks her for taking care of her and loving her every day. She says her mum is a beautiful and kind woman, and she wants to be just like her when she grows up.

1. When is Yu Xiaoming's birthday?

  1. On 28th July.
  2. On 13th June.
  3. On 8th June.
  4. On 9th January.
答案与解析

B 原文：My birthday is on 13th June. 可知于晓明的生日是 6 月 13 日。

2. What does Yu Xiaoming want to do for his 14th birthday?

  1. Plant a small tree.
  2. Take a photo with his family.
  3. Eat birthday noodles with eggs.
  4. Make a birthday cake for his family.
答案与解析

A 原文：This year, I want to plant a small tree for my 14th birthday. I'd like to watch it grow with me. 可知他想在 14 岁生日时种一棵小树。

3. When is Judy Clark's birthday?

  1. On 9th June.
  2. On 8th June.
  3. On 28th July.
  4. On 13th June.
答案与解析

C 原文：By: Judy Clark on 09/06 7:54 PM ... My birthday is on 28th July. 注意 09/06 是发帖日期，她的生日是 7 月 28 日。

4. What's the total price at the shop?

  2. Six yuan.
  3. Five yuan.
  4. 165 yuan.

D 原文：Teng Fei: What's the total? Woman: 165 yuan. 可知总价是 165 元。

6. Why does the father say he wouldn't change a thing about his son?
