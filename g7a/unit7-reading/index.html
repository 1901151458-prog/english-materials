Section A 3a · When Is Your Birthday?

Section A 3a 主题：生日与日期 · 同学对话

Kate: Hi, Jack. When is your birthday?

嗨，杰克。你的生日是什么时候？

词汇when 什么时候；birthday 生日 语法When is your birthday? 询问生日；birthday 前用 your。

Jack: It's on May 1st.

是五月一日。

词汇on May 1st 在五月一日 语法具体某一天用 on + 日期；序数词 1st = first。

Kate: May 1st? That's this Sunday!

五月一日？就是这周日啊！

词汇Sunday 星期日 语法this Sunday 表示“这个周日”。

Jack: How old are you, Kate?

凯特，你多大了？

词汇how old 多大（年龄） 语法How old are you? 询问年龄。

Kate: I'm twelve. My birthday is in August.

我十二岁。我的生日在八月。

词汇August 八月；twelve 十二 语法in + 月份；生日在某月用 in。

Jack: Do you have a birthday party every year?

你每年都办生日派对吗？

词汇party 派对；every year 每年 语法every year 表频率；Do you...? 一般疑问句。

Kate: Yes, I do. My friends come to my home.

是的。我的朋友们来我家。

词汇come to 来到 语法动词短语 come to + 地点。

Jack: What do you usually do at the party?

你们在派对上通常做什么？

词汇usually 通常；at the party 在派对上 语法What do you usually do...? 询问习惯性活动。

Kate: We usually sing songs and eat a big cake.

我们通常唱歌、吃大蛋糕。

词汇sing songs 唱歌；cake 蛋糕 语法usually 位于实义动词前。

Jack: That sounds great. I had a great party last year.

听起来很棒。去年我过了一个很棒的生日。

词汇last year 去年；have a party 办派对 语法一般过去时 had 表示过去发生的事。

Kate: What did you get for your birthday?

你生日收到了什么？

词汇get 得到；for your birthday 作为生日礼物 语法一般过去时疑问句 What did...?。

Jack: I got a new bike. I ride it to school every day.

我得到了一辆新自行车。我每天骑车上学。

词汇bike 自行车；ride 骑 语法一般过去时 got 与一般现在时 every day 对比。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话是凯特和杰克聊生日。凯特问杰克的生日是什么时候，杰克说是五月一日，凯特说那正是这周日。杰克接着问凯特多大了，凯特说自己十二岁，生日在八月。杰克问她是不是每年都办生日派对，凯特说会，朋友们会来她家，大家通常唱歌、吃大蛋糕。杰克说去年他也过了一个很棒的生日，凯特问他收到了什么礼物，杰克说是一辆新自行车，现在每天骑车上学。

English Summary

The conversation is about birthdays. Kate asks Jack when his birthday is, and he says it is on May 1st, which happens to be this Sunday. Jack then asks how old Kate is; she says she is twelve and her birthday is in August. Jack asks whether she has a birthday party every year, and Kate says yes—her friends come to her home and they usually sing songs and eat a big cake. Jack says he had a great party last year, and when Kate asks what he got, he says he got a new bike and rides it to school every day now.

语法点 Grammar

When is your birthday?

When 询问时间；birthday 前用 your。

It's on May 1st.

具体某一天用介词 on；May 1st 读作 May the first。

That's this Sunday!

this + 星期 表示“这个星期几”。

How old are you?

How old 询问年龄；be 动词随主语变化。

My birthday is in August.

in + 月份，表示“在某月”；只说月份用 in，具体到某天用 on。

Do you have a birthday party every year?

Do you...? 一般疑问句；every year 表频率。

What do you usually do at the party?

usually 位于实义动词前；at the party 表地点。

We usually sing songs and eat a big cake.

and 连接并列谓语。

I had a great party last year.

一般过去时 had；last year 是与过去时连用的时间状语。

What did you get for your birthday?

一般过去时特殊疑问句：What did + 主语 + 动词原形...?

I got a new bike.

一般过去时 got 讲过去发生的事。

I ride it to school every day.

一般现在时讲现在的习惯，与上句的过去时形成对比。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It's on May 1st.

是五月一日。

具体到某一天用 on；只说月份时用 in，如 in May。日期中的 1st 读作 first。

What did you get for your birthday?

你生日收到了什么？

一般过去时特殊疑问句用 What did + 主语 + 动词原形；for your birthday 表示“作为生日礼物”。

I got a new bike. I ride it to school every day.

我得到了一辆新自行车。我每天骑车上学。

同一段话里过去时讲过去的经历，现在时讲现在的习惯，注意时态区分。

Section B 1b · My Thirteenth Birthday

Section B 1b 主题：生日经历 · 记叙短文

Last Sunday was my thirteenth birthday, and it was a wonderful day.

上周日是我十三岁的生日，那是美好的一天。

词汇thirteenth 第十三；wonderful 美好的 语法was 是 be 的过去式；序数词前用 the。

My birthday is on October 12th, so it was on a Sunday this year.

我的生日是十月十二日，所以今年正好是周日。

词汇October 12th 十月十二日；so 所以 语法具体日期用 on；so 表结果。

In the morning, my parents gave me a beautiful present.

早上，爸爸妈妈给了我一份漂亮的礼物。

词汇present 礼物；beautiful 漂亮的 语法一般过去时 gave 是 give 的过去式。

It was a box of coloured pens, because I love drawing.

是一盒彩色笔，因为我喜欢画画。

词汇coloured 彩色的；because 因为 语法一般过去时 was；because 引导原因状语从句。

At noon, my best friend Lily came to my home.

中午，我最好的朋友莉莉来到我家。

词汇best friend 最好的朋友；noon 中午 语法一般过去时 came 是 come 的过去式。

She brought me a birthday card and a small cake.

她给我带来了生日卡片和小蛋糕。

词汇bring 带来；card 卡片 语法一般过去时 brought 是 bring 的过去式。

In the afternoon, we went to the park and flew a kite.

下午，我们去了公园放风筝。

词汇go 去；fly a kite 放风筝 语法一般过去时 went 是 go 的过去式；and 连接并列谓语。

We ran on the grass and took many photos together.

我们在草地上奔跑，一起拍了很多照片。

词汇run 跑；take photos 拍照 语法一般过去时 ran / took。

In the evening, my family had a big dinner.

晚上，我们一家人吃了丰盛的晚餐。

词汇evening 晚上；have dinner 吃晚饭 语法一般过去时 had。

My mother made noodles for me, because long noodles mean a long life.

妈妈给我做了面条，因为长面条象征着长寿。

词汇noodle 面条；mean 意味着 语法一般过去时 made；because 引导原因。

We also sang the birthday song and ate a cake with thirteen candles.

我们还唱了生日歌，吃了一个插着十三根蜡烛的蛋糕。

词汇candle 蜡烛；sing a song 唱歌 语法一般过去时 sang / ate。

I blew out the candles and made a wish.

我吹灭了蜡烛，许了一个愿。

词汇blow out 吹灭；make a wish 许愿 语法blow out / make a wish 是生日常用短语。

Now I am thirteen years old, and I hope next year will be even better.

现在我十三岁了，希望明年会更好。

词汇even better 更好 语法现在一般现在时 I am；next year 表将来。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文讲作者十三岁生日的经历。上周日就是他的生日，因为生日是十月十二日，今年正好碰上周日。早上爸爸妈妈送他一盒彩色笔，因为他喜欢画画；中午最好的朋友莉莉来他家，送给他生日卡片和小蛋糕；下午他们去公园放风筝、在草地上奔跑，还一起拍了很多照片；晚上全家吃丰盛的晚餐，妈妈给他做面条，因为长面条象征长寿，大家还唱生日歌、吃插着十三根蜡烛的蛋糕。他吹灭蜡烛许了愿，希望明年更好。

English Summary

The passage tells the story of the writer's thirteenth birthday. Last Sunday was his birthday, and since his birthday is on October 12th, it fell on a Sunday this year. In the morning his parents gave him a beautiful present—a box of coloured pens, because he loves drawing. At noon his best friend Lily came to his home with a birthday card and a small cake. In the afternoon they went to the park and flew a kite, ran on the grass and took many photos together. In the evening the family had a big dinner; his mother made noodles for him because long noodles mean a long life, and they sang the birthday song and ate a cake with thirteen candles. He blew out the candles and made a wish, hoping next year would be even better.

语法点 Grammar

Last Sunday was my thirteenth birthday.

was 是 be 的过去式；序数词 thirteenth 前用 my。

My birthday is on October 12th.

具体日期前用 on。

My parents gave me a beautiful present.

一般过去时 gave 是 give 的过去式；give sb. sth.。

It was a box of coloured pens, because I love drawing.

一般过去时 was；because 引导原因状语从句；love + 动名词。

My best friend Lily came to my home.

一般过去时 came 是 come 的过去式。

She brought me a birthday card.

bring sb. sth.；过去式 brought。

We went to the park and flew a kite.

一般过去时 went / flew；and 连接并列谓语。

My family had a big dinner.

过去式 had 是 have 的过去式。

My mother made noodles for me, because long noodles mean a long life.

过去式 made；because 引导原因状语从句；从句用一般现在时表普遍看法。

We sang the birthday song and ate a cake with thirteen candles.

过去式 sang / ate；with 表示“带有”。

I blew out the candles and made a wish.

过去式 blew / made；blow out 是“吹灭”。

Now I am thirteen years old.

一般现在时 I am 描述现在状态，与全文过去时形成对比。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

My birthday is on October 12th, so it was on a Sunday this year.

我的生日是十月十二日，所以今年正好是周日。

句中有两个时间层级：生日用一般现在时 is 表事实，今年发生用 was；月份日期用 on。

My mother made noodles for me, because long noodles mean a long life.

妈妈给我做了面条，因为长面条象征着长寿。

made 是过去式；because 引导原因状语从句；mean 在从句中用一般现在时表普遍看法。

I blew out the candles and made a wish.

我吹灭了蜡烛，许了一个愿。

blew 是 blow 的过去式，made 是 make 的过去式；blow out 为“动词 + 副词”短语。

Section B 3a · A Birthday Invitation

Section B 3a 主题：生日邀请 · 学生习作（书信）

Dear Emma,

亲爱的艾玛，

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

How are you? I am writing to invite you to my birthday party.

你好吗？我写信是想邀请你参加我的生日派对。

词汇invite 邀请；birthday party 生日派对 语法现在进行时表示写信的当前动作；invite sb. to sth.。

My birthday is on November 6th, and the party is in the evening.

我的生日是十一月六日，派对在晚上举行。

词汇November 6th 十一月六日；in the evening 在晚上 语法具体日期用 on；in the evening 表时间段。

It starts at six o'clock at my home.

派对六点在我家开始。

词汇start 开始；at six o'clock 在六点 语法at + 具体钟点。

We will sing songs, play games and eat a big cake.

我们会唱歌、玩游戏、吃大蛋糕。

词汇play games 玩游戏 语法will + 动词原形表示将来。

My mum makes wonderful food every year, and everyone loves it.

我妈妈每年都做美味的食物，大家都喜欢。

词汇wonderful 美味的；every year 每年 语法一般现在时表习惯；第三人称单数 makes。

Please come in your favourite clothes, and you can bring your little sister.

请穿上你最喜欢的衣服来，你也可以带上你妹妹。

词汇clothes 衣服；bring 带来 语法祈使句 + and；in + 衣服 表示穿着；can + 动词原形。

Last year, you gave me a great present, and I still use it every day.

去年你送了我一份很棒的礼物，我现在还每天用着。

词汇still 仍然 语法一般过去时 gave 与一般现在时 use 对比。

I hope you will come.

我希望你能来。

词汇hope 希望 语法hope + 宾语从句，从句用 will。

Please tell me before Friday, so I can buy enough food.

请在周五之前告诉我，这样我就能买足够的食物。

词汇enough 足够的；so 所以 语法so 引导结果；can + 动词原形。

I am sure we will have a good time together.

我相信我们会一起玩得开心。

词汇be sure 确信；have a good time 玩得开心 语法be sure + 宾语从句。

Yours,

你的朋友，

词汇yours 你的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Li Na

李娜

词汇— 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是李娜写给艾玛的生日邀请信。她先问好，说明写信是为了邀请艾玛参加生日派对；她是十一月六日生日，派对在晚上举行，六点在她家开始。她介绍了派对内容：唱歌、玩游戏、吃大蛋糕；还说妈妈每年都做美味的食物，大家都很喜欢，请艾玛穿上最喜欢的衣服来，也可以带上妹妹。她又回忆去年艾玛送了她一份很棒的礼物，她现在每天还在用，希望艾玛这次也能来。最后请艾玛在周五之前回复，以便准备足够的食物。

English Summary

This is Li Na's birthday invitation letter to Emma. She greets Emma and explains that she is writing to invite her to her birthday party. Her birthday is on November 6th, and the party is in the evening, starting at six o'clock at her home. She describes what they will do—sing songs, play games and eat a big cake—and says her mum makes wonderful food every year, which everyone loves. She asks Emma to come in her favourite clothes and says she can bring her little sister. Li Na remembers that Emma gave her a great present last year, which she still uses every day, and hopes she will come. Finally she asks Emma to tell her before Friday so she can buy enough food.

My birthday is on November 6th.

具体日期前用 on。

The party is in the evening.

in + 时间段（in the morning / evening）。

It starts at six o'clock at my home.

at + 具体钟点；第三人称单数 starts。

We will sing songs and eat a big cake.

will + 动词原形表示将来。

My mum makes wonderful food every year.

一般现在时表习惯；第三人称单数 makes。

Please come in your favourite clothes, and you can bring your little sister.

祈使句 + and 引导结果；in + 衣服表示穿着；can + 动词原形。

一般过去时 gave 讲过去，一般现在时 use 讲现在，形成对比。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I am writing to invite you to my birthday party.

我写信是想邀请你参加我的生日派对。

现在进行时表当前动作；不定式 to invite 作目的状语；invite sb. to sth. 表示“邀请某人参加某事”。

Last year, you gave me a great present, and I still use it every day.

去年你送了我一份很棒的礼物，我现在还每天用着。

前半句用一般过去时 gave 讲去年的事，后半句用一般现在时 use 讲现在的习惯。

Please tell me before Friday, so I can buy enough food.

请在周五之前告诉我，这样我就能买足够的食物。

祈使句提出请求；so 引导结果；before 后接时间点，不必加介词。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看信件格式，找出称呼语与落款。
  • 回忆邀请信要写清哪些要素：时间、地点、活动。

读中

  • 找出派对的日期、时间、地点和活动。
  • 画出 on / in / at 引出的时间短语。

读后

  • 仿照 3a 写一封生日邀请信。
  • 用一句话概括邀请信的三要素。
