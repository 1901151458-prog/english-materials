Section A 3a · Welcome to Our Yard!

Section A 3a 主题：欢迎来参观 · 院子里的问答对话

A: Welcome to our school yard!

欢迎来到我们学校的院子！

词汇welcome 欢迎；yard 院子；园圃 语法Welcome to + 地点! 欢迎来到某地。

B: Thank you! It's a big and beautiful yard.

谢谢！这是个又大又漂亮的院子。

词汇big 大的；beautiful 美丽的 语法主系表结构：It's + 形容词。

A: Look at the animals over there.

看那边的动物。

词汇look at 看；瞧；animal 动物 语法祈使句 Look at...!；over there 在那边。

B: Oh, I see a goose and some ducks.

哦，我看见一只鹅和几只鸭子。

词汇goose 鹅；duck 鸭子 语法I see... 描述看到的东西；and 连接并列宾语。

A: How many geese can you see?

你能看见多少只鹅？

词汇how many 多少 语法How many + 可数名词复数 can you see? 询问数量。

B: I can see two geese. One is white and one is grey.

我能看见两只鹅。一只是白色的，一只是灰色的。

词汇grey 灰色的；white 白色的 语法one...and one... 逐一说明。

A: Good! Now look at the vegetables here.

很好！现在看看这里的蔬菜。

词汇vegetable 蔬菜 语法祈使句 Look at...；here 在这里，与 over there 对照。

B: I can see carrots and tomatoes.

我能看见胡萝卜和西红柿。

词汇carrot 胡萝卜；tomato 西红柿 语法可数名词复数：carrots、tomatoes。

A: How many carrots are there?

那儿有多少根胡萝卜？

词汇there be 有 语法How many + 名词复数 + are there? 询问某处数量。

B: There are five carrots. I count one, two, three, four, five.

有五根胡萝卜。我数一、二、三、四、五。

词汇count 数数 语法There are + 复数；count 后逐个数。

A: Great! Count them and circle the carrots.

很好！数一数，把胡萝卜圈出来。

词汇circle 圈出 语法两个祈使句并列：Count... and circle...。

B: Look, I circle all the carrots. What else can you see?

你看，我把所有胡萝卜都圈出来了。其他你还能看到什么？

词汇else 其他的；别的；what else 还有什么 语法What else can you see? 询问其他的东西。

A: I can see another goose behind the tree. We have a lot of fun here!

我能看见树后还有一只鹅。我们在这里玩得很开心！

词汇another 另一；又一；fun 乐趣；快乐 语法another + 单数名词；have fun 玩得开心。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一段参观院子的对话。一方欢迎对方来到学校院子，并请对方看那边的动物；对方看见一只鹅和几只鸭子。问“你能看见多少只鹅”时，回答说看见两只，一只白色、一只灰色。接着两人看蔬菜，对方看见胡萝卜和西红柿；问“那儿有多少根胡萝卜”，回答说有五根，并一根根数出来。随后请他数一数并圈出胡萝卜，他圈出了全部胡萝卜，又问“其他你还能看到什么”，最后对方说树后还有一只鹅，大家都在院子里玩得很开心。

English Summary

This is a conversation about visiting a yard. One speaker welcomes the other to the school yard and asks him to look at the animals over there. The other sees a goose and some ducks. Asked how many geese there are, he says two, one white and one grey. Then they look at the vegetables, and he sees carrots and tomatoes. There are five carrots, and he counts them one, two, three, four, five. He is asked to count them and circle the carrots, and he circles all of them. Then he asks what else can be seen, and the first speaker says there is another goose behind the tree and that they have a lot of fun here.

语法点 Grammar

Welcome to our school yard!

Welcome to + 地点，表示欢迎来到某地。

It's a big and beautiful yard.

主系表结构：It's + 形容词。

Look at the animals over there.

祈使句用动词原形开头；Look at... 表示“看……”。

I see a goose and some ducks.

a goose 单数；some + 可数名词复数。

How many geese can you see?

How many + 可数名词复数；can you see 提问能力。

Now look at the vegetables here.

祈使句；here 与 over there 对照表地点。

How many carrots are there?

There be 句型提问数量：How many + 复数 + are there?。

There are five carrots.

There are + 可数名词复数。

Count them and circle the carrots.

两个祈使句用 and 并列；them 指代前文的 carrots。

What else can you see?

else 放在 what 之后，询问其他事物。

I can see another goose behind the tree.

another + 可数名词单数，表示“又一只”。

We have a lot of fun here!

have fun 玩得开心；fun 作不可数名词。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

How many geese can you see? / How many carrots are there?

你能看见多少只鹅？/ 那儿有多少根胡萝卜？

How many 后接可数名词复数（goose→geese）；can you see 问“你能看见”，are there 问“那里有”，两种问法都要会答。

Count them and circle the carrots.

数一数，把胡萝卜圈出来。

祈使句用动词原形开头，and 并列两个动作；them 是宾格代词，替代前面的复述名词 carrots。

I can see another goose behind the tree.

我能看见树后还有一只鹅。

another 表示“又一只、另一只”，后接可数名词单数而不用复数是本单元易错点；behind the tree 是地点状语。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题 Welcome!，猜这段对话在什么地方发生。
  • 认识本单元的关键词：yard、goose、carrot、count、circle。

读中

  • 边读边找出“看了什么”“数了多少”两类信息。
  • 圈出 How many...? 和 What else...? 的问句，看看对方是怎么回答的。

读后

  • 两人一组模仿对话，用 Look at... 请对方看图说话。
  • 数一数教室里的东西，用 How many...are there? 提问并回答。

Section B 1b · My Small Yard

Section B 1b 主题：院子里的东西 · 短文

Welcome to my home! This is our small yard.

欢迎来我家！这是我们的小院子。

词汇home 家；small 小的 语法Welcome to...!；This is... 介绍事物。

There is a big tree in the yard, and a goose is under it.

院子里有一棵大树，树下有一只鹅。

词汇there is 有；under 在……下面 语法There is + 单数名词，就近原则。

Look at the carrots in the garden.

看园子里的胡萝卜。

词汇look at 看；garden 菜园 语法祈使句；介词短语 in the garden 后置修饰。

Some of them are big and orange.

其中一些又大又橙。

词汇orange 橙色的；some of them 其中一些 语法some of + 复数 作主语，谓语用复数 are。

How many carrots are there? Let's count them.

那儿有多少根胡萝卜？我们来数一数。

词汇count 数数 语法How many...are there? 询问数量；Let's do sth. 提建议。

There are seven carrots, and I count them one by one.

有七根胡萝卜，我一根一根地数。

词汇one by one 一个接一个 语法There are + 复数；count one by one 逐个计数。

Now circle the carrots in the picture.

现在把图片里的胡萝卜圈出来。

词汇circle 圈出；picture 图片 语法祈使句 Circle + 名词。

Can you see another one under the tree?

你能看见树下的另一根吗？

词汇another 另一；又一 语法another + 单数名词，表示“再一个”。

Yes, I can! What else can you see in my yard?

是的，我能！在我家院子里你还能看到什么？

词汇else 其他的；别的 语法What else...? 询问其他事物。

There are some white geese near the fence.

栅栏边有几只白色的鹅。

词汇fence 栅栏；near 在……附近 语法There are + 复数名词；near the fence 表地点。

Look at them! They are walking slowly and singing loudly.

看它们！它们正慢慢走着，大声地叫着。

词汇walk 走；slowly 慢慢地；loudly 大声地 语法Look at...! 祈使句；现在进行时表示正在发生。

We count the carrots and the geese every day, and we have a lot of fun.

我们每天数胡萝卜和鹅，玩得很开心。

词汇every day 每天；have fun 玩得开心 语法一般现在时表示习惯；and 连接并列分句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍“我”家的小院子。作者先欢迎大家来家里，说院子里有一棵大树，树下一只鹅。接着请大家看菜园里的胡萝卜，其中一些又大又橙；问那儿有多少根胡萝卜，然后一根一根数出七根，并请大家把图片里的胡萝卜圈出来，还问对方能否看见树下另一根。最后说栅栏边有几只白鹅，它们正慢慢走着、大声叫着；作者每天数胡萝卜和鹅，玩得很开心。

English Summary

This passage introduces the writer's small yard. The writer welcomes you to the home and says there is a big tree in the yard with a goose under it. Then he asks you to look at the carrots in the garden; some of them are big and orange. He asks how many carrots there are and counts seven of them one by one, asks you to circle the carrots in the picture and whether you can see another one under the tree. Finally there are some white geese near the fence, walking slowly and singing loudly, and every day the writer counts the carrots and the geese and has a lot of fun.

语法点 Grammar

Welcome to my home!

Welcome to + 地点。

There is a big tree in the yard.

There is + 可数名词单数，表示“有”。

Look at the carrots in the garden.

Look at... 祈使句；in the garden 作后置定语。

Some of them are big and orange.

some of them 作主语，谓语用复数 are。

How many carrots are there?

There be 句型提问数量。

Let's count them.

Let's + 动词原形，表示提建议或邀请。

There are seven carrots.

There are + 可数名词复数。

Can you see another one under the tree?

another + 单数名词，表示“另一个”。

What else can you see in my yard?

What else...? 询问其他事物；else 位于 what 之后。

Look at them! They are walking slowly and singing loudly.

Look at + 宾格代词；现在进行时表示正在发生。

We count the carrots and the geese every day.

一般现在时表示习惯；every day 作时间状语。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

There is a big tree in the yard, and a goose is under it.

院子里有一棵大树，树下有一只鹅。

There is 表示“某地有某物”；it 指代前面的 a big tree。

How many carrots are there? Let's count them one by one.

那儿有多少根胡萝卜？我们来一根一根地数。

How many 后接复数 carrots；one by one 是“一个接一个地”的方式状语。

Can you see another one under the tree?

你能看见树下的另一根吗？

another one 中 one 替代 carrot，避免重复；another 后接单数。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题 My Small Yard，猜短文会介绍哪些东西。
  • 先找出院子里的动物和蔬菜名称。

读中

  • 边读边数：文中共有多少根胡萝卜、多少只鹅？
  • 圈出 There is、There are，比较后面接单数还是复数。

读后

  • 用“有什么—有多少—我做了什么”复述短文。
  • 画一画自己家的院子或教室，用英语介绍并数一数。

Section B 3a · Welcome to Our School Garden

Section B 3a 主题：校园菜园欢迎词 · 学生习作

Welcome to our school garden! Come and have a look.

欢迎来到我们学校的菜园！来看看吧。

词汇come and have a look 来看看 语法Welcome to...!；Come and do sth. 表示邀请。

Our garden is behind the red building.

我们的菜园在红色教学楼后面。

词汇behind 在……后面；building 楼房 语法主系表结构；behind... 表地点。

There is a small yard in it, and we plant carrots every spring.

里面有一个小院子，我们每年春天种胡萝卜。

词汇plant 种植；spring 春天 语法There is + 单数名词；every spring 表频率。

Look at the carrots. They are small and green now.

看这些胡萝卜。它们现在又小又绿。

词汇look at 看；green 绿色的 语法祈使句；主系表结构描述状态。

How many carrots can you count?

你能数出多少根胡萝卜？

词汇count 数数；how many 多少 语法How many + 复数名词 + can you count?。

There are twelve carrots in our small yard.

我们的小院子里有十二根胡萝卜。

词汇twelve 十二 语法There are + 数词 + 复数名词。

Let's count them together: one, two, three... twelve!

我们一起数：一、二、三……十二！

词汇together 一起 语法Let's do sth. 提建议；冒号后列举。

Now circle the carrots with your pencil.

现在用铅笔把胡萝卜圈出来。

词汇circle 圈出；pencil 铅笔 语法祈使句；with your pencil 表工具。

Is there another goose in the garden?

菜园里还有另一只鹅吗？

词汇another 另一；garden 菜园 语法Is there...? 一般疑问句询问是否存在。

Yes, there is. Look at the white goose near the gate.

是的，有。看大门边那只白鹅。

词汇gate 大门 语法Yes, there is. 简略回答；Look at... 祈使句。

Can you see anything else? Please write it down.

你还能看到别的东西吗？请把它写下来。

词汇anything else 其他任何东西；write down 写下 语法anything else 用于疑问句；祈使句 Please + 动词原形。

Welcome to our garden! We have a lot of fun here every day.

欢迎来我们的菜园！我们每天在这里玩得很开心。

词汇a lot of 许多；fun 乐趣 语法have a lot of fun 玩得开心；呼应文题。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇学生写的校园菜园欢迎词。作者先欢迎大家来学校菜园看看，说菜园在红色教学楼后面，里面有一个小院子，每年春天大家种胡萝卜。接着请大家看胡萝卜——现在又小又绿，并问能数出多少根，然后说小院里共有十二根，并一起数出来。之后请大家用铅笔把胡萝卜圈出来，问院子里还有没有另一只鹅，并说门前有一只白鹅。最后问还能看到什么别的东西、请写下来，并以“欢迎来我们的菜园，我们每天玩得很开心”收尾。

English Summary

This is a student's welcome speech to the school garden. The writer welcomes visitors to have a look and says the garden is behind the red building; there is a small yard in it, and they plant carrots every spring. Then the writer asks readers to look at the carrots, which are small and green now, asks how many they can count, says there are twelve carrots in the small yard and counts them together. After that the writer asks readers to circle the carrots with a pencil and whether there is another goose in the garden, and says there is a white goose near the gate. Finally the writer asks what else they can see and to write it down, and ends by welcoming everyone to the garden, where they have a lot of fun every day.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

How many 问数量，There are 回答数量；注意 carrots 用复数、are 用复数。

Can you see anything else? Please write it down.

你还能看到别的东西吗？请把它写下来。

else 放在 anything 之后；anything 用于疑问句和否定句；write sth. down 代词 it 要放在中间。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题和图片，猜这篇欢迎词带我们参观哪里。
  • 回忆 There is / There are 的区别。

读中

  • 找出菜园的位置和种了什么。
  • 圈出文中的祈使句（Look at...、Circle...、Come and...）。

读后

  • 仿照本文写一份欢迎词，带同学参观各自班级的某个地方。
  • 用 How many...are there? 采访同学并统计结果。

1. What does the boy see over there in Section A 3a?

  1. A goose and some ducks.
  2. Some cars.
  3. A big dog.
  4. Two cats.
答案与解析

A 原文：Oh, I see a goose and some ducks.

2. How many geese can the boy see in Section A 3a?

  1. One.
  2. Two.
  3. Three.
  4. Five.
答案与解析

B 原文：I can see two geese. One is white and one is grey.

3. How many carrots are there in Section A 3a?

  1. Three.
  2. Four.
  3. Five.
  4. Seven.
答案与解析

C 原文：There are five carrots. I count one, two, three, four, five.

4. What is behind the tree in Section A 3a?

  1. Another goose.
  2. A duck.
  3. A carrot.
  4. A big tree.
答案与解析

A 原文：I can see another goose behind the tree.

5. Where is the big tree in Section B 1b?

  1. In the yard.
  2. In the garden.
  3. Near the gate.
  4. Behind the building.
答案与解析

A 原文：There is a big tree in the yard.

6. How many carrots are there in Section B 1b?

  1. Five.
  2. Seven.
  3. Twelve.
  4. Two.
答案与解析

B 原文：There are seven carrots, and I count them one by one.

7. Where is the school garden in Section B 3a?

  3. In the reading room.
  4. Next to the library.
答案与解析

B 原文：Our garden is behind the red building.

8. What does the writer ask you to do with the carrots in Section B 3a?

  2. To water them.
  3. To circle them with a pencil.
  4. To put them in a box.
答案与解析
