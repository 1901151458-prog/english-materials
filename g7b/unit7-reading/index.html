Section A 3a · How Was Your School Trip?

Section A 3a 主题：谈论过去的学校旅行 · 对话

Tina: Hi, Ben. How was your school trip to the science museum yesterday?

嗨，本。你们昨天去科技馆的学校旅行怎么样？

词汇trip 旅行；museum 博物馆 语法How was...? 询问过去的情况，be 动词用过去式 was。

Ben: It was great! We saw a real robot and it could even talk.

太棒了！我们看到了一个真正的机器人，它甚至还会说话。

词汇real 真正的；robot 机器人；even 甚至 语法一般过去时；see 的过去式是 saw，can 的过去式是 could。

Tina: Wow! What did you do there?

哇！你们在那儿做了什么？

词汇there 在那里 语法did + 动词原形构成一般过去时的特殊疑问句。

Ben: First, a guide showed us around the space hall.

首先，一位讲解员带我们参观了太空展厅。

词汇guide 讲解员；show sb. around 带某人参观；space 太空；hall 展厅 语法show sb. around 带某人参观；序词 first 引出第一个活动。

Tina: That sounds cool. What did you see in the hall?

听起来很酷。你们在展厅里看到了什么？

词汇cool 酷的 语法过去时与现在时混用：现在评价用 sounds，过去动作问句用 did you see。

Ben: We wore special suits and tried to walk like astronauts.

我们穿上特制的衣服，试着像宇航员那样走路。

词汇wear 穿；try to do 试着做；astronaut 宇航员 语法wear 的过去式是 wore；try 的过去式是 tried（y 变 i 加 -ed）。

Tina: Were you good at it?

你擅长这个吗？

词汇be good at 擅长 语法be 动词过去式一般疑问句 Were you...?。

Ben: Not really! I fell over twice and everyone laughed.

不太行！我摔倒了两次，大家都笑了。

词汇fall over 摔倒；twice 两次；laugh 笑 语法fall 的过去式是 fell；一般过去时叙述过去的动作。

Tina: How funny! Did you buy anything there?

真有意思！你们在那儿买东西了吗？

词汇buy 买；anything 任何东西 语法Did you buy...? 一般过去时一般疑问句；anything 用于疑问句。

Ben: Yes, I did. I bought a small model rocket for my brother.

是的，我买了。我给弟弟买了一个小模型火箭。

词汇bought（buy 的过去式）；model 模型；rocket 火箭 语法buy 的过去式是 bought；for sb. 表示“给某人”。

Tina: Then what happened? Did you go straight home?

然后发生了什么？你们直接回家了吗？

词汇straight 直接地；happen 发生 语法Then 承接上文；happen 的过去式 happened。

Ben: After that, we waited for the bus, but it came an hour late, so we were all tired but happy.

之后，我们等公交车，可是车晚来了一小时，所以我们都很累但很开心。

词汇wait for 等待；late 晚的；tired 累的 语法but/so 连接分句，表达转折与结果；描述感受 happy。

Tina: Still, it was a day to remember!

不过，那真是难忘的一天！

词汇remember 记得 语法It was a day to remember. 是本单元主题句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一段对话。蒂娜问本昨天去科技馆的学校旅行怎么样，本说非常棒，他们看到了一个会说话的真正机器人。蒂娜用一般过去时追问他们在那里做了什么：先是一位讲解员带他们参观太空展厅；随后他们穿上特制衣服，试着像宇航员一样走路，本不太擅长，摔倒了两次，惹得大家哈哈大笑；本还给弟弟买了一个小模型火箭；之后他们等公交车，车晚到一小时，大家又累又开心。蒂娜最后说：这真是难忘的一天。

English Summary

This is a conversation between Tina and Ben. Tina asks Ben how his school trip to the science museum was yesterday, and Ben says it was great because they saw a real robot that could even talk. Tina asks what they did there, and Ben explains: first a guide showed them around the space hall; then they wore special suits and tried to walk like astronauts, and Ben fell over twice, which made everyone laugh. Ben also bought a small model rocket for his brother. After that they waited for the bus, but it came an hour late, so they were all tired but happy. Tina says it was a day to remember.

语法点 Grammar

How was your school trip to the science museum yesterday?

How was...? 用 be 动词过去式 was 询问过去的情况。

It was great! We saw a real robot.

一般过去时：be 动词 was 与实义动词 see 的过去式 saw。

It could even talk.

can 的过去式是 could，后接动词原形。

What did you do there?

一般过去时特殊疑问句：What + did + 主语 + 动词原形？

First, a guide showed us around the space hall.

show 的过去式 showed；show sb. around 带某人参观；first 引出第一个活动。

We wore special suits and tried to walk like astronauts.

wear 的过去式 wore；try 的过去式 tried，try to do sth. 试着做某事。

Were you good at it?

be 动词过去式一般疑问句：Were + 主语 + ...? 回答 Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.。

I fell over twice and everyone laughed.

fall 的过去式 fell；一般过去时并列谓语描述过去的动作。

Did you buy anything there?

Did + 主语 + 动词原形？疑问句中用 anything 而不用 something。

I bought a small model rocket for my brother.

buy 的过去式 bought；buy sth. for sb. 给某人买某物。

After that, we waited for the bus, but it came an hour late.

after that 表时间顺序；wait 的过去式 waited；but 表转折。

it was a day to remember

It was... 概括评价过去的一天；动词不定式 to remember 作后置定语。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

What did you do there? / First, a guide showed us around the space hall.

你们在那儿做了什么？首先，一位讲解员带我们参观了太空展厅。

一般过去时特殊疑问句用 did + 动词原形；回答用过去式 showed；show sb. around 是固定短语。

We wore special suits and tried to walk like astronauts.

我们穿上特制的衣服，试着像宇航员那样走路。

wear 是不规则动词，过去式 wore；try 是规则动词，过去式 tried（辅音字母 + y 变 i 加 -ed）。

After that, we waited for the bus, but it came an hour late, so we were all tired but happy.

之后，我们等公交车，可是车晚来了一小时，所以我们都很累但很开心。

after that 承接上文的动作顺序；but 表转折，so 表结果；tired but happy 用并列表语描写感受。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题和图片，猜这趟学校旅行去了哪里、看到了什么。
  • 回忆一般过去时，想好 be 动词和规则动词的过去式怎么写。

读中

  • 边读边在日历式时间线上标出 Ben 依次做了哪几件事。
  • 圈出所有过去式动词，并把不规则动词（saw、wore、fell、bought、came）单独记下。

读后

  • 两人一组，用一般过去时复述 Ben 的学校旅行。
  • 用 first、then、after that、finally 口头介绍自己的一次旅行。

Section B 1b · A Wonderful Trip to Green Lake

Section B 1b 主题：一次难忘的旅行 · 游记

Last Saturday, my family took a trip to Green Lake, a small village about two hours from our city.

上周六，我们一家人去绿湖旅行，那是离我们城市约两小时路程的小村庄。

词汇take a trip 去旅行；village 村庄 语法一般过去时；take 的过去式是 took。

First, we got up early, quickly finished breakfast, and then drove out of the city.

首先，我们早早起床，很快吃完早饭，然后开车出了城。

词汇get up 起床；drive out of 开出 语法get 过去式 got；first/then 交代时间顺序。

The road was busy at first, but after half an hour it became smooth and quiet.

一开始路上很挤，但半小时后变得平坦安静。

词汇road 道路；smooth 平坦的；quiet 安静的 语法become 的过去式是 became；but 表转折。

When we drove into the village, I saw a wide lake under the blue sky.

当我们开进村庄时，我看见蓝天下一片宽阔的湖。

词汇wide 宽阔的；lake 湖 语法when 引导时间状语从句；see 的过去式是 saw。

Then we rented a small boat and rowed slowly on the water.

然后我们租了一条小船，在水上慢慢划。

词汇rent 租；boat 船；row 划船 语法then 接着叙述；一般过去时描述连续动作。

While we were rowing, some small fish jumped out of the water near the boat.

我们划船的时候，一些小鱼在船边跳出水面。

词汇while 当……时；jump 跳 语法while 引导时间状语从句，常用过去进行时。

I was afraid at first, but my father told me to hold on and everything went well.

一开始我很害怕，但爸爸让我抓稳，一切都很顺利。

词汇afraid 害怕的；hold on 抓稳；go well 进展顺利 语法be afraid 表示感受；told 是 tell 的过去式，后接 sb. to do sth.。

After that, we had lunch in a small restaurant next to the lake.

之后，我们在湖边的一家小餐馆吃午饭。

词汇restaurant 餐馆；lunch 午饭 语法after that 承接上文；have 的过去式是 had。

The fish was fresh and delicious, and the old owner smiled at us warmly.

鱼很新鲜美味，老板娘和蔼地冲我们微笑。

词汇fresh 新鲜的；delicious 美味的；owner 店主 语法一般过去时；and 连接并列分句，smile at sb. 冲某人笑。

In the afternoon, we walked along the lake and took many photos of the mountains.

下午，我们沿着湖散步，拍了很多山的照片。

词汇walk along 沿着……走；take photos 拍照；mountain 山 语法take photos of... 给……拍照；walk 的过去式是 walked。

Finally, we went home when the sun went down, and I felt tired but happy.

最后，太阳下山时我们回家了，我觉得累但开心。

词汇finally 最后；go down 落下；feel 感觉 语法finally 表示顺序结尾；feel 的过去式是 felt，后接形容词表感受。

It was a long day, but I still remember every minute of it.

那是漫长的一天，但我至今记得每一分钟。

词汇minute 分钟 语法一般过去时 it was；remember 用一般现在时表示至今记得。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇游记。上周六，作者一家去离城市约两小时车程的小村庄绿湖旅行。首先，他们早早起床、快速吃完早饭，然后开车出城；一开始路上很挤，半小时后变得平坦安静；开进村庄时，作者看见蓝天下一片宽阔的湖。接着他们租了一条小船慢慢划，划船时一些小鱼在船边跳出水面；作者起初很害怕，爸爸让他抓稳，一切顺利。之后他们在湖边小餐馆吃午饭，鱼很新鲜美味，老板娘和蔼地冲他们微笑；下午他们沿湖散步，拍了许多山的照片。最后，太阳下山时他们回家，作者觉得累但开心。那是漫长的一天，但他至今记得每一分钟。

English Summary

This is a travel diary. Last Saturday the writer's family took a trip to Green Lake, a small village about two hours from the city. First they got up early, finished breakfast quickly and drove out of the city. The road was busy at first but became smooth and quiet after half an hour. When they drove into the village, the writer saw a wide lake under the blue sky. Then they rented a boat and rowed slowly; while they were rowing, some small fish jumped out of the water. The writer was afraid at first, but his father told him to hold on and everything went well. After that they had a fresh, delicious lunch in a small restaurant by the lake, and in the afternoon they walked along the lake and took many photos of the mountains. Finally they went home when the sun went down, tired but happy. It was a long day, but the writer still remembers every minute of it.

语法点 Grammar

Last Saturday, my family took a trip to Green Lake.

一般过去时；take 的过去式 took；last Saturday 是明确的过去时间状语。

First, we got up early, quickly finished breakfast, and then drove out of the city.

first、then 表示时间顺序；get 的过去式 got，drive 的过去式 drove。

The road was busy at first, but after half an hour it became smooth and quiet.

be 的过去式 was；become 的过去式 became；but 表转折。

When we drove into the village, I saw a wide lake under the blue sky.

when 引导时间状语从句；主句用过去式 saw，从句用 drove。

Then we rented a small boat and rowed slowly on the water.

then 承接动作顺序；规则动词过去式 rented、rowed，规则动词加 -ed。

While we were rowing, some small fish jumped out of the water near the boat.

while 引导时间状语从句，用过去进行时表示持续动作，主句用一般过去时 jump 的过去式 jumped。

I was afraid at first, but my father told me to hold on.

be afraid 表感受；tell sb. to do sth. 的过去式 told sb. to do sth.。

After that, we had lunch in a small restaurant next to the lake.

after that 表承接；have 的过去式 had；next to the lake 作后置定语。

The fish was fresh and delicious, and the old owner smiled at us warmly.

一般过去时；smile at sb. 冲某人微笑；warmly 作方式副词。

In the afternoon, we walked along the lake and took many photos of the mountains.

take photos of... 给……拍照；walk 的过去式 walked。

Finally, we went home when the sun went down, and I felt tired but happy.

finally 表顺序结尾；feel 的过去式 felt，后接形容词描写感受。

It was a long day, but I still remember every minute of it.

It was... 评价过去的一天；remember 用一般现在时表示至今仍记得。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

When we drove into the village, I saw a wide lake under the blue sky.

当我们开进村庄时，我看见蓝天下一片宽阔的湖。

when 引导时间状语从句，从句和主句都用一般过去时；under the blue sky 是地点状语，相当于 There was a wide lake under the blue sky。

While we were rowing, some small fish jumped out of the water near the boat.

我们划船的时候，一些小鱼在船边跳出水面。

while 强调“在……的过程中”，从句常用过去进行时 were rowing，主句用一般过去时 jumped 表示突然发生的动作。

Finally, we went home when the sun went down, and I felt tired but happy.

最后，太阳下山时我们回家了，我觉得累但开心。

finally 收尾时间线；when 引导时间状语从句；feel + 形容词表示人的感受，tired but happy 并列表语。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题和图片，猜作者去了哪里、玩了什么。
  • 注意时间状语 Last Saturday，确定全文用一般过去时。

读中

  • 按时间顺序画出 first、then、after that、finally 四个路标，把活动填到后面。
  • 找出所有含有 when 或 while 的句子，标出从句和主句各用什么时态。

读后

  • 用“时间 + 地点 + 活动 + 感受”表格复述游记。
  • 模仿 1b 写 3—5 句英文，介绍自己的一次旅行。

Section B 3a · A Day I Will Never Forget

Section B 3a 主题：难忘的一天 · 学生日记

Dear Diary, October 3rd was a special day for me, and I want to write it down before I forget.

亲爱的日记，10 月 3 日对我来说是特别的一天，我想趁还没忘记把它写下来。

词汇diary 日记；special 特别的；forget 忘记 语法write down 记下；一般过去时 was 叙述过去的事。

In the morning, I woke up late and was afraid that I would miss the school bus.

早上我醒来晚了，害怕会错过校车。

词汇wake up 醒来；miss 错过；school bus 校车 语法wake 的过去式是 woke；be afraid that... 后接从句表担心。

I quickly got dressed, ran downstairs, and ate my breakfast standing in the kitchen.

我赶紧穿好衣服，跑下楼，在厨房里站着吃完了早饭。

词汇get dressed 穿好衣服；downstairs 楼下；kitchen 厨房 语法一般过去时表示连续动作；standing... 作伴随状语。

When I got to school, I found that I had left my English book at home.

到学校时，我发现我把英语书落在家里了。

词汇leave sth. at home 把某物落在家里；find 发现 语法when 引导时间状语从句；find 的过去式是 found。

My best friend Leo sat next to me and let me use his book.

我最好的朋友利奥坐在我旁边，把他的书借给我用。

词汇best friend 好朋友；next to 在……旁边 语法let sb. do sth. 让某人做某事；sit 的过去式是 sat。

While we were reading the passage, he whispered the answers to me.

我们读文章时，他把答案小声告诉了我。

词汇while 当……时；whisper 低语；answer 答案 语法while + 过去进行时；whisper sth. to sb. 小声告诉某人。

After that, our teacher asked us to act out the story in groups.

之后，老师让我们分组表演这个故事。

词汇act out 表演；group 小组 语法ask sb. to do sth. 要求某人做某事；after that 表承接。

At first I was too nervous to speak, but Leo smiled and said, "You can do it!"

一开始我太紧张说不出话，但利奥笑着说：“你能行的！”

词汇nervous 紧张的；too...to 太……而不能 语法too + adj. + to do 表示“太……而不能做”；said 是 say 的过去式。

Finally, I stood in front of the class, took a deep breath, and began to speak.

终于，我站在全班面前，深吸一口气，开始说了起来。

词汇stand 站；in front of 在……前面；breathe 呼吸 语法finally 表示顺序结尾；stand 的过去式是 stood，begin 的过去式是 began。

Everyone listened quietly and clapped when I finished.

大家安静地听着，我说完后都鼓掌了。

词汇listen 听；clap 鼓掌；finish 完成 语法when 引导时间状语从句；描述集体的反应。

I was so happy and proud that I almost cried, because I thought I could not do it.

我又开心又骄傲，差点哭出来，因为我原以为自己做不到。

词汇proud 自豪的；almost 几乎；cry 哭 语法so...that 引导结果状语从句；because 引导原因状语从句。

That day taught me one thing: never be afraid to try.

那一天教会了我一件事：永远不要害怕去尝试。

词汇teach 教；afraid 害怕的 语法一般过去时 taught 总结经历；never + 动词原形表劝告。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一篇学生日记，写 10 月 3 日这个特别的日子。早上作者起晚了，怕错过校车，赶紧穿好衣服、跑下楼、站着吃完早饭；到学校后发现把英语书落在了家里。好朋友利奥坐在旁边，把自己的书借给他；两人读文章时，利奥小声把答案告诉了他。之后老师让分组表演故事。一开始作者太紧张说不出话，利奥笑着说“你能行的”；最后作者站在全班面前，深吸一口气开始说，大家安静地听，说完时鼓掌。作者又开心又骄傲，几乎哭了，因为他原以为自己做不到。那一天教会他：永远不要害怕去尝试。

English Summary

This is a student's diary about October 3rd, a special day. In the morning the writer woke up late and was afraid of missing the school bus, so he got dressed quickly, ran downstairs and ate breakfast standing in the kitchen. When he got to school, he found that he had left his English book at home. His best friend Leo sat next to him and let him use his book, and while they were reading the passage, Leo whispered the answers to him. After that the teacher asked the groups to act out the story. At first the writer was too nervous to speak, but Leo smiled and said, "You can do it!" Finally he stood in front of the class, took a deep breath and began to speak. Everyone listened quietly and clapped when he finished. He was so happy and proud that he almost cried. That day taught him one thing: never be afraid to try.

语法点 Grammar

October 3rd was a special day for me.

一般过去时；be 动词过去式 was 叙述过去的一天。

I woke up late and was afraid that I would miss the school bus.

wake 的过去式 woke；be afraid that... 后接宾语从句表担心。

I quickly got dressed, ran downstairs, and ate my breakfast.

一般过去时并列谓语，按顺序描述连续动作；eat 的过去式 ate。

When I got to school, I found that I had left my English book at home.

when 引导时间状语从句；leave sth. at home 把某物落在家里；find 的过去式 found。

My best friend Leo sat next to me and let me use his book.

sit 的过去式 sat；let sb. do sth. 让某人做某事（let 后接动词原形）。

While we were reading the passage, he whispered the answers to me.

while + 过去进行时表示过去持续的动作；whisper sth. to sb. 小声告诉某人。

After that, our teacher asked us to act out the story in groups.

after that 表时间顺序；ask sb. to do sth. 要求某人做某事。

At first I was too nervous to speak.

too + 形容词 + to do sth. 表示“太……而不能做某事”。

Finally, I stood in front of the class, took a deep breath, and began to speak.

finally 表顺序结尾；stand 的过去式 stood，begin 的过去式 began。

Everyone listened quietly and clapped when I finished.

一般过去时并列谓语：listen 的过去式 listened，clap 的过去式 clapped；when 引导时间状语从句，从句用过去式 finished。

I was so happy and proud that I almost cried.

so + 形容词 + that 引导结果状语从句；cry 的过去式 cried（y 变 i 加 -ed）。

That day taught me one thing: never be afraid to try.

teach 的过去式 taught；never + 动词原形表示劝告或总结教训。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

When I got to school, I found that I had left my English book at home.

到学校时，我发现我把英语书落在家里了。

when 引导时间状语从句；“发现”发生在过去，“书落在家里”发生在更早，所以宾语从句用过去完成时 had left；leave sth. at home 把某物落在家里。

While we were reading the passage, he whispered the answers to me.

我们读文章时，他把答案小声告诉了我。

while 后面用过去进行时表示持续的动作，主句用一般过去时表示期间发生的另一动作；whisper sth. to sb. 是“小声告诉某人”的固定搭配。

At first I was too nervous to speak, but Leo smiled and said, "You can do it!"

一开始我太紧张说不出话，但利奥笑着说：“你能行的！”

too nervous to speak 等于 so nervous that I could not speak；直接引语用引号标出，said 后接引语。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看日记格式（Dear Diary + 日期），确定叙述顺序和时间线。
  • 回忆不规则动词过去式：wake—woke、get—got、eat—ate、stand—stood、teach—taught。

读中

  • 边读边找出事情发生的先后顺序，把 at first / after that / finally 标出来。
  • 找出描写作者感受的形容词（afraid、nervous、happy、proud），说说感受是怎样变化的。

读后

  • 按“早上—到校—之后—表演—感受—收获”复述这篇日记。
  • 模仿 3a 写一篇 5—6 句的日记，写自己难忘的一天。

A 原文：Not really! I fell over twice and everyone laughed. 本试着像宇航员一样走路时摔了两次，惹得大家笑。

4. What did Ben buy at last?

  3. A special suit.
  4. A small model rocket.

D 原文：I bought a small model rocket for my brother. 他给弟弟买了一个小模型火箭。
