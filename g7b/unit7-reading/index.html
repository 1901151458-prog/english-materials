Section B 1b · Sam's Diary

Section B 1b 主题：农场劳动 · 难忘的一天

Friday, 30 May

5 月 30 日，星期五

词汇Friday 星期五；May 五月 语法日记的日期行；英文日期通常写作“星期 + 日期 + 月份”。

Today we went on a school trip to a farm!

今天我们参加了学校组织的农场之旅！

词汇go on a school trip 参加学校旅行；farm 农场 语法一般过去时：go 的过去式是 went；go on a school trip 是固定搭配。

It was tiring but great fun!

虽然很累，但非常有趣！

词汇tiring 令人疲倦的；fun 乐趣 语法but 连接两个并列成分；great fun 表示“非常有趣”。

In the morning, we explored the farm.

上午，我们参观了农场。

词汇in the morning 在上午；explore 探索；参观 语法in the morning 表示“在上午”；explore 的过去式 explored。

There were large tents with tomatoes, cucumbers and many other fruits and vegetables.

那里有一个个大棚，里面种着西红柿、黄瓜和许多其他水果蔬菜。

词汇large 大的；tent 棚；帐篷；cucumber 黄瓜 语法There be 句型的过去式 There were；and 连接并列名词。

The farmer told us about how these fruits and vegetables go from the fields to our tables.

农场主给我们讲了这些果蔬如何从田间来到我们的餐桌。

词汇farmer 农场主；field 田地；table 餐桌 语法tell sb. about sth. 告诉某人关于某事；how 引导宾语从句。

I was very interested because I usually only see them in the supermarket.

我很感兴趣，因为我通常只在超市里见到它们。

词汇interested 感兴趣的；usually 通常；supermarket 超市 语法be interested 表示“感兴趣”；because 引导原因状语从句。

Then we got straight to work!

然后我们就直接开始干活了！

词汇get straight to work 直接开始干活 语法get 的过去式 got；get straight to work 是固定表达。

First, we picked some strawberries.

首先，我们摘了一些草莓。

词汇first 首先；pick 采摘；strawberry 草莓 语法first 表示顺序；pick 的过去式 picked；strawberry 的复数是 strawberries。

The work seemed easy, but it took time to get it right.

这活儿看起来简单，但要做好需要花时间。

词汇seem 看起来；take time 花费时间；right 正确的 语法seem + 形容词；It takes time to do sth. 表示“做某事需要时间”。

Slowly, we filled many baskets.

慢慢地，我们装满了许多篮子。

词汇slowly 慢慢地；fill 装满；basket 篮子 语法副词 slowly 修饰句子；fill 的过去式 filled。

In the afternoon, the farmer taught us how to cut branches and leaves from tomato plants.

下午，农场主教我们如何修剪西红柿植株的枝叶。

词汇branch 枝条；leaf 叶子；plant 植物 语法teach sb. how to do sth. 教某人如何做某事；teach 的过去式 taught。

This helps them to grow more fruit.

这样能帮助它们结出更多的果实。

词汇help sb. (to) do sth. 帮助某人做某事；grow 生长 语法help sb. (to) do sth.；this 指代上文修剪枝叶的做法。

Finally, we watered the plants.

最后，我们给植物浇了水。

词汇finally 最后；water 浇水 语法finally 表示顺序；water 作动词表示“浇水”。

Plants need so much work!

种植物需要干好多活儿！

词汇need 需要；so much 如此多 语法so much + 不可数名词 work，表示“这么多活儿”。

It was tiring, but I enjoyed working with my hands.

虽然很累，但我喜欢动手干活。

词汇enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事；with my hands 用双手 语法enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事；but 表转折。

One thing I learnt today: Farming isn't easy!

今天我明白了一件事：务农并不容易！

词汇learn 学到；farming 务农；农业 语法learn 的过去式 learnt；One thing I learnt today 用于点题。

It made me think of the saying: "Every grain comes from hard work."

这让我想到那句谚语：“每一粒粮食都来自辛勤劳动。”

词汇make sb. do sth. 使某人做某事；saying 谚语；grain 粮食 语法make sb. do sth. 用不带 to 的动词原形；think of 表示“想到”。

The farmer let us take some vegetables home.

农场主让我们带了一些蔬菜回家。

词汇let sb. do sth. 让某人做某事；take sth. home 把某物带回家 语法let sb. do sth. 用不带 to 的动词原形。

My mum cooked some for dinner, and they were fresh and delicious!

我妈妈做了一些当晚餐，它们又新鲜又美味！

词汇cook 烹饪；dinner 晚餐；fresh 新鲜的；delicious 美味的 语法and 连接两个并列分句；they 指代蔬菜。

They certainly taste better when you work for them!

自己付出劳动后，它们尝起来当然更好吃！

词汇certainly 当然；taste 尝起来；work for 为……付出劳动 语法taste 是系动词，后接形容词比较级 better；when 引导时间状语从句。

Today was really a day to remember.

今天真是难忘的一天。

词汇really 真正地；a day to remember 难忘的一天 语法a day to remember 中不定式作后置定语，修饰 day。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P54 Section B 1b 中 Sam 的日记（5 月 30 日，星期五）。Sam 记录了学校组织的农场之旅：虽然很累，但非常有趣。上午他们参观了农场，看到大棚里种着西红柿、黄瓜等许多水果蔬菜；农场主介绍了这些果蔬如何从田间来到餐桌，Sam 很感兴趣，因为他通常只在超市里见到它们。接着他们直接开始劳动：先摘草莓，看似简单却要花时间才能做好，慢慢装满了许多篮子。下午农场主教他们修剪西红柿植株的枝叶，说这样能结出更多果实；最后他们给植物浇水。Sam 体会到务农并不容易，想到“每一粒粮食都来自辛勤劳动”的谚语；农场主让大家带了些蔬菜回家，妈妈做成了晚餐，新鲜又美味。Sam 说，自己付出劳动后尝起来当然更好吃，这真是难忘的一天。

English Summary

This is Sam's diary in Section B 1b on page 54 (Friday, 30 May). Sam writes about his school trip to a farm: it was tiring but great fun. In the morning they explored the farm and saw large tents with tomatoes, cucumbers and many other fruits and vegetables. The farmer told them how these fruits and vegetables go from the fields to their tables, and Sam was very interested because he usually only sees them in the supermarket. Then they got straight to work: first they picked strawberries; the work seemed easy but took time to get it right, and slowly they filled many baskets. In the afternoon the farmer taught them how to cut branches and leaves from tomato plants, which helps the plants grow more fruit, and finally they watered the plants. Sam learnt that farming isn't easy and thought of the saying "Every grain comes from hard work". The farmer let them take some vegetables home, and Sam's mum cooked some for dinner; they were fresh and delicious. Sam says they certainly taste better when you work for them, and it was really a day to remember.

语法点 Grammar

Today we went on a school trip to a farm!

一般过去时：go 的过去式是 went；go on a school trip 表示“参加学校旅行”。

It was tiring but great fun!

but 连接两个并列成分；fun 是不可数名词。

In the morning, we explored the farm.

in the morning 表示“在上午”；explore 的过去式是 explored。

There were large tents with tomatoes, cucumbers and many other fruits and vegetables.

There be 句型的过去式 There were；and 连接并列名词。

The farmer told us about how these fruits and vegetables go from the fields to our tables.

tell sb. about sth. 告诉某人关于某事；how 引导宾语从句。

I was very interested because I usually only see them in the supermarket.

be interested 表示“感兴趣”；because 引导原因状语从句。

First, we picked some strawberries.

first 表示顺序；pick 的过去式是 picked。

The work seemed easy, but it took time to get it right.

seem + 形容词；It takes time to do sth. 表示“做某事需要时间”。

In the afternoon, the farmer taught us how to cut branches and leaves from tomato plants.

teach sb. how to do sth. 教某人如何做某事；teach 的过去式是 taught。

This helps them to grow more fruit.

help sb. (to) do sth. 帮助某人做某事。

Finally, we watered the plants.

finally 表示顺序；water 作动词表示“浇水”。

It was tiring, but I enjoyed working with my hands.

enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事；but 表转折。

learn 的过去式是 learnt；One thing I learnt today 用于点题。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It was tiring but great fun!

虽然很累，但非常有趣！

tiring 表示“令人疲倦的”，修饰事情；fun 是不可数名词，great fun 表示“非常有趣”。

The work seemed easy, but it took time to get it right.

这活儿看起来简单，但要做好需要花时间。

seem 是系动词，后接形容词；It takes time to do sth. 表示“做某事需要时间”。

It made me think of the saying: "Every grain comes from hard work."

这让我想到那句谚语：每一粒粮食都来自辛勤的劳动。

make sb. do sth. 用不带 to 的动词原形；think of 表示“想到”；引号内是谚语。

Reading Plus · Lisa's Diary

Reading Plus 主题：日全食 · 难忘的一天

Wednesday, 22 July

7 月 22 日，星期三

词汇Wednesday 星期三；July 七月 语法日记的日期行；星期和月份首字母大写。

Today was an amazing day.

今天真是奇妙的一天。

词汇amazing 奇妙的；令人惊叹的 语法一般过去时：主语 + was + 形容词。

There was a total solar eclipse in Wuhan!

武汉出现了日全食！

词汇total 完全的；solar 太阳的；eclipse 日食 语法There be 句型的过去式 There was + 单数名词。

I woke up early because I couldn't wait to see it!

我早早醒来，因为我迫不及待地想看日全食！

词汇wake up 醒来；can't wait to do sth. 迫不及待做某事 语法wake 的过去式 woke；can't wait to do sth. 表示“迫不及待做某事”。

My friends and I went down to the Yangtze River to find a good place to watch it.

我和朋友们来到长江边，找一个好位置观看。

词汇the Yangtze River 长江；find 找到 语法go 的过去式 went；to find 表示目的。

We took cameras, chairs, food and drink, and special glasses so we could look at it.

我们带上相机、椅子、食物和饮料，还有专门的眼镜，以便观看。

词汇camera 相机；chair 椅子；special glasses 专门的眼镜 语法take 的过去式 took；so 引导目的状语从句；could 是 can 的过去式。

Lots of people gathered there to watch too.

许多人也聚集在那里观看。

词汇lots of 许多；gather 聚集 语法lots of + 可数名词复数；gather 的过去式 gathered。

Slowly, the moon moved in front of the sun.

月亮慢慢地移到了太阳前面。

词汇slowly 慢慢地；in front of 在……前面 语法副词 slowly 修饰整个句子；move 的过去式 moved。

It looked like someone hit a hole in the sun!

看起来就像有人在太阳上打了一个洞！

词汇look like 看起来像；hit 打；撞 语法look like 表示“看起来像”；hit 的过去式仍为 hit。

It got darker and darker as the moon slowly covered the sun.

随着月亮慢慢遮住太阳，天变得越来越暗。

词汇darker 更暗的；cover 遮住 语法get + 比较级 and 比较级 表示“越来越……”；as 引导时间状语从句。

At 9:26 a.m., the moon completely covered the sun.

上午 9:26，月亮完全遮住了太阳。

词汇a.m. 上午；completely 完全地；cover 遮住 语法at + 具体时刻；completely 修饰动词 covered。

The city went dark.

城市暗了下来。

词汇city 城市；go dark 变暗 语法go 的过去式 went；go + 形容词表示状态变化。

The crowd gasped and cheered.

人群惊叹欢呼。

词汇crowd 人群；gasp 惊叹；cheer 欢呼 语法and 连接两个并列谓语；gasp 和 cheer 的过去式加 -ed。

The light of the sun made a ring of light around the moon.

太阳的光在月亮周围形成了一圈光环。

词汇light 光；ring 环；光圈；around 围绕 语法make 的过去式 made；a ring of light 表示“一圈光”。

I took this photograph then.

我拍下了这张照片。

词汇photograph 照片；then 那时 语法take 的过去式 took；this photograph 指日记配图。

The total eclipse lasted for over five minutes!

日全食持续了五分多钟！

词汇last 持续；over 超过；minute 分钟 语法last for + 时间段表示“持续……”。

Then it passed and the sky started to get brighter again.

然后日全食结束了，天空又开始变亮。

词汇pass 过去；结束；start to do sth. 开始做某事；brighter 更亮的 语法and 连接两个并列分句；start to do sth. 表示“开始做某事”。

I was really inspired by the eclipse this morning.

今天上午，我被日全食深深震撼了。

词汇really 真正地；be inspired by 受到……的启发 语法be inspired by 表示“受到……的启发/震撼”。

In the afternoon, I spent hours reading about astronomers, eclipses, and how the planets move in our solar system.

下午，我花了好几个小时阅读关于天文学家、日食以及行星如何在我们太阳系中运行的知识。

词汇spend 花费；astronomer 天文学家；planet 行星；solar system 太阳系 语法spend + 时间 + doing sth. 表示“花时间做某事”；how 引导宾语从句。

The universe is simply amazing!

宇宙真是太神奇了！

词汇universe 宇宙；simply 简直 语法simply 用于强调，表示“简直、实在”。

I also watched some videos with famous Chinese astronauts.

我还看了一些中国著名宇航员的视频。

词汇watch 观看；famous 著名的；astronaut 宇航员 语法also 放在实义动词前；with 表示“关于、涉及”。

I want to become an astronomer or an astronaut someday too.

我希望有一天也能成为天文学家或宇航员。

词汇become 成为；someday 有一天 语法want to do sth. 想要做某事；or 连接两个并列名词。

The sky is the limit!

天空才是极限（前途无量）！

词汇limit 极限 语法The sky is the limit! 是英语习语，意为“没有什么是不可能的/前途无量”。

中文大意

这是教材 P71 Reading Plus 中 Lisa 的日记（7 月 22 日，星期三）。Lisa 记录了在武汉观测日全食的经历：今天非常奇妙，武汉出现了日全食，她早早起床，迫不及待地想看。她和朋友们来到长江边找好位置，带上相机、椅子、食物和饮料，还有专门的眼镜，以便观看；许多人聚集在那里观看。月亮慢慢移到太阳前面，看起来像在太阳上打了一个洞，天越来越暗。上午 9:26，月亮完全遮住太阳，城市暗了下来，人群惊叹欢呼；太阳的光在月亮周围形成一圈光环，Lisa 拍下了照片。日全食持续了五分多钟，之后天空重新变亮。Lisa 深受震撼，下午花了好几个小时阅读关于天文学家、日食以及行星运行的知识，觉得宇宙太神奇了；她还看了一些中国著名宇航员的视频，希望有一天也能成为天文学家或宇航员。她说：天空才是极限！

English Summary

This is Lisa's diary in the Reading Plus on page 71 (Wednesday, 22 July). Lisa writes about watching a total solar eclipse in Wuhan. It was an amazing day, and she woke up early because she couldn't wait to see it. She and her friends went down to the Yangtze River to find a good place to watch the eclipse. They took cameras, chairs, food and drink, and special glasses so they could look at it, and lots of people gathered there to watch too. Slowly the moon moved in front of the sun, and it looked like someone hit a hole in the sun; it got darker and darker. At 9:26 a.m., the moon completely covered the sun and the city went dark. The crowd gasped and cheered, and the light of the sun made a ring of light around the moon; Lisa took a photograph then. The total eclipse lasted for over five minutes, and then the sky started to get brighter again. Lisa was really inspired by the eclipse. In the afternoon she spent hours reading about astronomers, eclipses and how the planets move in our solar system, and she also watched videos with famous Chinese astronauts. She wants to become an astronomer or an astronaut someday too: the sky is the limit!

语法点 Grammar

Today was an amazing day.

一般过去时：主语 + was + 形容词。

There was a total solar eclipse in Wuhan!

There be 句型的过去式 There was + 单数名词。

I woke up early because I couldn't wait to see it!

wake up 的过去式是 woke up；can't wait to do sth. 表示“迫不及待做某事”。

My friends and I went down to the Yangtze River to find a good place to watch it.

go 的过去式是 went；to find 表示目的。

We took cameras, chairs, food and drink, and special glasses so we could look at it.

take 的过去式是 took；so 引导目的状语从句；could 是 can 的过去式。

Lots of people gathered there to watch too.

lots of + 可数名词复数；gather 的过去式是 gathered。

Slowly, the moon moved in front of the sun.

副词 slowly 修饰整个句子；move 的过去式是 moved。

It looked like someone hit a hole in the sun!

look like 表示“看起来像”；hit 的过去式仍为 hit。

It got darker and darker as the moon slowly covered the sun.

get + 比较级 and 比较级 表示“越来越……”；as 引导时间状语从句。

At 9:26 a.m., the moon completely covered the sun.

at + 具体时刻；completely 修饰动词 covered。

The light of the sun made a ring of light around the moon.

make 的过去式是 made；a ring of light 表示“一圈光”。

The total eclipse lasted for over five minutes!

last for + 时间段表示“持续……”。

I was really inspired by the eclipse this morning.

be inspired by 表示“受到……的启发/震撼”。

In the afternoon, I spent hours reading about astronomers, eclipses, and how the planets move in our solar system.

spend + 时间 + doing sth. 表示“花时间做某事”；how 引导宾语从句。

I want to become an astronomer or an astronaut someday too.

want to do sth. 想要做某事；or 连接两个并列名词。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

We took cameras, chairs, food and drink, and special glasses so we could look at it.

我们带上相机、椅子、食物和饮料，还有专门的眼镜，以便观看日全食。

so 引导目的状语从句；could 是 can 的过去式；look at it 中的 it 指日全食。

It got darker and darker as the moon slowly covered the sun.

随着月亮慢慢遮住太阳，天变得越来越暗。

get + 比较级 and 比较级 表示“越来越……”；as 引导时间状语从句，表示“随着”。

In the afternoon, I spent hours reading about astronomers, eclipses, and how the planets move in our solar system.

下午，我花了好几个小时阅读关于天文学家、日食以及行星如何在我们太阳系中运行的知识。

spend + 时间 + doing sth. 表示“花时间做某事”；how 引导宾语从句。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看 P71 日记的日期和配图，猜一猜 Lisa 记录了什么事。
  • 先通读日记，找出事情发生的时间、地点和人物。

读中

  • 边读边画出描写日全食过程的句子和时间点。
  • 找出描写 Lisa 感受和她梦想的句子。

读后

  • 用时间轴整理日全食的发生过程。
  • 仿照日记，用英语记录一次自己难忘的经历（5—6 句）。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面 2 篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. Where did Sam go on the school trip?

  1. To a zoo.
  2. To a farm.
  3. To a park.
  4. To a museum.
答案与解析

B 原文：Today we went on a school trip to a farm! 可知 Sam 参加的学校旅行去了农场。

2. What did Sam and his classmates pick first?

  1. Tomatoes.
  2. Cucumbers.
  3. Apples.
  4. Strawberries.
答案与解析

D 原文：First, we picked some strawberries. 可知他们首先摘的是草莓。

3. What did the farmer teach them to do in the afternoon?

  1. Cut branches and leaves from tomato plants.
  2. Pick strawberries.
  3. Water the plants.
  4. Cook vegetables.
答案与解析

A 原文：In the afternoon, the farmer taught us how to cut branches and leaves from tomato plants. 可知下午农场主教他们修剪西红柿植株的枝叶。

4. Why did Sam think the vegetables tasted better?

  1. Because they were from the supermarket.
  2. Because his mum cooked them.
  3. Because they worked for them.
  4. Because they were fresh.
答案与解析

C 原文：They certainly taste better when you work for them! 可知自己付出劳动后蔬菜尝起来更好吃。

5. Where did Lisa and her friends watch the eclipse?

  1. In a park.
  2. Down by the Yangtze River.
  3. At school.
  4. On a farm.
答案与解析

B 原文：My friends and I went down to the Yangtze River to find a good place to watch it. 可知他们在长江边观测日全食。

6. What time did the moon completely cover the sun?

  1. At 9:26 a.m.
  2. At 9:26 p.m.
  3. At 10:26 a.m.
  4. At 8:26 a.m.
答案与解析

A 原文：At 9:26 a.m., the moon completely covered the sun. 可知月亮在上午 9:26 完全遮住太阳。

7. How long did the total eclipse last?

  1. Over five hours.
  2. Over nine minutes.
  3. About two minutes.
  4. Over five minutes.
答案与解析

D 原文：The total eclipse lasted for over five minutes! 可知日全食持续了五分多钟。

8. What does Lisa want to become someday?

  1. A teacher.
  2. A farmer.
  3. An astronomer or an astronaut.
  4. A doctor.
答案与解析

C 原文：I want to become an astronomer or an astronaut someday too. 可知 Lisa 希望有一天成为天文学家或宇航员。
