Section A 3a · Talking About Pets

Section A 3a 主题：宠物与动物 · 对话

A: What's your favourite animal?

你最喜欢的动物是什么？

词汇favourite 最喜欢的；animal 动物 语法What's your favourite...? 询问喜好。

B: I like dogs best. They are friendly and clever.

我最喜欢狗。它们友好又聪明。

词汇friendly 友好的；clever 聪明的 语法like...best；形容词并列作表语。

A: Can dogs help people?

狗能帮助人吗？

词汇help 帮助 语法Can + 主语 + 动词原形...? 询问能力。

B: Yes, they can. Some dogs help blind people.

能。有些狗能帮助盲人。

词汇blind 失明的 语法can + 动词原形；help sb.。

A: That's amazing! What do you do with your dog?

太棒了！你和你的狗一起做什么？

词汇amazing 令人惊叹的 语法What do you do with...? 询问活动。

B: I take it for a walk every day and play with it.

我每天带它散步，和它一起玩。

词汇take...for a walk 带……散步；play with 和……玩 语法take sb. for a walk；every day 表示频率。

A: Does your dog eat a lot?

你的狗吃得多吗？

词汇eat 吃 语法Does + 主语 + 动词原形...? 一般现在时疑问句。

B: No, it doesn't. It only eats twice a day.

不多。它一天只吃两次。

词汇twice a day 一天两次 语法doesn't 否定；twice a day 表示频率。

A: I want to get a pet too. What should I keep?

我也想要一只宠物。我该养什么？

词汇pet 宠物；keep 饲养 语法should + 动词原形征求意见。

B: You can keep a cat. Cats are quiet and easy to take care of.

你可以养猫。猫安静又容易照顾。

词汇quiet 安静的；take care of 照顾 语法can + 动词原形；easy to take care of。

A: Good idea. Animals are our friends.

好主意。动物是我们的朋友。

词汇friend 朋友 语法主系表结构。

B: Yes, we should be kind to them.

是的，我们应该对它们友善。

词汇be kind to 对……友善 语法should + 动词原形。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕宠物展开。一人最喜欢狗，觉得狗友好又聪明；狗还能帮助盲人。他说每天带狗散步、和它玩，狗一天只吃两次。另一人也想养宠物，问该养什么，朋友建议养猫，因为猫安静又容易照顾。两人都认为动物是人类的朋友，应该对它们友善。

English Summary

The conversation is about pets. One speaker likes dogs best because they are friendly and clever, and dogs can help blind people. He takes his dog for a walk every day and plays with it, and the dog only eats twice a day. The other wants to get a pet and asks what to keep; the friend suggests a cat because cats are quiet and easy to take care of. Both agree that animals are our friends and we should be kind to them.

like...best 表示最喜欢。

Can + 主语 + 动词原形...? 询问能力。

take sb. for a walk；every day 表示频率。

Does + 主语 + 动词原形...? 一般现在时疑问句。

should + 动词原形征求意见。

Section B 1b · Protecting the Pandas

Section B 1b 主题：大熊猫 · 动物保护

Many people keep animals as pets, but some animals are in danger.

很多人把动物当宠物养，但有些动物正处在危险中。

词汇keep...as pets 把……当宠物；be in danger 处于危险中 语法but 转折；keep sth. as...。

The giant panda is one of them.

大熊猫就是其中之一。

词汇the giant panda 大熊猫 语法one of + 代词/复数名词。

Pandas live in the mountains of China, and they mainly eat bamboo.

熊猫生活在中国山区，主要吃竹子。

词汇mountain 山；mainly 主要地；bamboo 竹子 语法and 连接并列分句。

A baby panda is very small when it is born.

熊猫宝宝出生时非常小。

词汇be born 出生 语法when 引导时间状语从句。

It can't see anything at first.

一开始它什么也看不见。

词汇at first 起初 语法can't + 动词原形；否定代词 anything。

Its mother takes care of it day and night.

它的妈妈日夜照顾它。

词汇take care of 照顾；day and night 日夜 语法take care of sb.。

After a few months, the baby can walk and eat bamboo.

几个月后，熊猫宝宝就能走路、吃竹子了。

词汇a few 几个 语法can + 动词原形。

However, pandas are losing their homes because people cut down trees.

然而，由于人们砍树，熊猫正在失去家园。

词汇however 然而；cut down 砍倒；lose 失去 语法because 引导原因状语从句；现在进行时。

We should protect their homes and stop buying things made of ivory.

我们应该保护它们的家园，停止购买象牙制品。

词汇protect 保护；be made of 由……制成；ivory 象牙 语法stop doing sth.；made of 作后置定语。

Many people are helping pandas now.

现在很多人都在帮助熊猫。

词汇help 帮助 语法现在进行时表示当前情况。

With their help, the number of pandas is growing.

在他们的帮助下，熊猫的数量在增加。

词汇with one's help 在某人的帮助下；the number of ……的数量 语法with 短语作状语；the number of 作主语，谓语用单数。

Animals are important to us, and we should live with them in peace.

动物对我们很重要，我们应该与它们和平共处。

词汇be important to 对……重要；in peace 和平地 语法and 连接并列分句；live with...in peace。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

短文介绍大熊猫及保护问题。很多人把动物当宠物，但有些动物身处危险，大熊猫就是其中之一。它们生活在中国山区，主要吃竹子；熊猫宝宝出生时非常小，一开始什么也看不见，妈妈日夜照顾它，几个月后它就能走路、吃竹子。然而由于人们砍树，熊猫正在失去家园，我们应该保护它们的家园、停止购买象牙制品。很多人正在帮助熊猫，熊猫数量在增加；动物对我们很重要，应与它们和平共处。

English Summary

The passage introduces giant pandas and their protection. Many people keep animals as pets, but some are in danger, and the giant panda is one of them. Pandas live in the mountains of China and mainly eat bamboo. A baby panda is very small when born and cannot see anything at first; its mother takes care of it day and night, and after a few months it can walk and eat bamboo. However, pandas are losing their homes because people cut down trees, so we should protect their homes and stop buying things made of ivory. Many people are helping pandas and their number is growing; animals are important to us and we should live with them in peace.

Section B 3a · My Favourite Animal

Section B 3a 主题：我最喜欢的动物 · 大象

Hello, everyone! I want to talk about my favourite animal—the elephant.

大家好！我想谈谈我最喜欢的动物——大象。

词汇talk about 谈论；elephant 大象 语法I want to talk about...。

Elephants are the largest animals on land.

大象是陆地上最大的动物。

词汇the largest 最大的；on land 在陆地上 语法最高级 the largest。

They have long noses and big ears.

它们有长长的鼻子和大大的耳朵。

词汇nose 鼻子；ear 耳朵 语法have + 名词；形容词作定语。

Elephants are very clever and they can remember many things.

大象非常聪明，能记住很多事情。

词汇clever 聪明的；remember 记住 语法can + 动词原形。

They live in groups and help each other.

它们群居，互相帮助。

词汇live in groups 群居；help each other 互相帮助 语法and 连接并列谓语。

However, elephants are in danger.

然而，大象处境危险。

词汇be in danger 处于危险中 语法however 表示转折。

Some people kill them for their ivory.

有些人为了象牙而捕杀它们。

词汇kill 捕杀；for 为了 语法for... 表示目的。

We must stop this and protect elephants.

我们必须制止这种行为，保护大象。

词汇stop 制止；protect 保护 语法must + 动词原形。

We can also learn more about them and share the knowledge with others.

我们也可以多了解它们，并把这些知识分享给别人。

词汇knowledge 知识；share...with 与……分享 语法and 连接并列谓语；share sth. with sb.。

I hope that one day people and elephants can live together happily.

我希望有一天人类和大象能快乐地生活在一起。

词汇happily 快乐地；live together 一起生活 语法hope 后接宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文介绍作者最喜欢的动物大象。大象是陆地上最大的动物，有长鼻子和大耳朵；它们非常聪明，能记住很多事情，群居并互相帮助。然而大象处境危险，有些人为了象牙捕杀它们。作者认为我们必须制止这种行为、保护大象，也可以多了解它们并把这些知识分享给别人，希望有一天人类和大象能快乐地生活在一起。

English Summary

The passage introduces the writer's favourite animal, the elephant. Elephants are the largest animals on land, with long noses and big ears; they are very clever and can remember many things, live in groups and help each other. However, elephants are in danger because some people kill them for their ivory. The writer believes we must stop this and protect elephants, and we can also learn more about them and share the knowledge with others, hoping that one day people and elephants can live together happily.

1. What animal does the speaker like best?

  1. Cats.
  2. Dogs.
  3. Pandas.
  4. Elephants.
答案与解析

2. What can some dogs do?

  1. They can cook.
  2. They can help blind people.
  3. They can read.
  4. They can sing.
答案与解析

B 原文：Some dogs help blind people.

3. How often does the dog eat?

  1. Once a day.
  2. Twice a day.
  3. Three times a day.
  4. Never.
答案与解析

B 原文：It only eats twice a day.

4. Where do pandas live?

  1. In the sea.
  2. In the mountains of China.
  3. In Africa.
  4. In the desert.

5. What is a baby panda like when it is born?

  3. It can walk at once.
  4. It eats bamboo at once.

  3. Because there is no water.
  4. Because they move away.

  3. They live alone.
  4. They hide in caves.
