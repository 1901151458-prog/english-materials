Section A 2a · Our Favourite Animals

Section A 2a 主题：动物与喜好 · 语音辨词

Peter: The penguin is my favourite animal.

企鹅是我最喜欢的动物。

词汇penguin 企鹅；favourite 最喜欢的；animal 动物 语法favourite 作形容词，修饰 animal。

Peter: What's your favourite, Fu Xing?

你最喜欢什么，Fu Xing？

词汇what's = what is；favourite 最喜欢的（事物） 语法What's your favourite? 询问对方最喜欢的事物；favourite 后省略了 animal。

Fu Xing: I like penguins too.

我也喜欢企鹅。

词汇too 也 语法too 用于肯定句末，表示“也”。

Fu Xing: Why do you like them so much?

你为什么这么喜欢它们？

词汇why 为什么；so much 如此多 语法Why + do + you + 动词原形? 用来询问原因。

Peter: Because they're very cute / cool!

因为它们非常可爱 / 酷！

词汇because 因为；cute 可爱的；cool 酷的 语法because 引导原因；斜杠前后为教材两个选项。

Teng Fei: Yes, they look lovely / amazing.

是的，它们看起来很可爱 / 令人惊叹。

词汇look 看起来；lovely 可爱的；amazing 令人惊叹的 语法look 是系动词，后接形容词作表语。

Teng Fei: Let's go and see them.

我们去看看它们吧。

词汇let's = let us 让我们；go and see 去看看 语法Let's + 动词原形，表示建议。

Peter: Oh, there they are!

哦，它们在那儿！

词汇there 那里；there they are 它们在那儿 语法There they are! 是倒装句，用来指出看到的人或物。

Peter: They can't fly like other birds, but they can swim fast.

它们不能像其他鸟一样飞，但能游得很快。

词汇fly 飞；like 像；swim 游泳；fast 快地 语法can't + 动词原形表示“不能”；but 表转折。

Peter: I like how they walk.

我喜欢它们走路的样子。

词汇how they walk 它们走路的样子 语法how 引导宾语从句，表示“……的样子/方式”。

Peter: It's funny / interesting!

这真有趣 / 有意思！

词汇funny 有趣的；interesting 有意思的 语法It's + 形容词，对事物作出评价。

Teng Fei: Yes, it is!

是的，确实如此！

词汇yes 是的；it is 确实 语法Yes, it is! 用于赞同对方的评价。

Teng Fei: Where are they from?

它们来自哪里？

词汇where 哪里；be from 来自 语法Where + be + 主语 + from? 询问来源。

Fu Xing: Look here!

看这里！

词汇look 看；here 这里 语法祈使句，用动词原形开头。

Fu Xing: It says they're from Antarctica.

上面说它们来自南极洲。

词汇it says 上面写着；Antarctica 南极洲 语法It says... 表示“上面写着/说明”，是常见表达。

Fu Xing: It's very cold there, so they often stand close together.

那里非常冷，所以它们经常紧挨着站在一起。

词汇cold 冷的；often 经常；close together 紧靠在一起 语法so 表示结果；stand close together 表示“紧挨着站”。

Fu Xing: It helps them keep warm.

这能帮助它们保暖。

词汇help sb. do sth. 帮助某人做某事；keep warm 保暖 语法help sb. (to) do sth.；keep + 形容词表示“保持……”。

Peter: They're so clever / smart.

它们真聪明 / 机灵。

词汇clever 聪明的；smart 机灵的 语法They're = They are；so + 形容词表示程度。

Peter: What do they eat?

它们吃什么？

词汇eat 吃 语法What + do + 主语 + 动词原形? 询问吃什么。

Teng Fei: Fish and small sea animals, I think.

我想是鱼和小型海洋动物。

词汇sea animal 海洋动物；I think 我认为 语法I think 是插入语，放在句末表示“我认为”。

Fu Xing: I have a fish sandwich.

我有一份鱼肉三明治。

词汇sandwich 三明治 语法I have... 表示“我有……”。

Teng Fei: Don't give them your sandwich!

别把你的三明治给它们！

词汇give sb. sth. 给某人某物 语法否定祈使句：Don't + 动词原形。

Teng Fei: It's not good for them.

这对它们不好。

词汇be good for 对……有好处 语法be good for 表示“对……有益/有好处”，否定用 not good for。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P3 的听读圈词对话（听录音，圈出听到的彩色单词；斜杠前后为教材给出的两个选项）。Peter 说企鹅是他最喜欢的动物，并问 Fu Xing 最喜欢什么。Fu Xing 也喜欢企鹅，问 Peter 为什么这么喜欢企鹅。Peter 说因为它们非常可爱、酷；Teng Fei 觉得它们很可爱、令人惊叹，提议一起去看企鹅。看到企鹅后，Peter 说它们虽然不会像其他鸟那样飞，但游得很快，他喜欢它们走路的样子，觉得十分有趣。Teng Fei 赞同，并问企鹅来自哪里。Fu Xing 从介绍牌上读到它们来自南极洲，那里很冷，所以企鹅经常紧挨着站在一起取暖。Peter 觉得它们很聪明，问它们吃什么；Teng Fei 认为是鱼和小型海洋动物。Fu Xing 说他有鱼肉三明治，Teng Fei 赶紧提醒他不要把三明治给企鹅，因为那对它们不好。

English Summary

This is the listen-and-circle conversation on page 3 of the textbook: students listen and circle the coloured words they hear, and the words before and after each slash are the two choices in the textbook. Peter says the penguin is his favourite animal and asks Fu Xing what her favourite animal is. Fu Xing likes penguins too and asks why Peter likes them so much. Peter says they are very cute / cool, and Teng Fei agrees that they look lovely / amazing and suggests going to see them. When they see the penguins, Peter says penguins can't fly like other birds but can swim fast; he likes how they walk and finds it funny / interesting. Teng Fei agrees and asks where penguins are from. Fu Xing reads a sign saying they are from Antarctica, where it is very cold, so penguins often stand close together to keep warm. Peter thinks they are clever and asks what they eat; Teng Fei thinks they eat fish and small sea animals. Fu Xing has a fish sandwich, and Teng Fei tells him not to give it to the penguins because it is not good for them.

语法点 Grammar

The penguin is my favourite animal.

favourite 作形容词修饰名词；主语 + be + 形容词性物主代词 + 名词。

What's your favourite, Fu Xing?

What's your favourite? 询问对方最喜欢的事物，favourite 后省略了 animal。

I like penguins too.

too 表示“也”，常用于肯定句末。

Why do you like them so much?

Why + do + you + 动词原形? 询问原因；like ... so much 表示“非常喜欢”。

Because they're very cute / cool!

because 引导原因状语从句；斜杠前后为教材两个选项。

Yes, they look lovely / amazing.

look 是系动词，后接形容词作表语。

Let's go and see them.

Let's + 动词原形，表示建议；go and see 表示“去看看”。

They can't fly like other birds, but they can swim fast.

can't + 动词原形表示不能；like 是介词，表示“像”。

Where are they from?

Where + be + 主语 + from? 询问来源；be from 表示“来自”。

It says they're from Antarctica.

It says... 表示“上面写着/说明”；they're = they are。

It's very cold there, so they often stand close together.

so 连接结果；stand close together 表示“紧挨着站”。

It helps them keep warm.

help sb. do sth. 帮助某人做某事；keep + 形容词表示“保持……”。

Don't give them your sandwich!

否定祈使句：Don't + 动词原形；give sb. sth. 给某人某物。

It's not good for them.

be good for 表示“对……有好处”，否定形式为 be not good for。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

What's your favourite, Fu Xing?

你最喜欢什么，Fu Xing？

favourite 在此作名词，后面省略了 animal；逗号后的 Fu Xing 是称呼语。

It says they're from Antarctica.

上面说它们来自南极洲。

It says... 是英语中介绍标牌、说明内容的常用表达，不能按字面理解为“它说”。

Don't give them your sandwich!

别把你的三明治给它们！

否定祈使句以 Don't 开头；give sb. sth. 是双宾语结构，them 是间接宾语，your sandwich 是直接宾语。

Section B 1b · Malee's Post

Section B 1b 主题：泰国大象 · 人与自然

Hi, I'm Malee and I live in Thailand!

嗨，我是 Malee，我住在泰国！

词汇Thailand 泰国；live in 住在 语法I'm = I am；and 连接两个并列句。

The elephant is my favourite animal.

大象是我最喜欢的动物。

词汇elephant 大象；favourite 最喜欢的 语法favourite 作形容词，修饰 animal。

I love elephants because they are strong and clever.

我喜欢大象，因为它们强壮又聪明。

词汇strong 强壮的；clever 聪明的 语法because 引导原因状语从句。

They are also a symbol of good luck here.

在这里它们也是好运的象征。

词汇symbol 象征；good luck 好运 语法a symbol of... 表示“……的象征”；also 放在 be 动词后。

The elephant is our national animal.

大象是我们的国宝动物。

词汇national 国家的；national animal 国宝动物 语法our 是形容词性物主代词，修饰 national animal。

On 13 March, we celebrate Thai Elephant Day.

3 月 13 日，我们庆祝泰国大象节。

词汇celebrate 庆祝；Thai 泰国的 语法on + 具体日期；Thai Elephant Day 是专有名词，首字母大写。

Elephants look very different from other animals.

大象看起来与其他动物很不一样。

词汇different from 与……不同 语法look + 形容词；be different from 表示“与……不同”。

They are huge.

它们很庞大。

词汇huge 庞大的 语法They are + 形容词，描述特征。

They have large ears and long trunks.

它们有大耳朵和长鼻子。

词汇ear 耳朵；trunk 象鼻 语法have + 名词表示“有”；and 连接两个并列宾语。

They can pick up and carry heavy things with their trunks.

它们能用鼻子捡起并搬运重物。

词汇pick up 捡起；carry 搬运；heavy 重的 语法can + 动词原形；with their trunks 表示“用鼻子”。

Elephants are like us in some ways.

在某些方面，大象和我们很像。

词汇be like 像；in some ways 在某些方面 语法be like 表示“像”；in some ways 是固定短语。

They are very playful and love to play in the water.

它们非常爱玩，喜欢在水里玩耍。

词汇playful 爱玩的；in the water 在水里 语法love to do sth. 喜欢做某事；and 连接两个谓语。

They are great swimmers.

它们是游泳好手。

词汇swimmer 游泳者 语法a great swimmer 表示“游泳好手”；swimmers 用复数。

They are also clever.

它们也很聪明。

词汇also 也 语法also 放在 be 动词后，表示“也”。

For example, they can remember one another and places with food and water after many years.

例如，多年之后它们还能记住彼此以及有食物和水的地方。

词汇for example 例如；remember 记得；one another 彼此 语法for example 引出例子；one another 表示“彼此”。

Elephants are very kind too.

大象也非常善良。

词汇kind 善良的；too 也 语法too 放句末，表示“也”。

They look after other elephants when they don't feel well.

当其他大象不舒服时，它们会照顾它们。

词汇look after 照顾；feel well 感觉良好 语法when 引导时间状语从句；look after 是固定短语。

The big elephants also help the baby ones.

成年大象也会帮助小象。

词汇baby 幼小的；one 代指大象 语法ones 代替前面提到的复数名词 elephants，避免重复。

Elephants are an important part of Thai life and culture.

大象是泰国生活和文化的重要组成部分。

词汇important 重要的；culture 文化 语法an important part of... 表示“……的重要部分”。

However, they are in danger.

然而，它们正处于危险之中。

词汇however 然而；in danger 处于危险中 语法however 表示转折；in danger 是固定短语。

They live in forests, but people cut down too many trees.

它们生活在森林里，但人们砍伐了太多树木。

词汇forest 森林；cut down 砍倒 语法but 表转折；too many + 可数名词复数。

People also kill elephants for their ivory.

人们还为了象牙而猎杀大象。

词汇kill 杀死；ivory 象牙 语法for 表示目的，意为“为了”。

Let's save the forests and not buy things made of ivory.

让我们保护森林，不买象牙制品吧。

词汇save 保护；拯救；made of 由……制成 语法Let's + 动词原形；made of ivory 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

Every elephant counts.

每一头大象都很重要。

词汇count 有重要性；算数 语法count 在此表示“重要、有价值”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P6 Section B 1b 中 Malee 的帖子。Malee 住在泰国，她最喜欢大象：大象强壮又聪明，是泰国好运的象征，也是泰国的国宝动物；每年 3 月 13 日，泰国人庆祝大象节。大象看起来与其他动物很不一样——它们体型庞大，有大耳朵和长鼻子，能用鼻子捡起并搬运重物。在某些方面大象和我们很像：它们很爱玩，喜欢在水里玩耍，还是游泳好手；它们很聪明，能记住彼此和有食物、水的地方；它们也很善良，会照顾身体不舒服的同伴，成年大象还会帮助小象。大象是泰国生活和文化的重要组成部分，然而它们正处于危险之中：人们砍伐森林，为了象牙猎杀大象。Malee 呼吁大家保护森林、不买象牙制品，因为每一头大象都很重要。

English Summary

This is Malee's post in Section B 1b on page 6. Malee lives in Thailand and her favourite animal is the elephant. Elephants are strong and clever, they are a symbol of good luck in Thailand, and they are Thailand's national animal. On 13 March, Thai people celebrate Thai Elephant Day. Elephants look very different from other animals: they are huge, have large ears and long trunks, and can pick up and carry heavy things with their trunks. In some ways elephants are like us: they are playful, love to play in the water and are great swimmers; they are clever and can remember one another and places with food and water after many years; they are kind and look after other elephants that don't feel well, and the big elephants help the baby ones. Elephants are an important part of Thai life and culture. However, they are in danger: people cut down too many trees and kill elephants for their ivory. Malee calls on people to save the forests and not buy things made of ivory, because every elephant counts.

be like 表示“像”；in some ways 表示“在某些方面”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I love elephants because they are strong and clever.

我喜欢大象，因为它们强壮又聪明。

because 引导原因状语从句，回答 why 的提问；主句在前、原因在后是常见顺序。

For example, they can remember one another and places with food and water after many years.

例如，多年之后它们还能记住彼此以及有食物和水的地方。

for example 用于举例；one another 表示“彼此”；after many years 表示“多年之后”。

Let's save the forests and not buy things made of ivory.

让我们保护森林，不买象牙制品吧。

made of ivory 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 things；Let's 后接两个并列动词 save 和 not buy。

Reading Plus · The Amazing Octopus

Reading Plus 主题：奇妙的章鱼 · 保护自然

Among the many amazing animals in the world, few are as strange and unusual as the octopus.

世界上许多神奇的动物中，很少有像章鱼这样奇特又不寻常的。

词汇among 在……之中；strange 奇怪的；unusual 不寻常的；octopus 章鱼 语法among 表示“在……之中”；as + 形容词 + as 表示“和……一样”。

There are around 300 different kinds of octopuses, and they come in all sizes!

章鱼大约有 300 种，大小不一！

词汇around 大约；kind 种类；size 大小 语法There are + 复数名词；come in all sizes 表示“大小不一”。

The smallest is only around 2.5 centimetres long, but some octopuses can grow up to more than five metres long.

最小的只有约 2.5 厘米长，但有些章鱼能长到 5 米多长。

词汇smallest 最小的；centimetre 厘米；metre 米；grow up 长大 语法the + 形容词最高级表示“最……的”；but 表转折。

There was even an octopus over nine metres long!

甚至还有超过 9 米长的章鱼！

词汇even 甚至；over 超过 语法There was + 单数名词；over + 数量表示“超过”。

The octopus's body is very interesting.

章鱼的身体非常有趣。

词汇body 身体；interesting 有趣的 语法名词所有格 octopus's 表示“章鱼的”。

All octopuses have eight arms.

所有的章鱼都有八条腕。

词汇eight 八；arm 腕；手臂 语法have + 名词表示“有”；eight 后接复数名词 arms。

Their brain is not just in their head, but also in their arms!

它们的大脑不只在头部，也在腕里！

词汇brain 大脑；head 头 语法not just... but also... 表示“不仅……而且……”。

This helps them to think about and do more than one thing at once.

这帮助它们同时思考和做不止一件事。

词汇think about 思考；at once 同时 语法help sb. to do sth.；at once 表示“同时”。

Octopuses can also fit into small places because they do not have any bones!

章鱼还能钻进狭小的地方，因为它们没有骨头！

词汇fit into 钻进；塞进；bone 骨头 语法because 引导原因状语从句；fit into 表示“钻进；塞进”。

And did you know that octopuses have three hearts?

而且你知道章鱼有三颗心脏吗？

词汇heart 心脏 语法did you know that...? 表示“你知道吗……”，后接宾语从句。

These hearts work with different parts of the body.

这些心脏与身体的不同部位协同工作。

词汇work with 与……配合；part 部分 语法work with 在此表示“与……配合”。

Octopuses can also do many amazing things.

章鱼还能做许多神奇的事。

词汇amazing 神奇的 语法can + 动词原形；also 放在情态动词后。

They can change how they look.

它们能改变自己的外表。

词汇change 改变；look 外表 语法how they look 是宾语从句，表示“它们的样子”。

They can change their colour and shape to look like plants or other sea animals.

它们能改变颜色和形状，看起来像植物或其他海洋动物。

词汇colour 颜色；shape 形状；look like 看起来像 语法to do 作目的状语；look like 表示“看起来像”。

Octopuses can also make ink.

章鱼还能制造墨汁。

词汇make ink 制造墨汁 语法make + 名词表示“制造”；also 放在情态动词后。

They keep it inside very small bags in their body.

它们把墨汁储存在体内很小的囊中。

词汇keep 保存；inside 在……里面；bag 囊；袋 语法keep + 宾语 + 介词短语，表示“把……保存在……”。

When they produce ink, the ink makes it hard for other animals to see them.

当它们分泌墨汁时，墨汁使其他动物很难看清它们。

词汇produce 产生；分泌；hard 困难的 语法when 引导时间状语从句；make it hard for sb. to do sth. 表示“使某人难以做某事”。

This helps octopuses to get away fast.

这帮助章鱼快速逃走。

词汇get away 逃走；fast 快速地 语法help sb. to do sth.；get away 是固定短语。

What's more, when an octopus loses an arm, it can grow it back!

而且，章鱼失去一条腕后还能重新长出来！

词汇what's more 而且；lose 失去；grow back 重新长出 语法What's more 表示“而且”；when 引导时间状语从句。

Studying amazing animals like the octopus is important because we can learn a lot from them.

研究像章鱼这样的神奇动物很重要，因为我们可以从它们身上学到很多。

词汇study 研究；learn from 向……学习 语法动名词短语作主语；because 引导原因状语从句。

But they also show us how wonderful nature is and why it is important to protect it!

但它们也向我们展示了大自然是多么奇妙，以及为什么保护大自然很重要！

词汇wonderful 极好的；nature 大自然；protect 保护 语法how/why 引导宾语从句；it is important to do sth. 表示“做某事很重要”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇 Reading Plus 介绍了神奇的章鱼。世界上许多神奇的动物中，很少有像章鱼这样奇特又不寻常的。章鱼大约有 300 种，大小不一：最小的只有约 2.5 厘米长，最大的能长到 5 米多，甚至还有 9 米多长的章鱼。章鱼的身体很有趣：它们有八条腕；大脑不只在头部，也在腕里，这帮助它们同时思考和做不止一件事；它们没有骨头，所以能钻进狭小的地方；它们还有三颗心脏。章鱼能做许多神奇的事：能改变外表，改变颜色和形状来伪装成植物或其他海洋动物；还能制造墨汁，把墨汁储存在体内很小的囊中。遇到危险时，墨汁让其他动物难以看清它们，帮助它们快速逃走。章鱼失去一条腕还能重新长出来。研究章鱼这样的神奇动物很重要，因为我们可以从它们身上学到很多，也能感受到大自然的神奇，明白保护大自然的重要性。

English Summary

This Reading Plus text introduces the amazing octopus. Among the many amazing animals in the world, few are as strange and unusual as the octopus. There are around 300 different kinds of octopuses, and they come in all sizes: the smallest is only around 2.5 centimetres long, while some can grow to more than five metres long, and there was even an octopus over nine metres long. The octopus's body is very interesting. It has eight arms, and its brain is not just in its head but also in its arms, which helps it think about and do more than one thing at once. Having no bones, octopuses can fit into small places, and they have three hearts. Octopuses can do many amazing things: they can change how they look, changing their colour and shape to look like plants or other sea animals; they can also make ink and keep it inside very small bags in their body. The ink makes it hard for other animals to see them and helps them get away fast. An octopus can even grow a lost arm back. Studying amazing animals like the octopus is important because we can learn a lot from them and see how wonderful nature is and why we should protect it.

There are around 300 different kinds of octopuses, and they come in all sizes!

The octopus's body is very interesting.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Their brain is not just in their head, but also in their arms!

它们的大脑不只在头部，也在腕里！

not just ... but also ... 表示“不仅……而且……”；their 指代 octopuses。

These hearts work with different parts of the body.

这些心脏与身体的不同部位协同工作。

work with 在此表示“与……配合”；different parts of the body 表示“身体的不同部位”。

When they produce ink, the ink makes it hard for other animals to see them.

当它们分泌墨汁时，墨汁使其他动物很难看清它们。

make it hard for sb. to do sth. 中 it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是 to see them；hard 作宾语补足语。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面 3 篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What is Peter's favourite animal?

  1. The penguin.
  2. The elephant.
  3. The octopus.
  4. The wolf.
答案与解析

A 原文：Peter: The penguin is my favourite animal. 可知 Peter 最喜欢的动物是企鹅。

2. What can't penguins do?

  1. Swim fast.
  2. Walk in a funny way.
  3. Stand close together.
  4. Fly like other birds.
答案与解析

D 原文：They can't fly like other birds, but they can swim fast. 可知企鹅不会像其他鸟一样飞。

3. What does Teng Fei think penguins eat?

  1. Sandwiches.
  2. Fish and small sea animals.
  3. Plants.
  4. Small birds.
答案与解析

B 原文：Teng Fei: Fish and small sea animals, I think. 可知 Teng Fei 认为企鹅吃鱼和小型海洋动物。

4. Why do penguins often stand close together?

  1. Because they are shy.
  2. Because they are playing a game.
  3. Because it is very cold there.
  4. Because they are sleeping.
答案与解析

C 原文：It's very cold there, so they often stand close together. It helps them keep warm. 可知因为南极洲很冷，企鹅靠在一起取暖。

5. Where does Malee live?

  1. In China.
  2. In Antarctica.
  3. In the UK.
  4. In Thailand.
答案与解析

D 原文：Hi, I'm Malee and I live in Thailand! 可知 Malee 住在泰国。

6. What do Thai people celebrate on 13 March?

  1. Thai Elephant Day.
  2. National Animal Day.
  3. Thai Culture Day.
  4. Good Luck Day.
答案与解析

A 原文：On 13 March, we celebrate Thai Elephant Day. 可知 3 月 13 日是泰国大象节。

7. How many hearts do octopuses have?

  1. One.
  2. Two.
  3. Three.
  4. Four.
答案与解析

C 原文：And did you know that octopuses have three hearts? 可知章鱼有三颗心脏。

8. What can octopuses do when they lose an arm?

  1. They can make ink.
  2. They can grow it back.
  3. They can change colour.
  4. They can fit into small places.
答案与解析

B 原文：What's more, when an octopus loses an arm, it can grow it back! 可知章鱼失去一条腕后能重新长出来。
