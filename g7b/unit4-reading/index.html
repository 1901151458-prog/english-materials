Section A 2a · At the Restaurant

Section A 2a 主题：餐厅点餐 · 礼貌请求

Waiter: Hello! What would you like to order?

你好！你想点什么？

词汇waiter 服务员；order 点餐 语法What would you like to do? 是礼貌的询问；图中末词漏印，按教材补 order?。

Tom: What about Gongbao chicken?

宫保鸡丁怎么样？

词汇what about...? ……怎么样；Gongbao chicken 宫保鸡丁 语法What about...? 用于提出建议。

Tom: We often have it in America, but it may taste different here.

我们在美国经常吃这道菜，但这里的味道可能不一样。

词汇often 经常；taste 尝起来；different 不同的 语法may + 动词原形表示“可能”；taste 是系动词，后接形容词。

Tom: I'd like to try it.

我想尝尝。

词汇I'd like to 我想要；try 尝试 语法I'd = I would；I'd like to do sth. 表示“我想要做某事”。

Dad: Good idea!

好主意！

词汇good idea 好主意 语法Good idea! 用于赞同对方的建议。

Mum: Do you have anything with tofu?

你们有什么带豆腐的菜吗？

词汇anything 任何东西；tofu 豆腐 语法anything 用于疑问句；with tofu 是介词短语作后置定语。

Waiter: Yes.

有的。

词汇yes 是的 语法Yes. 是对一般疑问句的肯定回答。

Waiter: Would you like a hot tofu dish or a cold one?

你想要热豆腐还是凉豆腐？

词汇hot 热的；cold 凉的；dish 菜肴；one 代指 dish 语法Would you like...? 礼貌地询问意愿；one 代替前面出现的 dish。

Mum: A hot tofu dish, please.

请来一份热豆腐。

词汇a hot tofu dish 一份热豆腐 语法点餐常用省略句，完整意思是 I'd like a hot tofu dish.。

Waiter: We have Mapo tofu.

我们有麻婆豆腐。

词汇Mapo tofu 麻婆豆腐 语法have 在此表示“有、供应”。

Waiter: Would you like that?

你想要那个吗？

词汇that 那个 语法Would you like...? 用于礼貌地询问对方是否需要。

Mum: Yes, please.

好的，请来一份。

词汇yes, please 好的，请 语法Yes, please. 是接受对方提议时的礼貌回答。

Mum: We should also order some vegetables / a salad.

我们还应该点些蔬菜 / 一份沙拉。

词汇should 应该；order 点（餐）；vegetables 蔬菜；salad 沙拉 语法should + 动词原形表示建议；斜杠前后为教材两个选项。

Dad: How about a chicken and cabbage salad?

鸡肉卷心菜沙拉怎么样？

词汇how about...? ……怎么样；cabbage 卷心菜 语法How about...? 与 What about...? 同义，用于提出建议。

Tom: That sounds good.

听起来不错。

词汇sound 听起来 语法sound 是系动词，后接形容词 good。

Tom: Can we order / get soup too?

我们还能点 / 要汤吗？

词汇can 能；order 点；get 要；soup 汤；too 也 语法Can we...? 询问能否做某事；斜杠前后为教材两个选项。

Dad: OK.

好的。

词汇OK 好的 语法OK. 表示同意。

Dad: Let's see ...

让我看看……

词汇let's see 让我看看 语法Let's see ... 表示“让我想想/看看”，常用于思考或选择时。

Dad: Which soup would you like, chicken or fish?

你想喝哪种汤，鸡汤还是鱼汤？

词汇which 哪一个；chicken 鸡肉（汤）；fish 鱼（汤） 语法Which + 名词 + would you like...? 提供选项让对方选择。

Mum: Fish, please!

请来鱼汤！

词汇fish 鱼（汤） 语法省略句，完整意思是 Fish soup, please.。

Dad: Sure.

好的。

词汇sure 当然；好的 语法Sure. 是口语中表示同意的回答。

Dad: Now, would you like rice or dumplings / noodles?

现在，你想吃米饭还是饺子 / 面条？

词汇rice 米饭；dumplings 饺子；noodles 面条 语法would like 后接名词，用 or 连接两个选项。

Tom: Rice, please.

请来米饭。

词汇rice 米饭 语法省略句，完整意思是 Rice, please.。

Mum: OK. Let's get that.

好的。我们就点这些吧。

词汇get 拿到；that 那个（指所点的菜） 语法Let's + 动词原形，表示决定或建议。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P27 的餐厅听读圈词对话（听录音，圈出听到的彩色单词；斜杠前后为教材给出的两个选项）。服务员欢迎一家人并问他们想点什么。Tom 提议点宫保鸡丁，说在美国常吃这道菜，但这里的味道可能不同，想尝一尝；Dad 赞同。Mum 问有没有带豆腐的菜，服务员介绍了热豆腐和凉豆腐，并推荐麻婆豆腐；Mum 点了一份热豆腐，还说应该再点些蔬菜或一份沙拉。Dad 建议点鸡肉卷心菜沙拉，Tom 觉得不错，又问能不能再点汤。Dad 让大家选汤，Mum 要鱼汤。最后 Dad 问要米饭还是饺子/面条，Tom 要米饭，Mum 决定就点这些。

English Summary

This is the restaurant conversation on page 27. It is the listen-and-circle activity: students circle the coloured words they hear, and the words before and after each slash are the two choices in the textbook. The waiter greets the family and asks what they would like to order. Tom suggests Gongbao chicken, saying they often have it in America but it may taste different here, and he would like to try it; Dad thinks that is a good idea. Mum asks if they have anything with tofu, and the waiter offers a hot tofu dish or a cold one and mentions Mapo tofu. Mum orders a hot tofu dish and says they should also order some vegetables / a salad. Dad suggests a chicken and cabbage salad, Tom likes the idea and asks if they can order / get soup too. Dad lets everyone choose soup, and Mum asks for fish. Finally Dad asks whether they would like rice or dumplings / noodles; Tom chooses rice, and Mum decides that is their order.

语法点 Grammar

Hello! What would you like to order?

would like to do sth. 表示“想要做某事”，比 want 更礼貌。

What about Gongbao chicken?

What about ...? 用于提出建议。

We often have it in America, but it may taste different here.

may + 动词原形表示“可能”；but 表转折。

I'd like to try it.

I'd = I would；I'd like to do sth. 表示“我想要做某事”。

Do you have anything with tofu?

anything 用于疑问句；with tofu 是介词短语作后置定语。

Would you like a hot tofu dish or a cold one?

Would you like ...? 礼貌地询问意愿；one 代替 dish。

A hot tofu dish, please.

点餐常用省略句，完整意思是 I'd like a hot tofu dish.。

We should also order some vegetables / a salad.

should + 动词原形表示建议；斜杠前后为教材两个选项。

How about a chicken and cabbage salad?

How about ...? 与 What about ...? 同义，用于提出建议。

Can we order / get soup too?

Can we ...? 询问能否做某事；too 表示“也”。

Which soup would you like, chicken or fish?

Which + 名词 + would you like ...? 提供选项让对方选择。

Would you like rice or dumplings / noodles?

would like 后接名词，用 or 连接两个选项。

OK. Let's get that.

Let's + 动词原形，表示决定或建议。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

We often have it in America, but it may taste different here.

我们在美国经常吃这道菜，但这里的味道可能不一样。

have 在此表示“吃”；may 表示可能性；taste 是系动词，后接形容词 different。

Would you like a hot tofu dish or a cold one?

你想要热豆腐还是凉豆腐？

one 代替前面出现的 dish，避免重复；or 连接两个选择项。

Can we order / get soup too?

我们还能点 / 要汤吗？

order 和 get 都是“点（餐）、要（菜）”的意思，斜杠前后为教材两个选项。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看 P27 的插图，猜一猜这家人正在哪里、准备做什么。

  • 边读边画出点餐和询问意愿的句子（What about ...? / Would you like ...?）。

Section B 1b · Improve Your Eating Habits

Section B 1b 主题：饮食习惯 · 健康生活

Healthy eating is important for a healthy body and mind.

健康饮食对健康的身体和头脑很重要。

词汇healthy 健康的；mind 头脑 语法动名词短语 healthy eating 作主语，谓语用单数 is。

Both what we eat and how we eat are important!

我们吃什么和怎么吃都很重要！

词汇both...and... ……和……都；what we eat 我们吃什么 语法both ... and ... 连接两个主语从句，谓语用复数 are。

For example, some people have fast food every week.

例如，有些人每周都吃快餐。

词汇for example 例如；fast food 快餐 语法for example 用于举例；every week 表示频率。

It is easy and delicious!

它既方便又美味！

词汇easy 方便的；容易的；delicious 美味的 语法It 指代上文提到的 fast food。

But it often has lots of salt, fat, and sugar.

但它往往含有很多盐、脂肪和糖。

词汇salt 盐；fat 脂肪；sugar 糖 语法lots of + 不可数名词；but 表转折。

That makes us put on weight.

那会让我们发胖。

词汇make sb. do sth. 使某人做某事；put on weight 发胖 语法make sb. do sth. 用不带 to 的动词原形；put on weight 表示“发胖”。

Eating fast food like pizza and hamburgers too often may cause heart problems later.

太频繁地吃披萨、汉堡这类快餐，以后可能引发心脏问题。

词汇cause 引起；heart problem 心脏问题；later 以后 语法动名词短语作主语；may + 动词原形表示可能。

If you cook your own meals instead, you can make healthy balanced meals.

如果你改为自己做饭，就能做出健康均衡的饭菜。

词汇cook 做饭；instead 代替；balanced 均衡的 语法if 引导条件状语从句；instead 表示“代替、而”。

Eating three meals a day is important.

一日三餐很重要。

词汇three meals a day 一日三餐 语法动名词短语作主语；three meals a day 表示“一日三餐”。

But some people don't have breakfast.

但有些人不吃早饭。

词汇have breakfast 吃早饭 语法have 与三餐名词连用表示“吃”；breakfast 前不加冠词。

Maybe they don't feel hungry in the morning, or they are too busy to eat anything.

也许他们早上不觉得饿，或者太忙了，没时间吃东西。

词汇maybe 也许；feel hungry 感到饿；too...to... 太……而不能…… 语法or 连接两个并列原因；too ... to ... 表示“太……而不能……”。

But food gives us energy.

但食物给我们能量。

词汇energy 能量 语法give sb. sth. 双宾语结构；but 表转折。

It is easy to feel sleepy and find it hard to focus on our work or studies if we don't eat breakfast.

如果不吃早饭，我们很容易感到困倦，也很难集中精力学习或工作。

词汇sleepy 困倦的；focus on 专注于；studies 学业 语法It is + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；find it hard to do sth. 中 it 是形式宾语。

Some people also eat too many sweet things.

有些人还吃太多甜食。

词汇too many 太多；sweet things 甜食 语法too many + 可数名词复数。

Ice cream, sweets, and chocolate are delicious, but they usually have lots of sugar.

冰淇淋、糖果和巧克力很美味，但它们通常含有很多糖。

词汇ice cream 冰淇淋；sweets 糖果；chocolate 巧克力 语法but 表转折；usually 是频率副词。

This can cause tooth problems.

这会导致牙齿问题。

词汇cause 导致；tooth 牙齿 语法can + 动词原形表示可能；tooth problems 表示“牙齿问题”。

When you want a snack, why don't you have some fruit instead?

当你想吃零食时，为什么不改吃点水果呢？

词汇snack 零食；instead 代替 语法why don't you ...? 用于提出建议；when 引导时间状语从句。

After all, an apple a day keeps the doctor away.

毕竟，一天一苹果，医生远离我。

词汇after all 毕竟；keep...away 使……远离 语法an apple a day keeps the doctor away 是英语谚语。

Remember: You are what you eat.

记住：人如其食。

词汇remember 记住 语法You are what you eat 是谚语，意为“饮食习惯反映人的健康状况”。

Healthy eating means eating healthy food and having good eating habits.

健康饮食意味着吃健康的食物、养成良好的饮食习惯。

词汇mean 意味着；eating habits 饮食习惯 语法mean doing sth. 表示“意味着做某事”；and 连接两个动名词。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P30 Joy Clinic 通讯上的文章《改善你的饮食习惯》。文章说，健康饮食对健康的身体和头脑很重要，吃什么、怎么吃都很关键。比如，有些人每周都吃快餐，快餐方便又美味，但往往含有很多盐、脂肪和糖，会让人发胖；常吃披萨、汉堡还可能引发心脏问题，而自己做饭则可以做出健康均衡的饭菜。文章还指出，一日三餐很重要，但有些人不吃早饭，可能因为早上不饿或太忙；食物给我们能量，不吃早饭容易犯困、难以集中注意力。有些人还吃太多甜食，冰淇淋、糖果和巧克力虽然美味，但含糖多，会导致牙齿问题；想吃零食时不如吃点水果。最后文章提醒我们：人如其食，健康饮食意味着吃健康的食物、养成良好的饮食习惯。

English Summary

This is the article "Improve Your Eating Habits" from the Joy Clinic newsletter on page 30. It says that healthy eating is important for a healthy body and mind, and that both what we eat and how we eat matter. For example, some people have fast food every week; it is easy and delicious, but it often has lots of salt, fat and sugar, which makes us put on weight. Eating fast food like pizza and hamburgers too often may cause heart problems later, while cooking our own meals can make healthy balanced meals. The article also says that eating three meals a day is important, but some people don't have breakfast because they don't feel hungry in the morning or are too busy to eat anything. Food gives us energy, and without breakfast it is easy to feel sleepy and hard to focus on our work or studies. Some people also eat too many sweet things; ice cream, sweets and chocolate are delicious but usually have lots of sugar and can cause tooth problems, so it is better to have some fruit as a snack. Finally, the article reminds us: "You are what you eat" — healthy eating means eating healthy food and having good eating habits.

8. Why is eating balanced meals important when we are young?

  1. Because it makes us fat.
