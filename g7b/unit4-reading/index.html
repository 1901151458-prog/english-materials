Section A 3a · What Do You Usually Eat?

Section A 3a 主题：一日三餐与健康饮食 · 对话

Lily: Tom, what do you usually have for breakfast?

汤姆，你早饭通常吃什么？

词汇usually 通常；have...for breakfast 早饭吃…… 语法what do you usually have...? 询问饮食习惯。

Tom: I usually have two eggs, a piece of bread and a glass of milk.

我通常吃两个鸡蛋、一片面包和一杯牛奶。

词汇a piece of bread 一片面包；a glass of milk 一杯牛奶 语法可数名词用数词修饰；不可数名词用量词 a piece of / a glass of。

Lily: That sounds healthy.

听起来很健康。

词汇sound 听起来；healthy 健康的 语法sound + adj. 作表语；that 指代上文。

Tom: Yes, but I don't eat any meat in the morning.

是的，不过我早上不吃肉。

词汇meat 肉；in the morning 在早上 语法any 用于否定句，修饰不可数名词。

Lily: Do you eat vegetables every day?

你每天都吃蔬菜吗？

词汇vegetable 蔬菜 语法Do you...? 一般现在时疑问句；every day 表频率。

Tom: I eat some carrots or tomatoes for lunch.

我午饭会吃一些胡萝卜或西红柿。

词汇some 一些；carrot 胡萝卜；tomato 西红柿 语法some 用于肯定句，修饰复数可数名词。

Lily: Is there any juice in your fridge?

你冰箱里有果汁吗？

词汇fridge 冰箱；juice 果汁 语法Is there...? 询问可数/不可数事物的存在；any 用于疑问句。

Tom: Yes, there is, but there isn't any cola.

有的，不过没有可乐。

词汇cola 可乐 语法there be 结构；isn't = is not，there isn't any...。

Lily: I have three bottles of water in my bag.

我包里有三瓶水。

词汇bottle 瓶子；water 水 语法复数量词 bottles of + 不可数名词，谓语用复数。

Tom: That's good. Let's eat more fruit and less sugar.

那很好。我们多吃水果、少吃糖吧。

词汇fruit 水果；sugar 糖；less 更少的 语法Let's + 动词原形表建议；more / less 比较级。

Lily: OK. We should eat well to keep healthy.

好。为了保持健康，我们应该吃得好。

词汇keep healthy 保持健康 语法should + 动词原形；to keep healthy 表目的。

Tom: Right. Healthy food gives us energy every day.

对。健康食物每天给我们能量。

词汇energy 能量 语法give sb. sth. 双宾语结构。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕一日三餐展开。莉莉问汤姆早饭通常吃什么，汤姆说通常吃两个鸡蛋、一片面包和一杯牛奶，早上不吃肉。莉莉提醒他要吃蔬菜，汤姆说午饭会吃一些胡萝卜或西红柿。莉莉问冰箱里有没有果汁，汤姆说有的，但没有可乐；莉莉说自己包里有三瓶水。最后两人都表示要多吃水果、少吃糖，通过吃得好来保持健康，因为健康食物每天给我们能量。

English Summary

The conversation is about everyday meals. Lily asks Tom what he usually has for breakfast, and he says two eggs, a piece of bread and a glass of milk, with no meat in the morning. Lily reminds him about vegetables, and Tom says he eats some carrots or tomatoes for lunch. Then Lily asks if there is any juice in his fridge; Tom says there is, but there isn't any cola, and Lily says she has three bottles of water in her bag. Finally they agree to eat more fruit and less sugar and to eat well to keep healthy, because healthy food gives us energy every day.

语法点 Grammar

I usually have two eggs.

可数名词复数 eggs 用数词 two 修饰。

a piece of bread and a glass of milk

量词短语 a piece of / a glass of + 不可数名词。

I don't eat any meat in the morning.

any 用于否定句，修饰不可数名词 meat。

Do you eat vegetables every day?

一般现在时疑问句，助动词 Do 提前。

I eat some carrots or tomatoes for lunch.

some 用于肯定句，修饰复数可数名词。

Is there any juice in your fridge?

Is there any...? 询问不可数名词或单数可数名词是否存在。

Yes, there is, but there isn't any cola.

there is / there isn't any 表示存在与不存在。

I have three bottles of water in my bag.

复数量词 bottles of + 不可数名词，谓语用 have。

Let's eat more fruit and less sugar.

Let's + 动词原形表建议；more / less 修饰名词表比较。

We should eat well to keep healthy.

should + 动词原形；to keep healthy 作目的状语。

Healthy food gives us energy every day.

give sb. sth. 双宾语；不可数名词动词用第三人称单数形式。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I usually have two eggs, a piece of bread and a glass of milk.

我通常吃两个鸡蛋、一片面包和一杯牛奶。

bread 和 milk 不可数，要借助 a piece of / a glass of 这样的量词来表达数量。

Yes, there is, but there isn't any cola.

有的，不过没有可乐。

there be 的肯定与否定形式并列表述；any 用于否定句，表示“任何”。

We should eat well to keep healthy.

为了保持健康，我们应该吃得好。

should + 动词原形提建议；to keep healthy 是不定式作目的状语。

Section B 1b · Eat Well, Stay Healthy

Section B 1b 主题：食物与健康 · 说明文

Food is important for all of us.

食物对我们所有人都很重要。

词汇important 重要的 语法be important for sb.。

There is a lot of food in the supermarket, but not all of it is good for our health.

超市里有很多食物，但并不是所有食物都有利于健康。

词汇supermarket 超市；be good for 对……有益 语法there be 结构；but 表示转折。

Some food is healthy, and some food is not.

有些食物是健康的，有些则不是。

词汇some...some... 有些……有些…… 语法some 作主语，指代不同类别。

There are many kinds of healthy food, such as vegetables, fruit and fish.

健康食物有很多种，比如蔬菜、水果和鱼。

词汇kind 种类；such as 例如；fish 鱼 语法There are + 复数名词；such as 列举。

These foods give us energy and help us grow.

这些食物给我们能量，帮助我们成长。

词汇energy 能量；grow 成长 语法help sb. do sth.。

We should eat some vegetables and fruit every day.

我们应该每天吃一些蔬菜和水果。

词汇should 应该 语法should + 动词原形；every day 表频率。

But there is too much sugar in many drinks.

但很多饮料里的糖太多了。

词汇too much 太多；sugar 糖；drink 饮料 语法too much + 不可数名词。

There isn't any cola in a healthy lunch box.

健康的午餐盒里没有可乐。

词汇lunch box 午餐盒；cola 可乐 语法there isn't any... 否定存在；any 用于否定句。

Some students eat too many hamburgers and too few vegetables.

有些学生吃太多汉堡、吃太少蔬菜。

词汇too many 太多（可数）；too few 太少 语法too many / too few + 复数可数名词。

Is there any good way to eat well?

有没有什么吃好的办法呢？

词汇way 方法；eat well 吃得好 语法Is there any...? 询问是否存在。

Yes. We can have a glass of milk and a piece of bread for breakfast.

有。早饭我们可以喝一杯牛奶、吃一片面包。

词汇a glass of 一杯；a piece of 一片 语法量词短语修饰不可数名词。

We can also take some fruit to school and have it after lunch.

我们还可以带些水果去学校，午饭后吃。

词汇take 带；after lunch 午饭后 语法can + 动词原形；take sth. to... 把某物带到……。

If we eat well every day, we will stay healthy and feel happy.

如果我们每天吃得好，我们会保持健康、心情愉快。

词汇stay healthy 保持健康 语法if 引导条件状语从句，主将从现。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

短文说明食物与健康的关系。食物对每个人都很重要，超市里食物很多，但并非都对健康有益。健康食物有很多种，比如蔬菜、水果和鱼，它们给我们能量、帮助我们成长，我们应该每天吃一些。但很多饮料里糖分太多，健康的午餐盒里不会有可乐；有些学生吃太多汉堡、蔬菜又吃得太少。文中提出吃好的办法：早饭喝一杯牛奶、吃一片面包，还可以带水果去学校午饭后吃。如果每天吃得好，我们就会保持健康、心情愉快。

English Summary

The passage explains how food and health are connected. Food is important for everyone, and the supermarket has a lot of food, but not all of it is good for us. There are many kinds of healthy food, such as vegetables, fruit and fish; they give us energy and help us grow, so we should eat some every day. However, many drinks have too much sugar, a healthy lunch box has no cola, and some students eat too many hamburgers and too few vegetables. The passage suggests having a glass of milk and a piece of bread for breakfast and taking some fruit to school to eat after lunch. If we eat well every day, we will stay healthy and feel happy.

语法点 Grammar

Food is important for all of us.

不可数名词 food 作主语，谓语用 is。

There is a lot of food in the supermarket.

there be 结构；a lot of 修饰不可数名词。

Some food is healthy, and some food is not.

some 修饰不可数名词，谓语用单数 is。

There are many kinds of healthy food.

There are + 复数名词 kinds；many 修饰复数名词。

These foods give us energy and help us grow.

help sb. do sth.；give sb. sth.。

We should eat some vegetables and fruit every day.

should + 动词原形；some 用于肯定句。

there is too much sugar in many drinks

too much + 不可数名词，表示“太多”。

There isn't any cola in a healthy lunch box.

there isn't any...；any 用于否定句。

Some students eat too many hamburgers and too few vegetables.

too many / too few + 复数可数名词。

Is there any good way to eat well?

Is there any...? 询问是否存在；to eat well 作定语。

If we eat well every day, we will stay healthy.

if 引导条件状语从句，主将从现。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

There is a lot of food in the supermarket, but not all of it is good for our health.

超市里有很多食物，但并不是所有食物都有利于健康。

not all of it 是部分否定，意为“并非全部”；主语中心词为不可数名词，用 there is。

Some students eat too many hamburgers and too few vegetables.

有些学生吃太多汉堡、吃太少蔬菜。

too many 修饰复数可数名词，too few 表示“太少”，二者构成对比。

If we eat well every day, we will stay healthy and feel happy.

如果我们每天吃得好，我们会保持健康、心情愉快。

if 引导条件状语从句用一般现在时，主句用一般将来时 will do。

Section B 3a · A Letter About My Healthy Eating Plan

Section B 3a 主题：健康饮食计划 · 书信

Dear Amy,

亲爱的艾米，

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

How are you? I am writing to tell you about my healthy eating plan.

你好吗？我写信是想告诉你我的健康饮食计划。

词汇healthy eating plan 健康饮食计划 语法现在进行时表当前动作；to tell 作目的状语。

There is a small kitchen in our new school, and the food there is fresh.

我们新学校有一个小食堂，那里的饭菜很新鲜。

词汇kitchen 厨房；fresh 新鲜的 语法there be 结构；there 作地点副词后置。

For breakfast, I have a glass of milk, an egg and some bread.

早饭我喝一杯牛奶、吃一个鸡蛋和一些面包。

词汇an egg 一个鸡蛋；some bread 一些面包 语法a glass of 修饰不可数名词；some 修饰不可数名词。

There isn't any ice cream in my lunch box.

我的午餐盒里没有冰淇淋。

词汇ice cream 冰淇淋；lunch box 午餐盒 语法there isn't any... 否定存在。

In the afternoon, I eat an apple or some grapes.

下午我会吃一个苹果或一些葡萄。

词汇grape 葡萄 语法or 用于否定或选择；some 修饰复数名词。

At dinner, I usually have some rice, fish and vegetables.

晚饭我通常吃一些米饭、鱼和蔬菜。

词汇rice 米饭；vegetable 蔬菜 语法have sth. for dinner 晚饭吃……。

I drink two bottles of water every day, and I don't drink any cola.

我每天喝两瓶水，不喝可乐。

词汇two bottles of water 两瓶水 语法量词复数 + of + 不可数名词；any 用于否定句。

My teacher says we should eat more vegetables and less sugar.

老师说我们应该多吃蔬菜、少吃糖。

词汇less 更少的 语法should + 动词原形；more / less 比较级。

Now I feel much better and I have more energy for sports.

现在我感觉好多了，运动也更有精力了。

词汇feel better 感觉更好；energy 能量 语法feel + adj.；much 修饰比较级。

Please write and tell me about your meals.

请写信告诉我你的三餐。

词汇meal 一餐 语法祈使句；tell sb. about sth.。

Yours,

你的，

词汇yours 你的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Lin Hui

林慧

词汇Lin Hui 林慧（人名） 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是林慧写给艾米的一封信，介绍自己的健康饮食计划。学校新食堂不大，但饭菜很新鲜。她早饭喝一杯牛奶、吃一个鸡蛋和一些面包；午餐盒里没有冰淇淋；下午吃一个苹果或一些葡萄；晚饭通常吃米饭、鱼和蔬菜。她每天喝两瓶水，不喝可乐。老师说应该多吃蔬菜、少吃糖。现在她感觉好多了，运动也更有精力，还请艾米回信讲讲自己的三餐。

English Summary

This is Lin Hui's letter to Amy about her healthy eating plan. The new school kitchen is small but the food is fresh. For breakfast she has a glass of milk, an egg and some bread; there isn't any ice cream in her lunch box; in the afternoon she eats an apple or some grapes; for dinner she usually has some rice, fish and vegetables. She drinks two bottles of water every day and doesn't drink any cola. Her teacher says we should eat more vegetables and less sugar. Now she feels much better and has more energy for sports, and she asks Amy to write back about her meals.

I drink two bottles of water every day, and I don't drink any cola.

我每天喝两瓶水，不喝可乐。

“数量词 + of + 不可数名词”表数量；否定句中用 any 而不是 some。

My teacher says we should eat more vegetables and less sugar.

老师说我们应该多吃蔬菜、少吃糖。

says 后接宾语从句，从句用 should + 动词原形；more 修饰可数名词复数，less 修饰不可数名词。

读前

  • 看标题和书信格式，猜写信内容。
  • 注意结构：问候—计划—三餐—变化—回信。

读中

  • 按早饭、午餐、下午、晚饭列出食物。
  • 找出文中所有量词短语和 some / any 句子。

读后

  • 模仿 3a 给朋友写一封介绍三餐的信。
  • 说说自己打算怎样改变饮食习惯。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What does Tom usually have for breakfast?

  1. An egg, an apple and some juice.
  2. Two eggs, a piece of bread and a glass of milk.
  3. Some rice and fish.
  4. A hamburger and some cola.
答案与解析

B 原文：I usually have two eggs, a piece of bread and a glass of milk.

2. What does Tom eat for lunch?

  1. Some carrots or tomatoes.
  2. Two eggs.
  3. Some grapes.
  4. Ice cream.
答案与解析

A 原文：I eat some carrots or tomatoes for lunch.

3. What is in Lily's bag?

  1. A bottle of juice.
  2. Three bottles of water.
  3. Some bread.
  4. A lunch box.
答案与解析

B 原文：I have three bottles of water in my bag.

4. According to Section B, what can give us energy and help us grow?

  1. Cola and hamburgers.
  2. Sugar and ice cream.
  3. Vegetables, fruit and fish.
  4. Many drinks.
答案与解析

C 原文：There are many kinds of healthy food, such as vegetables, fruit and fish. These foods give us energy and help us grow.

5. What does the writer of Section B think about drinks?

  1. All drinks are healthy.
  2. Many drinks have too much sugar.
  3. We should drink more cola.
  4. Drinks give us no energy.
答案与解析

B 原文：But there is too much sugar in many drinks.

6. What does the teacher say students should do?

  1. Eat more vegetables and less sugar.
  2. Eat more meat and bread.
  3. Drink more cola.
  4. Eat fewer vegetables.
答案与解析

A 原文：My teacher says we should eat more vegetables and less sugar.

7. Why does Lin Hui feel much better now?

  1. Because she eats more meat.
  2. Because she eats well and has more energy.
  3. Because she doesn't do sports.
  4. Because she sleeps all day.
答案与解析

B 原文：Now I feel much better and I have more energy for sports.

8. What is the common theme of the three texts?

  1. Holiday plans.
  2. Rules at school.
  3. Healthy eating and why it matters.
  4. Animals on a farm.
答案与解析

C 三篇分别谈一日三餐、食物与健康的重要性、健康饮食计划，主题都是健康饮食。
