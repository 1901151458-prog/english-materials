Section A 2a · The Emperor's New Clothes

Section A 2a 主题：童话故事 · 皇帝的新装（教材 P59；教材为 circle the coloured words you hear，斜杠选项保留）

Here's The Emperor's New Clothes by Hans Christian Andersen.

这是汉斯·克里斯蒂安·安徒生的《皇帝的新装》。

词汇The Emperor's New Clothes 《皇帝的新装》；Hans Christian Andersen 安徒生（丹麦童话作家） 语法Here's = Here is；by 用于说明作者。

Once upon a time, an emperor loved new clothes very much.

从前，有一位皇帝非常喜欢新衣服。

词汇once upon a time 很久以前；emperor 皇帝；clothes 衣服 语法once upon a time 是故事开头的常用语；loved 是一般过去时。

He wanted to buy some new clothes.

他想买一些新衣服。

词汇want to do sth. 想要做某事；buy 买 语法want to do sth.；一般过去时 wanted。

Two brothers came and lied to him, "We can make wonderful clothes!"

两兄弟来了，对他说谎：“我们能做出奇妙的衣服！”

词汇brother 兄弟；lie 说谎；wonderful 奇妙的 语法and 连接两个并列谓语；直接引语用引号标出。

"But only clever / smart people can see them!"

“但是只有聪明的人才能看见它们！”

词汇only 只有；clever 聪明的；smart 聪明的；see 看见 语法only + 名词；can 是情态动词；斜杠选项 clever / smart 意思相近。

They pretended to make the clothes.

他们假装在制作衣服。

词汇pretend 假装；make 制作 语法pretend to do sth. 假装做某事。

The emperor's officials couldn't see the clothes, but they said, "What lovely clothes!"

皇帝的官员们看不见这些衣服，但他们说：“多么漂亮的衣服啊！”

词汇official 官员；couldn't = could not；lovely 漂亮的 语法but 表转折；What lovely clothes! 是感叹句。

The emperor couldn't see anything either, but he said, "They're beautiful!"

皇帝也什么都看不见，但他说：“它们真漂亮！”

词汇anything 任何东西；either 也（不）；beautiful 美丽的 语法either 用于否定句末尾；They're = They are。

No one wanted to look silly.

没有人想显得愚蠢。

词汇no one 没有人；silly 愚蠢的 语法no one 作主语时谓语用单数；look + 形容词。

The emperor decided to show everyone his new clothes.

皇帝决定给大家看他的新衣服。

词汇decide 决定；show 展示；everyone 每个人 语法decide to do sth.；show sb. sth. 双宾语结构。

People in the street / city praised them.

街上 / 城里的人们都称赞这些衣服。

词汇in the street 在街上；praise 称赞；city 城市 语法in the street 作后置定语；斜杠选项 street / city。

They were afraid to look silly too!

他们也害怕显得愚蠢！

词汇be afraid to do sth. 害怕做某事；too 也 语法be afraid to do sth.；too 用于肯定句末尾。

Suddenly, a boy cried / shouted, "Look! The emperor has no clothes on!"

突然，一个男孩喊道：“看！皇帝没有穿衣服！”

词汇suddenly 突然；cry 喊叫；shout 喊叫 语法suddenly 作状语；直接引语用引号；斜杠选项 cried / shouted。

Everyone looked at one another.

大家互相看着。

词汇look at 看；one another 互相 语法look at one another 表示“互相看”。

They started / began laughing.

他们开始大笑起来。

词汇start 开始；begin 开始；laugh 大笑 语法start / begin doing sth. 开始做某事；斜杠表示两种可选说法。

The end!

故事结束！

词汇the end 结束 语法故事结尾常用语。

Did you like the story?

你喜欢这个故事吗？

词汇like 喜欢；story 故事 语法Did + 主语 + 动词原形? 一般过去时一般疑问句。

Please tell me what you think!

请告诉我你的想法！

词汇tell 告诉；think 认为 语法祈使句 Please + 动词原形；what you think 作宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇故事由 Emma 讲给全班同学听，改编自安徒生童话《皇帝的新装》。从前有一位皇帝非常喜欢新衣服。两个兄弟骗他说能做出奇妙的衣服，而且只有聪明人才能看见，他们假装在制作。皇帝派去的官员们看不见衣服，却称赞衣服漂亮；皇帝自己也看不见，也只好跟着称赞，因为没有人想显得愚蠢。皇帝决定穿上新衣服给大家看，街上的人们也称赞衣服，因为大家同样害怕显得愚蠢。最后，一个男孩喊道：“皇帝没有穿衣服！”大家互相看着，开始大笑起来。

English Summary

Emma shares the story of The Emperor's New Clothes by Hans Christian Andersen. Once upon a time, an emperor loved new clothes very much. Two brothers lied to him that they could make wonderful clothes which only clever people could see, and they pretended to make them. The emperor's officials couldn't see the clothes but said they were lovely, and the emperor himself couldn't see anything either but said they were beautiful — no one wanted to look silly. The emperor decided to show everyone his new clothes, and people in the street praised them because they were afraid to look silly too. Suddenly a boy cried that the emperor had no clothes on, and everyone looked at one another and started laughing.

语法点 Grammar

Here's The Emperor's New Clothes by Hans Christian Andersen.

Here's = Here is；by 用于说明作者。

Once upon a time, an emperor loved new clothes very much.

once upon a time 是故事开头的常用语；一般过去时。

He wanted to buy some new clothes.

want to do sth. 想要做某事；一般过去时 wanted。

Two brothers came and lied to him, "We can make wonderful clothes!"

and 连接两个并列谓语；直接引语用引号标出。

"But only clever / smart people can see them!"

only + 名词；can 是情态动词；斜杠选项 clever / smart。

They pretended to make the clothes.

pretend to do sth. 假装做某事。

The emperor's officials couldn't see the clothes, but they said, "What lovely clothes!"

but 表转折；What lovely clothes! 是感叹句。

The emperor couldn't see anything either, but he said, "They're beautiful!"

either 用于否定句末尾；They're = They are。

No one wanted to look silly.

no one 作主语，谓语用单数；look + 形容词。

The emperor decided to show everyone his new clothes.

decide to do sth.；show sb. sth. 双宾语结构。

People in the street / city praised them.

in the street 作后置定语；斜杠选项 street / city。

They were afraid to look silly too!

be afraid to do sth.；too 用于肯定句末尾。

Suddenly, a boy cried / shouted, "Look! The emperor has no clothes on!"

suddenly 作状语；直接引语；斜杠选项 cried / shouted。

Everyone looked at one another.

look at one another 表示“互相看”。

They started / began laughing.

start / begin doing sth. 开始做某事；斜杠表示两种可选说法。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

The emperor's officials couldn't see the clothes, but they said, "What lovely clothes!"

皇帝的官员们看不见这些衣服，但他们说：“多么漂亮的衣服啊！”

couldn't = could not；but 表转折；What lovely clothes! 是感叹句，省略了主语和谓语（完整说法是 What lovely clothes they are!）。

The emperor couldn't see anything either, but he said, "They're beautiful!"

皇帝也什么都看不见，但他说：“它们真漂亮！”

either 用于否定句末尾，表示“也（不）”；They're = They are；直接引语用引号标出。

They started / began laughing.

他们开始大笑起来。

start / begin doing sth. 表示“开始做某事”；斜杠表示两种可选说法，意思相近。

Section B 1b · The Ugly Duckling

Section B 1b 主题：童话故事 · 丑小鸭（教材 P62，改编自安徒生童话）

Once upon a time, there was a mother duck.

从前，有一只鸭妈妈。

词汇once upon a time 很久以前；mother duck 鸭妈妈 语法There was + 单数名词，是故事开头的常用句型。

She had six little ducklings.

她有六只小鸭子。

词汇six 六；little 小的；duckling 小鸭子 语法have 的过去式 had；数词 six 修饰复数名词。

Five of the ducklings were small and yellow, but the last duckling was big and grey.

五只小鸭子又小又黄，但最后一只小鸭子又大又灰。

词汇five 五；yellow 黄色的；grey 灰色的；last 最后的 语法but 表转折；be 的过去式 were / was。

"He doesn't look like us!"

“他看起来不像我们！”

词汇look like 看起来像；us 我们（宾格） 语法look like 表示“看起来像”；doesn't + 动词原形。

"What an ugly duckling!" the other ducklings said.

“多丑的小鸭子啊！”其他小鸭子说。

词汇what 多么；ugly 丑的 语法What + a/an + 形容词 + 名词！感叹句；said 是 say 的过去式。

"That's not nice!" said Mother Duck.

“那可不好！”鸭妈妈说。

词汇nice 好的；That's = That is 语法直接引语后用 said + 主语。

"He's still a duck like us!"

“他仍然像我们一样是一只鸭子！”

词汇still 仍然；like 像 语法He's = He is；still 表示“仍然”。

At the river, the ugly duckling swam well, just like all the other ducklings.

在河里，丑小鸭游得很好，就像其他小鸭子一样。

词汇river 河流；swim 游泳；just like 就像 语法一般过去时 swam；just like 表示“就像”。

But the other ducklings still laughed at him and told him to go away.

但其他小鸭子仍然嘲笑它，让它走开。

词汇laugh at 嘲笑；go away 走开 语法laugh at sb. 嘲笑某人；tell sb. to do sth.。

It made the poor duckling very sad, so he decided to search for a new home.

这让可怜的丑小鸭非常伤心，于是它决定寻找一个新家。

词汇poor 可怜的；sad 伤心的；search for 寻找 语法make sb. + 形容词；so 表结果；decide to do sth.。

He met many other birds, but all of them thought that he was ugly too.

它遇到许多别的鸟，但它们都觉得它也很丑。

词汇other 其他的；bird 鸟；thought 认为（think 的过去式） 语法all of them 作主语；thought 后接宾语从句。

They did not want him to live with them.

它们不想让它和自己一起生活。

词汇want 想要；live with 和……一起生活 语法want sb. to do sth.；live with sb.。

One day, the duckling met a cat and a hen.

有一天，丑小鸭遇到一只猫和一只母鸡。

词汇meet 遇到；cat 猫；hen 母鸡 语法一般过去时 met；a cat and a hen 作宾语。

"May I stay with you?" he asked hopefully.

“我可以和你们待在一起吗？”它满怀希望地问。

词汇may 可以；stay with 和……待在一起；hopefully 满怀希望地 语法May I...? 礼貌地请求允许；hopefully 修饰 asked。

But the two animals said no.

但这两只动物拒绝了。

词汇animal 动物；say no 说不 语法said 是 say 的过去式；say no 表示“拒绝”。

They did not like him because he could not purr or lay eggs!

它们不喜欢它，因为它既不会发出呼噜声，也不会下蛋！

词汇purr 猫的呼噜声；lay eggs 下蛋 语法because 引导原因状语从句；could not + 动词原形。

Some days later, the ugly duckling came to a lake.

几天后，丑小鸭来到一个湖边。

词汇some days later 几天后；lake 湖 语法some days later 表示“几天后”；一般过去时。

There, he saw three beautiful swans with long necks and white feathers.

在那里，它看见三只长着长脖子、白色羽毛的美丽天鹅。

词汇swan 天鹅；neck 脖子；feather 羽毛 语法see 的过去式 saw；with 短语作后置定语。

"I wish I looked like you!" he cried.

“我希望能像你们一样！”它喊道。

词汇wish 希望；look like 看起来像 语法wish 后接宾语从句，从句用虚拟语气；he cried 是说明部分。

"My friend, you do look like us!"

“我的朋友，你确实看起来像我们！”

词汇do 确实（强调）；look like 看起来像 语法do 用于强调谓语动词。

"Look in the water!" one of the swans said.

“看看水里！”一只天鹅说。

词汇in the water 在水里 语法祈使句；one of the swans 表示“天鹅中的一只”。

The duckling looked down, and to his surprise, he saw that he was a beautiful white swan too!

丑小鸭低头一看，令他惊讶的是，它发现自己也是一只美丽的白天鹅！

词汇look down 低头看；to one's surprise 令某人惊讶的是 语法to one's surprise 固定短语；saw 后接宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇故事改编自安徒生的《丑小鸭》。从前，鸭妈妈孵出了六只小鸭子，其中五只又小又黄，最后一只却又大又灰，其他小鸭子都嘲笑它。丑小鸭在河里游得很好，但大家还是嘲笑它、让它走开，它很伤心，决定去寻找一个新家。它遇到许多别的鸟，大家都觉得它丑，不愿和它一起生活；它遇到一只猫和一只母鸡，也没有被接纳。几天后，丑小鸭来到一个湖边，看见三只美丽的白天鹅；它低头一看，惊讶地发现自己也是一只美丽的白天鹅。

English Summary

This story is adapted from Hans Christian Andersen's The Ugly Duckling. Once upon a time, a mother duck had six little ducklings. Five of them were small and yellow, but the last one was big and grey, and the other ducklings laughed at it. The ugly duckling swam well at the river, but the others still laughed at it and told it to go away, so it decided to search for a new home. It met many other birds, but they all thought it was ugly and did not want it to live with them. It also met a cat and a hen, but they did not like it either. Some days later, the ugly duckling came to a lake and saw three beautiful swans; it looked down and, to its surprise, found that it was a beautiful white swan too!

Reading Plus · The Fisherman and the Golden Fish (A play!)

Reading Plus 主题：改编剧本 · 渔夫和金鱼的故事（教材 P72；角色 Narrator / Fisherman / Wife / Golden fish）

Adapted from The Tale of the Fisherman and the Golden Fish by Alexander Pushkin

改编自普希金的《渔夫和金鱼的故事》

词汇adapt from 改编自；Alexander Pushkin 亚历山大·普希金（俄国诗人） 语法adapted from 表示“改编自”；by 说明原作者。

Narrator: There was once a fisherman who lived in a small house by the sea with his wife.

从前有一位渔夫，和妻子住在海边的一座小房子里。

词汇fisherman 渔夫；by the sea 在海边；wife 妻子 语法There was + 名词 + who 引导的定语从句。

Narrator: One day, something very special happened.

有一天，发生了一件非常特别的事。

词汇one day 有一天；special 特别的；happen 发生 语法something 作主语，谓语用单数；一般过去时。

Scene One: [The fisherman's hut.]

第一场：[渔夫的小屋。]

词汇scene 场；hut 小屋（方括号是舞台提示） 语法剧本场景标题的写法；方括号表示舞台提示。

Fisherman: An amazing thing happened when I went fishing today!

今天我去钓鱼时发生了一件奇妙的事！

词汇amazing 奇妙的；go fishing 去钓鱼 语法when 引导时间状语从句；go fishing 固定搭配。

Fisherman: I caught a little golden fish.

我钓到了一条小金鱼。

词汇catch 钓到；golden 金色的 语法catch 的过去式 caught。

Fisherman: It could speak!

它会说话！

词汇could 能够；speak 说话 语法could 是 can 的过去式，表示“能够”。

Fisherman: It promised me whatever I wanted if I set it free.

它答应我，只要我放了它，我想要什么都可以。

词汇promise 承诺；whatever 无论什么；set free 释放 语法promise sb. sth.；if 引导条件状语从句。

Wife: At last, fortune smiles upon us!

终于，幸运降临到我们头上了！

词汇at last 终于；fortune 幸运；smile upon 对……微笑 语法at last 表示“终于”；一般现在时表示此刻的感叹。

Wife: What did you wish for?

你许了什么愿？

词汇wish for 希望得到 语法What did + 主语 + 动词原形? 一般过去时特殊疑问句。

Fisherman: Well... I felt sorry for the fish, so I just put it back into the sea.

嗯……我为那条鱼感到难过，所以就把它放回海里了。

词汇feel sorry for 为……感到难过；put back 放回 语法feel 的过去式 felt；so 表结果。

Narrator: His wife was very angry and made him find the fish.

他的妻子非常生气，让他去找那条鱼。

词汇angry 生气的；make 让 语法make sb. do sth. 让某人做某事。

Scene Two: [By the sea.]

第二场：[海边。]

词汇by the sea 在海边 语法场景标题 + 舞台提示的写法。

Fisherman: Little golden fish, are you there?

小金鱼，你在吗？

词汇there 在那里 语法称呼语 + 一般疑问句。

Golden fish: Hello, kind fisherman.

你好，善良的渔夫。

词汇kind 善良的 语法问候语；形容词 kind 修饰 fisherman。

Golden fish: What's the matter?

怎么了？

词汇what's the matter 怎么了 语法What's the matter? 是询问“发生了什么”的常用语。

Fisherman: My wife was very angry because I didn't make a wish.

我的妻子非常生气，因为我没有许愿。

词汇make a wish 许愿 语法because 引导原因状语从句；didn't + 动词原形。

Fisherman: We need a new pot.

我们需要一个新盆。

词汇need 需要；pot 盆；罐 语法need + 名词；we 作主语。

Fisherman: Please give us one!

请给我们一个吧！

词汇give 给；us 我们（宾格） 语法祈使句 Please + 动词原形；one 指代 a new pot。

Narrator: The golden fish said yes, and it went back into the water.

金鱼答应了，然后它回到水中。

词汇say yes 答应；go back 回去 语法and 连接并列谓语；went 是 go 的过去式。

Scene Three: [The fisherman's hut.]

第三场：[渔夫的小屋。]

词汇scene 场；hut 小屋 语法场景标题的写法。

Narrator: Back home, the fisherman found a new pot outside the house.

回到家，渔夫发现屋外有一个新盆。

词汇back home 回到家；outside 在……外面 语法back home 作状语；found 是 find 的过去式。

Fisherman: Look, a new pot!

看，一个新盆！

词汇look 看 语法祈使句 Look 引起注意。

Wife: You fool!

你这个傻瓜！

词汇fool 傻瓜 语法感叹句，表达指责。

Wife: Can't you see we need a new house instead?

难道你看不出我们需要的是新房子吗？

词汇instead 代替；反而；house 房子 语法Can't you see...? 反问句；instead 表示“而是”。

Wife: Go back again and ask for one!

再回去要一个！

词汇go back 回去；again 再一次；ask for 请求 语法祈使句；and 连接两个动作。

Scene Four: [By the sea.]

第四场：[海边。]

词汇by the sea 在海边 语法场景标题的写法。

Fisherman: Little golden fish, are you still there?

小金鱼，你还在吗？

词汇still 仍然 语法still 表示“仍然”。

Golden fish: Hello again, kind fisherman.

又见面了，善良的渔夫。

词汇again 再一次 语法问候语；again 表示“又一次”。

Fisherman: My wife wants a new house!

我的妻子想要一座新房子！

词汇want 想要；house 房子 语法want + 名词；一般现在时。

Fisherman: Can you help me please?

你能帮帮我吗？

词汇help 帮助 语法Can you...? 请求帮助。

Narrator: Again, the golden fish granted his wish.

金鱼又一次满足了他的愿望。

词汇grant 满足；wish 愿望 语法grant one's wish 表示“满足某人的愿望”。

Scene Five: [The fisherman's hut.]

第五场：[渔夫的小屋。]

词汇scene 场；hut 小屋 语法场景标题的写法。

Narrator: The fisherman went back home.

渔夫回到家。

词汇go back home 回家 语法went 是 go 的过去式；home 是副词。

Narrator: But his house was now big and beautiful.

但他的房子现在又大又漂亮。

词汇beautiful 漂亮的；now 现在 语法but 表转折；was 是 is 的过去式。

Fisherman: Wow!

哇！

词汇wow 哇（感叹词） 语法感叹词，表达惊喜。

Fisherman: Our house looks amazing!

我们的房子看起来太棒了！

词汇amazing 令人惊叹的 语法look + 形容词，表示“看起来……”。

Wife: The house is nice, but I want to be rich too!

房子是不错，但我也想变得富有！

词汇rich 富有的；too 也 语法but 表转折；want to be + 形容词。

Wife: Go back and ask for money!

回去要钱！

词汇money 钱；ask for 请求 语法祈使句；and 连接两个动作。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇剧本改编自普希金的《渔夫和金鱼的故事》。旁白介绍：从前有一位渔夫，和妻子住在海边的小房子里。第一天，渔夫钓到一条小金鱼，金鱼会说话，答应只要放它回海，就满足他的愿望，但善良的渔夫什么愿望也没提就放走了它。妻子非常生气，让他回去找金鱼要一个新盆，金鱼答应了；妻子又要新房子，金鱼也满足了。可妻子还不满足，又让渔夫去要钱，想要变得富有。剧本最后用省略号表示故事还没有结束。

English Summary

This play is adapted from Alexander Pushkin's The Tale of the Fisherman and the Golden Fish. The narrator introduces a fisherman who lived in a small house by the sea with his wife. One day the fisherman caught a little golden fish. It could speak and promised to give him whatever he wanted if he set it free, but the kind fisherman put it back into the sea without making a wish. His wife was very angry and made him find the fish again. She wanted a new pot, and the golden fish granted the wish; then she wanted a new house, and the fish granted that too. But she was still not satisfied and asked the fisherman to get money so that they could be rich. The play ends with an ellipsis, showing that the story continues.

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 教材 P72 是 Reading Plus 板块的剧本（A play!），角色有 Narrator、Fisherman、Wife、Golden fish，本页按版面顺序逐句呈现。

  • 边读边画出场景标题（Scene One—Scene Five），弄清故事发生在哪里。

1. What did the two brothers say about the clothes?

  1. Only clever people could see them.
  2. The clothes were very cheap.
  3. The emperor could not wear them.
  4. The clothes were made of gold.
答案与解析

A 原文：But only clever / smart people can see them! 可知两兄弟说只有聪明人才能看见衣服。

2. What did the boy cry out?

  1. The clothes are beautiful!
  2. The emperor has no clothes on!
  3. The emperor is very clever!
  4. The clothes are too big!
答案与解析

B 原文：Suddenly, a boy cried / shouted, "Look! The emperor has no clothes on!" 可知男孩喊的是“皇帝没有穿衣服”。

3. Why did the other ducklings laugh at the ugly duckling?

  1. Because it swam very well.
  2. Because it was very small.
  3. Because it was big and grey.
  4. Because it had many friends.
答案与解析

C 原文：Five of the ducklings were small and yellow, but the last duckling was big and grey. 可知它因为又大又灰而被嘲笑。

4. What did the ugly duckling become at the end?

  1. A grey duck.
  2. A big hen.
  3. A white cat.
  4. A beautiful white swan.
答案与解析

D 原文：he saw that he was a beautiful white swan too! 可知丑小鸭最后变成了美丽的白天鹅。

5. What did the fisherman do with the golden fish at first?

  1. He put it back into the sea.
  2. He took it home.
  3. He sold it in the market.
  4. He gave it to his wife.
答案与解析

A 原文：I felt sorry for the fish, so I just put it back into the sea. 可知渔夫一开始把金鱼放回了海里。

6. What did the fisherman's wife want first?

  1. Money.
  2. A new pot.
  3. A new house.
  4. A golden fish.
答案与解析

B 原文：We need a new pot. Please give us one! 可知妻子第一个愿望是要一个新盆。

7. What did the fisherman's wife want after the new pot?

8. What does the wife want at the end of the play?
