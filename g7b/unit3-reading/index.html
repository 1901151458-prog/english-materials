Section A 3a · How Do You Keep Fit?

Section A 3a 主题：健康习惯与运动 · 对话

Mike: Lin Hui, you look really healthy. What do you do to keep fit?

林慧，你看起来很健康。你做什么来保持健康呢？

词汇look healthy 看起来健康；keep fit 保持健康；do 助动词 语法What do you do to do sth.? 询问习惯，do 为主语 you 的实义动词。

Lin Hui: I always get up early and run for twenty minutes before school.

我总是很早起床，上学前跑步二十分钟。

词汇get up 起床；run 跑步；early 早地；before school 上学前 语法一般现在时；always 用一般现在时表经常。

Mike: Twenty minutes every day? That's a lot.

每天二十分钟？那可真不少。

词汇a lot 很多；every day 每天 语法That's = That is；every day 时间状语。

Lin Hui: It isn't hard when you get used to it.

当你习惯以后就不难了。

词汇be hard 难；get used to 习惯于 语法when 引导时间状语从句；it 指跑步这件事。

Mike: I usually stay in bed until seven, so I seldom have time for breakfast.

我通常在床上躺到七点，所以我很少有时间吃早饭。

词汇usually 通常；seldom 很少；have time for 有时间做 语法usually / seldom 是一般现在时的频率副词，位于实义动词前。

Lin Hui: Breakfast is important. You should eat something every morning.

早饭很重要。你应该每天早上都吃点东西。

词汇important 重要的；something 一些东西 语法should + 动词原形表示建议。

Mike: But I can only have a glass of milk on the bus.

但我只能在公交车上喝一杯牛奶。

词汇a glass of 一杯；on the bus 在公交车上 语法can + 动词原形；only 表限制。

Lin Hui: That's better than nothing. What sport do you like?

那总比什么都没有强。你喜欢什么运动？

词汇better than nothing 比没有好 语法What sport do you like? 特殊疑问句，助动词 do。

Mike: I like basketball, and I sometimes play it after school.

我喜欢篮球，有时放学后打。

词汇play basketball 打篮球；after school 放学后 语法sometimes 位于主语后、实义动词前。

Lin Hui: Then you can play it often. It's good for your health.

那你可以经常打。它对健康有好处。

词汇play it 代词作宾语；be good for 对……有好处；health 健康 语法can + 动词原形；be good for 表示对……有益。

Mike: You're right. I never eat junk food, but I seldom exercise.

你说得对。我从不吃垃圾食品，但很少锻炼。

词汇never 从不；junk food 垃圾食品；exercise 锻炼 语法never / seldom 表示频率极低，用一般现在时。

Lin Hui: So let's exercise together! We should help each other keep fit.

那我们一起锻炼吧！我们应该互相帮助保持健康。

词汇exercise together 一起锻炼；each other 彼此；keep fit 保持健康 语法Let's do sth. 提建议；should 表示建议。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，迈克称赞林慧看起来很健康，并问她如何保持健康。林慧说她总是早起、上学前跑二十分钟；迈克每天睡到七点，很少有时间吃早饭，林慧建议他每天早上都吃点东西，迈克说只能在公交车上喝杯牛奶。林慧问迈克喜欢什么运动，迈克说喜欢篮球、有时放学后打，林慧建议他多打。迈克承认自己从不吃垃圾食品，但很少锻炼，两人决定一起锻炼、互相帮助保持健康。

English Summary

In this conversation, Mike says Lin Hui looks healthy and asks how she keeps fit. She explains that she always gets up early and runs for twenty minutes before school. Mike usually stays in bed until seven and seldom has time for breakfast; Lin Hui advises him to eat something every morning, and he says he can only have a glass of milk on the bus. Asked what sport he likes, Mike says he likes basketball and sometimes plays it after school. He admits he never eats junk food but seldom exercises, so they decide to exercise together and help each other keep fit.

语法点 Grammar

What do you do to keep fit?

一般现在时特殊疑问句，do 为助动词；to keep fit 作目的状语。

I always get up early and run for twenty minutes before school.

一般现在时；always 置于实义动词前，表经常性动作。

That's a lot.

That's = That is，指代上文说的二十分钟。

It isn't hard when you get used to it.

一般现在时否定式；when 引导时间状语从句。

I usually stay in bed until seven, so I seldom have time for breakfast.

usually / seldom 频率副词放在实义动词前；so 表结果。

You should eat something every morning.

should + 动词原形，表示建议。

But I can only have a glass of milk on the bus.

can + 动词原形表示能力/可能；only 表示限制。

What sport do you like?

一般现在时特殊疑问句；助动词 do 后接动词原形。

I like basketball, and I sometimes play it after school.

sometimes 位于主语后、实义动词前；it 指代 basketball。

Then you can play it often.

频率副词 often 放句末；can + 动词原形。

I never eat junk food, but I seldom exercise.

never / seldom 表频率极低，用一般现在时；but 表转折。

So let's exercise together! We should help each other keep fit.

Let's do sth. 提建议；help sb. (to) do sth.。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It isn't hard when you get used to it.

当你习惯以后就不难了。

get used to sth. 表示“习惯于”，这里的 it 指跑步；when 引导时间状语从句。

I usually stay in bed until seven, so I seldom have time for breakfast.

我通常在床上躺到七点，所以我很少有时间吃早饭。

until seven 表示“直到七点”；usually 与 seldom 都是一般现在时的频率副词。

So let's exercise together! We should help each other keep fit.

那我们一起锻炼吧！我们应该互相帮助保持健康。

Let's do sth. 提出建议；help sb. (to) do sth. 中不定式可省略 to。

Section B 1b · What Healthy People Have in Common

Section B 1b 主题：健康的生活方式 · 说明文

Healthy people have a lot in common, and health is something we can all build.

健康的人有很多共同点，而健康是我们每个人都能打造的。

词汇in common 共同之处；build 建立；something 可数名词 语法health 为抽象名词作主语。

First of all, they never stay up too late.

首先，他们从不熬夜太晚。

词汇first of all 首先；stay up 熬夜；too late 太晚 语法never 表示频率为零，用一般现在时。

Most of them sleep for about eight hours every night, so they always have enough energy.

他们大多数人每晚睡大约八小时，因此总是精力充沛。

词汇most of them 他们中的大多数；sleep 睡觉；enough energy 充足的精力 语法most of + 代词 作主语；so 表结果。

Second, they usually eat a balanced diet.

其次，他们通常饮食均衡。

词汇usually 通常；a balanced diet 均衡饮食 语法第三人称复数 they，动词用原形。

They often have vegetables, fruit and milk, and they seldom eat junk food.

他们常吃蔬菜、水果和牛奶，很少吃垃圾食品。

词汇vegetables 蔬菜；fruit 水果；junk food 垃圾食品 语法often / seldom 频率副词，位置在实义动词前。

Third, they move their bodies every day.

第三，他们每天都活动身体。

词汇third 第三；move 移动；body 身体 语法every day 表示习惯性动作。

Some of them run in the park, some swim, and others play ball games with friends.

有些人去公园跑步，有些人游泳，还有些人和朋友一起打球。

词汇some... others... 一些……另一些……；in the park 在公园；ball games 球类运动 语法some ... others ... 并列结构，动词均为一般现在时。

You do not need to do a lot to keep fit.

你不需要做很多事就能保持健康。

词汇need to do sth. 需要做某事；keep fit 保持健康 语法do not need to 表示不必，是一般现在时否定式。

Thirty minutes of exercise a day is enough, and you should drink more water.

每天三十分钟锻炼就够了，而且你应该多喝水。

词汇thirty minutes of exercise 三十分钟锻炼；be enough 足够；drink water 喝水 语法动名词/名词短语作主语；should + 动词原形。

Many students have a headache or feel tired in class because they seldom drink water.

许多学生上课头疼或感到疲倦，因为他们很少喝水。

词汇have a headache 头疼；feel tired 感到疲倦；in class 上课时 语法because 引导原因状语从句；feel + adj.。

Exercise also makes us happy, so it is good for the mind as well as the body.

锻炼还能让我们快乐，所以它对身心都有好处。

词汇make sb. happy 使某人快乐；the mind 心理；as well as 以及 语法make + 宾语 + adj.；as well as 连接并列成分。

We should get off the sofa and try one small change this week.

我们应该离开沙发，这周尝试一个小改变。

词汇get off 离开；sofa 沙发；change 改变 语法should + 动词原形；祈使意味的建议。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

短文介绍健康的人有哪些共同点。首先，他们从不熬夜，大多数人每晚睡约八小时，总是精力充沛。其次，他们通常饮食均衡，常吃蔬菜、水果和牛奶，很少吃垃圾食品。第三，他们每天活动身体：有人跑步，有人游泳，还有人打球。保持健康其实不需要做很多，每天三十分钟运动和喝足够的水就够；许多学生上课头疼或疲倦，就是因为他们很少喝水。锻炼还能让人快乐，对身心都有好处，所以我们应离开沙发，这周就尝试一个小改变。

English Summary

The passage tells us what healthy people have in common. First, they never stay up too late; most of them sleep about eight hours a night and always have enough energy. Second, they usually eat a balanced diet, often having vegetables, fruit and milk and seldom eating junk food. Third, they move their bodies every day: some run, some swim, and others play ball games. Keeping fit does not take much — thirty minutes of exercise and enough water are enough; many students get headaches or feel tired because they seldom drink water. Exercise also makes us happy, so it is good for the mind as well as the body, and we should make one small change this week.

语法点 Grammar

Healthy people have a lot in common, and health is something we can all build.

and 连接并列句；something we can all build 是省略 that 的定语从句。

they never stay up too late

never 表零频率，用一般现在时；stay up 表示熬夜。

Most of them sleep for about eight hours every night, so they always have enough energy.

most of + 代词 作主语；always 位于实义动词前；so 表结果。

they usually eat a balanced diet

usually + 一般现在时，表经常性动作。

They often have vegetables, fruit and milk, and they seldom eat junk food.

often / seldom 位置在实义动词前。

Some of them run in the park, some swim, and others play ball games with friends.

some ... others ... 表示“一些……另一些……”。

You do not need to do a lot to keep fit.

need to do sth. 的否定式是一般现在时；to keep fit 作目的状语。

Thirty minutes of exercise a day is enough, and you should drink more water.

名词短语作主语看作单数，用 is；should + 动词原形。

Many students have a headache or feel tired in class because they seldom drink water.

because 引导原因状语从句；or 连接并列谓语。

Exercise also makes us happy, so it is good for the mind as well as the body.

make + 宾语 + adj.；as well as 连接并列成分。

We should get off the sofa and try one small change this week.

should + 动词原形；and 连接两个并列动作。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Most of them sleep for about eight hours every night, so they always have enough energy.

他们大多数人每晚睡大约八小时，因此总是精力充沛。

most of + 代词宾格作主语，谓语动词随主语用复数；so 引导结果的并列句。

Some of them run in the park, some swim, and others play ball games with friends.

有些人去公园跑步，有些人游泳，还有些人和朋友一起打球。

some ... others ... 表示“一些……另一些……”；三个分句之间用逗号和 and 连接。

Exercise also makes us happy, so it is good for the mind as well as the body.

锻炼还能让我们快乐，所以它对身心都有好处。

make + 宾语 + 形容词；as well as 连接两个并列成分，it 指 exercise。

Section B 3a · A Letter About My Healthy Life

Section B 3a 主题：健康生活分享 · 书信/习作

Dear Lily,

亲爱的莉莉：

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语后用逗号。

Thank you for your letter! You asked how I keep fit, so I am glad to tell you.

谢谢你的来信！你问我如何保持健康，我很高兴告诉你。

词汇thank you for 感谢；glad 高兴的；tell you 告诉你 语法Thank you for sth.；how I keep fit 是宾语从句。

Before school I always run for fifteen minutes with my dad.

上学前我总是和爸爸一起跑十五分钟。

词汇before school 上学前；run for 跑……时间；with my dad 和爸爸一起 语法always 位于实义动词前；时间状语前置。

After that I usually have breakfast: an egg, some bread and a glass of milk.

之后我通常吃早饭：一个鸡蛋、一些面包和一杯牛奶。

词汇after that 之后；an egg 一个鸡蛋；a glass of milk 一杯牛奶 语法usually 置于实义动词 have 之前。

P.E. is my favourite subject, and I never miss it.

体育是我最喜欢的科目，我从不错过。

词汇P.E. 体育；favourite 最喜欢的；miss 错过 语法never 表频率为零，用一般现在时。

Our teacher says we should exercise at least three times a week.

我们老师说我们每周至少应该锻炼三次。

词汇at least 至少；three times a week 每周三次 语法says 后接宾语从句，从句用 should + 动词原形。

I sometimes play ping-pong with my classmates after class.

我有时下课后和同学打乒乓球。

词汇play ping-pong 打乒乓球；after class 下课后 语法sometimes 位于主语后、实义动词前。

I seldom eat hamburgers or drink cola, because they are not healthy.

我很少吃汉堡或喝可乐，因为它们不健康。

词汇hamburger 汉堡；cola 可乐；healthy 健康的 语法seldom 少于此频率用一般现在时；because 引导原因状语从句。

Now I go to bed before ten and get up at six thirty, so I do not feel tired in class.

现在我在十点前睡觉、六点半起床，所以上课不会感到疲倦。

词汇go to bed 上床睡觉；in class 上课时；not feel tired 不感到疲倦 语法and 连接两个并列动作；so 表示结果。

Sometimes I am busy with my homework and want to skip exercise, but I tell myself that health should come first and one short run is better than nothing.

有时我忙于做作业就想跳过锻炼，但我告诉自己健康应该放在第一位，短跑一次也比不跑强。

词汇be busy with 忙于；skip 跳过；come first 放在第一位；better than nothing 比没有好 语法be busy with sth.；want to do sth.；but 表转折；tell sb. that 从句；should + 动词原形。

I hope you can stay fit too. Write to me soon!

我希望你也能保持健康。快给我写信吧！

词汇hope 希望；stay fit 保持健康；write to sb. 给某人写信 语法hope + 宾语从句，用 can；祈使句收尾。

Yours,

你的朋友，

词汇yours 你的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Wang Ming

王明

词汇Wang Ming 王明（人名） 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是王明写给笔友莉莉的信，介绍自己的健康生活。上学前他总跟爸爸跑十五分钟，然后通常吃一个鸡蛋、一些面包和一杯牛奶；体育是他最喜欢的科目，他从不错过，老师说每周至少应该锻炼三次；他有时和同学打乒乓球，很少吃汉堡或喝可乐。现在他十点前睡、六点半起床，上课不会感到疲倦。有时作业忙就不想锻炼，但他提醒自己健康应放在第一位，短跑一次也比不跑强，并希望莉莉也能保持健康。

English Summary

This is Wang Ming's letter to his pen friend Lily about his healthy life. Before school he always runs for fifteen minutes with his dad, then usually has an egg, some bread and a glass of milk. P.E. is his favourite subject, and he never misses it; his teacher says they should exercise at least three times a week. He sometimes plays ping-pong with classmates and seldom eats hamburgers or drinks cola. Now he goes to bed before ten and gets up at six thirty, so he does not feel tired in class. When he is busy with homework and wants to skip exercise, he tells himself that health should come first; one short run is better than nothing. He hopes Lily can stay fit too.

语法点 Grammar

You asked how I keep fit, so I am glad to tell you.

how 引导宾语从句，从句用陈述语序；so 表结果。

Before school I always run for fifteen minutes with my dad.

always 用一般现在时；时间状语 before school 可置于句首。

After that I usually have breakfast: an egg, some bread and a glass of milk.

usually 位于实义动词前；冒号后列举用 and 连接。

P.E. is my favourite subject, and I never miss it.

一般现在时；never 表零频率，位于实义动词前。

Our teacher says we should exercise at least three times a week.

says 后接宾语从句；should + 动词原形表示建议。

I sometimes play ping-pong with my classmates after class.

sometimes 位于主语后、实义动词前。

I seldom eat hamburgers or drink cola, because they are not healthy.

seldom 用于一般现在时；否定句中列举用 or；because 引导原因状语从句。

Now I go to bed before ten and get up at six thirty, so I do not feel tired in class.

and 连接并列谓语；so 表结果；一般现在时否定式。

Sometimes I am busy with my homework and want to skip exercise, but I tell myself that health should come first and one short run is better than nothing.

be busy with sth.；want to do sth.；but 表转折；tell sb. that 从句；should + 动词原形；better than nothing 比较级结构。

I hope you can stay fit too.

hope + 宾语从句，用 can 表示愿望。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

After that I usually have breakfast: an egg, some bread and a glass of milk.

之后我通常吃早饭：一个鸡蛋、一些面包和一杯牛奶。

usually 放在实义动词 have 前；冒号后列举食物，some bread 不可数不加 s。

I seldom eat hamburgers or drink cola, because they are not healthy.

我很少吃汉堡或喝可乐，因为它们不健康。

seldom 表极低频率；否定意义的句子中并列用 or；because 引导原因状语从句。

Sometimes I am busy with my homework and want to skip exercise, but I tell myself that health should come first and one short run is better than nothing.

有时我忙于做作业就想跳过锻炼，但我告诉自己健康应该放在第一位，短跑一次也比不跑强。

be busy with sth.；want to do sth.；but 表转折；tell sb. that 从句；should + 动词原形；better than nothing 固定表达。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What does Lin Hui do before school?

  1. She plays basketball.
  2. She gets up early and runs for twenty minutes.
  3. She stays in bed until seven.
  4. She eats junk food.
答案与解析

B 原文：I always get up early and run for twenty minutes before school.

2. What does Mike usually do in the morning?

  1. He runs with his dad.
  2. He eats a big breakfast.
  3. He stays in bed until seven.
  4. He plays ping-pong.
答案与解析

C 原文：I usually stay in bed until seven, so I seldom have time for breakfast.

3. Which word tells us how often Mike eats junk food?

  1. Usually.
  2. Often.
  3. Sometimes.
  4. Never.
答案与解析

D 原文：I never eat junk food, but I seldom exercise.

4. According to Section B, how long do most healthy people sleep every night?

  1. About four hours.
  2. About six hours.
  3. About eight hours.
  4. About ten hours.
答案与解析

C 原文：Most of them sleep for about eight hours every night.

5. How much exercise does the writer of Section B say is enough each day?

  1. Thirty minutes.
  2. Two hours.
  3. Fifteen minutes.
  4. Three hours.
答案与解析

A 原文：Thirty minutes of exercise a day is enough.

6. Why do many students have a headache or feel tired in class?

  1. They sleep too much.
  2. They seldom drink water.
  3. They eat too much fruit.
  4. They never sleep.
答案与解析

B 原文：Many students have a headache or feel tired in class because they seldom drink water.

7. What is Wang Ming's favourite subject?

  1. Maths.
  2. English.
  3. P.E.
  4. Music.
答案与解析

C 原文：P.E. is my favourite subject, and I never miss it.

8. What is the common theme of the three texts?

  1. Healthy habits and keeping fit.
  2. Holiday travel.
  3. School rules.
  4. Weather and the seasons.
答案与解析

A 三篇文章分别谈对话中的健康习惯、健康的人的共同点和自己的健康书信，主题都是健康习惯与保持健康。
