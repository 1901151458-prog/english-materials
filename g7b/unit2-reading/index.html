Section A 3a · Rules at Home

Section A 3a 主题：家规与许可 · 对话

A: Mum, can I go out and play now?

妈妈，我现在可以出去玩吗？

词汇go out 出去；play 玩 语法Can I...? 征求许可。

B: No, you can't. You must finish your homework first.

不行。你必须先写完作业。

词汇finish 完成；homework 作业；must 必须 语法must + 动词原形表示必须。

A: But it's Saturday today!

可今天是星期六啊！

词汇Saturday 星期六 语法but 表示转折。

B: I know. But rules are rules. You have to do your homework every day.

我知道。但规则就是规则。你每天都必须做作业。

词汇rule 规则；have to 不得不 语法have to + 动词原形表示客观要求。

A: OK. What else must I do?

好吧。我还必须做什么？

词汇else 其他 语法must + 动词原形。

B: You must clean your room and make your bed.

你必须整理房间、整理床铺。

词汇clean 打扫；make the bed 整理床铺 语法must + 动词原形；and 连接并列动作。

A: Can I watch TV after that?

那之后我可以看电视吗？

词汇watch TV 看电视 语法Can I...? 征求许可。

B: You can, but only for half an hour.

可以，但只能看半小时。

词汇only 只；half an hour 半小时 语法but 表限制；only for...。

A: What about my friends? Can they come over?

那我的朋友呢？他们能过来吗？

词汇come over 过来 语法What about...? 引出话题。

B: They can come, but you must keep quiet and not shout.

他们可以来，但你们必须保持安静，不能大喊。

词汇keep quiet 保持安静；shout 喊叫 语法but 转折；must + 动词原形。

A: I see. Rules help us live better together.

我明白了。规则让我们更好地一起生活。

词汇live together 一起生活 语法help sb. do sth.；better 比较级。

B: Exactly. That's why we have rules at home.

正是如此。这就是家里要有规则的原因。

词汇exactly 正是；at home 在家 语法That's why... 引导表语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕家规展开。孩子想出去玩，妈妈说不行，必须先写完作业。孩子说今天是星期六，妈妈回答规则就是规则，每天都必须做作业。孩子又问还要做什么，妈妈说必须打扫房间、整理床铺；可以看电视，但只能看半小时；朋友可以来，但必须保持安静、不能大喊。最后孩子明白了：规则让人更好地一起生活。

English Summary

The conversation is about rules at home. The child asks to go out and play, but the mother says no because homework must be finished first. When the child points out it is Saturday, she replies that rules are rules and homework has to be done every day. She adds that the child must clean the room and make the bed; TV is allowed but only for half an hour; friends can come but they must keep quiet and not shout. In the end the child understands that rules help us live better together.

Section B 1b · No Rules, No Order

Section B 1b 主题：规则的意义 · 说明文

Every family has rules, and so does every school.

每个家庭都有规则，学校也是。

词汇rule 规则；so does 也是如此 语法so + 助动词 + 主语 表示“……也是”。

Rules are not there to make us unhappy.

规则的存在并不是为了让我们不开心。

词汇unhappy 不开心的 语法be there to do sth.。

They help us stay safe and get along with others.

它们帮助我们保持安全、与他人和睦相处。

词汇stay safe 保持安全；get along with 与……相处 语法help sb. do sth.。

At school, we must arrive on time and wear the school uniform.

在学校，我们必须准时到校并穿校服。

词汇arrive on time 准时到达；school uniform 校服 语法must + 动词原形。

We have to listen to the teacher and cannot run in the hallways.

我们不得不听老师的话，不能在走廊里跑。

词汇hallway 走廊；cannot 不能 语法have to；cannot + 动词原形。

In the library, we should keep quiet and return books on time.

在图书馆，我们应该保持安静、按时还书。

词汇return 归还；on time 按时 语法should + 动词原形。

If we break the rules, we will get into trouble.

如果违反规则，我们就会惹上麻烦。

词汇break the rule 违反规则；get into trouble 惹上麻烦 语法if 引导条件状语从句，主将从现。

But rules also give us freedom.

但规则也给了我们自由。

词汇freedom 自由 语法but 表示对比。

When everyone follows the rules, we can all enjoy school life.

当每个人都遵守规则时，我们都能享受校园生活。

词汇follow the rule 遵守规则；enjoy 享受 语法when 引导时间状语从句；can + 动词原形。

So remember: no rules, no order.

所以记住：没有规则，就没有秩序。

词汇order 秩序 语法祈使句；名句引用。

Good rules make a good team and a happy family.

好的规则成就好的团队和幸福的家庭。

词汇team 团队 语法make + 宾语。

We should follow them and also help make them better.

我们应该遵守规则，也应帮助完善它们。

词汇follow 遵守 语法should + 动词原形；help do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

短文说明规则的意义。每个家庭、每所学校都有规则，规则不是为了让人不开心，而是帮助我们保持安全、与他人和睦相处。在学校必须准时到校、穿校服、听老师的话、不能在走廊里跑；在图书馆应保持安静、按时还书；违反规则会惹上麻烦。但规则也给我们自由：当每个人都遵守规则时，大家都能享受校园生活。没有规则，就没有秩序；好的规则成就好团队和幸福家庭。

English Summary

The passage explains the meaning of rules. Every family and every school has rules; they are not there to make us unhappy but to help us stay safe and get along with others. At school we must arrive on time, wear the school uniform, listen to the teacher and not run in the hallways; in the library we should keep quiet and return books on time. Breaking rules gets us into trouble. But rules also give us freedom: when everyone follows them, we can all enjoy school life. No rules, no order; good rules make a good team and a happy family.

Section B 3a · A Letter About Classroom Rules

Section B 3a 主题：班规建议 · 书信

Dear Ms Li,

亲爱的李老师，

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

I am writing to talk about the rules in our classroom.

我写信是想谈谈我们教室里的规则。

词汇write to 写信给；rule 规则 语法现在进行时表示当前动作；talk about。

I think some rules are good.

我认为有些规定是好的。

词汇think 认为 语法I think 后接宾语从句。

For example, we must not use phones in class, and we should be quiet during exams.

比如，我们上课不能使用手机，考试时必须安静。

词汇for example 例如；in class 在课上；during 在……期间 语法must not 表示禁止；should + 动词原形。

However, I have a suggestion.

不过，我有一个建议。

词汇however 不过；suggestion 建议 语法however 表示转折。

Could we have ten minutes to rest after each class?

每节课后能不能有十分钟休息？

词汇rest 休息；after each class 每节课后 语法Could we...? 委婉请求。

Now we sometimes feel tired and cannot focus in the next class.

现在我们有时会感到疲倦，下一节课无法集中注意力。

词汇feel tired 感到疲倦；focus 集中注意力 语法cannot + 动词原形。

I believe a short break will help us study better.

我相信短暂休息能帮助我们更好地学习。

词汇short break 短暂休息 语法that 从句省略；help sb. do sth.。

We will also try our best to follow the rules.

我们也会尽力遵守规则。

词汇try one's best 尽力 语法will do；try one's best to do sth.。

Thank you for your time.

感谢您抽时间阅读。

词汇thank you for 感谢 语法Thank you for sth.。

Yours sincerely,

您诚挚的，

词汇yours sincerely 您诚挚的（信件落款） 语法书信结束语。

Zhang Hua

张华

词汇Zhang Hua 张华（人名） 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是张华写给李老师的一封信，谈教室里的规则。他认为有些规定是好的，比如上课不能使用手机、考试时要安静；但他提出一个建议：每节课后能否有十分钟休息，因为现在他们会感到疲倦、无法集中注意力，短暂休息能帮助学习更有效率。他表示也会尽力遵守规则，并感谢老师。

English Summary

This is Zhang Hua's letter to Ms Li about classroom rules. He thinks some rules are good, such as not using phones in class and being quiet during exams, but he makes a suggestion: could students have ten minutes to rest after each class? He explains that they sometimes feel tired and cannot focus in the next class, and believes a short break will help them study better. He promises to try his best to follow the rules and thanks the teacher.

  2. Finish the homework.
