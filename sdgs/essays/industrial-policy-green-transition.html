Industrial Policy for the Green Transition

绿色转型的产业政策

SDG 9 is not only about building infrastructure; it is about who captures the value of the next economy

SDG 9 · 产业、创新和基础设施 / Industry, Innovation and InfrastructureC1–C2 · 雅思 7.5+ / 托福 105+6 min read

Industrial policy is back; the question is whether it will be inclusive and green, or a new form of exclusion.

The return of the visible hand

For decades, industrial policy was treated as a relic. Today, the United States, European Union, China, India and others are spending billions on clean technology, semiconductors and critical minerals. The stated goals are security, jobs and decarbonisation.

This is not necessarily a return to the old protectionism. Green industrial policy can correct market failures: innovation spillovers, network effects and the need to de-risk new technologies. But it can also become a subsidy race that favours rich countries and fragments the global economy.

Infrastructure and productive capacity

SDG 9 calls for resilient infrastructure, inclusive and sustainable industrialisation and innovation. Infrastructure is not only roads and ports; it includes electricity grids, digital networks, water systems and testing laboratories.

Productive capacity matters because countries that only export raw materials capture little value. Industrialisation can create jobs and diversify exports, but it needs stable energy, skilled workers, finance and predictable rules. Green industrialisation adds another requirement: low-carbon production processes.

Technology transfer and the rules of the game

Developing countries need access to green technologies, not only finished products. Intellectual property rules, investment agreements and export controls shape whether they can build local capacity.

Technology transfer is often treated as charity. It is better understood as a condition for a global transition. If green technologies are treated as strategic assets to be hoarded, the transition will be slower and more unequal.

A green industrial policy that includes

An inclusive green industrial policy would combine public investment, stable demand signals and social protection for affected workers. It would support local content and skills without violating international rules, and it would provide finance and technology access to low-income countries.

SDG 9 is not a licence for any kind of industrialisation. It is a demand for infrastructure and industry that are sustainable, inclusive and innovative. The next economy will be built somewhere; the question is whether it will be built by many countries or only a few.

中文译文 · CHINESE

绿色转型的产业政策

几十年来，产业政策被视为过时遗物。今天，美国、欧盟、中国、印度等正在清洁技术、半导体与关键矿产上投入数十亿。公开目标是安全、就业与脱碳。这不一定回到旧保护主义；绿色产业政策可以纠正市场失灵：创新溢出、网络效应与新技术去风险。但它也可能变成补贴竞赛，偏向富国、割裂全球经济。

SDG 9 要求有韧性的基础设施、包容可持续的工业化与创新。基础设施不只是道路港口，还包括电网、数字网络、水系统与检测实验室。生产能力重要，因为只出口原材料的国家捕获价值很少。工业化可以创造就业、多元化出口，但需要稳定能源、熟练工人、金融与可预测规则。绿色工业化增加另一个要求：低碳生产过程。

发展中国家需要获得绿色技术，而不只是成品。知识产权规则、投资协议与出口管制决定它们能否建设本地能力。技术转移常被当作慈善，更应被理解为全球转型的条件。若绿色技术被当作囤积的战略资产，转型会更慢、更不平等。

包容的绿色产业政策应结合公共投资、稳定需求信号与对受影响工人的社会保护；支持本地含量与技能，同时不违反国际规则；并向低收入国家提供融资与技术准入。SDG 9 不是任何工业化的许可证，而是对可持续、包容、创新基础设施与产业的要求。下一个经济会在某处建成；问题是由许多国家建成，还是只由少数国家建成。
