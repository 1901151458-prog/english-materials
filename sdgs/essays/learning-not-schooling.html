Learning, Not Schooling

要学习，而不只是上学

The learning crisis is not a failure of enrolment; it is a failure of teaching, assessment and purpose

SDG 4 · 优质教育 / Quality EducationC1 · 雅思 7.5+ / 托福 100+6 min read

The learning crisis is not a failure of enrolment; it is a failure of teaching, assessment and purpose.

The gap between access and learning

Over the past two decades, millions of children entered school for the first time. Yet many leave without foundational literacy and numeracy. The World Bank calls this “learning poverty”: the inability to read a simple text with comprehension by late primary school.

The gap is not evenly distributed. It falls along income, gender, disability, language and geography. A child who cannot read cannot learn other subjects; the consequences compound across a lifetime. Access without learning is a promise unfulfilled.

Teachers and the conditions of teaching

Teachers are the most important school-level factor in learning. Yet many systems treat them as interchangeable inputs: hire quickly, pay poorly, provide little training and evaluate through inspection.

Improving learning requires investing in teachers: pre-service education, continuous professional development, manageable class sizes, supportive school leadership and decent pay. It also requires trusting teachers with professional judgment rather than reducing education to test preparation.

Assessment and the purposes of education

What gets measured gets taught. If examinations reward memorisation, classrooms will produce memorisers. Assessment should inform learning, not only rank students.

Education also has purposes beyond employability: democratic citizenship, critical thinking, moral judgment and the capacity to live with others. SDG 4 speaks of quality education and lifelong learning, not merely years of schooling.

AI and the future of learning

Artificial intelligence could personalise learning, support teachers and expand access. It could also deepen inequality if access is uneven, if data are extractive and if students are trained to depend on machines rather than think for themselves.

The task is not to ban technology but to govern it: protect student data, invest in teacher capacity, ensure that AI supports rather than replaces human relationships, and teach the judgment needed to use information critically.

中文译文 · CHINESE

要学习，而不只是上学

过去二十年，数百万儿童第一次进入学校。但许多人离开时仍不具备基础读写与算术能力。世界银行称之为“学习贫困”：到小学高年级仍无法理解地阅读简单文本。这一差距并非平均分布，而是沿收入、性别、残障、语言与地理分布。不会阅读的儿童无法学习其他科目，后果会在一生中累积。只有入学而没有学习，是未兑现的承诺。

教师是学校层面最重要的学习因素。但许多体系把教师当作可替换的投入：快速招聘、低薪支付、很少培训、用检查评估。改善学习需要投资教师：职前教育、持续专业发展、合理班额、支持性学校领导与体面薪酬；也需要信任教师的专业判断，而不是把教育简化为应试准备。

衡量什么都教什么。若考试奖励记忆，课堂就会生产记忆者。评估应服务学习，而不只是给学生排名。教育还有就业之外的目的：民主公民、批判性思维、道德判断与与他人共同生活的能力。SDG 4 说的是优质教育与终身学习，而不只是在校年限。

人工智能可以个性化学习、支持教师、扩大机会；若机会不均、数据被剥削、学生被训练成依赖机器而非独立思考，它也会加深不平等。任务不是禁止技术，而是治理技术：保护学生数据、投资教师能力、确保 AI 支持而非替代人际关系，并教授批判使用信息所需的判断力。
