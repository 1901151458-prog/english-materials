01

some 和 any

some 肯定，any 否/疑/条件

A: Are there any apples in the fridge?
B: Yes, there are some. / No, there aren't any.

疑问句用 any，肯定回答用 some。

A: Is there any water in the bottle?
B: Yes, there is some water.

不可数名词同样适用。

例外：在「Would you like some tea?」这类疑问句中用 some，因为说话人希望得到对方肯定的答复；「Could I have some water?」同理。

02

many 和 much

many 可数复数，much 不可数

In the future, many people will live in outer space.

people 可数复数 → many

There isn't much water left in the bottle.

water 不可数 → much

口诀：能数个数用 many，不能数用 much。

03

few / a few / little / a little

有没有 a，肯定否定大不同

词修饰含义例句
few可数复数几乎没有（否定）Few people know what happened.
a few可数复数一些（肯定）We stayed there for a few days.
little不可数几乎没有（否定）There is little hope of success.
a little不可数一些（肯定）There is a little time left.

关键：没有 a 的 few / little 侧重否定（「几乎没有」）；有 a 的 a few / a little 侧重肯定（「有一些」）。

04

复合不定代词

作主语用单数；定语后置

由 some、any、no、every 加上 -body、-one、-thing 构成。除 no one 外都写成一个词。

Everyone says Yulong Mountain is beautiful.

作主语，谓语用单数 says。

Nobody knows what the future will be like.

作主语，谓语用单数 knows。

定语后置：复合不定代词有定语修饰时，定语放后面。

Did you do anything fun on your holiday? / I did nothing special. / I met someone interesting.
