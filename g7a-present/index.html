01

一般现在时的意义

状态 / 习惯 / 能力

  • 现在的状态：Where are the twins from? / She doesn't have a brother.
  • 经常发生的或习惯性的动作：My school begins at 9:00. / I often listen to music on the way.
  • 主语具备的性格和能力：Colin's father works hard.
02

系动词 be

am / is / are

主语be否定
Iamam not
you / we / theyareare not (aren't)
he / she / itisis not (isn't)

缩略形式：I'm、you're、he's、she's、it's、we're、they're；isn't、aren't。

03

三单变化

加 -s / -es、y 变 i

情况变化例
一般情况加 -shelp → helps、like → likes、play → plays
以 -s、-x、-ch、-sh 结尾加 -esguess → guesses、fix → fixes、teach → teaches、finish → finishes
辅音字母 + y 结尾y 变 i 加 -esstudy → studies
04

否定与疑问 + 陈述句

don't / doesn't；Do / Does

实义动词否定

  • I / You / We / They do not (don't) like
  • He / She / It does not (doesn't) like
  • 后面一律用动词原形

实义动词疑问

  • Do you like oranges? — Yes, I do.
  • Does he like oranges? — No, he doesn't.

陈述句：肯定结构谓语不含否定词；否定结构的三种方式 —— ① be + not；② don't / doesn't + 原形；③ never / no 等否定词。陈述句句末用句号。
