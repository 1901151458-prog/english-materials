\section{一般现在时的意义}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{现在的状态}：Where are the twins from? / She doesn't have a brother.
\item \textbf{经常发生的或习惯性的动作}：My school begins at 9:00. / I often listen to music on the way.
\item \textbf{主语具备的性格和能力}：Colin's father works hard.
\end{itemize}

\hline

\section{否定、疑问与陈述句}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{实义动词否定}：I / You / We / They \underline{do not (don't)} like；He / She / It \underline{does not (doesn't)} like；后面一律用\textbf{动词原形}。
\item \textbf{实义动词疑问}：\underline{Do} you like oranges? --- Yes, I do. / \underline{Does} he like oranges? --- No, he doesn't.
\item \textbf{陈述句}：肯定结构谓语不含否定词；否定结构的三种方式 —— ① be + not；② don't / doesn't + 原形；③ never / no 等否定词。陈述句句末用\textbf{句号}。
\end{itemize}

\section*{答案与解析}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{P1-1 D}：过去某时正在进行用过去进行时；一般现在时表状态、习惯、能力。
