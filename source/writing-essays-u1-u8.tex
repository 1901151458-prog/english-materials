\fanunit{1}{The Changing World}{变化中的世界}{一般过去时 vs 现在完成时；used to do sth}

\fansection{〇、本单元写作常用句型}
\begin{fanpatterns}

\fanpat{I used to be afraid of speaking in class.}{我过去害怕在课堂上发言。}{used to do 表示过去的习惯或状态，暗示“现在已经不再这样”。}

\fanpat{There used to be a small river near my home.}{我家附近过去有一条小河。}{There used to be 表示过去存在的事物，常用于描写家乡的环境变化。}

\fanpat{Great changes have taken place in my hometown.}{我的家乡发生了巨大的变化。}{现在完成时强调变化已经发生，是“变化类”作文的万能开头句。}

\fanpat{Our life has become better and better.}{我们的生活变得越来越好。}{现在完成时与“比较级 + and + 比较级”连用，表达持续向好的变化。}

\fanpat{In the past, the streets were narrow and dirty.}{过去，街道又窄又脏。}{一般过去时描写过去的状况，与对现在的描写形成今昔对比。}

\fanpat{Chengdu is no longer what it used to be.}{成都已不再是过去的样子了。}{what it used to be 表示“过去的样子”，用于总述一座城市的变化。}

\fanpat{Compared with the past, life is much more convenient now.}{与过去相比，现在的生活方便多了。}{Compared with... 引出今昔对比，是写作中的高频表达。}

\fanpat{I have lived in Chengdu for ten years.}{我已经在成都住了十年。}{现在完成时与 for 连用，表示从过去一直持续到现在的状态。}

\end{fanpatterns}

\fansection{一、满分范文}

\fanessay{范文一}{我生活的变化}{Changes in My Life}{约126词}
Great changes have taken place in my life over the past few years. I used to spend most of my free time watching TV, which made me forget my homework. Now, I have developed a habit of reading and exercising. I often go jogging along the Tianfu Greenway, which is famous for its beautiful views.\par
What's more, my family used to live in a small apartment, but now we live in a bright new one. Life has become so convenient that I can do many things with a smartphone. Not only has my life become more colorful, but I have also become more confident. I believe these changes have taught me to value every day. I will keep working hard to make my life even better.\par
\begin{analysisbox}
1）开篇用现在完成时 Great changes have taken place 总起全篇，直接点题；2）used to spend... watching TV 与 now 形成鲜明对比，两个 which 引导的非限制性定语从句让叙述更加地道流畅；3）结尾 Not only has my life become more colorful, but I have also become more confident. 使用倒装结构，句式高级且表意自然，升华“变化让我成长”的主题。
\par\smallskip{\sffamily\bfseries\color{mainblue}亮点句：}{\itshape Not only has my life become more colorful, but I have also become more confident.}\quad{\small\color{textgray}（译文：我的生活不仅变得更加丰富多彩，我自己也变得更加自信。）}
\end{analysisbox}

\fanessay{范文二}{家乡成都的变化}{Changes in My Hometown}{约137词}
Chengdu, my hometown, has changed a lot over the past ten years. I still remember the narrow streets that were full of people and old bikes. At that time, few people could imagine the changes that were coming. The city has built a large metro network, so people can travel to almost every corner of the city quickly. Wide roads have taken the place of small paths, and high buildings have risen where low houses once stood.\par
Moreover, Chengdu has become a famous ``Park City''. The Tianfu Greenway, which stretches across the whole city, offers people a wonderful place to exercise and relax. Not only is the environment much better now, but people's lives are also much more convenient. I am proud of my hometown and I believe it will become even more beautiful in the future.\par
\begin{analysisbox}
1）两处定语从句 that were full of...、that were coming 描写旧时街道，画面感强；2）Wide roads have taken the place of small paths 与 high buildings have risen where low houses once stood 用 take the place of、rise 两个动词生动呈现新旧更替，where 引导地点状语从句；3）Not only is the environment... but people's lives are also... 倒装结构提升句式层次，结尾 I am proud of 升华情感。
\par\smallskip{\sffamily\bfseries\color{mainblue}亮点句：}{\itshape High buildings have risen where low houses once stood.}\quad{\small\color{textgray}（译文：高楼大厦在昔日低矮房屋所在的地方拔地而起。）}
\end{analysisbox}

\fanessay{范文三}{给外国笔友的信：介绍成都的变化}{A Letter About the Changes in Chengdu}{约140词}
Dear Tom,\par
How is everything going? I'm glad to tell you that great changes have taken place in Chengdu, my hometown.\par
Chengdu used to be a city of low houses and narrow streets. But now it has become a modern ``Park City''. The metro network has made traveling so convenient that you can reach almost everywhere in the city quickly. What's more, the Tianfu Greenway, which is loved by people of all ages, offers a wonderful place for running and cycling. Not only has the environment become cleaner, but the city has also built more parks and wide roads. Last week I rode along the Greenway with my parents, and I was amazed by the beautiful views.\par
I hope you can come and see the new Chengdu yourself. I'm sure you will fall in love with it.\par
Yours,\par
Li Hua\par
\begin{analysisbox}
1）书信格式完整规范，开头 How is everything going? 符合笔友通信的语气，结尾自然收束；2）used to be 与 has become 形成今昔对比，so...that... 与 which 非限制性定语从句并用，表达丰富地道；3）插入 Last week I rode along the Greenway with my parents 这一亲身经历，用一般过去时写具体事例，增强真实感和说服力。
\par\smallskip{\sffamily\bfseries\color{mainblue}亮点句：}{\itshape The metro network has made traveling so convenient that you can reach almost everywhere in the city quickly.}\quad{\small\color{textgray}（译文：地铁网络让出行变得如此便捷，你可以很快到达这座城市的几乎任何地方。）}
\end{analysisbox}

\fanessay{范文四}{过去与现在的我}{How I Have Changed}{约144词}
Looking back at the past three years, I find that I have changed a lot, not only in appearance but also in personality.\par
I used to be a quiet and shy boy. When teachers asked questions, I never raised my hand because I was afraid of making mistakes. However, now I have become confident and outgoing. I often join in class discussions and share my ideas with others. I used to hate sports, but now I have fallen in love with running. Every weekend, I go running with my father along the Tianfu Greenway, which makes me healthy and energetic. What's more, I have made great progress in my study. I have developed good learning habits, so my grades have improved a lot.\par
These changes have taught me an important lesson: as long as we try our best, we can become better and better.\par
\begin{analysisbox}
1）开头用非谓语动词 Looking back at... 引出回忆，not only...but also... 概括变化维度；2）两处 used to... but now... 对比性格与爱好，一般过去时 never raised my hand 与现在完成时 have fallen in love with 切换准确；3）which makes me healthy and energetic 定语从句自然衔接，结尾 as long as 条件句点出变化带来的成长感悟。
\par\smallskip{\sffamily\bfseries\color{mainblue}亮点句：}{\itshape I used to hate sports, but now I have fallen in love with running.}\quad{\small\color{textgray}（译文：我过去讨厌运动，但现在我已经爱上了跑步。）}
\end{analysisbox}

\fanessay{范文五}{环境的变化与我们的行动}{Changes in the Environment and What We Can Do}{约142词}
The environment has changed greatly in recent years. In the past, some rivers were so dirty that fish could not live in them, and many people used to throw rubbish everywhere. However, great changes have taken place. The water has become cleaner, the streets are much tidier, and trees have been planted along the wide roads. Chengdu is now praised as a ``Park City'', and the Tianfu Greenway has become a popular place for walking and cycling.\par
These changes did not happen by themselves. Not only has the government worked hard to protect the environment, but ordinary people like us have also taken action. We have learned to sort rubbish and use public transport more often.\par
The environment is our home. I believe that as long as everyone plays a part, our city will become an even better place to live in.\par
\begin{analysisbox}
1）过去用 so dirty that... 与 used to throw rubbish 描写环境问题，现在完成时 have taken place、has become 呈现变化成果，对比鲜明；2）These changes did not happen by themselves. 独立成段、短句有力，not only...but also... 倒装结构引出政府与个人的共同努力；3）结尾 as long as 条件句发出倡议，everyone plays a part 呼应单元话题，主题升华。
\par\smallskip{\sffamily\bfseries\color{mainblue}亮点句：}{\itshape Not only has the government worked hard to protect the environment, but ordinary people like us have also taken action.}\quad{\small\color{textgray}（译文：不仅政府努力保护环境，像我们这样的普通人也已经行动起来。）}
\end{analysisbox}

\fansection{二、高分好背好文（适合基础薄弱同学）}

\fanessay{范文六}{我的变化}{My Changes}{约87词}
I have changed a lot in the past three years. Now let me tell you about my changes.\par
First, I used to be short, but now I am much taller than before. Second, I used to be shy and quiet, but now I like talking with my classmates. Finally, I used to dislike reading, but now I read books for half an hour every day.\par
I think these changes are good for me. I am very happy about them, and I want to become better and better.\par
\begin{transbox}
在过去的三年里，我变化很大。现在让我告诉你我的变化。首先，我过去个子矮，但现在我比以前高多了。其次，我过去害羞又安静，但现在我喜欢和同学们聊天。最后，我过去不喜欢读书，但现在我每天读半小时书。我认为这些变化对我有好处。我对这些变化感到非常高兴，我想变得越来越好。
\end{transbox}

\fanessay{范文七}{我们家乡的变化}{Our Hometown Has Changed}{约88词}
Our hometown Chengdu has changed a lot in recent years. Now let me tell you about the changes.\par
First, the roads used to be narrow, but now they are wide and clean. Second, there used to be only a few buses, but now the metro network can take us to many places. Finally, people used to live in low houses, but now many people live in tall buildings.\par

\begin{fanpatterns}

\fanpat{Chopin is a great musician who has encouraged millions of people.}{肖邦是一位激励了千百万人的伟大音乐家。}{who 引导定语从句，修饰先行词 a great musician，在从句中作主语，先行词指人时常用 who。}

\fanunit{8}{More than a Game}{不止是一场比赛}{主谓一致（subject-verb agreement）}

\fansection{〇、本单元写作常用句型}
\begin{fanpatterns}

\fanpat{Playing team sports is a good way to make friends.}{参加团队运动是交朋友的好方式。}{动名词短语 Playing team sports 作主语时视为单数，谓语动词用 is，这是主谓一致最基本的规则之一。}

\fanpat{To become a professional player takes years of hard training.}{成为一名职业运动员需要多年的刻苦训练。}{不定式短语 To become a professional player 作主语时，谓语动词用单数 takes，符合主谓一致的要求。}

\fanpat{Either your parents or your coach has to sign the form.}{要么你的父母，要么你的教练必须在表格上签字。}{either...or... 连接两个主语时遵循就近原则，谓语与最近的 your coach 保持一致，用 has，这是主谓一致的常考规则。}

\fanpat{Neither the players nor the coach was happy with the result.}{队员们和教练对比赛结果都不满意。}{neither...nor... 连接并列主语时采用就近原则，谓语与最近的 the coach 一致，用单数 was，体现主谓一致。}

\fanpat{Not only the students but also their teacher is excited about the school sports meeting.}{不仅学生们，他们的老师也对学校运动会感到兴奋。}{not only...but also... 连接主语时遵循就近原则，谓语与 their teacher 保持一致，用 is，是主谓一致的重要考点。}

\fanpat{There is a running track and two basketball courts in our school.}{我们学校有一条跑道和两个篮球场。}{there be 句型遵循就近原则，谓语动词与最近的主语 a running track 保持一致，用 is，属于主谓一致的常考句型。}

\fanpat{A number of students are training hard for the sports meeting, and the number of volunteers is growing.}{许多学生正在为运动会刻苦训练，志愿者的数量也在增加。}{a number of 意为“许多”，谓语用复数 are；the number of 意为“……的数量”，中心词是 number，谓语用单数 is，这一对比是主谓一致的高频易错点。}

\fanpat{Our class is going to cheer for the runners, and each student has prepared a small flag.}{我们班将去为跑步的同学们加油，每个学生都准备了一面小旗。}{集合名词 class 作为一个整体作主语时谓语用单数 is；each 加单数名词作主语时谓语也用单数 has，二者都是主谓一致的常见考点。}

\end{fanpatterns}

\fansection{一、满分范文}

\fanessay{范文一}{学校运动会}{The School Sports Meeting}{约139词}
Our school sports meeting was held at Fenghuangshan Sports Park last Friday, and it was an unforgettable day for all of us.\par
Early in the morning, all the students arrived at the stadium excitedly. The opening ceremony was short but wonderful, and our class marched in with bright flags. Then the competitions began. Taking part in the relay race was the most exciting part for me, and every runner in our team tried his best. Our class won two first prizes, which made us cheer loudly. What impressed me most was not the result but the spirit. When a boy from Class 5 fell down, several students from other classes ran to help him. In my opinion, taking part is much more important than winning.\par
The sports meeting taught me that teamwork and friendship are more than a game.\par
\begin{analysisbox}
1）开篇交代运动会的时间与地点，并点出凤凰山体育公园这一成都元素，自然亲切；2）中间段落按“开幕式—比赛—暖心瞬间”的顺序推进，Taking part in the relay race was... 用动名词作主语、谓语用单数，every runner... tried his best 中 every 加单数名词与谓语保持一致，多处体现主谓一致；3）结尾 teamwork and friendship are more than a game 用复数主语与 are 呼应，点明“体育不止是一场比赛”的主题，升华自然。
\par\smallskip{\sffamily\bfseries\color{mainblue}亮点句：}{\itshape Taking part in the relay race was the most exciting part for me, and every runner in our team tried his best.}\quad{\small\color{textgray}（译文：对我来说，参加接力赛是最激动人心的部分，我们队的每个队员都尽了最大努力。）}
\end{analysisbox}

\fanessay{范文二}{接力赛现场报道}{A Report on the Relay Race}{约135词}

\par\smallskip{\sffamily\bfseries\color{mainblue}亮点句：}{\itshape Neither the athletes nor the coach looks calm at this moment.}\quad{\small\color{textgray}（译文：此时此刻，无论是运动员们还是教练都无法平静。）}
\end{analysisbox}

\fanessay{范文三}{我最敬佩的运动员苏炳添}{Su Bingtian, the Athlete I Admire Most}{约141词}

\fanessay{范文四}{不止是一场比赛}{More than a Game}{约134词}
