Section A · 3a · Water Festivals

Section A · 3a 主题：节日体验 · 泼水节

Teng Fei: What's on your calendar next month, Clarice?

Clarice，你下个月的日程表上有什么安排？

词汇calendar 日程表；next month 下个月 语法What's on ...? 询问安排。

Clarice: I'm going to Thailand to experience their New Year celebrations.

我要去泰国体验他们的新年庆祝活动。

词汇Thailand 泰国；experience 体验；celebration 庆祝 语法be going to 表示计划；to experience 表示目的。

Teng Fei: Wow, that must be fun, although I believe it's quite hot there.

哇，那一定很有趣，不过我觉得那里很热。

词汇must be 一定是；although 虽然 语法must be 表示肯定推测；although 引导让步状语从句。

Clarice: Yes, that's true, but there's a water festival there from the 13th to 15th of April.

是的，没错，不过那里 4 月 13 日到 15 日有泼水节。

词汇water festival 泼水节；from ... to ... 从……到…… 语法There be 句型；but 表转折。

Teng Fei: That must cool people down.

那一定让人感到凉爽。

词汇cool down 使凉快 语法must + 动词原形表示推测。

Teng Fei: Do you know you can also do the same in China?

你知道在中国也能做同样的事吗？

词汇the same 同样的事 语法Do you know + 宾语从句。

Clarice: Really? Where?

真的吗？在哪里？

词汇really 真的 语法省略问句。

Teng Fei: Well, there's also a water festival in Yunnan Province.

嗯，云南也有泼水节。

词汇Yunnan Province 云南省 语法There be 句型。

Clarice: How exciting! I wonder if the two water festivals are similar.

太令人兴奋了！我想知道这两个泼水节是否相似。

词汇exciting 令人兴奋的；wonder 想知道；similar 相似的 语法How + 形容词! 感叹句；if 引导宾语从句。

Teng Fei: Yes, it's actually the Dai people's New Year celebration in mid-April as well.

是的，它其实也是傣族在 4 月中旬的新年庆祝活动。

词汇actually 实际上；the Dai people 傣族；mid-April 四月中旬 语法also 表示“也”。

Teng Fei: People go into the streets and throw water at each other.

人们走上街头，互相泼水。

词汇street 街道；throw water 泼水 语法and 连接并列谓语。

Clarice: That sounds interesting. But why do they do that?

听起来很有趣。但他们为什么这样做呢？

词汇sound interesting 听起来有趣 语法sound 是系动词；why 引导特殊疑问句。

Teng Fei: Well, according to their culture, the water washes away bad things.

嗯，按照他们的文化，水会冲走不好的东西。

词汇according to 根据；culture 文化；wash away 冲走 语法according to 表示“根据”。

Teng Fei: Then they'll have good luck in the new year.

这样他们在新的一年里就会有好运。

词汇good luck 好运 语法will + 动词原形表示将来。

Clarice: I hope I'll get some good luck in Thailand!

我希望我在泰国能交到好运！

词汇hope 希望 语法hope + 宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P4 Section A · 3a 中 Teng Fei 和 Clarice 的对话。Teng Fei 问 Clarice 下个月有什么安排；Clarice 说要去泰国体验新年庆祝活动。Teng Fei 说那一定很有趣，不过那里很热；Clarice 说 4 月 13 日到 15 日那里有泼水节。Teng Fei 说那一定让人凉爽，并告诉 Clarice 中国也有同样的节日。Clarice 问在哪里；Teng Fei 说云南也有泼水节，而且它其实也是傣族在 4 月中旬的新年庆祝活动，人们走上街头互相泼水。Clarice 问为什么这样做；Teng Fei 解释说，按照他们的文化，水会冲走不好的东西，这样新的一年就会有好运。Clarice 希望自己在泰国也能交到好运。

English Summary

This is the conversation between Teng Fei and Clarice in Section A · 3a on page 4. Teng Fei asks what is on Clarice's calendar next month, and Clarice says she is going to Thailand to experience their New Year celebrations. Teng Fei says that must be fun although it is quite hot there, and Clarice says that is true but there is a water festival there from 13 to 15 April. Teng Fei says that must cool people down and tells Clarice that she can do the same in China. Clarice asks where, and Teng Fei says there is also a water festival in Yunnan Province; it is actually the Dai people's New Year celebration in mid-April, when people go into the streets and throw water at each other. Clarice asks why they do that, and Teng Fei explains that according to their culture the water washes away bad things, so they will have good luck in the new year. Clarice hopes she will get some good luck in Thailand!

语法点 Grammar

What's on your calendar next month, Clarice?

What's on ...? 询问安排。

I'm going to Thailand to experience their New Year celebrations.

be going to 表示计划；to experience 表示目的。

Wow, that must be fun, although I believe it's quite hot there.

must be 表示肯定推测；although 引导让步状语从句。

Yes, that's true, but there's a water festival there from the 13th to 15th of April.

There be 句型；from ... to ... 表示时间范围。

That must cool people down.

must + 动词原形表示推测。

Do you know you can also do the same in China?

Do you know + 宾语从句。

Well, there's also a water festival in Yunnan Province.

There be 句型；also 表示“也”。

I wonder if the two water festivals are similar.

if 引导宾语从句，表示“是否”。

It's actually the Dai people's New Year celebration in mid-April as well.

名词所有格 the Dai people's；as well 表示“也”。

People go into the streets and throw water at each other.

and 连接并列谓语。

But why do they do that?

why 引导特殊疑问句。

According to their culture, the water washes away bad things.

according to 表示“根据”。

Then they'll have good luck in the new year.

Section B 1b · New Year Celebrations around the World

Section B 1b 主题：世界各地新年（教材 P6–P7 两页）

New Year is a wonderful time when family and friends come together to remember the old year and welcome the new one.

新年是家人和朋友聚在一起回顾旧年、迎接新年的美好时光。

词汇wonderful 美好的；come together 聚在一起 语法when 引导定语从句；to remember/welcome 表示目的。

People all around the world celebrate New Year with music, special food, and fireworks.

世界各地的人们用音乐、特别的食物和烟花庆祝新年。

词汇fireworks 烟花 语法with 表示方式。

In fact, they even do it at different times.

事实上，他们甚至在不同时间庆祝。

词汇in fact 事实上；even 甚至 语法in fact 表示“事实上”。

Let's look at how this festival is celebrated in some parts of the world.

我们来看看世界一些地方是如何庆祝这个节日的。

词汇look at 看看；part 部分 语法how 引导宾语从句；被动语态 is celebrated。

In the UK, many people gather in London on 31 December to see the famous Big Ben clock tower and count down to midnight, while millions watch it on TV.

在英国，许多人 12 月 31 日聚集在伦敦，观看著名的大本钟并一起倒计时到午夜，同时数百万人通过电视观看。

词汇gather 聚集；count down 倒计时；midnight 午夜 语法while 表示“同时”；to see 表示目的。

At midnight they sing Auld Lang Syne, a Scottish song, to remember friends and family.

午夜时他们唱苏格兰歌曲《友谊地久天长》，以纪念朋友和家人。

词汇Scottish 苏格兰的 语法Auld Lang Syne 是专有名词；to remember 表示目的。

In the US, people have similar customs.

在美国，人们也有类似的习俗。

词汇similar 相似的；custom 习俗 语法have customs 表示“有习俗”。

A famous tradition is the dropping of a big ball in New York.

一个著名的传统是在纽约降下大球。

词汇tradition 传统；drop 落下 语法动名词短语作表语。

Huge crowds gather in Times Square to watch this.

大批人群聚集在时代广场观看这一活动。

词汇huge 巨大的；crowd 人群 语法to watch 表示目的。

When the ball reaches the bottom at exactly midnight, they all wish each other "Happy New Year!"

当大球在午夜准时落到底部时，大家互相祝愿“新年快乐！”

词汇reach 到达；bottom 底部；exactly 恰好；wish 祝愿 语法when 引导时间状语从句。

Chinese people consider the Spring Festival the most important.

中国人认为春节最重要。

词汇consider 认为；the Spring Festival 春节 语法consider + 宾语 + 宾语补足语。

It is a time for everyone to say goodbye to the old year and give a warm welcome to the new.

这是大家告别旧年、热烈迎接新年的时刻。

词汇say goodbye to 告别；give a warm welcome 热烈欢迎 语法It is a time for sb. to do sth. 句型。

There are lots of customs, such as sweeping out dust from houses, putting up couplets, setting off fireworks, and watching lion dances and dragon dances.

有很多习俗，比如扫尘、贴春联、放烟花、看舞狮和舞龙。

词汇sweep out 扫除；couplet 春联；set off 燃放；lion dance 舞狮 语法such as 用于举例；并列动名词短语。

One very important custom is for people to return home and reunite with their families.

一个非常重要的习俗是人们回家与家人团聚。

词汇reunite with 与……团聚 语法for sb. to do sth. 作表语。

They especially enjoy the big family dinner on the eve of the Spring Festival.

他们尤其享受除夕的大家庭晚餐。

词汇especially 尤其；on the eve of 在……前夕 语法enjoy + 名词。

This is when grandparents, parents, and children come together to eat delicious food and express best wishes for the whole family.

这时祖父母、父母和孩子聚在一起，品尝美食，为全家表达美好祝愿。

词汇grandparent 祖父母；express 表达；best wishes 美好祝愿 语法when 引导表语从句；and 连接并列动作。

In Egypt, people celebrate Sham El-Nessim in April to mark the beginning of spring.

在埃及，人们四月庆祝沙姆恩奈西姆节，以标志春天的开始。

词汇Egypt 埃及；mark 标志 语法to mark 表示目的。

The name of the festival means "smelling the breeze".

这个节日的名字意思是“闻微风”。

词汇breeze 微风 语法mean 表示“意思是”。

At this time, the weather becomes brighter, and the flowers and trees begin to show their colours.

这时天气变得更明朗，花草树木开始展现色彩。

词汇brighter 更明朗的；colour 色彩 语法and 连接并列分句。

People paint eggs which are symbols of new beginnings.

人们画彩蛋，彩蛋是新开始的象征。

词汇paint 画；symbol 象征 语法which 引导定语从句。

They also go out to enjoy picnics, dance, sing, and watch plays at the theatre.

他们还外出野餐、跳舞、唱歌，去剧院看戏。

词汇picnic 野餐；theatre 剧院 语法go out to do sth.；并列动词。

Although New Year celebrations are different throughout the world, something remains the same.

虽然世界各地的新年庆祝方式不同，但有些东西始终不变。

词汇throughout 遍及；remain 保持 语法although 引导让步状语从句。

In every country, it is a special time for people to spend time with their family and friends and show hope for the coming year.

在每个国家，这都是人们与亲友共度时光、对未来一年寄予希望的特殊时刻。

词汇spend time with 与……共度时光；hope 希望 语法it is a time for sb. to do sth.；and 连接并列动作。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P6–P7 两页 Section B 1b 的文章《New Year Celebrations around the World》（世界各地的新年庆祝）。文章说，新年是家人朋友聚在一起回顾旧年、迎接新年的美好时光；世界各地的人用音乐、特别的食物和烟花庆祝新年，而且庆祝时间各不相同。文章介绍了几个地方：在英国，人们 12 月 31 日聚集在伦敦看大本钟并倒计时到午夜，午夜时唱苏格兰歌曲《友谊地久天长》纪念亲友，数百万人通过电视观看；在美国，人们在纽约时代广场观看大球降落，球在午夜准时落到底部时大家互祝“新年快乐”。中国人认为春节最重要，春节习俗有扫尘、贴春联、放烟花、看舞狮舞龙等；最重要的习俗是回家与家人团聚，尤其享受除夕的大家庭晚餐。在埃及，人们四月庆祝沙姆恩奈西姆节，标志春天开始，节日名字意为“闻微风”，人们画彩蛋、野餐、跳舞、唱歌、看戏。文章最后说，虽然世界各地的新年庆祝方式不同，但有些东西不变：在每个国家，这都是人们与亲友共度时光、对未来寄予希望的特殊时刻。

English Summary

This is the article "New Year Celebrations around the World" in Section B 1b, on pages 6–7. It says that New Year is a wonderful time when family and friends come together to remember the old year and welcome the new one, and that people all around the world celebrate New Year with music, special food and fireworks — in fact, at different times. In the UK, many people gather in London on 31 December to see the famous Big Ben clock tower and count down to midnight while millions watch on TV; at midnight they sing Auld Lang Syne, a Scottish song, to remember friends and family. In the US, people have similar customs: a famous tradition is the dropping of a big ball in New York, and huge crowds gather in Times Square to watch it, wishing each other "Happy New Year!" when the ball reaches the bottom at exactly midnight. Chinese people consider the Spring Festival the most important; it is a time to say goodbye to the old year and welcome the new, with customs such as sweeping out dust, putting up couplets, setting off fireworks, and watching lion and dragon dances. One very important custom is returning home to reunite with family, and people especially enjoy the big family dinner on the eve of the Spring Festival. In Egypt, people celebrate Sham El-Nessim in April to mark the beginning of spring; the name means "smelling the breeze", and people paint eggs, enjoy picnics, dance, sing and watch plays. Finally, although New Year celebrations are different throughout the world, something remains the same: in every country it is a special time to spend time with family and friends and show hope for the coming year.

This is the Reading Plus article "The 24 Solar Terms" on pages 51–52. More than 2,000 years ago the ancient Chinese developed the 24 solar terms, a unique "calendar" to understand nature; they divided the year into 24 periods, each lasting 15 or 16 days. 在芒种——夏季第三个节气——期间，中国北方农民收割冬小麦，而南方农民忙着插秧。

which 引导非限制性定语从句；while 表示对比。

They remind us of how nature changes through the year and reflect the Chinese wisdom of living in harmony with nature.

它们提醒我们大自然一年中的变化，也体现了中国人与自然和谐相处的智慧。

remind sb. of sth.；how 引导宾语从句；living in harmony with nature 是动名词短语。

阅读上面四篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. Where is Clarice going next month?

  1. To Thailand.
  2. To Yunnan Province.
  3. To London.
  4. To Brazil.
答案与解析

A 原文：I'm going to Thailand to experience their New Year celebrations. 可知 Clarice 下个月要去泰国。

2. Why do people throw water at each other during the water festival?

  1. To have fun.
  2. To wash away bad things and have good luck.
  3. To celebrate the harvest.
  4. To welcome visitors.
答案与解析

B 原文：according to their culture, the water washes away bad things. Then they'll have good luck in the new year. 可知泼水是为了冲走不好的东西、迎来好运。

3. Where do people in the US watch the ball drop on New Year's Eve?

  1. In London.
  2. At the beach.
  3. In Times Square, New York.
  4. In the streets of Rio.
答案与解析

C 原文：A famous tradition is the dropping of a big ball in New York. Huge crowds gather in Times Square to watch this. 可知人们在纽约时代广场观看大球降落。

4. How long do the 24 solar terms divide the year into?

  1. 12 periods, each lasting a month.
  2. 4 seasons of 3 months.
  3. 52 weeks of 7 days.
  4. 24 periods, each lasting 15 or 16 days.
答案与解析

D 原文：They divided the year into 24 periods, each lasting 15 or 16 days. 可知一年被分为 24 个时段。

5. What do people eat on the day of Start of Winter?

  1. Dumplings.
  2. Mooncakes.
  3. Spring rolls.
  4. Eggs.
答案与解析

A 原文：A traditional custom on the day of Start of Winter is to eat dumplings. 可知立冬吃饺子。

6. What is Jason's favourite festival?

  1. The Spring Festival.
  2. The Carnival in Rio de Janeiro.
  3. Sham El-Nessim.
  4. The Mid-Autumn Festival.
答案与解析

B 原文：I am enjoying the Carnival in my hometown, Rio de Janeiro. This is my favourite festival. 可知 Jason 最喜欢的节日是里约狂欢节。

7. What do people usually do during the Carnival in Rio?

  1. They stay at home.
  2. They watch the ball drop.
  3. They see parades and dance the samba.
  4. They paint eggs.
答案与解析

C 原文：You can see parades everywhere for days. A very traditional dance is the samba. 可知狂欢节期间有游行和桑巴舞。

8. Why do people say this solar term sounds like "busy with planting"?

  1. Because farmers are lazy.
  2. Because it is the coldest term.
  3. Because people eat dumplings.
  4. Because it is called mangzhong in Chinese.
答案与解析

D 原文：It is no wonder why this term sounds like "busy with planting" (mangzhong) in Chinese! 可知芒种的中文读音听起来像“忙着种植”。
