Section A · 3a · Talking About Rubbish

Section A · 3a 主题：垃圾分类 · 3R 理念

Host: The problem of rubbish is becoming more and more serious nowadays.

如今垃圾问题越来越严重。

词汇rubbish 垃圾；serious 严重的 语法比较级 more and more serious 表示“越来越严重”。

Host: Dr Wu, what can we do about it?

吴博士，我们能为此做些什么？

词汇do about 对……采取措施 语法what can we do about it? 询问解决办法。

Dr Wu: Have you heard of the three Rs: reduce, reuse, and recycle?

你听说过 3R 吗：减量、再用、回收？

词汇hear of 听说；reduce 减量；reuse 再用；recycle 回收 语法现在完成时 have heard of；冒号后解释。

Host: Recycle!

回收！

词汇recycle 回收 语法简短的感叹应答。

Host: Everyone knows about that!

人人都知道那个！

词汇know about 了解 语法Everyone knows ... 表示“人人都知道”。

Host: We can recycle materials like glass, paper, plastic, and metal.

我们可以回收玻璃、纸、塑料和金属等材料。

词汇material 材料；metal 金属 语法like 表示“例如”。

Dr Wu: Yes, but while recycling is good, not everything can be recycled.

是的，但虽然回收很好，并不是所有东西都能回收。

词汇not everything 并非所有东西 语法while 表示“虽然”；部分否定 not everything。

Dr Wu: The first thing we need to do is reduce our waste.

我们需要做的第一件事就是减少垃圾。

词汇waste 废物；垃圾 语法need to do sth.；不定式作表语。

Host: Could you tell us what we should do?

你能告诉我们该怎么做吗？

词汇could 能（委婉） 语法Could you ...? 礼貌请求。

Dr Wu: Well, you can buy products with less packaging.

嗯，你可以买包装更少的产品。

词汇packaging 包装 语法with less packaging 作后置定语。

Dr Wu: Also, buy only the food you need to reduce food waste.

另外，只买你需要的食物，以减少食物浪费。

词汇food waste 食物浪费 语法祈使句；you need 是省略关系词的定语从句。

Host: And please tell us about the last "R".

请再给我们讲讲最后一个“R”。

词汇the last R 最后一个 R 语法please + 祈使句。

Dr Wu: Sure.

好的。

词汇sure 当然 语法口语应答。

Dr Wu: Reuse means using things again and again.

再用意味着反复使用物品。

词汇mean doing sth. 意味着做某事 语法mean doing sth. 表示“意味着做某事”。

Dr Wu: Many things we use daily, like glass bottles and plastic bags, can be reused.

我们日常使用的许多东西，比如玻璃瓶和塑料袋，都可以重复使用。

词汇daily 日常地；bottle 瓶子 语法we use daily 是定语从句；被动语态 can be reused。

Dr Wu: We can also donate our clothes, books, and toys to people who need them.

我们还可以把衣服、书和玩具捐给需要的人。

词汇donate 捐赠 语法donate sth. to sb.；who 引导定语从句。

Host: I think we can solve the problem if everyone follows the three Rs!

我认为如果大家都遵循 3R，我们就能解决这个问题！

词汇solve 解决；follow 遵循 语法I think + 宾语从句；if 引导条件状语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P34 Section A · 3a 中主持人（Host）采访吴博士的访谈（3a 要求：弄清吴博士介绍了哪些处理垃圾的方法）。主持人说如今垃圾问题越来越严重，问吴博士能做什么。吴博士提出 3R：reduce（减量）、reuse（再用）、recycle（回收）。主持人说大家都知道回收，可以回收玻璃、纸、塑料和金属等。吴博士说回收虽好，但并非一切都能回收；首先要减少垃圾——买包装更少的产品、只买需要的食物以减少浪费。主持人请她讲最后一个“R”；吴博士说 reuse 就是反复使用，玻璃瓶、塑料袋等日常用品都可重复使用，还可以把衣服、书和玩具捐给需要的人。主持人最后说，如果人人遵循 3R，这个问题就能解决。

English Summary

This is the interview between the host and Dr Wu in Section A · 3a on page 34; in 3a, students read the interview and find out what ways to deal with rubbish Dr Wu introduces. The host says the problem of rubbish is becoming more and more serious and asks what we can do. Dr Wu introduces the three Rs: reduce, reuse and recycle. The host says everyone knows about recycling and that we can recycle materials like glass, paper, plastic and metal, but Dr Wu points out that while recycling is good, not everything can be recycled, and the first thing we need to do is reduce our waste — buy products with less packaging and only buy the food we need. When the host asks about the last "R", Dr Wu explains that reuse means using things again and again; many things we use daily, like glass bottles and plastic bags, can be reused, and we can also donate our clothes, books and toys to people who need them. The host concludes that we can solve the problem if everyone follows the three Rs.

语法点 Grammar

The problem of rubbish is becoming more and more serious nowadays.

比较级 more and more serious 表示“越来越严重”。

Have you heard of the three Rs: reduce, reuse, and recycle?

现在完成时 have heard of；冒号后解释。

We can recycle materials like glass, paper, plastic, and metal.

like 表示“例如”。

Yes, but while recycling is good, not everything can be recycled.

while 表示“虽然”；部分否定 not everything。

The first thing we need to do is reduce our waste.

need to do sth.；不定式作表语。

Could you tell us what we should do?

Could you ...? 礼貌请求。

Well, you can buy products with less packaging.

with less packaging 作后置定语。

Also, buy only the food you need to reduce food waste.

祈使句；you need 是省略关系词的定语从句。

Reuse means using things again and again.

mean doing sth. 表示“意味着做某事”。

Many things we use daily, like glass bottles and plastic bags, can be reused.

we use daily 是定语从句；被动语态 can be reused。

We can also donate our clothes, books, and toys to people who need them.

donate sth. to sb.；who 引导定语从句。

I think we can solve the problem if everyone follows the three Rs!

I think + 宾语从句；if 引导条件状语从句。

Everyone knows about that!

Everyone knows ... 表示“人人都知道”。

We need to take action before the problem gets worse.

before 引导时间状语从句。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

while 表示“虽然”；not everything 是部分否定，意为“并非一切”。

Reuse means using things again and again.

再用意味着反复使用物品。

mean doing sth. 表示“意味着做某事”；again and again 表示“反复地”。

I think we can solve the problem if everyone follows the three Rs!

我认为如果大家都遵循 3R，我们就能解决这个问题！

I think + 宾语从句；if 引导条件状语从句。

Section B 1b · The Yangtze River and Its Guardians

Section B 1b 主题：长江保护 · 环保行动（教材 P36–P37 两页）

Every morning, 53-year-old Chen Ming walks 15 kilometres along the Yangtze River.

每天早上，53 岁的陈明沿着长江走 15 公里。

词汇kilometre 公里；along 沿着 语法一般现在时；合成形容词 53-year-old 作定语。

As he does so, he often stops to talk to the people picnicking beside the water or to watch the birds catch fish in the river's clear water.

他一边走一边常停下来，和水边野餐的人交谈，或观看鸟儿在清澈的江水中捕鱼。

词汇picnic 野餐；beside 在……旁边 语法as 引导时间状语从句；or 连接两个不定式。

While one might think that he is just out for exercise, he is actually working.

人们也许以为他只是出来锻炼，但他实际上是在工作。

词汇out for exercise 出来锻炼；actually 实际上 语法while 表示“虽然”表对比。

Mr Chen is a "Guardian of the River".

陈先生是一名“长江守护者”。

词汇guardian 守护者 语法专有称号用引号。

His job is to walk along the river and make sure that no one is fishing in it or polluting it.

他的工作是沿着江边行走，确保没有人在江里捕鱼或污染江水。

词汇make sure 确保；fish 捕鱼；pollute 污染 语法不定式作表语；make sure + 从句。

Mr Chen was not a "Guardian of the River" before.

陈先生以前并不是“长江守护者”。

词汇before 以前 语法一般过去时。

He grew up on the banks of the Yangtze River and used to be a fisherman, like his father.

他在长江边长大，过去和他父亲一样是个渔夫。

词汇grow up 长大；bank 岸；fisherman 渔夫 语法used to be 表示“过去是”。

Back then, parts of the river were smelly and yellow because of the waste from factories and homes.

那时，部分江段又臭又黄，因为工厂和家庭排放的废物。

词汇smelly 发臭的；waste 废物；factory 工厂 语法because of 后接名词；back then 表示“那时”。

Fishermen were also catching too many fish.

渔夫们还捕捞了太多鱼。

词汇too many 太多 语法过去进行时表示过去持续的动作。

As a result, the number of fish and plants in the river was dropping quickly.

结果，河中鱼类和植物的数量迅速减少。

词汇as a result 结果；drop 下降 语法as a result 表示结果；the number of ... 作主语。

The government took action to deal with the problem before it was too late.

政府及时采取行动处理这个问题。

词汇take action 采取行动；deal with 处理 语法before it was too late 表示“趁还不算太晚”。

New waste centres were built to clean the wastewater before it went into the river.

新建了废物处理中心，在污水流入长江前进行净化。

词汇wastewater 废水 语法被动语态 were built；before 引导时间状语从句。

Laws were made to stop new factories from being built near the river.

制定法律，阻止在江边新建工厂。

词汇law 法律；stop...from... 阻止……做…… 语法stop sb./sth. from doing sth. 结构。

Boats were sent out to clean up the rubbish.

派出了船只清理垃圾。

词汇send out 派出；clean up 清理 语法被动语态 were sent out。

Finally, fishing was banned in the Yangtze River for 10 years from 2020.

最终，从 2020 年起长江禁渔 10 年。

词汇ban 禁止 语法被动语态 was banned；for 10 years 表示时长。

Mr Chen and other fishermen were given new jobs as guardians to protect the river.

陈先生和其他渔夫获得了守护长江的新工作。

词汇be given 被给予 语法被动语态；give sb. sth. 的被动形式。

At first, Mr Chen was not used to working on the riverbank.

起初，陈先生不习惯在江边工作。

词汇be used to doing sth. 习惯于做某事；riverbank 河岸 语法be used to doing sth. 表示“习惯于做某事”。

Now he feels happy because he has a good job and a steady income.

现在他感到开心，因为他有一份好工作和稳定的收入。

词汇steady 稳定的；income 收入 语法because 引导原因状语从句。

He is also glad to see that the Yangtze River has returned to what it used to be like when he was a child, with a lot of fish swimming in the clear water and beautiful birds enjoying their life on the bank.

他也很高兴看到长江恢复到他小时候的样子：清澈的江水里有许多鱼游动，美丽的鸟儿在岸边生活。

词汇return to 恢复到；used to be like 过去的样子 语法现在完成时 has returned；what 引导宾语从句；with 短语作伴随。

He is proud of his job.

他为自己的工作感到自豪。

词汇be proud of 为……感到自豪 语法be proud of sth.。

"Look there! That's my wife," Mr Chen said to the reporter.

“看那边！那是我妻子，”陈先生对记者说。

词汇reporter 记者 语法直接引语。

"She has joined a river clean-up team, and we work together to protect our mother river.

“她加入了一支河流清理队，我们一起努力保护母亲河。

词汇clean-up 清理；mother river 母亲河 语法现在完成时 has joined；and 连接并列分句。

We must protect it for our future generations."

我们必须为子孙后代保护它。”

词汇future generations 子孙后代 语法直接引语结尾；must + 动词原形。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P36–P37 两页 Section B 1b 的文章《The Yangtze River and Its Guardians》（长江及其守护者）。文章介绍：每天早上，53 岁的陈明沿着长江走 15 公里，一边和水边野餐的人交谈、看鸟儿在清澈江水中捕鱼；人们也许以为他在锻炼，其实他在工作——他是一名“长江守护者”，负责沿江巡查，确保无人捕鱼或污染。陈明以前不是守护者：他在江边长大，过去和父亲一样是渔夫。那时部分江段又臭又黄，因为工厂和家庭的废物，加上渔夫过度捕捞，江中鱼类和植物数量迅速减少。政府及时采取行动：新建废物处理中心净化污水、立法阻止在江边新建工厂、派船清理垃圾，并从 2020 年起长江禁渔 10 年；陈明和其他渔夫因此成为守护长江的新工作人员。起初他不习惯江边的工作，现在他因有好工作和稳定收入而开心，也很高兴看到长江恢复到他小时候的样子；他为自己的工作自豪，并向记者介绍妻子也加入了河流清理队，两人一起保护母亲河，为子孙后代守护长江。

English Summary

This is the article "The Yangtze River and Its Guardians" in Section B 1b, on pages 36–37. Every morning, 53-year-old Chen Ming walks 15 kilometres along the Yangtze River; as he does so, he often stops to talk to people picnicking beside the water or to watch birds catch fish in the river's clear water. While one might think he is just out for exercise, he is actually working — Mr Chen is a "Guardian of the River", and his job is to walk along the river and make sure that no one is fishing in it or polluting it. Mr Chen was not a guardian before: he grew up on the banks of the Yangtze River and used to be a fisherman like his father. Back then, parts of the river were smelly and yellow because of the waste from factories and homes, and fishermen were catching too many fish, so the number of fish and plants in the river was dropping quickly. The government took action before it was too late: new waste centres were built to clean wastewater, laws were made to stop new factories from being built near the river, boats were sent out to clean up the rubbish, and fishing was banned in the Yangtze River for 10 years from 2020; Mr Chen and other fishermen were given new jobs as guardians to protect the river. At first Mr Chen was not used to working on the riverbank, but now he feels happy because he has a good job and a steady income, and he is glad to see that the Yangtze River has returned to what it used to be like when he was a child. He is proud of his job and tells the reporter that his wife has joined a river clean-up team and that they work together to protect their mother river and future generations.

Section B · 3a · A Post About the Beach

We are deeply worried about the poor condition of our city's beach.

The Problem with Plastic

塑料的问题

词汇title 标题 语法文章标题。

Plastic is one of the most useful materials in the world.

塑料是世界上最有用的材料之一。

词汇useful 有用的；material 材料 语法one of + 形容词最高级 + 复数名词。

It provides many benefits to our lives because it is cheap and convenient.

它为我们的生活提供许多好处，因为它便宜又方便。

词汇provide 提供；benefit 好处；convenient 方便的 语法because 引导原因状语从句。

For example, two or three hundred years ago, clothes were quite expensive, so many people only had one or two sets of clothes to wear.

例如，两三百年前，衣服相当昂贵，所以许多人只有一两套衣服可穿。

词汇for example 例如；expensive 昂贵的；wear 穿 语法for example 引出例子；so 表示结果。

Now, with plastics like nylon and polyester, clothes have become much cheaper.

如今，有了尼龙和聚酯等塑料，衣服变得便宜多了。

词汇plastic 塑料；cheaper 更便宜的 语法with 表示“有了”；现在完成时 have become。

Plastic can also be used to replace other things that are rare or valuable.

塑料还可以用来替代稀有或贵重的东西。

词汇replace 替代；rare 稀有的；valuable 贵重的 语法被动语态 can be used；that 引导定语从句。

例如，曾经用象牙制作的钢琴琴键，现在通常用塑料制作。

词汇piano key 琴键；ivory 象牙 语法which 引导非限制性定语从句；be made from 表示“由……制成”。

This saved the lives of many elephants.

这挽救了许多大象的生命。

词汇save 拯救 语法一般过去时。

However, while plastic has many benefits, it also causes lots of problems.

然而，虽然塑料有许多好处，它也造成许多问题。

词汇benefit 好处；cause 引起 语法while 表示“虽然”。

Some plastic is so strong that it takes more than 400 years to break down before returning safely to the environment.

有些塑料非常坚固，需要 400 多年才能分解，安全回归环境。

When plastic is put in a landfill, the harmful materials inside can get into the soil and water underground.

词汇burnt 燃烧；harmful gas 有害气体 语法fill ... with ... 表示“使……充满……”。

Another problem is that plastic rubbish is often thrown on the ground.

另一个问题是塑料垃圾常被扔在地上。

Rain washes that rubbish into the rivers.

雨水把垃圾冲进河里。

从那里，它被冲到海里。

词汇sweep out 冲走；sea 海洋 语法被动语态 is swept out。

Sea animals can get caught in it or hurt by it.

海洋动物可能被它困住或伤害。

词汇get caught 被困住；hurt 伤害 语法被动结构 get done。

Worse yet, sometimes fish eat the plastic as food, and it gets into their bodies.

What can we do about this problem?
