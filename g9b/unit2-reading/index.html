Section A · 3a · A Visit to the Museum

Section A · 3a 主题：博物馆参观 · 对话（教材 P14）

Guide: Follow me, please.

请跟我来。

词汇follow 跟随 语法祈使句，表示请求或指示。

Guide: Does anybody know what this is?

有谁知道这是什么吗？

词汇anybody 任何人 语法宾语从句 what this is 作 know 的宾语。

Rose: That could be a mummy?

那可能是一具木乃伊？

词汇could be 可能是；mummy 木乃伊 语法could be 表示推测。

Guide: Well, it's similar to a mummy.

嗯，它和木乃伊相似。

词汇be similar to 与……相似 语法be similar to 是固定搭配。

Guide: It is actually called Xin Zhui, also known as Lady Dai.

它实际上叫辛追，又称辛追夫人。

词汇be called 被称为；be known as 以……著称/被称为 语法被动语态；also known as... 作同位语。

Guide: She died in 163 BCE.

她死于公元前 163 年。

词汇BCE 公元前 语法一般过去时；BCE 表示“公元前”。

Guide: In 1972, archaeologists found her body in almost perfect condition.

1972 年，考古学家发现她的遗体几乎完好无损。

词汇archaeologist 考古学家；in perfect condition 完好无损 语法时间状语前置；in ... condition 作状语。

Guide: She still had her own hair and a soft body.

她仍然保留着自己的头发和柔软的肌肤。

词汇still 仍然；soft 柔软的 语法still 表示“仍然”；and 连接并列宾语。

Adam: Wow!

哇！

词汇wow 哇 语法感叹词，表示惊讶。

Adam: How did the ancient people keep her body in such good condition?

古人是怎么让她的遗体保存得这么好的？

词汇ancient 古代的；keep ... in good condition 使……保持良好状态 语法特殊疑问句；keep + 宾语 + 介词短语作宾补。

Guide: That's still a mystery.

那仍然是个谜。

词汇mystery 谜 语法That's = That is；still 表仍然。

Guide: But it's believed she must have lived a wealthy life.

但据信她一定过着富足的生活。

词汇be believed 据信；wealthy 富有的 语法It's believed (that)...；must have done 表示对过去的肯定推测。

Guide: Archaeologists found her body along with thousands of ancient items.

考古学家发现她的遗体时，还发现了成千上万件古代物品。

词汇along with 连同；thousands of 成千上万的；item 物品 语法along with 引出伴随成分；thousands of 表示数量。

Guide: Look at this piece of clothing.

看看这件衣服。

词汇piece of clothing 一件衣服 语法祈使句；a piece of 表示“一件”。

Guide: Can you guess what it might be made of?

你能猜出它可能是用什么做的吗？

词汇guess 猜；be made of 由……制成 语法宾语从句 what it might be made of；might be made of 表推测的被动语态。

Adam: It looks so thin.

它看起来很薄。

词汇thin 薄的 语法look 作系动词，后接形容词。

Adam: It can't be very heavy.

它不可能很重。

词汇can't be 不可能是 语法can't be 表示否定推测。

Adam: Could it be made of cotton or silk?

它可能是棉或丝做的吗？

词汇cotton 棉；silk 丝 语法Could it be made of...? 表示推测的疑问句。

Guide: It is made of silk.

它是蚕丝做的。

词汇be made of 由……制成 语法被动语态 be made of。

Guide: It's only about 49 grams, or 0.1 pounds.

它只有大约 49 克，也就是 0.1 磅。

词汇gram 克；pound 磅 语法数字表达；or 表示“即/也就是”。

Guide: And it can be folded into a small matchbox.

而且它可以折叠进一个小火柴盒里。

词汇fold 折叠；matchbox 火柴盒 语法情态动词被动语态 can be folded。

Rose: Amazing!

太惊人了！

词汇amazing 令人惊叹的 语法感叹句。

Rose: Ancient Chinese people must have been really good at making silk clothing.

古代中国人一定非常擅长制作丝绸衣服。

词汇be good at 擅长；silk clothing 丝绸衣服 语法must have been 表示对过去的肯定推测；be good at doing sth.。

Guide: That's correct!

没错！

词汇correct 正确的 语法口语中表示肯定与赞同。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在博物馆里。讲解员带着参观者参观：她先请大家跟着她，问有没有人知道眼前的展品是什么。Rose 猜是木乃伊，讲解员解释说它和木乃伊相似，实际上叫辛追，又称辛追夫人，死于公元前 163 年；1972 年考古学家发现她的遗体时几乎完好无损，头发和肌肤仍在。Adam 好奇古人如何保存遗体，讲解员说这仍是个谜，但据信辛追生前生活富足，遗体旁还出土了成千上万件古代物品。讲解员又让大家看一件薄薄的衣服，Adam 猜是棉或丝做的；讲解员说它是丝制，仅约 49 克（0.1 磅），可以折叠进小火柴盒，Rose 惊叹古代中国人一定非常擅长制作丝绸衣服。

English Summary

This conversation takes place in a museum. The guide asks the visitors to follow her and asks if anyone knows what the exhibit is. Rose guesses it is a mummy; the guide explains that it is similar to a mummy and is actually called Xin Zhui, also known as Lady Dai, who died in 163 BCE. When archaeologists found her body in 1972, it was in almost perfect condition, with her own hair and a soft body. Adam wonders how the ancient people kept her body in such good condition; the guide says it is still a mystery but that she must have lived a wealthy life, and her body was found along with thousands of ancient items. The guide then shows them a thin piece of clothing; Adam guesses it might be made of cotton or silk, and the guide says it is made of silk, weighs only about 49 grams (0.1 pounds) and can be folded into a small matchbox. Rose is amazed, saying ancient Chinese people must have been really good at making silk clothing, and the guide agrees.

Section B 1b · A Discovery That Shocked the World

Section B 1b 主题：三星堆考古发现 · 说明文（教材 P16–P17 两页合并）

Of the many archaeological sites and museums throughout China, the Sanxingdui Museum in Guanghan, Sichuan Province, attracts millions of visitors around the world every year.

在中国众多考古遗址和博物馆中，位于四川省广汉的三星堆博物馆每年吸引着世界各地数百万游客。

词汇archaeological site 考古遗址；throughout 遍及；province 省；attract 吸引 语法of 短语作状语；一般现在时；millions of。

Once visitors walk through the gates of the museum, they will be surprised by the amazing collection of cultural artefacts from the Bronze Age.

游客一走进博物馆大门，就会惊叹于这里收藏的青铜时代文化瑰宝。

词汇once 一旦；be surprised by 对……感到惊叹；artefact 文物；Bronze Age 青铜时代 语法once 引导时间状语从句；will be surprised by 是一般将来时被动结构。

The ancient people of Sanxingdui lived by the Yangtze River more than 3,000 years ago.

三星堆古人三千多年前生活在长江边。

词汇ancient 古代的；Yangtze River 长江 语法一般过去时；by the river 表示“在河边”。

However, people were not aware of them until the 20th century when Sanxingdui was discovered.

然而，直到 20 世纪三星堆被发现，人们才注意到他们。

词汇be aware of 意识到；century 世纪；discover 发现 语法not...until 句型；when 引导定语从句；was discovered 是被动语态。

Thousands of gold, bronze, jade, and ivory objects were found underground.

成千上万件金、铜、玉和象牙器物在地下被发现。

词汇thousands of 成千上万的；bronze 青铜；jade 玉；ivory 象牙 语法一般过去时被动语态 were found。

The first big discoveries were in two pits in 1986.

1986 年，两个祭祀坑带来了最初的重大发现。

词汇pit 坑；discovery 发现 语法系表结构；in 1986 表时间。

More than 1,000 artefacts were found and many were repaired, including the world-famous bronze tree, the huge mask, the bronze man, and the gold stick.

一千多件文物被发现，许多得到修复，包括世界闻名的青铜神树、大面具、青铜立人和金杖。

词汇repair 修复；world-famous 世界闻名的；mask 面具 语法and 连接并列的被动结构；including 是介词，后接名词短语。

More recently, in 2019, six more pits were found and dug in the area.

更近一些，2019 年，该地区又发现并发掘了六个祭祀坑。

词汇dig 发掘（过去式 dug） 语法一般过去时被动语态 were found and dug。

More amazing artefacts have been found in them, including some 3,000-year-old gold masks and bronze zun.

其中又发现了更多令人惊叹的文物，包括一些有三千年历史的金面具和青铜尊。

词汇have been found 已被发现；3,000-year-old 有 3000 年历史的；zun 尊 语法现在完成时被动语态；复合形容词作定语。

By using modern technologies, researchers even found some remains of silk items.

借助现代技术，研究人员甚至发现了一些丝绸残迹。

词汇by using 通过使用；technology 技术；remain 遗迹；残留物 语法by + 动名词作方式状语。

All the artefacts show that people from that era must have been highly skilled in metalwork as well as carving.

所有文物都表明，那个时代的人们一定非常精通金属加工和雕刻。

词汇era 时代；be skilled in 精通；metalwork 金属加工；carving 雕刻；as well as 以及 语法that 引导宾语从句；must have been 表示对过去的肯定推测。

They might also have developed the skill of making silk.

他们可能还掌握了制作丝绸的技艺。

词汇might have done 可能已经做过；develop the skill 掌握技艺 语法might have done 表示对过去的推测。

The discovery of Sanxingdui shocked the world, and it has been called one of the world's greatest archaeological finds of the 20th century.

三星堆的发现震惊了世界，它被称为 20 世纪世界上最伟大的考古发现之一。

词汇shock 使震惊；archaeological find 考古发现；century 世纪 语法and 连接并列分句；现在完成时被动语态 have been called；one of + 最高级 + 复数名词。

It was especially mysterious as there was no writing found with it, and the art style seemed very different and unique.

它尤其神秘，因为其中没有发现文字，而且艺术风格也显得与众不同、独一无二。

词汇mysterious 神秘的；writing 文字；unique 独特的 语法as 引导原因状语从句；there was no writing found 是 there be 结构的被动含义。

Historians think Sanxingdui gives important evidence for the study of the development of Chinese civilization.

历史学家认为，三星堆为研究中华文明的发展提供了重要证据。

词汇historian 历史学家；evidence 证据；civilization 文明 语法think 后接宾语从句；give evidence for。

For discoveries like Sanxingdui, it is never enough just to find the artefacts and their history.

对于三星堆这样的发现，仅仅找到文物和它们的历史是远远不够的。

词汇discovery 发现；never enough 永远不够 语法it is + adj. + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语。

The Sanxingdui Museum has held exhibitions around the world.

三星堆博物馆已在世界各地举办展览。

词汇hold exhibitions 举办展览 语法现在完成时 has held。

Visitors have all been amazed by these treasures.

参观者都被这些珍宝所震撼。

词汇treasure 珍宝；be amazed by 被……震撼 语法现在完成时被动结构。

And now with digital technology, the Sanxingdui Museum is looking for more ways to bring the past back to life.

如今借助数字技术，三星堆博物馆正在寻找更多让过去重现生机的方式。

词汇digital technology 数字技术；look for 寻找；bring...back to life 使……重现生机 语法with 短语作状语；look for；不定式 to bring 作定语。

One of its aims is to show more people these mysteries and to make young people more curious about history, the world, and more importantly, our human story.

它的目标之一是向更多人展示这些奥秘，并让年轻人对历史、世界——更重要的是对人类自己的故事——更加好奇。

词汇aim 目标；curious 好奇的；human story 人类的故事 语法one of... 作主语，谓语用 is；不定式作表语；make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇跨 P16–P17 两页的文章介绍震惊世界的三星堆考古发现。在中国众多考古遗址和博物馆中，四川广汉的三星堆博物馆每年吸引世界各地数百万游客。走进博物馆，人们会惊叹于青铜时代的文化瑰宝：三星堆古人三千多年前生活在长江边，直到 20 世纪三星堆被发现，人们才注意到他们，地下出土了成千上万件金、铜、玉、象牙器物。1986 年两个祭祀坑带来最初的重大发现，出土一千多件文物，包括青铜神树、大面具、青铜立人和金杖；2019 年又发现并发掘六个祭祀坑，出土金面具、青铜尊等，研究者还借助现代技术发现了丝绸残迹，说明当时的人精通金属加工、雕刻，可能还会制丝。三星堆的发现震惊世界，被称为 20 世纪最伟大的考古发现之一；它尤其神秘，因为没有发现文字，艺术风格也独一无二，历史学家认为它为研究中华文明发展提供了重要证据。P17 最后一段指出：对三星堆这样的发现来说，只找到文物和历史远远不够；三星堆博物馆已在世界各地办展，如今借助数字技术寻找更多让过去重现生机的方式，目标之一就是让更多人了解这些奥秘，让年轻人对历史、世界和人类自己的故事更感兴趣。

English Summary

This article, which runs across pages 16–17, introduces the Sanxingdui discovery that shocked the world. Among the many archaeological sites and museums in China, the Sanxingdui Museum in Guanghan, Sichuan Province, attracts millions of visitors from around the world every year. Once visitors walk through its gates, they are surprised by the amazing collection of cultural artefacts from the Bronze Age. The ancient people of Sanxingdui lived by the Yangtze River more than 3,000 years ago, but people were not aware of them until the 20th century, when thousands of gold, bronze, jade and ivory objects were found underground. The first big discoveries came from two pits in 1986, with more than 1,000 artefacts found and many repaired, including the world-famous bronze tree, the huge mask, the bronze man and the gold stick. In 2019 six more pits were found and dug, yielding 3,000-year-old gold masks and bronze zun, and modern technologies even revealed remains of silk items — showing that people of that era must have been highly skilled in metalwork and carving and might also have developed the skill of making silk. The discovery has been called one of the world's greatest archaeological finds of the 20th century; it is especially mysterious because no writing was found with it and its art style seems very different and unique, and historians think it gives important evidence for the study of the development of Chinese civilization. The final paragraph notes that finding the artefacts and their history is never enough: the museum has held exhibitions around the world, and with digital technology it is looking for more ways to bring the past back to life, aiming to show more people these mysteries and make young people more curious about history, the world and, more importantly, our human story.

English Summary

This presentation script is for a history exhibition at school. The speaker greets the audience and says she would like to present a historical site from the Mayan civilization. She notes that people may have heard about the Egyptian pyramids, but the Mayan people also built hundreds of pyramids throughout Central America from about 1,000 BCE to 1,500 CE, and here we can see the most famous example — the Pyramid of Kukulkan. For the Mayans, Kukulkan was a snake god. Twice a year, when day and night are in balance, the setting sun creates a strange shadow on one side of the pyramid, in the shape of a snake crawling down the steps. Interestingly, the building has a total of 365 steps, which the Mayans could have built to show the number of days in the solar year. The speaker adds that inside there are two more pyramids, and the Mayans must have built each pyramid on top of the other over a long period of time. She concludes that from the Pyramid of Kukulkan we can learn a lot about the ancient Mayan civilization, and thanks the audience.

中文大意

这篇跨 P53–P54 两页的文章围绕人类对未知历史之谜的好奇展开。文章先举两个著名例子——大西洋海底的失落之城、中东古城中悬在空中的花园，指出数千年来人们一直着迷于历史之谜；原因在于人类天生好奇、喜欢发挥想象，即使阅读也爱读那些现实中无法造访的地方和时代，还喜欢为尚未完全理解的事情寻找解释。接着，文章探讨“失落的亚特兰蒂斯城是真的吗”：约公元前 360 年，古希腊作家柏拉图讲述了亚特兰蒂斯的故事——大西洋中的一座岛屿，创建者半神半人，富有强大，后来因贪婪邪恶被众神用一夜火焰与地震沉入海底。尽管很可能只是传说，后来的欧洲作家却相信它可能为真；考古学家们为确定它的真实位置争论不休，有人认为在地中海、西班牙海岸附近，甚至南极洲，但这座岛是否真实存在，我们也许永远无法知道。文章最后探讨“巴比伦空中花园在哪里”：它是古代世界七大奇迹之一，位于巴比伦城（今伊拉克巴格达以南）；与其它奇迹不同，今天科学家争论这些花园是否真的存在过。牛津大学一位研究者声称确有花园，但可能在完全不同的城市尼尼微（今伊拉克北部摩苏尔附近）；不幸的是两处遗址都毁于战火，考古学家也许很难解开这个谜。

English Summary

This article, which runs across pages 53–54, explores people's curiosity about historical mysteries. It begins with two famous examples — a lost city under the sea in the Atlantic Ocean and gardens up in the air in an ancient Middle Eastern city — and explains that people have been fascinated by such mysteries for thousands of years because humans are naturally curious and like to use their imagination; even when reading, people love to read about places and times they might not be able to visit in real life, and they like to find explanations for things they do not fully understand. The article then asks whether the lost city of Atlantis is real: around 360 BCE the ancient Greek writer Plato told the story of Atlantis, an island in the Atlantic Ocean whose founders were half god and half human; they became very rich and powerful, but when they became too greedy and wicked, the gods made a terrible night of fire and earthquakes and the island sank into the sea. Although it is probably just a legend, later European writers believed it could be true, and archaeologists have argued about its actual location — some thought the Mediterranean Sea, others off the coast of Spain, and yet another idea was Antarctica — but we may never know whether the island was real. Finally, the article asks where the Hanging Gardens of Babylon were: one of the seven wonders of the ancient world, they were in the ancient city of Babylon, south of today's Baghdad; unlike the other wonders, scientists today argue about whether the gardens ever existed at all. A researcher from Oxford University claimed such gardens existed but could have been in a completely different city, Nineveh, near modern Mosul in northern Iraq, yet both sites suffered damage from wars, so it may be difficult for archaeologists ever to solve this mystery.

语法点 Grammar

A lost city under the sea in the Atlantic Ocean.

名词短语，省略谓语，起列举作用。

These are just two famous examples of historical mysteries that have fascinated people for thousands of years.

that 引导定语从句；现在完成时。

Why are people so interested in such things?

特殊疑问句；be interested in。

Even when reading, many people love to read about places and times that might not be possible to visit in real life.

even when 引导时间状语；that 引导定语从句。

Also, we like to find explanations for the things we do not fully understand.

we do not fully understand 是省略关系代词的定语从句。

Sometimes we may just want answers to the simple questions of where and why.

may 表推测；of where and why 作后置定语。

Around 360 BCE, the ancient Greek writer, Plato, told the story of Atlantis, an island in the Atlantic Ocean.

同位语；一般过去时。

The founders of the island were half god and half human, and they became very rich and powerful.

and 连接并列分句。

However, when they became too greedy and wicked, the gods made a terrible night of fire and earthquakes, and the island sank into the sea.

when 引导时间状语从句；make + 名词短语；sank 是 sink 的过去式。

Although it is probably just a legend, later European writers believed it could be true.

although 引导让步状语从句；believed 后接宾语从句。

There were many arguments as archaeologists tried to identify the actual location of the island...

as 引导时间状语从句；直接引语。

Some thought it could be in the Mediterranean Sea, while others said it might be off the coast of Spain.

some...while others... 表对比。

However, unlike the other wonders, scientists today argue about whether these gardens ever existed at all.

unlike 引导对比状语；whether 引导宾语从句。

Unfortunately, both sites suffered damage from wars, so it may be difficult for archaeologists ever to solve this mystery.

so 表结果；it may be difficult for sb. to do sth. 句型。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Even when reading, many people love to read about places and times that might not be possible to visit in real life.

即使在阅读时，许多人也爱读那些在现实生活中可能无法造访的地方和时代。

even when 引导时间状语；that 引导定语从句，修饰 places and times。

However, when they became too greedy and wicked, the gods made a terrible night of fire and earthquakes, and the island sank into the sea.

然而，当他们变得过于贪婪邪恶时，众神制造了一个充满火焰与地震的可怕夜晚，岛屿沉入海中。

when 引导时间状语从句；make + 名词短语；sank 是 sink 的过去式。

Unfortunately, both sites suffered damage from wars, so it may be difficult for archaeologists ever to solve this mystery.

不幸的是，两处遗址都在战争中遭到破坏，因此考古学家也许很难解开这个谜。

so 表结果；it may be difficult for sb. to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 教材 P53–P54 是 Reading Plus 板块，文章 Into the Unknown 跨两页，本页合并为完整一篇。
  • 读前看两个小标题，猜一猜文章会讲哪些历史之谜。

读中

  • 找出人类对历史之谜感兴趣的原因（curious、imagination、explanations）。
  • 分别记录亚特兰蒂斯和空中花园的关键信息：时间、地点、争议。

读后

  • 用表格对比两个历史之谜的已知信息与未解之处。
  • 讨论：你还知道哪些历史之谜？科学家应如何研究它们？
