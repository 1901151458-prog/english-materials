Section A · 3a · A Great Trip to the Louvre

Section A · 3a 主题：美术馆之旅 · 艺术欣赏

Joe: Wow, that was a great trip!

哇，那真是一次很棒的旅行！

词汇trip 旅行；great 很棒的 语法一般过去时 that was 对过去的事情作评价；Wow 表示感叹。

Joe: What did you enjoy most during our trip to the Louvre?

这次卢浮宫之行你最喜欢什么？

词汇enjoy 喜欢；most 最；the Louvre 卢浮宫 语法What did you enjoy most? 询问“最喜欢什么”；during 表示“在……期间”。

Betty: I loved the pyramid in front of the museum!

我喜欢博物馆前面的金字塔！

词汇pyramid 金字塔；in front of 在……前面 语法一般过去时 loved；in front of 表方位。

Dave: Yes, the Louvre Pyramid by I.M. Pei!

对，贝聿铭设计的卢浮宫金字塔！

词汇by 由……（设计）；I.M. Pei 贝聿铭 语法by 表示“由……设计/创作”；省略句。

Dave: It's new and modern but fits perfectly with the old classical buildings around it.

它既新又现代，却与周围古老的古典建筑完美契合。

词汇modern 现代的；fit with 与……相配；classical 古典的 语法but 表示转折；fit with 表示“与……相配、契合”；around it 是后置定语。

Dave: I just love how the light makes the glass shine!

我特别喜欢光线让玻璃闪闪发光的样子！

词汇light 光线；glass 玻璃；shine 闪耀 语法how 引导宾语从句；make sth. do sth. 表示“使某物做某事”。

Betty: Then there was the Venus de Milo.

然后还有《米洛的维纳斯》。

词汇then 然后；the Venus de Milo 米洛的维纳斯（雕像名） 语法There be 句型；专有名词作主语。

Betty: She's such a beauty!

她真是个大美人！

词汇such 如此；beauty 美人；美的事物 语法such + a/an + 名词；She 把雕像拟人化。

Dave: Yeah!

是啊！

词汇yeah 是的（口语） 语法口语应答，表示赞同。

Dave: The ancient sculptor really understood the human body.

古代雕塑家真正懂得人体。

词汇ancient 古代的；sculptor 雕塑家；human body 人体 语法一般过去时 understood；the human body 表示“人体”。

Joe: What about the Mona Lisa?

那《蒙娜丽莎》呢？

词汇the Mona Lisa 蒙娜丽莎（画名） 语法What about ...? 用于询问“……怎么样”。

Betty: I really like that painting.

我真的很喜欢那幅画。

词汇painting 绘画；画作 语法一般现在时表示喜好。

Betty: She looks alive!

她看起来栩栩如生！

词汇alive 活着的；栩栩如生的 语法look 是系动词，后接形容词作表语；She 指画中人物。

Betty: Her eyes seem so gentle, and her skin seems so soft.

她的眼神看起来那么温柔，她的皮肤看起来那么柔软。

词汇gentle 温柔的；skin 皮肤；soft 柔软的 语法seem + 形容词表示“看起来……”；and 连接两个并列分句。

Joe: It took years for Dr. Vinci to finish the Mona Lisa.

达·芬奇花了多年时间才完成《蒙娜丽莎》。

词汇take years 花费多年；finish 完成 语法It takes sb. time to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语。

Joe: He put his heart and soul into it.

他把全部心血都倾注在这幅画上。

词汇put ... into 把……投入到；heart and soul 心血；全心全意 语法put one's heart and soul into sth. 表示“全身心投入某事”。

Betty: That must be why the painting looks like it could talk to us.

难怪这幅画看起来像能和我们说话。

词汇must be 一定是；that's why ……的原因 语法must be 表示肯定推测；why 引导表语从句；look like 后接从句。

Dave: And of course, there's her smile!

当然，还有她的微笑！

词汇of course 当然；smile 微笑 语法There be 句型；her smile 指画中人的微笑。

Dave: I'd really love to know why she was smiling!

我真想知道她为什么微笑！

词汇would love to do sth. 很想做某事 语法I'd = I would；why 引导宾语从句。

Joe: Nobody knows for sure.

没人能确切知道。

词汇nobody 没有人；for sure 确切地 语法不定代词 nobody 作主语，谓语用第三人称单数。

Joe: But it's the mystery that makes the art so beautiful!

但正是这种神秘感让艺术如此美丽！

词汇mystery 神秘；mysterious 神秘的；art 艺术 语法It is ... that ... 强调句型；make + 宾语 + 形容词。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

Joe、Betty 和 Dave 在参观卢浮宫后聊起各自最喜欢的展品。Betty 喜欢博物馆前的玻璃金字塔；Dave 说这是贝聿铭设计的卢浮宫金字塔，既新又现代，却与周围古老的古典建筑完美契合，他很喜欢光线让玻璃闪耀的样子。Betty 还提到《米洛的维纳斯》，赞叹她如此美丽；Dave 说古代雕塑家真正懂得人体。谈到《蒙娜丽莎》，Betty 觉得她栩栩如生、眼神温柔、肌肤柔软；Joe 说达·芬奇花了多年才完成这幅画，倾注了全部心血。Dave 想知道她为什么微笑，Joe 说没人确切知道，但正是这种神秘感让艺术如此美丽。

English Summary

After visiting the Louvre, Joe, Betty and Dave talk about their favourite works. Betty loved the glass pyramid in front of the museum, and Dave explains that the Louvre Pyramid by I.M. Pei is new and modern yet fits perfectly with the old classical buildings around it; he loves how the light makes the glass shine. Betty also mentions the Venus de Milo and calls her such a beauty, and Dave says the ancient sculptor really understood the human body. Talking about the Mona Lisa, Betty thinks she looks alive, with gentle eyes and soft skin; Joe says it took Dr. Vinci years to finish the painting and that he put his heart and soul into it. Betty thinks that is why the painting looks as if it could talk to us. Dave would love to know why she was smiling, and Joe says nobody knows for sure—but it is the mystery that makes the art so beautiful.

Section B 1b · Experiencing Chinese Calligraphy

Section B 1b 主题：中国书法 · 文化体验（P26–P27 两页合并）

Experiencing Chinese Calligraphy

体验中国书法

词汇experience 体验；calligraphy 书法 语法标题：动名词短语，点明文章主题。

Last weekend, a friend and I went to visit an art museum.

上周末，我和一位朋友去参观了一家美术馆。

词汇last weekend 上周末；art museum 美术馆 语法一般过去时；a friend and I 是并列主语。

We found many works of traditional Chinese calligraphy on show.

我们看到许多中国传统书法作品正在展出。

词汇works 作品；traditional 传统的；on show 展出 语法on show 作后置定语，表示“正在展出”。

It was my first time to visit such an exhibition, and I was really excited.

那是我第一次参观这样的展览，我非常兴奋。

词汇first time 第一次；exhibition 展览；excited 兴奋的 语法It was one's first time to do sth. 句型；and 连接并列分句。

Although I don't know much about Chinese calligraphy, I can see it everywhere in China.

虽然我对中国书法了解不多，但在中国到处都能看到它。

词汇although 虽然；everywhere 到处 语法although 引导让步状语从句。

It is in advertisements, on posters, and on buildings.

它出现在广告里、海报上和建筑上。

词汇advertisement 广告；poster 海报 语法三个地点状语并列，介词 in / on 搭配不同。

Many homes have it on their walls or front doors.

许多家庭把它挂墙上或贴在门上。

词汇wall 墙；front door 前门 语法have sth. on ... 表示“把某物放在……上”。

You may even come across people writing calligraphy with water and a big brush on the ground in a park.

你甚至可能在公园里遇到人们用清水和大毛笔在地上写书法。

词汇come across 偶遇；brush 毛笔；on the ground 在地上 语法come across sb. doing sth. 表示“偶然遇到某人正在做某事”。

I came to learn that calligraphy is viewed by Chinese people as one of the highest forms of art.

我逐渐了解到，书法被中国人视为最高艺术形式之一。

词汇come to learn 逐渐了解到；be viewed as 被视为；form 形式 语法come to learn 表示“逐渐了解到”；be viewed as 是被动语态，意为“被视为”。

In the museum, my Chinese friend told me an interesting story about calligraphy.

在博物馆里，我的中国朋友给我讲了一个关于书法的有趣故事。

词汇tell sb. sth. 告诉某人某事；story 故事 语法一般过去时 told；tell sb. sth. 双宾语结构。

Wang Xizhi (303–361) was one of the best calligraphers in history.

王羲之（303—361）是历史上最优秀的书法家之一。

词汇calligrapher 书法家；in history 在历史上 语法one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。

He once attended a party of the Orchid Pavilion.

他曾参加兰亭的一次聚会。

词汇attend 参加；the Orchid Pavilion 兰亭 语法一般过去时；the Orchid Pavilion 是专有名词。

Every guest was expected to write a poem during the party.

每位客人都被要求在聚会上写一首诗。

词汇guest 客人；be expected to do sth. 被期望做某事；poem 诗 语法一般过去时被动语态 was expected to do sth.。

At the end of the party, Wang wrote the introduction to the poems.

聚会结束时，王羲之为这些诗写了序言。

词汇at the end of 在……结束时；introduction 序言 语法at the end of 作时间状语；introduction to ... 表示“……的序言”。

The next day, Wang looked at his calligraphy—the Lanting Xu (Introduction to the poems written at the Orchid Pavilion)—and realized that it was his best calligraphy ever.

第二天，王羲之看着自己的书法——《兰亭集序》（为兰亭集会所作诗篇写的序言）——意识到这是他最好的书法作品。

词汇the Lanting Xu 《兰亭集序》；realize 意识到 语法破折号插入同位语；that 引导宾语从句；written at the Orchid Pavilion 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

He then tried to recreate the exact style of calligraphy, but he found it impossible to do so.

然后他试图重现那种书法风格，但他发现不可能做到。

词汇recreate 重现；exact 确切的；impossible 不可能的 语法find it + 形容词 + to do sth. 中 it 是形式宾语，真正宾语是 to do so。

When he was at the party, he was very happy and relaxed.

参加聚会时，他非常快乐、放松。

词汇relaxed 放松的 语法when 引导时间状语从句；两个形容词并列作表语。

This made his calligraphy especially lively.

这使他的书法格外生动。

词汇especially 尤其；lively 生动的 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词表示“使……怎样”。

People later said that the calligraphy in the Lanting Xu looked like a Chinese dragon at play.

后来人们说，《兰亭集序》里的书法就像一条嬉戏的中国龙。

词汇look like 看起来像；dragon 龙；at play 在玩耍 语法that 引导宾语从句；look like 表示“看起来像”。

It is a pity that the original version of the Lanting Xu has been lost, but it was copied and studied by many calligraphers.

遗憾的是《兰亭集序》的原作已经失传，但它被许多书法家临摹、研究。

词汇pity 遗憾；original version 原作；be lost 失传；copy 临摹 语法It is a pity that ... 句型；现在完成时 has been lost；一般过去时被动语态 was copied and studied。

In fact, calligraphy students today still copy the Lanting Xu, along with other famous works in history.

事实上，今天的书法学生仍然临摹《兰亭集序》以及历史上其他名作。

词汇in fact 事实上；along with 与……一起 语法along with 连接并列宾语。

Doing so not only helps them to improve their own calligraphy, but also develops their artistic senses.

这样做不仅帮助他们提高自己的书法水平，还能培养他们的艺术感觉。

词汇do so 这样做；improve 提高；artistic senses 艺术感觉 语法动名词短语 Doing so 作主语；not only ... but also ... 连接两个并列谓语。

While I still don't understand enough about Chinese calligraphy, I began to see the beauty in the calligraphy apart from the meaning of the words.

虽然我对中国书法的了解还不够，但我开始看到书法超越字义的美。

词汇apart from 除了……之外；meaning 意义 语法while 引导让步状语从句；apart from 表示“除了……之外”。

As an art form, it is meant to inspire, to make us think, and to show our understanding of beauty.

作为一种艺术形式，它旨在启发我们、让我们思考，并展现我们对美的理解。

词汇art form 艺术形式；be meant to 旨在；inspire 启发 语法as 表示“作为”；be meant to do sth. 旨在做某事；三个不定式并列。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

（教材 P26–P27 两页合并）文章讲述作者参观中国书法展的经历。上周末作者和朋友参观美术馆，第一次看到如此多的中国传统书法作品，十分兴奋；虽然不太了解书法，但作者发现书法在中国随处可见：广告、海报、建筑、家里的墙上和门上，甚至在公园里有人用清水和大毛笔在地上写书法；作者逐渐明白书法被中国人视为最高艺术形式之一。在博物馆里，中国朋友讲了王羲之的故事：王羲之（303—361）是历史上最优秀的书法家之一，他在兰亭聚会上为诗作写序，第二天发现这就是自己最好的作品《兰亭集序》；他再想重现那种风格却做不到，因为当时他快乐放松，字写得格外生动，人们说《兰亭集序》的书法像嬉戏的中国龙。原作虽已失传，但被许多书法家临摹研究，今天的书法学生仍在临摹它，这既提高书法水平，也培养艺术感觉。最后作者说，虽然自己了解得还不够，但已开始看到书法超越字义的美；作为艺术形式，书法旨在启发人、让人思考、展现对美的理解。

English Summary

(Merged from pages 26–27.) The text describes the author's visit to a Chinese calligraphy exhibition. Last weekend the author and a friend visited an art museum and found many works of traditional Chinese calligraphy on show; although the author doesn't know much about calligraphy, they can see it everywhere in China—in advertisements, on posters and buildings, on walls and front doors of homes, and even people writing calligraphy with water and a big brush on the ground in a park. The author came to learn that calligraphy is viewed by Chinese people as one of the highest forms of art. In the museum, a Chinese friend told the story of Wang Xizhi (303–361), one of the best calligraphers in history, who at a party of the Orchid Pavilion wrote the introduction to the poems; the next day he realized it was his best calligraphy ever—the Lanting Xu—and could never recreate that exact style, because he had been happy and relaxed, which made his calligraphy especially lively; people later said the calligraphy looked like a Chinese dragon at play. Although the original has been lost, it was copied and studied by many calligraphers, and students today still copy it, which not only improves their own calligraphy but also develops their artistic senses. Finally, the author begins to see the beauty of calligraphy apart from the meaning of the words—as an art form it is meant to inspire, to make us think, and to show our understanding of beauty.

In 1875 he had a chance to visit Italy, found new inspiration in Michelangelo's works, and completed his breakthrough work—The Age of Bronze—that same year; it was so realistic that people thought he had just made a cast of a human body, and when he proved it was truly his work he became the talk of the town. While The Thinker is his most famous sculpture, his sculpture of Balzac is also influential. Balzac (1799–1850) was one of France's most beloved writers; after his death it was decided in 1891 to create a statue in his honour. Rodin was supposed to finish it in 18 months but took seven years, and at first the public rejected it because they did not think it looked like Balzac at all. Rodin replied that the statue was not supposed to look like Balzac's body but to show his character and spirit; he took it home and refused to show it again until the first bronze version was created and put on display in 1939. Today Balzac is celebrated as one of the first truly modern sculptures and one of the greatest ever made, because Rodin broke away from traditional Greek and Roman styles and created something entirely new, full of emotion and life—which is why Rodin is still celebrated and studied by artists today.

语法点 Grammar

One of the most famous modern sculptors is Auguste Rodin (1840–1917).

one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。

Rodin was born in Paris, and he trained to be a sculptor from an early age.

一般过去时；train to be 表示“训练成为”。

For many years, he had to make a living by decorating buildings.

have to do sth.；make a living by doing sth.。

Things changed for Rodin in 1875 when he had a chance to visit Italy.

when 引导时间状语从句；have a chance to do sth.。

His breakthrough work—The Age of Bronze—was completed that same year.

破折号插入作品名；一般过去时被动语态 was completed。

It was so realistic that people thought he had just made a cast of a human body.

so ... that ... 引导结果状语从句；过去完成时 had just made。

When Rodin proved that it was truly his work, he became the talk of the town.

when 引导时间状语从句；that 引导宾语从句。

While The Thinker is Rodin's most famous sculpture, his sculpture of Balzac is also influential.

while 引导让步状语从句；also 表示“也”。

After his death, it was decided in 1891 that a statue should be created in his honour.

it was decided that ... 句型；should be created 是情态动词被动语态。

Rodin was supposed to complete the statue in 18 months, but in the end it took seven years!

be supposed to do sth.；take + 时间。

To make things worse, at first it was rejected by the public because people did not think it looked like Balzac at all.

to make things worse 作插入语；一般过去时被动语态；because 引导原因状语从句。

It was not until 1939 that the first bronze version of the sculpture was created and put on display.

It was not until ... that ... 强调句型；was created 与 put 是并列被动结构。

This is why Rodin is still celebrated and studied by artists today.

why 引导表语从句；一般现在时被动语态。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It was so realistic that people thought he had just made a cast of a human body.

它如此逼真，以至于人们以为他刚刚做了一个人体翻模。

so ... that ... 引导结果状语从句；had just made 是过去完成时，表示在“认为”之前已发生。

Rodin's reply was that the statue was not supposed to look like Balzac's body—it was supposed to show Balzac's character and spirit.

罗丹的回答是：这座雕像本不应该看起来像巴尔扎克的身体——它应该展现巴尔扎克的性格和精神。

that 引导表语从句；be supposed to do sth. 表示“本应做某事”；破折号后补充说明。

It was not until 1939 that the first bronze version of the sculpture was created and put on display.

直到 1939 年，这座雕塑的第一件青铜版本才被制作出来并展出。

It was not until ... that ... 是强调句型；was created 和 (was) put 是并列的被动结构。
