Section A · 3a · Graduation Plans

Section A · 3a 主题：毕业去向 · 对话（教材 P44）

Ms Gao: Peter, Emma, and Ella, you're going back to your home countries soon, aren't you?

彼得、艾玛、艾拉，你们很快就要回国了，不是吗？

词汇home country 祖国；soon 很快 语法反意疑问句 aren't you；be going back to 表将来。

Peter: Yes, next month, in fact.

是的，事实上就在下个月。

词汇in fact 事实上 语法省略回答；in fact 补充说明。

Peter: I'm already starting to miss China.

我已经开始想念中国了。

词汇start to do sth. 开始做某事；miss 想念 语法现在进行时表示已经开始的状态。

Ms Gao: What did you enjoy the most about China?

你们最喜欢中国的什么？

词汇enjoy 喜欢 语法最高级 the most 作状语；一般过去时。

Emma: Chinese food, of course!

当然是中餐！

词汇of course 当然 语法省略句，口语应答。

Peter: I really enjoyed living here and learning about a different culture.

我真的很喜欢在这里生活、了解不同的文化。

词汇enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事；culture 文化 语法enjoy doing sth.；and 连接并列动名词。

Ms Gao: I'm glad to hear that.

很高兴听你这么说。

词汇be glad to do sth. 很高兴做某事 语法be glad to do sth. 表示乐于听到某事。

Ms Gao: So where do you see yourselves in the future?

那么，你们觉得自己未来会怎样？

词汇see oneself 设想自己；in the future 在未来 语法特殊疑问句；see oneself in the future 表示“设想自己的未来”。

Peter: I want to come back to China for university or work.

我想回中国上大学或工作。

词汇come back 回来；university 大学 语法want to do sth.；for 表示目的。

Ella: I plan to become a photographer or a reporter so I can meet different people and travel abroad!

我打算成为摄影师或记者，这样我就能结识不同的人、到国外旅行！

词汇plan to do sth. 计划做某事；photographer 摄影师；reporter 记者；abroad 在国外 语法plan to do sth.；so 引导目的状语从句。

Ms Gao: Wow, I hope all your dreams come true.

哇，希望你们所有梦想都能实现。

词汇come true 实现 语法hope 后接宾语从句；come true 是固定短语。

Ella & Peter: Thanks, Ms Gao.

谢谢您，高老师。

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法口语致谢。

Ms Gao: What about you, Emma?

你呢，艾玛？

词汇what about ... ……呢 语法What about...? 询问对方。

Emma: Hmm, no plans yet.

嗯，还没有计划。

词汇yet 还（用于否定） 语法省略句；yet 用于否定句末。

Emma: I'll decide after I graduate from high school.

我高中毕业后再决定。

词汇decide 决定；graduate from 从……毕业 语法after 引导时间状语从句。

Ms Gao: It's good to just focus on the present too!

只专注于当下也很好！

词汇focus on 专注于；present 现在 语法it is + adj. + to do sth. 句型。

Ms Gao: Make each day count.

让每一天都有意义。

词汇make ... count 使……有意义 语法祈使句；make + 宾语 + 动词原形。

Emma: Thanks for your advice, Ms Gao.

谢谢您的建议，高老师。

词汇advice 建议 语法Thank you for sth. 的变体。

Ms Gao: Oh, it's so hard to say goodbye.

哦，说再见真难。

词汇say goodbye 道别 语法it is + adj. + to do sth. 句型。

Ms Gao: I'll miss you all!

我会想念你们所有人的！

词汇miss 想念 语法will 表将来；all 作同位语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，高老师问即将回国的 Peter、Emma 和 Ella 对中国最深的感受以及未来的打算。Peter 说下个月就回国，已经开始想念中国；他最喜欢在这里生活、了解不同文化，并想以后回中国上大学或工作。Emma 最喜欢中餐。Ella 打算成为摄影师或记者，这样可以结识不同的人、到国外旅行。高老师祝他们梦想成真。Emma 说自己还没有计划，高中毕业后再决定；高老师建议她也可以专注当下，让每一天都有意义。最后高老师说，说再见真难，会想念大家。

English Summary

In this conversation, Ms Gao talks with Peter, Emma and Ella, who are going back to their home countries soon. She asks what they enjoyed most about China and what they see themselves doing in the future. Peter says he is leaving next month and already starting to miss China; he really enjoyed living here and learning about a different culture, and wants to come back to China for university or work. Emma enjoyed Chinese food most. Ella plans to become a photographer or a reporter so she can meet different people and travel abroad. Ms Gao hopes all their dreams come true. Emma says she has no plans yet and will decide after she graduates from high school; Ms Gao says it is good to focus on the present too and tells her to make each day count. Finally, Ms Gao says it is so hard to say goodbye and that she will miss them all.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

那么，你们觉得自己未来会怎样？

see oneself in the future 是固定表达，意为“设想自己的未来”；特殊疑问句用一般现在时。

I plan to become a photographer or a reporter so I can meet different people and travel abroad!

我打算成为摄影师或记者，这样我就能结识不同的人、到国外旅行！

plan to do sth.；so 引导目的状语从句；or 连接并列名词。

it is + adj. + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语；focus on 表示“专注于”。

Section B 1b · A Head Teacher's Graduation Speech

Section B 1b 主题：校长毕业典礼致辞（教材 P46–P47 两页合并）

Good morning, students, teachers, and guests.

同学们、老师们、各位来宾，早上好。

词汇guest 来宾 语法演讲开场问候语。

Welcome to our junior high graduation ceremony!

欢迎参加我们的初中毕业典礼！

词汇junior high school 初中；graduation ceremony 毕业典礼 语法welcome to + 名词。

To begin with, I would like to congratulate all the new graduates.

首先，我想向所有新毕业生表示祝贺。

词汇to begin with 首先；congratulate 祝贺；graduate 毕业生 语法to begin with 作插入语；would like to do sth.。

You have worked a long time for this moment.

为了这一刻，你们已经努力了很久。

词汇work hard 努力 语法现在完成时 have worked。

I remember meeting you when you first came here, full of energy and curiosity.

我记得你们初来时的样子，充满活力与好奇。

词汇remember doing sth. 记得做过某事；energy 活力；curiosity 好奇 语法remember doing sth.；when 引导时间状语从句；full of... 作伴随状语。

Today, I'm full of pride as I see a room filled with talented youths, full of hope for the future.

今天，当我看到满屋子才华横溢、对未来充满希望的年轻人时，我满心自豪。

词汇be full of 充满；pride 自豪；talented 有才华的；youth 年轻人 语法as 引导时间状语从句；filled with 作后置定语；full of hope 作伴随状语。

As you set out on your life's new journey, first, I would like to say, find your passion, and follow your dream!

当你们踏上人生的新征程时，首先我想说：找到你的热情，追随你的梦想！

词汇set out on 出发；开始；passion 热情 语法as 引导时间状语从句；祈使句。

Find what you really love and put all your energy and effort into it.

找到你真正热爱的事，并把全部精力和努力投入其中。

词汇put ... into 把……投入 语法祈使句；what 引导宾语从句。

Turn your eyes to the stars and keep your feet on the ground.

眼望星空，脚踏实地。

词汇keep one's feet on the ground 脚踏实地 语法并列祈使句；比喻表达。

Second, never give up!

第二，永不放弃！

词汇never 从不；give up 放弃 语法祈使句否定式。

After graduation, everybody will be entering different schools.

毕业后，大家将进入不同的学校。

词汇enter 进入 语法after + 名词；将来进行时 will be entering。

No matter where you go, you will face new challenges.

无论你们去哪里，都会面对新的挑战。

词汇no matter where 无论哪里；challenge 挑战 语法no matter where 引导让步状语从句。

See every challenge as an opportunity: an opportunity to grow, to learn, and to succeed.

把每个挑战看作一次机遇：成长、学习和成功的机会。

词汇see ... as 把……看作；opportunity 机会；succeed 成功 语法see...as...；冒号后是解释性的同位语。

So be brave and deal with your challenges through hard work.

所以，要勇敢，通过努力去应对挑战。

词汇deal with 应对；through 通过 语法so 表结果；and 连接并列谓语。

Always bear in mind: Diligence leads to success in one's life.

永远记住：勤奋会带来人生的成功。

词汇bear in mind 牢记；diligence 勤奋；lead to 导致 语法bear in mind 后接宾语从句；lead to。

Hard work will not fail you.

努力不会辜负你。

词汇fail 辜负；失败 语法一般将来时否定句。

Third, learn to be thankful.

第三，学会感恩。

词汇thankful 感恩的 语法learn to do sth.。

While you have worked hard to finish junior high school, you did not work alone.

虽然你们为完成初中学业付出了很多努力，但你们并非独自奋斗。

词汇while 虽然；finish 完成 语法while 引导让步状语从句；现在完成时。

Many others have lent hands to help you, and they have worked equally hard to get you to this moment.

许多人伸出援手帮助过你们，他们也同样努力，才让你们走到今天。

词汇lend a hand 伸出援手；equally 同样地 语法and 连接并列分句；现在完成时。

These people—your parents, teachers, and friends—have helped make you who you are today.

这些人——你们的父母、老师和朋友——帮助造就了今天的你们。

词汇make sb. who ... 造就某人成为…… 语法破折号中间是同位语；help do sth.；make + 宾语 + 表语。

Always be thankful for them.

永远对他们心怀感恩。

词汇be thankful for 对……感恩 语法祈使句。

Cherish their love and care forever.

永远珍惜他们的爱与关怀。

词汇cherish 珍惜；care 关怀 语法祈使句。

Always remember who you are and where you came from.

永远记住你是谁，来自哪里。

词汇remember 记住 语法remember 后接两个并列宾语从句。

Last but not least, the end of junior high is also the beginning of a new life.

最后但同样重要的是，初中的结束也是新生活的开始。

词汇last but not least 最后但同样重要的是 语法last but not least 作插入语；主系表结构。

Give your very best and have no regrets.

尽力而为，不留遗憾。

词汇give one's best 尽力；regret 遗憾 语法并列祈使句。

I wish you all success, and I hope we will meet again one day!

祝你们一切顺利，希望我们有一天再相聚！

词汇wish sb. sth. 祝愿某人…… 语法wish sb. sth.；and 连接并列分句；hope 后接宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇跨 P46–P47 两页的校长毕业典礼致辞首先向学生、老师和来宾问好，祝贺全体毕业生，并回忆他们初来时的活力与好奇，为满屋子才华横溢、充满希望的年轻人感到自豪。接着校长给出四点建议：第一，找到热情、追随梦想，把精力投入真正热爱的事，眼望星空、脚踏实地；第二，永不放弃，把每个挑战看作成长、学习和成功的机会，勇敢面对，牢记“勤奋带来成功”；第三，学会感恩，别忘记父母、老师和朋友们的帮助，珍惜他们的爱与关怀；最后，初中的结束也是新生活的开始，要尽力而为、不留遗憾，祝大家成功并期待再相聚。

English Summary

This head teacher's graduation speech, which runs across pages 46–47, greets the students, teachers and guests, congratulates the new graduates and recalls meeting them when they first arrived, full of energy and curiosity, expressing pride in a room full of talented youths full of hope. The head teacher then gives several pieces of advice: first, find your passion and follow your dream, put all your energy into what you really love, turn your eyes to the stars and keep your feet on the ground; second, never give up, see every challenge as an opportunity to grow, learn and succeed, deal with challenges through hard work, and bear in mind that diligence leads to success; third, learn to be thankful, remembering that parents, teachers and friends helped make you who you are today, and cherish their love and care forever; last but not least, the end of junior high is the beginning of a new life — give your very best, have no regrets, and the speaker wishes everyone success and hopes to meet again one day.

Section B · 2a · A Student's Graduation Speech

Section B · 3a 主题：学生毕业致辞（教材 P49）

Dear teachers, parents, and fellow students,

亲爱的老师们、家长们、同学们：

词汇fellow 同伴的 语法演讲称呼语。

Junior high school has been a great journey for me.

English Summary

This Reading Plus page presents two English poems (textbook pages 57–58; page 59 contains exercises only and is not included; page 57 was rendered from the scanned PDF). The first, See It Through by Edgar Guest, tells readers that when they are up against a trouble they should meet it squarely, lift their chin, set their shoulders and plant their feet; when it is vain to dodge it, they should do the best they can — they may fail but may conquer, so they should see it through. The second poem, It's a Long Way by William Braithwaite, says that although the sea-winds blow a long way over the blue sea-plains, the speaker's heart has far longer to go before its dreams come true; we must work and love and do our best, taking the hope of dreams in trust to keep us day by day, and though the sea-winds still blow a long way, somewhere lies a shore and the speaker's dreams will flow down the tide of time forevermore.

倒装句；before 引导时间状语从句；longer far 中 far 修饰比较级。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 教材 P57–P58 是 Reading Plus 的两首英文诗：See It Through 与 It's a Long Way；P59 为练习，不收。
  • 读前看诗歌标题，猜一猜两首诗分别想表达什么。

读中

  • 朗读诗歌，注意押韵与节奏（如 face / brace、you / do）。
  • 找出两首诗中的比喻与拟人表达。

读后

  • 用一两句话概括每首诗的主旨。
  • 完成 P59 的练习并讨论：诗歌如何给人力量？

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面四篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What does Peter want to do in the future?

  1. Come back to China for university or work.
  2. Become a photographer.
  3. Travel abroad as a reporter.
  4. Stay in his home country.
答案与解析

A 原文：I want to come back to China for university or work. 可知彼得想回中国上大学或工作。

2. What does Ella plan to become?

  1. A teacher.
  2. A photographer or a reporter.
  3. A doctor.
  4. A head teacher.
答案与解析

B 原文：I plan to become a photographer or a reporter 可知艾拉打算成为摄影师或记者。

3. What does the head teacher advise students to do first?

  1. Never give up.
  2. Learn to be thankful.
  3. Find their passion and follow their dream.
  4. Give their very best.
答案与解析

C 原文：first, I would like to say, find your passion, and follow your dream! 可知校长首先建议找到热情、追随梦想。

4. According to the speech, what leads to success in one's life?

  1. Good luck.
  2. Friendship.
  3. Wealth.
  4. Diligence.
答案与解析

D 原文：Always bear in mind: Diligence leads to success in one's life. 可知勤奋带来人生的成功。

5. What does the student love most at school?

  1. The drama club.
  2. The sports club.
  3. The music club.
  4. The science club.
答案与解析

A 原文：many of you know how much I love our drama club! 可知学生最爱戏剧社。

6. What does the speaker wish for all the graduates?

  1. A long holiday.
  2. Every success in the future.
  3. A new school.
  4. More homework.
答案与解析

B 原文：I wish each of us every success in the future! 可知他祝大家未来成功。

7. In "See It Through", what should you do when you face a trouble?

  1. Run away from it.
  2. Wait for help.
  3. Meet it squarely, face to face.
  4. Give up at once.
答案与解析

C 原文：When you're up against a trouble, meet it squarely, face to face; 可知要正面迎接困难。

8. What does "It's a Long Way" say about dreams?

  1. They never come true.
  2. They are easy to reach.
  3. They are not worth fighting for.
  4. My heart has a longer way to go before its dreams come true.
答案与解析

D 原文：But longer far has my heart to go before its dreams come true. 可知在梦想实现之前心还要走更远的路。
