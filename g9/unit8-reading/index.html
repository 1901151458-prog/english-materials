Section A · 3a · Watching the Winter Olympics

Section A · 3a 主题：冬奥会观赛 · 对话（教材 P74）

Ella: The Winter Olympics start next month.

冬奥会下个月就要开始了。

词汇Winter Olympics 冬奥会；start 开始；next month 下个月 语法一般现在时表按计划将要发生；start 在这里作不及物动词。

Ella: What event would you like to watch?

你想看哪个项目？

词汇event 比赛项目；would like 想要 语法would like to do sth.；what event 作 watch 的宾语。

Teng Fei: Ski jumping is at the top of my list.

跳台滑雪是我最想看的。

词汇ski jumping 跳台滑雪；at the top of one's list 在某人名单的最前面 语法at the top of one's list 表示“最想看/最想要”。

Ella: Same here!

我也是！

词汇same here 我也是 语法口语中表示“我也是”。

Teng Fei: Really?

真的吗？

词汇really 真的 语法口语中表示惊讶或确认。

Teng Fei: I just love seeing the athletes fly through the air!

我就是喜欢看运动员在空中飞跃！

词汇love doing sth. 喜欢做某事；athlete 运动员；through the air 在空中 语法love doing sth.；see sb. do sth. 表示“看见某人做某事”。

Ella: I agree.

我同意。

词汇agree 同意 语法一般现在时表示观点。

Ella: It's not easy to do what they do.

做到他们做的事并不容易。

词汇easy 容易的 语法It's + adj. + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语；what they do 是宾语从句。

Teng Fei: My parents and I also enjoy watching figure skating.

我和父母也喜欢看花样滑冰。

词汇enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事；figure skating 花样滑冰 语法My parents and I 作主语；enjoy doing sth.。

Teng Fei: I'm always amazed by their strength and technique.

我总是惊叹于他们的力量和技巧。

词汇be amazed by 对……感到惊叹；strength 力量；technique 技巧 语法be amazed by 表示“对……感到惊叹”。

Ella: Like you, my family watch sports together, especially speed skating.

和你家一样，我们一家人也一起看比赛，尤其是速度滑冰。

词汇especially 尤其；speed skating 速度滑冰 语法like you 作状语；family 是集合名词，这里谓语用 watch。

Ella: It's truly amazing.

真是太精彩了。

词汇truly 真正地；amazing 令人惊叹的 语法It's + 形容词，表示评价。

Ella: What about the summer games?

夏季奥运会呢？

词汇what about ... ……呢；summer games 夏季奥运会 语法What about...? 用于引出话题。

Ella: Do you have a favourite event?

你有最喜欢的项目吗？

词汇favourite 最喜欢的 语法Do you have...? 询问“你有没有……”。

Teng Fei: Oh yes!

哦，有！

词汇oh yes 哦，有 语法口语应答。

Teng Fei: I especially like the men's swimming.

我特别喜欢男子游泳。

词汇especially 尤其；men's swimming 男子游泳 语法like + 名词；men's 是名词所有格。

Ella: You must be a good swimmer.

那你一定是个游泳好手。

词汇must be 一定是；swimmer 游泳者 语法must be 表示肯定推测。

Teng Fei: Not really, but I love watching it anyway.

其实不是，但我还是很喜欢看。

词汇not really 其实不是；anyway 无论如何 语法but 表转折；anyway 常放句末。

Teng Fei: For me, the swimming events are so exciting because swimmers often win by just 0.01 seconds.

对我来说，游泳比赛非常刺激，因为选手们常常仅以 0.01 秒之差获胜。

词汇for me 对我来说；exciting 令人兴奋的；by 表示差距 语法for me 表示“在我看来”；because 引导原因状语从句；by 表示差距。

Teng Fei: My family also watch team sports together, like women's volleyball.

我们家人也一起看团队项目，比如女排。

词汇team sports 团队项目；like 例如；women's volleyball 女子排球 语法like 用于举例。

Teng Fei: That's my mum's favourite.

那是我妈妈的最爱。

词汇favourite 最喜欢的人或物 语法That's = That is；my mum's 是名词所有格。

Ella: Cool!

太酷了！

词汇cool 酷的 语法口语中表示赞叹。

Ella: Oh, don't forget to watch the opening ceremony.

哦，别忘了看开幕式。

词汇forget 忘记；opening ceremony 开幕式 语法否定祈使句 Don't + 动词原形；forget to do sth. 忘记去做某事。

Teng Fei: Yeah.

是啊。

词汇yeah 是啊 语法口语中表示同意。

Teng Fei: That's always exciting!

那总是很激动人心！

词汇always 总是；exciting 令人兴奋的 语法That's = That is；always 表频率。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，Ella 和 Teng Fei 聊起即将到来的冬奥会。Teng Fei 最想看跳台滑雪，Ella 也这么想；Teng Fei 说喜欢看运动员在空中飞跃，Ella 觉得他们的动作很不容易。Teng Fei 和父母还喜欢看花样滑冰，惊叹于运动员的力量和技巧；Ella 家则一起看速度滑冰。聊到夏季奥运会时，Teng Fei 说特别喜欢男子游泳，虽然自己不是好泳者，但觉得游泳比赛很刺激，因为选手常以 0.01 秒之差获胜；他家还一起看女排等团队项目，那是他妈妈的最爱。最后 Ella 提醒不要错过开幕式。

English Summary

In the conversation, Ella and Teng Fei talk about the Winter Olympics starting next month. Teng Fei says ski jumping is at the top of his list and loves seeing athletes fly through the air; Ella agrees and says what they do is not easy. Teng Fei and his parents also enjoy watching figure skating, amazed by the athletes' strength and technique, while Ella's family watch sports together, especially speed skating. When they talk about the Summer Games, Teng Fei says he especially likes men's swimming; although he is not a good swimmer, he finds swimming events exciting because swimmers often win by just 0.01 seconds. His family also watch team sports such as women's volleyball, his mum's favourite. Finally Ella reminds him not to forget the opening ceremony.

一般现在时表按计划将发生；start 作不及物动词。

What event would you like to watch?

Ski jumping is at the top of my list.

I just love seeing the athletes fly through the air!

love doing sth.；see sb. do sth.。

It's not easy to do what they do.

It's + adj. + to do sth. 句型；what they do 是宾语从句。

My parents and I also enjoy watching figure skating.

My parents and I 作主语；enjoy doing sth.。

I'm always amazed by their strength and technique.

be amazed by 表示“对……感到惊叹”。

Like you, my family watch sports together, especially speed skating.

like you 作状语；family 是集合名词。

What about...? 用于引出话题。

must be 表示肯定推测。

For me, the swimming events are so exciting because swimmers often win by just 0.01 seconds.

for me 表示“在我看来”；because 引导原因状语从句；by 表示差距。

Section B 1b · A Speech about Beijing's Two Olympic Games

Section B 1b 主题：北京双奥之城 · 演讲稿（教材 P76–P77 两页合并）

Good morning, everyone!

大家早上好！

词汇good morning 早上好；everyone 大家 语法演讲开场的问候语。

Today, I'd like to talk about my favourite sports event.

今天，我想谈谈我最喜欢的体育赛事。

词汇would like to do sth. 想要做某事；sports event 体育赛事 语法I'd = I would；would like to do sth.；talk about。

Actually, when I used the word "event", it means two events: the 2008 Summer Olympics and the 2022 Winter Olympics.

其实，我用“event”这个词时，它指的是两件事：2008 年夏季奥运会和 2022 年冬季奥运会。

词汇actually 实际上；mean 意思是 语法when 引导时间状语从句；it 指 the word "event"。

They were held in Beijing—the first city ever to host both the Summer and the Winter Olympics.

它们都在北京举办——北京是第一个同时举办过夏季奥运会和冬季奥运会的城市。

词汇be held 被举办；host 举办；both...and... 两者都 语法一般过去时被动语态 were held；the first + 名词 + to do 作同位语。

The most recent was the 2022 Winter Olympics.

最近的一届是 2022 年冬奥会。

词汇most recent 最近的 语法最高级 the most recent 作主语。

A total of nearly 2,900 athletes attended and competed in 109 events in seven Olympic winter sports.

共有近 2,900 名运动员参加 7 个冬奥大项中的 109 个小项。

词汇a total of 总共；attend 参加；compete in 参加（比赛） 语法a total of 作主语；and 连接并列谓语；compete in。

There were many exciting moments.

有许多激动人心的时刻。

词汇exciting 令人兴奋的；moment 时刻 语法There be 句型。

Nearly the whole world watched the fantastic figure skating and ski jumping competitions.

全世界几乎都观看了精彩的花样滑冰和跳台滑雪比赛。

词汇nearly 几乎；fantastic 精彩的；figure skating 花样滑冰；competition 比赛 语法nearly 修饰 the whole world；一般过去时。

I watched the freestyle skiing, and like everyone else, I fell in love with those brave and talented athletes.

我看了自由式滑雪，而且和大家一样，爱上了那些勇敢而有天赋的运动员。

词汇freestyle skiing 自由式滑雪；fall in love with 爱上；talented 有天赋的 语法and 连接并列分句；like everyone else 是插入语。

I also became a huge fan of the mascot, Bing Dwen Dwen.

我还成了吉祥物冰墩墩的超级粉丝。

词汇huge fan 超级粉丝；mascot 吉祥物 语法become a fan of 表示“成为……的粉丝”。

And we shall not forget the amazing achievements in the 2008 Summer Olympics.

我们也不会忘记 2008 年夏季奥运会上那些惊人的成就。

词汇shall 将；achievement 成就 语法shall 用于正式语气；not forget。

I watched in wonder as Usain Bolt smiled his way across the finish line in the 100 metres, setting a new world record.

我惊叹地看着尤塞恩·博尔特在 100 米比赛中笑着冲过终点线，创造了新的世界纪录。

词汇in wonder 惊叹地；finish line 终点线；set a record 创造纪录 语法as 引导时间状语从句；setting... 是现在分词作结果状语。

Then I watched the drama of the shooting event.

接着我看了射击比赛的戏剧性场面。

词汇drama 戏剧性场面；shooting 射击 语法then 表示时间顺序；of 短语作定语。

Du Li won her second Olympic gold medal, after the first one in 2004.

杜丽赢得了她的第二枚奥运金牌，第一枚是在 2004 年。

词汇gold medal 金牌；after 在……之后 语法一般过去时 won；after 短语作时间状语。

Both were dramatic come-from-behind victories.

两场都是戏剧性的逆转胜利。

词汇dramatic 戏剧性的；come-from-behind 逆转的；victory 胜利 语法both 作主语；were 是系动词。

Hosting two Olympics brought challenges but also opportunities to Beijing.

举办两届奥运会给北京带来了挑战，也带来了机遇。

词汇host 举办；challenge 挑战；opportunity 机遇 语法动名词 Hosting 作主语；bring sth. to sb.；but also。

Both Games showed China's success in its reform and opening-up on the world stage.

两届奥运会都展示了中国改革开放在世界舞台上的成功。

词汇reform and opening-up 改革开放；world stage 世界舞台 语法show sth.；on the world stage 作状语。

China's great achievements have gained worldwide attention.

中国的伟大成就引起了全世界的关注。

词汇achievement 成就；worldwide 全世界的；attention 关注 语法现在完成时 have gained；gain attention。

In both events, many volunteers and citizens helped out.

在两届赛事中，许多志愿者和市民都伸出了援手。

词汇volunteer 志愿者；citizen 市民；help out 帮忙 语法in both events 作状语；help out 是固定短语。

They worked with pride to make sure that people from around the world had a pleasant stay.

他们自豪地工作，确保来自世界各地的人们过得愉快。

词汇with pride 自豪地；make sure 确保；pleasant 愉快的 语法不定式 to make sure 表目的；make sure that... 引导宾语从句。

Events like the Olympic Games build bridges between countries and form friendships between people.

像奥运会这样的赛事在国与国之间搭建桥梁，在人与人之间建立友谊。

词汇build bridges 搭建桥梁；form 建立；friendship 友谊 语法like 表示“像……这样的”；and 连接并列谓语。

The purpose of the Olympics is to build relationships between people, to celebrate the Olympic spirit, and to bring people together for peace and harmony.

奥运会的目的是建立人与人之间的关系、弘扬奥林匹克精神，并把人们团结在一起，追求和平与和谐。

词汇purpose 目的；celebrate 弘扬；spirit 精神；peace 和平；harmony 和谐 语法三个不定式短语并列作表语；bring people together 表示“把人们团结起来”。

This is shown in both the motto of the 2008 Olympics, "One World, One Dream", and the motto of the 2022 Olympics, "Together for a Shared Future".

这体现在 2008 年奥运会口号“同一个世界，同一个梦想”和 2022 年奥运会口号“一起向未来”中。

词汇motto 口号；be shown in 体现在 语法被动语态 is shown；both...and... 连接两个并列成分。

These mottos touch my heart quite deeply, as I'm sure they touch yours.

这些口号深深打动了我的心，我相信也打动了你们的心。

词汇touch one's heart 打动某人的心；deeply 深深地 语法as 引导方式状语从句；I'm sure 后接宾语从句。

Thank you for listening!

感谢大家的聆听！

词汇thank you for 感谢……；listen 听 语法Thank you for doing sth.；演讲结束语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇演讲稿跨 P76–P77 两页。演讲者首先问好，说自己要谈最喜欢的体育赛事——“event”在这里指两件事：2008 年夏季奥运会和 2022 年冬季奥运会，它们都在北京举办，北京是第一个同时举办夏奥会和冬奥会的城市。2022 年冬奥会有近 2,900 名运动员参加 109 个小项、7 个大项；全世界都观看了花样滑冰和跳台滑雪，演讲者则看了自由式滑雪，爱上了勇敢有才华的运动员，还成了吉祥物冰墩墩的超级粉丝。2008 年夏奥会上，博尔特在 100 米中笑着冲过终点并打破世界纪录，杜丽继 2004 年后再次夺得射击金牌，两场都是戏剧性的逆转胜利。举办两届奥运会给北京带来挑战也带来机遇，展示了中国改革开放的成功，志愿者和市民也以自豪的心情帮助外国朋友。奥运会这样的赛事在国与国之间搭建桥梁、在人与人之间建立友谊，目的是弘扬奥林匹克精神、促进和平与和谐——这正是“同一个世界，同一个梦想”和“一起向未来”两个口号的体现。最后演讲者感谢听众。

English Summary

This speech runs across pages 76–77. The speaker greets the audience and talks about their favourite sports event — actually two events: the 2008 Summer Olympics and the 2022 Winter Olympics, both held in Beijing, the first city ever to host both the Summer and Winter Olympics. The 2022 Winter Olympics had nearly 2,900 athletes competing in 109 events in seven winter sports; the whole world watched figure skating and ski jumping, while the speaker watched freestyle skiing, fell in love with the brave and talented athletes, and became a huge fan of the mascot Bing Dwen Dwen. At the 2008 Summer Olympics, Usain Bolt smiled across the finish line in the 100 metres and set a new world record, and Du Li won her second Olympic gold in shooting after 2004 — both dramatic come-from-behind victories. Hosting two Olympics brought challenges but also opportunities to Beijing and showed China's success in reform and opening-up; many volunteers and citizens helped to make visitors' stay pleasant. Events like the Olympics build bridges between countries and friendships between people, and the purpose of the Olympics is to build relationships, celebrate the Olympic spirit and bring people together for peace and harmony — as shown in the mottos "One World, One Dream" and "Together for a Shared Future". The speaker thanks the audience for listening.

This article, which runs across pages 92–93, introduces the Paralympics. Every two years millions of people watch the Summer or Winter Olympics, but a few weeks later the same city hosts another major event — the Paralympic Games, an international sports event for athletes with physical disabilities. The idea came from a doctor named Ludwig Guttmann in England shortly after World War II; interested in improving the lives of soldiers dependent on wheelchairs, he arranged a sports competition between a small group of patients on the same day as the opening of the 1948 Olympic Games. The event slowly grew and in 1960 became known as the first Paralympic Games; since then the Paralympics have been held in the same year and country as the Olympics, and nowadays take place in the same cities and venues a short time later. At first open only to people in wheelchairs, the Paralympics included athletes with visual and other physical disabilities in 1976, and athletes generally compete only against others with the same kind of disability. The Paralympics have produced many heroes and champions, including Natalia Partyka, who was born without a right hand, and Chinese Paralympian Liu Cuiqing, who won gold medals with her guide Xu Donglin. The Paralympics' goal is to help those with disabilities improve their physical health and bring them joy and purpose; following the Olympic motto "Faster, Higher, Stronger—Together", the Games show the amazing strength and spirit of athletes with physical disabilities.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

He therefore decided to arrange a sports competition between a small group of patients on the same day as the opening of the 1948 Olympic Games.

因此，他决定在 1948 年奥运会开幕的同一天，为一小群病人组织一场体育比赛。

therefore 表结果；decide to do sth.；on the same day as 表示“与……同一天”。

It was decided that from then on the Paralympics would be held in the same year and country as the Olympic Games.

后来决定，从那时起，残奥会将在与奥运会相同的年份和国家举办。

It was decided that... 是形式主语句型；would be held 是过去将来时被动语态；from then on 表示“从那时起”。

The Paralympics' goal has always been to help those with disabilities to improve their physical health and bring them joy and purpose.

现在完成时 has been；不定式 to help 作表语；help sb. (to) do sth.；those with disabilities 表示“残障人士”。
