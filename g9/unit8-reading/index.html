Section A 3a · Let's Talk About Sports Events

Section A 3a 主题：奥运项目 · 喜好

Ella: The Winter Olympics start next month. What event would you like to watch?

冬奥会下个月开始。你想看哪个项目？

词汇the Winter Olympics 冬奥会；event 比赛项目；would like to 想要 语法What event would you like to watch? 特殊疑问句；would like to do sth.。

Teng Fei: Ski jumping is at the top of my list.

跳台滑雪是我的首选。

词汇ski jumping 跳台滑雪；at the top of one's list 某人的首选 语法at the top of one's list 是固定表达。

Ella: Same here!

我也是！

词汇same here 我也一样 语法Same here! 是口语中表示“我也一样”的省略句。

Teng Fei: Really? I just love seeing the athletes fly through the air!

真的吗？我就是喜欢看运动员在空中飞翔！

词汇athlete 运动员；fly through the air 在空中飞翔 语法love doing sth.；see sb. do sth. 表示看到某人做某事。

Ella: I agree. It's not easy to do what they do.

我同意。他们做的事可不容易。

词汇agree 同意；be easy to do 做……容易 语法It is + adj. + to do sth.；what they do 是宾语从句。

Teng Fei: My parents and I also enjoy watching figure skating.

我和父母也喜欢看花样滑冰。

词汇enjoy doing 喜欢做；figure skating 花样滑冰 语法enjoy + 动名词；My parents and I 作主语。

Teng Fei: I'm always amazed by their strength and technique.

我总是惊叹于他们的力量和技巧。

词汇be amazed by 对……感到惊叹；strength 力量；technique 技巧 语法be amazed by 是固定搭配。

Ella: Like you, my family watch sports together, especially speed skating.

和你一样，我们全家一起看体育比赛，尤其是速度滑冰。

词汇like you 像你一样；especially 尤其；speed skating 速度滑冰 语法Like you 作状语；watch sports together。

Ella: It's truly amazing.

真是令人惊叹。

词汇truly 真正地；amazing 令人惊叹的 语法It's + adj. 表示评价。

Ella: What about the Summer Games? Do you have a favourite event?

那夏季奥运会呢？你有最喜欢的项目吗？

词汇the Summer Games 夏季奥运会；favourite 最喜欢的 语法What about...? 询问；Do you have...? 一般疑问句。

Teng Fei: Oh yes! I especially like the men's swimming.

哦，有！我特别喜欢男子游泳。

词汇especially 尤其；men's swimming 男子游泳 语法especially 修饰动词 like；the men's swimming 作宾语。

Ella: You must be a good swimmer.

你一定很会游泳。

词汇must 一定；swimmer 游泳者 语法must 表示肯定推测；be a good swimmer。

Teng Fei: Not really, but I love watching it anyway.

其实不怎么样，但我还是喜欢看。

词汇not really 其实不；anyway 不管怎样 语法not really 表示委婉否定；but 转折。

Teng Fei: For me, the swimming events are so exciting because swimmers often win by just 0.01 seconds.

对我来说，游泳项目非常刺激，因为选手们常常只以 0.01 秒优势获胜。

词汇exciting 刺激的；win by 以……优势获胜；second 秒 语法why 引导原因状语从句；win by just 0.01 seconds。

Teng Fei: My family also watch team sports together, like women's volleyball. That's my mum's favourite.

我们全家也一起看团队项目，比如女子排球。那是我妈妈的最爱。

词汇team sports 团队项目；volleyball 排球；favourite 最爱 语法like 举例；That's + 名词性物主代词。

Ella: Cool! Oh, don't forget to watch the opening ceremony.

酷！哦，别忘了看开幕式。

词汇forget to do 忘记做；opening ceremony 开幕式 语法祈使句否定式 Don't forget to do sth.。

Teng Fei: Yeah. That's always exciting!

是啊。那总是很令人激动！

词汇always 总是；exciting 令人激动的 语法That's + adj. 表示评价。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕“最想看的奥运项目”展开。Ella 问 Teng Fei 想看什么项目，Teng Fei 说跳台滑雪是首选，因为他喜欢看运动员在空中飞翔。Teng Fei 和父母还喜欢看花样滑冰，惊叹于运动员的力量和技巧；Ella 一家则喜欢一起看速度滑冰。夏季奥运会方面，Teng Fei 尤其喜欢男子游泳，因为选手常常只以 0.01 秒优势获胜；他一家也看女子排球，那是他妈妈的最爱。Ella 提醒他别忘看开幕式。

English Summary

The conversation is about favourite Olympic events. Ella asks Teng Fei what event he would like to watch. Teng Fei says ski jumping is at the top of his list because he loves seeing athletes fly through the air. Teng Fei and his parents also enjoy figure skating and are amazed by the athletes' strength and technique; Ella's family watch speed skating together. For the Summer Games, Teng Fei especially likes men's swimming because swimmers often win by just 0.01 seconds; his family also watch women's volleyball, his mum's favourite. Ella reminds him not to forget the opening ceremony.

语法点 Grammar

What event would you like to watch?

would like to do sth.；特殊疑问句。

Ski jumping is at the top of my list.

at the top of one's list 是固定表达。

I just love seeing the athletes fly through the air!

love doing sth.；see sb. do sth.。

It's not easy to do what they do.

It is + adj. + to do sth.；what they do 是宾语从句。

My parents and I also enjoy watching figure skating.

enjoy + 动名词；My parents and I 作主语。

I'm always amazed by their strength and technique.

be amazed by 是固定搭配。

my family watch sports together, especially speed skating

family 作整体时谓语可用复数；especially 修饰名词短语。

the swimming events are so exciting because swimmers often win by just 0.01 seconds

because 引导原因状语从句；win by 表示以……优势获胜。

That's my mum's favourite.

名词性物主代词 my mum's 表示“我妈妈最喜欢的（项目）”。

don't forget to watch the opening ceremony

Don't forget to do sth. 表示“别忘了做某事”。

Section B 1b · Beijing: The Olympic City

Section B 1b 主题：双奥之城 · 奥运精神

Good morning, everyone!

大家早上好！

词汇good morning 早上好 语法演讲开场问候语。

Today, I'd like to talk about my favourite sports event.

今天我想谈谈我最喜欢的体育赛事。

词汇would like to do 想要做；sports event 体育赛事 语法I'd like to do sth.；talk about。

Actually, when I used the word "event", I meant two events: the 2008 Summer Olympics and the 2022 Winter Olympics.

其实，我所说的“赛事”指两项：2008 年夏季奥运会和 2022 年冬季奥运会。

词汇actually 其实；mean 指；the Summer/Winter Olympics 夏/冬奥会 语法when 引导时间状语从句；meant 是 mean 的过去式；冒号后解释。

They were held in Beijing—the first city ever to host both the Summer and the Winter Olympics.

它们都在北京举办——北京是首个既举办夏奥又举办冬奥的城市。

词汇be held 举办；host 主办；both...and... 两者都 语法were held 是一般过去时被动语态；the first city ever to host... 中 to host 作后置定语。

The most recent was the 2022 Winter Olympics.

最近的是 2022 年冬奥会。

词汇recent 最近的 语法the most recent 是最高级；was 是系动词。

A total of nearly 2,900 athletes attended and competed in 109 events in seven Olympic winter sports.

近 2900 名运动员参加并角逐了 7 项冬季奥林匹克运动会的 109 个项目。

词汇a total of 总共；athlete 运动员；attend 参加；compete in 参加角逐 语法a total of + 数词；attend and competed 并列谓语。

There were many exciting moments.

留下无数精彩瞬间。

词汇exciting 令人激动的；moment 瞬间 语法There be 句型。

Nearly the whole world watched the fantastic figure skating and ski jumping competitions.

几乎全世界的人都观看了精彩绝伦的花样滑冰和跳台滑雪比赛。

词汇nearly 几乎；the whole world 全世界；fantastic 精彩的；competition 比赛 语法nearly 修饰 the whole world；figure skating / ski jumping 作定语。

I watched the freestyle skiing, and like everyone else, I fell in love with those brave and talented athletes.

我观看了自由式滑雪项目，像其他所有人一样，我被那些勇敢且才华横溢的运动员深深吸引。

词汇freestyle skiing 自由式滑雪；fall in love with 爱上；talented 有才华的 语法and 连接并列谓语；like everyone else 作插入语。

I also became a huge fan of the mascot, Bing Dwen Dwen.

同时，我也成了吉祥物“冰墩墩”的忠实粉丝。

词汇a huge fan of ……的忠实粉丝；mascot 吉祥物 语法become a fan of；Bing Dwen Dwen 是同位语。

And we shall not forget the amazing achievements in the 2008 Summer Olympics.

我们也不会忘记 2008 年夏奥会的辉煌成就。

词汇shall 将；achievement 成就 语法shall 用于正式演讲；not forget。

I watched in wonder as Usain Bolt smiled his way across the finish line in the 100 metres, setting a new world record.

我惊奇地目睹了尤塞恩·博尔特微笑着冲过 100 米终点线那一幕，并由此创下了一项新的世界纪录。

词汇in wonder 惊奇地；finish line 终点线；set a record 创纪录 语法as 引导时间状语从句；setting... 是现在分词作结果状语。

Then I watched the drama of the shooting event.

随后我又观看了射击项目的精彩赛事。

词汇drama 戏剧性场面；shooting 射击 语法the drama of... 表示“……的戏剧性”。

Du Li won her second Olympic gold medal, after the first one in 2004.

杜丽继 2004 年首夺奥运金牌后，再次摘得一枚奥运金牌。

词汇gold medal 金牌；second 第二 语法win a gold medal；after 引导时间状语。

Both were dramatic come-from-behind victories.

这两次夺冠都堪称戏剧性的逆转胜绩。

词汇dramatic 戏剧性的；come-from-behind 逆转的；victory 胜利 语法both 作主语；come-from-behind 是合成形容词。

Hosting two Olympics brought challenges but also opportunities to Beijing.

举办两届奥运会既给北京带来了挑战，也带来了机遇。

词汇host 主办；challenge 挑战；opportunity 机遇 语法动名词 Hosting 作主语；bring sth. to sb.。

Both Games showed China's success in its reform and opening-up on the world stage.

两届奥运会都彰显了中国改革开放进程中在世界舞台上的成就。

词汇reform and opening-up 改革开放；world stage 世界舞台 语法show sth.；on the world stage 作状语。

China's great achievements have gained worldwide attention.

中国取得的巨大成就已引起全球瞩目。

词汇achievement 成就；gain 获得；worldwide 全球的；attention 关注 语法现在完成时 have gained。

Events like the Olympic Games build bridges between countries and form friendships between people.

奥运会这样的赛事搭建起国家间的桥梁，缔结人民间的友谊。

词汇build bridges 搭建桥梁；form friendships 缔结友谊 语法like 举例；between...and...。

The purpose of the Olympics is to build relationships between people, to celebrate the Olympic spirit, and to bring people together for peace and harmony.

奥运会的宗旨是增进人与人的联系，弘扬奥运精神，凝聚人心，共创和平与和谐。

词汇purpose 目的；relationship 联系；celebrate 弘扬；spirit 精神；peace 和平；harmony 和谐 语法The purpose is to do...；三个不定式并列作表语。

This is shown in both the motto of the 2008 Olympics, "One World, One Dream", and the motto of the 2022 Olympics, "Together for a Shared Future".

这一点体现在 2008 年奥运会的口号“同一个世界，同一个梦想”和 2022 年奥运会的口号“一起向未来”中。

词汇motto 口号；both...and... 两者都；shared future 共同未来 语法is shown 是被动语态；both...and... 连接两个名词短语。

These mottos touch my heart quite deeply, as I'm sure they touch yours.

这些口号深深触动了我的心，我相信它们也同样触动了你们的心。

词汇touch one's heart 触动某人的心；deeply 深深地 语法as 引导方式状语从句；they touch yours 是省略句。

Thank you for listening!

感谢大家的聆听！

词汇thank you for doing 感谢做某事 语法Thank you for doing sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇演讲稿介绍北京作为“双奥之城”。北京是首个既举办 2008 年夏奥会、又举办 2022 年冬奥会的城市。2022 年冬奥会有近 2900 名运动员参加 109 个项目；作者喜欢自由式滑雪和吉祥物冰墩墩。2008 年夏奥会也有难忘时刻：博尔特破世界纪录、杜丽逆转夺冠。举办两届奥运会给北京带来挑战和机遇，展示中国改革开放的成就。奥运会搭起国家间的桥梁，宗旨是增进联系、弘扬奥运精神、促进和平和谐，体现在“同一个世界，同一个梦想”和“一起向未来”的口号中。

English Summary

The speech introduces Beijing as the Olympic city. Beijing is the first city to host both the 2008 Summer Olympics and the 2022 Winter Olympics. The 2022 Winter Games had nearly 2,900 athletes in 109 events; the speaker loved freestyle skiing and the mascot Bing Dwen Dwen. The 2008 Games also had memorable moments: Usain Bolt set a world record, and Du Li won a come-from-behind gold. Hosting both Games brought challenges and opportunities and showed China's achievements in reform and opening-up. The Olympics build bridges between countries; their purpose is to build relationships, celebrate the Olympic spirit and bring people together for peace and harmony, as shown in the mottos "One World, One Dream" and "Together for a Shared Future".

Section B 3a · An Olympic Swimmer Who Nearly Drowned

Section B 3a 主题：奥运精神 · 永不放弃

You may have never heard of my favourite athlete, Eric Moussambani, the first Olympic swimmer from Equatorial Guinea.

你可能从未听说过我最喜欢的运动员——埃里克·莫桑巴尼，赤道几内亚首位奥运游泳选手。

词汇hear of 听说；athlete 运动员；swimmer 游泳者；Equatorial Guinea 赤道几内亚 语法may have done 表示对过去的推测；the first... 是同位语。

Eric swam in the 100-metre freestyle at the 2000 Sydney Olympics.

埃里克参加了 2000 年悉尼奥运会的 100 米自由泳项目。

词汇100-metre freestyle 100 米自由泳；Sydney Olympics 悉尼奥运会 语法一般过去时；at the Olympics 表示赛事。

However, he had just begun swimming only eight months before the Games!

然而，他仅在奥运会开幕前 8 个月才开始游泳！

词汇however 然而；begin doing 开始做；before the Games 在奥运会前 语法过去完成时 had begun；before 表示时间。

The only swimming pool for him was a hotel pool about 13 metres long, and even then he could only use it for an hour every morning.

他能用的唯一泳池是一个长约 13 米的酒店泳池，即便如此，他每天上午也只能使用一小时。

词汇swimming pool 游泳池；about 大约；even then 即便如此 语法about 13 metres long 作后置定语；even then 作状语。

In fact, the first time he ever saw an Olympic-sized 50-metre swimming pool was at the Olympics.

事实上，他第一次见到奥运标准的 50 米泳池就是在奥运会上。

词汇in fact 事实上；Olympic-sized 奥运标准的；50-metre 50 米的 语法the first time 引导时间状语从句；was 是系动词。

During his race, he swam well for the first 50 metres, but the last 15 metres of the race were especially difficult for him.

比赛过程中，前 50 米他游得不错，但最后 15 米对他来说格外艰难。

词汇during 在……期间；race 比赛；especially 尤其；difficult 困难的 语法but 转折；the last 15 metres 作主语。

In fact, it looked like he was going to drown!

事实上，看上去他快要溺水了！

词汇look like 看起来像；drown 溺水 语法look like + 从句；was going to 表示将要。

But he tried hard and finished the race, winning loud cheers from the crowd.

但他奋力坚持完成了比赛，赢得了观众热烈的欢呼。

词汇try hard 努力；finish 完成；cheer 欢呼；crowd 观众 语法winning... 是现在分词作结果状语。

Though Eric Moussambani is not an Olympic medal winner, he is still my favourite athlete.

虽然埃里克·莫桑巴尼不是奥运奖牌得主，但他仍是我最喜欢的运动员。

词汇though 虽然；medal 奖牌；winner 获胜者 语法though 引导让步状语从句；不能与 but 连用。

He never gave up no matter how hard it was.

无论多难，他从未放弃。

词汇give up 放弃；no matter how 无论多么 语法no matter how 引导让步状语从句。

With hard work and practice, Eric later became quite fast at the 100 metres.

通过刻苦训练和练习，埃里克后来在 100 米游泳项目上游得相当快。

词汇hard work 努力；practice 练习；become fast at 在……方面变快 语法with 表示方式；become + adj.。

His time for the 100-metre event improved from 1 minute 52 seconds, his Olympic record, to under 57 seconds!

他的 100 米项目成绩从奥运纪录的 1 分 52 秒提升到了 57 秒以内！

词汇time 成绩；improve 提高；record 纪录；under 少于 语法improve from...to...；his Olympic record 是同位语。

He later became the coach of the national swimming team for Equatorial Guinea, and he is a true example of the Olympic spirit.

他后来成为了赤道几内亚国家游泳队的教练，是奥林匹克精神的真正典范。

词汇coach 教练；national 国家的；true 真正的；example 典范 语法become the coach of；a true example of...。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍作者最喜欢的运动员埃里克·莫桑巴尼——赤道几内亚首位奥运游泳选手。他在 2000 年悉尼奥运会参加 100 米自由泳，但赛前仅 8 个月才开始游泳，唯一能用的泳池是约 13 米长的酒店泳池，每天只能用一小时；他第一次见到 50 米奥运泳池就是在奥运会上。比赛中前 50 米游得不错，最后 15 米非常艰难，看上去快要溺水，但他坚持完成，赢得观众欢呼。他虽未获奖牌，却从未放弃；后来 100 米成绩从 1 分 52 秒提高到 57 秒以内，还成为国家队教练，是奥林匹克精神的真正典范。

English Summary

The passage introduces the writer's favourite athlete, Eric Moussambani, the first Olympic swimmer from Equatorial Guinea. He swam the 100-metre freestyle at the 2000 Sydney Olympics, but had begun swimming only eight months before the Games. His only pool was a hotel pool about 13 metres long, and he could use it for just one hour each morning; he first saw a 50-metre Olympic pool at the Olympics. In the race he swam well for the first 50 metres, but the last 15 metres were very hard and he looked like drowning. He kept going and finished, winning loud cheers. He did not win a medal, but never gave up; later he improved his 100-metre time from 1:52 to under 57 seconds and became the national swimming coach—a true example of the Olympic spirit.
