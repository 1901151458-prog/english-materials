Section A · 3a · Talking About Memory

Section A · 3a 主题：记忆与遗忘 · 对话

Chen Jie: Why do you look worried, Rick?

你为什么看起来很担心，Rick？

词汇worried 担心的 语法Why do you look + 形容词? 询问原因和状态。

Rick: Yesterday I visited my grandma, but she kept calling me Johnny.

昨天我去看望奶奶，可她却一直叫我 Johnny。

词汇keep doing sth. 一直做某事 语法一般过去时；keep doing sth. 表示“反复、一直做某事”。

Chen Jie: Who's Johnny?

Johnny 是谁？

词汇who 谁 语法Who's = Who is；特殊疑问句。

Rick: My dad!

我爸爸！

词汇dad 爸爸 语法省略句，完整意思是 He is my dad.。

Chen Jie: Oh, dear!

哦，天哪！

词汇oh dear 天哪 语法Oh, dear! 表示惊讶或同情。

Rick: And then she told me not to eat the cookies because she was saving them for her grandson.

然后她让我别吃那些饼干，因为她要把饼干留给她的孙子。

词汇tell sb. not to do sth. 告诉某人不要做某事；save 留；cookie 饼干；grandson 孙子 语法tell sb. (not) to do sth.；because 引导原因状语从句。

Chen Jie: Who's that?

那是谁？

词汇that 那个 语法Who's that? 询问某人身份。

Rick: Me!

就是我！

词汇me 我（宾格） 语法省略句，完整意思是 It's me!。

Chen Jie: Well, that's not a good sign.

嗯，这可不是好兆头。

词汇sign 迹象；兆头 语法that's = that is；a good sign 表示“好兆头”。

Rick: No.

是的（不是好兆头）。

词汇no 不 语法No. 在此表示同意对方的否定说法。

Rick: My parents are taking her to hospital tomorrow.

我父母明天要带她去医院。

词汇take sb. to hospital 带某人去医院 语法现在进行时表示按计划即将发生的动作。

Chen Jie: Good.

很好。

词汇good 好的 语法简短评价。

Chen Jie: Follow what the doctor says.

要听医生的话。

词汇follow 听从；doctor 医生 语法祈使句；what the doctor says 是宾语从句。

Chen Jie: She may need special care.

她可能需要特殊的照料。

词汇may 可能；special care 特殊照料 语法may + 动词原形表示可能性。

Rick: Yeah.

是的。

词汇yeah 是的（口语） 语法口语中的肯定应答。

Rick: I just feel so sad.

我只是感到很难过。

词汇sad 难过的 语法feel + 形容词，表示感受。

Rick: I don't understand what she's going through.

我不明白她正在经历什么。

词汇understand 理解；go through 经历 语法what she's going through 是宾语从句。

Chen Jie: Well, you know how some people's memory gets worse as they get older.

嗯，你知道有些人的记忆会随着年龄增长而变差。

词汇memory 记忆；get worse 变差 语法as 引导时间状语从句，表示“随着”。

Chen Jie: It happened to my grandpa too.

我爷爷也是这样。

词汇happen to sb. 发生在某人身上；grandpa 爷爷 语法happen to sb. 表示“发生在某人身上”。

Rick: Oh, no.

哦，不。

词汇oh no 哦不 语法表示难过或惋惜。

Rick: What happened?

发生了什么？

词汇happen 发生 语法What happened? 询问发生了什么事。

Chen Jie: We used to fly kites in the park.

我们过去常在公园里放风筝。

词汇used to do sth. 过去常常做某事；fly kites 放风筝 语法used to do sth. 表示“过去常常做某事”。

Chen Jie: But now he forgets how to reach the park.

但现在他忘了怎么去公园。

词汇forget 忘记；reach 到达 语法how to reach the park 是“疑问词 + 不定式”结构。

Chen Jie: So, I walk him there every weekend.

所以，我每个周末都陪他走到那里。

词汇walk sb. there 陪某人走到那里 语法so 表示结果；every weekend 表示频率。

Rick: That's sweet!

真贴心！

词汇sweet 贴心的 语法That's + 形容词，表示评价。

Chen Jie: Thanks.

谢谢。

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法口语中的感谢用语。

Chen Jie: Instead of what he forgets, I keep in mind what he remembers.

我不去想他忘了什么，而是记住他记得什么。

词汇instead of 而不是；keep in mind 记住 语法instead of 表示“而不是”；keep sth. in mind 表示“记住”。

Chen Jie: In your case, your grandma still remembers that you love cookies!

在你奶奶的情况下，她还记得你爱吃饼干！

词汇in one's case 在某人情况下；remember 记得 语法in one's case 表示“在……情况下”；that 引导宾语从句。

Rick: You're right!

你说得对！

词汇right 正确的 语法You're right! 表示赞同。

Rick: Thanks, Chen Jie!

谢谢你，Chen Jie！

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法感谢用语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P34 Section A · 3a 中 Chen Jie 和 Rick 的对话（3a 要求：读对话并完成表格）。Rick 看起来心事重重，Chen Jie 问他为什么担心。Rick 说昨天去看奶奶，奶奶却一直叫他 Johnny；原来 Johnny 是他爸爸的名字。奶奶还让他别吃饼干，说要留给孙子——Rick 自己就是孙子。Chen Jie 说这不是好兆头；Rick 说父母明天要带奶奶去医院。Chen Jie 让他听医生的话，奶奶可能需要特殊照料。Rick 很难过，说不明白奶奶正在经历什么。Chen Jie 说她知道有些人的记忆会随着年龄增长而变差，她爷爷也是这样：他们过去常在公园放风筝，现在爷爷忘了怎么去公园，所以她每个周末都陪他走过去。Rick 觉得很贴心。Chen Jie 说，与其想着爷爷忘了什么，不如记住他记得什么——比如 Rick 的奶奶还记得他爱吃饼干。Rick 赞同并感谢她。

English Summary

This is the conversation between Chen Jie and Rick in Section A · 3a on page 34; in 3a, students read the conversation and complete the table. Rick looks worried, so Chen Jie asks him why. Rick says that when he visited his grandma yesterday, she kept calling him Johnny — his dad's name. She also told him not to eat the cookies because she was saving them for her grandson, who is Rick himself. Chen Jie says that is not a good sign, and Rick says his parents are taking his grandma to hospital tomorrow. Chen Jie tells him to follow what the doctor says because she may need special care. Rick feels sad and says he doesn't understand what his grandma is going through. Chen Jie says that some people's memory gets worse as they get older, and that it happened to her grandpa too: they used to fly kites in the park, but now he forgets how to reach the park, so she walks him there every weekend. Rick thinks that is sweet. Chen Jie says that instead of what he forgets, she keeps in mind what he remembers — in Rick's case, his grandma still remembers that he loves cookies. Rick agrees and thanks her.

语法点 Grammar

Why do you look worried, Rick?

Why + do + 主语 + 动词原形? 询问原因；look 是系动词。

Yesterday I visited my grandma, but she kept calling me Johnny.

一般过去时；keep doing sth. 表示“反复、一直做某事”。

And then she told me not to eat the cookies because she was saving them for her grandson.

tell sb. (not) to do sth.；because 引导原因状语从句；过去进行时 was saving。

Well, that's not a good sign.

that's = that is；a good sign 表示“好兆头”。

My parents are taking her to hospital tomorrow.

现在进行时表示按计划即将发生的动作。

Follow what the doctor says.

祈使句；what the doctor says 是宾语从句。

She may need special care.

may + 动词原形表示可能性。

I don't understand what she's going through.

what she's going through 是宾语从句；go through 表示“经历”。

Well, you know how some people's memory gets worse as they get older.

as 引导时间状语从句，表示“随着”；get worse 表示“变差”。

It happened to my grandpa too.

happen to sb. 表示“发生在某人身上”。

We used to fly kites in the park.

used to do sth. 表示“过去常常做某事”。

But now he forgets how to reach the park.

how to reach the park 是“疑问词 + 不定式”结构。

So, I walk him there every weekend.

so 表示结果；walk sb. there 表示“陪某人走到那里”。

Instead of what he forgets, I keep in mind what he remembers.

instead of 表示“而不是”；keep sth. in mind 表示“记住”。

In your case, your grandma still remembers that you love cookies!

in one's case 表示“在……情况下”；that 引导宾语从句。

tell sb. (not) to do sth. 表示“告诉某人（不要）做某事”；because 引导原因状语从句。

Well, you know how some people's memory gets worse as they get older.

嗯，你知道有些人的记忆会随着年龄增长而变差。

how 引导宾语从句；as 引导时间状语从句，表示“随着”。

Section B 1b · How to Boost Your Memory

Section B 1b 主题：记忆方法 · 健康生活（教材 P36–P37 两页）

Would you like to improve your memory?

你想改善自己的记忆吗？

词汇improve 改善；memory 记忆 语法Would you like to do sth.? 礼貌地询问意愿。

To start with, it is important to understand how memory works.

首先，理解记忆是如何运作的很重要。

词汇to start with 首先；work 运作 语法it is + 形容词 + to do sth.；how 引导宾语从句。

There are two main kinds of memory: short-term memory and long-term memory.

记忆主要有两种：短时记忆和长时记忆。

词汇short-term 短期的；long-term 长期的 语法There are + 复数名词；冒号后解释说明。

Short-term memory helps you remember things that you may only need for a short time, like phone numbers when you need to make a phone call.

短时记忆帮助你记住那些你可能只需要很短时间的东西，比如打电话时需要的电话号码。

词汇only 只；phone number 电话号码 语法that 引导定语从句；like 表示“例如”。

Long-term memory is the ability to remember things for days, years, or even a lifetime.

长时记忆是把事情记住几天、几年甚至一生的能力。

词汇ability 能力；lifetime 一生 语法the ability to do sth. 表示“做某事的能力”。

So the real question is: How do you make sure what you learn enters your long-term memory?

所以真正的问题是：你如何确保所学的东西进入长时记忆？

词汇make sure 确保；enter 进入 语法make sure + 从句；what you learn 是宾语从句。

By using some clever memory techniques and leading a healthy life, you will see some great results.

通过使用一些巧妙的记忆技巧并保持健康的生活，你会看到很好的效果。

词汇clever 巧妙的；technique 技巧；lead a ... life 过……的生活 语法by doing sth. 表示方式；and 连接两个动名词。

Let's take a look.

我们来看看。

词汇take a look 看一看 语法Let's + 动词原形，表示建议。

First, pay attention.

第一，要集中注意力。

词汇pay attention 集中注意力 语法祈使句，用动词原形开头。

However, you might fail to remember all the details.

然而，你可能记不住所有细节。

词汇however 然而；fail to do sth. 未能做某事；detail 细节 语法might + 动词原形表示可能性；fail to do sth.。

So, try taking notes and writing down key points.

所以，试着记笔记并写下要点。

词汇take notes 记笔记；key point 要点 语法try doing sth. 表示“尝试做某事”。

After all, it is easier to remember a summary than every word in a passage.

毕竟，记住一份摘要比记住一篇文章里的每个词更容易。

词汇after all 毕竟；summary 摘要 语法it is + 形容词比较级 + to do sth.；than 表示比较。

Second, use it or lose it.

第二，不用就会忘。

词汇use it or lose it 不用就会忘 语法祈使句；or 表示“否则”。

The more you review what you learn, the more likely you will remember it.

你复习所学内容越多，就越可能记住它。

词汇review 复习；likely 可能的 语法the more ... the more ... 表示“越……越……”。

However, you should not wear out your brain.

然而，你不应该让大脑过度疲劳。

词汇wear out 使疲劳；brain 大脑 语法should not + 动词原形表示建议。

Try studying for 40 minutes, and then take a break.

试着学习 40 分钟，然后休息一下。

词汇take a break 休息一下 语法try doing sth.；and then 表示先后顺序。

You can also review information by discussing it with others or by teaching others.

你也可以通过与他人讨论或教别人来复习信息。

词汇discuss 讨论；teach 教 语法by doing sth. 表示方式；or 连接两个并列方式。

A third way is to make use of senses.

第三种方法是利用感官。

词汇make use of 利用；sense 感官 语法a third way 表示“第三种方法”；to do 作表语。

Create images by using tables, graphs, and mind maps to organize information.

通过使用表格、图表和思维导图来组织信息，创造视觉图像。

词汇image 图像；graph 图表；mind map 思维导图 语法by doing sth. 表示方式；to organize 表示目的。

You can also create a story with information and view it in your mind like a film.

你也可以用信息编一个故事，在脑海中像看电影一样观看它。

词汇view 观看 语法and 连接并列谓语；like a film 表示方式。

In addition, make use of your other senses.

此外，还要利用你的其他感官。

词汇in addition 此外 语法in addition 表示“此外”；祈使句。

For example, listening to recorded information can be very effective.

例如，听录制好的信息可能非常有效。

词汇recorded 录制的；effective 有效的 语法动名词短语作主语；can + 动词原形表示可能。

Last but not least, boost your memory by staying healthy.

最后但同样重要的是，通过保持健康来增强记忆。

词汇last but not least 最后但同样重要；boost 增强 语法by doing sth. 表示方式。

Sleep well.

睡得好。

词汇sleep 睡觉 语法祈使句。

While you sleep, your brain organizes your memories.

在你睡觉时，大脑会整理你的记忆。

词汇while 当……时；organize 整理 语法while 引导时间状语从句。

Bad sleep makes it harder for new information to enter your long-term memory.

睡眠不好会让新信息更难进入你的长时记忆。

词汇bad sleep 睡眠不好 语法make it + 形容词 + for sb./sth. + to do sth. 句型。

Eat well.

吃得好。

词汇eat 吃 语法祈使句。

Your brain needs food like an engine needs fuel.

你大脑需要食物，就像发动机需要燃料一样。

词汇engine 发动机；fuel 燃料 语法like 引导方式状语从句。

Healthy food and drink will help your brain work much better.

健康的食物和饮料会帮助你的大脑更好地工作。

词汇healthy 健康的 语法help sb. do sth.；much 修饰比较级。

Exercise.

锻炼。

词汇exercise 锻炼 语法祈使句。

A healthy and active body boosts both your memory and your mind.

健康而活跃的身体既能增强记忆力，也有益于头脑。

词汇active 活跃的；boost 增强；mind 头脑 语法both ... and ... 连接两个并列宾语。

Many more memory techniques can be found in books or online.

在书本或网络上可以找到更多记忆技巧。

词汇be found 被发现 语法被动语态 can be found。

Try some different methods to see which ones work best for you.

尝试一些不同的方法，看看哪些对你最有效。

词汇method 方法；work 起作用 语法to see 表示目的；which ones work best 是宾语从句。

You are sure to improve your memory!

你一定能改善自己的记忆！

词汇be sure to do sth. 一定做某事 语法be sure to do sth. 表示“一定能……”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P36–P37 两页 Section B 1b 的文章《How to Boost Your Memory》（如何增强记忆）。文章先问读者是否想改善记忆，并指出先要理解记忆如何运作。记忆主要有两种：短时记忆和长时记忆——短时记忆帮助我们记住只需短暂保留的信息（如打电话时的电话号码），长时记忆则是把事情记住几天、几年甚至一生的能力。真正的问题是：如何确保所学内容进入长时记忆？作者说，使用巧妙的记忆技巧并保持健康生活，就会有好效果。接着给出具体方法：第一，集中注意力，记笔记、写下要点，因为记住摘要比记住每个词容易；第二，多用才能记住，复习越多越可能记住，但不要让大脑过度疲劳，可以学 40 分钟后休息，也可以通过讨论或教别人来复习；第三，利用感官，用表格、图表、思维导图组织信息，把信息编成故事在脑海中像电影一样观看，还可以利用其他感官，比如听录音很有效；最后但同样重要的是保持健康——睡好（睡觉时大脑整理记忆，睡眠不好会妨碍新信息进入长时记忆）、吃好（大脑需要食物就像发动机需要燃料）、锻炼（健康活跃的身体能增强记忆和头脑）。文章最后说，书本或网络上还有更多记忆技巧，多尝试不同的方法，你一定能改善记忆。

English Summary

This is the article "How to Boost Your Memory" in Section B 1b, on pages 36–37. It first asks whether you would like to improve your memory and says that, to start with, it is important to understand how memory works. Last but not least, boost your memory by staying healthy: sleep well, because while you sleep your brain organizes your memories and bad sleep makes it harder for new information to enter long-term memory; eat well, because the brain needs food like an engine needs fuel and healthy food and drink help the brain work better; and exercise, because a healthy and active body boosts both memory and mind. This is the Reading Plus article "Brain Sports" on page 86. It first asks how good your memory is and how many words you can memorize in 15 minutes; some people can memorize 335 words, and even more amazingly, someone once memorized the order of 2,530 playing cards in just one hour. Just as there are sports competitions using our bodies, there are also competitions using our minds, such as the World Memory Championships, which were held in Wuhan, China, in 2019. There were many different events: for instance, the "brain athletes" had to remember as many names and faces as they could, or as many words as possible, in 15 minutes. While you might think these competitors were all born with a strong memory, this is often not true: for example, one champion has an average memory but makes full use of memory techniques, so if he wants to remember the names of the Great Lakes in the US, he thinks of HOMES because each letter in HOMES stands for a different lake. Many competitors use what is called a "memory palace" to remember things: while most people are terrible at remembering lists, they are quite good at remembering where things are. Think about your bedroom — you can probably picture where everything is; now picture the things on the list in different places in your bedroom, and later, if you want to remember the words, all you need to do is remember the picture. Finally, the article says memory competitions are not for everyone and not everyone is a brain athlete, but on the other hand, some of their memory techniques may be useful in your studies, so you should give them a try!
