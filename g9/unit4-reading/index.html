Section A 3a · A talk about Grandma's memory

Section A 3a 主题：记忆与关怀 · 对话

Chen Jie: Why do you look worried, Rick?

里克，你怎么看起来忧心忡忡？

词汇worried 担心的；look 看起来 语法Why 引导特殊疑问句；look + adj. 表示“看起来……”。

Rick: Yesterday I visited my grandma, but she kept calling me Johnny.

昨天我去看奶奶，可她一直叫我 Johnny。

词汇visit 看望；keep doing 一直做；call sb. sth. 叫某人…… 语法一般过去时 visited；keep doing sth. 表示反复/持续；but 转折。

Chen Jie: Who's Johnny?

Johnny 是谁？

词汇who 谁 语法Who's = Who is；特殊疑问句。

Rick: My dad!

是我爸爸！

词汇dad 爸爸 语法省略回答，完整为 Johnny is my dad.

Chen Jie: Oh, dear!

天哪！

词汇oh dear 天哪（表示惊讶/难过） 语法感叹表达。

Rick: And then she told me not to eat the cookies because she was saving them for her grandson.

然后她还让我别吃那些饼干，因为她是留给她孙子的。

词汇tell sb. not to do 告诉某人不要做；cookie 饼干；save 保留；grandson 孙子 语法tell sb. not to do sth.；because 引导原因状语从句；was saving 是过去进行时。

Chen Jie: Who's that?

她孙子是谁？

词汇that 指 her grandson 语法Who's that? 询问身份。

Rick: Me!

就是我！

词汇me 我（宾格） 语法省略回答，完整为 That's me.

Chen Jie: Well, that's not a good sign.

唉，这可不是个好兆头。

词汇sign 迹象；good sign 好兆头 语法that's = that is；not a good sign 作表语。

Rick: No. My parents are taking her to hospital tomorrow.

是啊。我爸妈明天要带她去医院。

词汇take sb. to 带某人去；hospital 医院；tomorrow 明天 语法现在进行时 are taking 表示按计划将要发生；tomorrow 表将来。

Chen Jie: Good. Follow what the doctor says. She may need special care.

这样好。听从医生的建议。她可能需要特别护理。

词汇follow 听从；doctor 医生；special care 特别护理 语法祈使句 Follow...；what the doctor says 是宾语从句；may 表示推测。

Rick: Yeah. I just feel so sad. I don't understand what she's going through.

嗯。我就是很难过。我不明白她正在经历什么。

词汇feel sad 感到难过；understand 理解；go through 经历 语法what she's going through 是宾语从句；现在进行时表示正在经历。

Chen Jie: Well, you know how some people's memory gets worse as they get older. It happened to my grandpa too.

你知道的，有些人年纪大了记忆力会变差。我爷爷也是这样。

词汇memory 记忆；get worse 变差；as 随着；happen to 发生在……身上 语法how 引导宾语从句；as 引导时间状语从句；get + 形容词表示变化。

Rick: Oh, no. What happened?

啊，不会吧。怎么回事？

词汇happen 发生 语法What happened? 询问发生了什么。

Chen Jie: We used to fly kites in the park. But now he forgets how to reach the park. So, I walk him there every weekend.

我们以前常去公园放风筝。可现在他忘了怎么去公园。所以我每个周末都陪他走路去。

词汇used to do 过去常常；fly kites 放风筝；forget how to do 忘记如何做；walk sb. 陪某人走 语法used to 与 now 对比；how to reach 作 forget 的宾语；so 连接结果。

Rick: That's sweet!

你真贴心！

词汇sweet 贴心的；甜美的 语法That's + adj. 表示评价。

Chen Jie: Thanks. Instead of what he forgets, I keep in mind what he remembers. In your case, your grandma still remembers that you love cookies!

谢谢。我不去想他忘了什么，而是记住他还记得什么。就你来说，你奶奶还记着你爱吃饼干呢！

词汇instead of 而不是；keep in mind 记住；in one's case 就某人而言；remember 记得 语法instead of + 名词/从句；keep in mind + 宾语；that 引导宾语从句。

Rick: You're right! Thanks, Chen Jie!

你说得对！谢谢陈洁！

词汇right 正确的 语法You're right. 表示同意。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕 Rick 奶奶的记忆问题展开。奶奶把 Rick 叫成 Johnny，还把留给孙子的饼干说成是给别人的，Rick 因此担心。Chen Jie 安慰他，并用自己的经历说明：爷爷的记忆也在变差，忘了怎么去公园，但她每个周末陪他走路去。她建议 Rick 不要只盯着奶奶忘了什么，而要记住她还记得什么——比如她仍记得 Rick 爱吃饼干。

English Summary

In the conversation, Rick is worried about his grandma's memory. She calls him Johnny and says the cookies are saved for her grandson, which confuses Rick. Chen Jie comforts him and shares her own experience: her grandpa's memory is getting worse and he forgets how to reach the park, so she walks him there every weekend. She suggests focusing not on what he forgets but on what he remembers—for example, Rick's grandma still remembers that he loves cookies.

Section B 1b · How to Boost Your Memory

Section B 1b 主题：记忆方法 · 说明文

Would you like to improve your memory?

你想提高记忆力吗？

词汇would like to do 想要做；improve 提高；memory 记忆 语法Would you like to do...? 礼貌问句。

To start with, it is important to understand how memory works.

首先，了解记忆是如何运作的很重要。

词汇to start with 首先；understand 理解；work 运作 语法to start with 作插入语；It is + adj. + to do；how 引导宾语从句。

There are two main kinds of memory: short-term memory and long-term memory.

记忆主要有两种：短时记忆和长时记忆。

词汇main 主要的；kind 种类；short-term 短期的；long-term 长期的 语法There be 句型；two main kinds of...；冒号后解释。

Short-term memory helps you remember things that you may only need for a short time, like phone numbers when you need to make a phone call.

短时记忆帮你记住可能只需保留很短时间的信息，比如打电话时需要的电话号码。

词汇help sb. do 帮助某人做；short time 短时间；phone number 电话号码；make a phone call 打电话 语法that 引导定语从句；like 举例；when 引导时间状语从句。

Long-term memory is the ability to remember things for days, years, or even a lifetime.

长时记忆是把事情记住几天、几年甚至一辈子的能力。

词汇ability 能力；lifetime 一生 语法the ability to do sth.；for days/years 表示时间跨度。

So the real question is: How do you make sure what you learn enters your long-term memory?

所以真正的问题是：怎样才能让你学到的东西进入长时记忆？

词汇real 真正的；make sure 确保；enter 进入 语法is 后接表语从句；what you learn 作 make sure 的宾语从句；how 引导问句。

By using some clever memory techniques and leading a healthy life, you will see some great results.

运用一些巧妙的记忆技巧并保持健康生活，你会看到明显效果。

词汇by doing 通过做；technique 技巧；lead a healthy life 过健康生活；result 结果 语法by + 动名词作方式状语；and 连接两个动名词短语。

Let's take a look.

我们来看看。

词汇take a look 看一看 语法Let's do sth. 祈使句。

First, pay attention.

首先要专心。

词汇pay attention 注意 语法祈使句。

However, you might fail to remember all the details.

不过你可能无法记住所有细节。

词汇however 然而；fail to do 未能做；detail 细节 语法might 表示可能；fail to do sth.。

So try to take notes and write down key points.

所以试着记笔记、写要点。

词汇take notes 记笔记；write down 写下；key point 要点 语法try to do；and 连接两个动词短语。

After all, it is easier to remember a summary than every word in a passage.

毕竟，记住一段文章的概要比记住每个词更容易。

词汇after all 毕竟；summary 概要；passage 文章 语法It is + adj. + to do；easier...than... 比较级。

Second, use it or lose it.

第二，不用则废。

词汇use 使用；lose 失去 语法祈使句；or 表示“否则”。

The more you review what you learn, the more likely you will remember it.

你复习所学内容越多，记住的可能性就越大。

词汇review 复习；likely 可能的 语法the + 比较级..., the + 比较级...；what you learn 是宾语从句。

However, you should not wear out your brain.

但不要过度用脑。

词汇wear out 使疲惫；brain 大脑 语法should not do；wear out 固定短语。

Try studying for 40 minutes and then take a break.

可以学 40 分钟休息一下。

词汇try doing 尝试做；take a break 休息 语法try doing sth.；and then 连接顺序。

You can also review information by discussing it with others or teaching someone else.

也可以通过和别人讨论或教别人来复习信息。

词汇review 复习；discuss 讨论；teach 教 语法by + 动名词；or 连接两个动名词短语。

Third, make use of your senses.

第三，利用感官。

词汇make use of 利用；sense 感官 语法祈使句。

Create images by using tables, graphs, and mind maps to organize information.

用表格、图表、思维导图制作图像来整理信息。

词汇create 创造；image 图像；table 表格；graph 图表；mind map 思维导图；organize 整理 语法by + 动名词；to organize 作目的状语。

You can also create a story with information and view it in your mind like a film.

也可以把信息编成故事，在脑海里像看电影一样回放。

词汇create 创造；view 观看；in one's mind 在脑海中；film 电影 语法can do；and 连接并列谓语；like a film 作方式状语。

In addition, make use of your other senses.

此外，调动其他感官。

词汇in addition 此外；other 其他的 语法in addition 作连接词；祈使句。

For example, listening to recorded information can be very effective.

比如，听录音资料会非常有效。

词汇for example 例如；recorded 录制的；effective 有效的 语法动名词 listening 作主语；can be + adj.。

Last but not least, boost your memory by staying healthy.

最后同样重要的是，靠保持健康来提升记忆。

词汇last but not least 最后同样重要的是；boost 提升；stay healthy 保持健康 语法by + 动名词；祈使句。

Sleep well.

睡好。

词汇sleep 睡觉 语法祈使句；well 是副词。

While you sleep, your brain organizes your memories.

睡觉时大脑会整理记忆。

词汇while 当……时；brain 大脑；organize 整理 语法while 引导时间状语从句。

Bad sleep makes it harder for new information to enter your long-term memory.

睡眠差会让新信息更难进入长时记忆。

词汇bad sleep 睡眠差；make it harder 使……更难；enter 进入 语法make + it + adj. + for sb./sth. + to do；it 是形式宾语。

Eat well.

吃好。

词汇eat 吃 语法祈使句。

Your brain needs food like an engine needs fuel.

大脑需要食物，就像发动机需要燃料。

词汇need 需要；engine 发动机；fuel 燃料 语法like 引导方式状语从句（非正式）。

Healthy food and drink will help your brain work much better.

健康饮食能让大脑运转得更好。

词汇healthy 健康的；help sb. do 帮助某人做；work 运转 语法help sb. do sth.；much better 比较级。

Exercise.

多运动。

词汇exercise 锻炼 语法祈使句/名词独词句。

A healthy and active body boosts both your memory and your mind.

健康有活力的身体能提升记忆和思维。

词汇active 活跃的；boost 提升；mind 思维 语法both...and... 连接并列宾语。

Exercise also reduces stress and anxiety.

运动还能减轻压力和焦虑。

词汇reduce 减少；stress 压力；anxiety 焦虑 语法一般现在时；also 表示“还”。

Many more memory techniques can be found in books or online.

书和网上还有更多记忆技巧。

词汇technique 技巧；online 在网上 语法被动语态 can be found；in books or online 作地点状语。

Try some different methods to see which ones work best for you.

尝试不同方法，看看哪些最适合你。

词汇method 方法；work 起作用；best 最好 语法try sth.；to see 作目的状语；which ones 引导宾语从句。

You are sure to improve your memory!

你一定能提高记忆力！

词汇be sure to do 一定做；improve 提高 语法be sure to do sth. 表示肯定判断。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍提高记忆力的方法。先说明记忆分为短时记忆和长时记忆，真正的问题是让所学内容进入长时记忆。方法包括：专心、记笔记和要点；及时复习，但不要过度用脑；利用感官，用表格、图表、思维导图、故事和录音；保持健康，睡好、吃好、多运动。最后鼓励尝试不同方法，找到最适合自己的。

English Summary

The passage explains how to improve memory. It first introduces short-term and long-term memory, and the key question is how to get what we learn into long-term memory. Methods include paying attention and taking notes; reviewing before we forget but not wearing out the brain; using our senses with tables, graphs, mind maps, stories and recordings; and staying healthy with good sleep, food and exercise. It ends by encouraging readers to try different methods and find what works best.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

比利在日记中记录自己备战演讲的经历。过去他一演讲就紧张，花很多时间背稿却仍会忘词。上周他决定寻找正确方法：上网查资料、问朋友，学会了写演讲提纲、先记关键词并在脑海中形成图像、按顺序串联；把演讲分成小段，逐段练习直到能完全复述；最后朋友告诉他要自信，忘词时别慌，记住关键词继续讲。最终他的演讲非常成功。

English Summary

Billy's diary records how he prepared for a speech. In the past, he felt stressed and spent a lot of time memorizing every word, but still forgot parts. Last week he looked for the right method: he learned to make an outline, remember key words first, form images of them and link them in order; he divided the speech into short sections and practised each until he could recall it fully. A friend also reminded him to be confident and not to panic. In the end, his speech was a great success.
