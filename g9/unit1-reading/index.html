Section A · 3a · A Changing Hometown

Section A · 3a 主题：家乡变化 · 访谈

Reporter: How has life changed in your hometown over the last 20 years?

过去 20 年你家乡的生活发生了哪些变化？

词汇over the last 20 years 在过去 20 年里 语法现在完成时 How has ... changed ...? 询问从过去到现在的变化。

Mr Yan: Oh, it has changed quite a lot!

哦，变化可大了！

词汇quite a lot 相当多 语法现在完成时 has changed；quite a lot 表示程度。

Mr Yan: Many young people used to work in big cities.

许多年轻人过去在大城市工作。

词汇used to do sth. 过去常常做某事 语法used to do sth. 表示“过去常常做（现在不做了）”。

Mr Yan: Now, more of them have come back to live and work here.

现在，他们中更多的人回来生活和工作了。

词汇come back 回来 语法现在完成时 have come back；to live and work 表示目的。

Reporter: Great!

太好了！

词汇great 很好的 语法简短的感叹，表示赞许。

Reporter: Have there been any other changes?

还有别的变化吗？

词汇other 其他的 语法There be 句型的现在完成时 Have there been ...?。

Ms Wu: Yes, we've changed the way we farm.

是的，我们改变了耕作方式。

词汇the way 方式；farm 耕作 语法the way + 从句表示“……的方式”；现在完成时 have changed。

Ms Wu: I used to grow a few vegetables on a small farm to sell at the local market.

我过去在小农场上种少量蔬菜，卖到当地市场。

词汇a few 少量；local market 当地市场 语法used to do sth.；to sell 表示目的。

Ms Wu: Now, I have large, high-tech greenhouses.

现在，我有了大型高科技温室。

词汇high-tech 高科技的；greenhouse 温室 语法一般现在时表示现在的状态；high-tech 作定语。

Ms Wu: There, I grow tons of fruit and vegetables, and sell them in large cities.

在那里，我种植大量水果蔬菜，并把它们卖到大城市。

词汇tons of 大量的；sell 卖 语法there 指“在温室里”；and 连接并列谓语。

Reporter: Wow, the new highway and railway must have helped a lot!

哇，新公路和铁路一定帮了大忙！

词汇highway 公路；railway 铁路 语法must have done sth. 表示对过去的肯定推测。

Mr Yan: Oh, they have!

哦，确实帮了大忙！

词汇they have 确实如此 语法简短的肯定回应，用 have 回应上文的现在完成时。

Mr Yan: Our village has become a tourist attraction.

我们村成了旅游景点。

词汇tourist attraction 旅游景点 语法现在完成时 has become 表示变化的结果。

Mr Yan: We can now sell local products to tourists.

我们现在可以把当地产品卖给游客。

词汇local products 当地产品；tourist 游客 语法sell sth. to sb. 把某物卖给某人；can + 动词原形。

Mr Yan: We also sell products online to people all over China.

我们还通过网络把产品卖给全国各地的人。

词汇online 在网上；all over China 全中国 语法online 作状语；all over China 表示范围。

Reporter: Have these changes greatly improved your lives?

这些变化大大改善了你们的生活吗？

词汇greatly 大大地；improve 改善 语法现在完成时的一般疑问句；greatly 修饰动词 improved。

Ms Wu: Yes!

是的！

词汇yes 是的 语法肯定的简短回答。

Ms Wu: We now have new houses.

我们现在有了新房子。

词汇new houses 新房子 语法一般现在时表示现在的状态。

Ms Wu: And our village has just built a new hospital and a library.

而且我们村刚刚建了新医院和图书馆。

词汇just 刚刚；hospital 医院；library 图书馆 语法现在完成时 has just built；and 连接并列宾语。

Reporter: Wonderful!

太好了！

词汇wonderful 极好的 语法感叹词，表示赞叹。

Reporter: Together you are building a new socialist village.

你们正在共同建设社会主义新农村。

词汇together 共同；socialist 社会主义的 语法现在进行时 are building 表示正在进行的动作。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P4 Section A · 3a 中记者与两位村民的访谈（3a 要求：弄清村民的生活发生了哪些变化）。记者问过去 20 年家乡的生活发生了哪些变化。严先生说变化很大：许多年轻人过去在大城市工作，现在更多人回来生活和工作。记者又问还有哪些变化；吴女士说他们改变了耕作方式——她过去在小农场上种少量蔬菜卖到当地集市，现在有了大型高科技温室，种植大量水果蔬菜并卖到大城市。记者感叹新公路和铁路一定帮了大忙；严先生说确实如此，村子成了旅游景点，他们可以把当地产品销售给游客，也通过网络把产品卖到全国各地。记者问这些变化是否大大改善了生活；吴女士说是的，他们有了新房，村里还刚刚建了新医院和图书馆。记者赞叹大家正在共同建设社会主义新农村。

English Summary

This is the interview between a reporter and two villagers in Section A · 3a on page 4; in 3a, students read the interview to find out in what ways the villagers' lives have changed. The reporter asks how life has changed in their hometown over the last 20 years. Mr Yan says it has changed quite a lot: many young people used to work in big cities, but now more of them have come back to live and work here. When the reporter asks whether there have been any other changes, Ms Wu says they have changed the way they farm — she used to grow a few vegetables on a small farm to sell at the local market, but now she has large, high-tech greenhouses where she grows tons of fruit and vegetables and sells them in large cities. The reporter says the new highway and railway must have helped a lot, and Mr Yan agrees: the village has become a tourist attraction, and they can sell local products to tourists and also online to people all over China. When the reporter asks whether these changes have greatly improved their lives, Ms Wu says yes — they now have new houses, and the village has just built a new hospital and a library. The reporter concludes that together they are building a new socialist village.

语法点 Grammar

How has life changed in your hometown over the last 20 years?

现在完成时询问从过去到现在的变化；over the last 20 years 表示时间跨度。

Oh, it has changed quite a lot!

现在完成时 has changed 表示变化已完成；quite a lot 表示程度。

Many young people used to work in big cities.

used to do sth. 表示“过去常常做某事”（现在不了）。

Now, more of them have come back to live and work here.

现在完成时 have come back；to live and work 表示目的。

Have there been any other changes?

There be 句型的现在完成时 Have there been ...?。

Yes, we've changed the way we farm.

the way + 从句表示“……的方式”；现在完成时 have changed。

Now, I have large, high-tech greenhouses.

一般现在时表示现在的状态；high-tech 作定语。

There, I grow tons of fruit and vegetables, and sell them in large cities.

there 指“在温室里”；and 连接并列谓语。

Wow, the new highway and railway must have helped a lot!

must have done sth. 表示对过去的肯定推测“一定已经……”。

Our village has become a tourist attraction.

现在完成时 has become 表示变化的结果。

We can now sell local products to tourists.

sell sth. to sb. 把某物卖给某人；can + 动词原形。

We also sell products online to people all over China.

online 作状语；all over China 表示范围。

Have these changes greatly improved your lives?

现在完成时的一般疑问句；greatly 修饰动词 improved。

Together you are building a new socialist village.

现在进行时 are building 表示正在进行的动作；together 表示“共同”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Many young people used to work in big cities.

许多年轻人过去在大城市工作。

used to do sth. 表示“过去常常做某事”，暗示现在不再如此。

Wow, the new highway and railway must have helped a lot!

哇，新公路和铁路一定帮了大忙！

must have done sth. 表示对过去情况的肯定推测，意为“一定已经……”。

Now, more of them have come back to live and work here.

现在，他们中更多的人回来生活和工作了。

现在完成时 have come back 强调结果；to live and work 是不定式表示目的。

Section B 1b · On the Fast Track

Section B 1b 主题：交通变化 · 国际合作

In 2017, the new 480-kilometre Mombasa–Nairobi railway was opened.

2017 年，全长 480 公里的蒙巴萨—内罗毕铁路开通了。

词汇railway 铁路；be opened 开通 语法一般过去时的被动语态 was opened；480-kilometre 作定语。

Before then, the only transport links between Mombasa, Kenya's main port, and Nairobi, Kenya's capital, were rough roads and an old railway line built in 1901.

在那之前，蒙巴萨（肯尼亚主要港口）与内罗毕（肯尼亚首都）之间唯一的交通联系是崎岖的道路和一条建于 1901 年的旧铁路。

词汇transport link 交通联系；main port 主要港口；capital 首都；rough 崎岖的 语法before then 表示“在那之前”；built in 1901 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

The old trains were very slow.

旧火车非常慢。

词汇slow 慢的 语法一般过去时 was/were + 形容词。

It took 10 hours to get to Nairobi from Mombasa by train.

乘火车从蒙巴萨到内罗毕要 10 个小时。

词汇take 花费（时间） 语法It takes/took + 时间 + to do sth. 表示“做某事花费多少时间”。

However, the new railway, built with China's help, has already cut the travel time between Mombasa and Nairobi to about four hours for passengers.

然而，在中国帮助下建成的新铁路，已经把两地之间的旅行时间缩短到约 4 小时。

词汇cut 缩短；travel time 旅行时间；passenger 乘客 语法built with China's help 是过去分词短语作后置定语；现在完成时 has cut。

Now, more than two million people use it every year.

现在每年有两百多万人使用它。

词汇more than 超过；million 百万 语法more than 表示“超过”；two million 表示“两百万”。

Local Kenyans have benefited a lot from the new railway.

当地肯尼亚人从新铁路中受益很多。

词汇benefit from 从……中受益 语法现在完成时 have benefited；benefit from 表示“从……中受益”。

A government worker said, "I'm very happy with the new railway built by our Chinese friends. Many changes have taken place in the town over the last few years, and the people who live here have become rich."

一位政府工作人员说：“我非常满意中国朋友修建的这条新铁路。过去几年镇上发生了很大变化，住在这里的人们变得富裕了。”

词汇government worker 政府工作人员；take place 发生 语法直接引语；be happy with 表示“对……满意”；who live here 是定语从句。

A businessman said, "My experience of using the train since 2017 has been good. It is very convenient and doesn't cost much money. I am able to get to places on time and make business deals with ease."

一位商人说：“自 2017 年以来我乘火车的体验很好。它非常方便，花费也不多。我能准时到达各地，轻松地谈生意。”

词汇businessman 商人；convenient 方便的；with ease 轻松地 语法直接引语；since 2017 与现在完成时连用；be able to do sth. 能够做某事。

The railway has also made the transport of food easier.

这条铁路还让食物运输变得更容易。

词汇transport 运输 语法现在完成时 has made；make + 宾语 + 形容词比较级。

"Food used to take hundreds of lorries to carry between the two cities, but now it takes only two or three trains," said a supermarket manager.

一位超市经理说：“过去两个城市之间运送食物需要数百辆卡车，现在只需要两三列火车。”

词汇hundreds of 数百；lorry 卡车；manager 经理 语法直接引语；used to do sth.；hundreds of 表示“数百”。

As a result, people now have more fresh food to eat.

因此，人们现在有更多新鲜食物可吃。

词汇as a result 因此；fresh 新鲜的 语法as a result 表示“因此”；to eat 作后置定语。

Although China and Kenya are far away from each other, the railway shows how different countries can work together to build a better world.

虽然中国和肯尼亚相距遥远，但这条铁路表明不同国家可以携手共建更美好的世界。

词汇although 虽然；work together 合作 语法although 引导让步状语从句；how 引导宾语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P6 Section B 1b 的文章《On the Fast Track》（驶入快车道）。文章介绍 2017 年开通的蒙巴萨—内罗毕铁路：这条 480 公里长的新铁路开通前，蒙巴萨（肯尼亚主要港口）与内罗毕（肯尼亚首都）之间只有崎岖的道路和一条建于 1901 年的旧铁路，旧火车非常慢，乘火车从蒙巴萨到内罗毕要 10 小时。而在中国帮助下建成的新铁路，已把两地之间的旅行时间缩短到约 4 小时；现在每年有两百多万人使用它。当地肯尼亚人从新铁路中受益很多：一位政府工作人员说，他为中国朋友修建的新铁路感到高兴，近几年镇上发生了很大变化，居民们变得富裕了；一位商人说，自 2017 年以来乘火车的体验很好，既方便又便宜，他能准时到达各地、轻松谈生意。铁路还让食物运输更容易：一位超市经理说，过去两个城市之间运送食物需要数百辆卡车，现在只需要两三列火车，因此人们现在能吃上更多新鲜食物。文章最后说，虽然中国和肯尼亚相距遥远，但这条铁路表明不同国家可以携手合作，共建更美好的世界。

English Summary

This is the article "On the Fast Track" in Section B 1b on page 6. It introduces the Mombasa–Nairobi railway opened in 2017. Before this new 480-kilometre railway was opened, the only transport links between Mombasa, Kenya's main port, and Nairobi, Kenya's capital, were rough roads and an old railway line built in 1901; the old trains were very slow, and it took 10 hours to get to Nairobi from Mombasa by train. However, the new railway, built with China's help, has already cut the travel time between the two cities to about four hours for passengers, and now more than two million people use it every year. Local Kenyans have benefited a lot from it: a government worker says he is very happy with the new railway built by their Chinese friends and that many changes have taken place in the town over the last few years, with local people becoming rich; a businessman says his experience of using the train since 2017 has been good because it is convenient and doesn't cost much, and he can get to places on time and make business deals with ease. The railway has also made the transport of food easier: a supermarket manager says food used to take hundreds of lorries to carry between the two cities but now takes only two or three trains, so people have more fresh food to eat. Finally, the article says that although China and Kenya are far away from each other, the railway shows how different countries can work together to build a better world.

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Before then, the only transport links between Mombasa, Kenya's main port, and Nairobi, Kenya's capital, were rough roads and an old railway line built in 1901.

在那之前，蒙巴萨（肯尼亚主要港口）与内罗毕（肯尼亚首都）之间唯一的交通联系是崎岖的道路和一条建于 1901 年的旧铁路。

built with China's help 是过去分词短语作后置定语；cut ... to ... 表示“把……缩短到……”。

with 短语表示伴随；现在进行时 are contributing / growing。

Although he is more than 70 years old, he still spends most of his days working in the sun on the farmland.

although 引导让步状语从句；spend one's days doing sth. 表示“把时间花在……上”。

He wears a wide hat, and his skin is brown from the sun.

and 连接并列分句；from the sun 表示原因。

He loves farming and has spent his life researching ways to improve it.

现在完成时 has spent；spend one's life doing sth. 表示“一生从事……”。

On his days off, Guanghui provides local farmers with technical training and guidance for free.

provide sb. with sth. 为某人提供某物；for free 表示“免费”。

With joint efforts of the government, farmers, and experts like him, the farming economy in the province has grown by more than one billion yuan over the years.

with 短语表示条件/伴随；现在完成时 has grown；grow by 表示“增长了”。

Jiang Hui's friends and fellow villagers call her the "flower girl".

call + 宾语 + 宾语补足语；名词所有格 Jiang Hui's。

When she was young, Hui often visited her aunt's flower field after school, where she learnt how to grow flowers.

when 引导时间状语从句；where 引导非限制性定语从句。

She also showed great talent in putting different flowers together beautifully.

show talent in doing sth. 表示“在……方面展现天赋”。

After graduating from university, Hui decided to return to her hometown and start her own flower business.

after doing sth. 表示“在……之后”；decide to do sth. 决定做某事。

To promote sales, she started to sell her flowers online.

to do 表示目的；start to do sth. 开始做某事。

It was hard at first, but she didn't give up.

it 指代创业这件事；give up 表示“放弃”。

Gradually, her online shop became more popular.

gradually 表示“逐渐地”；become + 形容词比较级。

As the first person from her village to graduate from university, Bailing decided to return to her hometown to become a teacher.

as 表示“作为”；the first person to do sth. 表示“第一个做某事的人”。

Though the area still needs further development, through the courage and hard work of its people—like Bailing—it has a bright future ahead.

though 引导让步状语从句；through 表示方式；have a bright future 表示“有光明的未来”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

With joint efforts of the government, farmers, and experts like him, the farming economy in the province has grown by more than one billion yuan over the years.

在政府、农民和他这样的专家的共同努力下，全省的农业经济多年来增长超过十亿元。

with 短语表示条件；现在完成时 has grown；grow by 表示“增长了（幅度）”。

When she was young, Hui often visited her aunt's flower field after school, where she learnt how to grow flowers.

小时候，慧常常放学后去姑姑的花田，在那里学会了种花。

when 引导时间状语从句；where 引导非限制性定语从句，修饰 flower field。

Though the area still needs further development, through the courage and hard work of its people—like Bailing—it has a bright future ahead.

虽然这个地区还需要进一步发展，但凭借像白玲这样的人的勇气和努力，它有着光明的未来。

though 引导让步状语从句；破折号中间 like Bailing 是插入语；have a bright future ahead 表示“前途光明”。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看 P81 的文章标题，猜一猜文章会讲哪些人建设家乡的故事。
  • 先浏览全文，找出文章介绍了哪三个人物。

读中

  • 边读边画出每个人物为家乡做了什么的句子。
  • 注意三个小标题的作用，梳理文章结构。

读后

  • 用表格整理三个人物的身份、贡献和精神。
  • 讨论：作为学生，我们可以为家乡做些什么？

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面四篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What did many young people in Mr Yan's hometown use to do?

  1. Work in big cities.
  2. Work on farms.
  3. Study abroad.
  4. Sell products online.
答案与解析

A 原文：Many young people used to work in big cities. 可知许多年轻人过去在大城市工作。

2. Which of the following is NOT a change in Ms Wu's farming?

  1. She has large, high-tech greenhouses.
  2. She grows tons of fruit and vegetables.
  3. She sells vegetables only at the local market.
  4. She sells her products in large cities.
答案与解析

C 原文：Now, I have large, high-tech greenhouses ... sell them in large cities. 可知她不再只把蔬菜卖到当地市场，因此 C 项不是变化。

3. How long did the old train take to get from Mombasa to Nairobi?

  1. About 4 hours.
  2. 10 hours.
  3. About 2 hours.
  4. More than a day.
答案与解析

B 原文：It took 10 hours to get to Nairobi from Mombasa by train. 可知旧火车要 10 小时。

4. What does the new railway show according to the article?

  1. China is a rich country.
  2. Kenya has no ports.
  3. Old trains are better.
  4. Different countries can work together to build a better world.
答案与解析

D 原文：Although China and Kenya are far away from each other, the railway shows how different countries can work together to build a better world.

5. What did the student's family move to?

  1. A smaller flat.
  2. A house near the school.
  3. A much larger flat.
  4. A house in the countryside.
答案与解析

C 原文：My family used to live in a small flat, but now we have moved to a much larger one. 可知他们搬进了大得多的公寓。

6. What stays the same in the student's life?

  1. The feeling of love and happiness shared by the family.
  2. The way of going to school.
  3. The size of the home.
  4. The school.
答案与解析

A 原文：one thing stays the same: the feeling of love and happiness shared by my family. 可知不变的是家人共享的爱与幸福。

7. Why did Yuan Bailing return to her hometown?

  1. To build roads.
  2. To sell flowers.
  3. To grow watermelons.
  4. To become a teacher and help more children.
答案与解析

D 原文：Bailing decided to return to her hometown to become a teacher. She wants to help more children get a good education ... 可知她回乡当老师、帮助更多孩子。

8. How does Jiang Hui help other villagers?

  1. She grows watermelons for them.
  2. She teaches them how to sell agricultural products online.
  3. She builds bridges for them.
  4. She gives them free hats.
答案与解析

B 原文：She also helped and taught other villagers how to sell agricultural products online. 可知她教其他村民在网上销售农产品。
