Section A 3a · 采访村民

Section A 3a 主题：家乡的变化 · 采访

Reporter: How has life changed in your hometown over the last 20 years?

过去 20 年里，你们家乡的生活发生了怎样的变化？

词汇reporter 记者；hometown 家乡；over the last 20 years 在过去的 20 年里 语法现在完成时 How has life changed...? 询问从过去到现在的变化；over the last 20 years 常与现在完成时连用。

Mr Yan: Oh, it has changed quite a lot!

哦，变化很大！

词汇quite a lot 很多；change v. 变化 语法现在完成时 has changed；it 指 life。

Mr Yan: Many young people used to work in big cities.

很多年轻人过去在大城市工作。

词汇used to do sth. 过去常常做；young people 年轻人；big cities 大城市 语法used to + 动词原形，表示过去的习惯，暗示现在不这样了。

Mr Yan: Now, more of them have come back to live and work here.

现在，他们中有更多人已经回来，在这里生活和工作。

词汇more of them 他们中更多人；come back 回来；live and work 生活和工作 语法现在完成时 have come back；to live and work 是不定式，表示回来的目的。

Reporter: Great! Have there been any other changes?

太好了！还有其他变化吗？

词汇any other 其他的；change n. 变化 语法There be 句型的现在完成时 Have there been...? 询问“是否已经有过”。

Ms Wu: Yes, we've changed the way we farm.

是的，我们改变了耕作方式。

词汇change v. 改变；the way we farm 我们的耕作方式 语法the way + 定语从句 we farm（省略 that / in which）；现在完成时 have changed。

Ms Wu: I used to grow a few vegetables on a small farm to sell at the local market.

过去我在一个小农场种一点蔬菜，拿到当地市场去卖。

词汇used to do 过去常常；grow 种植；a few 一些；local 当地的；market 市场 语法used to + 动词原形；to sell 作目的状语；on a small farm / at the local market 是介词搭配。

Ms Wu: Now, I have large, high-tech greenhouses.

现在，我有了大型的高科技温室。

词汇high-tech 高科技的；greenhouse 温室；large 大型的 语法一般现在时 have 表示现在拥有；large, high-tech 并列修饰 greenhouses。

Ms Wu: There, I grow tons of fruit and vegetables, and sell them in large cities.

在那里，我种大量水果和蔬菜，并把它们卖到大城市。

词汇tons of 大量的；fruit and vegetables 水果和蔬菜；sell...in... 在……销售 语法There 指在温室里；and 连接并列谓语 grow 和 sell；them 指 fruit and vegetables。

Reporter: Wow, the new highway and railway must have helped a lot!

哇，新的公路和铁路一定帮了大忙！

词汇highway 公路；railway 铁路；help a lot 帮助很大 语法must have + 过去分词，表示对过去情况的肯定推测；must 不是“必须”。

Mr Yan: Oh, they have!

哦，确实如此！

词汇they 指公路和铁路；have 代替 have helped 语法简略回答用 have 代替重复的现在完成时谓语。

Mr Yan: Our village has become a tourist attraction.

我们村已经成了一个旅游景点。

词汇village 村庄；become 变成；tourist attraction 旅游景点 语法现在完成时 has become，强调现在的结果；a tourist attraction 作表语。

Mr Yan: We can now sell local products to tourists.

现在我们可以把当地产品卖给游客。

词汇sell sth. to sb. 把某物卖给某人；local products 当地产品；tourist 游客 语法can + 动词原形；sell sth. to sb. 是双宾语结构。

Mr Yan: We also sell products online to people all over China.

我们还通过网购把产品卖给全中国的人。

词汇online 在网上；all over China 全中国 语法sell products online 表示网上销售；to people... 表示销售对象。

Reporter: Have these changes greatly improved your lives?

这些变化大大改善了你们的生活吗？

词汇greatly 大大地；improve 改善；lives 生活（life 的复数） 语法现在完成时 Have...improved；greatly 修饰动词 improve。

Ms Wu: Yes! We now have new houses.

是的！我们现在有了新房子。

词汇new houses 新房子 语法一般现在时 have 表示现在拥有。

Ms Wu: And our village has just built a new hospital and a library.

村里还刚刚建了一所新医院和一座图书馆。

词汇just 刚刚；build 建造；hospital 医院；library 图书馆 语法现在完成时 has built；just 常与现在完成时连用，表示“刚刚”。

Reporter: Wonderful! Together you are building a new socialist village.

太好了！你们正在一起建设社会主义新农村。

词汇together 一起；build 建设；socialist 社会主义的；village 村庄 语法现在进行时 are building 表示正在进行的动作；together 强调“共同”。

语法点 Grammar

How has life changed in your hometown over the last 20 years?

现在完成时：have/has + 过去分词，表示过去发生、对现在有影响的变化；over the last 20 years 常与现在完成时连用。

Many young people used to work in big cities. Now, more of them have come back to live and work here.

used to do 表示“过去常常做（现在不做了）”；now 与现在完成时 have come back 形成对比，突出变化。

we've changed the way we farm.

the way + 定语从句：the way we farm 相当于 the way that/in which we farm，表示“我们耕作的方式”。

Our village has become a tourist attraction.

现在完成时 has become 表示“已经变成”，强调现在的结果；a tourist attraction 作表语。

The new highway and railway must have helped a lot!

must have done 表示对过去情况的肯定推测：“一定帮了大忙”。must 在这里不是“必须”。

Together you are building a new socialist village.

现在进行时 are building 表示正在进行的建设；together 强调“共同”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Many young people used to work in big cities. Now, more of them have come back to live and work here.

很多年轻人过去在大城市工作。现在，他们中更多人已经回来，在这里生活和工作。

used to 讲过去，have come back 讲现在的结果；to live and work 是不定式，表示回来的目的。

I used to grow a few vegetables on a small farm to sell at the local market.

过去我在一个小农场种一点蔬菜，拿到当地市场去卖。

to sell 作目的状语；on a small farm 表示地点；at the local market 表示销售地点。

The new highway and railway must have helped a lot!

新的公路和铁路一定帮了大忙！

must have + 过去分词表示对过去的肯定推测，语气较强。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题和插图，猜“家乡会有什么变化”。
  • 注意文体：采访，有 reporter / Mr Yan / Ms Wu 三个说话人。

读中

  • 圈出时间：over the last 20 years。
  • 画出 used to 与 now 的对比。
  • 找出变化：工作、耕作、交通、旅游、住房、医院、图书馆。

读后

  • 用 Past / Now 两栏复述变化。
  • 和同伴分角色朗读采访。
  • 用 “used to ... Now ...” 说一处自己家乡的变化。

Section B 1b · On the Fast Track

Section B 1b 主题：蒙巴萨—内罗毕铁路 · 交通与合作

In 2017, the new 480-kilometre Mombasa-Nairobi railway was opened.

2017 年，全长 480 公里的蒙巴萨—内罗毕新铁路开通。

词汇kilometre 公里；railway 铁路；open 开通 语法一般过去时的被动语态 was opened；480-kilometre 作定语。

Before then, the only transport links between Mombasa, Kenya's main port, and Nairobi, Kenya's capital, were rough roads and an old railway line built in 1901.

在那之前，肯尼亚主要港口蒙巴萨和首都内罗毕之间唯一的交通连接，是崎岖的道路和一条建于 1901 年的旧铁路。

词汇transport 交通；link 连接；port 港口；capital 首都；rough 崎岖的；railway line 铁路线 语法主干是 the only transport links ... were ...；between A and B 连接两地；built in 1901 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

The old trains were very slow.

旧火车非常慢。

词汇old trains 旧火车；slow 慢的 语法一般过去时 were；主语 the old trains 是复数。

It took 10 hours to get to Nairobi from Mombasa by train.

从蒙巴萨乘火车到内罗毕需要 10 个小时。

词汇take 花费；get to 到达；by train 乘火车 语法It takes sb. some time to do sth. 句型；it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to get to Nairobi。

However, the new railway, built with China's help, has already cut the travel time between Mombasa and Nairobi to about four hours for passengers.

然而，在中国帮助下修建的新铁路，已经把蒙巴萨到内罗毕的旅行时间缩短到大约四小时。

词汇however 然而；with one's help 在某人的帮助下；cut...to... 把……缩短到……；travel time 旅行时间；passenger 乘客 语法built with China's help 是过去分词短语作定语；has already cut 是现在完成时；for passengers 表示“对乘客来说”。

Now, more than two million people use it every year.

现在每年有超过两百万人使用它。

词汇more than 超过；million 百万；use 使用 语法一般现在时 use 表示现在的常态；it 指新铁路。

Local Kenyans have benefited a lot from the new railway.

肯尼亚当地人从新铁路中受益匪浅。

词汇local 当地的；benefit from 从……中受益；a lot 很多 语法现在完成时 have benefited；benefit from 是固定搭配。

A government worker said, "I'm very happy with the new railway built by our Chinese friends."

一位政府工作人员说：“我对中国朋友修建的新铁路非常满意。”

词汇government 政府；worker 工作人员；be happy with 对……满意 语法直接引语；built by our Chinese friends 是过去分词短语，修饰 the new railway。

"Many changes have taken place in the town over the last few years, and the people who live here have become rich."

“过去几年，镇上发生了很多变化，住在这里的人们也富裕起来了。”

词汇take place 发生；over the last few years 在过去几年里；rich 富裕的 语法现在完成时 have taken place / have become；who live here 是定语从句，修饰 the people。

A businessman said, "My experience of using the train since 2017 has been good."

一位商人说：“从 2017 年开始乘坐这条铁路，我的体验很好。”

词汇businessman 商人；experience 经历；since 自从 语法现在完成时 has been；since 2017 常与现在完成时连用。

"It is very convenient and doesn't cost much money."

“它非常方便，花费也不多。”

词汇convenient 方便的；cost 花费 语法it 指铁路；doesn't cost 是一般现在时的否定形式。

"I am able to get to places on time and make business deals with ease."

“我能准时到达各地，轻松做成生意。”

词汇be able to 能够；on time 准时；business deal 生意；with ease 轻松地 语法be able to + 动词原形；and 连接两个并列的不定式短语。

The railway has also made the transport of food easier.

这条铁路还让食品运输变得更加容易。

词汇transport 运输；make...easier 使……更容易 语法现在完成时 has made；make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾语补足语。

"Food used to take hundreds of lorries to carry between the two cities, but now it takes only two or three trains," said a supermarket manager.

一位超市经理说：“过去在两座城市之间运送食品需要数百辆卡车，现在只需要两三列火车。”

词汇used to do 过去常常；hundreds of 数百；lorry 卡车；supermarket 超市；manager 经理 语法take...to carry 表示“运输需要……”；used to 与 now 形成对比；it 指运输食品这件事。

As a result, people now have more fresh food to eat.

结果，人们现在能吃上更多新鲜食物。

词汇as a result 结果；fresh 新鲜的 语法as a result 作结果状语；to eat 是不定式，作 food 的后置定语。

Although China and Kenya are far away from each other, the railway shows how different countries can work together to build a better world.

虽然中国和肯尼亚相距遥远，但这条铁路展示了不同国家如何携手合作，建设更美好的世界。

词汇although 虽然；far away from 远离；work together 合作；build a better world 建设更美好的世界 语法although 引导让步状语从句，不能与 but 连用；how 引导宾语从句，作 shows 的宾语。

一般过去时的被动语态：was/were + 过去分词；铁路是“被开通”的。

It took 10 hours to get to Nairobi from Mombasa by train.

It takes sb. some time to do sth. 句型；it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to get to Nairobi。

现在完成时 has cut，强调新铁路带来的结果；cut ... to ... 表示“缩短到”。

the people who live here have become rich

Although China and Kenya are far away from each other, the railway shows how different countries can work together ...

although 引导让步状语从句，不能与 but 连用；how 引导宾语从句，作 shows 的宾语。

Section B 3a · Changes in My Life

Section B 3a 主题：个人生活的变化 · 成长

Now that I am older, my life is different from when I was in primary school.

现在我长大了，我的生活和上小学时不一样了。

词汇now that 既然/现在；be different from 与……不同；primary school 小学 语法now that 引导原因/时间状语从句；from 后接 when 引导的宾语从句。

For example, my primary school was near my home, so I used to walk to school with my parents.

比如，我的小学离家很近，所以我过去常和父母一起步行上学。

词汇for example 例如；near 靠近；used to 过去常常；walk to school 步行上学 语法so 连接结果；used to + 动词原形。

Now my junior high school is farther from home.

现在我的初中离家更远。

词汇junior high school 初中；farther 更远 语法farther 是 far 的比较级；be farther from 表示“离……更远”。

I take a bus every day with my friends.

我每天和朋友们一起坐公交车。

词汇take a bus 乘公交车；every day 每天 语法一般现在时表示习惯性动作。

My home has changed too.

我的家也变了。

词汇change 变化 语法现在完成时 has changed，强调现在的结果。

My family used to live in a small flat, but now we have moved to a much larger one.

我家过去住在一套小公寓里，但现在我们搬进了一套大得多的房子。

词汇used to live 过去住；flat 公寓；move to 搬到；much larger 大得多 语法used to 与 now 形成对比；have moved 是现在完成时；one 代替 flat，避免重复。

My grandma lives with us now as well.

奶奶现在也和我们一起住。

词汇live with 和……一起住；as well 也 语法一般现在时 lives；as well 放在句末表示“也”。

She has her own room and her own TV set.

她有自己的房间和电视机。

词汇own 自己的；TV set 电视机 语法have 表示拥有；own 作形容词，修饰 room 和 TV set。

I am glad that I get to see her every day.

我很高兴每天都能见到她。

词汇be glad that 高兴；get to do 有机会做 语法that 引导宾语从句；get to see 表示“得以见到”。

The way I study is also different.

我的学习方式也不同了。

词汇the way I study 我的学习方式；different 不同的 语法the way + 定语从句 I study，修饰 the way；主句谓语是 is。

I have become more confident in my studies and often attend group discussions.

我对学习更有信心，经常参加小组讨论。

词汇become 变得；confident 自信的；be confident in 对……有信心；attend 参加；group discussion 小组讨论 语法现在完成时 have become；and 连接并列谓语；be confident in 是固定搭配。

I enjoy studying with my friends.

我喜欢和朋友们一起学习。

词汇enjoy doing 喜欢做；study 学习 语法enjoy 后接动名词 studying。

It's amazing how much we have achieved together.

我们一起取得了这么多成就，真是令人惊叹。

词汇amazing 令人惊叹的；achieve 取得；together 一起 语法it 是形式主语，how much... 是真正主语；have achieved 是现在完成时。

My life has changed in many ways, but one thing stays the same: the feeling of love and happiness shared by my family.

我的生活在许多方面发生了变化，但有一件事始终不变：家人之间共享的爱与幸福。

词汇in many ways 在许多方面；stay the same 保持不变；feeling 感觉；shared 共享的 语法but 表示转折；冒号后解释 one thing；shared by my family 是过去分词短语作后置定语；stays 用一般现在时。

语法点 Grammar

I used to walk to school with my parents. Now ... I take a bus every day.

used to do 表示过去的习惯；now 后用一般现在时，形成“过去—现在”的对比。

My home has changed too. / we have moved to a much larger one

现在完成时 has/have + 过去分词，强调变化对现在的影响；one 代替 a flat，避免重复。

my junior high school is farther from home

farther 是 far 的比较级，表示“更远”；farther from home 表示“离家更远”。

The way I study is also different.

the way + 定语从句 I study 修饰 the way，相当于 the way that/in which I study。

It's amazing how much we have achieved together.

it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 how much we have achieved together；how much 引导主语从句。have achieved 是现在完成时。

the feeling of love and happiness shared by my family

shared by my family 是过去分词短语，后置修饰 the feeling，表示“全家人共享的”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Now that I am older, my life is different from when I was in primary school.

现在我长大了，我的生活和上小学时不一样了。

now that 引导原因/时间状语从句；be different from 后接 when 引导的宾语从句。

It's amazing how much we have achieved together.

我们一起取得了这么多成就，真是令人惊叹。

it 是形式主语；how much we have achieved together 是真正主语；have achieved 用现在完成时。

My life has changed in many ways, but one thing stays the same: the feeling of love and happiness shared by my family.

我的生活在许多方面发生了变化，但有一件事始终不变：家人之间共享的爱与幸福。

but 连接转折；冒号后解释 one thing；shared by my family 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看标题 Changes in My Life，预测会从哪些方面写变化。
  • 注意时间线：primary school → junior high school。

读中

  • 找三组对比：上学方式、住房、学习方式。
  • 画出 used to 和 now 的句子。
  • 注意结尾“不变”的是什么。

读后

  • 用“变的是……，不变的是……”复述。
  • 仿写一段自己的变化。
  • 体会“成长”主题。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面三篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What does Mr Yan say about young people in his hometown?

  1. They all left for big cities.
  2. More of them have returned to live and work there.
  3. They no longer work.
  4. They only work online.
答案与解析

B 原文：Many young people used to work in big cities. Now, more of them have come back to live and work here.

2. How did Ms Wu farm in the past?

  1. She used high-tech greenhouses.
  2. She grew vegetables on a small farm and sold them at the local market.
  3. She sold products online.
  4. She worked in a big city.
答案与解析

B 原文：I used to grow a few vegetables on a small farm to sell at the local market.

3. What does “must have helped a lot” suggest?

  1. The reporter is sure the new road and railway were useless.
  2. The reporter is making a strong guess that they helped.
  3. They are still being built.
  4. They helped only a little.
答案与解析

B must have done 表示对过去情况的肯定推测，意为“一定帮了大忙”。

4. How long did the old train journey from Mombasa to Nairobi take?

  1. About 2 hours.
  2. About 4 hours.
  3. About 10 hours.
  4. About 480 hours.
答案与解析

C 原文：It took 10 hours to get to Nairobi from Mombasa by train.

5. What is one benefit of the new railway for food transport?

  1. It takes hundreds of lorries.
  2. It takes only two or three trains.
  3. It makes food more expensive.
  4. It stops people eating fresh food.
答案与解析

B 原文：Food used to take hundreds of lorries to carry ... but now it takes only two or three trains.

6. What can we infer about the railway?

  1. It was built only by Kenya.
  2. It helps Kenya and China cooperate.
  3. It is not popular with local people.
  4. It only carries tourists.
答案与解析

B 文章提到 built with China's help，结尾说不同国家可以 work together，因此可推断它促进了肯中合作。

7. Why does the writer say “one thing stays the same”?

  1. Because the writer's school has not changed.
  2. Because the family's love and happiness remain.
  3. Because the writer still walks to school.
  4. Because the grandma has no TV.
答案与解析

B 原文：one thing stays the same: the feeling of love and happiness shared by my family.

8. What is the main purpose of Section B 3a?

  1. To introduce a new railway.
  2. To show changes in the writer's life while family love remains.
  3. To teach how to farm.
  4. To describe a tourist attraction.
答案与解析

B 文章从上学方式、住房、学习方式三方面写变化，结尾点明“爱与幸福不变”，主旨是成长中的变与不变。
