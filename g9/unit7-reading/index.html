Section A · 3a · Talking About a Concert

Section A · 3a 主题：音乐会 · 音乐感受

Clarice: That was a great concert!

那真是一场很棒的音乐会！

词汇concert 音乐会 语法That was + 名词，对过去的事情作评价。

Clarice: Which performance did you like the best, Jack?

Jack，你最喜欢哪个表演？

词汇performance 表演；the best 最 语法Which + 名词 + did you like the best? 询问最喜欢哪一个。

Jack: I thought Horse Racing was especially good.

我觉得《赛马》特别好。

词汇especially 尤其 语法I thought + 宾语从句；was 是系动词。

Jack: It really gave me the feeling of a horse race.

它真的给了我赛马的感觉。

词汇feeling 感觉；race 比赛 语法give sb. sth. 双宾语结构。

Debbie: I love music which is energetic!

我喜欢充满活力的音乐！

词汇energetic 充满活力的 语法which is energetic 是定语从句，修饰 music。

Debbie: Their use of the keyboard and erhu made me imagine horses running down a track.

他们对键盘和二胡的运用让我想象马在跑道上奔跑。

词汇keyboard 键盘；erhu 二胡；imagine 想象；track 跑道 语法make sb. do sth. 使某人做某事；imagine sb. doing sth.。

Clarice: Yes, but for me, I really like music which is full of joy and celebration, such as Dance of the Golden Snake.

是的，但对我来说，我真的很喜欢充满欢乐和庆典气氛的音乐，比如《金蛇狂舞》。

词汇be full of 充满；joy 欢乐；celebration 庆祝；such as 例如 语法which 引导定语从句；such as 用于举例。

Teng Fei: The use of the erhu, pipa, and drums was exciting, but I prefer music that touches me—that's why I liked Butterfly Lovers more.

二胡、琵琶和鼓的运用很令人兴奋，但我更喜欢打动我的音乐——这就是我更喜欢《梁祝》的原因。

词汇pipa 琵琶；drum 鼓；prefer 更喜欢；touch 打动 语法that touches me 是定语从句；that's why ... 表示“这就是……的原因”。

Teng Fei: The music tells a sad and romantic story.

这首音乐讲述了一个悲伤而浪漫的故事。

词汇romantic 浪漫的 语法tells a story 表示“讲述故事”。

Jack: Yeah, and it's interesting that the performers used both Chinese and Western instruments.

是的，而且有趣的是，演奏者既用了中国乐器，也用了西方乐器。

词汇performer 演奏者；instrument 乐器 语法it is interesting that ... 句型；both ... and ... 连接并列成分。

Jack: I have never heard the erhu played together with the violin.

我从没听过二胡和小提琴一起演奏。

词汇violin 小提琴；together with 和……一起 语法现在完成时 have never heard；hear sth. done 表示“听到某物被演奏”。

Teng Fei: I also liked Voices of Spring.

我也喜欢《春之声》。

词汇Voices of Spring 《春之声》 语法also 放在实义动词前表示“也”。

Teng Fei: I never realized that you could dance to it!

我从没意识到还可以跟着它跳舞！

词汇realize 意识到；dance to 跟着……跳舞 语法realize + 宾语从句；dance to music 表示“跟着音乐跳舞”。

English Summary

This is the conversation among Clarice, Jack, Debbie and Teng Fei in Section A · 3a on page 64; in 3a, students read the conversation and circle T or F for statements about the concert. Clarice says the concert was great and asks Jack which performance he liked best. Jack thinks Horse Racing was especially good because it really gave him the feeling of a horse race. Debbie loves energetic music and says their use of the keyboard and erhu made her imagine horses running down a track. Clarice likes music full of joy and celebration, such as Dance of the Golden Snake. Teng Fei thinks the use of the erhu, pipa and drums was exciting, but he prefers music that touches him — that is why he liked Butterfly Lovers more, which tells a sad and romantic story. Jack finds it interesting that the performers used both Chinese and Western instruments, and he has never heard the erhu played together with the violin. Teng Fei also liked Voices of Spring and never realized that you could dance to it.

I thought Horse Racing was especially good.

I thought + 宾语从句；was 是系动词。

which is energetic 是定语从句，修饰 music。

it is interesting that ... 句型；both ... and ... 连接并列成分。

Butterfly Lovers tells a sad and romantic story.

Section B 1b · Chopin: A Great Inspiration

Section B 1b 主题：肖邦 · 音乐的力量（教材 P66–P67 两页）

When the great composer Robert Schumann first heard a work which was written by young Chopin, he said, "Hats off, gentlemen! A genius!"

当伟大的作曲家罗伯特·舒曼第一次听到年轻的肖邦创作的作品时，他说：“脱帽致敬吧，先生们！一个天才！”

词汇composer 作曲家；work 作品；genius 天才 语法when 引导时间状语从句；which 引导定语从句修饰 work；Hats off 表示“脱帽致敬”。

Frédéric Chopin (1810–1849) was one of the greatest musicians in history.

弗雷德里克·肖邦（1810—1849）是历史上最伟大的音乐家之一。

词汇musician 音乐家；in history 在历史上 语法one of + 形容词最高级 + 复数名词。

During his short lifetime, he was celebrated as one of the world's greatest pianists—even though he only gave around 30 concerts!

在他短暂的一生中，他被誉为世界上最伟大的钢琴家之一——尽管他只举办了大约 30 场音乐会！

词汇lifetime 一生；celebrate 赞誉；pianist 钢琴家 语法during 表示时间；even though 引导让步状语从句。

He was also a gifted composer who left behind more than 200 musical works.

他也是一位有天赋的作曲家，留下了 200 多部音乐作品。

词汇gifted 有天赋的；leave behind 留下 语法who 引导定语从句；more than 表示“超过”。

Chopin was born in a village in Poland.

肖邦出生在波兰的一个村庄里。

词汇Poland 波兰 语法be born 表示“出生”；一般过去时。

He didn't attend music school until he was 16, but soon outdid his teachers.

他直到 16 岁才上音乐学校，但很快就超过了老师。

词汇attend 上（学）；outdid 超过（outdo 的过去式） 语法not ... until ... 表示“直到……才……”；but 表转折。

His parents sent him to Vienna in 1829 to widen his musical experience.

1829 年，父母送他去维也纳，以拓宽他的音乐视野。

词汇Vienna 维也纳；widen 拓宽；experience 经验 语法send sb. to 派某人去；to widen 表示目的。

By 1832, he had made his way to Paris, the city that he would call home for the rest of his life.

到 1832 年，他已来到巴黎，这座城市将成为他余生称为家的地方。

词汇make one's way to 前往；the rest of 剩余的 语法by + 时间与过去完成时连用；that 引导定语从句。

Chopin was not only a great pianist, but also a respected piano teacher.

肖邦不仅是一位伟大的钢琴家，也是一位受人尊敬的钢琴教师。

词汇respected 受尊敬的 语法not only ... but also ... 表示“不但……而且……”。

He often told his students, "Put all your soul into it! Play the way you feel!"

他常对学生说：“把全部心灵投入其中！按你的感觉去演奏！”

词汇soul 心灵；feel 感觉 语法直接引语；祈使句；the way you feel 表示“你感觉的方式”。

This is advice that is still followed by musicians today.

这是至今仍被音乐家们遵循的建议。

词汇advice 建议；follow 遵循 语法that 引导定语从句；被动语态 is followed。

Polish folk music was a kind of music that some people looked down on at the time—it was seen as too simple and common.

波兰民间音乐是当时有些人看不起的一种音乐——它被视为过于简单、普通。

词汇Polish 波兰的；folk music 民间音乐；look down on 看不起 语法that 引导定语从句；be seen as 表示“被视为”。

But it became an important characteristic in Chopin's music.

但它成了肖邦音乐中的一个重要特征。

词汇characteristic 特征 语法but 表转折；become + 名词表示“成为”。

His use of Polish folk music caused people to see it as a valuable art form.

他对波兰民间音乐的运用让人们把它看作一种有价值的艺术形式。

词汇cause sb. to do sth. 使某人做某事；valuable 有价值的；art form 艺术形式 语法cause sb. to do sth.；see ... as ... 表示“把……看作……”。

His music also helped build a nation which was divided up by other countries.

他的音乐还帮助建设一个被其他国家瓜分的民族。

词汇nation 民族；divide up 瓜分 语法help sb. do sth.；which 引导定语从句。

By using Polish folk music, Chopin seemed to be saying to the world, "We are our own country with our own culture! We are our own people!"

通过运用波兰民间音乐，肖邦似乎在向世界说：“我们是有自己文化的自己的国家！我们是我们自己的人民！”

词汇by doing sth. 通过做某事；culture 文化 语法by doing sth. 表示方式；直接引语。

Chopin's music encouraged his people and helped them keep going through years of difficulty.

肖邦的音乐鼓舞了他的人民，帮助他们在艰难的岁月里坚持下去。

词汇encourage 鼓励；keep going 坚持下去；difficulty 困难 语法and 连接并列谓语；help sb. do sth.。

Today, the Polish people proudly think of Poland as the country of Chopin.

今天，波兰人民自豪地把波兰看作肖邦的祖国。

词汇proudly 自豪地；think of ... as ... 把……看作 语法think of ... as ... 表示“把……看作”。

When Chopin died from ill health at 39, thousands of people travelled from faraway countries to attend his funeral.

肖邦 39 岁因健康不佳去世时，成千上万的人从遥远的国家赶来参加他的葬礼。

词汇die from 死于；ill health 健康不佳；funeral 葬礼 语法when 引导时间状语从句；thousands of 表示“数千”。

Today, people around the world still listen to his moving and often sad music.

今天，世界各地的人们仍在听他那动人而常常悲伤的音乐。

词汇moving 动人的 语法still 表示“仍然”。

It is studied in music schools, played by pianists, heard in films and on TV, and loved by listeners young and old.

它被音乐学校研究、被钢琴家演奏、在电影和电视中被听到，并受到老少听众的喜爱。

词汇listener 听众 语法并列被动结构；young and old 表示“老老少少”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P66–P67 两页 Section B 1b 的文章《Chopin: A Great Inspiration》（肖邦：伟大的激励）。文章开头说，伟大的作曲家舒曼第一次听到年轻的肖邦的作品时赞叹：“脱帽致敬吧，先生们！一个天才！”接着介绍肖邦（1810—1849）：他是历史上最伟大的音乐家之一，一生虽短却被誉为世界最伟大的钢琴家之一——尽管只举办了约 30 场音乐会；他还是一位有天赋的作曲家，留下 200 多部作品。肖邦出生在波兰的村庄，16 岁才上音乐学校，但很快超过老师；父母 1829 年送他去维也纳拓宽音乐视野，到 1832 年他已在巴黎安家。他不仅是伟大的钢琴家，也是受尊敬的钢琴教师，常对学生说“把全部心灵投入其中！按你的感觉演奏”，这句话至今仍被音乐家遵循。当时有些人看不起波兰民间音乐，认为它太简单普通，但它却成了肖邦音乐的重要特征，让人们把它看作有价值的艺术形式。他的音乐还帮助建设被瓜分的民族：通过波兰民间音乐，肖邦似乎在向世界宣告“我们是有自己文化的自己的国家”，他的音乐鼓舞人民度过艰难岁月。今天，波兰人民自豪地把波兰称为肖邦的祖国。文章最后说，肖邦 39 岁病逝时，成千上万的人从远方赶来参加葬礼；今天全世界的人仍在听他那动人而常带忧伤的音乐——音乐学校里研究它、钢琴家演奏它、电影电视里播放它，老少听众都喜爱它。

English Summary

This is the article "Chopin: A Great Inspiration" in Section B 1b, on pages 66–67. It begins with the great composer Robert Schumann, who, on first hearing a work written by young Chopin, said, "Hats off, gentlemen! A genius!" It then introduces Frédéric Chopin (1810–1849), one of the greatest musicians in history: during his short lifetime he was celebrated as one of the world's greatest pianists even though he gave only around 30 concerts, and he was also a gifted composer who left behind more than 200 musical works. Chopin was born in a village in Poland; he did not attend music school until he was 16, but soon outdid his teachers. His parents sent him to Vienna in 1829 to widen his musical experience, and by 1832 he had made his way to Paris, the city he would call home for the rest of his life. He was not only a great pianist but also a respected piano teacher who often told his students, "Put all your soul into it! Play the way you feel!" — advice still followed by musicians today. Polish folk music was looked down on by some people at the time as too simple and common, but it became an important characteristic of Chopin's music, and his use of it caused people to see it as a valuable art form. His music also helped build a nation divided up by other countries: through Polish folk music Chopin seemed to tell the world that they were their own country with their own culture and their own people, and his music encouraged his people and helped them keep going through years of difficulty. Today the Polish people proudly think of Poland as the country of Chopin. Finally, when Chopin died from ill health at 39, thousands of people travelled from faraway countries to attend his funeral; today people around the world still listen to his moving and often sad music, which is studied in music schools, played by pianists, heard in films and on TV, and loved by listeners young and old.

中文大意

这是教材 P90–P91 两页 Reading Plus 的文章《The Beauty of Chinese Music》（中国音乐之美）。文章作者说，昨晚一位中国朋友带他去听了一场中国传统音乐会；他一直想现场观看中国传统音乐表演。他认出的最常见中国乐器是二胡、琵琶、古筝和笛子。这场音乐会是中国艺术家的精彩演出，老老少少都来观看；作为一名外国观众，他感受到中国传统音乐在中国的传承与延续，也深深感受到它的魅力。第一首曲子是熟悉的《茉莉花》，它是最早在中国之外流行起来的中国民歌之一；这首浪漫的歌曲用中国音乐特有的简单五声音阶演奏，表达了对自然美的热爱；它被普契尼改编成意大利歌剧，还在 2008 年北京奥运会、2010 年上海世博会开幕式和 2014 年南京青奥会开幕式上演奏，难怪听起来如此熟悉。作者最喜欢的表演是二胡独奏《二泉映月》：曲名意为“月光映照在第二泉上”；演奏者极富表现力，让他听着几乎流泪；这首曲子被认为是中国弦乐的代表作之一，也是阿炳最著名的作品，其忧伤的美让他想起阿炳生活的艰辛，但在忧伤之下他感受到音乐家的乐观和对生活的深爱。音乐会在《春节序曲》中结束，这是李焕之《春节组曲》的第一乐章；乐曲创作于 20 世纪 50 年代，以陕北民间音乐为基础，描绘了抗日战争时期陕北人民庆祝春节的场景；开头活泼，随后放慢成舒缓的曲调；随着旋律，作者仿佛置身于当年陕北庆祝春节的人群中，分享着人们昂扬的精神和对美好未来的希望。

English Summary

This is the Reading Plus article "The Beauty of Chinese Music" on pages 90–91. The writer says that last night one of his Chinese friends took him to a traditional Chinese music concert; he had always wanted to see traditional Chinese music performed live. The most common Chinese instruments he recognized were the erhu, pipa, guzheng and dizi. The concert was a superb performance by Chinese artists, attended by both the elderly and the young, and as a foreign audience member he felt the legacy and continuation of traditional Chinese music in China and deeply felt its charm. The first song performed was the familiar tune Molihua (Jasmine Flower), one of the first Chinese folk songs to become popular outside China; this romantic song, played in a simple five-note scale characteristic of Chinese music, expresses the love for natural beauty. It was adapted into an Italian opera by Puccini and was also played during the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games, at the opening ceremony of the 2010 Shanghai Expo and at the opening ceremony of the Youth Olympics in Nanjing in 2014 — no wonder it sounded so familiar. The writer's favourite performance was the erhu solo Er Quan Ying Yue, whose poetic name means "Moon Reflected on Second Spring"; the gifted performer was so expressive that the writer almost cried along with it. The piece is considered one of the masterpieces of Chinese string music and Abing's most famous piece, and its sad beauty reminded the writer of the hardships in Abing's life, though under the sadness he sensed the musician's optimism and deep love for life. The concert ended with Chunjie Xuqu (the Spring Festival Overture), the first movement of Li Huanzhi's Spring Festival Suite. Composed in the 1950s and based on folk music of northern Shaanxi, it depicts the Spring Festival celebrated in northern Shaanxi during the Chinese People's War of Resistance against Japanese Aggression; it had a lively beginning and then slowed down to a soothing tune, and as the melody played the writer felt as though he was among the crowd celebrating the Spring Festival in Shaanxi back then, sharing their uplifting spirit and their hopes for a bright future.

语法点 Grammar

Last night, one of my Chinese friends took me to a traditional Chinese music concert.

take sb. to 带某人去；one of ... 表示“……之一”。

I have always wanted to see traditional Chinese music performed live.

现在完成时 have wanted；see sth. performed 表示“看到某物被表演”。

The most common Chinese instruments that I recognized were the erhu, pipa, guzheng, and dizi.

that 引导定语从句；were 与复数主语一致。

The concert was a superb performance by Chinese artists, attended by both the elderly and the young.

过去分词短语 attended by ... 作后置定语。

As a foreign audience member, I felt the legacy and continuation of traditional Chinese music in China.

as 表示“作为”；and 连接并列宾语。

The first song that was performed was a familiar tune, Molihua (Jasmine Flower).

that 引导定语从句；Molihua 是汉语拼音。

It is one of the first Chinese folk songs to become popular outside of China.

one of + 复数名词；to become 作后置定语。

The romantic song, played in a simple five-note scale that is characteristic of Chinese music, expressed the love for natural beauty.

played ... 是过去分词短语作后置定语；that 引导定语从句。

It was adapted into an Italian opera by Puccini.

一般过去时的被动语态 was adapted into。

It was also played during the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games, the opening ceremony for the 2010 Shanghai Expo, and the opening ceremony of the Youth Olympics in Nanjing in 2014.

被动语态 was played；during 表示时间。

No wonder it sounded so familiar!

No wonder + 从句，表示“难怪……”。

The piece has a poetic name, which means Moon Reflected on Second Spring.

which 引导非限制性定语从句。

The gifted performer was so expressive that I almost cried along with it as I listened.

so ... that ... 引导结果状语从句；as 引导时间状语从句。

The piece is considered one of the masterpieces of Chinese string music.

be considered + 名词，表示“被认为”。

Its sad beauty reminded me of the hardships in Abing's life, but under the sadness, I sensed the musician's optimism and deep love for life.

remind sb. of sth.；but 表转折。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

The most common Chinese instruments that I recognized were the erhu, pipa, guzheng, and dizi.

我认出的最常见的中国乐器是二胡、琵琶、古筝和笛子。

that 引导定语从句修饰 instruments；were 与复数主语一致。

The romantic song, played in a simple five-note scale that is characteristic of Chinese music, expressed the love for natural beauty.

这首浪漫的歌曲用中国音乐特有的简单五声音阶演奏，表达了对自然美的热爱。

played ... 是过去分词短语作后置定语；that 引导定语从句；five-note scale 表示“五声音阶”。

Its sad beauty reminded me of the hardships in Abing's life, but under the sadness, I sensed the musician's optimism and deep love for life.

它忧伤的美让我想起阿炳生活中的艰辛，但在忧伤之下，我感受到了这位音乐家的乐观和对生活的深爱。

remind sb. of sth. 表示“使某人想起”；but 表转折；sensed 是 sense 的过去式。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看 P90 的文章标题 The Beauty of Chinese Music，猜一猜文章会介绍哪些中国音乐。
  • 先浏览两页文章，找出文中提到的中国乐器和乐曲名。

读中

  • 边读边画出文中提到的乐曲名称及其特点。
  • 找出作者表达感受的句子。

读后

  • 用表格整理三首乐曲的信息和作者感受。
  • 讨论：你最喜欢的中国传统音乐是什么？它给你什么感受？

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面四篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. Which performance did Jack like best?

  1. Horse Racing.
  2. Dance of the Golden Snake.
  3. Butterfly Lovers.
  4. Voices of Spring.
答案与解析

A 原文：Jack: I thought Horse Racing was especially good. 可知 Jack 最喜欢《赛马》。

2. What kind of music does Clarice like?

  1. Music that is energetic.
  2. Music full of joy and celebration.
  3. Music that tells sad stories.
  4. Music played on Western instruments.
答案与解析

B 原文：I really like music which is full of joy and celebration, such as Dance of the Golden Snake. 可知 Clarice 喜欢充满欢乐和庆典气氛的音乐。

3. What did Schumann say when he heard young Chopin's work?

  1. "What a pity!"
  2. "How boring!"
  3. "Hats off, gentlemen! A genius!"
  4. "I don't like it."
答案与解析

C 原文：he said, "Hats off, gentlemen! A genius!" 可知舒曼称赞肖邦是天才。

4. How many concerts did Chopin give during his lifetime?

  1. Around 200.
  2. Around 300.
  3. Around 3,000.
  4. Around 30.
答案与解析

D 原文：even though he only gave around 30 concerts! 可知肖邦一生只举办了约 30 场音乐会。

5. What was the present that changed Jack's life?

  1. A guitar.
  2. A piano.
  3. A band.
  4. A song.
答案与解析

A 原文：The present that changed my life was the guitar my father bought me for my eighth birthday. 可知改变 Jack 人生的礼物是吉他。

6. Why did Jack become good at playing the guitar?

  1. Because he took lessons at school.
  2. Because he practised until his fingers hurt.
  3. Because his father played for him.
  4. Because he bought a new guitar.
答案与解析

B 原文：Then I went to my room and practised until my fingers hurt. ... gradually, I became good at playing the guitar. 可知他刻苦练习。

7. Which Chinese instrument was NOT mentioned as recognized by the writer?

  1. The erhu.
  2. The pipa.
  3. The guitar.
  4. The dizi.
答案与解析

C 原文：The most common Chinese instruments that I recognized were the erhu, pipa, guzheng, and dizi. 可知吉他不是文中提到的中国乐器。

8. Why did Molihua sound familiar to the writer?

  1. Because he had heard it in Italy.
  2. Because it was his favourite song as a child.
  3. Because it was written by his friend.
  4. Because it was played at many big events, such as the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games.
答案与解析

D 原文：It was also played during the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games ... No wonder it sounded so familiar! 可知它曾在许多大型活动中演奏，所以作者觉得熟悉。
