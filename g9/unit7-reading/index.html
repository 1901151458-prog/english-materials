Section A 3a · Let's Talk About That Concert

Section A 3a 主题：音乐会 · 音乐偏好

Clarice: That was a great concert! Which performance did you like the best, Jack?

这场音乐会太棒了！杰克，你最喜欢哪个节目？

词汇concert 音乐会；performance 节目；best 最好 语法which 引导特殊疑问句；like...best 表示“最喜欢”。

Jack: I thought Horse Racing was especially good.

我觉得《赛马》格外精彩。

词汇especially 尤其；good 好的 语法I thought 后接宾语从句；was 是系动词。

Jack: It really gave me the feeling of a horse race.

听完仿佛身临其境，真的有万马奔腾的画面。

词汇feeling 感觉；horse race 赛马 语法give sb. the feeling of... 给某人……的感觉。

Debbie: Me too! I love music which is energetic!

我也是！我喜欢充满活力的乐曲！

词汇energetic 充满活力的 语法which 引导定语从句，修饰 music。

Debbie: Their use of the keyboard and erhu made me imagine horses running down a track.

键盘搭配二胡的演奏，让我脑海里浮现出骏马奔腾的画面。

词汇keyboard 键盘；erhu 二胡；imagine 想象；track 跑道 语法make sb. do sth.；imagine sb./sth. doing sth.。

Clarice: Yes, but for me, I really like music which is full of joy and celebration, such as Dance of the Golden Snake.

确实，但我更喜欢喜庆欢快的曲子，比如《金蛇狂舞》。

词汇be full of 充满；joy 欢乐；celebration 庆祝；such as 例如 语法which 引导定语从句；be full of；such as 举例。

Teng Fei: The use of the erhu, pipa, and drums was exciting, but I prefer music that touches me — that's why I liked Butterfly Lovers more.

二胡、琵琶和锣鼓的合奏确实振奋人心，但我偏爱能触动内心的乐曲，所以我更喜欢《梁祝》。

词汇pipa 琵琶；drum 鼓；prefer 更喜欢；touch 触动 语法that 引导定语从句；prefer...more；that's why... 引导表语从句。

Teng Fei: The music tells a sad and romantic story.

这首曲子讲述了一段凄美浪漫的故事。

词汇sad 悲伤的；romantic 浪漫的 语法tell a story 讲故事。

Jack: Yeah, and it's interesting that the performers used both Chinese and Western instruments.

没错，表演者同时融合中西乐器，特别新颖。

词汇performer 表演者；both...and... 两者都；instrument 乐器 语法It is + adj. + that 从句；both...and... 连接并列成分。

Jack: I have never heard the erhu played together with the violin.

我从没听过二胡和小提琴合奏。

词汇hear 听见；play 演奏；violin 小提琴 语法现在完成时 have never heard；hear + 宾语 + 过去分词作宾补。

Teng Fei: I also liked Voices of Spring.

我也喜欢《春之声》。

词汇also 也 语法一般过去时。

Teng Fei: I never realized that you could dance to it!

我从没想到还能伴着它跳舞呢！

词汇realize 意识到；dance to 伴着……跳舞 语法that 引导宾语从句；dance to music 固定搭配。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕一场音乐会展开。Clarice 问 Jack 最喜欢哪个节目。Jack 最喜欢《赛马》，觉得它让人有赛马的感觉；Debbie 也喜欢，认为键盘和二胡的演奏让人想象马在跑道上奔跑。Clarice 喜欢充满欢乐和庆祝气氛的音乐，如《金蛇狂舞》；Teng Fei 更喜欢能触动他的《梁祝》，觉得它讲述了一个凄美浪漫的故事。Jack 觉得中西乐器合奏很有趣；Teng Fei 还喜欢《春之声》，没想到还能跟着它跳舞。

English Summary

The conversation is about a concert. Clarice asks Jack which performance he liked best. Jack liked Horse Racing best because it gave him the feeling of a horse race; Debbie also liked it and said the keyboard and erhu made her imagine horses running down a track. Clarice likes music full of joy and celebration, such as Dance of the Golden Snake. Teng Fei prefers music that touches him, so he liked Butterfly Lovers more; it tells a sad and romantic story. Jack found the mix of Chinese and Western instruments interesting, and Teng Fei also liked Voices of Spring and was surprised that people could dance to it.

Section B 1b · Chopin: A Great Inspiration

Section B 1b 主题：肖邦 · 音乐与民族

When the great composer Robert Schumann first heard a work which was written by young Chopin, he said, "Hats off, gentlemen! A genius!"

伟大作曲家罗伯特·舒曼第一次听到年轻的肖邦创作的一部作品时，他说：“先生们，脱帽致敬吧！这是一位天才！”

词汇composer 作曲家；work 作品；genius 天才 语法when 引导时间状语从句；which 引导定语从句；was written 是一般过去时被动语态。

Frédéric Chopin (1810–1849) was one of the greatest musicians in history.

弗雷德里克·肖邦（1810—1849）是历史上最伟大的音乐家之一。

词汇musician 音乐家；in history 历史上 语法one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。

During his short lifetime, he was celebrated as one of the world's greatest pianists — even though he only gave around 30 concerts!

在他短暂的一生中，他被誉为世界上最伟大的钢琴家之一——尽管他一生只举办了大约 30 场音乐会！

词汇lifetime 一生；be celebrated as 被誉为；pianist 钢琴家；concert 音乐会 语法be celebrated as；even though 引导让步状语从句。

He was also a gifted composer who left behind more than 200 musical works.

他还是一位才华横溢的作曲家，留下了 200 多部音乐作品。

词汇gifted 有天赋的；composer 作曲家；leave behind 留下；musical work 音乐作品 语法who 引导定语从句；leave behind。

Chopin was born in a village in Poland.

肖邦出生在波兰的一个村庄。

词汇be born 出生；Poland 波兰 语法一般过去时被动结构 be born；in a village 作地点状语。

He didn't attend music school until he was 16, but soon outdid his teachers.

他直到 16 岁才上音乐学校，但很快就超过了他的老师们。

词汇attend 上（学）；until 直到；outdo 超过 语法not...until... 直到……才；but 转折。

His parents sent him to Vienna in 1829 to widen his musical experience.

1829 年，他的父母送他去维也纳，以拓宽他的音乐视野。

词汇send sb. to 送某人去；widen 拓宽；experience 经验；视野 语法send sb. to；to widen 作目的状语。

By 1832, he had made his way to Paris, the city that he would call home for the rest of his life.

到 1832 年，他来到了巴黎，这座城市成了他余生的家。

词汇make one's way to 前往；the rest of one's life 余生；call...home 把……称为家 语法by + 时间与过去完成时连用；that 引导定语从句。

Chopin was not only a great pianist, but also a respected piano teacher.

肖邦不仅是一位伟大的钢琴家，也是一位受人尊敬的钢琴教师。

词汇not only...but also... 不仅……而且……；respected 受尊敬的 语法not only...but also... 连接并列表语。

He often told his students, "Put all your soul into it! Play the way you feel!"

他经常告诉学生们：“全身心地投入进去！按照你的感受去演奏！”

词汇soul 灵魂；put...into 投入；the way 方式 语法直接引语；祈使句；the way you feel 是省略关系词的定语从句。

This is advice that is still followed by musicians today.

这是一条如今仍被音乐人遵循的箴言。

词汇advice 建议；follow 遵循；musician 音乐人 语法that 引导定语从句；is followed 是一般现在时被动语态。

Polish folk music was a kind of music that some people looked down on at the time, it was seen as too simple and common.

波兰民间音乐在当时是一种被人看不起的音乐，它被认为过于简单和普通。

词汇Polish 波兰的；folk music 民间音乐；look down on 看不起；common 普通的 语法that 引导定语从句；look down on；was seen as 是一般过去时被动语态。

But it became an important characteristic of Chopin's music.

但它成了肖邦音乐的一个重要特征。

词汇characteristic 特征 语法become + 名词；but 转折。

His use of Polish folk music caused people to see it as a valuable art form.

他对波兰民间音乐的运用，使人们将其视为一种有价值的艺术形式。

词汇cause sb. to do 使某人做；valuable 有价值的；art form 艺术形式 语法cause sb. to do sth.；see...as...。

His music also helped build a nation which was divided up by other countries.

他的音乐还帮助凝聚了一个被其他国家瓜分的民族。

词汇nation 民族；divide up 瓜分；country 国家 语法help do sth.；which 引导定语从句；was divided up 是被动语态。

By using Polish folk music, Chopin seemed to be saying to the world, "We are our own country with our own culture! We are our own people!"

通过使用波兰民间音乐，肖邦似乎在对世界说：“我们有自己的国家，有自己的文化！我们是自己的民族！”

词汇seem to be doing 似乎正在做；culture 文化 语法by + 动名词作方式状语；seem to be saying。

Chopin's music encouraged his people and helped them keep going through years of difficulty.

肖邦的音乐鼓舞了他的同胞，帮助他们度过艰难的岁月。

词汇encourage 鼓舞；keep going 继续前进；difficulty 困难 语法encourage sb.；help sb. do sth.；through 表示经历。

Today, the Polish people proudly think of Poland as the country of Chopin.

如今，波兰人民自豪地将波兰视为肖邦之国。

词汇proudly 自豪地；think of...as... 把……视为 语法think of...as...；一般现在时。

When Chopin died from ill health at 39, thousands of people travelled from faraway countries to attend his funeral.

当肖邦于 39 岁因病去世时，成千上万的人从遥远的国家赶来参加他的葬礼。

词汇die from 死于；ill health 健康不佳；faraway 遥远的；funeral 葬礼 语法when 引导时间状语从句；thousands of；to attend 作目的状语。

Today, people around the world still listen to his moving and often sad music.

今天，世界各地的人们仍在聆听他动人而常常带着悲伤的音乐。

词汇moving 动人的；sad 悲伤的 语法listen to；around the world 作后置定语。

It is studied in music schools, played by pianists, heard in films and on TV, and loved by listeners young and old.

它在音乐学校被研究，由钢琴家演奏，在电影和电视中响起，深受老幼听众的喜爱。

词汇study 研究；pianist 钢琴家；listener 听众 语法被动语态并列：is studied/played/heard/loved；young and old 作后置定语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍伟大音乐家肖邦。舒曼第一次听到肖邦的作品就赞叹他是天才。肖邦是历史上最伟大的音乐家之一，短暂一生中只举办约 30 场音乐会，却留下 200 多部作品。他出生在波兰，16 岁才上音乐学校，很快超过老师；1829 年去维也纳，1832 年到巴黎并定居。他不仅是钢琴家，也是受尊敬的钢琴教师，主张“全身心投入，按自己的感受演奏”。他让当时被看不起的波兰民间音乐成为有价值的艺术形式，并用音乐凝聚被瓜分的民族、鼓舞同胞。今天波兰以肖邦为荣，全世界仍在听他的音乐。

English Summary

The passage introduces Chopin. When Schumann first heard young Chopin's work, he called him a genius. Chopin was one of history's greatest musicians; in his short life he gave only about 30 concerts but left over 200 works. Born in a Polish village, he did not attend music school until 16 but soon outdid his teachers; he went to Vienna in 1829 and settled in Paris by 1832. He was not only a great pianist but also a respected teacher who told students to put their soul into their playing. He made Polish folk music, once looked down on, a valuable art form, and his music helped build a divided nation and encouraged his people. Today Poland is proud of him and the world still listens to his music.

Section B 3a · My Music Story

Section B 3a 主题：吉他 · 音乐与成长

The present that changed my life was the guitar my father bought me for my eighth birthday.

改变我人生的那份礼物，是爸爸在我八岁生日时给我买的吉他。

词汇present 礼物；change one's life 改变某人的生活；guitar 吉他 语法that 引导定语从句；my father bought me 是省略关系词的定语从句；buy sb. sth. 双宾语。

I spent the next several months learning to play the guitar.

接下来的几个月我都在学弹吉他。

词汇spend time doing 花时间做；several 几个 语法spend time doing sth.。

Some of my schoolmates invited me to join their band.

Then I realized that I enjoyed music more when I shared it with others.

词汇realize 意识到；enjoy 享受；share...with... 与……分享 语法that 引导宾语从句；when 引导时间状语从句。

Music makes me happy when I am feeling sad, and it relaxes me when I am stressed.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章讲述 Jack 的音乐故事。八岁生日时爸爸送他一把吉他，改变了他的人生。接下来几个月他学吉他，常坐在爸爸身边学，再回房间练到手指发疼；起初困难很多，但渐渐擅长。同学邀请他加入乐队，他起初太害羞不敢在别人面前演奏，后来意识到分享音乐更快乐，和乐队成员成了好朋友。如今乐队越来越受欢迎，常参加校内外演出。虽然今年更忙，他仍热爱吉他、抽时间练习；音乐在他悲伤时让他快乐，压力大时让他放松，将永远是他生活的一部分。

English Summary

Jack tells his music story. The guitar his father gave him for his eighth birthday changed his life. He spent months learning to play, often sitting beside his father and then practising in his room until his fingers hurt; at first he had trouble, but gradually became good at it. Schoolmates invited him to join their band. He was too shy to play in front of people at first, but realized he enjoyed music more when he shared it; he had fun and made good friends. Now the band is getting more and more popular and often performs inside and outside school. Although he is busier this year, he still loves the guitar and finds time for it; music makes him happy when he is sad and relaxes him when he is stressed, and it will always be part of his life.
