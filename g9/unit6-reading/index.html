Section A 3a · Why Do We Explore Space?

Section A 3a 主题：探索太空的原因 · 采访

Host: Our question today is: Why do we explore space?

我们今天的问题是：我们为什么要探索太空？

词汇explore 探索；space 太空 语法why 引导表语从句；explore space 是固定搭配。

Host: Let's talk to space scientist Dr Yang.

让我们请教太空科学家杨博士。

词汇space scientist 太空科学家 语法Let's do sth. 表示建议；talk to sb.。

Host: Welcome, Dr Yang!

杨博士，欢迎您！

词汇welcome 欢迎 语法Welcome! 是省略句。

Dr Yang: Thank you very much.

非常感谢。

词汇thank you very much 非常感谢 语法感谢表达。

Dr Yang: Well, there are three good reasons to explore space.

探索太空有三个重要原因。

词汇reason 原因；explore 探索 语法There be 句型；three good reasons to do sth. 作定语。

Dr Yang: First, to improve our knowledge of Earth and its place in the universe.

第一，加深我们对地球及其在宇宙中位置的认知。

词汇improve 提高；knowledge 知识；universe 宇宙 语法to improve 是不定式作目的状语；its 指 Earth's。

Host: Could you explain that a bit more?

您能再解释一下吗？

词汇explain 解释；a bit 一点 语法Could you...? 礼貌请求；a bit more 表示程度。

Dr Yang: Sure.

当然。

词汇sure 当然 语法省略回答。

Dr Yang: The study of other planets helps us better understand Earth's geology, history, and relationship with other planets.

研究其他行星有助于我们更好地了解地球的地质、历史以及与其他行星的关系。

词汇study 研究；planet 行星；geology 地质；relationship 关系 语法help sb. do sth.；better 是副词比较级。

Host: Oh, right.

哦，对。

词汇right 对的 语法表示理解/同意。

Dr Yang: Second, technology developed for space can benefit us here on Earth.

第二，为太空研发的技术能造福地球。

词汇technology 技术；develop 研发；benefit 造福 语法developed for space 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

Host: Could you give our listeners an example?

能给我们的听众举个例子吗？

词汇listener 听众；example 例子 语法give sb. an example 给某人举例。

Dr Yang: Of course.

当然。

词汇of course 当然 语法省略回答。

Dr Yang: Some satellites, for example, help us deal with problems caused by climate change.

比如，一些人造卫星能帮助我们应对气候变化带来的问题。

词汇satellite 卫星；deal with 应对；climate change 气候变化 语法for example 作插入语；caused by climate change 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

Host: Yes, that's very useful.

是的，非常实用。

词汇useful 有用的 语法that 指上文提到的卫星用途。

Host: And the third reason?

那第三个原因呢？

词汇third 第三个；reason 原因 语法省略问句，完整为 What is the third reason?

Dr Yang: Well, at some point in the far future, we may find new resources in outer space.

嗯，在遥远的未来，我们也许会在外太空发现新资源。

词汇at some point 在某个时候；far future 遥远的未来；resource 资源；outer space 外太空 语法may 表示可能；in the far future 作时间状语。

Dr Yang: It might sound crazy, but our Earth may run out of resources.

这听起来可能有些疯狂，但我们的地球可能会耗尽资源。

词汇sound 听起来；crazy 疯狂的；run out of 耗尽 语法might 表示可能；sound + adj.；run out of 是固定短语。

Dr Yang: In that case, a new home for us human beings will be needed.

那样的话，人类就需要一个新的家园。

词汇in that case 那样的话；human being 人类 语法in that case 作状语；will be needed 是一般将来时被动语态。

Host: Wow, interesting!

哇，真有意思！

词汇interesting 有趣的 语法感叹句。

Host: Maybe Mars will be turned into our new home in the future.

也许未来火星会被改造成我们的新家园。

词汇maybe 也许；Mars 火星；be turned into 被改造成 语法will be turned into 是一般将来时被动语态。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段采访围绕“我们为什么要探索太空”展开。杨博士给出三个理由：第一，加深对地球及其在宇宙中位置的认识；第二，为太空研发的技术能造福地球，例如卫星帮助应对气候变化；第三，遥远的未来可能在外太空发现新资源，地球资源可能耗尽，人类需要新的家园，火星也许会成为新家园。

English Summary

In the interview, Dr Yang gives three reasons for exploring space. First, it improves our knowledge of Earth and its place in the universe. Second, technology developed for space can benefit us on Earth, for example satellites help us deal with climate change. Third, in the far future we may find new resources in outer space; Earth may run out of resources, so humans may need a new home—perhaps Mars.

to improve 是不定式作目的状语。

The study of other planets helps us better understand Earth's geology...

help sb. do sth.；better 是副词比较级。

technology developed for space can benefit us

developed for space 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

problems caused by climate change

Section B 1b · To the Stars and Beyond

Section B 1b 主题：星际旅行 · 挑战与希望

Since the beginning of human history, people have looked up at the stars and wondered what they were like.

自人类历史开端起，人们便仰望星空，好奇那些星辰究竟是什么样子。

词汇since 自从；look up at 仰望；wonder 想知道；star 恒星 语法since 引导时间状语；现在完成时 have looked up and wondered；what they were like 是宾语从句。

Many ancient cultures believed that these points of light were from gods or other mysterious worlds.

许多古老文明都相信，这些光点来自神明或其他神秘世界。

词汇ancient 古老的；culture 文明；point of light 光点；mysterious 神秘的 语法that 引导宾语从句；were from 表示来源。

However, as technology and science developed, we learnt the truth about stars.

然而，随着技术与科学的发展，我们了解了关于恒星的真相。

词汇however 然而；as 随着；develop 发展；truth 真相 语法as 引导时间状语从句；learn the truth about...。

They are real places like our own sun, and they are farther away than anyone imagined.

它们是如同我们自己的太阳一样真实存在的地方，而且距离之远超出任何人的想象。

词汇real 真实的；farther 更远；imagine 想象 语法farther...than... 比较级；anyone imagined 是省略关系代词的定语从句。

This raises the question: Can we visit the stars one day?

这就引出了一个疑问：我们有朝一日能否造访那些星辰？

词汇raise 提出；question 问题；visit 造访 语法冒号后解释 question；Can we...? 疑问句。

The man-made object which has travelled farthest from Earth is Voyager 1.

迄今飞离地球最远的人造物体是“旅行者 1 号”。

词汇man-made 人造的；object 物体；travelled 旅行；farthest 最远 语法which 引导定语从句；farthest 是副词最高级。

This spacecraft was launched in 1977.

这艘航天器于 1977 年发射。

词汇spacecraft 航天器；launch 发射 语法一般过去时被动语态 was launched。

In case Voyager 1 is found by alien life, a special record with sounds and pictures from Earth is carried on board.

万一“旅行者 1 号”被外星生命发现，它搭载着一张载有地球声音与影像的特殊唱片。

词汇in case 万一；alien 外星的；record 唱片；on board 在船上/机上 语法in case 引导条件状语从句；with sounds and pictures 作后置定语；is carried 是一般现在时被动语态。

It shows who we are and where we are from.

它诉说着我们是谁、我们来自何处。

词汇show 展示；where 哪里 语法who/where 引导宾语从句；and 连接两个从句。

By 2025, Voyager 1 was over 25 billion kilometres from Earth, but that is still a long way from Earth's second-closest star, Proxima Centauri.

到 2025 年，“旅行者 1 号”距离地球已超过 250 亿公里，但这距离地球第二近的恒星——比邻星——仍有漫长的路程。

词汇by 2025 到 2025 年；billion 十亿；a long way from 离……很远；second-closest 第二近的 语法by + 时间表示“到……时”；but 转折；second-closest 是合成形容词。

That star is 4.2 light years away—about 40 trillion kilometres!

那颗恒星距离地球 4.2 光年，约合 40 万亿公里！

词汇light year 光年；trillion 万亿 语法be...away 表示距离；破折号后补充说明。

The challenge of visiting the stars gets even bigger for space flights with humans.

对于载人航天而言，造访星辰的挑战更加艰巨。

词汇challenge 挑战；space flight 航天飞行；human 人类 语法get + 形容词比较级表示变化；with humans 作后置定语。

The farthest that any human has travelled in space is only about 400,000 kilometres, to the far side of the moon.

人类在太空中到达的最远距离仅约 40 万公里，也就是月球的背面。

词汇farthest 最远；travel 旅行；far side 背面 语法that 引导定语从句；to the far side of the moon 作补充说明。

Time is a big problem.

时间是个大问题。

词汇time 时间 语法主系表结构。

At present, even a journey to the planet Mars will take around six to nine months.

目前，即便是前往火星的旅程也要花大约 6 到 9 个月。

词汇at present 目前；journey 旅程；Mars 火星；take 花费 语法even 强调；will take 一般将来时。

What will the astronauts eat and drink during this period?

在此期间宇航员吃什么、喝什么？

词汇astronaut 宇航员；during 在……期间；period 时期 语法特殊疑问句；during this period 作时间状语。

And how about their health and safety?

他们的健康与安全又该如何保障？

词汇health 健康；safety 安全 语法How about...? 询问情况。

These problems grow when we talk about visiting another star.

当我们谈论造访另一颗恒星时，这些问题会变得更加严峻。

词汇grow 增长；talk about 谈论 语法when 引导时间状语从句；grow 表示“变得更大”。

It might take hundreds of thousands of years to get there!

抵达那里可能需要几十万年！

词汇hundreds of thousands of 几十万；get there 到达那里 语法It might take...to do...；it 是形式主语。

Scientists, however, believe that visiting the stars is not a completely wild dream.

然而，科学家相信，造访星辰并非完全是痴人说梦。

词汇scientist 科学家；completely 完全地；wild 疯狂的；dream 梦想 语法however 作插入语；that 引导宾语从句；动名词 visiting 作主语。

One idea is to have the astronauts sleep during their journey like some animals do in winter.

其中一个设想是让宇航员在旅途中像某些动物冬眠那样沉睡。

词汇idea 设想；have sb. do 让某人做；journey 旅程 语法one idea is to do...；have sb. do sth.；like 引导方式状语从句。

Another is to develop much faster spacecraft.

另一个设想是研发速度远超当今的航天器。

词汇another 另一个；develop 研发；spacecraft 航天器 语法another is to do...；much 修饰比较级 faster。

While this is not possible right now, with nations and scientists cooperating with one another, there is hope that the necessary technology will be developed.

虽然目前还无法实现，但随着各国与科学家彼此协作，有望研发出所需的技术。

词汇while 虽然；possible 可能的；cooperate 合作；necessary 必要的 语法while 引导让步状语从句；with...cooperating 是独立主格；that 引导同位语从句。

Humans have set foot on the moon, successfully landed rovers on Mars, and even photographed other galaxies.

人类已经踏足月球，成功让火星车登陆火星，甚至拍摄到了其他星系的照片。

词汇set foot on 踏上；successfully 成功地；rover 火星车；galaxy 星系 语法现在完成时 have set/landed/photographed；三个谓语并列。

In time, it seems likely that one day we will reach the stars as people once dream.

随着时间的推移，总有一天我们或许能像人们曾经梦想的那样抵达星辰。

词汇in time 随着时间的推移；likely 可能的；reach 到达；once 曾经 语法it seems likely that...；as 引导方式状语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章讨论人类能否造访恒星。人类自古以来仰望星空，科技发展让我们了解恒星的真相。飞得最远的人造物体是 1977 年发射的“旅行者 1 号”，它携带地球声音和影像的唱片；到 2025 年它已距地球 250 多亿公里，但离比邻星仍有 4.2 光年。载人星际旅行挑战更大：人类最远只到过月球背面约 40 万公里，去火星要 6—9 个月，去另一颗恒星可能需要几十万年。科学家提出让宇航员像动物冬眠、研发更快航天器等设想；随着国际合作，未来有望实现。

English Summary

The passage asks whether humans can visit the stars. People have looked up at the stars since ancient times, and science has revealed what they are. Voyager 1, launched in 1977, is the farthest man-made object from Earth; it carries a record of Earth's sounds and pictures. By 2025 it was over 25 billion kilometres away, but Proxima Centauri is still 4.2 light years away. Human space travel faces even bigger challenges: the farthest humans have gone is about 400,000 kilometres to the far side of the moon; a trip to Mars takes six to nine months, and reaching another star might take hundreds of thousands of years. Scientists suggest ideas such as sleeping during the journey and developing faster spacecraft; with international cooperation, there is hope.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍作者的太空梦：有朝一日在月球上建研究站。作者认为这有三大好处：第一，研究站能让科学家合作开展太空研究，研发、测试和运行新技术，这些技术还能用于火星和其他行星；第二，可能发现更多资源，月球或许有对地球有用的特殊能源；第三，人们或许能在那里生活和工作，游客也能参观。作者希望成为首批访客，并希望人类能在太空度假、探索宇宙，月球研究站能在有生之年建成。

English Summary

The writer's space dream is to build a research station on the moon. There are several benefits. First, it would be a good place for scientists to cooperate on space research and develop, test and operate new technologies, which could later help build stations on Mars and other planets. Second, more resources may be found, perhaps a special energy resource useful to Earth. Third, people may live and work there, and tourists may visit. The writer wants to be one of the first visitors and hopes humans can spend holidays in space, explore the universe, and see the station built in his lifetime.
