Section A · 3a · Why Do We Explore Space?

Section A · 3a 主题：太空探索 · 访谈

Host: Our question today is: Why do we explore space?

我们今天的问题是：我们为什么要探索太空？

词汇explore 探索；space 太空 语法why 引导表语从句；explore space 是固定搭配。

Host: Let's talk to space scientist Dr Yang.

让我们来和太空科学家杨博士聊聊。

词汇space scientist 太空科学家；talk to 和……交谈 语法Let's do sth. 表示建议；talk to sb.。

Host: Welcome, Dr Yang!

杨博士，欢迎您！

词汇welcome 欢迎 语法Welcome! 是省略句，欢迎嘉宾时使用。

Dr Yang: Thank you very much.

非常感谢。

词汇thank you very much 非常感谢 语法感谢的表达。

Dr Yang: Well, there are three good reasons to explore space.

嗯，探索太空有三个很好的理由。

词汇reason 原因；three good reasons 三个好理由 语法There be 句型；three good reasons to do sth. 中不定式作定语。

Dr Yang: First, to improve our knowledge of Earth and its place in the universe.

第一，增进我们对地球及其在宇宙中位置的认识。

词汇improve 增进；提高；knowledge 知识；universe 宇宙 语法不定式 to improve 作目的状语；its 指代 Earth。

Host: Could you explain that a bit more?

您能再解释一下吗？

词汇explain 解释；a bit more 再多一点 语法Could you ...? 委婉请求；a bit 修饰比较级 more。

Dr Yang: Sure.

当然可以。

词汇sure 当然 语法口语简答，表示同意。

Dr Yang: The study of other planets helps us better understand Earth's geology, history, and relationship with other planets.

对其他行星的研究帮助我们更好地理解地球的地质、历史以及它与其他行星的关系。

词汇study 研究；geology 地质；relationship 关系 语法动名词短语 The study of other planets 作主语；help sb. do sth.。

Host: Oh, right.

哦，明白了。

词汇right 明白了 语法口语应答，表示理解对方的话。

Dr Yang: Second, technology developed for space can benefit us here on Earth.

第二，为太空开发的技术也能让地球上的我们受益。

词汇technology 技术；benefit 使受益 语法developed for space 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 technology。

Host: Could you give our listeners an example?

您能给听众们举个例子吗？

词汇listener 听众；example 例子 语法Could you ...? 委婉请求；give sb. sth. 双宾语。

Dr Yang: Of course.

当然。

词汇of course 当然 语法口语应答，表示肯定。

Dr Yang: Some satellites, for example, help us deal with problems caused by climate change.

例如，一些卫星帮助我们处理气候变化引起的问题。

词汇satellite 卫星；deal with 处理；climate change 气候变化 语法for example 作插入语；help sb. do sth.；caused by ... 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

Host: Yes, that's very useful.

是的，那非常有用。

词汇useful 有用的 语法that 指代上句提到的卫星用途。

Host: And the third reason?

那第三个理由呢？

词汇third 第三 语法省略问句：(What is) the third reason?。

Dr Yang: Well, at some point in the far future, we may find new resources in outer space.

嗯，在遥远的未来某一时刻，我们或许能在太空找到新的资源。

词汇at some point 在某个时刻；far future 遥远的未来；resource 资源；outer space 太空 语法may 表示可能性；at some point in the future 作时间状语。

Dr Yang: It might sound crazy, but our Earth may run out of resources.

这听起来可能很疯狂，但我们的地球可能会耗尽资源。

词汇sound crazy 听起来疯狂；run out of 用完；耗尽 语法sound 是系动词，后接形容词；might / may 表示推测；but 表示转折。

Dr Yang: In that case, a new home for us human beings will be needed.

那样的话，我们人类就需要一个新的家园。

词汇in that case 那样的话；human being 人类 语法in that case 作状语；will be needed 是一般将来时被动语态。

Host: Wow, interesting!

哇，真有意思！

词汇interesting 有趣的 语法感叹句，表示惊叹。

Host: Maybe Mars will be made into our new home in the future.

也许未来火星会被建设成我们的新家园。

词汇Mars 火星；be made into 被建成 语法maybe 表示“也许”；will be made 是一般将来时被动语态。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是对太空科学家杨博士的访谈。主持人问：我们为什么要探索太空？杨博士给出三个理由：第一，增进我们对地球及其在宇宙中位置的认识——对其他行星的研究能帮助我们更好地理解地球的地质、历史以及它与其他行星的关系；第二，为太空开发的技术能让地球上的我们受益，例如一些卫星帮助我们处理气候变化引起的问题；第三，在遥远的未来，我们或许能在太空找到新资源，地球可能耗尽资源，那时人类需要一个新的家园。主持人觉得很有意思，并猜想未来火星或许会成为人类的新家园。

English Summary

This is an interview with space scientist Dr Yang. The host asks why we explore space, and Dr Yang gives three reasons. First, space exploration improves our knowledge of Earth and its place in the universe: the study of other planets helps us better understand Earth's geology, history and relationship with other planets. Second, technology developed for space can benefit us here on Earth—for example, satellites help us deal with problems caused by climate change. Third, in the far future we may find new resources in outer space; our Earth may run out of resources, and in that case a new home for human beings will be needed. The host finds this interesting and suggests that Mars may be made into our new home in the future.

语法点 Grammar

Our question today is: Why do we explore space?

why 引导表语从句；explore space 是固定搭配。

Let's talk to space scientist Dr Yang.

Let's do sth. 表示建议；talk to sb. 表示“和某人交谈”。

Well, there are three good reasons to explore space.

There be 句型；three good reasons to do sth. 中不定式作定语。

First, to improve our knowledge of Earth and its place in the universe.

不定式 to improve 作目的状语；its 指代 Earth。

Could you explain that a bit more?

Could you ...? 委婉请求；a bit 修饰比较级 more。

The study of other planets helps us better understand Earth's geology, history, and relationship with other planets.

动名词短语作主语；help sb. do sth.。

Second, technology developed for space can benefit us here on Earth.

developed for space 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

Could you give our listeners an example?

Could you ...? 委婉请求；give sb. sth. 双宾语。

Some satellites, for example, help us deal with problems caused by climate change.

help sb. do sth.；caused by ... 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

It might sound crazy, but our Earth may run out of resources.

sound 是系动词；might / may 表示推测；but 表示转折。

In that case, a new home for us human beings will be needed.

in that case 作状语；will be needed 是一般将来时被动语态。

Maybe Mars will be made into our new home in the future.

maybe 表示“也许”；will be made 是一般将来时被动语态。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Second, technology developed for space can benefit us here on Earth.

第二，为太空开发的技术也能让地球上的我们受益。

developed for space 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 technology；benefit 在此作动词，意为“使受益”。

In that case, a new home for us human beings will be needed.

那样的话，我们人类就需要一个新的家园。

in that case 表示“那样的话”；will be needed 是一般将来时的被动语态；us human beings 中 human beings 是同位语。

Some satellites, for example, help us deal with problems caused by climate change.

例如，一些卫星帮助我们处理气候变化引起的问题。

for example 作插入语；help sb. do sth.；caused by climate change 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 problems。

Section B 1b · To the Stars and Beyond

Section B 1b 主题：星际旅行 · 科学探索（P56–P57 两页合并）

To the Stars and Beyond

飞向星辰，超越星辰

词汇beyond 超越；在……之外；star 星星 语法标题：介词短语 to the stars and beyond 表示“飞向星辰乃至更远”。

Since the beginning of human history, people have looked up at the stars and wondered what they were like.

自人类历史开始以来，人们就一直仰望星空，想知道星星是什么样子。

词汇since 自从；look up at 仰望；wonder 想知道 语法since 引导时间状语；现在完成时 have looked up / wondered；what they were like 是宾语从句。

Many ancient cultures believed that these points of light were from gods or other mysterious worlds.

许多古代文化认为这些光点来自神灵或其他神秘世界。

词汇ancient 古代的；culture 文化；mysterious 神秘的 语法that 引导宾语从句；一般过去时 believed / were。

However, as technology and science developed, we learnt the truth about stars.

然而，随着科技的发展，我们了解到了关于星星的真相。

词汇however 然而；as 随着；develop 发展；truth 真相 语法as 引导时间状语从句；learnt 是 learn 的过去式。

They are real places like our own sun, and they are further away than anyone imagined.

它们是像我们太阳一样的真实天体，而且比任何人想象的都要遥远。

词汇real 真实的；further 更远的；imagine 想象 语法further 是 far 的比较级；than 引导比较状语；like our own sun 作定语。

This raises the question: Can we visit the stars one day?

这引出了一个问题：我们有一天能访问那些恒星吗？

词汇raise 提出；question 问题；one day 有一天 语法raise the question 表示“提出问题”；冒号后是同位语从句（用疑问句形式）。

The man-made object which has travelled furthest from Earth is Voyager 1.

距离地球最远的人造物体是旅行者 1 号。

词汇man-made 人造的；object 物体；travelled 旅行（过去分词） 语法which 引导定语从句修饰 object；furthest 是 far 的最高级。

This spacecraft was launched in 1977.

这艘宇宙飞船于 1977 年发射。

词汇spacecraft 宇宙飞船；launch 发射 语法一般过去时被动语态 was launched；in 1977 表时间。

In case Voyager 1 is found by alien life, a special record with sounds and pictures from Earth is carried on board.

万一旅行者 1 号被外星生命发现，船上携带了一张载有地球声音和图片的特殊唱片。

词汇in case 以防万一；alien 外星的；record 唱片；on board 在船上 语法in case 引导条件状语从句；is carried 是一般现在时被动语态。

It shows who we are and where we are from.

它展示了我们是谁、来自哪里。

词汇show 展示 语法who / where 引导宾语从句。

Now, Voyager 1 is an amazing 23.5 billion kilometres from Earth, but that is still a long way from Earth's nearest star, Proxima Centauri.

如今，旅行者 1 号距离地球已有惊人的 235 亿公里，但这距离地球最近的恒星比邻星仍然很远。

词汇billion 十亿；a long way 很远；nearest 最近的 语法23.5 billion 表示数字；but 表示转折；Proxima Centauri 是同位语。

That star is 4.2 light years away—about 40 trillion kilometres!

那颗恒星距离我们 4.2 光年——约 40 万亿公里！

词汇light year 光年；trillion 万亿 语法light year 是距离单位；破折号后补充说明具体距离。

The challenge of visiting the stars gets even bigger for space flights with humans.

对于载人太空飞行来说，访问恒星的挑战就更大了。

词汇challenge 挑战；space flight 太空飞行 语法of visiting the stars 作后置定语；with humans 表示“载人的”。

The furthest that any human has gone in space is only about 400,000 kilometres, around the other side of the moon.

人类在太空中到过的最远距离只有约 40 万公里，即月球背面的附近。

词汇furthest 最远的；the other side of the moon 月球背面 语法that 引导定语从句修饰 The furthest；现在完成时 has gone。

Time is a big problem.

时间是个大问题。

词汇time 时间；problem 问题 语法主系表结构。

At present, even a journey to the planet Mars will take around six to nine months.

目前，即使去火星的旅程也需要大约六到九个月。

词汇at present 目前；journey 旅程；take 花费 语法will take 表示将来；take + 时间 表示“花费时间”。

What will the astronauts eat and drink during this period?

在这段时间里，宇航员吃什么、喝什么呢？

词汇astronaut 宇航员；during 在……期间 语法what 引导特殊疑问句；during this period 作时间状语。

And how about their health and safety?

他们的健康和安全又怎么办呢？

词汇health 健康；safety 安全 语法How about ...? 用于询问情况。

These problems grow when we talk about visiting another star.

当我们谈到访问另一颗恒星时，这些问题会更多。

词汇grow 增加；扩大 语法when 引导时间状语从句。

It might take hundreds of thousands of years to get there!

到那里可能要花数十万年！

词汇hundreds of thousands of 数以十万计的 语法might 表示推测；it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to get there。

Scientists, however, believe that visiting the stars is not a completely wild dream.

然而，科学家们相信，访问恒星并非完全不切实际的幻想。

词汇scientist 科学家；completely 完全地；wild 不切实际的 语法however 作插入语表示转折；that 引导宾语从句，动名词短语作从句主语。

One idea is to have the astronauts sleep during their journey like some animals do in winter.

一个想法是让宇航员在旅途中像一些动物在冬天那样睡觉。

词汇have sb. do sth. 让某人做某事；journey 旅程 语法have sb. do sth. 表示“让某人做某事”；like 引导方式状语从句。

Another is to develop much faster spacecraft.

另一个想法是研制快得多的宇宙飞船。

词汇another 另一个；develop 研制 语法Another 指代 another idea；不定式作表语；much 修饰比较级 faster。

While this is not possible right now, with nations and scientists cooperating with one another, there is hope that the necessary technology will be developed.

虽然目前这还不可能，但随着各国和科学家齐心协力，有希望开发出必要的技术。

词汇while 虽然；cooperate 合作；necessary 必要的 语法while 引导让步状语从句；with + 宾语 + cooperating 是 with 复合结构；that 引导同位语从句说明 hope；will be developed 是将来时被动语态。

Humans have set foot on the moon, successfully landed rovers on Mars, and even photographed other galaxies.

人类已经踏上月球，成功把探测车送上火星，甚至拍摄到了其他星系。

词汇set foot on 踏上；rover 探测车；galaxy 星系 语法现在完成时 have set / landed / photographed 三个谓语并列。

In time, it seems likely that one day we may reach the stars as people once dreamt.

最终，似乎有一天我们可能会像人们曾经梦想的那样抵达星辰。

词汇in time 最终；seem likely 似乎很可能 语法it seems likely that ... 中 it 是形式主语；as 引导方式状语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

（教材 P56–P57 两页合并）这篇科普文章探讨人类能否访问恒星。文章从人类自古仰望星空、好奇星星是什么说起，指出星星是像太阳一样的真实天体，只是极其遥远。接着以旅行者 1 号为例：它是距离地球最远的人造物体，1977 年发射，携带记录地球声音和图片的唱片；它现在距地球约 235 亿公里，但离最近的恒星比邻星（4.2 光年，约 40 万亿公里）仍很远。载人星际旅行更难：人类到过的最远距离只有约 40 万公里，即使去火星也要六到九个月，途中宇航员的吃住、健康和安全都是问题，访问恒星甚至可能要数十万年。不过科学家相信这并非完全不切实际的幻想：一个想法是让宇航员像冬眠动物一样在旅途中睡觉，另一个是研制更快的飞船；只要各国和科学家合作，就有希望开发出所需技术。人类已登月、 successfully 把探测车送上火星并拍摄到其他星系，最终也许会像人们梦想的那样抵达星辰。

English Summary

(Merged from pages 56–57.) This popular science article asks whether humans can visit the stars. It begins with people looking up at the stars since the beginning of human history and wondering what they were like; stars are real places like our own sun, only much further away than anyone imagined. It then gives the example of Voyager 1, the man-made object that has travelled furthest from Earth: launched in 1977 and carrying a special record of sounds and pictures from Earth, it is now about 23.5 billion kilometres away, yet still a long way from Earth's nearest star, Proxima Centauri—4.2 light years, or about 40 trillion kilometres away. Visiting the stars is even harder for manned space flight: the furthest any human has gone is only about 400,000 kilometres, even a journey to Mars will take about six to nine months, and astronauts' food, health and safety are big problems; visiting another star might take hundreds of thousands of years. Scientists, however, believe it is not a completely wild dream: one idea is to have astronauts sleep during the journey like hibernating animals, and another is to develop much faster spacecraft. While this is not possible right now, with nations and scientists cooperating there is hope that the necessary technology will be developed. Humans have set foot on the moon, successfully landed rovers on Mars and photographed other galaxies, so in time it seems likely that we may reach the stars as people once dreamt.

  • weather satellite 气象卫星
  • positioning satellite 定位卫星

语法点 Grammar

In October 1957, people listening to radios around the world heard something no one had ever heard before.

现在分词短语 listening to radios 作后置定语；过去完成时 had ever heard。

The beep itself was not all that amazing.

not all that + 形容词，表示“没那么……”。

The amazing thing was the place where it came from—space.

where 引导定语从句；破折号补充说明。

The beep came from the world's first man-made satellite, Sputnik 1.

man-made 是复合形容词；Sputnik 1 是同位语。

Sputnik 1 was launched by the Soviet Union on 4 October 1957.

一般过去时被动语态 was launched；on + 具体日期。

It weighed about 84 kilograms, travelled at 29,000 kilometres an hour, and took 96.2 minutes to circle Earth.

三个并列谓语；at + 速度；take time to do sth.。

Sputnik 1 stopped beeping when its radio battery died on 26 October, and it burnt up when it fell back to Earth on 4 January 1958.

stop doing sth.；when 引导时间状语从句；burnt 是 burn 的过去式。

The first satellites were used for research.

一般过去时被动语态；be used for + 名词。

They also send back photographs that help us to make better maps of our world.

that 引导定语从句；help sb. to do sth.。

These allow TV and radio signals to be sent across oceans or to places thousands of kilometres away.

allow sth. to be done；to be sent 是不定式的被动式。

This is how people can watch TV across continents and oceans.

how 引导表语从句。

This satellite works with a highly complicated system, like the BDS (BeiDou Navigation Satellite System), which can tell us where our car or even our mobile phone is.

which 引导非限制性定语从句；where 引导宾语从句。

The only real limit to their use is our own imagination.

the limit to ... 表示“……的局限”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

They also send back photographs that help us to make better maps of our world.

它们还会发回照片，帮助我们绘制更准确的世界地图。

that 引导定语从句修饰 photographs；help sb. to do sth.。

These allow TV and radio signals to be sent across oceans or to places thousands of kilometres away.

这些卫星使电视和无线电信号能够跨越大洋，发送到数千公里之外的地方。

allow sth. to be done 表示“允许/使……被……”；to be sent 是不定式的被动式。

This satellite works with a highly complicated system, like the BDS (BeiDou Navigation Satellite System), which can tell us where our car or even our mobile phone is.

这种卫星与高度复杂的系统协同工作，比如北斗卫星导航系统，它能告诉我们汽车甚至手机的位置。

which 引导非限制性定语从句；where 引导宾语从句。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面四篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What is the first reason Dr Yang gives for exploring space?

  1. To improve our knowledge of Earth and its place in the universe.
  2. To find new homes for animals.
  3. To build more satellites for TV.
  4. To travel to the moon for holidays.
答案与解析

A 原文：First, to improve our knowledge of Earth and its place in the universe. 可知第一个理由是增进对地球及其在宇宙中位置的认识。

2. Which spacecraft has travelled furthest from Earth?

  1. Shenzhou.
  2. Voyager 1.
  3. Sputnik 1.
  4. Chang'e.
答案与解析

B 原文：The man-made object which has travelled furthest from Earth is Voyager 1. 可知是旅行者 1 号。

3. What does the writer of My Space Dream want to be?

  1. A space scientist.
  2. A teacher.
  3. One of the first visitors to the moon space station.
  4. An astronaut on Mars.
答案与解析

C 原文：I want to be one of the first visitors. 可知作者想成为第一批参观者之一。

4. When and by whom was Sputnik 1 launched?

  1. In October 1957 by the USA.
  2. In January 1958 by the Soviet Union.
  3. In October 1957 by China.
  4. In October 1957 by the Soviet Union.
答案与解析

D 原文：Sputnik 1 was launched by the Soviet Union on 4 October 1957. 可知是 1957 年 10 月由苏联发射。

5. Which technology does Dr Yang mention as an example of space technology helping us on Earth?

  1. Satellites that help deal with problems caused by climate change.
  2. Rockets that carry tourists.
  3. Space stations on the moon.
  4. Telescopes that photograph galaxies.
答案与解析

A 原文：Some satellites, for example, help us deal with problems caused by climate change. 可知卫星是太空技术服务地球的例子。

6. Why is visiting the stars difficult for humans?

  1. Because stars are too bright.
  2. Because the distances are huge and the journeys would take a very long time.
  3. Because there is no air in space.
  4. Because astronauts cannot sleep in space.
答案与解析

B 原文：Time is a big problem. ... even a journey to the planet Mars will take around six to nine months ... It might take hundreds of thousands of years to get there! 可知距离远、耗时极长是主要困难。

7. What is the second benefit in My Space Dream?

  1. More tourists can visit the moon.
  2. New technologies can be tested there.
  3. More resources will be found.
  4. People can live and work there.
答案与解析

C 原文：Second, more resources will be found. 可知第二个好处是会发现更多资源。

8. What can weather satellites tell us?

  1. Where our car is.
  2. How to make better maps of the world.
  3. When TV signals are sent.
  4. When a storm is coming or whether it will snow next week.
答案与解析

D 原文：These can tell us when a storm is coming or whether it will snow next week. 可知气象卫星能预报暴风雨和降雪。
