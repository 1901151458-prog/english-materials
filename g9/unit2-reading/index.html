Section A 3a · Who do we admire?

Section A 3a 主题：钦佩的人 · 对话

Adam: Hi, everyone. Who is the person you admire the most?

大家好。你们最钦佩的人是谁？

词汇admire 钦佩；the most 最 语法who 引导宾语从句；the person you admire 后省略关系代词 who/that，you admire 是定语从句。

Yaming: My favourite person is I.M. Pei.

我最喜欢的人是贝聿铭。

词汇favourite 最喜欢的；I.M. Pei 贝聿铭（著名建筑师） 语法favourite 作定语；I.M. Pei 是专有名词。

Adam: Did he design the glass pyramid at the Louvre Museum?

他设计了卢浮宫的玻璃金字塔吗？

词汇design 设计；glass pyramid 玻璃金字塔；Louvre Museum 卢浮宫博物馆 语法一般过去时 Did...design...?；at the Louvre Museum 表示地点。

Yaming: Yes. It was very creative of him to design it.

是的。他设计它，真是非常有创意。

词汇creative 有创造力的；of him 表示“他具有这种品质” 语法It was + adj. + of sb. + to do sth.；creative 描述人的品质，用 of。

Yaming: The Suzhou Museum is also one of his amazing designs.

苏州博物馆也是他令人惊叹的设计之一。

词汇Suzhou Museum 苏州博物馆；one of... ……之一；amazing 令人惊叹的 语法one of + 形容词最高级/复数名词；designs 用复数。

Yaming: He created a beautiful balance between the East and the West.

他在东西方之间创造了一种美妙的平衡。

词汇create 创造；balance 平衡；between...and... 在……和……之间 语法between the East and the West 表示“东西方之间”；a beautiful balance 作宾语。

Adam: Wow, you really know a lot about him!

哇，你真的很了解他！

词汇know a lot about 对……了解很多 语法感叹句；know about 是固定搭配。

Yaming: I read books and reports about him.

我读过关于他的书和报道。

词汇report 报道；about 关于 语法一般过去时 read；books and reports 并列宾语。

Yaming: I want to be a creative person like him.

我想成为一个像他一样有创造力的人。

词汇want to be 想成为；like 像 语法want to do sth.；like him 作后置定语/介词短语。

Adam: What about you, Chen Jie? Who inspires you?

你呢，陈洁？谁激励着你？

词汇what about... ……呢；inspire 激励 语法What about...? 是省略问句；who 作主语。

Chen Jie: Wang Yaping, of course, China's first woman to walk in space.

当然是王亚平，中国第一位进行太空行走的女性。

词汇of course 当然；walk in space 太空行走 语法China's first woman to walk... 中 to walk 作后置定语。

Chen Jie: She is full of courage and wisdom.

她充满勇气和智慧。

词汇be full of 充满；courage 勇气；wisdom 智慧 语法be full of + 名词；courage 和 wisdom 并列。

Chen Jie: She was also the first woman to teach a live class from space.

她也是第一位从太空进行直播授课的女性。

词汇live class 直播课；from space 从太空 语法the first + 名词 + to do 结构；from space 表示地点来源。

Chen Jie: What about you, Adam? Who do you look up to?

你呢，亚当？你钦佩谁？

词汇look up to 钦佩；仰慕 语法look up to sb. 是固定短语；who 作介词宾语。

Adam: Me? My biggest inspiration is not a famous person.

我？我最大的激励者不是名人。

词汇inspiration 激励者/灵感；famous 著名的 语法biggest 是 big 的最高级；not a famous person 作表语。

Adam: It's my father.

是我的父亲。

词汇father 父亲 语法it 指“我最大的激励者”。

Adam: It's really very nice of him to support and encourage my exchange study here.

他支持并鼓励我在这里交换学习，真是太好了。

词汇support 支持；encourage 鼓励；exchange study 交换学习 语法It is + adj. + of sb. + to do sth.；nice 描述人的品质，用 of。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕“你最钦佩谁”展开。Yaming 钦佩建筑师贝聿铭，因为他设计了卢浮宫玻璃金字塔和苏州博物馆，在东西方之间创造了美的平衡；Chen Jie 钦佩王亚平，因为她是中国第一位太空行走的女性，也是第一位从太空直播授课的女性；Adam 最钦佩自己的父亲，因为父亲支持并鼓励他来中国交换学习。

English Summary

In the conversation, Adam asks his classmates who they admire most. Yaming admires architect I.M. Pei because he designed the glass pyramid at the Louvre Museum and the Suzhou Museum, creating a beautiful balance between East and West. Chen Jie admires Wang Yaping, China's first woman to walk in space and the first woman to teach a live class from space. Adam's biggest inspiration is his father, who supports and encourages his exchange study in China.

语法点 Grammar

Who is the person you admire the most?

who 引导宾语从句；the person you admire 中省略了关系代词 who/that，you admire 是定语从句。

It was very creative of him to design it.

It is/was + adj. + of sb. + to do sth.；creative 描述人的品质，用 of。

The Suzhou Museum is also one of his amazing designs.

one of + 形容词 + 复数名词，表示“……之一”。

He created a beautiful balance between the East and the West.

between...and... 表示“在……和……之间”。

She was also the first woman to teach a live class from space.

the first + 名词 + to do sth.，不定式作后置定语。

It's really very nice of him to support and encourage my exchange study here.

It is + adj. + of sb. + to do sth.；nice 描述人的品质，用 of。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Who is the person you admire the most?

你们最钦佩的人是谁？

the person 后接省略关系代词的定语从句 you admire；the most 是副词最高级。

It was very creative of him to design it.

他设计它，真是非常有创意。

it 是形式主语；to design it 是真正主语；of him 表示“他具有 creative 这种品质”。

It's really very nice of him to support and encourage my exchange study here.

他支持并鼓励我在这里交换学习，真是太好了。

It is + adj. + of sb. + to do；support 和 encourage 并列；here 修饰 exchange study。

Section B 1b · The Inspiring Seed Scientist

Section B 1b 主题：种子科学家钟扬 · 人物故事

On 25 September 2017, the scientific world was shaken by the news that Chinese professor Zhong Yang had died in a car accident.

2017 年 9 月 25 日，中国教授钟扬因车祸去世的消息震惊了科学界。

词汇scientific world 科学界；be shaken by 被……震惊；professor 教授；car accident 车祸 语法一般过去时被动语态 was shaken by；that 引导同位语从句，说明 news 的内容；had died 是过去完成时。

The well-respected biologist had spent his whole life researching plants and seeds.

这位备受尊敬的生物学家一生都在研究植物和种子。

词汇well-respected 备受尊敬的；biologist 生物学家；spend...doing 花费……做 语法had spent 是过去完成时；spend one's life doing sth. 表示“一生从事……”。

Over the years, he collected many important seeds for China's seed banks.

多年来，他为中国的种子库收集了许多重要的种子。

词汇over the years 多年来；collect 收集；seed bank 种子库 语法一般过去时；for China's seed banks 表示目的/对象。

These special banks protect all kinds of plant seeds, including the ones we need for food, and they make sure that important plants do not disappear from the world forever.

这些特殊的种子库保护各种植物种子，包括我们需要作为食物的种子，并确保重要植物不会从世界上永远消失。

词汇protect 保护；all kinds of 各种各样的；including 包括；make sure 确保；disappear 消失 语法including 是介词；make sure that... 引导宾语从句；do not disappear 表示否定。

Zhong Yang was born in 1964.

钟扬出生于 1964 年。

词汇be born 出生 语法一般过去时被动结构 be born。

When he was young, he showed a deep love and curiosity for the things he studied.

年轻时，他对所研究的事物表现出深深的热爱和好奇。

词汇curiosity 好奇心；show 表现出；the things he studied 他研究的事物 语法when 引导时间状语从句；he studied 是省略关系代词的定语从句。

He was an excellent student, entering university at the early age of 15.

他是个优秀的学生，15 岁就上了大学。

词汇excellent 优秀的；enter university 上大学；at the age of 15 在 15 岁 语法entering... 是现在分词短语作伴随状语；at the early age of 15。

There, he studied radio engineering and later became interested in plants.

在那里，他学习无线电工程，后来对植物产生了兴趣。

词汇radio engineering 无线电工程；become interested in 对……产生兴趣 语法and 连接并列谓语 studied 和 became；be interested in 是固定搭配。

The research and study of plants, especially the seeds, became the focus of his life after he became a professor at Fudan University.

植物研究，尤其是种子研究，在他成为复旦大学教授后成为他生活的重心。

词汇research 研究；especially 尤其；focus 焦点；professor 教授 语法after 引导时间状语从句；the focus of his life 作表语。

It is important for seed banks to include many different kinds of seeds.

种子库包含许多不同种类的种子是很重要的。

词汇include 包含；different kinds of 不同种类的 语法It is + adj. + for sb./sth. + to do sth.；it 是形式主语。

When Zhong Yang discovered that there was not enough research of the seeds from Xizang, he went there to collect seeds himself.

钟扬发现对西藏种子的研究不足时，便亲自去那里收集种子。

词汇discover 发现；not enough 不足；Xizang 西藏；collect 收集 语法when 引导时间状语从句；that 引导宾语从句；to collect 作目的状语。

In 2001, he began his travels across Xizang.

2001 年，他开始穿越西藏的考察之旅。

词汇begin 开始；travel 旅行；across 穿越 语法一般过去时；across Xizang 表示范围。

It was very difficult for him to brave the cold weather and great heights.

对他而言，勇敢面对寒冷天气和高海拔非常困难。

词汇brave v. 勇敢面对；cold weather 寒冷天气；great heights 高海拔 语法It was + adj. + for sb. + to do sth.；brave 在这里是动词。

He sometimes fell sick, but he always continued with his work.

他有时会生病，但总是继续工作。

词汇fall sick 生病；continue with 继续 语法but 连接转折；continued 是过去式。

Zhong Yang knew it was better to include more people when doing important work.

钟扬知道做重要工作时，让更多人参与更好。

词汇include 包括；when doing 当做……时 语法knew 后接宾语从句；it was better to include...；when doing 是省略主语的时间状语。

He began a programme to find and train local students in field research to become scientists.

他启动了一个项目，寻找并培养当地学生做野外研究，使他们成为科学家。

词汇programme 项目；train 培养；field research 野外研究 语法to find and train 作目的状语；train sb. to do sth. 培养某人做某事。

Together, he and his team collected more than 40 million seeds from around 1,000 plants.

他和团队一起从约 1,000 种植物中收集了超过 4,000 万粒种子。

词汇together 一起；more than 超过；million 百万；collect 收集 语法he and his team 作主语；from around 1,000 plants 表示来源。

Their hard work provided the world with lots of new information about the plants in China.

他们的辛勤工作为世界提供了大量关于中国植物的新信息。

词汇provide sb. with sth. 为某人提供某物；information 信息 语法provide the world with...；about the plants in China 作后置定语。

Zhong Yang left behind more than just a huge collection of seeds.

钟扬留下的不仅仅是一大批种子。

词汇leave behind 留下；more than just 不仅仅是；collection 收藏 语法more than just 强调“不只是”；a huge collection of 大量……。

His love for his job and research lives on in his students.

他对工作和研究的热爱在学生身上延续。

词汇live on 继续存在；love for 对……的热爱 语法live on 是固定短语；in his students 表示范围。

He once said, "My students will continue on the road of scientific exploration and the seeds we have collected may grow hundreds of years from now."

他曾说：“我的学生将继续走在科学探索的道路上，我们收集的种子也许几百年后还会生长。”

词汇once 曾经；continue 继续；exploration 探索；hundreds of years 数百年 语法直接引语；will continue 一般将来时；we have collected 是现在完成时的定语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍中国植物学家、种子科学家钟扬。2017 年他因车祸去世，科学界为之震惊。他一生研究植物和种子，为中国种子库收集大量重要种子。钟扬 1964 年出生，15 岁上大学，后来成为复旦大学教授。因为西藏的种子研究不足，他 2001 年起赴藏收集种子，克服寒冷、高海拔和疾病，并培养当地学生；团队收集了 4,000 多万粒种子。他留下的不只是种子，还有对科研的热爱和继续探索的学生。

English Summary

The passage remembers Chinese botanist and seed scientist Zhong Yang, who died in a car accident in 2017. He spent his life researching plants and seeds and collected many important seeds for China's seed banks. Born in 1964, he entered university at 15 and later became a professor at Fudan University. Concerned about the lack of research on seeds in Xizang, he began collecting there in 2001, braving cold, high altitude and illness, and trained local students. His team collected over 40 million seeds from about 1,000 plants. He left behind not only seeds but also his love for research and students who continue his work.

Section B 3a · Mozi

Section B 3a 主题：思想家墨子 · 和平与爱

To me, one of the most inspiring people in Chinese history is Mozi (about 468-376 BCE).

在我看来，中国历史上最鼓舞人心的人物之一是墨子（约公元前 468—前 376 年）。

词汇inspiring 鼓舞人心的；one of... ……之一；BCE 公元前 语法one of + the most + 形容词 + 复数名词；to me 表示“在我看来”。

It is said that Mozi was a soldier, good at woodwork, and was the inventor of the kite.

相传墨子是一名士兵，擅长木工，还是风筝的发明者。

词汇it is said that 据说；soldier 士兵；be good at 擅长；inventor 发明者；kite 风筝 语法It is said that... 是固定句型；good at woodwork 是形容词短语作补充说明。

But in fact, above all, he was a great thinker.

但实际上，最重要的是，他是一位伟大的思想家。

词汇in fact 事实上；above all 最重要的是；thinker 思想家 语法above all 作插入语；was 是系动词。

Mozi lived during the Warring States Period.

墨子生活在战国时期。

词汇Warring States Period 战国时期 语法during 表示时间；一般过去时。

Back then, many kingdoms were fighting against each other.

那时，许多诸侯国互相攻伐。

词汇back then 那时；kingdom 王国/诸侯国；fight against 与……作战 语法过去进行时 were fighting 表示当时持续的动作。

In these wars, the common people were always hurt.

在这些战争中，普通百姓总是受到伤害。

词汇common people 普通百姓；hurt 伤害 语法一般过去时被动语态 were hurt。

To Mozi, this had to stop, so he spent his life trying to bring peace.

在墨子看来，这种情况必须停止，因此他一生都在努力带来和平。

词汇have to 必须；spend one's life doing 一生做……；bring peace 带来和平 语法this had to stop 表示“必须停止”；so 连接结果；spent...trying 是固定结构。

His idea of love was one reason that Mozi wanted peace.

他的“爱”的思想是墨子想要和平的一个原因。

词汇idea of love 爱的思想；reason 原因；peace 和平 语法that 引导同位语从句/定语从句，说明 reason 的内容。

It was important to him that everyone should show the same kindness and care to everyone else, whether they were family, friends, or strangers.

对他而言，每个人都应该对他人表现出同样的善意和关怀，无论对方是家人、朋友还是陌生人，这一点很重要。

词汇kindness 善意；care 关怀；whether...or... 无论……还是……；stranger 陌生人 语法It was + adj. + that 从句；whether...or... 引导让步状语从句。

He also believed states should avoid attacking one another.

他还认为各诸侯国应该避免互相攻击。

词汇state 国家；avoid doing 避免做；attack 攻击；one another 互相 语法believed 后接宾语从句；avoid + 动名词。

Though Mozi lived more than 2,000 years ago, his ideas live on.

虽然墨子生活在两千多年前，但他的思想仍然存在。

词汇though 虽然；live on 继续存在 语法though 引导让步状语从句，不能与 but 连用。

I think he is still a great inspiration to many people.

我认为他仍然是许多人的巨大激励。

词汇inspiration 激励；still 仍然 语法I think 后接宾语从句；inspiration to sb. 对某人的激励。

Now, more than ever, people around the world need to hear the message of peace and love, and work together to build a community of a shared future for mankind.

现在，世界各国人民比以往任何时候都更需要听到和平与爱的信息，并携手共建人类命运共同体。

词汇more than ever 比以往任何时候都；message 信息；work together 合作；community of a shared future for mankind 人类命运共同体 语法need to do 需要做；and 连接两个不定式；more than ever 作插入语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍中国历史上鼓舞人心的人物墨子。墨子约生活于公元前 468—前 376 年，是战国时期的思想家。他反对诸侯混战，因为战争伤害百姓，所以一生追求和平。他主张“兼爱”，认为人们应平等地关爱所有人，不论亲疏；也主张国家之间不要互相攻击。虽然墨子生活在两千多年前，但他的思想仍激励着今天的人们，呼吁世界和平与爱，共建人类命运共同体。

English Summary

The passage introduces Mozi, one of the most inspiring people in Chinese history. Living during the Warring States Period, he opposed the wars between kingdoms because they hurt common people, and spent his life trying to bring peace. He believed in universal love and kindness toward everyone, whether family, friends or strangers, and thought states should avoid attacking one another. Although he lived over 2,000 years ago, his ideas still inspire people today to work for peace and love and build a community of a shared future for mankind.

语法点 Grammar

one of the most inspiring people in Chinese history is Mozi

one of + the most + 形容词 + 复数名词，作主语时谓语用单数 is。

It is said that Mozi was a soldier...

It is said that... 是固定句型，it 是形式主语，that 从句是真正主语。

he spent his life trying to bring peace

spend one's life doing sth.；trying 是动名词作宾语补足语。

It was important to him that everyone should show the same kindness and care...

It was + adj. + that 从句；that 从句是真正主语。

whether they were family, friends, or strangers

whether...or... 引导让步状语从句。

He also believed states should avoid attacking one another.

believed 后接宾语从句；avoid + 动名词。

Though Mozi lived more than 2,000 years ago, his ideas live on.

though 引导让步状语从句；live on 表示“继续存在”。

people ... need to hear the message of peace and love, and work together to build a community of a shared future for mankind

need to do sth.；and 连接两个不定式；community of a shared future for mankind 是固定表达。

1. Who does Yaming admire most?

  1. His father.
  2. I.M. Pei.
  3. Wang Yaping.
  4. Mozi.

2. Why does Chen Jie admire Wang Yaping?

  1. Because she is a famous singer.
  2. Because she was China's first woman to walk in space and taught a live class from space.
  3. Because she designed the Suzhou Museum.
  4. Because she is Adam's mother.
答案与解析

B 原文：China's first woman to walk in space ... the first woman to teach a live class from space.

3. Why does Adam look up to his father?

  1. His father is a famous person.
  2. His father supports and encourages his exchange study in China.
  3. His father designed the Louvre Museum.
  4. His father is a scientist.
答案与解析

B 原文：It's really very nice of him to support and encourage my exchange study here.

4. What does “the scientific world was shaken by the news” suggest?

  1. The news was not important.
  2. The news shocked the scientific world.
  3. The news made people happy.
  4. The news was about a football match.
答案与解析

B be shaken by 表示“被……震惊”；说明钟扬去世的消息让科学界震惊。

5. Why did Zhong Yang go to Xizang?

  1. To travel for fun.
  2. Because there was not enough research on seeds from Xizang.
  3. To become a teacher there.
  4. Because he was born there.
答案与解析

B 原文：When Zhong Yang discovered that there was not enough research of the seeds from Xizang, he went there to collect seeds himself.

6. What did Zhong Yang's team achieve?

  1. They collected over 40 million seeds from about 1,000 plants.
  2. They built a new university.
  3. They invented the kite.
  4. They wrote a book about Mozi.
答案与解析

A 原文：Together, he and his team collected more than 40 million seeds from around 1,000 plants.

7. What did Mozi believe?

  1. People should only care for their family.
  2. Everyone should show kindness and care to others, and states should avoid attacking one another.
  3. War is the best way to solve problems.
  4. Only soldiers should be respected.
答案与解析

B 原文：everyone should show the same kindness and care to everyone else ... states should avoid attacking one another.

8. What do the three texts have in common?

  4. They all talk about space travel.

B 三篇文章分别介绍贝聿铭/王亚平/亚当父亲、钟扬和墨子，主题都是“鼓舞人心的人及其品质”。
