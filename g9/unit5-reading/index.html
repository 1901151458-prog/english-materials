Section A 3a · The most useful invention

Section A 3a 主题：最有用发明 · 对话

Fu Xing: What do you think is the most useful invention of all time, Peter?

彼得，你认为有史以来最有用的发明是什么？

词汇invention 发明；all time 有史以来；useful 有用的 语法What do you think...? 中 do you think 是插入语，后面用陈述语序；the most useful 是最高级。

Peter: It has to be the radio.

肯定是收音机。

词汇have to be 肯定是；radio 收音机 语法has to be 表示肯定推测；it 指最有用的发明。

Fu Xing: The radio? Why?

收音机？为什么？

词汇why 为什么 语法省略问句，完整为 Why do you think so?

Peter: Before the radio was invented, information travelled very slowly.

在收音机发明之前，信息传播非常缓慢。

词汇invent 发明；information 信息；travel 传播；slowly 缓慢地 语法before 引导时间状语从句；was invented 是一般过去时被动语态；travelled 是过去式。

Peter: The world was made much smaller by the radio.

收音机让世界变得小多了。

词汇make 使；much smaller 小得多 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补；much 修饰比较级；by the radio 表示方式。

Peter: Suddenly, news could be spread around the world in seconds.

突然间，新闻能在几秒钟内传遍全世界。

词汇suddenly 突然；spread 传播；around the world 全世界；in seconds 几秒钟内 语法could be spread 是情态动词的被动语态；in + 时间表示“在……之内”。

Fu Xing: Right. Who was the inventor?

没错。发明者是谁？

词汇inventor 发明者 语法who 作主语；was 是系动词。

Peter: An Italian inventor called Guglielmo Marconi.

一位名叫古列尔莫·马可尼的意大利发明家。

词汇Italian 意大利的；called 名叫 语法called... 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

Peter: He developed the idea in the 1890s.

他在 19 世纪 90 年代提出了这一构想。

词汇develop 发展；提出；idea 构想；in the 1890s 在 19 世纪 90 年代 语法一般过去时；in the + 年份复数表示某个年代。

Peter: In 1901, a radio message was sent across the Atlantic Ocean for the first time.

1901 年，无线电信息首次横跨大西洋发送成功。

词汇message 信息；send 发送；across 横跨；Atlantic Ocean 大西洋；for the first time 首次 语法一般过去时被动语态 was sent；across 表示“横跨”。

Fu Xing: Wow! That was quite a distance at that time.

哇！这在当时可是相当远的距离。

词汇distance 距离；at that time 在当时 语法that 指横跨大西洋这件事；quite a distance 表示“相当远”。

Peter: Yeah. And because of the radio, some other important inventions were developed as well.

是啊。而且因为有了收音机，其他一些重要发明也随之被研发出来。

词汇because of 因为；as well 也 语法because of + 名词短语；were developed 是一般过去时被动语态。

Fu Xing: Like the television?

比如电视？

词汇television 电视 语法Like...? 是省略问句。

Peter: That's right. It also had a big effect on music. The radio made it much easier for people to enjoy music from artists around the world.

没错。它还对音乐产生了巨大影响。收音机让人们更容易欣赏到来自世界各地艺术家的音乐。

词汇have an effect on 对……有影响；make it easier 使……更容易；artist 艺术家 语法have a big effect on；make + it + adj. + for sb. + to do sth.；from around the world 作后置定语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕“有史以来最有用的发明”展开。付星问彼得，彼得认为是收音机。收音机发明前信息传播很慢；收音机让世界变小，新闻能在几秒内传遍世界。发明者是意大利人马可尼，他在 19 世纪 90 年代提出构想，1901 年无线电信息首次横跨大西洋。收音机还促进了电视等其他发明，并让人们更容易欣赏世界各地艺术家的音乐。

English Summary

Fu Xing asks Peter what the most useful invention of all time is. Peter says it has to be the radio. Before the radio was invented, information travelled slowly; the radio made the world much smaller and allowed news to spread around the world in seconds. The inventor was Guglielmo Marconi, who developed the idea in the 1890s; in 1901 a radio message crossed the Atlantic for the first time. The radio also led to other inventions such as television and made it easier to enjoy music from artists around the world.

Section B 1b · An Invention That Changed the World

Section B 1b 主题：造纸术 · 改变世界的发明

Can you imagine a world without books, newspapers, or magazines?

你能想象一个没有书籍、报纸或杂志的世界吗？

词汇imagine 想象；newspaper 报纸；magazine 杂志 语法Can you imagine...? 情态动词疑问句；without... 作后置定语。

All of these things depend on one invention — papermaking.

所有这些事物都依赖于一项发明——造纸术。

词汇depend on 依赖；papermaking 造纸术 语法depend on 是固定搭配；破折号后解释 one invention。

It, more than any other invention, was responsible for the rapid spread of information that shaped our modern world.

比起其他任何发明，正是它推动了信息的快速传播，从而塑造了我们现代的世界。

词汇more than any other 比任何其他……都；be responsible for 对……负责/是……的原因；rapid 快速的；spread 传播；shape 塑造 语法more than any other invention 是插入语；that shaped... 是定语从句。

In ancient China, many different materials were used to write on, such as animal bones, bronze wares, stones, wood, bamboo, and even silk.

在中国古代，人们使用许多不同的材料来书写，例如兽骨、青铜器、石头、木头、竹子，甚至丝绸。

词汇ancient 古代的；material 材料；be used to 被用来；bronze 青铜；silk 丝绸 语法被动语态 were used to write on；such as 举例。

In the Western Han Dynasty, there was also a very early form of paper made from jute — a fibre that comes from plants.

西汉时期，也出现过一种用黄麻（一种来自植物的纤维）制成的非常早期的纸。

词汇Western Han Dynasty 西汉；form 形式；be made from 由……制成；jute 黄麻；fibre 纤维 语法made from jute 是过去分词短语作后置定语；that comes from plants 是定语从句。

However, all of these materials were either difficult to use and store or very expensive.

然而，所有这些材料要么难以使用和存放，要么价格昂贵。

词汇however 然而；store 存放；expensive 昂贵的 语法either...or... 连接两个并列表语；be difficult to do。

As the head officer of the palace workshop for the emperor in the Eastern Han Dynasty, Cai Lun became interested in trying to solve the problem.

作为东汉时期皇家作坊的主管，蔡伦开始对解决这一问题产生兴趣。

词汇head officer 主管；palace workshop 宫廷作坊；emperor 皇帝；become interested in 对……产生兴趣；solve 解决 语法as 表示身份；become interested in + 动名词；try to do。

Different methods for creating something to write on were tried, but none were ideal.

人们尝试了各种不同的方法来制造书写材料，但没有一种是理想的。

词汇method 方法；create 创造；try 尝试；ideal 理想的 语法被动语态 were tried；none 作主语，谓语用 were；but 转折。

In his times, some kinds of bark — the outside part of trees — were used to make clothes.

在蔡伦所处的时代，某些种类的树皮——树木的外部部分——被用来制作衣服。

词汇bark 树皮；outside part 外部；be used to make 被用来制作 语法破折号中间是同位语解释 bark；were used to make。

From past experiences, Cai Lun got the idea that bark, certain plants, and old clothes could be used to make a special material suitable to write on.

从过往的经验中，蔡伦萌生了一个想法：树皮、特定的植物以及旧衣物，都可以用来制造一种适合书写的特殊材料。

词汇experience 经验；get the idea 萌生想法；certain 某些；suitable 合适的 语法that 引导同位语从句，说明 idea 的内容；be suitable to do。

In the first step of Cai Lun's experiment, all the ingredients were mixed and boiled in water.

在蔡伦实验的第一步，所有的原料被混合后在水中煮沸。

词汇step 步骤；experiment 实验；ingredient 原料；mix 混合；boil 煮沸 语法一般过去时被动语态 were mixed and boiled；in water 表示地点。

Then, this material was beaten, cut up, and mixed with water again.

接着，这种材料被打碎、切断，再次与水混合。

词汇beat 打碎；cut up 切碎；mix with 与……混合 语法被动语态 was beaten, cut up, mixed；again 表示再次。

Next, the liquid was poured over a bamboo screen, leaving just the fibre.

然后，液体被倾倒在竹筛上，只留下纤维。

词汇liquid 液体；pour 倾倒；bamboo screen 竹筛；leave 留下；fibre 纤维 语法被动语态 was poured；leaving... 是现在分词作结果状语。

Finally, this fibre was dried and pressed to form paper.

最后，这些纤维经干燥和按压后形成了纸张。

词汇finally 最后；dry 干燥；press 按压；form 形成 语法被动语态 was dried and pressed；to form 作目的状语。

To everyone's surprise, this paper was soft, light, easy to make and use, and most importantly, cheap.

令所有人惊讶的是，这种纸柔软、轻便、易于制作和使用，最重要的是，价格低廉。

词汇to one's surprise 令某人惊讶的是；soft 柔软的；light 轻的；cheap 便宜的 语法to everyone's surprise 作插入语；soft/light/easy/cheap 并列作表语。

In 105 CE, Cai Lun showed his creation to the emperor.

公元 105 年，蔡伦向皇帝展示了他的发明。

词汇CE 公元；show sth. to sb. 向某人展示某物；creation 创造物 语法show sth. to sb. 双宾语结构；一般过去时。

The emperor was very pleased and praised Cai Lun for his great work.

皇帝非常高兴，赞扬了蔡伦的伟大功绩。

词汇pleased 高兴的；praise sb. for sth. 因某事赞扬某人 语法be pleased；praise...for...；and 连接并列谓语。

Later on, the papermaking technique did not only speed up the development of Chinese culture, but also changed civilizations around the world.

后来，造纸术不仅加速了中国文化的发展，也改变了世界各地的文明。

词汇later on 后来；technique 技术；speed up 加速；civilization 文明 语法not only...but also... 连接并列谓语；did not only... 是强调结构。

It first spread to China's neighbouring countries and finally reached Europe through Arabic countries in the 12th century.

它首先传播到中国的邻国，最终在 12 世纪经由阿拉伯国家传到欧洲。

词汇spread 传播；neighbouring 邻近的；reach 到达；Arabic 阿拉伯的 语法first...finally... 表示顺序；through 表示途径。

When the use of paper and printing came together in later centuries, it led to a rapid increase in the number of books.

当纸张的使用与印刷术在后来的几个世纪中结合在一起时，导致了书籍数量的迅速增长。

词汇printing 印刷术；come together 结合；lead to 导致；increase 增长 语法when 引导时间状语从句；lead to + 名词。

This resulted in a new birth of knowledge, creating the world we have today.

这催生了知识的新生，造就了今天的这个世界。

词汇result in 导致；birth 诞生；knowledge 知识；create 创造 语法result in；creating... 是现在分词作结果状语；we have today 是省略关系代词的定语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍改变世界的发明——造纸术。古代中国用兽骨、青铜器、石头、木头、竹子、丝绸等书写，西汉有黄麻纸，但这些材料要么难用难存，要么昂贵。东汉蔡伦任宫廷作坊主管，从树皮制衣等经验中获得灵感，用树皮、植物和旧衣物做实验：混合煮沸、打碎、再混合、倒竹筛、干燥压制成纸。105 年献给皇帝，纸柔软轻便便宜。造纸术传到邻国、阿拉伯并到欧洲，与印刷术结合使书籍大增，推动知识新生和现代世界形成。

English Summary

The passage introduces papermaking, an invention that changed the world. In ancient China people wrote on animal bones, bronze, stone, wood, bamboo and silk; an early paper was made from jute in the Western Han Dynasty, but these materials were hard to use or store, or expensive. Cai Lun, head of the palace workshop in the Eastern Han Dynasty, got the idea of using bark, certain plants and old clothes. The ingredients were boiled, beaten, mixed with water, poured over a bamboo screen, dried and pressed. In 105 CE he showed the paper to the emperor; it was soft, light and cheap. Papermaking spread to neighbouring countries, Arabic countries and Europe, and together with printing led to more books and a new birth of knowledge.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍飞机的发明。飞机极大改变了世界，但莱特兄弟最初并没想改变世界，只想像鸟一样飞。为解决比空气重的飞行问题，他们研究滑翔机，面对两个挑战：如何让滑翔机升空、飞行中如何控制。他们通过观察鸟的飞行找到解决办法，之后给滑翔机装上发动机，1903 年 12 月完成世界首次飞机飞行。飞机让旅行更快更容易，过去需要数天、数周甚至数月的旅程现在只需几小时，促进了国际交流，世界变得更小。

English Summary

The passage is about the invention of the plane. It has greatly changed the world, but the Wright brothers did not set out to change the world—they just wanted to fly like birds. To solve heavier-than-air flight, they worked with gliders and faced two challenges: getting the glider into the air and controlling it in flight. They found solutions by studying how birds fly, then added an engine. In December 1903 the world's first plane flights were made. Planes make travel faster and easier, turning journeys of days, weeks or months into a few hours and bringing the world closer.
