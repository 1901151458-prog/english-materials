Section A 3a · Ella's podcast: learning English

Section A 3a 主题：学习方法 · 播客采访

Ella: Hi, this is Ella's podcast. Today's guest is Binbin. He's going to give us some ideas for learning English.

大家好，这里是埃拉的播客。今天的嘉宾是彬彬，他将给我们分享一些学英语的方法。

词汇podcast 播客；guest 嘉宾；idea 方法；learn English 学英语 语法this is... 介绍；be going to do 表示计划；give sb. sth. 双宾语。

Binbin: Hi, Ella. Thanks for having me! For learning English, I think first you need to become an active learner.

你好，埃拉。谢谢邀请我！学英语的话，我觉得首先得做一个主动学习者。

词汇thanks for doing 感谢做某事；active 主动的；learner 学习者 语法Thanks for doing sth.；I think 后接宾语从句；need to do sth.。

Ella: What do you mean by that?

你这话是什么意思？

词汇mean by 意思是；that 指上面的话 语法What do you mean by...? 是固定问法；by 表示方式。

Binbin: Well, because I want to speak English well, I practise English all the time.

嗯，因为我想把英语说好，所以一直都在练英语。

词汇practise 练习；all the time 一直 语法because 引导原因状语从句；want to do sth.；practise + 宾语。

Ella: Oh, you must spend a lot of time studying!

哦，那你一定花了很多时间学习吧！

词汇must 一定；spend time doing 花时间做 语法must 表示肯定推测；spend time doing sth.。

Binbin: Well, it doesn't mean you have to study all the time.

其实这不代表你得一直“学”。

词汇mean 意味着；have to 必须 语法mean + 宾语从句；have to do sth.；doesn't 否定。

Binbin: You need to spend time using the language.

你要花时间去“用”这门语言。

词汇spend time doing 花时间做；use 使用 语法need to do sth.；spend time doing sth.。

Binbin: For example, I speak English with friends.

比如，我和朋友用英语聊天。

词汇for example 例如；speak with 和……说 语法一般现在时表示习惯；with friends 作状语。

Binbin: I repeat the dialogues from the films I like.

我跟读我喜欢电影里的对话。

词汇repeat 重复；dialogue 对话；film 电影 语法I like 是省略关系代词的定语从句，修饰 films。

Binbin: I read English books, and sometimes I write my diary in English.

我读英文书，有时还用英语写日记。

词汇read 阅读；diary 日记；in English 用英语 语法and 连接并列句；sometimes 频度副词。

Ella: Wow! I guess you really enjoy learning.

哇！看来你真的很享受学习。

词汇guess 猜想；enjoy doing 喜欢做 语法I guess 后接宾语从句；enjoy + 动名词。

Binbin: Yes, it's true! But you see, I started out at zero and came across problems.

对，确实是。不过你看，我也是从零开始，中途碰到过很多问题。

词汇start out 开始；at zero 从零；come across 遇到 语法but 转折；and 连接两个并列谓语；started/came 过去式。

Binbin: I mean you just have to take things step by step.

我的意思是得循序渐进。

词汇mean 意思是；step by step 一步一步地 语法have to do；step by step 作方式状语。

Binbin: Start simple and build up.

从简单的入手，一点点积累。

词汇start simple 从简单开始；build up 积累 语法祈使句；and 连接两个动词短语。

Ella: That's so true.

太对了。

词汇true 真实的；so 如此 语法That's + adj. 表示认同。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段播客采访围绕“如何学英语”展开。Binbin 认为首先要成为主动学习者：不是一直“学”，而是花时间“用”英语，例如和朋友说英语、跟读电影对话、读英文书、用英语写日记。他还说自己也是从零开始，遇到过问题，所以要循序渐进，从简单的内容开始，逐步积累。

English Summary

In the podcast, Binbin shares ideas for learning English. He believes learners should first become active learners: instead of studying all the time, they should spend time using the language, such as speaking English with friends, repeating film dialogues, reading English books and writing a diary in English. He also says he started from zero and met problems, so learners should take things step by step, start simple and build up.

Section B 1b · How to Become a Successful Learner

Section B 1b 主题：学习科学 · 成长型思维

Everyone has the ability to learn.

每个人都有学习的能力。

词汇ability 能力；learn 学习 语法have the ability to do sth. 有做某事的能力。

Experts on the science of learning will tell you that anyone can become a successful learner.

学习科学领域的专家会告诉你，任何人都能成为成功的学习者。

词汇expert 专家；science of learning 学习科学；successful 成功的 语法that 引导宾语从句；anyone 作主语，谓语用 can become。

However, many students do not realize this, and they have trouble learning.

然而，许多学生没有意识到这一点，在学习上遇到困难。

词汇however 然而；realize 意识到；have trouble doing 做某事有困难 语法have trouble doing sth.；and 连接两个并列分句。

Studies show that the first key to becoming a successful learner is to have a growth mindset.

研究表明，成为成功学习者的首要关键是拥有成长型思维。

词汇study 研究；key to ……的关键；growth mindset 成长型思维 语法that 引导宾语从句；the key to doing sth.；to have 作表语。

This means, in part, believing in yourself.

这在某种程度上意味着相信自己。

词汇mean 意味着；in part 部分地；believe in 相信 语法mean doing sth.；in part 作插入语。

It also means setting goals, taking action, and meeting challenges.

也意味着设定目标、采取行动、迎接挑战。

词汇set goals 设定目标；take action 采取行动；meet challenges 迎接挑战 语法mean doing；三个动名词并列作宾语。

People with a growth mindset see mistakes as chances for learning, as they teach us how to improve.

拥有成长型思维的人把错误看作学习的机会，因为错误教会我们如何进步。

词汇mistake 错误；see...as... 把……看作；chance 机会；improve 进步 语法see sth. as sth.；as 引导原因状语从句；how to improve 作 teach 的宾语。

Research also shows that practice is important for learning.

研究还表明，练习对学习很重要。

词汇research 研究；practice 练习；be important for 对……重要 语法that 引导宾语从句；be important for。

The more we practise, the better we learn.

我们练习得越多，学得就越好。

词汇the more...the better... 越……越…… 语法the + 比较级..., the + 比较级... 句型。

We learn best by practising new and difficult things.

练习新的、有难度的内容，学习效果最佳。

词汇learn best 学得最好；by doing 通过做；difficult 困难的 语法by + 动名词表示方式；best 是副词最高级。

Sometimes it is also important to get guidance from a teacher or a coach.

有时从老师或教练那里获得指导也很重要。

词汇guidance 指导；coach 教练 语法It is + adj. + to do sth.；get...from...。

They can show us the right way to practise and give us important feedback and advice.

他们能告诉我们正确的练习方法，并给出重要反馈和建议。

词汇show sb. sth. 给某人看；feedback 反馈；advice 建议 语法show sb. sth.；way to do sth.；give sb. sth.。

In addition, studies show that students learn more when they hang out with other students who are serious about study.

此外，研究表明，和认真学习的学生相处，会学到更多。

词汇in addition 此外；hang out with 和……相处；serious about 认真对待 语法when 引导时间状语从句；who are serious about study 是定语从句。

First, by being together, students can easily learn from each other.

第一，在一起时，学生可以互相学习。

词汇by being together 通过在一起；learn from 向……学习；each other 互相 语法by + 动名词作方式状语；learn from each other。

Second, students are challenged to do better by each other.

第二，彼此会激励对方做得更好。

词汇challenge 挑战；do better 做得更好 语法be challenged to do sth.；by each other 表示施动者。

This has been proven to encourage learner growth.

这已被证明能促进学习者成长。

词汇prove 证明；encourage 促进；growth 成长 语法现在完成时被动语态 has been proven；be proven to do sth.。

Successful learners also use the best learning materials they can find.

成功的学习者也会使用能找到的优质学习材料。

词汇material 材料；find 找到 语法they can find 是省略关系代词的定语从句，修饰 materials。

In the 21st century, this means using new technology and resources, not just textbooks.

在 21 世纪，这意味着运用新技术和资源，而不只是课本。

词汇technology 技术；resource 资源；not just 不只是 语法mean doing sth.；not just... 表示“不只是”。

Since there are so many learning materials on the internet, it is important to think carefully and choose wisely what and how you study.

因为网上学习材料很多，认真思考、明智选择“学什么、怎么学”很重要。

词汇since 因为；choose 选择；wisely 明智地 语法since 引导原因状语从句；it is + adj. + to do；what and how 引导宾语从句。

Take advantage of the best tools and resources to learn wisely and efficiently.

充分利用优质工具和资源，做到智慧、高效学习。

词汇take advantage of 充分利用；tool 工具；efficiently 高效地 语法祈使句；to learn... 作目的状语。

Finally, successful learners see learning as part of daily life.

最后，成功的学习者把学习看作日常生活的一部分。

词汇finally 最后；see...as... 把……看作；daily life 日常生活 语法see sth. as sth.；part of...。

It is something they will do even after they leave school, and even when they are old.

即使毕业、即使年老，他们仍会学习。

词汇even 即使；leave school 毕业 语法they will do 是定语从句；even after/when 引导让步/时间状语。

As you continue on your learning journey, you should try to walk at your own speed and enjoy the trip.

在你的学习旅程中，应按自己的节奏前行，享受过程。

词汇continue 继续；journey 旅程；at one's own speed 按自己的节奏 语法as 引导时间状语从句；try to do；and 连接两个动词。

Never lose heart.

永远不要灰心。

词汇lose heart 灰心 语法祈使句否定式 Never + 动词原形。

The road ahead of you may be long and may have many mountains and valleys, but it also has great treasure.

前路可能很长，也会有高山低谷，但也藏着重大的收获。

词汇ahead of 在……前面；mountain 山；valley 山谷；treasure 宝藏 语法may be/may have 表示推测；but 转折。

You will find more joy in living a life with learning!

你会在终身学习的生活里找到更多快乐！

词汇find joy in 在……中找到快乐；living a life 过一种生活 语法find joy in doing sth.；with learning 作后置定语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍成为成功学习者的几个关键。首先要有成长型思维：相信自己，设定目标，采取行动，迎接挑战，把错误看作学习机会；其次要重视练习，练习越多学得越好，必要时向老师或教练寻求指导和反馈；还要和认真学习的学生相处，互相学习、互相激励；同时要善于选择优质学习资源，明智高效地学习；最后把学习当作日常生活的一部分，按自己的节奏前进，永不灰心。

English Summary

The passage explains how to become a successful learner. First, have a growth mindset: believe in yourself, set goals, take action, meet challenges and see mistakes as chances to learn. Second, practice matters: the more we practise, the better we learn, and guidance from teachers or coaches can help. Third, spend time with serious students so you can learn from and challenge each other. Also, choose good learning materials and use technology wisely. Finally, see learning as part of daily life, walk at your own speed and never lose heart.

Section B 3a · My Love of Books

Section B 3a 主题：阅读习惯 · 成长故事

My love of books comes from my mother.

我对书籍的热爱源于我的母亲。

词汇love of books 对书的热爱；come from 来自 语法一般现在时；come from 表示来源。

Because of her, our home is full of books on different subjects.

因为她，我们家到处都是各种题材的书。

When I was a child, my mother used to sit me on her knee, tell me stories, and teach me how to read.

小时候，母亲常常让我坐在她的膝盖上，给我讲故事，教我识字。

Soon, I was reading by myself.

很快，我就能够自己阅读了。

词汇soon 很快；by oneself 独自 语法过去进行时 was reading；by myself 作方式状语。

Over time, I found children's books no longer kept me interested.

渐渐地，我发现儿童读物不再能引起我的兴趣。

词汇over time 渐渐地；no longer 不再；keep sb. interested 使某人保持兴趣 语法find + 宾语 + 宾语补足语；keep sb. + adj.。

So I started reading other books, newspapers, and magazines.

于是我开始读其他的书、报纸和杂志。

词汇start doing 开始做；newspaper 报纸；magazine 杂志 语法so 连接结果；start + 动名词。

I especially enjoyed popular science books and books about the lives of famous people.

我特别喜欢科普读物和名人传记。

词汇especially 尤其；popular science 科普；famous 著名的 语法enjoy doing/名词；about the lives of famous people 作定语。

After dinner, I would just pick a book from the shelf to read in my room.

晚饭后，我总会从书架上拿一本书回房间读。

词汇pick 挑选；shelf 书架；would 过去常常 语法would do 表示过去习惯；to read 作目的状语。

Reading opened up new worlds and allowed me to discover new things for myself.

阅读为我打开了新世界的大门，让我自己去发现新事物。

词汇open up 打开；allow sb. to do 允许某人做；discover 发现 语法动名词 Reading 作主语；allow sb. to do sth.。

In general, I prefer print books, but sometimes I read e-books.

总的来说，我更偏爱纸质书，但有时也会读电子书。

词汇in general 总的来说；prefer 更喜欢；print book 纸质书；e-book 电子书 语法prefer 一般现在时；but 转折。

I think that reading is great fun.

我觉得阅读非常有趣。

词汇think 认为；great fun 很大的乐趣 语法that 引导宾语从句；fun 是不可数名词。

Now that I am older, I can always find time to read in the school library.

如今我长大了，总能抽出时间在学校图书馆里看书。

I believe that reading has helped me a lot with my schoolwork and made me more confident about my future.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇文章讲述陈磊（15 岁）对书的热爱来自母亲。小时候母亲把他抱在膝上讲故事、教他识字，后来他开始自己阅读；随着年龄增长，儿童书不再吸引他，他开始读其他书、报纸和杂志，尤其喜欢科普读物和名人传记。阅读为他打开新世界，也让他发现新事物。他更偏爱纸质书，但有时也读电子书。现在他常在学校图书馆阅读，并相信阅读帮助了学业，也让他对未来更自信。

English Summary

Chen Lei, aged 15, explains that his love of books comes from his mother. As a child, he sat on her knee and listened to stories; soon he was reading by himself. As he grew older, children's books no longer interested him, so he began reading other books, newspapers and magazines, especially popular science and biographies. Reading opened up new worlds and let him discover new things. He prefers print books but sometimes reads e-books. Now he often reads in the school library and believes reading has helped his schoolwork and made him more confident about the future.
