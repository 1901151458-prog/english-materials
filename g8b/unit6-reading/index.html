Section A 3a · English Table Manners

Section A 3a 主题：餐桌礼仪 · 建议

Sam: What's wrong, Hongli?

红丽，你怎么了？

词汇wrong 出问题的 语法What's wrong? 是询问烦恼的固定问法。

Hongli: I'm having dinner at my friend's house tonight.

我今晚要去朋友家吃晚餐。

词汇have dinner 吃晚餐；at one's house 在某人家里 语法现在进行时表示计划中的将来。

Hongli: But English table manners are so confusing that I'm a little worried.

但英国的餐桌礼仪太让人困惑了，我有点担心。

词汇table manners 餐桌礼仪；confusing 令人困惑的；a little 一点 语法so...that... 引导结果状语从句。

Sam: Well, I'm from London. Ask me anything!

嗯，我来自伦敦。有什么问题尽管问我！

词汇be from 来自；anything 任何事 语法祈使句 Ask me anything!。

Hongli: Thanks! Well, first, should I bring something?

谢谢！嗯，首先，我应该带点什么吗？

词汇bring 带；something 某物 语法should + 动词原形征求意见。

Sam: Yes. Bring a small gift, but don't bring food unless the host asks you to.

是的。带个小礼物，但不要带食物，除非主人要求你带。

词汇gift 礼物；unless 除非；host 主人 语法祈使句；unless 引导条件状语从句。

Sam: And remember to wait for the host to start before you eat.

而且记住，要等主人先动筷你再吃。

词汇remember to do 记得做；wait for 等待 语法remember to do sth.；before 引导时间状语从句。

Hongli: Good to know!

知道了！

词汇good to know 知道了（有用） 语法省略句 Good to know.

Hongli: Could you tell me the proper way to use a knife and fork too?

你能也告诉我正确使用刀叉的方法吗？

词汇proper 正确的；knife and fork 刀叉 语法Could you tell me...? 礼貌请求；the way to do sth.。

Sam: Just hold the knife in your right hand, and the fork in your left.

就用右手拿刀，左手拿叉。

词汇hold 拿着；right hand 右手；left 左边 语法祈使句；in one's right hand 表示位置。

Sam: If you get confused, watch what everyone else does.

如果你搞不清楚，看看别人怎么做。

词汇get confused 搞不清楚；everyone else 其他人 语法if 引导条件状语从句；what 引导宾语从句。

Hongli: OK. Anything else?

好的。还有别的吗？

词汇anything else 其他任何事 语法省略问句 Anything else?。

Sam: Hmm. After the meal, thank the host and say that everything was delicious.

嗯。饭后，要感谢主人，并且说每样东西都很美味。

词汇after the meal 饭后；thank 感谢；delicious 美味的 语法祈使句；that 引导宾语从句。

Sam: And, most importantly...

还有，最重要的是……

词汇most importantly 最重要的是 语法插入语。

Hongli: Yes?

是什么？

词汇yes 是 语法省略问句表示追问。

Sam: Enjoy yourself!

玩得开心！

词汇enjoy oneself 玩得开心 语法祈使句；enjoy oneself 反身代词。

Hongli: Thanks, I will!

谢谢，我会的！

词汇will 将会 语法简略回答。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，红丽要去朋友家吃晚饭，但觉得英国餐桌礼仪太让人困惑，有点担心。来自伦敦的山姆给她建议：带个小礼物，但不要带食物，除非主人要求；要等主人先动筷；右手拿刀、左手拿叉，搞不清就看别人怎么做；饭后感谢主人，说食物很美味；最重要的是玩得开心。红丽表示会照做。

English Summary

In the conversation, Hongli is going to have dinner at a friend's house but finds English table manners confusing and is a little worried. Sam, who is from London, gives advice: bring a small gift but not food unless the host asks; wait for the host to start before eating; hold the knife in the right hand and the fork in the left, and watch others if confused; after the meal thank the host and say everything was delicious; and most importantly, enjoy herself. Hongli says she will.

Section B 1b · Party Manners in France

Section B 1b 主题：法国派对礼仪 · 邮件

Hi Diana,

嗨，戴安娜，

词汇hi 嗨（问候） 语法书信/邮件称呼语。

It's so great to hear from you.

收到你的来信真是太棒了。

词汇hear from 收到……的来信 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.；hear from sb.。

Life in Paris is pretty good!

在巴黎的生活相当不错！

词汇pretty 相当；good 好的 语法pretty 修饰 good。

I was a little lonely at first, but I'm starting to make some friends.

一开始我有点孤独，但现在开始交到一些朋友了。

词汇lonely 孤独的；at first 起初；start to do 开始做 语法but 转折；start to do sth.。

They helped me to learn more about the customs here after I had some embarrassing experiences at a party last month.

在上个月的一个派对上有过一些尴尬的经历之后，他们帮我更多地了解了这里的习俗。

词汇custom 习俗；embarrassing 尴尬的；experience 经历 语法help sb. to do sth.；after 引导时间状语从句。

I'd like to share some tips with you.

我想和你分享一些小贴士。

词汇would like to 想要；share...with 与……分享；tip 小贴士 语法I'd like to do sth.；share sth. with sb.。

First, you shouldn't arrive early to a party.

首先，参加派对不应该到得太早。

词汇arrive 到达；early 早 语法shouldn't + 动词原形表示不建议。

It's normal to be on time or a few minutes late, but arriving early can cause problems.

准时或者晚几分钟到是正常的，但到得太早会引发问题。

词汇on time 准时；a few minutes 几分钟；cause problems 引发问题 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.；动名词 arriving 作主语。

Once, I arrived 10 minutes early to a party.

有一次，我提前 10 分钟到了一个派对。

词汇once 有一次；arrive 到达 语法一般过去时；10 minutes early 作状语。

Usually that's the polite thing to do, right?

通常那是礼貌的做法，对吧？

词汇usually 通常；polite 礼貌的 语法反义疑问句 right?。

But my friend looked so surprised to see me!

但我的朋友看到我时显得很惊讶！

词汇surprised 惊讶的 语法look + adj.；be surprised to do sth.。

He was still getting everything ready.

他还在准备一切呢。

词汇get ready 准备 语法过去进行时 was getting ready。

Second, it's important to bring a gift when you visit someone.

其次，拜访别人时带礼物很重要。

词汇visit 拜访；someone 某人 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.；when 引导时间状语从句。

When I attended my friend's party, I didn't bring anything.

我参加朋友的派对时，什么都没带。

词汇attend 参加 语法when 引导时间状语从句；一般过去时否定。

It wasn't his birthday, but everyone else brought gifts like chocolates or flowers to thank him for the party.

那天不是他的生日，但其他人都带了像巧克力或鲜花之类的礼物来感谢他举办派对。

词汇birthday 生日；everyone else 其他人；chocolate 巧克力；flower 花 语法but 转折；to thank 作目的状语。

I was so embarrassed!

我尴尬极了！

词汇embarrassed 尴尬的 语法so + adj. 表示程度。

Third, you should always try to dress for the occasion.

第三，你应该总是尽量根据场合着装。

词汇dress 穿衣；occasion 场合 语法try to do sth.；dress for。

At that party it was surprising to see how nicely everyone was dressed.

在那次派对上，看到每个人都穿得那么讲究，真是令人惊讶。

词汇surprising 令人惊讶的；dress 穿着 语法It was + adj. + to do sth.；how 引导宾语从句。

It was summer, so I was wearing a simple blouse and shorts.

当时是夏天，所以我穿了一件简单的衬衫和短裤。

词汇blouse 女式衬衫；shorts 短裤 语法过去进行时 was wearing；so 连接结果。

But everyone else was dressed so well that I felt silly.

但其他人都穿得那么好，我觉得自己很傻。

词汇feel silly 感到傻 语法so...that... 引导结果状语从句；被动 was dressed。

Fourth, it's best not to ask personal questions unless you know the person quite well.

第四，除非你很了解一个人，否则最好不要问私人问题。

词汇personal 私人的；unless 除非 语法It's best not to do sth.；unless 引导条件状语从句。

Many French people are quite private.

很多法国人都相当注重隐私。

词汇French 法国的；private 私人的；注重隐私的 语法be + adj.。

They don't like it when others ask them about their age, their family, whether they are married, or how much money they make.

他们不喜欢别人问他们的年龄、家庭、是否已婚或者收入多少。

词汇marry 结婚；money 钱 语法it 是形式宾语，when 从句是真正宾语；whether 引导宾语从句。

Food and culture, however, are safe conversation topics.

不过，食物和文化是安全的谈话话题。

词汇however 不过；conversation topic 谈话话题 语法however 作插入语。

Last but not least, make sure you use French whenever you can.

最后但同样重要的是，尽可能使用法语。

词汇last but not least 最后但同样重要的是；whenever 无论何时 语法make sure + 从句；whenever 引导时间状语从句。

Lots of people speak English here, but you can develop closer relationships with them if you use French.

这里很多人说英语，但如果你用法语，你可以和他们建立更亲密的关系。

词汇lots of 许多；develop 建立；relationship 关系 语法but 转折；if 引导条件状语从句；比较级 closer。

It's very important in their culture.

这在他们的文化中非常重要。

词汇culture 文化 语法It's + adj. + in...。

I found that even a simple "merci" goes a long way!

我发现即使是一句简单的“merci（谢谢）”也很有用！

词汇merci 谢谢（法语）；go a long way 起很大作用 语法find 后接宾语从句。

You'll see what I mean as soon as you get here!

你一到这里就会明白我的意思！

词汇see 明白；as soon as 一……就…… 语法what 引导宾语从句；as soon as 引导时间状语从句。

Au revoir!

再见！

词汇au revoir 再见（法语） 语法书信结束语。

Nancy

南希

词汇Nancy 南希（人名） 语法署名。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是南希写给戴安娜的邮件，分享在法国的派对礼仪。她先说自己开始交到朋友，他们帮她了解了当地习俗。建议有：不要早到；拜访时要带礼物；要根据场合着装；除非很熟，否则别问私人问题（年龄、家庭、婚姻、收入），食物和文化是安全话题；最后尽量用法语，哪怕是简单的“merci”也很有用。

English Summary

This is Nancy's email to Diana about party manners in France. She says she is making friends who helped her learn the local customs. Her tips: don't arrive early at a party; bring a gift when visiting someone; dress for the occasion; don't ask personal questions unless you know the person well—food and culture are safe topics; and finally, use French whenever possible, since even a simple "merci" goes a long way.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是蒂娜写给亚明的邮件，请他为即将到来的中国家访提供建议。她在邮件头标明了收件人、发件人和主题；正文先说一周后到上海，将在赵家住两周，介绍赵家的情况，然后询问：该不该带礼物、正确的餐桌礼仪、合适的谈话话题，以及还有其他需要注意的事。她担心说错话做错事留下错误印象，期待尽快回信。

English Summary

This is Tina's email to Yaming asking for advice before her home visit in China. The header shows the recipient, sender and subject. In the body she says she will arrive in Shanghai in a week and stay with the Zhaos for two weeks, describes the family, and asks questions: should she bring a gift, what the right table manners are, what good conversation topics there are, and whether there is anything else she should know. She worries about doing something silly and giving a false impression, and looks forward to a reply.

1. Why is Hongli worried?
