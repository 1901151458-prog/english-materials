Section A · 3a · What Should I Do at Dinner?

Section A · 3a 主题：餐桌礼仪 · 文化差异

Sam: What's wrong, Hongli?

虹丽，怎么了？

词汇wrong 有问题的；hongli 虹丽（人名） 语法What's wrong? 是询问“怎么了、出什么事了”的常用问句。

Hongli: I'm having dinner at my friend's house tonight.

今晚我要去朋友家吃饭。

词汇have dinner 吃饭；tonight 今晚 语法现在进行时表示将来的安排；at my friend's house 表示地点。

Hongli: But English table manners are so confusing that I'm a little worried.

但是英国的餐桌礼仪太让人困惑了，我有点担心。

词汇table manners 餐桌礼仪；confusing 令人困惑的；worried 担心的 语法so + 形容词 + that 引导结果状语从句，表示“如此……以至于……”。

Sam: Well, I'm from London.

嗯，我来自伦敦。

词汇be from 来自 语法be from + 地点表示“来自某地”。

Sam: Ask me anything!

随便问我什么都可以！

词汇anything 任何事 语法祈使句 + anything，表示“尽管问我”。

Hongli: Thanks!

谢谢！

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法口语致谢用语。

Hongli: Well, first, should I bring something?

嗯，首先，我应该带点什么吗？

词汇should 应该；bring 带来；something 某物 语法should + 主语 + 动词原形? 用于征求意见。

Sam: Yes.

是的。

词汇yes 是的 语法简略回答，肯定对方的想法。

Sam: Bring a small gift, but don't bring food unless the host asks you to.

带一份小礼物，但除非主人请你带食物，否则不要带。

词汇gift 礼物；unless 除非；host 主人 语法祈使句；unless 引导条件状语从句，表示“除非”。

Sam: And remember to wait for the host to start before you eat.

还要记得等主人先开始吃，你才能吃。

词汇remember to do sth. 记得做某事；wait for 等待 语法remember to do sth. 记得做某事；before 引导时间状语从句。

Hongli: Good to know!

知道了！

词汇good to know 知道了（口语） 语法口语省略句：(That's) good to know! 表示“了解到了”。

Hongli: Could you tell me the proper way to use a knife and fork too?

你也能告诉我正确使用刀叉的方法吗？

词汇proper 恰当的；knife 刀；fork 叉子 语法Could you ...? 委婉请求；the way to do sth. 做某事的方法。

Sam: Just hold the knife in your right hand, and the fork in your left.

只要右手拿刀，左手拿叉。

词汇hold 拿；right hand 右手；left 左边 语法祈使句；in one's right hand 表示“在某人右手中”。

Sam: If you get confused, watch what everyone else does.

如果你感到困惑，就看看别人是怎么做的。

词汇get confused 感到困惑；everyone else 其他人 语法if 引导条件状语从句；what everyone else does 是宾语从句。

Hongli: OK.

好的。

词汇OK 好的 语法口语应答语。

Hongli: Anything else?

还有别的吗？

词汇anything else 别的什么 语法省略问句：(Is there) anything else? 表示“还有别的吗？”。

Sam: Hmm.

嗯……

词汇hmm 嗯（表示思考） 语法口语感叹词，表示思考、犹豫。

Sam: After the meal, thank the host and say that everything was delicious.

饭后要感谢主人，并说一切都很美味。

词汇after the meal 饭后；delicious 美味的 语法after 引导时间状语；say + that 宾语从句。

Sam: And, most importantly ...

而且，最重要的是……

词汇most importantly 最重要的是 语法省略号表示话未说完，引出下文。

Hongli: Yes?

怎么了？

词汇yes 表示疑问、催促对方说下去 语法用升调的 Yes? 表示“然后呢？”。

Sam: Enjoy yourself!

尽情享受吧！

词汇enjoy oneself 玩得开心；尽情享受 语法祈使句；enjoy oneself 表示“玩得开心”。

Hongli: Thanks, I will!

谢谢，我会的！

词汇will 会（表示意愿） 语法I will. 是省略回答，完整形式为 I will enjoy myself.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是 Sam 和 Hongli 的一段对话。Hongli 今晚要去朋友家吃饭，但她觉得英国的餐桌礼仪太让人困惑，有点担心。来自伦敦的 Sam 给她建议：可以带一份小礼物，但除非主人请你带食物，否则不要带；要等主人先开始吃；刀要右手拿、叉要左手拿；如果不懂就看看别人怎么做；饭后要感谢主人，并说一切都很美味；最重要的是——尽情享受！Hongli 谢谢他，并说自己会的。

English Summary

This is a conversation between Sam and Hongli. Hongli is having dinner at her friend's house tonight, but she finds English table manners so confusing that she is a little worried. Sam, who is from London, gives her some advice: bring a small gift, but don't bring food unless the host asks you to; wait for the host to start before you eat; hold the knife in your right hand and the fork in your left; if you get confused, watch what everyone else does; after the meal, thank the host and say that everything was delicious. Most importantly, enjoy yourself! Hongli thanks him and says she will.

语法点 Grammar

What's wrong, Hongli?

What's wrong? 是询问“怎么了、出什么事了”的常用问句。

I'm having dinner at my friend's house tonight.

现在进行时表示将来的安排；at my friend's house 表示地点。

But English table manners are so confusing that I'm a little worried.

so + 形容词 + that 引导结果状语从句，表示“如此……以至于……”。

Well, I'm from London.

be from + 地点表示“来自某地”。

Ask me anything!

祈使句 + anything，表示“尽管问我”。

Well, first, should I bring something?

should + 主语 + 动词原形? 用于征求意见。

Bring a small gift, but don't bring food unless the host asks you to.

祈使句；unless 引导条件状语从句，表示“除非”。

And remember to wait for the host to start before you eat.

remember to do sth. 记得做某事；before 引导时间状语从句。

Could you tell me the proper way to use a knife and fork too?

Could you ...? 委婉请求；the way to do sth. 做某事的方法。

Just hold the knife in your right hand, and the fork in your left.

祈使句；in one's right hand 表示“在某人右手中”。

If you get confused, watch what everyone else does.

if 引导条件状语从句；what everyone else does 是宾语从句。

After the meal, thank the host and say that everything was delicious.

after 引导时间状语；say + that 宾语从句。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

But English table manners are so confusing that I'm a little worried.

但是英国的餐桌礼仪太让人困惑了，我有点担心。

so + 形容词 + that 引导结果状语从句；confusing 修饰物，表示“令人困惑的”，worried 修饰人，表示“感到担心的”。

Bring a small gift, but don't bring food unless the host asks you to.

带一份小礼物，但除非主人请你带食物，否则不要带。

unless 引导条件状语从句，相当于 if ... not；ask sb. to do sth. 中 to 后省略了上文提到的动词 bring food。

If you get confused, watch what everyone else does.

如果你感到困惑，就看看别人是怎么做的。

if 引导条件状语从句；what everyone else does 是宾语从句，作 watch 的宾语。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 先看 P54 的 3a：Hongli 要去朋友家吃饭，却对英国餐桌礼仪感到困惑；读前想一想：你知道哪些中西方餐桌礼仪的差异？
  • 读前扫读对话，找出 Hongli 的担心和 Sam 给的几条建议。

读中

  • 边读边用表格整理：餐前、餐中、餐后分别要注意什么。
  • 画出 Sam 提建议的句型（should / bring / remember to / if ...）。

读后

  • 两人一组分角色朗读对话，注意提出建议和委婉请求的语气。
  • 结合 3d，用英语给即将到中国人家做客的外国朋友提 3–4 条餐桌礼仪建议。

Section B · 1b · Nancy's Email: Party Manners in France

Section B · 1b 主题：聚会礼仪 · 跨文化适应（P56–P57 两页合并）

To: diana@frenchlearning.com

收件人：diana@frenchlearning.com

词汇to 收件人；frenchlearning.com 邮箱域名 语法邮件头部字段：To 后写收件人邮箱。

From: nancy@student.com

发件人：nancy@student.com

词汇from 发件人；student.com 邮箱域名 语法邮件头部字段：From 后写发件人邮箱。

Subject: Re: How's life in France?

主题：回复：在法国生活怎么样？

词汇subject 主题；Re 回复 语法邮件头部字段：Subject 为主题；Re: 表示这是回复邮件。

Hi Diana,

嗨，戴安娜：

词汇hi 嗨；Diana 戴安娜（人名） 语法英文邮件的称呼语，后面用逗号。

It's so great to hear from you.

收到你的来信真是太好了。

词汇hear from sb. 收到某人的来信 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；hear from sb. 表示“收到某人的来信”。

Life in Paris is pretty good!

巴黎的生活很不错！

词汇life 生活；pretty 相当 语法Life in Paris 表示“巴黎的生活”；pretty 在此作副词，表示“相当”。

I was a little lonely at first, but I'm starting to make some friends.

起初我有点孤单，但我开始交到一些朋友了。

词汇lonely 孤独的；at first 起初；make friends 交朋友 语法at first 表示“起初”；start to do sth. 开始做某事；but 表转折。

They helped me to learn more about the customs here after I had some embarrassing experiences at a party last month.

上个月我在一次聚会上经历了一些尴尬的事情之后，他们帮我更多地了解了这里的风俗。

词汇help sb. to do sth. 帮助某人做某事；custom 风俗；embarrassing 令人尴尬的 语法help sb. to do sth. 帮助某人做某事；after 引导时间状语从句。

I'd like to share some tips with you.

我想和你分享一些建议。

词汇share ... with ... 和……分享……；tip 建议 语法I'd like to do sth. = I would like to do sth.；share sth. with sb.。

First, you shouldn't arrive early to a party.

首先，你不应该太早到聚会。

词汇first 首先；shouldn't 不应该；arrive early 早到 语法shouldn't + 动词原形表示“不应该”。

It's normal to be on time or a few minutes late, but arriving early can cause problems.

准时或晚几分钟到都很正常，但早到可能会引起麻烦。

词汇normal 正常的；on time 准时；cause problems 引起麻烦 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth.；动名词短语 arriving early 作主语。

Once, I arrived 10 minutes early to a party.

有一次，我提前 10 分钟到了聚会。

词汇once 有一次；10 minutes early 提前 10 分钟 语法once 在句首表示“有一次”；arrive + 时间 + early 表示早到多久。

Usually that's the polite thing to do, right?

通常这是礼貌的做法，对吧？

词汇usually 通常；polite 有礼貌的 语法the thing to do 表示“该做的事”；right 用于确认。

But my friend looked so surprised to see me!

但我朋友看到我时显得非常惊讶！

词汇surprised 惊讶的 语法look + 形容词表示“看起来……”；be surprised to do sth. 表示“做某事很惊讶”。

He was still getting everything ready.

他还在准备一切。

词汇get ... ready 把……准备好 语法过去进行时表示当时正在进行的动作；get sth. ready 表示“把某物准备好”。

Second, it's important to bring a gift when you visit someone.

第二，去拜访别人时带礼物很重要。

词汇second 第二；important 重要的 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；when 引导时间状语从句。

When I attended my friend's party, I didn't bring anything.

我去参加朋友的聚会时，什么礼物都没带。

词汇attend 参加；bring 带 语法when 引导时间状语从句；anything 用于否定句。

It wasn't his birthday, but everyone else brought gifts like chocolates or flowers to thank him for the party.

那不是他的生日，但其他人都带了巧克力或花之类的礼物，感谢他举办聚会。

词汇everyone else 其他所有人；chocolate 巧克力；thank sb. for sth. 因某事感谢某人 语法like 表示“像……这样”；不定式 to thank 作目的状语。

I was so embarrassed!

我太尴尬了！

词汇embarrassed 感到尴尬的 语法so 修饰形容词表示程度。

Third, you should always try to dress for the occasion.

第三，你应该始终努力按场合穿着得体。

词汇third 第三；dress for the occasion 按场合穿着 语法try to do sth. 努力做某事；dress for the occasion 表示“按场合穿着”。

At that party it was surprising to see how nicely everyone was dressed.

在那次聚会上，看到大家穿得那么好，真令人惊讶。

词汇surprising 令人惊讶的；nicely 很好地 语法It was + 形容词 + to do sth.；how 引导宾语从句。

It was summer, so I was just wearing a simple blouse and shorts.

当时是夏天，所以我只穿了一件简单的衬衫和短裤。

词汇summer 夏天；blouse 女式衬衫；shorts 短裤 语法so 表示结果；过去进行时表示当时的穿着。

But everyone else was dressed so well that I felt silly.

但其他人都穿得那么讲究，我觉得自己很傻。

词汇everyone else 其他所有人；silly 傻的 语法so + 副词 + that 引导结果状语从句；be dressed 表示“穿着”。

Fourth, it's best not to ask personal questions unless you know the person quite well.

第四，除非你跟对方很熟，否则最好不要问私人问题。

词汇personal 私人的；unless 除非 语法It's best not to do sth. 表示“最好别做某事”；unless 引导条件状语从句。

Many French people are quite private.

许多法国人相当注重隐私。

词汇French 法国的；private 私密的；注重隐私的 语法quite 修饰形容词 private，表示程度。

They don't like it when others ask them about their age, their family, whether they are married, or how much money they make.

他们不喜欢别人问他们的年龄、家庭、是否结婚，或者挣多少钱。

词汇age 年龄；whether 是否；be married 结婚 语法when 引导时间状语从句；whether 引导宾语从句；or 连接并列成分。

Food and culture, however, are safe conversation topics.

不过，食物和文化是安全的话题。

词汇however 然而；conversation topic 聊天话题；safe 安全的 语法however 作插入语表示转折；主语 Food and culture 是复数，用 are。

Last but not least, make sure you use French whenever you can.

最后但同样重要的是，只要可以，就一定要使用法语。

词汇last but not least 最后但同样重要的是；whenever 无论何时 语法make sure + 从句；whenever 引导时间状语从句。

Lots of people speak English here, but you can develop closer relationships with them if you use French.

这里很多人会说英语，但如果你用法语，就能和他们建立更亲密的关系。

词汇lots of 许多；develop 建立；relationship 关系 语法but 表转折；if 引导条件状语从句；closer 是 close 的比较级。

It's very important in their culture.

这在他们的文化中非常重要。

词汇culture 文化 语法It 指代“使用法语”这件事；in one's culture 表示“在某人的文化中”。

I found that even a simple "merci" goes a long way!

我发现，哪怕一句简单的“merci（谢谢）”也很有用！

词汇merci 谢谢（法语）；go a long way 很有用；大有帮助 语法that 引导宾语从句；go a long way 是习语，表示“很有帮助”。

You'll see what I mean as soon as you get here!

等你到了这里，就会明白我的意思了！

词汇mean 意思是；as soon as 一……就…… 语法what I mean 是宾语从句；as soon as 引导时间状语从句。

Au revoir!

再见！

词汇au revoir 再见（法语） 语法法语告别用语，意为“再见”。

Nancy

南希

词汇Nancy 南希（人名） 语法邮件署名。

中文大意

English Summary

(Merged from pages 56–57.) This is Nancy's email to Diana about party manners in France. 中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

（教材 P89–P90 两页合并）这篇 Reading Plus 文章介绍几种中国文化符号。开篇指出中国文化历史悠久、传统和信仰丰富，在中国看到、听到甚至尝到的东西都可能是具有特殊含义的符号。接着分三部分介绍：一是“龙”——西方故事里的龙是可怕的怪物，但在中国，龙象征力量和好运，龙的形象出现在花瓶、建筑、钱币、衣服、绘画和珠宝上，还有“望子成龙”的成语，中国人自称“龙的传人”；二是“红色”——红色在中国有特殊含义，象征好运和财富，节日里到处是红色装饰，传统婚礼上新郎新娘都穿红色，但在很多其他国家红色意味着危险；三是“幸运数字”——六、二、八、九因为谐音而被视为吉利，四因为谐音“死”而被避讳，文章还提到美国以七为幸运数字、十三为不吉利数字。

English Summary

(Merged from pages 89–90.) This Reading Plus text introduces several symbols of Chinese culture. It begins by noting that Chinese culture is one of the oldest in the world and is rich in traditions and beliefs, so many of the things people see, hear, or even taste in China may be symbols with special meanings. It then introduces three kinds of symbols. First, dragons: in many Western stories dragons are frightening monsters, but in China people are not frightened of them; instead they think dragons are a symbol of power and good fortune, and images of dragons appear on vases, buildings, money, clothes, paintings and jewellery—the word 'dragon' even appears in names and idioms, and Chinese people call themselves the descendants of the dragon. Second, the colour red: red has special meaning in China and is believed to bring good luck and wealth, so red decorations are everywhere during festivals, and at traditional Chinese weddings both the bride and groom wear red; in many other countries, however, red means danger. Third, lucky numbers: six, two, eight and nine are lucky because they sound like other Chinese words with positive meanings, while four sounds like 'death' and is avoided by some people; in America, for example, seven is lucky and thirteen is unlucky.
