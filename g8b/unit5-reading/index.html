Section A · 3a · Preparing for the Typhoon

Section A · 3a 主题：台风与家庭准备（教材 P44，Haitao / Lisa 对话）

Haitao: Hi, Lisa.

嗨，丽莎。

词汇hi 嗨；Lisa 丽莎（人名） 语法日常问候语；称呼语放句末，前面用逗号。

Haitao: Did your family have any trouble during the typhoon?

台风期间你家遇到什么麻烦了吗？

词汇trouble 麻烦；during 在……期间；typhoon 台风 语法Did + 主语 + 动词原形? 一般过去时一般疑问句。

Lisa: Yes, it was awful.

是的，太糟糕了。

词汇awful 糟糕的 语法一般过去时 was；awful 作表语。

Haitao: I'm sorry to hear that.

听到这个我很难过。

词汇sorry 难过的；hear 听到 语法I'm sorry to hear that. 是表示同情、安慰的常用语。

Haitao: Did you know that a typhoon was coming?

你知道台风要来了吗？

词汇know 知道；come 来 语法that 引导宾语从句；过去进行时 was coming 表示当时即将发生。

Lisa: No, we didn't know about it.

不，我们不知道这件事。

词汇know about 了解；知道 语法didn't + 动词原形；it 指台风。

Lisa: We were driving home from our relatives' house when the strong winds started.

我们正从亲戚家开车回家，这时刮起了强风。

词汇drive home 开车回家；relatives 亲戚；strong wind 强风 语法过去进行时 + when 一般过去时，表示“正在……时突然……”。

Haitao: Oh no!

哦，不！

词汇oh no 哦，不 语法感叹语，表示惊讶和同情。

Haitao: It's dangerous to travel during a typhoon.

台风期间出行很危险。

词汇dangerous 危险的；travel 出行 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语。

Lisa: I know.

我知道。

词汇know 知道 语法简略回答，表示认同。

Lisa: It was raining hard while we were driving back.

我们开车返回时正下着大雨。

词汇rain hard 下大雨；drive back 开车返回 语法while 引导时间状语从句，主从句都用过去进行时。

Lisa: We almost got into an accident when a truck nearly hit us.

一辆卡车差点撞到我们，我们差点出事故。

词汇almost 几乎；get into an accident 出事故；truck 卡车；nearly 差点 语法when 引导时间状语从句；get into an accident 是固定搭配。

Haitao: Oh dear, I'm glad you were safe!

天哪，还好你们没事！

词汇oh dear 天哪；glad 高兴的；safe 安全的 语法be glad + 从句表示“高兴……”。

Lisa: Yes, but when we got home later, we found water everywhere.

是的，但后来我们到家时，发现到处都是水。

词汇everywhere 到处；find 发现 语法when 引导时间状语从句；find + 宾语 + 地点。

Lisa: The house looked awful!

房子看起来糟透了！

词汇look 看起来 语法look + 形容词表示“看起来……”。

Lisa: Was yours OK?

你家的房子没事吧？

词汇yours 你的（名词性物主代词） 语法yours = your house；一般疑问句。

Haitao: Yes.

没事。

词汇yes 是的 语法简略回答。

Haitao: Luckily, we covered our windows and moved our things off the floor before the typhoon started.

幸运的是，台风来临前我们遮好了窗户，把东西从地板上移开了。

词汇luckily 幸运的是；cover 遮住；move ... off 把……从……移开；floor 地板 语法luckily 作状语；and 连接并列谓语；before 引导时间状语从句。

Haitao: We stored food and water too.

我们还储存了食物和水。

词汇store 储存；food 食物 语法一般过去时 stored；too 用于肯定句末。

Lisa: That's good!

那很好！

词汇good 好的 语法That's = That is；口语中表示赞同。

Lisa: We'll do that next time.

下次我们也要这样做。

词汇next time 下次 语法We'll = We will；next time 表示“下次”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在 Haitao 和 Lisa 之间。Haitao 问 Lisa 家在台风期间有没有遇到麻烦，Lisa 说很糟糕：他们事先不知道台风要来，从亲戚家开车回家的路上遇到强风，当时雨下得很大，差点因为一辆卡车而发生事故。回到家后，他们发现家里到处都是水，房子看起来很糟。Haitao 说他家比较幸运，台风来临前他们遮好了窗户、把东西从地板上移开，还储存了食物和水。Lisa 说下次他们也要这样做。

English Summary

This is a conversation between Haitao and Lisa. Haitao asks Lisa whether her family had any trouble during the typhoon, and Lisa says it was awful. They didn't know a typhoon was coming; while they were driving home from their relatives' house, the strong winds started and it was raining hard, and they almost got into an accident when a truck nearly hit them. When they got home later, they found water everywhere and the house looked awful. Haitao says his family was luckier: they covered their windows, moved things off the floor and stored food and water before the typhoon started. Lisa says they will do that next time.

I'm sorry to hear that. 是表示同情、安慰的常用语。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

We were driving home from our relatives' house when the strong winds started.

我们正从亲戚家开车回家，这时刮起了强风。

过去进行时 were driving 表示“当时正在开车”；when 引导的从句用一般过去时，表示突然发生的动作。

The wind was too strong for me to use my umbrella!

风太大了，我都没法打伞！

too + 形容词 + for sb. to do sth. 表示“太……以至于某人不能做某事”。

Luckily, we covered our windows and moved our things off the floor before the typhoon started.

幸运的是，我们在台风来临前遮好窗户，把东西从地板上移开了。

luckily 作状语；and 连接两个并列谓语；before 引导时间状语从句，说明动作先后。

Section B · 1b · How One Girl Saved Many Lives

Section B · 1b 主题：海啸中的知识与勇气（教材 P46–P47 两页合并）

On 26 December 2004, Tilly Smith, a 10-year-old English schoolgirl, and her family were on holiday in Phuket, Thailand.

2004 年 12 月 26 日，10 岁的英国女学生蒂莉·史密斯和家人正在泰国普吉岛度假。

词汇on holiday 在度假；schoolgirl 女学生；Phuket 普吉岛；Thailand 泰国 语法一般过去时；a 10-year-old English schoolgirl 是同位语。

Everyone was enjoying the beautiful weather that morning.

那天早上，大家都在享受美好的天气。

词汇enjoy 享受；weather 天气 语法过去进行时 was enjoying 表示当时正在发生。

Some people were swimming in the sea, and some were relaxing on the beach.

一些人在海里游泳，一些人在海滩上放松。

词汇swim 游泳；relax 放松；beach 海滩 语法and 连接两个并列的过去进行时分句。

But while Tilly was walking along the beach with her family, she noticed something strange.

但是，当蒂莉和家人沿着海滩散步时，她注意到一些奇怪的现象。

词汇walk along 沿着……走；notice 注意到；strange 奇怪的 语法while 引导时间状语从句；notice something strange。

Thick white froth was forming on top of the waves.

海浪上正在形成厚厚的白色泡沫。

词汇thick 厚的；froth 泡沫；wave 海浪 语法过去进行时 was forming；on top of 表示“在……上面”。

Next, she saw that the waves were coming in instead of going out.

接着，她看到海浪是涌进来而不是退出去。

词汇instead of 而不是；go out 退出去 语法that 引导宾语从句；instead of 表示“而不是”。

Just then, she remembered something important.

就在那时，她想起了一件重要的事。

词汇just then 就在那时；remember 想起；important 重要的 语法一般过去时 remembered；just then 作时间状语。

Two weeks before, her geography teacher had explained that earthquakes under the sea could create dangerous waves called tsunamis!

两周前，她的地理老师解释过，海底的地震会产生被称为海啸的危险海浪！

词汇geography 地理；earthquake 地震；create 产生；tsunami 海啸 语法过去完成时 had explained；that 宾语从句；called tsunamis 作定语。

"I know what's going to happen!"

“我知道会发生什么！”

词汇what's going to happen 将会发生什么 语法what's going to happen 作宾语从句；be going to 表将来。

"There's going to be a big wave," Tilly cried.

“会有巨浪的，”蒂莉喊道。

词汇there's going to be 将会有；cry 喊叫 语法There's going to be... 表示“将会有……”；直接引语。

Unfortunately, her family didn't believe her and wanted to continue their walk.

不幸的是，家人不相信她，还想继续散步。

词汇unfortunately 不幸的是；believe 相信；continue 继续 语法unfortunately 作状语；and 连接并列谓语。

However, Tilly refused to take a single step further.

然而，蒂莉拒绝再往前走一步。

词汇however 然而；refuse 拒绝；step 步 语法refuse to do sth.；however 表转折。

Finally, her mother decided to continue the walk alone, and the rest of her family returned to the hotel.

最后，她母亲决定独自继续散步，其余家人回到了酒店。

词汇finally 最后；alone 独自；the rest of 其余的；return 返回 语法decide to do sth.；and 连接并列分句。

Back at the hotel, Tilly's father told a security guard about the tsunami.

回到酒店后，蒂莉的父亲把海啸的事告诉了一名保安。

词汇security guard 保安；tell sb. about sth. 告诉某人某事 语法tell sb. about sth.；back at the hotel 作地点状语。

The security guard was shocked.

保安大吃一惊。

词汇shocked 震惊的 语法be shocked 表示“感到震惊”。

He knew about the huge earthquake in nearby Indonesia.

他知道附近的印度尼西亚发生了大地震。

词汇huge 巨大的；nearby 附近的；Indonesia 印度尼西亚 语法know about 表示“了解”；nearby 作定语修饰 Indonesia。

It meant that a tsunami was possible!

这意味着海啸是有可能的！

词汇mean 意味着；possible 可能的 语法that 引导宾语从句；It 指前面提到的情况。

He jumped into action right away and rushed to get people off the beach.

他立刻行动起来，冲去让人们撤离海滩。

词汇jump into action 迅速行动；right away 立刻；rush 冲；get ... off 让……离开 语法jump into action 是固定搭配；rush to do sth.。

Everyone began to panic.

大家开始惊慌起来。

词汇panic 惊慌 语法begin to do sth. 开始做某事。

People were screaming and running as fast as they could when they saw a huge wall of water coming from the sea.

当人们看到一堵巨大的水墙从海上来时，他们尖叫着拼命奔跑。

词汇scream 尖叫；as fast as they could 尽可能快地；a wall of water 水墙 语法when 引导时间状语从句；see sth. doing 表示“看见某物正在……”。

Tilly was right!

蒂莉是对的！

词汇right 正确的 语法be right 表示“是对的”。

Her mother was one of the last people on the beach, but thankfully, her family made it to safety just as the water hit the hotel.

她的母亲是海滩上最后离开的人之一，但幸运的是，就在海水冲进酒店的那一刻，她的家人安全脱险了。

词汇one of ……之一；thankfully 幸运的是；make it to safety 安全脱险 语法one of the + 形容词最高级 + 复数名词；just as 表示“正当……时”。

The 2004 tsunami caused over 200,000 deaths, but no one on the beach died that day.

2004 年的海啸造成 20 多万人死亡，但那天那片海滩上没有人遇难。

词汇cause 造成；death 死亡；no one 没有人 语法but 表转折；over 表示“超过”。

This true story shows how a single lesson could mean the difference between life and death.

这个真实的故事说明，一堂课可能意味着生与死的差别。

词汇single 单个的；lesson 课；difference 差别；life and death 生死 语法how 引导宾语从句；the difference between A and B。

Thanks to her knowledge and quick thinking, one schoolgirl was able to save over 100 lives.

多亏了她的知识和敏捷的思维，一个女学生救了 100 多条生命。

词汇thanks to 多亏；knowledge 知识；quick thinking 敏捷的思维；save 拯救 语法thanks to 表示“多亏”；be able to do sth. 能够做某事。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇跨 P46–P47 两页的文章讲述了英国女孩 Tilly Smith 在 2004 年海啸中救人的故事。2004 年 12 月 26 日，10 岁的 Tilly 和家人正在泰国普吉岛度假。她在海滩上散步时发现海浪上出现厚厚的白色泡沫，而且海浪是涌进来而不是退出去。她想起两周前地理老师讲过，海底地震会引发危险的海啸。Tilly 大喊将会有巨浪，但家人起初不相信她；她拒绝再往前走，母亲独自继续散步，其余家人回到酒店。Tilly 的父亲把海啸的事告诉保安，保安得知附近印尼发生大地震后立刻行动，让人们撤离海滩。Tilly 是对的：海水冲到酒店时，她的家人已安全脱险。2004 年海啸造成 20 多万人死亡，但那片海滩上没有人遇难。多亏她的知识和敏捷思考，一个女学生救了 100 多条生命。

English Summary

This article, which runs across pages 46–47, tells how Tilly Smith, a 10-year-old English schoolgirl, saved lives in the 2004 tsunami. On 26 December 2004, Tilly and her family were on holiday in Phuket, Thailand. While walking along the beach, she noticed thick white froth forming on the waves and saw that the waves were coming in instead of going out. She remembered that two weeks before, her geography teacher had explained that earthquakes under the sea could create dangerous tsunamis. Tilly cried that a big wave was coming, but her family didn't believe her. However, she refused to walk any further; her mother continued alone, and the rest of the family returned to the hotel. Back at the hotel, Tilly's father told a security guard about the tsunami. The guard knew about the huge earthquake in nearby Indonesia and immediately rushed to get people off the beach. Tilly was right — her family made it to safety as the water hit the hotel. The 2004 tsunami caused over 200,000 deaths, but no one on that beach died. Thanks to her knowledge and quick thinking, one schoolgirl saved over 100 lives.

语法点 Grammar

Are you afraid of big storms?

be afraid of 表示“害怕……”。

But did you know some people go out to chase tornadoes?

Did + 主语 + 动词原形?；know 后接宾语从句（省略 that）。

Such storms can hit suddenly and move very quickly, so storm chasers always have to be prepared.

such + 名词；so 表结果；have to 表示“必须”。

When a tornado is about to hit, professional storm chasers try and get close to it to collect important information.

when 时间状语从句；be about to do sth.；不定式表目的。

Storm chaser Tim Samaras designed and built many different instruments for studying tornadoes.

and 连接并列谓语；for doing sth. 表示用途。

Throughout his career, the engineer chased down more than 120 tornadoes.

throughout 表示“贯穿”；more than 表示“超过”。

Often, he filmed the storms while they were happening just a few kilometres away.

while 引导时间状语从句；a few 修饰复数名词。

During a storm, storm chasers put special equipment in the way of the tornado.

during + 名词；in the way of 表示“在……经过的路线上”。

The equipment collects information about things such as wind speed and air pressure.

such as 用于举例；information 是不可数名词。

This helps scientists to make better forecasts.

help sb. (to) do sth.；make forecasts。

It can even help people understand how powerful the winds can be, and as a result, change how we build our houses.

how 引导宾语从句；as a result 表示“结果”。

Storm chasing can be exciting, but also very dangerous.

but also 表示“但也”。

The storm chasers' knowledge and experience are not always enough to keep them safe.

not always 表示“并不总是”；be enough to do sth.。

On 31 May 2013, Samaras and his team were chasing the El Reno tornado in Oklahoma, USA.

过去进行时；日期前用介词 on。

While the tornado was moving towards them, Samaras and his team were driving as fast as they could.

while 引导时间状语从句；as ... as one can/could。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

When a tornado is about to hit, professional storm chasers try and get close to it to collect important information.

龙卷风即将来临时，专业风暴追逐者会设法靠近它，以收集重要信息。

be about to do sth. 表示“即将做某事”；try and do sth. 表示“设法做某事”；不定式 to collect 表目的。

It can even help people understand how powerful the winds can be, and as a result, change how we build our houses.

它甚至能帮助人们了解风的威力有多大，从而改变我们建造房屋的方式。

help sb. do sth.；how powerful... 是宾语从句；as a result 表示“结果”；how we build... 也是宾语从句。

Though storm chasing remains a dangerous job, the goal of storm chasers is to learn more about storms to keep people safe.

虽然追逐风暴仍然是一项危险的工作，但风暴追逐者的目标是更多地了解风暴，以保护人们的安全。

though 引导让步状语从句；不定式 to learn / to keep 分别表目的。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 教材 P87–P88 是 Reading Plus 板块，文章 Storm Chasing: A Dangerous but Important Job 跨两页，本页把 P87 开头与 P88 续文合并成完整一篇。
  • 读前看标题，猜一猜 “storm chasing” 是一项什么样的工作，为什么危险却重要。

读中

  • 边读边找出风暴追逐者收集信息的目的和方式。
  • 注意文中的数字与时间表达：120 tornadoes、31 May 2013、4.2 kilometres、480 kilometres per hour。

读后

  • 用 4—5 句话复述 Tim Samaras 的故事和他的贡献。
  • 讨论：你认为风暴追逐这样的工作值得吗？为什么？

1. Did Lisa's family know the typhoon was coming?

  1. No, they didn't.

  3. They knew, but they didn't care.

2. What did Haitao's family do before the typhoon?

  1. They went to their relatives' house.
  2. They covered the windows and stored food and water.
  3. They drove home from the beach.
  4. They bought a new house.
答案与解析

B 原文：we covered our windows and moved our things off the floor before the typhoon started. We stored food and water too. 可知他们遮窗户、储存食物和水。

  3. 10 years old.
  4. 12 years old.

C 原文：Tilly Smith, a 10-year-old English schoolgirl 可知她当时 10 岁。

4. What did Tilly remember from her geography class?

  1. That the beach was dangerous.
  2. That her family should go home.

8. What is the goal of storm chasers?
