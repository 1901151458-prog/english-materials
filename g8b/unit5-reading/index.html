Section A 3a · After the Typhoon

Section A 3a 主题：台风经历 · 过去进行时

Haitao: Hi, Lisa. Did your family have any trouble during the typhoon?

嗨，莉萨。你们家在台风期间遇到麻烦了吗？

词汇trouble 麻烦；typhoon 台风 语法Did...have...? 一般过去时疑问句；during 表示时间。

Lisa: Yes, it was awful.

是的，太糟糕了。

词汇awful 糟糕的 语法一般过去时 was；it 指当时的情况。

Haitao: I'm sorry to hear that.

听到这个消息我很遗憾。

词汇be sorry to hear 听到……很遗憾 语法be sorry to hear that 是固定表达。

Haitao: Did you know that a typhoon was coming?

你们知道台风要来了吗？

词汇come 来；typhoon 台风 语法Did you know 后接宾语从句；was coming 过去进行时表示将要发生。

Lisa: No, we didn't know about it.

不知道，我们没听说。

词汇know about 知道 语法一般过去时否定；know about。

Lisa: We were driving home from our relatives' house when the strong winds started.

大风开始的时候，我们正从亲戚家开车回家。

词汇drive 开车；relatives' house 亲戚家；strong winds 大风 语法过去进行时 were driving 与 when 引导的时间状语从句搭配。

Haitao: Oh no! It's dangerous to travel during a typhoon.

哦，不！台风期间出行很危险。

词汇dangerous 危险的；travel 出行 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.；during 表示时间。

Lisa: I know.

我知道。

词汇know 知道 语法省略回答。

Lisa: It was raining hard while we were driving back.

我们往回开的时候，雨下得很大。

词汇rain hard 下大雨；drive back 往回开 语法过去进行时 was raining 与 while 引导的时间状语从句搭配。

Lisa: We almost got into an accident when a truck nearly hit us.

一辆卡车差点撞到我们，我们差点出车祸。

词汇almost 几乎；get into an accident 出车祸；nearly 差点 语法when 引导时间状语从句；过去进行时/一般过去时搭配。

Haitao: Oh dear, I'm glad you were safe!

天哪，真高兴你们没事！

词汇be glad 高兴；safe 安全的 语法I'm glad + 从句。

Lisa: Yes, but when we got home later, we found water everywhere.

是的，但后来我们到家时，发现到处都是水。

词汇get home 到家；everywhere 到处 语法when 引导时间状语从句；find + 宾语 + 状语。

Lisa: The house looked awful!

房子看起来糟透了！

词汇look awful 看起来糟糕 语法look + adj. 表示“看起来……”。

Lisa: Was yours OK?

你们家还好吗？

词汇yours 你们的（家） 语法名词性物主代词 yours；省略结构。

Haitao: Yes. Luckily, we covered our windows and moved our things off the floor before the typhoon started.

还好。幸运的是，台风开始前我们把窗户遮起来了，还把东西从地板上移开了。

词汇luckily 幸运地；cover 遮盖；move...off 把……移开；floor 地板 语法before 引导时间状语从句；and 连接并列谓语。

Haitao: We stored food and water too.

我们也储存了食物和水。

词汇store 储存；food 食物 语法一般过去时；too 表示“也”。

Lisa: That's good!

那很好！

词汇good 好的 语法That's + adj. 表示评价。

Lisa: We'll do that next time.

下次我们也会这么做。

词汇next time 下次 语法一般将来时 will do。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，海涛询问莉萨一家在台风期间是否遇到麻烦。莉萨说很糟糕：他们不知道台风要来，正从亲戚家开车回家时大风开始；往回开时雨下得很大，一辆卡车差点撞到他们。到家后发现到处都是水，房子看起来很糟。海涛说他们家提前遮好窗户、把东西移离地板，还储存了食物和水，所以还好。莉萨说下次也会这么做。

English Summary

In the conversation, Haitao asks Lisa whether her family had trouble during the typhoon. Lisa says it was awful: they didn't know a typhoon was coming, and the strong winds started while they were driving home from their relatives' house. It was raining hard while they drove back, and a truck nearly hit them. When they got home, they found water everywhere and the house looked awful. Haitao says his family covered the windows, moved things off the floor and stored food and water before the typhoon, so they were fine. Lisa says they will do that next time.

Section B 1b · How One Girl Saved Many Lives

Section B 1b 主题：海啸预警 · 知识与勇气

On 26 December 2004, Tilly Smith, a 10-year-old English schoolgirl, and her family were on holiday in Phuket, Thailand.

2004 年 12 月 26 日，10 岁的英国女学生蒂莉·史密斯和她的家人正在泰国普吉岛度假。

词汇on holiday 度假；schoolgirl 女学生 语法be on holiday；a 10-year-old... 作同位语。

Everyone was enjoying the beautiful weather that morning.

那天早上，每个人都在享受美好的天气。

词汇enjoy 享受；weather 天气 语法过去进行时 was enjoying 表示当时的持续状态。

Some people were swimming in the sea, and some were relaxing on the beach.

一些人在海里游泳，一些人在海滩上放松。

词汇relax 放松；beach 海滩 语法过去进行时并列；some...some...。

But while Tilly was walking along the beach with her family, she noticed something strange.

但当蒂莉和家人沿着海滩散步时，她注意到一些奇怪的现象。

词汇while 当……时；notice 注意到；strange 奇怪的 语法while 引导时间状语从句；notice sth.。

Thick white froth was forming on top of the waves.

海浪顶部形成了厚厚的白色泡沫。

词汇froth 泡沫；form 形成；wave 海浪 语法过去进行时 was forming；on top of 表示位置。

Next, she saw that the waves were coming in instead of going out.

接着，她看到海浪是涌进来而不是退出去。

词汇come in 涌进来；instead of 而不是；go out 退出去 语法that 引导宾语从句；instead of + 动名词。

Just then, she remembered something important.

就在这时，她想起了一件重要的事。

词汇just then 就在那时；remember 想起 语法一般过去时 remembered。

Two weeks before, her geography teacher had explained that earthquakes under the sea could create dangerous waves called tsunamis!

两周前，她的地理老师解释过，海底地震会产生被称为海啸的危险海浪！

词汇geography 地理；earthquake 地震；create 产生；tsunami 海啸 语法过去完成时 had explained；that 引导宾语从句；called... 是过去分词短语作定语。

"I know what's going to happen! There's going to be a big wave," Tilly cried.

“我知道会发生什么了！会有一个大浪，”蒂莉喊道。

词汇happen 发生；wave 浪 语法what 引导宾语从句；be going to 表示将要。

Unfortunately, her family didn't believe her and wanted to continue their walk.

不幸的是，她的家人不相信她，还想继续散步。

词汇unfortunately 不幸的是；believe 相信；continue 继续 语法didn't believe；want to do sth.。

However, Tilly refused to take a single step further.

然而，蒂莉拒绝再往前走一步。

词汇however 然而；refuse to do 拒绝做；step 步 语法refuse to do sth.；single 强调“一步也不”。

Finally, her mother decided to continue the walk alone, and the rest of her family returned to the hotel.

最后，她的母亲决定独自继续散步，家人的其余成员则返回了酒店。

词汇decide to do 决定做；alone 独自；the rest of 其余的 语法decide to do sth.；and 连接并列分句。

Back at the hotel, Tilly's father told a security guard about the tsunami.

回到酒店后，蒂莉的父亲把海啸的事告诉了一名保安。

词汇security guard 保安；tell sb. about sth. 告诉某人某事 语法tell sb. about sth.；Back at the hotel 作地点状语。

The security guard was shocked.

保安很震惊。

词汇be shocked 震惊 语法过去分词作表语。

He knew about the huge earthquake in nearby Indonesia.

他知道附近印度尼西亚发生了大地震。

词汇huge 巨大的；nearby 附近的；Indonesia 印度尼西亚 语法know about；nearby 作定语。

It meant that a tsunami was possible!

这意味着海啸有可能发生！

词汇mean 意味着；possible 可能的 语法that 引导宾语从句。

He jumped into action right away and rushed to get people off the beach.

他立即行动起来，急忙把人们从海滩上疏散开。

词汇jump into action 立即行动；right away 立刻；rush 冲；get sb. off 使某人离开 语法and 连接并列谓语；get people off the beach。

Everyone began to panic.

每个人都开始恐慌。

词汇panic 恐慌 语法begin to do sth.。

People were screaming and running as fast as they could when they saw a huge wall of water coming from the sea.

当人们看到一堵巨大的水墙从海里涌来时，都尖叫着，尽可能快地奔跑。

词汇scream 尖叫；as fast as one can 尽可能快；a wall of water 水墙 语法过去进行时 were screaming and running；as...as one can；when 引导时间状语从句。

Tilly was right!

蒂莉是对的！

词汇right 正确的 语法主系表结构。

Her mother was one of the last people on the beach, but thankfully, her family made it to safety just as the water hit the hotel.

她的母亲是海滩上最后一批人之一，但值得庆幸的是，就在海水冲击酒店的时候，她的家人安全了。

词汇thankfully 值得庆幸的是；make it to safety 安全到达；hit 冲击 语法one of the last people；just as 引导时间状语从句。

The 2004 tsunami caused over 200,000 deaths, but no one on the beach died that day.

2004 年的海啸造成了 20 多万人死亡，但那天海滩上没有人丧生。

词汇cause 导致；death 死亡；die 死亡 语法but 转折；no one 作主语。

This true story shows how a single lesson could mean the difference between life and death.

这个真实的故事表明，一堂课可能意味着生与死的差别。

词汇true 真实的；difference 差别 语法how 引导宾语从句；the difference between...。

Thanks to her knowledge and quick thinking, one schoolgirl was able to save over 100 lives.

多亏了她的知识和敏捷的思维，一个女学生得以拯救了 100 多条生命。

词汇thanks to 多亏；knowledge 知识；quick thinking 敏捷的思维；save 拯救 语法thanks to；be able to do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章讲述 2004 年海啸中一个女孩拯救众人的真实故事。2004 年 12 月 26 日，10 岁的英国女孩蒂莉和家人在泰国普吉岛度假。她发现海浪顶部有厚厚白沫、海水不断涌进，想起两周前地理课上学过的海啸知识，于是大喊会有大浪，但家人不相信。最后母亲独自留下散步，其余人回酒店。父亲告诉保安，保安得知附近印尼地震后立即疏散海滩人群，人们看到水墙后奋力逃跑。蒂莉的母亲是最后撤离的人之一，但全家安全。2004 年海啸造成 20 多万人死亡，那天海滩上却无人丧生。

English Summary

The text tells a true story from the 2004 tsunami. On 26 December 2004, ten-year-old Tilly Smith and her family were on holiday in Phuket, Thailand. She noticed thick white froth on the waves and the sea coming in instead of going out, and remembered her geography lesson about tsunamis two weeks before. She cried that a big wave was coming, but her family did not believe her; her mother continued the walk alone while the others returned to the hotel. Tilly's father told a security guard, who knew about the huge earthquake in Indonesia and rushed to clear the beach. People screamed and ran when they saw the wall of water. Tilly's mother was among the last on the beach, but the family reached safety. The tsunami caused over 200,000 deaths, yet no one on that beach died.

Section B 3a · Ma Li's Bad Day

Section B 3a 主题：倒霉的一天 · 叙事顺序

Last Friday started terribly.

上周五一开始就很糟糕。

词汇start 开始；terribly 糟糕地 语法start + adv.；一般过去时。

First, my alarm clock didn't go off, so I woke up late and had to rush to the bus stop.

首先，我的闹钟没响，所以我醒得很晚，不得不匆匆赶往公交站。

词汇alarm clock 闹钟；go off 响；rush 冲；bus stop 公交站 语法go off；so 连接结果；have to do。

I had a test that morning, so I was reviewing my notes while I was waiting for my bus.

那天早上我有一场考试，所以我在等公交车的时候复习笔记。

词汇test 考试；review 复习；notes 笔记；wait for 等 语法过去进行时 was reviewing 与 while 引导的时间状语从句搭配。

All of a sudden, thunder rolled across the sky.

突然，雷声在天空中响起。

词汇all of a sudden 突然；thunder 雷；roll 滚动；sky 天空 语法all of a sudden 作状语。

Then it started to pour.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇短文讲述马丽倒霉的一天。上周五闹钟没响，她起晚了，匆忙赶到公交站；等车时复习笔记，突然打雷下雨，风大得没法用伞。车来时她又冷又湿，司机说因为常走的路上山体滑坡会迟到。她气喘吁吁跑到教室，老师已在门口等着，很多人也因暴雨迟到，结果老师说根本不考试了——倒霉的一天竟然有了好运。

English Summary

The passage describes Ma Li's bad day. Last Friday her alarm clock didn't go off, so she woke up late and rushed to the bus stop. While waiting she reviewed her notes; then thunder rolled and it started to pour, and the wind was too strong for her umbrella. By the time the bus came she was cold and wet, and the driver said they would be late because of a landslide on their usual road. She reached the classroom out of breath, the teacher was already waiting at the door, and many others were also late. As a result, the teacher said they weren't having a test after all—so her unlucky day brought some luck.
