Section A · 3a · Volunteering at a Nursing Home

Section A · 3a 主题：养老院志愿服务（教材 P74，Ella / Teng Fei 对话）

Ella: Hi, Teng Fei.

嗨，腾飞。

词汇Teng Fei 腾飞（人名） 语法日常问候语；称呼语放句末，前面用逗号。

Ella: What are your plans for the summer holidays?

你暑假有什么计划？

词汇plans 计划；summer holidays 暑假 语法What are your plans for...? 用于询问计划。

Teng Fei: I think I'll do some volunteer work at a nursing home with my youth group.

我想和我的青年团体一起在养老院做一些志愿工作。

词汇volunteer work 志愿工作；nursing home 养老院；youth group 青年团体 语法I think 后接宾语从句；will 表将来。

Teng Fei: I've been a volunteer there since last year.

从去年起我就一直在那里做志愿者。

词汇since 自从；last year 去年 语法现在完成时 have been；since + 时间点表示“自从……”。

Ella: Oh!

哦！

词汇oh 哦 语法感叹词，表示惊讶。

Ella: What do you do?

你都做些什么？

词汇what 什么；do 做 语法一般现在时询问日常活动。

Teng Fei: I exercise with the elderly to help them stay active.

我和老人们一起锻炼，帮助他们保持活力。

词汇exercise 锻炼；the elderly 老年人；stay active 保持活力 语法不定式 to help 表目的；help sb. do sth.。

Teng Fei: Sometimes I play Chinese chess with them.

有时我和他们下中国象棋。

词汇sometimes 有时；Chinese chess 中国象棋 语法sometimes 是频度副词；play + 棋类名词。

Teng Fei: I've even taught some of them how to use a mobile phone.

我甚至还教过他们中的一些人怎么用手机。

词汇even 甚至；teach 教；mobile phone 手机 语法现在完成时；teach sb. how to do sth.；some of them。

Ella: That's great.

那真好。

词汇great 很好的 语法That's + 形容词，表示评价。

Ella: Do their families visit them often?

他们的家人常来看望他们吗？

词汇family 家人；visit 看望；often 经常 语法Do + 主语 + 动词原形? 一般现在时一般疑问句。

Teng Fei: Some do, but not all.

有些来，但不是所有人都来。

词汇some 一些；not all 不是全部 语法do 代替 visit them often；but not all 表部分否定。

Teng Fei: Many elderly people are very lonely.

许多老人非常孤独。

词汇lonely 孤独的 语法many + 复数名词；lonely 作表语。

Ella: That makes me sad.

那让我很难过。

词汇make 使；sad 难过的 语法make sb. + 形容词表示“使某人处于某种状态”。

Ella: We'll all be old one day too.

我们有一天也都会变老。

词汇one day 有一天；old 年老的 语法will 表将来；one day 表示“将来某一天”。

Ella: It's important to care for the elderly.

关爱老人很重要。

词汇important 重要的；care for 照顾；关爱 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语。

Teng Fei: That's right!

说得对！

词汇that's right 说得对 语法表示赞同的口语表达。

Teng Fei: You know, they actually have so many interesting life stories and experiences to share with us.

你知道吗，他们其实有很多有趣的人生故事和经历可以和我们分享。

词汇actually 其实；experience 经历；share with 与……分享 语法You know 是插入语；不定式 to share 作后置定语。

Ella: Can I join your group of volunteers?

我可以加入你们的志愿者队伍吗？

词汇join 加入；group 团体 语法Can I...? 请求允许；join + 团体。

Ella: I'd love to help.

我很愿意帮忙。

词汇I'd love to 我很愿意 语法I'd = I would；would love to do sth.。

Teng Fei: Yes, come along!

当然，一起来吧！

词汇come along 一起来 语法Come along! 是邀请用语。

Teng Fei: It's fun to volunteer together.

一起做志愿者很有趣。

词汇fun 有趣的；together 一起 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在 Ella 和 Teng Fei 之间。Ella 问 Teng Fei 暑假有什么计划，Teng Fei 说他打算和青年团体一起去养老院做志愿工作，从去年起他就一直在那里做志愿者。他陪老人们锻炼、帮他们保持活力，有时和他们下中国象棋，还教他们用手机。Ella 问老人们的家人是否常来看望，Teng Fei 说有些人来、有些人来不了，许多老人很孤独；Ella 听了很难过，觉得每个人都会变老，关爱老人很重要。Teng Fei 说老人们有很多有趣的人生故事可以分享，Ella 于是请求加入志愿队伍，Teng Fei 欢迎她一起来。

English Summary

This is a conversation between Ella and Teng Fei. Ella asks Teng Fei about his plans for the summer holidays, and he says he will do some volunteer work at a nursing home with his youth group; he has been a volunteer there since last year. He exercises with the elderly to help them stay active, sometimes plays Chinese chess with them, and has even taught some of them how to use a mobile phone. Ella asks whether their families visit them often, and Teng Fei says some do but not all — many elderly people are very lonely. Ella feels sad and says we will all be old one day too, so it is important to care for the elderly. Teng Fei adds that the elderly have many interesting life stories and experiences to share. Ella asks to join the group of volunteers, and Teng Fei welcomes her.

语法点 Grammar

What are your plans for...? 询问计划。

I think I'll do some volunteer work at a nursing home with my youth group.

I think 后接宾语从句；will 表将来。

I've been a volunteer there since last year.

现在完成时 have been；since + 时间点表示“自从……”。

What do you do?

一般现在时询问职业或日常活动。

I exercise with the elderly to help them stay active.

不定式 to help 表目的；help sb. do sth.。

Sometimes I play Chinese chess with them.

sometimes 频度副词；play + 棋类名词。

I've even taught some of them how to use a mobile phone.

现在完成时；teach sb. how to do sth.；some of them。

Do + 主语 + 动词原形? 一般现在时一般疑问句。

do 代替 visit them often；but not all 表部分否定。

many + 复数名词；lonely 作表语。

That makes me sad.

make sb. + 形容词表示“使某人处于某种状态”。

will 表将来；one day 表示“将来某一天”。

It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I've been a volunteer there since last year.

从去年起我就一直在那里做志愿者。

现在完成时 have been 表示从过去持续到现在的状态；since + 时间点表示“自从……”。

I exercise with the elderly to help them stay active.

我和老人们一起锻炼，帮助他们保持活力。

不定式 to help 作目的状语；help sb. (to) do sth.；the elderly 表示“老年人（群体）”。

It's important to care for the elderly.

关爱老人很重要。

It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型，it 是形式主语；care for 表示“照顾、关爱”。

Section B · 1b · Blue Sky Rescue Volunteer's Speech

Section B · 1b 主题：蓝天救援队志愿者演讲（教材 P76–P77 两页合并）

Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for inviting me to speak to you about Blue Sky Rescue today.

女士们、先生们，感谢你们邀请我今天来向大家介绍蓝天救援队。

词汇ladies and gentlemen 女士们、先生们；Blue Sky Rescue 蓝天救援队；speak to sb. 向某人讲述 语法thank sb. for doing sth.；invite sb. to do sth.。

Let me begin with my own story.

让我从自己的故事讲起。

词汇begin with 以……开始 语法Let sb. do sth.；begin with 表示“以……开始”。

Back in 2008, I was hiking in the mountains with some friends when one of them fell and got a bad head injury.

早在 2008 年，我和几个朋友在山里徒步时，其中一人摔倒，头部受了重伤。

词汇hike 徒步；mountains 山区；head injury 头部受伤 语法过去进行时 + when 一般过去时；one of them 作从句主语。

We had to find help quickly, so we called an emergency number.

我们必须尽快求助，于是拨打了紧急求救电话。

词汇emergency number 紧急求救电话；quickly 迅速地 语法have to 的过去式 had to；so 表结果。

It was already dark, and we couldn't even see where we were.

天已经黑了，我们甚至看不清自己在哪儿。

词汇already 已经；dark 黑暗的；where 哪里 语法couldn't = could not；where 引导宾语从句。

After some time, we heard dogs barking and the voices of the Blue Sky Rescue team.

过了一段时间，我们听到狗叫声和蓝天救援队的声音。

词汇after some time 过了一段时间；bark 狗叫；voice 声音 语法hear sb./sth. doing sth. 表示“听见……正在做”。

I nearly started crying.

我差点哭出来。

词汇nearly 差点；cry 哭 语法start doing sth.；nearly 修饰动词。

We were so thankful that they found us!

我们非常感激他们找到了我们！

词汇thankful 感激的 语法so + 形容词 + that 从句表示“如此……以至于……”。

I've never forgotten that day.

我从未忘记那一天。

词汇never 从不；forget 忘记 语法现在完成时 have forgotten；never 表示“从不”。

Since then, I've wanted to help others too.

从那时起，我也一直想帮助别人。

词汇since then 从那时起；others 其他人 语法since then 常与现在完成时连用。

I signed up with Blue Sky Rescue in 2009, and I've been a volunteer ever since.

2009 年我报名加入了蓝天救援队，从那时起就一直是志愿者。

词汇sign up 报名参加；ever since 从那时起 语法一般过去时 + 现在完成时；ever since 表示“从那时起”。

We've been to schools and other communities to teach children and adults how to stay safe and prepare for the worst.

我们去过学校和其他社区，教孩子和大人如何保持安全、为最坏的情况做准备。

词汇community 社区；adult 成年人；prepare for 为……做准备 语法现在完成时；不定式 to teach 表目的；how to do sth.。

We've also rescued people during emergencies.

我们也在紧急情况下救过人。

词汇rescue 营救；emergency 紧急情况 语法现在完成时 have rescued。

We've provided medical aid and searched for missing people after natural disasters, like the 2013 earthquake in Ya'an and the typhoon in the Philippines in the same year.

自然灾害后，我们提供医疗援助、搜寻失踪人员，比如 2013 年雅安地震和同年的菲律宾台风。

词汇medical aid 医疗援助；search for 搜寻；natural disaster 自然灾害；earthquake 地震；typhoon 台风 语法现在完成时；provide sth.；like 用于举例。

In 2023, we even helped in places as far away as Türkiye!

2023 年，我们甚至远赴土耳其提供帮助！

词汇as far away as 远至；Türkiye 土耳其 语法as far away as 表示“远至……”。

Working as a volunteer has taught me many practical life-saving skills.

做志愿者教会了我许多实用的救生技能。

词汇practical 实用的；life-saving 救生的；skill 技能 语法动名词 Working 作主语；现在完成时。

It has also helped me understand the power of teamwork because it's the key to successful rescues.

它也帮助我理解了团队合作的力量，因为团队合作是成功救援的关键。

词汇power 力量；teamwork 团队合作；key 关键 语法现在完成时；help sb. do sth.；because 引导原因状语从句。

Most of all, being a volunteer has taught me the value of every life.

最重要的是，做志愿者教会了我每一条生命的价值。

词汇most of all 最重要的是；value 价值 语法动名词 being 作主语；现在完成时。

The work is difficult.

这项工作很艰难。

词汇difficult 艰难的 语法主系表结构。

It's voluntary, so we aren't paid.

它是自愿的，所以没有报酬。

词汇voluntary 自愿的；paid 有报酬的 语法so 表结果；被动语态 aren't paid。

We often go without food or sleep.

我们常常顾不上吃饭和睡觉。

词汇go without 没有……也凑合；sleep 睡眠 语法go without 表示“没有……也勉强应付”。

But our goal has always been to save as many people as we can.

但我们的目标一直是救尽可能多的人。

词汇goal 目标；save 拯救；as many ... as we can 尽可能多 语法现在完成时；as ... as one can 表示“尽可能……”。

I'm just an ordinary person, but I'll rush into danger, just like everyone else in Blue Sky Rescue.

我只是一个普通人，但我会像蓝天救援队的每个人一样冲进危险。

词汇ordinary 普通的；rush into 冲进；danger 危险 语法but 表转折；just like 表示“就像”。

To the people we've saved and their loved ones, I know we're their heroes.

对被我们救过的人和他们的亲人来说，我知道我们就是他们的英雄。

词汇loved ones 亲人；hero 英雄 语法To + 人 表示“对……来说”；we've saved 是定语从句。

And that makes everything we do worth it.

那让我们所做的一切都值得。

词汇worth it 值得 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词；worth it 表示“值得”。

We want to do even more, but we can't do it all alone.

我们想做更多，但单靠我们做不到。

词汇even more 更多；alone 独自 语法but 表转折；all alone 表示“完全靠自己”。

Will you join us as a volunteer?

你愿意加入我们做志愿者吗？

词汇join 加入；as 作为 语法Will you...? 用于邀请或请求。

Together we can make a difference!

我们一起就能带来改变！

词汇together 一起；make a difference 带来改变 语法make a difference 是固定搭配。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇跨 P76–P77 两页的演讲稿来自一位蓝天救援队志愿者。他先感谢邀请，然后讲自己的故事：2008 年他和朋友在山里徒步时，一位朋友摔倒受重伤；天黑了，他们找不到方向，只好拨打紧急电话。后来他们听到狗叫声和蓝天救援队的声音，终于获救，他激动得差点哭出来。从那天起他就想帮助别人，2009 年加入蓝天救援队，一直做志愿者至今。他们去学校和社区教孩子和大人如何保持安全、做最坏的准备，在紧急情况下救人，在自然灾害后提供医疗援助、搜寻失踪者，比如 2013 年雅安地震和同年的菲律宾台风；2023 年他们甚至远赴土耳其救援。他说志愿服务教会他实用的救生技能、团队合作的力量，最重要的是每条生命的价值。工作艰难、没有报酬，常常顾不上吃饭睡觉，但目标始终是救尽可能多的人。他只是一个普通人，却愿意冲进危险；对获救者和他们的亲人来说，他们就是英雄。最后他号召大家加入志愿者队伍：“Together we can make a difference!”

English Summary

This speech, which runs across pages 76–77, is given by a Blue Sky Rescue volunteer. He thanks the audience and begins with his own story: back in 2008, while hiking in the mountains with friends, one of them fell and got a bad head injury. It was dark and they couldn't see where they were, so they called an emergency number. After some time they heard dogs barking and the voices of the Blue Sky Rescue team — they were so thankful that they found them! He has never forgotten that day and has wanted to help others ever since. He signed up with Blue Sky Rescue in 2009 and has been a volunteer ever since. They have taught children and adults how to stay safe and prepare for the worst, rescued people during emergencies, provided medical aid and searched for missing people after natural disasters like the 2013 earthquake in Ya'an and the typhoon in the Philippines; in 2023 they even helped as far away as Türkiye. Volunteering has taught him practical life-saving skills, the power of teamwork, and the value of every life. The work is difficult and unpaid, and they often go without food or sleep, but their goal is to save as many people as they can. He is just an ordinary person, but he will rush into danger like everyone else in Blue Sky Rescue. Finally he calls on the audience to join them, because together they can make a difference!

A regular volunteer at the hospital, Anna, was there with her therapy dog, Banjo.

A regular volunteer at the hospital 是 Anna 的同位语。

Anna was kind enough to share her story with me.

be kind enough to do sth. 好心地做某事。

Often, just playing with them was enough to help her feel happy and relaxed after a long day.

动名词短语作主语，谓语用 was；be enough to do sth.。

But one day, Anna realized that not everyone was lucky enough to have a pet.

that 引导宾语从句；not everyone 表部分否定。

She started thinking: What could she do?

start doing sth.；冒号后是直接引语。

Banjo has worked as a therapy dog for almost three years.

现在完成时；work as 表示“作为……工作”。

When people are stressed, it often takes them longer to get better.

when 时间状语从句；It takes sb. time to do sth. 句型。

A therapy dog helps people feel better and be less stressed, often just by being there!

help sb. do sth.；by doing sth. 表方式。

If I'm lucky enough to have a dog of my own in the future, I'll try to volunteer just like Anna and Banjo!

if 条件状语从句（主将从现）；try to do sth.；a dog of my own。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Those were words I never expected to hear from my mum when I visited her in hospital.

那是我去医院看望妈妈时从没想过会听到的话。

I never expected to hear... 是定语从句修饰 words；when 引导时间状语从句。

Often, just playing with them was enough to help her feel happy and relaxed after a long day.

常常，仅仅和它们玩耍就足以让她在漫长的一天后感到快乐和放松。

动名词短语 just playing with them 作主语，谓语用 was；be enough to do sth.。

If I'm lucky enough to have a dog of my own in the future, I'll try to volunteer just like Anna and Banjo!

如果将来我有幸养一只自己的狗，我也要像 Anna 和 Banjo 那样去做志愿者！

if 引导条件状语从句，主句用一般将来时（主将从现）；a dog of my own 表示“我自己的狗”。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 教材 P93–P94 是 Reading Plus 板块，文章 Unusual Volunteers 是一篇博客，跨两页，本页把 P93 与 P94 续文合并成完整一篇。
  • 读前看标题和插图，猜一猜 “unusual volunteers” 指的是什么样的志愿者。

读中

  • 边读边找出 Linda 妈妈的变化，以及 Anna 和 Banjo 做了什么。
  • 注意文中的现在完成时和 if 条件句。

读后

  • 用 4—5 句话复述 Linda 博客的内容。
  • 讨论：为什么说动物也能成为志愿者？你身边有类似的例子吗？

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面 4 篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. Where does Teng Fei volunteer?

  1. At a nursing home.
  2. At a hospital.
  3. At a primary school.
  4. At a museum.
答案与解析

A 原文：I'll do some volunteer work at a nursing home with my youth group. 可知腾飞在养老院做志愿者。

2. What does Teng Fei do with the elderly?

  1. He cleans their rooms.
  2. He exercises with them and plays Chinese chess.
  3. He cooks meals for them.
  4. He takes them to the hospital.
答案与解析

B 原文：I exercise with the elderly to help them stay active. Sometimes I play Chinese chess with them. 可知他陪老人锻炼、下中国象棋。

3. What happened to one of the speaker's friends in 2008?

  1. He got lost in the city.
  2. He broke his leg.
  3. He fell and got a bad head injury.
  4. He was caught in a typhoon.
答案与解析

C 原文：one of them fell and got a bad head injury. 可知一位朋友摔倒受了重伤。

4. Why does the speaker volunteer with Blue Sky Rescue?

  1. Because he is paid well.
  2. Because he wants to be famous.
  3. Because his parents asked him to.
  4. Because he was saved by the team and wants to help others.
答案与解析

D 原文：I've never forgotten that day. Since then, I've wanted to help others too. 可知他因获救而决心帮助他人。

5. Who are the student volunteers going to help?

  1. International students.
  2. Old people in a nursing home.
  3. Sick children in hospital.
  4. Farmers in the countryside.
答案与解析

A 原文：Join us to be a friend to our international students! 可知他们要帮助国际学生。

6. How can students sign up as volunteers?

  1. By making a phone call.
  2. By dropping the school an email.
  3. By visiting the school office.
  4. By filling in a form online.
答案与解析

B 原文：To volunteer with us, just drop us an email! 可知报名方式是发邮件。

7. What kind of animal is Banjo?

  1. A cat.
  2. A rabbit.
  3. A golden retriever.
  4. A parrot.
答案与解析

C 原文：a golden retriever! 可知 Banjo 是一只金毛寻回犬。

8. What does Banjo do at the hospital?

  1. It helps the doctors.
  2. It carries medicine.
  3. It cleans the rooms.
  4. It helps people feel better and less stressed.
答案与解析

D 原文：A therapy dog helps people feel better and be less stressed 可知治疗犬帮助人们感觉更好、压力更小。
