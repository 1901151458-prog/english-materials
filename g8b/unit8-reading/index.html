Section A 3a · Summer Volunteer Plans

Section A 3a 主题：志愿服务 · 现在完成时

Ella: Hi, Teng Fei. What are your plans for the summer holidays?

嗨，腾飞。你暑假有什么计划？

词汇plan 计划；summer holidays 暑假 语法What are your plans...? 一般现在时询问计划。

Teng Fei: I think I'll do some volunteer work at a nursing home with my youth group.

我想我会和我的青年团体一起去养老院做一些志愿工作。

词汇volunteer work 志愿工作；nursing home 养老院；youth group 青年团体 语法I think 后接宾语从句；will do 表将来。

Teng Fei: I've been a volunteer there since last year.

从去年开始我就在那里做志愿者了。

词汇volunteer 志愿者；since 自从 语法现在完成时 have been；since last year 常与现在完成时连用。

Ella: Oh! What do you do?

哦！你做些什么？

词汇do 做 语法一般现在时询问日常做法。

Teng Fei: I exercise with the elderly to help them stay active.

我和老人们一起锻炼，帮助他们保持活力。

词汇exercise 锻炼；the elderly 老人；stay active 保持活力 语法the elderly 表示“老年人”群体；to help 作目的状语。

Teng Fei: Sometimes I play Chinese chess with them.

有时候我和他们下中国象棋。

词汇sometimes 有时；Chinese chess 中国象棋 语法一般现在时表示习惯。

Teng Fei: I've even taught some of them how to use a mobile phone.

我甚至还教他们中的一些人如何使用手机。

词汇even 甚至；teach sb. how to do 教某人如何做；mobile phone 手机 语法现在完成时 have taught；teach sb. how to do sth.。

Ella: That's great.

那很棒。

词汇great 很棒的 语法That's + adj. 表示评价。

Ella: Do their families visit them often?

他们的家人经常来看望他们吗？

词汇family 家人；visit 看望；often 经常 语法一般现在时疑问句；频度副词 often。

Teng Fei: Some do, but not all.

有些人会，但不是所有人都这样。

词汇some 一些；all 所有 语法简略回答 some do；but not all 表示部分否定。

Teng Fei: Many elderly people are very lonely.

很多老人都很孤独。

词汇elderly 年长的；lonely 孤独的 语法主系表结构。

Ella: That makes me sad.

这让我很难过。

词汇make sb. sad 使某人难过 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词。

Ella: We'll all be old one day too.

我们总有一天也会变老的。

词汇one day 总有一天 语法will be 一般将来时。

Ella: It's important to care for the elderly.

照顾老人很重要。

词汇care for 照顾；the elderly 老人 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.。

Teng Fei: That's right!

对呀！

词汇right 正确的 语法表示同意。

Teng Fei: You know, they actually have so many interesting life stories and experiences to share with us.

你知道吗，实际上他们有很多有趣的人生故事和经历可以和我们分享。

词汇actually 实际上；experience 经历；share with 与……分享 语法have sth. to do sth.；share sth. with sb.。

Ella: Can I join your group of volunteers?

我能加入你们的志愿者小组吗？

词汇join 加入；group 小组 语法Can I...? 请求。

Ella: I'd love to help.

我很乐意帮忙。

词汇would love to 很乐意 语法I'd love to do sth.。

Teng Fei: Yes, come along!

可以呀，一起来吧！

词汇come along 一起来 语法祈使句 Come along!。

Teng Fei: It's fun to volunteer together.

一起做志愿工作很有趣。

词汇fun 有趣的；volunteer 做志愿工作 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，埃拉问腾飞暑假的计划，腾飞说会和青年团体去养老院做志愿工作，从去年起就是那里的志愿者。他和老人一起锻炼、下中国象棋，甚至教他们用手机。埃拉问老人家人是否常来，腾飞说有些人会，但很多老人很孤独；埃拉说这让她难过，我们也会变老，照顾老人很重要。腾飞说老人有很多有趣的故事和经历可以分享，埃拉想加入志愿者小组。

English Summary

In the conversation, Ella asks about Teng Fei's summer plans. He says he will do volunteer work at a nursing home with his youth group and has been a volunteer there since last year. He exercises with the elderly, plays Chinese chess with them and has even taught some of them to use a mobile phone. Ella asks whether their families visit often; Teng Fei says some do but not all, and many elderly people are lonely. Ella says that makes her sad and that caring for the elderly is important. Teng Fei says the elderly have interesting stories to share, and Ella asks to join the volunteer group.

Section B 1b · A Blue Sky Rescue Volunteer's Speech

Section B 1b 主题：志愿服务 · 演讲

Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for inviting me to speak to you about Blue Sky Rescue today.

女士们、先生们，感谢你们今天邀请我来和大家讲讲蓝天救援队。

词汇ladies and gentlemen 女士们先生们；invite 邀请；rescue 救援 语法thank you for doing sth.；invite sb. to do sth.。

Let me begin with my own story.

让我从自己的故事讲起。

词汇begin with 从……开始 语法Let me do sth.；begin with。

Back in 2008, I was hiking in the mountains with some friends when one of them fell and got a bad head injury.

2008 年，我和一些朋友在山里徒步旅行时，其中一个人摔倒了，头部受了重伤。

词汇hike 徒步；fall 摔倒；injury 伤 语法过去进行时 + when 引导的时间状语从句。

We had to find help quickly, so we called an emergency number.

我们必须尽快寻求帮助，所以我们拨打了紧急电话。

词汇emergency 紧急情况；emergency number 急救电话 语法have to do；so 连接结果。

It was already dark, and we couldn't even see where we were.

天已经黑了，我们甚至都看不清自己在哪里。

词汇already 已经；even 甚至 语法where 引导宾语从句。

After some time, we heard dogs barking and the voices of the Blue Sky Rescue team.

过了一会儿，我们听到了狗叫声和蓝天救援队队员的声音。

词汇hear sb./sth. doing 听到……正在做；voice 声音 语法hear sth. doing sth. 现在分词作宾补。

I nearly started crying.

我差点哭了出来。

词汇nearly 几乎 语法start doing sth.。

We were so thankful that they found us!

我们非常感激他们找到了我们！

词汇be thankful 感激的 语法so + adj. + that... 引导结果状语从句。

I've never forgotten that day.

我从未忘记那一天。

词汇forget 忘记 语法现在完成时 have never forgotten。

Since then, I've wanted to help others too.

从那以后，我也想帮助别人。

词汇since then 从那以后；help others 帮助别人 语法since then 常与现在完成时连用。

I signed up with Blue Sky Rescue in 2009, and I've been a volunteer ever since.

我在 2009 年报名加入了蓝天救援队，从那以后就一直是一名志愿者。

词汇sign up 报名；ever since 从那以后 语法一般过去时 signed up；现在完成时 have been。

We've been to schools and other communities to teach children and adults how to stay safe and prepare for the worst.

我们去过学校和其他社区，教儿童和成年人如何保持安全并为最坏的情况做准备。

词汇community 社区；adult 成年人；prepare for 为……做准备 语法have been to；teach sb. how to do sth.；prepare for the worst。

We've also rescued people during emergencies.

我们也在紧急情况下救援过人们。

词汇rescue 救援 语法现在完成时 have rescued。

We've provided medical aid and searched for missing people after natural disasters, like the 2013 earthquake in Ya'an and the typhoon in the Philippines in the same year.

我们提供过医疗援助，还在自然灾害后搜寻过失踪人员，比如 2013 年的雅安地震和同年菲律宾的台风灾害。

词汇medical aid 医疗援助；search for 搜寻；missing 失踪的；natural disaster 自然灾害 语法现在完成时 have provided / have searched；like 举例。

In 2023, we even helped in places as far away as Türkiye!

2023 年，我们甚至还去了远在土耳其的地方提供帮助！

词汇as far away as 远至；Türkiye 土耳其 语法even 强调；as far away as。

Working as a volunteer has taught me many practical life-saving skills.

做志愿者教会了我许多实用的救生技能。

词汇practical 实用的；life-saving 救生的；skill 技能 语法动名词 Working 作主语；现在完成时 has taught。

It has also helped me understand the power of teamwork because it's the key to successful rescues.

它也帮助我理解了团队合作的力量，因为这是成功救援的关键。

词汇teamwork 团队合作；key 关键；successful 成功的 语法现在完成时 has helped；because 引导原因状语从句；the key to。

Most of all, being a volunteer has taught me the value of every life.

最重要的是，做志愿者让我懂得了每个生命的价值。

词汇most of all 最重要的是；value 价值 语法动名词 being 作主语；现在完成时。

The work is difficult.

这份工作很艰难。

词汇difficult 困难的 语法主系表结构。

It's voluntary, so we aren't paid.

它是志愿性质的，所以我们没有报酬。

词汇voluntary 志愿的；be paid 得到报酬 语法被动语态 aren't paid；so 连接结果。

We often go without food or sleep.

我们经常没吃的、没觉睡。

词汇go without 没有……也得过 语法go without；or 连接并列名词。

But our goal has always been to save as many people as we can.

但我们的目标一直是尽可能多地拯救生命。

词汇goal 目标；save 拯救 语法现在完成时 has been；as many as we can。

I'm just an ordinary person, but I'll rush into danger, just like everyone else in Blue Sky Rescue.

我只是一个普通人，但我会冲向危险，就像蓝天救援队的每一个人一样。

词汇ordinary 普通的；rush into 冲进；danger 危险 语法but 转折；just like 引导比较。

To the people we've saved and their loved ones, I know we're their heroes.

对于我们拯救过的人以及他们的亲人来说，我知道我们是他们的英雄。

词汇loved ones 亲人；hero 英雄 语法To... 表示“对……来说”；we've saved 是定语从句。

And that makes everything we do worth it.

而这让我们所做的一切都值得。

词汇make...worth it 使……值得 语法make + 宾语 + worth it；we do 是定语从句。

We want to do even more, but we can't do it all alone.

我们想做更多，但我们不能单靠自己做到。

词汇alone 独自 语法but 转折；all alone 强调。

Will you join us as a volunteer?

你们愿意作为志愿者加入我们吗？

词汇join 加入 语法Will you...? 发出邀请。

Together we can make a difference!

我们一起就能有所作为！

词汇make a difference 有所作为 语法can do；make a difference。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是蓝天救援队志愿者的演讲。他先讲自己的故事：2008 年徒步时朋友头部重伤，救援队找到他们，他差点哭出来。从那以后他也想帮助别人，2009 年加入蓝天救援队并一直做志愿者。他们去学校和社区教安全知识，在灾害中救援、搜寻失踪者，甚至 2023 年去土耳其帮忙。做志愿者让他学会救生技能、懂得团队合作和每个生命的价值。工作艰难、没有报酬，但目标始终是救更多人。最后他邀请大家加入，一起有所作为。

English Summary

This is a speech by a Blue Sky Rescue volunteer. He begins with his own story: in 2008 a friend was badly injured while hiking, and the rescue team found them; he nearly cried. Since then he has wanted to help others, joined Blue Sky Rescue in 2009 and has been a volunteer ever since. The team teaches safety at schools and communities, rescues people in emergencies, searches for missing people after disasters, and even helped in Türkiye in 2023. Volunteering has taught him life-saving skills, the power of teamwork and the value of every life. The work is hard and unpaid, but the goal is to save as many people as possible. He ends by inviting the audience to join.

Section B 3a · Beijing: The Olympic City

Section B 3a 主题：北京双奥之城 · 演讲稿

Good morning, everyone!

大家早上好！

词汇good morning 早上好 语法问候语。

Today, I'd like to talk about my favourite sports event.

今天我想谈谈我最喜欢的体育赛事。

词汇would like to do 想要做；sports event 体育赛事 语法I'd like to do sth.；talk about。

Actually, when I used the word "event", I meant two events: the 2008 Summer Olympics and the 2022 Winter Olympics.

其实，我所说的“赛事”指两项：2008 年夏季奥运会和 2022 年冬季奥运会。

词汇actually 其实；mean 指 语法when 引导时间状语从句；meant 是 mean 的过去式；冒号后解释。

They were held in Beijing—the first city ever to host both the Summer and the Winter Olympics.

它们都在北京举办——北京是首个既举办夏奥又举办冬奥的城市。

词汇be held 举办；host 主办 语法were held 被动语态；the first city ever to host... 中 to host 作后置定语。

The most recent was the 2022 Winter Olympics.

最近的是 2022 年冬奥会。

词汇recent 最近的 语法the most recent 最高级；was 系动词。

A total of nearly 2,900 athletes attended and competed in 109 events in seven Olympic winter sports.

近 2900 名运动员参加并角逐了 7 项冬季奥林匹克运动会的 109 个项目。

词汇a total of 总共；athlete 运动员；compete in 参加角逐 语法a total of + 数词；attended and competed 并列谓语。

There were many exciting moments.

留下无数精彩瞬间。

词汇exciting 令人激动的；moment 瞬间 语法There be 句型。

Nearly the whole world watched the fantastic figure skating and ski jumping competitions.

几乎全世界的人都观看了精彩绝伦的花样滑冰和跳台滑雪比赛。

词汇nearly 几乎；fantastic 精彩的；competition 比赛 语法nearly 修饰 the whole world。

I watched the freestyle skiing, and like everyone else, I fell in love with those brave and talented athletes.

我观看了自由式滑雪项目，像其他所有人一样，我被那些勇敢且才华横溢的运动员深深吸引。

词汇freestyle skiing 自由式滑雪；fall in love with 爱上；talented 有才华的 语法and 连接并列谓语；like everyone else 作插入语。

I also became a huge fan of the mascot, Bing Dwen Dwen.

同时，我也成了吉祥物“冰墩墩”的忠实粉丝。

词汇a huge fan of ……的忠实粉丝；mascot 吉祥物 语法become a fan of；同位语。

And we shall not forget the amazing achievements in the 2008 Summer Olympics.

我们也不会忘记 2008 年夏奥会的辉煌成就。

词汇shall 将；achievement 成就 语法shall 用于正式演讲。

I watched in wonder as Usain Bolt smiled his way across the finish line in the 100 metres, setting a new world record.

我惊奇地目睹了尤塞恩·博尔特微笑着冲过 100 米终点线那一幕，并由此创下了一项新的世界纪录。

词汇in wonder 惊奇地；finish line 终点线；set a record 创纪录 语法as 引导时间状语从句；setting... 是现在分词作结果状语。

Then I watched the drama of the shooting event.

随后我又观看了射击项目的精彩赛事。

词汇drama 戏剧性场面；shooting 射击 语法the drama of... 表示“……的戏剧性”。

Du Li won her second Olympic gold medal, after the first one in 2004.

杜丽继 2004 年首夺奥运金牌后，再次摘得一枚奥运金牌。

词汇gold medal 金牌 语法win a gold medal；after 引导时间状语。

Both were dramatic come-from-behind victories.

这两次夺冠都堪称戏剧性的逆转胜绩。

词汇dramatic 戏剧性的；come-from-behind 逆转的；victory 胜利 语法both 作主语；合成形容词。

Hosting two Olympics brought challenges but also opportunities to Beijing.

举办两届奥运会既给北京带来了挑战，也带来了机遇。

词汇host 主办；challenge 挑战；opportunity 机遇 语法动名词 Hosting 作主语；bring sth. to sb.。

Both Games showed China's success in its reform and opening-up on the world stage.

两届奥运会都彰显了中国改革开放进程中在世界舞台上的成就。

词汇reform and opening-up 改革开放；world stage 世界舞台 语法show sth.；on the world stage 作状语。

China's great achievements have gained worldwide attention.

中国取得的巨大成就已引起全球瞩目。

词汇achievement 成就；gain 获得；worldwide 全球的；attention 关注 语法现在完成时 have gained。

Events like the Olympic Games build bridges between countries and form friendships between people.

奥运会这样的赛事搭建起国家间的桥梁，缔结人民间的友谊。

词汇build bridges 搭建桥梁；form friendships 缔结友谊 语法like 举例；between...and...。

The purpose of the Olympics is to build relationships between people, to celebrate the Olympic spirit, and to bring people together for peace and harmony.

奥运会的宗旨是增进人与人的联系，弘扬奥运精神，凝聚人心，共创和平与和谐。

词汇purpose 目的；relationship 联系；celebrate 弘扬；spirit 精神；peace 和平；harmony 和谐 语法The purpose is to do...；三个不定式并列作表语。

This is shown in both the motto of the 2008 Olympics, "One World, One Dream", and the motto of the 2022 Olympics, "Together for a Shared Future".

这一点体现在 2008 年奥运会的口号“同一个世界，同一个梦想”和 2022 年奥运会的口号“一起向未来”中。

词汇motto 口号；shared future 共同未来 语法is shown 被动语态；both...and...。

These mottos touch my heart quite deeply, as I'm sure they touch yours.

这些口号深深触动了我的心，我相信它们也同样触动了你们的心。

词汇touch one's heart 触动某人的心；deeply 深深地 语法as 引导方式状语从句；省略句。

Thank you for listening!

感谢大家的聆听！

词汇listen 聆听 语法Thank you for doing sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇演讲稿介绍北京作为“双奥之城”。北京是首个既举办 2008 年夏奥会、又举办 2022 年冬奥会的城市。2022 年冬奥会有近 2900 名运动员参加 109 个项目；作者喜欢自由式滑雪和吉祥物冰墩墩。2008 年夏奥会也有难忘时刻：博尔特破世界纪录、杜丽逆转夺冠。举办两届奥运会给北京带来挑战和机遇，展示中国改革开放的成就。奥运会搭起国家间的桥梁，宗旨是增进联系、弘扬奥运精神、促进和平和谐，体现在“同一个世界，同一个梦想”和“一起向未来”的口号中。

English Summary

The speech introduces Beijing as the Olympic city. Beijing is the first city to host both the 2008 Summer Olympics and the 2022 Winter Olympics. The 2022 Winter Games had nearly 2,900 athletes in 109 events; the speaker loved freestyle skiing and the mascot Bing Dwen Dwen. The 2008 Games also had memorable moments: Usain Bolt set a world record, and Du Li won a come-from-behind gold. Hosting both Games brought challenges and opportunities and showed China's achievements in reform and opening-up. The Olympics build bridges between countries; their purpose is to build relationships, celebrate the Olympic spirit and bring people together for peace and harmony, as shown in the mottos "One World, One Dream" and "Together for a Shared Future".
