Section A · 3a · At the Doctor's

Section A · 3a 主题：看病 · 描述病情与给出建议（教材 P14）

Doctor: Take a seat, Helen.

请坐，海伦。

词汇take a seat 坐下；seat 座位 语法祈使句，动词原形开头，表示客气的邀请。

Doctor: How are you feeling?

你感觉怎么样？

词汇feel 感觉 语法现在进行时询问近况；How are you feeling? 是医生常用问句。

Helen: Not good ... I have a runny nose and a fever.

不太好……我流鼻涕，还发烧。

词汇runny nose 流鼻涕；fever 发烧 语法have + 症状表示“有……症状”；省略号表示停顿。

Doctor: Let me take your temperature ...

让我给你量一下体温……

词汇temperature 体温；take one's temperature 量体温 语法Let me do sth. 让我做某事。

Doctor: Oh, it's quite high.

哦，体温挺高的。

词汇quite 相当；high 高的 语法It's + 形容词，It 指体温；quite 修饰形容词。

Helen: Yes, it started yesterday.

是的，昨天开始的。

词汇start 开始；yesterday 昨天 语法一般过去时 started；it 指病情。

Helen: I felt so cold, and I didn't have much energy.

我觉得很冷，而且没有多少精力。

词汇feel 感觉（过去式 felt）；energy 精力 语法feel 的过去式 felt；didn't + 动词原形。

Helen: When I tried to get out of bed, I almost fell and hurt myself.

当我试着下床时，差点摔倒，还伤到了自己。

词汇try to do sth. 试着做某事；get out of bed 下床；hurt oneself 伤到自己 语法when 引导时间状语从句；hurt oneself 中反身代词用 myself。

Helen: My throat hurts too.

我的喉咙也疼。

词汇throat 喉咙；hurt 疼 语法主语 + hurt(s) 表示“某处疼”；too 用于肯定句末。

Doctor: Let's see.

让我看看。

词汇let's = let us 让我们 语法Let's + 动词原形，表示建议或提议。

Doctor: Yes, the back of your throat looks very red.

是的，你的喉咙后部看起来很红。

词汇the back of ……的后部；look 看起来 语法look + 形容词表示“看起来……”；the back of 表位置。

Doctor: We'll need to do a test, but I believe you have the flu.

我们需要做一个检查，但我相信你得了流感。

词汇need to do sth. 需要做某事；test 检查；believe 相信；flu 流感 语法We'll = We will；I believe 后接宾语从句。

Doctor: It's flu season.

现在是流感季节。

词汇season 季节 语法It's + 名词表示“现在是……时节”。

Helen: What should I do, Doctor?

我该怎么办，医生？

词汇should 应该 语法What should + 主语 + do? 用于征求建议。

Doctor: You should take some medicine and drink enough water.

你应该吃些药，多喝水。

词汇medicine 药；enough 足够的 语法should + 动词原形表示建议；and 连接两个并列动作。

Helen: OK, thank you.

好的，谢谢您。

词汇OK 好的 语法口语应答语，表示感谢。

Helen: Can I go to school tomorrow?

我明天可以去上学吗？

词汇go to school 去上学；tomorrow 明天 语法Can I...? 请求允许。

Doctor: No, you should rest at home.

不行，你应该在家休息。

词汇rest 休息；at home 在家 语法should + 动词原形表示建议；否定回答用 No。

Doctor: When you're outside, please wear a mask.

你在外面时，请戴上口罩。

词汇outside 在外面；wear 戴着；mask 口罩 语法when 引导时间状语从句；祈使句 Please + 动词原形。

Doctor: It stops us from passing the flu virus to others easily.

它能防止我们轻易把流感病毒传染给别人。

词汇stop ... from doing sth. 阻止……做某事；virus 病毒；pass 传递 语法stop sb. from doing sth.；pass sth. to sb. 表示“把某物传给某人”。

Helen: I will!

我会的！

词汇will 将会 语法I will 是简略回答，表示“我会这样做”。

Doctor: And remember, your health is in your hands.

还要记住，健康掌握在你自己手中。

词汇remember 记住；health 健康；in one's hands 在某人手中 语法祈使句 Remember 提醒对方；in one's hands 是固定搭配。

Doctor: If you take good care of yourself, you'll get better soon.

如果你好好照顾自己，很快就会好起来。

词汇take good care of 好好照顾；get better 好转 语法if 引导条件状语从句；主句用一般将来时（主将从现）。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话发生在诊室里，医生询问 Helen 的病情。Helen 说她流鼻涕、发烧，体温很高；她昨天就开始不舒服，觉得冷、没有精力，下床时差点摔倒，喉咙也疼。医生检查后认为她喉咙后部很红，需要做检查，很可能是得了流感。医生建议她吃些药、多喝水，在家休息，出门时戴口罩，以免把病毒传染给别人。最后医生提醒她：健康掌握在自己手中，只要好好照顾自己，很快就会好起来。

English Summary

This is a conversation between a doctor and Helen in the clinic. Helen says she has a runny nose and a fever, and her temperature is quite high. It started yesterday; she felt cold and had little energy, almost fell when she tried to get out of bed, and her throat hurts too. After the check, the doctor says the back of her throat looks very red and she needs a test — she probably has the flu because it is flu season. The doctor advises her to take some medicine, drink enough water, rest at home and wear a mask outside so that she doesn't pass the virus to others. Finally, the doctor reminds her that health is in her hands and that she will get better soon if she takes good care of herself.

Section B · 1b · A Cooking Accident

Section B · 1b 主题：居家安全 · 油锅起火与急救（教材 P16）

James lived in the city with his friend Allen.

詹姆斯和好朋友艾伦住在城里。

词汇live in the city 住在城里；friend 朋友 语法一般过去时 lived；with 表伴随。

It was their first time living away from their families, and they enjoyed learning how to do many things themselves.

这是他们第一次离开家人生活，他们很享受学着自己做很多事情。

词汇living away from 远离……生活；family 家人；enjoy 享受 语法It was one's first time doing sth.；enjoy doing sth.。

One Saturday, James decided to make fried chicken.

一个星期六，詹姆斯决定做炸鸡。

词汇Saturday 星期六；decide 决定；fried chicken 炸鸡 语法decide to do sth.；fried chicken 作宾语。

First, he poured oil into a pan.

首先，他把油倒进锅里。

词汇first 首先；pour 倒；oil 油；pan 平底锅 语法顺序副词 First；pour ... into ... 把……倒进……。

Then he turned on the stove and left the oil to heat while he prepared the chicken.

然后他打开炉灶，让油加热，同时准备鸡肉。

词汇turn on 打开；stove 炉灶；heat 加热；prepare 准备 语法and 连接并列谓语；while 引导时间状语从句；leave sth. to do sth.。

Just then, he heard the front door open, followed by the sound of Allen singing.

就在这时，他听到前门开了，接着传来艾伦唱歌的声音。

词汇just then 就在那时；front door 前门；sound 声音 语法hear sb./sth. do sth.；followed by 过去分词短语作状语。

"Guess what," Allen called out happily.

“猜猜看，”艾伦高兴地喊道。

词汇guess 猜；call out 大声喊 语法直接引语用引号；call out 表示“大声喊”。

"We won!"

“我们赢了！”

词汇win 赢（过去式 won） 语法直接引语；won 是 win 的过去式。

"No way!" James followed him into the living room.

“不可能吧！”詹姆斯跟着他走进客厅。

词汇no way 不可能；follow 跟随；living room 客厅 语法No way! 表示惊讶；follow sb. into... 表示“跟着某人进入……”。

He wanted to hear all about the football game!

他想听听足球比赛的全部情况！

词汇hear about 听说；football game 足球比赛 语法want to do sth.；hear about 表示“了解……的情况”。

Suddenly, Allen stopped talking and looked up.

突然，艾伦停止说话，抬起头来。

词汇suddenly 突然；stop doing sth. 停止做某事；look up 抬头看 语法stop doing sth.；and 连接并列谓语。

"Hey, do you smell something burning?"

“嘿，你闻到什么东西烧焦了吗？”

词汇smell 闻到；burning 燃烧的 语法smell sth. doing 表示“闻到某物正在……”。

"Oh no!"

“哦，不！”

词汇oh no 哦，不 语法感叹语，表示惊慌。

They both rushed back into the kitchen.

他们俩冲回厨房。

词汇rush 冲；kitchen 厨房 语法一般过去时 rushed；both 作同位语。

The pan was on fire!

锅着火了！

词汇be on fire 着火 语法be on fire 表示“着火”。

Large flames were jumping into the air.

巨大的火焰蹿向空中。

词汇large 大的；flame 火焰；jump 蹿 语法过去进行时 were jumping，表示动作正在发生。

In a panic, James turned off the stove at once.

惊慌之中，詹姆斯立刻关掉了炉灶。

词汇in a panic 惊慌失措；turn off 关掉；at once 立刻 语法in a panic 作状语；at once 表示“立刻”。

But he was so close to the pan that a flame jumped onto his shirt.

但他离锅太近了，一团火焰蹿到了他的衬衫上。

词汇close to 靠近；jump onto 跳到……上 语法so ... that ... 引导结果状语从句。

James cried out and threw himself to the floor.

詹姆斯大喊一声，扑倒在地。

词汇cry out 大喊；throw oneself to 扑向 语法and 连接并列谓语；throw oneself to the floor 表示“扑倒在地”。

He put out the fire by rolling on the ground, but the pan was still burning!

他在地上打滚扑灭了自己身上的火，但锅还在燃烧！

词汇put out 扑灭；roll 打滚；ground 地面 语法by doing sth. 表示方式；but 表转折。

"Get water, quick!" he shouted.

“快去拿水！”他喊道。

词汇get 去拿；quick 快 语法祈使句；直接引语后用 he shouted。

Allen was about to do so when he stopped short.

艾伦正要这么做，却突然停住了。

词汇be about to do sth. 正要做某事；stop short 突然停住 语法be about to do sth. 表示“即将做某事”；when 表示“这时突然”。

Thanks to the first aid and safety classes he took at school, he knew that water could make oil fires much worse!

多亏了他在学校上的急救和安全课，他知道水会让油火变得更糟！

词汇thanks to 多亏；first aid 急救；safety 安全；worse 更糟 语法thanks to 表示“多亏”；he took at school 是定语从句；make + 宾语 + 形容词。

Instead, he ran to the living room to get the fire extinguisher.

相反，他跑到客厅去拿灭火器。

词汇instead 相反；extinguisher 灭火器 语法instead 表示“反而、相反”；不定式 to get 表目的。

In seconds, the fire in the burning pan was put out.

几秒钟内，燃烧的锅里的火就被扑灭了。

词汇in seconds 几秒钟内；burning 燃烧的 语法被动语态 was put out；in seconds 表示“很快”。

"Are you OK?" Allen asked his friend.

“你还好吗？”艾伦问他的朋友。

词汇OK 好的 语法直接引语；一般疑问句 Are you OK?

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇故事讲述了一次厨房事故。James 和好朋友 Allen 住在一起。一个星期六，James 决定做炸鸡：他往锅里倒油，打开炉灶让油加热。这时 Allen 唱着歌回家，James 跟着他走进客厅想听足球比赛的消息。突然 Allen 闻到烧焦的味道，两人冲回厨房，发现油锅着火了，火焰蹿得很高。James 惊慌中关掉炉灶，但火焰还是烧到了他的衬衫；他在地上打滚扑灭了自己身上的火，可锅还在燃烧。Allen 想起学校急救课上学到的知识：水会让油火更糟，于是他跑去拿灭火器，很快把火扑灭了。

English Summary

This story is about a cooking accident. James lived with his friend Allen in the city. One Saturday, James decided to make fried chicken. He poured oil into a pan, turned on the stove and left the oil to heat. Just then, Allen came home singing, and James followed him into the living room to hear about the football game. Suddenly Allen smelled something burning, and they both rushed back into the kitchen — the pan was on fire! In a panic, James turned off the stove, but a flame jumped onto his shirt. He cried out and put out the fire on himself by rolling on the ground, but the pan was still burning. Allen remembered his first aid classes: water would make oil fires worse, so he ran to get the fire extinguisher and put the fire out in seconds.

Next, there is a sudden blast of air coming out of our lungs and then our nose.

There is + 名词；现在分词 coming 作后置定语。

As the air goes out our nose, it carries germs, dust, and tiny drops of water with it.

as 引导时间状语从句；carry ... with it 表示“带着……一起”。

If people have an allergy, certain plants or animals might also make them sneeze.

if 引导条件状语从句；make sb. do sth.。

If they are allergic to certain plants, they might sneeze a lot in the seasons when the plants bloom, but not in others.

when 引导定语从句修饰 seasons；be allergic to；but not 表对比。

While sneezing is good for the person doing it, it can be very bad for the people nearby.

while 表示对比“虽然”；be good / bad for 表示“对……有益/有害”。

Millions of germs from one sneeze can travel up to about 8 metres at a speed of 160 kilometres per hour!

millions of 表示“数百万的”；up to 高达；at a speed of 表示“以……的速度”。

If you sneeze, always cover your nose and mouth.

if 引导条件状语从句；祈使句表达建议。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

As the air goes out our nose, it carries germs, dust, and tiny drops of water with it.

当空气从鼻子出去时，它会带走细菌、灰尘和微小的水滴。

as 引导时间状语从句，表示“当……时”；carry ... with it 表示“带着……一起”。

While sneezing is good for the person doing it, it can be very bad for the people nearby.

虽然打喷嚏对打喷嚏的人有好处，却可能对附近的人非常不利。

while 在此表示对比，意为“虽然”；the person doing it 中 doing it 是现在分词作后置定语；be good / bad for 表示“对……有益/有害”。

Millions of germs from one sneeze can travel up to about 8 metres at a speed of 160 kilometres per hour!

一次喷嚏喷出的数百万细菌能以每小时 160 公里的速度传播到约 8 米远！

millions of 表示“数百万的”；up to 表示“高达”；at a speed of 表示“以……的速度”。
