Section A 3a · At the Doctor's

Section A 3a 主题：生病就医 · 对话

Doctor: Take a seat, Helen. How are you feeling?

请坐，海伦。你感觉怎么样？

词汇take a seat 请坐；feel 感觉 语法祈使句 Take a seat；How are you feeling? 现在进行时询问感受。

Helen: Not good... I have a runny nose and a fever.

不太好……我流鼻涕，还发烧。

词汇runny nose 流鼻涕；fever 发烧 语法have a runny nose / a fever 是固定搭配。

Doctor: Let me take your temperature... Oh, it's quite high.

让我给你量量体温……哦，体温很高。

词汇take one's temperature 量体温；quite 相当 语法Let me do sth. 让我做某事；it 指体温。

Helen: Yes, it started yesterday.

是的，昨天就开始了。

词汇start 开始；yesterday 昨天 语法一般过去时 started；it 指症状。

Helen: I felt so cold, and I didn't have much energy.

我感觉很冷，而且没什么力气。

词汇feel cold 感到冷；energy 力气；精力 语法一般过去时 felt；didn't have 否定。

Helen: When I tried to get out of bed, I almost fell and hurt myself.

当我试图从床上起来时，差点摔倒伤到自己。

词汇get out of bed 起床；almost 几乎；hurt oneself 伤到自己 语法when 引导时间状语从句；try to do sth.；hurt oneself 反身代词。

Helen: My throat hurts too.

我的喉咙也疼。

词汇throat 喉咙；hurt 疼 语法一般现在时 hurts；主语 My throat 是单数。

Doctor: Let's see. Yes, the back of your throat looks very red.

让我看看。是的，你的喉咙后部看起来很红。

词汇the back of ……的后部；look red 看起来红 语法look + adj. 表示“看起来……”；the back of your throat。

Doctor: We'll need to do a test, but I believe you have the flu.

我们需要做个检查，但我认为你得了流感。

词汇do a test 做检查；believe 认为；flu 流感 语法will need to do；I believe 后接宾语从句。

Doctor: It's flu season.

现在是流感季节。

词汇season 季节 语法It's + 名词 表示当前情况。

Helen: What should I do, Doctor?

医生，我该怎么办？

词汇should 应该 语法should + 动词原形表示建议。

Doctor: You should take some medicine and drink enough water.

你应该吃点药，多喝水。

词汇take medicine 吃药；enough 足够的 语法should + 动词原形；and 连接并列谓语。

Helen: OK, thank you. Can I go to school tomorrow?

好的，谢谢。我明天能去上学吗？

词汇go to school 上学；tomorrow 明天 语法Can I...? 征求意见。

Doctor: No, you should rest at home.

不行，你应该在家休息。

词汇rest at home 在家休息 语法should + 动词原形；at home 作地点状语。

Doctor: When you're outside, please wear a mask.

当你在外面的时候，请戴上口罩。

词汇outside 在外面；wear a mask 戴口罩 语法when 引导时间状语从句；祈使句 please wear。

Doctor: It stops us from passing the flu virus to others easily.

它能防止我们轻易地把流感病毒传给别人。

词汇stop sb. from doing 阻止某人做；virus 病毒；easily 容易地 语法stop sb. from doing sth.；pass sth. to sb.。

Helen: I will!

我会的！

词汇will 将会 语法简略回答，省略后面的动作。

Doctor: And remember, your health is in your hands.

还有记住，健康掌握在你自己手中。

词汇remember 记住；health 健康 语法祈使句；in your hands 表示“由你掌控”。

Doctor: If you take good care of yourself, you'll get better soon.

如果你好好照顾自己，你很快就会好起来的。

词汇take care of 照顾；get better 好转；soon 很快 语法if 引导条件状语从句，主将从现。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话是海伦看医生的过程。海伦流鼻涕、发烧、怕冷、没力气，起床时差点摔倒，喉咙也疼。医生给她量了体温，发现很高，检查后判断她得了流感，因为现在是流感季节。医生建议她吃药、多喝水、在家休息，不要上学；出门时要戴口罩，防止把病毒传给别人。最后医生提醒她：健康掌握在自己手中，好好照顾自己就会很快好起来。

English Summary

The conversation is about Helen seeing a doctor. She has a runny nose and a fever, feels cold and has little energy; she almost fell when getting out of bed, and her throat hurts. The doctor takes her temperature, finds it high, and after a check believes she has the flu because it is flu season. The doctor advises her to take medicine, drink enough water, rest at home instead of going to school, and wear a mask outside so as not to pass the virus to others. The doctor reminds her that health is in her own hands and she will get better soon if she takes good care of herself.

I have a runny nose and a fever.

have a runny nose / have a fever 是固定搭配。

Let me take your temperature...

Let me do sth. 表示“让我做某事”。

When I tried to get out of bed, I almost fell and hurt myself.

when 引导时间状语从句；try to do sth.；hurt oneself。

the back of your throat looks very red

look + adj. 表示“看起来……”；the back of...。

We'll need to do a test, but I believe you have the flu.

What should I do, Doctor?

should + 动词原形表示应当。

You should take some medicine and drink enough water.

should + 动词原形；and 连接并列谓语。

When you're outside, please wear a mask.

Section B 1b · A Cooking Accident

Section B 1b 主题：厨房用火安全 · 记叙文

James lived in the city with his friend Allen.

詹姆斯和他的朋友艾伦一起住在城市里。

词汇live with 和……一起住 语法一般过去时 lived；with his friend Allen 作伴随状语。

It was their first time living away from their families, and they enjoyed learning how to do many things themselves.

这是他们第一次离开家人生活，他们很喜欢学习自己动手做很多事。

词汇away from 离开；enjoy doing 喜欢做；themselves 他们自己 语法It was one's first time doing sth.；enjoy + 动名词；how to do sth. 作宾语。

One Saturday, James decided to make fried chicken.

一个星期六，詹姆斯决定做炸鸡。

词汇decide to do 决定做；fried chicken 炸鸡 语法decide to do sth.；一般过去时。

First, he poured oil into a pan.

首先，他往平底锅里倒了油。

词汇pour 倒；oil 油；pan 平底锅 语法pour sth. into sth.；First 表示顺序。

Then he turned on the stove and left the oil to heat while he prepared the chicken.

然后他打开炉灶，在准备鸡肉的时候让油加热。

词汇turn on 打开；stove 炉灶；heat 加热；prepare 准备 语法turn on；leave sth. to do sth.；while 引导时间状语从句。

Just then, he heard the front door open, followed by the sound of Allen singing.

就在这时，他听到前门开了，接着是艾伦唱歌的声音。

词汇just then 就在那时；hear sb. do 听到某人做；followed by 接着是 语法hear sth. do sth.；followed by 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

"Guess what," Allen called out happily. "We won!"

“你猜怎么着，”艾伦高兴地喊道，“我们赢了！”

词汇guess what 你猜怎么着；call out 喊；win 赢 语法直接引语；一般过去时 won。

"No way!" James followed him into the living room. He wanted to hear all about the football game!

“不会吧！”詹姆斯跟着他走进客厅。他想听听足球比赛的全部情况！

词汇no way 不会吧；living room 客厅；hear about 听说 语法follow sb. into...；want to do sth.。

Suddenly, Allen stopped talking and looked up.

突然，艾伦停止说话，抬起头。

词汇suddenly 突然；stop doing 停止做；look up 抬起头 语法stop doing sth.；look up。

"Hey, do you smell something burning?" "Oh no!"

“嘿，你闻到有东西烧焦的味道了吗？”“哦，不！”

词汇smell 闻；burning 烧焦的 语法smell sth. doing sth. 闻到某物正在……；现在分词作宾补。

They both rushed back into the kitchen. The pan was on fire!

他们俩都冲回厨房。平底锅着火了！

词汇rush 冲；kitchen 厨房；be on fire 着火 语法rush into；be on fire 是固定短语。

Large flames were jumping into the air.

大团火焰蹿到空中。

词汇flame 火焰；jump 跳跃 语法过去进行时 were jumping 表示当时情景。

In a panic, James turned off the stove at once.

惊慌之下，詹姆斯立刻关掉了炉灶。

词汇in a panic 惊慌地；turn off 关掉；at once 立刻 语法in a panic 作状语；turn off；at once。

But he was so close to the pan that a flame jumped onto his shirt.

但他离平底锅太近了，一团火焰蹿到了他的衬衫上。

词汇close to 靠近；shirt 衬衫 语法so...that... 引导结果状语从句；jump onto。

James cried out and threw himself to the floor.

詹姆斯大叫一声，扑倒在地上。

词汇cry out 大叫；throw oneself to 扑倒在 语法throw oneself to the floor；and 连接并列谓语。

He put out the fire by rolling on the ground, but the pan was still burning!

他在地上打滚扑灭了身上的火，但平底锅里的火还在烧！

词汇put out 扑灭；roll 打滚；still 仍然 语法put out；by + 动名词表示方式；but 转折。

"Get water, quick!" he shouted.

“快拿水来！”他喊道。

词汇quick 快；shout 喊 语法祈使句；直接引语。

Allen was about to do so when he stopped short.

艾伦正要去做，却突然停了下来。

词汇be about to do 正要做；stop short 突然停下 语法be about to do sth. when... 表示“正要做某事时突然……”。

Thanks to the first aid and safety classes he took at school, he knew that water could make oil fires much worse!

多亏了他在学校上过的急救和安全课程，他知道水会让油火变得更严重！

词汇thanks to 多亏；first aid 急救；safety 安全；make...worse 使……更糟 语法thanks to；he took at school 是省略关系词的定语从句；that 引导宾语从句。

Instead, he ran to the living room to get the fire extinguisher.

相反，他跑到客厅去拿灭火器。

词汇instead 相反；fire extinguisher 灭火器 语法instead 表示转折；to get 作目的状语。

In seconds, the fire in the burning pan was put out.

几秒钟后，燃烧的平底锅里的火就被扑灭了。

词汇in seconds 几秒内；put out 扑灭 语法被动语态 was put out；in seconds 作时间状语。

"Are you OK?" Allen asked his friend. "I think so," James said.

“你还好吗？”艾伦问他的朋友。“我想还好，”詹姆斯说。

词汇I think so 我想是这样 语法Are you OK? 询问状况；I think so 是省略回答。

It was lucky that his shirt wasn't too thin!

幸运的是，他的衬衫不是很薄！

词汇lucky 幸运的；thin 薄的 语法It was lucky that... 引导主语从句。

His arm was red, but it didn't hurt very much.

他的胳膊红了，但不是很疼。

词汇arm 胳膊；hurt 疼 语法but 转折；didn't hurt 否定。

"Maybe we should eat out tonight," James said as he ran cool water over his arm.

“也许我们今晚应该出去吃，”詹姆斯一边往胳膊上冲凉水一边说。

词汇maybe 也许；eat out 出去吃；cool water 凉水 语法should + 动词原形；as 引导时间状语从句。

"Sure, we could do that. What do you want?"

“当然可以，我们可以那样做。你想吃什么？”

词汇sure 当然；want 想要 语法could do；What do you want? 特殊疑问句。

"Anything but fried chicken," he said sadly.

“除了炸鸡什么都行，”他难过地说。

词汇anything but 除了……什么都行；sadly 难过地 语法anything but + 名词表示“绝不是/除了”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章讲一次厨房火灾。詹姆斯和艾伦第一次离开家独立生活，一个周六詹姆斯做炸鸡：倒油、开炉灶加热，然后去准备鸡肉。艾伦回来喊“我们赢了”，詹姆斯跟到客厅听足球赛，两人闻到烧焦味，冲回厨房发现油锅起火。詹姆斯关掉炉灶，但火焰蹿到他衬衫上，他在地上打滚灭火；艾伦想起急救课上学到的知识，知道水会让油火更严重，于是跑去拿灭火器把火扑灭。詹姆斯胳膊发红但伤得不重，最后两人决定出去吃。

English Summary

The passage tells a kitchen fire. James and Allen were living on their own for the first time. One Saturday James decided to make fried chicken: he poured oil into a pan, turned on the stove and left the oil to heat while he prepared the chicken. Allen came home shouting that they had won, and James went into the living room to hear about the football game. Then they smelled something burning and rushed back—the pan was on fire. James turned off the stove, but a flame jumped onto his shirt; he put it out by rolling on the floor. Allen remembered his first-aid classes and knew water makes oil fires worse, so he fetched the fire extinguisher and put out the fire. James's arm was red but not badly hurt, and they decided to eat out.

Section B 3a · A Day to Remember

Section B 3a 主题：食物过敏 · 短篇故事

One Saturday, Julie invited Mark and Lucy to her house for lunch.

一个星期六，朱莉邀请马克和露西去她家吃午饭。

词汇invite sb. to 邀请某人去；lunch 午饭 语法invite sb. to sth.；一般过去时。

Her parents weren't home, so they had it all to themselves.

她的父母不在家，所以就他们几个单独相处。

词汇have sth. to oneself 独占某物 语法have sth. to oneself；so 连接结果。

They ordered pizza, salad, and juice.

他们点了披萨、沙拉和果汁。

词汇order 点（餐）；pizza 披萨；salad 沙拉；juice 果汁 语法order sth.；三者并列作宾语。

But in the middle of the meal, Mark started to feel strange.

但吃到一半时，马克开始感觉不对劲。

词汇in the middle of 在……中间；meal 一餐；strange 奇怪的 语法in the middle of 作时间状语；start to do sth.。

His face felt warm, and his throat felt tight.

他觉得脸发烫，喉咙发紧。

词汇face 脸；warm 温暖的；throat 喉咙；tight 紧的 语法feel + adj. 表示感觉；and 连接并列分句。

"Were there any peanuts in our food?" he asked nervously.

“我们的食物里有花生吗？”他紧张地问道。

词汇peanut 花生；food 食物；nervously 紧张地 语法there be 句型的疑问句；nervously 修饰 asked。

"I think I tasted peanuts in the salad," said Julie. "What's the matter?"

“我觉得我在沙拉里尝到了花生，”朱莉说。“怎么了？”

词汇taste 尝到；matter 问题 语法I think 后接宾语从句；What's the matter? 是固定问法。

"Oh no, I'm allergic to peanuts!" Mark cried.

“哦，不，我对花生过敏！”马克叫道。

词汇be allergic to 对……过敏 语法be allergic to sth. 是固定搭配。

His friends jumped into action and took him to the hospital at once.

他的朋友们立即行动起来，马上把他送到了医院。

词汇jump into action 立即行动；take sb. to 带某人去；at once 立刻 语法and 连接并列谓语；take sb. to the hospital。

At the hospital, Mark's friends told Dr Cooper what happened, and she gave Mark some medicine right away.

在医院里，马克的朋友们把发生的事告诉了库珀医生，她立刻给马克用了些药。

词汇hospital 医院；happen 发生；give sb. sth. 给某人某物；right away 立刻 语法what happened 是宾语从句；give sb. sth. 双宾语。

When Mark felt better, the doctor asked him some questions and gave him some advice.

当马克感觉好些了，医生问了他一些问题并给了他一些建议。

词汇feel better 感觉好些；advice 建议 语法when 引导时间状语从句；ask sb. sth.；give sb. sth.。

"I'll take better care of myself from now on," Mark promised.

“从现在起，我会更好地照顾自己，”马克承诺道。

词汇take care of 照顾；from now on 从现在起；promise 承诺 语法will do；from now on 作时间状语；直接引语。

It was a day he would never forget.

这是他永远不会忘记的一天。

词汇forget 忘记 语法he would never forget 是省略关系词的定语从句，修饰 a day。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一个关于食物过敏的短篇故事。一个星期六，朱莉邀请马克和露西到她家吃午饭，父母不在家，他们点了披萨、沙拉和果汁。吃到一半时马克感觉不对：脸发烫、喉咙发紧，问食物里有没有花生；朱莉说沙拉里似乎有，马克说自己对花生过敏。朋友们立即行动，把他送到医院；医生马上给药，并问了一些问题、给出建议。马克承诺以后会更好地照顾自己，这一天他永远不会忘记。

English Summary

This is a short story about a food allergy. One Saturday Julie invited Mark and Lucy to her house for lunch. Her parents were not home, and they ordered pizza, salad and juice. In the middle of the meal Mark began to feel strange: his face felt warm and his throat felt tight. He asked whether there were peanuts in the food; Julie thought she had tasted peanuts in the salad, and Mark said he was allergic to peanuts. His friends jumped into action and took him to the hospital at once. Dr Cooper gave him medicine right away, asked questions and gave advice. Mark promised to take better care of himself, and it was a day he would never forget.
