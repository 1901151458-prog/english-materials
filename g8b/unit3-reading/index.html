Section A · What's Wrong, Judy?

Section A · Dialogue 主题：成长烦恼 · 沟通与建议

Lily: What's wrong, Judy?

朱迪，怎么了？

词汇wrong 有问题的；judy 朱迪（人名） 语法What's wrong? 是询问“怎么了、出什么事了”的常用问句。

Judy: Well, my parents aren't happy with my grades.

唉，我父母对我的成绩不满意。

词汇be happy with 对……满意；grade 成绩 语法be happy with 表示“对……满意”；aren't = are not。

Lily: Really?

真的吗？

词汇really 真的吗 语法单独成句的 Really? 用升调表示惊讶或追问。

Lily: Weren't they pretty good this term?

你这学期成绩不是挺好吗？

词汇pretty 相当；term 学期 语法否定疑问句 Weren't they ...? 表示惊讶；this term 表示“这个学期”。

Judy: Yeah, but my parents have very high standards.

是啊，但我父母的标准很高。

词汇standard 标准；水平 语法but 表示转折；have high standards 表示“要求高、标准高”。

Judy: They often compare me with my cousin, Kate.

他们经常把我和我的表姐凯特比较。

词汇compare ... with ... 把……和……比较；cousin 表姐；堂姐妹 语法compare A with B 表示“把 A 和 B 比较”；Kate 是 cousin 的同位语。

Judy: She gets better grades, plays the piano well, often wins awards ...

她成绩更好，钢琴弹得好，还经常获奖……

词汇piano 钢琴；award 奖项；win awards 获奖 语法三个并列谓语都用第三人称单数；省略号表示列举未完。

Lily: I'm sorry to hear that.

听到这个我很难过。

词汇be sorry to hear that 听到……很难过 语法I'm sorry to hear that. 是表示同情、安慰的常用语。

Lily: But I'm sure your parents just want you to do well, although they may not show it.

但我相信你父母只是希望你表现好，虽然他们可能没有表现出来。

词汇be sure 确信；do well 表现好；although 虽然；show 表现 语法be sure + 从句表示“确信”；although 引导让步状语从句。

Lily: Why don't you talk to them?

你为什么不和他们谈谈呢？

词汇why don't you 为什么不……；talk to 和……谈谈 语法Why don't you ...? 用于提出建议。

Lily: Until you talk to them, they might not know how you feel.

除非你和他们谈谈，否则他们可能不知道你的感受。

词汇until 直到；might 可能；feel 感觉 语法until 引导时间状语从句；might 表示可能性；how you feel 是宾语从句。

Judy: I guess ...

我想……

词汇guess 猜想；认为 语法省略号表示犹豫、停顿，口语中常见。

Judy: I know my parents love me, but sometimes I think they love Kate more.

我知道父母爱我，但有时我觉得他们更爱凯特。

词汇sometimes 有时；more 更 语法but 表示转折；more 是 much 的比较级，修饰 love。

Judy: I'm afraid I'll cry if we talk.

我怕我们一谈我就会哭。

词汇be afraid 害怕；担心；cry 哭 语法be afraid + 从句表示“担心、害怕”；if 引导条件状语从句。

Lily: How about writing them a letter so that you can get your message across clearly?

给他们写封信怎么样？这样你就能把自己的意思表达清楚了。

词汇how about ……怎么样；get ... across 把……表达清楚；message 信息 语法How about doing sth.? 表示建议；so that 引导目的状语从句。

Judy: Oh, that's a good idea.

哦，那是个好主意。

词汇idea 主意 语法That's + 名词，表示评价；Oh 表示语气。

Lily: Writing things down can also make you feel better.

把事情写下来也能让你感觉好一些。

词汇write down 写下；make sb. feel + 形容词 使某人感到…… 语法动名词短语 Writing things down 作主语；make sb. feel better 表示“让某人感觉好些”。

Judy: Thanks!

谢谢！

词汇thanks 谢谢 语法口语致谢用语。

Judy: Just talking to you makes me feel better already!

只是和你聊一聊就已经让我感觉好多了！

词汇just 只是；make sb. feel better 让某人感觉好些；already 已经 语法动名词短语 Just talking to you 作主语，谓语用第三人称单数 makes。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是 Lily 和 Judy 的一段对话。Judy 心情不好，因为父母对她的成绩不满意，还常常拿她和表姐 Kate 比较：Kate 成绩更好、钢琴弹得好、经常获奖。Lily 安慰她说，父母只是希望她表现好，虽然他们可能没有表现出来；并建议她和父母谈谈——除非她说出来，父母可能不知道她的感受。Judy 担心一谈就会哭，Lily 又建议她给父母写封信，把意思表达清楚。Judy 觉得这是个好主意，并感谢 Lily，说和她聊一聊就感觉好多了。

English Summary

This is a conversation between Lily and Judy. Judy is unhappy because her parents aren't happy with her grades and often compare her with her cousin Kate, who gets better grades, plays the piano well and often wins awards. Lily says she is sorry to hear that, but she is sure Judy's parents just want her to do well, although they may not show it. She suggests that Judy talk to them, because they might not know how she feels until she does. Judy is afraid she will cry if they talk, so Lily suggests writing them a letter to get her message across clearly. Judy thinks it is a good idea and thanks Lily, saying that just talking to her makes her feel better already.

语法点 Grammar

What's wrong, Judy?

What's wrong? 是询问“怎么了、出什么事了”的常用问句。

Well, my parents aren't happy with my grades.

be happy with 表示“对……满意”；aren't = are not。

Weren't they pretty good this term?

否定疑问句 Weren't they ...? 表示惊讶；this term 表示“这个学期”。

They often compare me with my cousin, Kate.

compare A with B 表示“把 A 和 B 比较”；often 是频度副词。

She gets better grades, plays the piano well, often wins awards ...

三个并列谓语都用第三人称单数；省略号表示列举未完。

But I'm sure your parents just want you to do well, although they may not show it.

be sure + 从句表示“确信”；although 引导让步状语从句。

Why don't you talk to them?

Why don't you ...? 用于提出建议；talk to sb. 表示“和某人谈谈”。

Until you talk to them, they might not know how you feel.

until 引导时间状语从句；might 表示可能性；how you feel 是宾语从句。

I know my parents love me, but sometimes I think they love Kate more.

but 表示转折；more 是 much 的比较级，修饰 love。

I'm afraid I'll cry if we talk.

be afraid + 从句表示“担心、害怕”；if 引导条件状语从句。

How about writing them a letter so that you can get your message across clearly?

How about doing sth.? 表示建议；so that 引导目的状语从句。

Just talking to you makes me feel better already!

动名词短语 Just talking to you 作主语；make sb. feel + 形容词。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

They often compare me with my cousin, Kate.

他们经常把我和我的表姐凯特比较。

compare A with B 表示“把 A 和 B 进行比较”；Kate 是 cousin 的同位语。

Until you talk to them, they might not know how you feel.

除非你和他们谈谈，否则他们可能不知道你的感受。

until 引导时间状语从句，意为“直到……”；might 表示可能性；how you feel 是宾语从句。

How about writing them a letter so that you can get your message across clearly?

给他们写封信怎么样？这样你就能把自己的意思表达清楚了。

How about doing sth.? 是提建议的句型；so that 引导目的状语从句；get sth. across 表示“把……表达清楚”。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 先看 P24 的对话：Lily 和 Judy 在谈心，读前想一想：如果你遇到烦恼，会找谁倾诉？
  • 读前扫读对话，找出 Judy 遇到了什么问题、Lily 给了什么建议。

读中

  • 边读边画出 Judy 的烦恼和 Lily 的建议，理清对话脉络。
  • 注意表示建议的句型（Why don't you ...? / How about ...?）。

读后

  • 两人一组分角色朗读对话，注意语气和语调。
  • 结合对话内容，给 Judy 写一张便条，转述 Lily 的建议。

Section B 1b · You Can't Win Them All

Section B 1b 主题：成长故事 · 面对失败与保持积极

You Can't Win Them All

你不可能每次都赢

词汇win 赢（过去式 won）；them 它们（指比赛） 语法故事标题；you can't win them all 是英语习语，意为“不可能样样都赢”。

When the bell rang, Matt entered the classroom with a heavy heart.

铃响时，马特心情沉重地走进教室。

词汇bell 铃；ring 响（过去式 rang）；enter 进入；with a heavy heart 心情沉重地 语法when 引导时间状语从句；with a heavy heart 是习语，表示“心情沉重”。

"Hey, Matt," his best friend Tom greeted him.

“嘿，马特，”他最好的朋友汤姆跟他打招呼。

词汇greet 问候；迎接 语法best friend 最好的朋友；Tom greeted him 中 greet sb. 表示“向某人问候”。

He and Tom were in the school's basketball team.

他和汤姆都在校篮球队。

词汇basketball team 篮球队 语法He and Tom 作并列主语，be 动词用 were；school's 是名词所有格。

"Why the long face? Are you still feeling blue about Saturday night's game?" Tom asked.

“为什么愁眉苦脸的？你还在为周六晚上的比赛难过吗？”汤姆问。

词汇long face 拉长的脸；不高兴；feel blue 感到忧郁 语法Why the long face? 是口语问句，意为“为什么闷闷不乐？”；feel blue about ... 表示“因……而忧郁”。

"Of course I am," Matt said quietly.

“我当然难过，”马特轻声说。

词汇of course 当然；quietly 轻声地 语法Of course I am. 是省略回答，完整形式为 Of course I am feeling blue.。

He didn't dare to look at Tom as he spoke.

他说话时不敢看汤姆。

词汇dare 敢；look at 看；speak 说话（过去式 spoke） 语法dare to do sth. 表示“敢于做某事”；as 引导时间状语从句。

"I'm really sorry about letting everybody down. The other team got those points because of me."

“我真的很抱歉让大家失望。对方球队得分是因为我。”

词汇let sb. down 让某人失望；because of 因为 语法be sorry about doing sth. 表示“因做某事而抱歉”；let sb. down 是习语。

"Don't be too hard on yourself!" Tom said.

“别对自己太苛刻！”汤姆说。

词汇be hard on sb. 对某人苛刻 语法祈使句的否定形式：Don't + 动词原形；be hard on sb. 表示“对某人太严厉、太苛刻”。

"Although you pushed their player, it was just an accident. Everyone knows that!"

“虽然你推了他们的球员，但那只是个意外。大家都知道！”

词汇push 推；player 球员；accident 意外；事故 语法although 引导让步状语从句；it 指“推人”这件事。

Tom was a good friend.

汤姆是个好朋友。

词汇good friend 好朋友 语法主系表结构；承上启下的过渡句。

But Matt knew the referee didn't share Tom's opinion.

但马特知道裁判并不认同汤姆的看法。

词汇referee 裁判；share 分享；赞同；opinion 看法 语法share one's opinion 表示“认同某人的看法”；knew 是 know 的过去式。

When the referee saw Matt push another player, he gave the other team two free throws.

裁判看到马特推了另一名球员，就判给对方两次罚球。

词汇see sb. do sth. 看见某人做某事；free throw 罚球 语法see sb. do sth. 表示“看见某人做某事”；give sb. sth. 是双宾语结构。

Matt was as shocked as anyone when the accident happened, but it was too late. He couldn't take it back.

意外发生时，马特和大家一样震惊，但已经太迟了。他无法收回这一切。

词汇shocked 震惊的；as ... as 和……一样；take back 收回 语法as ... as 表示“和……一样”；too late 表示“太迟了”；couldn't 是 can't 的过去式。

"I guess we have to respect his decision," Matt said after a while.

“我想我们得尊重他的决定，”马特过了一会儿说。

词汇guess 猜想；respect 尊重；decision 决定；after a while 过了一会儿 语法have to do sth. 表示“不得不做某事”；after a while 表示“过了一会儿”。

"But we had a good chance to win until I made that mistake. Why wasn't I more careful? I could just kick myself! I really could."

“但我们本来很有机会赢，直到我犯了那个错误。为什么我没更小心一点呢？我真想踢自己一脚！我真的会。”

词汇a good chance to win 赢的好机会；careful 小心的；kick oneself 自责 语法have a chance to do sth. 表示“有机会做某事”；Why wasn't I ...? 是自责式疑问句；kick oneself 是习语。

"Listen, Matt," said Tom, "try to look on the bright side. The other team is the best in our area, but our scores were really close. I'm proud of us, and I know our coach and everyone else in our school are as well."

“听着，马特，”汤姆说，“试着往好的方面看。对方是我们这个地区最好的球队，但我们的比分真的很接近。我为我们感到骄傲，我知道我们的教练和学校里的其他人也一样。”

词汇look on the bright side 往好的方面想；proud 自豪的；coach 教练 语法give advice 用 try to do sth.；be proud of 表示“为……感到自豪”；as well 表示“也”。

Matt felt a bit better.

马特感觉好了一点。

词汇a bit 有点儿；better 更好 语法a bit 修饰比较级 better，表示“好一点儿”。

"Thanks, Tom. I guess it's just hard to lose."

“谢谢你，汤姆。我想输了比赛就是很难受。”

词汇hard 难的；lose 输 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；hard to lose 表示“难以接受失败”。

"Hey, you can't win them all," said Tom.

“嘿，你不可能每次都赢，”汤姆说。

词汇win 赢 语法you can't win them all 是习语；said Tom 是倒装语序（主语在引语后）。

"It's more important to learn from your mistakes so that you don't repeat them in future. We'll keep working together as a team. One for all, and all for one, right?"

“更重要的是从错误中学习，这样以后就不会再犯。我们会继续作为一个团队一起努力。人人为我，我为人人，对吧？”

词汇learn from 从……中学习；repeat 重复；in future 以后；one for all, and all for one 人人为我，我为人人 语法It's + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型；so that 引导目的状语从句；as a team 表示“作为一个团队”。

"You're right. If we pull together, we should win next time," Matt smiled.

“你说得对。如果我们齐心协力，下次就应该能赢，”马特笑了。

词汇pull together 齐心协力；next time 下次 语法if 引导条件状语从句；should + 动词原形表示推测。

He was lucky to have a friend like Tom!

他很幸运有汤姆这样的朋友！

词汇lucky 幸运的；like 像 语法be lucky to do sth. 表示“做某事很幸运”；like Tom 作后置定语修饰 friend。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一个关于成长的故事。篮球赛上，马特不小心推了对方球员，裁判判给对方两次罚球，马特因此心情沉重地走进教室。好朋友汤姆看出他不对劲，安慰他说那只是个意外，不要太苛责自己。但马特仍很自责，觉得是自己的错让球队失去了赢球的机会。汤姆开导他：对方是本地区最强的球队，但比分很接近，教练和全校都为他们感到骄傲；更重要的是从错误中学习、以后不再犯，全队继续齐心协力，下次一定能赢。马特在朋友的鼓励下重新振作起来。

English Summary

This is a story about growing up. At Saturday night's basketball game, Matt accidentally pushed a player, and the referee gave the other team two free throws. On Monday, Matt entered the classroom with a heavy heart. His best friend Tom noticed and told him not to be too hard on himself because it was just an accident. But Matt still blamed himself for letting the team down. Tom helped him look on the bright side: the other team was the best in their area, but their scores were really close, and the coach and everyone else in the school were proud of them. He also told Matt that it is more important to learn from mistakes so as not to repeat them, and that the team would keep working together—one for all, and all for one. Encouraged by his friend, Matt smiled and believed they would win next time.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这篇 Reading Plus 文章从身体和心情两个方面说明笑的好处。文章开头用谚语点题——笑是最好的良药。人们从小就学会笑，连看不见、听不见的婴儿也会笑，笑是生活中自然的一部分。首先，笑对身体有好处：笑的时候身体吸入更多氧气，心脏、胃和肩膀都得到锻炼，肌肉随之放松，开怀大笑能让人放松长达 45 分钟；长期来看，笑还能让身体更强壮、抵御更多疾病、产生减轻疼痛的化学物质。其次，笑对心情有好处：它能减轻压力和焦虑，促使大脑释放让人平静、快乐的化学物质。文章还提到，人们和别人在一起时发笑的概率要大 30 倍，笑能表达亲近与友好；有些人甚至参加大笑小组。最后，文章建议：不开心时想想有趣的事，即使难过时不想笑，笑一笑也能让你感觉好些。

English Summary

This Reading Plus text explains the benefits of laughter for our body and our mood. It begins with the saying that laughter is the best medicine. People start laughing from a young age—even babies that cannot see or hear can laugh—and laughter is a natural part of life. First, laughter is good for our body: when we laugh, our body takes in more oxygen, our heart, stomach and shoulders work hard, and our muscles feel more relaxed afterwards; a good laugh can help you feel relaxed for up to 45 minutes, and over time laughter helps our body get stronger, fight off more diseases and create chemicals that help us feel less pain. Laughter is also good for our mood: it makes us feel less stressed or anxious and causes our brain to release chemicals that make us feel calm and happy. The text also notes that we are 30 times more likely to laugh when we are around others, that laughter can show we want to be close to them, and that some people even join laughing groups. Finally, it advises us to think about something funny when we feel unhappy, because doing so can make us feel better.

语法点 Grammar

Do you often laugh with friends?

一般现在时的一般疑问句；often 是频度副词。

If you do, that is great, because just as the saying goes: Laughter is the best medicine!

if 引导条件状语从句；just as the saying goes 表示“正如俗话所说”。

At around three months old, babies learn to laugh.

learn to do sth. 学会做某事；at around ... old 表示“大约……大时”。

Even babies that cannot see or hear can laugh.

that 引导定语从句修饰 babies；even 表示“甚至”。

First of all, laughter is good for our body.

first of all 表示“首先”；be good for 表示“对……有益”。

When we laugh, our body takes in more oxygen.

when 引导时间状语从句；take in 表示“吸入”。

Laughter also makes our body work out.

make sb./sth. do sth. 表示“使……做某事”。

A good laugh can help you feel relaxed for up to 45 minutes.

help sb. (to) do sth. 帮助某人做某事；up to 表示“多达、长达”。

Over time, laughter helps our body to get stronger, fight off more diseases, and even create special chemicals to help us feel less pain!

over time 表示“随着时间的推移”；三个动词短语并列作 helps 的宾语补足语。

We are 30 times more likely to laugh when we are around others.

be likely to do sth. 表示“可能做某事”；30 times more likely 是倍数表达。

Monkeys clean one another's fur to show friendship.

one another's 表示“彼此的”；不定式 to show friendship 作目的状语。

But people laugh so that others will know that they are being friendly.

so that 引导目的状语从句；that 引导宾语从句。

Although they force themselves to laugh at first, after hearing other people laugh, their laughter becomes real!

although 引导让步状语从句；force oneself to do sth. 表示“强迫自己做某事”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

A good laugh can help you feel relaxed for up to 45 minutes.

开怀大笑能帮你放松长达 45 分钟。

help sb. (to) do sth. 帮助某人做某事；up to 表示“多达、长达”。

We are 30 times more likely to laugh when we are around others.

和别人在一起时，我们发笑的概率要大 30 倍。

be likely to do sth. 表示“可能做某事”；30 times more likely 是倍数表达。

Although they force themselves to laugh at first, after hearing other people laugh, their laughter becomes real!

虽然起初他们强迫自己笑，但听到别人笑之后，他们的笑就变得真实了！

although 引导让步状语从句；force oneself to do sth. 表示“强迫自己做某事”；at first 表示“起初”。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 先看 P84 的标题 Laughter Is the Best Medicine，猜一猜：笑为什么能“治病”？
  • 读前想一想：你最近一次开怀大笑是什么时候？笑之后感觉怎样？

读中

  • 边读边找出文章从哪两个方面说明笑的好处（body / mood），用思维导图整理。
  • 画出表示好处和程度、频率的表达（take in more oxygen、up to 45 minutes、30 times more likely 等）。

读后

  • 完成 P85 的思维导图，并回答两个讨论问题。
  • 用 3–5 句话写一写：怎样让自己和身边的人多笑一笑？

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面 4 篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. Why isn't Judy happy?

  1. Because she doesn't like her cousin Kate.
  2. Because her parents aren't happy with her grades.
  3. Because she failed her piano exam.
  4. Because Lily is angry with her.
答案与解析

B 原文：Well, my parents aren't happy with my grades. 可知朱迪因为父母对她的成绩不满意而不开心。

2. What mistake did Matt make at the basketball game?

  1. He missed the final shot.
  2. He was late for the game.
  3. He pushed a player and the other team got two free throws.
  4. He argued with the referee.
答案与解析

C 原文：When the referee saw Matt push another player, he gave the other team two free throws. 可知马特推了对方球员，导致对方获得两次罚球。

3. What did Kelly use to think about science?

  1. It was boring and difficult.
  2. It was easy and interesting.
  3. It was her favourite subject.
  4. It was not a real subject.
答案与解析

A 原文：I used to think that science was boring and difficult. 可知凯利过去觉得科学既无聊又难。

4. How many times more likely are we to laugh when we are around others?

  1. 3 times.
  2. 13 times.
  3. 30 times.
  4. 45 times.
答案与解析

C 原文：We are 30 times more likely to laugh when we are around others. 可知和别人在一起时，我们发笑的概率要大 30 倍。

5. What does Lily advise Judy to do?

  1. To study harder.
  2. To write her parents a letter.
  3. To talk to her cousin Kate.
  4. To stop seeing her parents.
答案与解析

B 原文：How about writing them a letter so that you can get your message across clearly? 可知莉莉建议朱迪给父母写一封信。

6. How did Kelly feel after she talked with Mrs Thompson that day?

  1. Much better.
  2. More worried.
  3. Very tired.
  4. Quite angry.
答案与解析

A 原文：You listened to me and talked with me so that I felt much better. 可知和老师谈过之后，凯利感觉好多了。

7. What did Tom advise Matt to do?

  1. To forget the game and go home.
  2. To blame the referee for the decision.
  3. To practise basketball alone every day.
  4. To learn from his mistake and keep positive as a team.
答案与解析

D 原文：It's more important to learn from your mistakes so that you don't repeat them in future. We'll keep working together as a team. 可知汤姆建议马特从错误中学习、全队继续齐心协力。

8. What does the writer think about laughter?

  1. It is only useful for babies.
  2. It makes people feel more stressed.
  3. It is not as important as medicine.
  4. It is the best medicine for our body and mood.
答案与解析

D 原文：Laughter is the best medicine! 且全文从 body 和 mood 两方面说明笑的好处，可知作者认为笑对身心都是最好的良药。
