Section A 3a · Talking with Parents

Section A 3a 主题：成长与沟通 · 对话

Lily: What's wrong, Judy?

朱迪，你怎么了？

词汇wrong 出问题的 语法What's wrong? 是询问情况/烦恼的固定问法。

Judy: Well, my parents aren't happy with my grades.

嗯，我父母对我的成绩不满意。

词汇be happy with 对……满意；grade 成绩 语法一般现在时否定；be happy with。

Lily: Really? Weren't they pretty good this term?

真的吗？这学期不是挺好的吗？

词汇pretty 相当；term 学期 语法Weren't they...? 否定疑问句表示惊讶；pretty good 表示“相当好”。

Judy: Yeah, but my parents have very high standards.

是啊，但我父母要求很高。

词汇standard 标准；要求 语法have high standards 表示“要求高”。

Judy: They often compare me with my cousin, Kate.

他们经常把我和我的表妹凯特作比较。

词汇compare A with B 把 A 与 B 比较；cousin 表亲 语法compare sb. with sb. 是固定搭配。

Judy: She gets better grades, plays the piano well, often wins awards...

她成绩更好，钢琴弹得好，还经常获奖……

词汇grade 成绩；play the piano 弹钢琴；award 奖 语法比较级 better；and 连接并列谓语。

Lily: I'm sorry to hear that.

听到这个我很遗憾。

词汇be sorry to hear 听到……很遗憾 语法be sorry to hear that 是固定表达。

Lily: But I'm sure your parents just want you to do well, although they may not show it.

但我相信你的父母只是希望你能做好，尽管他们可能没有表现出来。

词汇be sure 确信；want sb. to do 希望某人做；although 尽管；show 表现 语法I'm sure 后接宾语从句；want sb. to do sth.；although 引导让步状语从句。

Lily: Why don't you talk to them?

你为什么不和他们谈谈呢？

词汇why don't you 为什么不 语法Why don't you...? 是提建议的固定句式。

Lily: Until you talk to them, they might not know how you feel.

除非你和他们谈，否则他们可能不知道你的感受。

词汇until 直到；might 可能；feel 感受 语法not...until... 的变体；how you feel 是宾语从句。

Judy: I guess ... I know my parents love me, but sometimes I think they love Kate more.

我想……我知道我父母爱我，但有时候我觉得他们更爱凯特。

词汇guess 猜想；sometimes 有时 语法I guess / I think 后接宾语从句；but 转折。

Judy: I'm afraid I'll cry if we talk.

我担心如果谈话我会哭。

词汇be afraid 担心；cry 哭 语法be afraid (that)...；if 引导条件状语从句。

Lily: How about writing them a letter so that you can get your message across clearly?

给他们写封信怎么样？这样你就能把你的意思清楚地传达给他们了。

词汇how about 怎么样；get across 传达；clearly 清楚地 语法How about doing sth.?；so that 引导目的状语从句。

Judy: Oh, that's a good idea.

哦，好主意。

词汇idea 主意 语法That's a good idea. 表示赞同建议。

Lily: Writing things down can also make you feel better.

把事情写下来也能让你感觉好一些。

词汇write down 写下；make sb. feel better 使某人感觉更好 语法动名词 Writing 作主语；make sb. do sth.。

Judy: Thanks! Just talking to you makes me feel better already!

谢谢！和你聊聊天就让我感觉好多了！

词汇already 已经 语法动名词 talking 作主语；make sb. do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，朱迪向莉莉倾诉烦恼：父母对她的成绩不满意，要求很高，还常把她和表妹凯特比较，她担心父母更爱凯特，也害怕一谈话就哭。莉莉安慰她，说父母只是希望她好，只是没有表现出来；建议她和父母好好谈谈，或者写封信把心意清楚地传达出去。朱迪接受了建议，并说和莉莉聊天已经让她感觉好多了。

English Summary

In the conversation, Judy tells Lily her trouble: her parents are not happy with her grades and have very high standards, often comparing her with her cousin Kate. Judy worries her parents love Kate more and is afraid she will cry if they talk. Lily comforts her, saying her parents just want her to do well even if they don't show it, and suggests talking to them or writing a letter so her message gets across clearly. Judy accepts the idea and says talking to Lily already makes her feel better.

语法点 Grammar

What's wrong, Judy?

What's wrong? 是询问烦恼的固定问法。

Weren't they pretty good this term?

否定疑问句表示惊讶；pretty good 表示“相当好”。

They often compare me with my cousin, Kate.

compare sb. with sb. 是固定搭配。

I'm sure your parents just want you to do well, although they may not show it.

I'm sure 后接宾语从句；want sb. to do sth.；although 引导让步状语从句。

Why don't you talk to them?

Why don't you...? 是提建议的固定句式。

Until you talk to them, they might not know how you feel.

until 引导状语；how you feel 是宾语从句。

I'm afraid I'll cry if we talk.

be afraid (that)...；if 引导条件状语从句。

How about writing them a letter so that you can get your message across clearly?

How about doing sth.?；so that 引导目的状语从句。

Writing things down can also make you feel better.

动名词 Writing 作主语；make sb. do sth.。

Just talking to you makes me feel better already!

动名词 talking 作主语；make sb. do sth.。

Section B 1b · You Can't Win Them All

Section B 1b 主题：比赛失利 · 友谊与成长

When the bell rang, Matt entered the classroom with a heavy heart.

铃声响起时，马特心情沉重地走进教室。

词汇bell 铃；ring 响；enter 进入；with a heavy heart 心情沉重地 语法when 引导时间状语从句；with... 作伴随状语。

"Hey, Matt," his best friend Tom greeted him.

“嘿，马特，”他最好的朋友汤姆跟他打招呼。

词汇greet 问候 语法直接引语；best friend 作同位语。

He and Tom were in the school's basketball team.

他和汤姆都在学校篮球队。

词汇basketball team 篮球队 语法were in 表示“是……的成员”。

"Why the long face? Are you still feeling blue about Saturday night's game?" Tom asked.

“为什么闷闷不乐？你还在为周六晚上的比赛难过吗？”汤姆问道。

词汇long face 不高兴的脸；feel blue 难过 语法Why the long face? 是省略句；feel blue 是固定表达。

"Of course I am," Matt said quietly.

“我当然难过，”马特轻声说。

词汇of course 当然；quietly 轻声地 语法简略回答 Of course I am；副词修饰 said。

He didn't dare to look at Tom as he spoke.

他说话时都不敢看汤姆。

词汇dare to do 敢做；look at 看 语法didn't dare to do；as 引导时间状语从句。

"I'm really sorry about letting everybody down."

“我真的很抱歉让大家失望了。”

词汇be sorry about 为……抱歉；let sb. down 让某人失望 语法be sorry about + 动名词；let sb. down。

"The other team got those points because of me."

“对方球队因为我才得到那些分数。”

词汇the other team 对方球队；point 分数；because of 因为 语法because of + 名词/代词。

"Don't be too hard on yourself!" Tom said. "Although you pushed their player, it was just an accident. Everyone knows that!"

“别对自己太苛刻了！”汤姆说，“虽然你推了他们的球员，但那只是个意外。大家都知道的！”

词汇be hard on sb. 对某人苛刻；push 推；accident 意外 语法祈使句否定式；although 引导让步状语从句；that 指代前句。

Tom was a good friend. But Matt knew the referee didn't share Tom's opinion.

汤姆是个好朋友。但马特知道裁判和汤姆看法不同。

词汇referee 裁判；share one's opinion 与某人看法相同 语法But 转折；knew 后接宾语从句。

When the referee saw Matt push another player, he gave the other team two free throws.

当裁判看到马特推了另一名球员时，给了对方球队两次罚球机会。

词汇see sb. do 看到某人做；free throw 罚球 语法when 引导时间状语从句；see sb. do sth.；give sb. sth.。

Matt was shocked as anyone when the accident happened, but it was too late.

事故发生的时候，马特和其他人一样震惊，但已经太迟了。

词汇be shocked 震惊；happen 发生；too late 太迟 语法as...as anyone 表示“和任何人一样”；but 转折。

He couldn't take it back.

他无法挽回。

词汇take back 收回；挽回 语法couldn't + 动词原形。

"I guess we have to respect his decision," Matt said after a while.

“我想我们得尊重他的判罚，”过了一会儿，马特说。

词汇respect 尊重；decision 决定；after a while 过了一会儿 语法have to do；after a while 作时间状语。

"But we had a good chance to win until I made that mistake."

“但在我犯那个错误之前，我们本来很有机会赢的。”

词汇have a chance to do 有机会做；mistake 错误 语法had a chance to do；until 引导时间状语从句。

"Why wasn't I more careful? I could just kick myself!"

“我为什么不更小心点呢？我真该自责！”

词汇careful 小心的；kick oneself 自责 语法反义疑问句；could + 动词原形表示遗憾。

"Listen, Matt," said Tom, "try to look on the bright side."

“听着，马特，”汤姆说，“试着往好的方面想。”

词汇look on the bright side 往好的方面想 语法祈使句；倒装 said Tom。

"The other team is the best in our area, but our scores were really close."

“另一支球队是我们地区最好的，但我们的比分真的很接近。”

词汇area 地区；score 比分；close 接近的 语法the best 是最高级；but 转折。

"I'm proud of us, and I know our coach and everyone else in our school are as well."

“我为我们感到骄傲，而且我知道我们的教练和学校里的其他人也一样。”

词汇be proud of 为……骄傲；coach 教练 语法be proud of；as well 表示“也一样”。

Matt felt a bit better. "Thanks, Tom. I guess it's just hard to lose."

马特感觉好了一点。“谢谢，汤姆。我想输了比赛就是很难受。”

词汇a bit 一点；hard 困难的；lose 输 语法feel + adj.；It's + adj. + to do sth.。

"Hey, you can't win them all," said Tom.

“嘿，你不可能常胜，”汤姆说。

词汇win 赢 语法can't + 动词原形；倒装 said Tom。

"It's more important to learn from your mistakes so that you don't repeat them in future."

“更重要的是从错误中学习，这样你以后就不会再犯了。”

词汇learn from 向……学习；repeat 重复；in future 今后 语法It's + adj. + to do sth.；so that 引导目的状语从句。

"We'll keep working together as a team. One for all, and all for one, right?"

“我们会继续作为一个团队一起努力。人人为我，我为人人，对吧？”

词汇keep doing 继续做；as a team 作为团队 语法keep doing sth.；One for all, and all for one 是名句。

"You're right. If we pull together, we should win next time," Matt smiled.

“你说得对。如果我们齐心协力，下次我们应该能赢，”马特笑了。

词汇pull together 齐心协力；next time 下次 语法if 引导条件状语从句；should + 动词原形。

He was lucky to have a friend like Tom!

他很幸运有汤姆这样的朋友！

词汇lucky 幸运的 语法be lucky to do sth.。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

故事讲述马特在篮球赛后的失落。因为他不小心推了对方球员，裁判给了对方两次罚球，球队因此失分。马特非常自责，觉得让大家失望了；好朋友汤姆安慰他：那只是意外，别对自己太苛刻，并向好的方面想——对方是地区最强的队伍，比分却很接近。汤姆还说要从错误中学习，以后不再犯；只要齐心协力，下次能赢。马特心情好了一些。

English Summary

The story is about Matt's sadness after a basketball game. He accidentally pushed a player from the other team, the referee gave two free throws, and his team lost points. Matt blames himself for letting everyone down. His best friend Tom comforts him: it was just an accident, don't be too hard on yourself, and look on the bright side—the other team is the best in the area but the scores were close. Tom adds that learning from mistakes matters and that if they pull together they should win next time. Matt feels a bit better.

Section B 3a · A Thank-you Letter

Section B 3a 主题：感谢信 · 师生情

Dear Mrs Thompson,

亲爱的汤普森夫人：

词汇dear 亲爱的（信件称呼） 语法书信称呼语。

I want to thank you for being my teacher.

我想感谢您做我的老师。

词汇thank sb. for doing 感谢某人做 语法thank sb. for doing sth.。

You took the time to help me, although you were so busy.

尽管您非常忙，您还是花时间帮助我。

词汇take the time to do 花时间做；although 尽管；busy 忙碌的 语法although 引导让步状语从句。

Your classes always made me happy.

您的课总是让我很开心。

词汇make sb. happy 使某人开心 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词。

I used to think that science was boring and difficult, and I did badly in class.

我过去认为科学既无聊又难，而且我在课上表现很差。

词汇used to do 过去常常；boring 无聊的；do badly 表现差 语法used to do sth.；that 引导宾语从句；do badly in。

I never liked science until I took your class.

直到上了您的课，我才开始喜欢科学。

词汇until 直到；take class 上课 语法not...until... 表示“直到……才……”。

You encouraged me and made science lessons fun, and slowly, I grew more confident.

您鼓励我，让科学课变得有趣，慢慢地，我变得更有自信了。

词汇encourage 鼓励；make sth. fun 使某事有趣；confident 自信的 语法make + 宾语 + 形容词；grow + 形容词比较级。

I also want to thank you for being kind to me.

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是凯利写给汤普森老师的感谢信。她感谢老师花时间帮助忙碌的自己，感谢老师的课堂让自己快乐；过去她觉得科学无聊又难，成绩也不好，直到上了老师的课才喜欢科学，变得更有自信。她也感谢老师在她祖母去世后陪伴、倾听自己，让自己感觉好多了。最后，她感谢老师不仅是老师，还是朋友。

English Summary

This is Kelly's thank-you letter to Mrs Thompson. She thanks her for taking time to help although she was busy, and for classes that made her happy. Kelly used to think science was boring and difficult and did badly; she never liked it until she took the teacher's class, and her teacher encouraged her and made lessons fun, so she grew more confident. She also thanks her for being kind after her grandmother passed away—sitting with her, listening and talking made her feel much better. Finally she thanks her for being not only her teacher but also her friend.
