Section A · 3a: Rivers Around the World

Section A · 3a 主题：世界河流 · 自然奇观

Yaming: Welcome back, Ella!

欢迎回来，Ella！

词汇welcome back 欢迎回来 语法Welcome back! 用于迎接旅行归来的人。

Yaming: How was your trip?

你的旅行怎么样？

词汇trip 旅行 语法How was ...? 用一般过去时询问过去的情况。

Ella: It was great!

太棒了！

词汇great 很棒的 语法It was + 形容词，对过去的经历作出评价。

Ella: We saw the Hukou Waterfall on the Yellow River.

我们在黄河上看到了壶口瀑布。

词汇Hukou Waterfall 壶口瀑布；the Yellow River 黄河；waterfall 瀑布 语法一般过去时：see 的过去式是 saw；on the Yellow River 表示“在黄河上”。

Ella: Is that the longest river in China?

那是中国最长的河流吗？

词汇the longest river 最长的河流 语法the + 形容词最高级 longest 表示“最长的”。

Yaming: No, the Yangtze River is the longest river in China.

不是，长江才是中国最长的河流。

词汇the Yangtze River 长江 语法最高级 the longest river in China 表示“中国最长的河流”。

Yaming: It travels the farthest and is about 6,300 kilometres long.

它流程最远，长约 6,300 公里。

词汇travel 流程；farthest 最远地；kilometre 公里 语法travel 的第三人称单数是 travels；数词 + kilometres long 表示长度。

Yaming: The Yellow River is 5,464 kilometres long and is the second longest.

黄河长 5,464 公里，是第二长的河流。

词汇the second longest 第二长的 语法the second longest 表示“第二长的”；and 连接两个并列谓语。

Yaming: By the way, do you know why it's called the Yellow River?

顺便问一下，你知道它为什么叫黄河吗？

词汇by the way 顺便问一下；be called 被称为 语法by the way 是固定短语；why 引导宾语从句，用陈述语序。

Ella: Because it's yellow?

因为它是黄色的吗？

词汇because 因为 语法Because ...? 是省略回答，完整回答为 Because it's yellow.。

Yaming: Right.

对。

词汇right 对的 语法Right. 表示同意对方的猜测。

Yaming: Some parts of the river carry brown and yellow earth.

河流的一些河段携带棕黄色的泥土。

词汇parts of ……的部分；carry 携带；earth 泥土 语法some parts of ... 表示“一些部分”；carry 表示“携带”。

Ella: That's interesting!

真有趣！

词汇interesting 有趣的 语法That's = That is；That's + 形容词，对事物作出评价。

Ella: I like learning about rivers.

我喜欢了解河流。

词汇learn about 了解 语法like doing sth. 喜欢做某事。

Ella: My favourite is the Nile.

我最喜欢的是尼罗河。

词汇the Nile 尼罗河 语法favourite 在此作名词，指“最喜欢的事物”。

Ella: Did you know it's the longest river in Africa?

你知道它是非洲最长的河流吗？

词汇Africa 非洲 语法Did you know ...? 用一般过去时询问对方是否知道；it's = it is。

Yaming: Yes, it's 6,671 kilometres long.

知道，它长 6,671 公里。

词汇kilometre 公里 语法数词 + kilometres long 表示长度。

Yaming: It's very important in Egypt's history.

它在埃及历史上非常重要。

词汇Egypt 埃及；history 历史 语法Egypt's 是名词所有格；be important in ... 表示“在……中很重要”。

Ella: That's right.

没错。

词汇that's right 没错 语法That's right. 用于赞同对方的说法。

Ella: Both the Nile and the Yellow River helped ancient civilizations to develop.

尼罗河和黄河都帮助了古代文明的发展。

词汇both...and... ……和……都；ancient 古代的；civilization 文明；develop 发展 语法both ... and ... 连接两个并列主语；help ... (to) do sth. 帮助……做某事。

Ella: And they're still very important today.

而且它们今天仍然非常重要。

词汇still 仍然 语法and 连接上文；today 作时间状语。

Yaming: Yes.

是的。

词汇yes 是的 语法Yes. 是对上文的肯定回应。

Yaming: In China, we even call the Yellow River our "mother river"!

在中国，我们甚至把黄河称为我们的“母亲河”！

词汇even 甚至；mother river 母亲河 语法call + 宾语 + 宾语补足语；our 是形容词性物主代词。

Ella: We should protect them by all means.

我们应该尽一切办法保护它们。

词汇protect 保护；by all means 尽一切办法 语法should + 动词原形表示建议；by all means 表示“尽一切办法”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P34 Section A · 3a 中 Yaming 和 Ella 的对话（3a 要求：读对话，画出提到的三条河流）。Ella 旅行回来，Yaming 问她旅途怎么样；Ella 说很棒，她们在黄河上看到了壶口瀑布，还问黄河是不是中国最长的河流。Yaming 说不是，长江才是中国最长的河流，流程最远，长约 6,300 公里；黄河长 5,464 公里，是第二长的河流。Yaming 还问 Ella 知不知道黄河为什么叫黄河，Ella 猜是因为它是黄色的；Yaming 说对，因为河流的一些河段携带棕黄色的泥土。Ella 觉得很有趣，说她喜欢了解河流，她最喜欢尼罗河，并问 Yaming 知不知道尼罗河是非洲最长的河流。Yaming 说知道，尼罗河长 6,671 公里，在埃及历史上非常重要。Ella 说尼罗河和黄河都帮助了古代文明的发展，今天仍然很重要；Yaming 说在中国人们甚至把黄河称为“母亲河”。最后 Ella 说我们应该尽一切努力保护它们。

English Summary

This is the conversation between Yaming and Ella in Section A · 3a on page 34. In 3a, students read the conversation and underline the three rivers mentioned. Ella is back from a trip, and Yaming asks how it was. Ella says it was great: they saw the Hukou Waterfall on the Yellow River, and she asks whether it is the longest river in China. Yaming says no — the Yangtze River is the longest river in China; it travels the farthest and is about 6,300 kilometres long, while the Yellow River is 5,464 kilometres long and is the second longest. Yaming asks whether Ella knows why it is called the Yellow River, and Ella guesses that it is because it is yellow; Yaming explains that some parts of the river carry brown and yellow earth. Ella finds that interesting and says she likes learning about rivers; her favourite is the Nile, and she asks whether Yaming knows it is the longest river in Africa. Yaming says yes, it is 6,671 kilometres long and is very important in Egypt's history. Ella says both the Nile and the Yellow River helped ancient civilizations to develop and are still very important today; Yaming adds that in China people even call the Yellow River their "mother river". Finally Ella says we should protect them by all means.

语法点 Grammar

Welcome back, Ella!

Welcome back! 表示“欢迎回来”，用于迎接旅行归来的人。

How was your trip?

How was ...? 用一般过去时询问过去的情况。

We saw the Hukou Waterfall on the Yellow River.

一般过去时：see 的过去式是 saw；on the Yellow River 表示“在黄河上”。

Is that the longest river in China?

the + 形容词最高级 longest 表示“最长的”。

No, the Yangtze River is the longest river in China.

the longest river in China 表示“中国最长的河流”。

It travels the farthest and is about 6,300 kilometres long.

travel 的第三人称单数是 travels；数词 + kilometres long 表示长度。

The Yellow River is 5,464 kilometres long and is the second longest.

the second longest 表示“第二长的”；and 连接两个并列谓语。

By the way, do you know why it's called the Yellow River?

by the way 表示“顺便问一下”；why 引导宾语从句；be called 表示“被称为”。

Because it's yellow?

Because ...? 是省略回答，完整回答为 Because it's yellow.。

Some parts of the river carry brown and yellow earth.

some parts of ... 表示“一些部分”；carry 表示“携带”。

I like learning about rivers.

like doing sth. 喜欢做某事。

Did you know it's the longest river in Africa?

Did you know ...? 用一般过去时询问对方是否知道；it's = it is。

It's very important in Egypt's history.

Egypt's 是名词所有格；be important in ... 表示“在……中很重要”。

both ... and ... 连接两个并列主语；help ... (to) do sth. 帮助……做某事。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

It travels the farthest and is about 6,300 kilometres long.

它流程最远，长约 6,300 公里。

the farthest 是 far 的最高级，表示“最远”；about 表示“大约”； kilometres long 表示长度。

By the way, do you know why it's called the Yellow River?

顺便问一下，你知道它为什么叫黄河吗？

by the way 是固定短语；why it's called ... 是宾语从句，用陈述语序；be called 表示“被称为”。

Both the Nile and the Yellow River helped ancient civilizations to develop.

尼罗河和黄河都帮助了古代文明的发展。

both ... and ... 连接两个并列主语，谓语动词用复数；help ... (to) do sth. 表示“帮助……做某事”。

Section B 1b · Reaching New Heights

Section B 1b 主题：珠峰攀登 · 勇气与探索（教材 P36–P37 两页）

To most people, Mount Qomolangma is one of the greatest wonders of the world.

在大多数人看来，珠穆朗玛峰是世界上最伟大的奇观之一。

词汇Mount Qomolangma 珠穆朗玛峰；wonder 奇观 语法one of + 形容词最高级 + 复数名词，表示“最……的之一”。

On the highest place on earth, you will feel like you are able to touch the sky.

站在地球最高处，你会感觉自己能触摸到天空。

词汇the highest place 最高处；feel like 感觉像；touch 触摸 语法the highest 是最高级；feel like + 从句表示“感觉像……”。

But only the best climbers reach the top, and that is only if they survive the terrible conditions first.

但只有最好的登山者才能登顶，而且前提是他们先在恶劣的条件中活下来。

词汇climber 登山者；reach the top 登顶；survive 生存；condition 条件 语法only if 表示“只有当……”；survive 表示“在……中幸存”。

On the mountain, temperatures can fall to −30 degrees.

山上温度可以降到零下 30 度。

词汇temperature 温度；fall to 降到；degree 度 语法can + 动词原形表示可能性；fall to 表示“降到”。

Climbers must also deal with thin air, high cliffs, and changeable weather.

登山者还必须应对稀薄的空气、高高的悬崖和多变的天气。

词汇deal with 应对；thin air 稀薄的空气；cliff 悬崖；changeable 多变的 语法must + 动词原形表示必须；deal with 表示“应对”。

Before 1960, no one was able to reach the top of Qomolangma from the northern side.

1960 年以前，没有人能从北坡登顶珠峰。

词汇northern side 北坡 语法before + 年份表示“在……之前”；be able to do sth. 表示“能够做某事”。

Mountain climbers even called that way the "Death Road", but a Chinese climbing team was determined to make the climb.

登山者甚至把那条路称为“死亡之路”，但一支中国登山队决心完成攀登。

词汇call 称呼；determined 坚定的；make the climb 完成攀登 语法call + 宾语 + 宾语补足语；be determined to do sth. 表示“决心做某事”。

On 24 May 1960, climbers Liu Lianman, Wang Fuzhou, Qu Yinhua, and Gong Bu arrived at the Second Step.

1960 年 5 月 24 日，登山者刘连满、王富洲、屈银华和贡布到达第二台阶。

词汇arrive at 到达；the Second Step 第二台阶 语法on + 具体日期；arrive at + 地点表示“到达”。

It was 8,600 metres above sea level.

那里海拔 8,600 米。

词汇above sea level 海拔 语法数词 + metres above sea level 表示海拔高度。

People said it was so high that even birds could not reach it.

人们说那里高得连鸟都飞不上去。

词汇so...that... 如此……以至于…… 语法so + 形容词 + that ... 引导结果状语从句。

Liu allowed his teammates to step on his shoulders.

刘连满让队友踩着他的肩膀。

词汇allow 允许；teammate 队友；shoulder 肩膀 语法allow sb. to do sth. 允许某人做某事。

Bit by bit, the climbers pulled themselves up, and they made it past the Second Step.

登山者们一点一点地向上爬，越过了第二台阶。

词汇bit by bit 一点一点地；pull oneself up 向上爬；make it 成功做到 语法bit by bit 表示“一点一点地”；make it 表示“成功做到”。

At 4:20 a.m. on 25 May, the Chinese national flag flew above the world's highest mountain for the first time.

5 月 25 日凌晨 4:20，中国国旗第一次飘扬在世界最高峰上。

词汇national flag 国旗；fly 飘扬；for the first time 第一次 语法at + 具体时刻；fly 的过去式是 flew；for the first time 表示“第一次”。

In 1975, another Chinese team climbed to the top of Qomolangma to do research.

1975 年，另一支中国登山队登顶珠峰进行考察。

词汇another 另一；research 研究 语法to do research 是不定式作目的状语。

This time, they brought a ladder to the Second Step.

这一次，他们把一架梯子带到了第二台阶。

词汇ladder 梯子 语法bring 的过去式是 brought。

Until 2007, almost all climbers from the northern side used this "Chinese Ladder" to reach the top much faster.

直到 2007 年，几乎所有从北坡攀登的登山者都用这架“中国梯”更快地登顶。

词汇until 直到；almost 几乎；much faster 快得多 语法until 表示“直到”；to reach 表示目的；much 修饰比较级。

In 2020, a third team returned to Qomolangma to measure its height—8,848.86 metres.

2020 年，第三支队伍重返珠峰，测量它的高度——8,848.86 米。

词汇measure 测量；height 高度 语法a third team 表示“第三支队伍”；to measure 表示目的。

Climbing Qomolangma is dangerous.

攀登珠峰很危险。

词汇climbing 攀登；dangerous 危险的 语法动名词短语 Climbing Qomolangma 作主语。

Some climbers reach the top successfully, but many fail.

一些登山者成功登顶，但许多人失败了。

词汇successfully 成功地；fail 失败 语法but 表转折；some ... many ... 形成对比。

Why do people still risk their lives to climb the highest mountain in the world?

人们为什么仍然冒着生命危险去攀登世界最高峰？

词汇risk one's life 冒着生命危险 语法why 引导特殊疑问句；risk one's life to do sth. 表示“冒着生命危险做某事”。

It is because human curiosity and ambition cannot be stopped.

这是因为人类的好奇心和雄心无法被阻止。

词汇curiosity 好奇心；ambition 雄心；stop 阻止 语法because 引导表语从句；cannot be stopped 是被动语态。

Or, as the English explorer George Mallory put it, it is simply "because it's there"!

或者，正如英国探险家乔治·马洛里所说，就是“因为山在那里”！

词汇explorer 探险家；simply 仅仅；put it 表达 语法as ... put it 表示“正如……所说”；simply 表示“仅仅、就是”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P36–P37 两页 Section B 1b 的文章《Reaching New Heights》（攀登新高度）。文章介绍珠穆朗玛峰：在大多数人看来，珠峰是世界最伟大的奇观之一；站在地球最高处，你会感觉能触摸到天空，但只有最好的登山者才能登顶，而且要先在恶劣条件中活下来——山上温度可降到零下 30 度，登山者还要应对稀薄的空气、高高的悬崖和多变的天气。1960 年以前，没有人能从北坡登顶，登山者把那条路称为“死亡之路”，但一支中国登山队决心攀登。1960 年 5 月 24 日，登山队员刘连满、王富洲、屈银华和贡布到达海拔 8,600 米的第二台阶；人们说那里高得连鸟都飞不上去。刘连满让队友踩着自己的肩膀，一点一点向上爬，越过了第二台阶。5 月 25 日凌晨 4:20，中国国旗第一次飘扬在世界最高峰上。1975 年，另一支中国登山队登顶考察，把梯子带到第二台阶；直到 2007 年，几乎所有从北坡攀登的登山者都用这架“中国梯”更快登顶。2020 年，第三支中国登山队重返珠峰，测量出它的高度——8,848.86 米。文章最后说，攀登珠峰很危险，一些人成功登顶，很多人失败，但人们为什么仍要冒着生命危险攀登世界最高峰？因为人类的好奇心和雄心无法阻挡；或者用英国探险家乔治·马洛里的话说，就是“因为山在那里”！

English Summary

This is the article "Reaching New Heights" from Section B 1b, on pages 36–37. It introduces Mount Qomolangma: to most people, it is one of the greatest wonders of the world. On the highest place on earth you will feel like you can touch the sky, but only the best climbers reach the top, and only if they survive the terrible conditions first — temperatures can fall to −30 degrees, and climbers must deal with thin air, high cliffs and changeable weather. Before 1960 no one was able to reach the top from the northern side; climbers called that way the "Death Road", but a Chinese climbing team was determined to make the climb. On 24 May 1960, climbers Liu Lianman, Wang Fuzhou, Qu Yinhua and Gong Bu arrived at the Second Step at 8,600 metres above sea level; people said it was so high that even birds could not reach it. Liu allowed his teammates to step on his shoulders, and bit by bit they pulled themselves up and made it past the Second Step. At 4:20 a.m. on 25 May, the Chinese national flag flew above the world's highest mountain for the first time. In 1975 another Chinese team climbed to the top to do research and brought a ladder to the Second Step; until 2007 almost all climbers from the northern side used this "Chinese Ladder" to reach the top much faster. 中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P86 Reading Plus 的文章《The Sahara Desert》（撒哈拉沙漠）。文章说，撒哈拉沙漠位于非洲，横跨非洲大陆 30% 的面积；这片广袤的沙漠在阿拉伯语中也被称为“大沙漠”，面积约 900 万平方公里，至少已有 460 万年历史，是世界上最古老的地方之一。所有沙漠都很干燥，而撒哈拉既干燥又炎热，是世界上最大的热沙漠；我们可能以为所有沙漠都很热，但事实上有些沙漠非常寒冷，许多科学家认为南极洲就是一种寒漠。撒哈拉的大部分是岩石地面，但也有草地、湖泊、山脉甚至“沙海”；沙海顾名思义，看起来就像一片沙子的海洋，风吹过时沙子看起来像波浪。撒哈拉的生活很艰难，大约有两百万人生活在撒哈拉，他们通常不断迁徙；撒哈拉少数地区有水，有些人常年住在这些地方。能在如此干热气候中生活的动物不多，它们必须适应环境才能生存：例如骆驼可以长时间不喝水，一旦找到水，能在 13 分钟内喝下 100 多升水！沙漠中还有耳廓狐，它们的大耳朵帮助身体保持凉爽。今天，世界上最大的热沙漠还在变得更大，比大约一个世纪前扩大了约 10%；为什么会扩大？一些科学家认为部分原因是气候变化。随着气温上升，非洲的夏天更热、降雨更少，水体变小，地面越来越干。如果我们不采取有效措施阻止气候变化，沙漠将继续扩大！

English Summary

This is the Reading Plus article "The Sahara Desert" on page 86. It says that the Sahara Desert, located in Africa, spreads across 30 per cent of the continent. Also known as "the great desert" in Arabic, this vast desert is about 9 million square kilometres in size and at least 4.6 million years old, which means it is one of the oldest places in the world. All deserts are dry, and the Sahara is both dry and hot; it is the largest hot desert in the world. We may think that all deserts are hot, but in fact some deserts are very cold — many scientists believe that Antarctica, for example, is a type of cold desert. Most of the Sahara is rocky ground, but there are also places with grass, lakes, mountains and even sand seas. A sand sea looks like a sea of sand, and when the wind blows the sand looks like waves. Life in the Sahara is difficult. About two million people live there and usually move from place to place; there is water in a few parts of the Sahara, and some people live in these places all year round. Not many animals can live in such a dry and hot climate, and they must adapt to survive: camels, for example, can go for a long time without drinking water, but when they find water they can drink more than 100 litres of it in 13 minutes; fennec foxes also live in the desert and have large ears to help their bodies stay cool. Today the world's largest hot desert is getting even bigger — about 10 per cent larger than it was about a century ago. Why? Some scientists believe part of the reason is climate change: as temperatures rise, summers in Africa grow hotter and less rain falls, water bodies get smaller and the ground gets drier and drier. If we do not take effective measures to stop climate change, the desert will keep growing!

语法点 Grammar

Located in Africa, the Sahara Desert spreads across 30 per cent of the continent.

located in Africa 是过去分词短语作状语；spread 的第三人称单数是 spreads。

This vast desert, also known as "the great desert" in Arabic, is about 9 million square kilometres in size.

also known as ... 是过去分词短语作后置定语；in size 表示“在大小上”。

It is also at least 4.6 million years old.

at least 表示“至少”；数词 + years old 表示年龄。

This means that it is one of the oldest places in the world.

mean that ... 后接宾语从句；one of the oldest places 表示“最古老的地方之一”。

We may think that all deserts are hot, but in fact some deserts are very cold!

may 表示可能性；in fact 表示“事实上”；but 表转折。

Many scientists believe that Antarctica, for example, is a type of cold desert.

believe that ... 后接宾语从句；for example 作插入语。

A sand sea is just what it sounds like—it looks like a sea of sand!

what it sounds like 是表语从句；look like 表示“看起来像”。

When the wind blows, the sand looks like waves.

when 引导时间状语从句；blow 的第三人称单数是 blows。

About two million people live in the Sahara.

about 表示“大约”；two million 表示“两百万”。

They usually move from place to place.

from place to place 表示“从一个地方到另一个地方”。

There is water in a few parts of the Sahara, and some people live in these places all year round.

There is + 不可数名词；all year round 表示“全年”。

Not many animals can live in a place with such a dry and hot climate.

not many 表示“不多”；such + 形容词 + 名词表示“如此……的”。

Camels, for example, go for a long time without drinking water.

for example 作插入语；without doing sth. 表示“不……”。

When they do find water, they can drink more than 100 litres of it in 13 minutes!

do 在此表示强调；more than 表示“超过”。

If we do not take effective measures to stop climate change, the desert will keep growing!

if 引导条件状语从句；keep doing sth. 表示“持续做某事”。
