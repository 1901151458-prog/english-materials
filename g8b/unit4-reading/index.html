Section A 3a · Talking About Rivers

Section A 3a 主题：河流 · 最高级

Yaming: Welcome back, Ella! How was your trip?

欢迎回来，埃拉！你的旅行怎么样？

词汇welcome back 欢迎回来；trip 旅行 语法How was...? 一般过去时询问过去情况。

Ella: It was great! We saw the Hukou Waterfall on the Yellow River.

很棒！我们看到了黄河上的壶口瀑布。

词汇waterfall 瀑布；the Yellow River 黄河 语法一般过去时 saw；on the Yellow River 作后置定语。

Ella: Is that the longest river in China?

那是中国最长的河流吗？

词汇longest 最长的 语法Is that...? 疑问句；the longest 是最高级。

Yaming: No, the Yangtze River is the longest river in China.

不是，长江是中国最长的河流。

词汇the Yangtze River 长江；longest 最长的 语法最高级 the longest 前加 the；作表语。

Yaming: It travels the farthest and is about 6,300 kilometres long.

它流程最远，大约有 6300 公里长。

词汇travel 流程；farthest 最远；kilometre 公里 语法the farthest 是副词最高级；about...long 表示长度。

Yaming: The Yellow River is 5,464 kilometres long and is the second longest.

黄河长 5464 公里，是第二长的河流。

词汇second longest 第二长的 语法the second longest 表示“第二长的”。

Yaming: By the way, do you know why it's called the Yellow River?

顺便问一下，你知道它为什么叫黄河吗？

词汇by the way 顺便问一下；be called 被称为 语法why 引导宾语从句；被动语态 is called。

Ella: Because it's yellow?

因为它是黄色的？

词汇because 因为 语法省略回答，完整为 Because it's yellow.

Yaming: Right. Some parts of the river carry brown and yellow earth.

对。河流的一些河段携带棕色和黄色的泥土。

词汇part 部分；carry 携带；earth 泥土 语法Some parts of... 作主语；and 连接并列形容词。

Ella: That's interesting! I like learning about rivers.

真有趣！我喜欢了解河流。

词汇learn about 了解 语法like doing sth.；learn about。

Ella: My favourite is the Nile.

我最喜欢的是尼罗河。

词汇the Nile 尼罗河；favourite 最喜欢的 语法名词性物主代词结构 My favourite is...。

Ella: Did you know it's the longest river in Africa?

你知道它是非洲最长的河流吗？

词汇Africa 非洲 语法Did you know...? 后接宾语从句。

Yaming: Yes, it's 6,671 kilometres long.

知道，它有 6671 公里长。

词汇kilometre 公里 语法It's...long 表示长度。

Yaming: It's very important in Egypt's history.

它在埃及历史上非常重要。

词汇Egypt 埃及；history 历史 语法be important in...；名词所有格 Egypt's。

Ella: That's right. Both the Nile and the Yellow River helped ancient civilizations to develop.

对的。尼罗河和黄河都帮助了古代文明的发展。

词汇ancient 古代的；civilization 文明；develop 发展 语法both...and... 连接并列主语；help sb./sth. to do sth.。

Ella: And they're still very important today.

而且它们在今天仍然非常重要。

词汇still 仍然 语法still 修饰 important。

Yaming: Yes. In China, we even call the Yellow River our "mother river"!

是的。在中国，我们甚至把黄河称为我们的“母亲河”！

词汇even 甚至；call A B 称 A 为 B 语法call sb./sth. + 名词作宾补。

Ella: We should protect them by all means.

我们应该想尽一切办法保护它们。

词汇protect 保护；by all means 想尽一切办法 语法should + 动词原形；by all means。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话中，埃拉旅行回来看到了黄河壶口瀑布，问亚明黄河是不是中国最长的河流。亚明说长江最长，流程最远，约 6300 公里；黄河长 5464 公里，是第二长，因为一些河段携带黄褐色泥土而得名。埃拉喜欢了解河流，最喜欢非洲的尼罗河（6671 公里），它在埃及历史上很重要。两人都认为这两条河帮助了古代文明发展，今天仍然重要；亚明说在中国黄河被称作“母亲河”，埃拉说我们应该想尽办法保护它们。

English Summary

After her trip, Ella says she saw the Hukou Waterfall on the Yellow River and asks whether the Yellow River is China's longest river. Yaming says the Yangtze is the longest, travelling the farthest at about 6,300 kilometres, while the Yellow River is 5,464 kilometres long and the second longest; it is called the Yellow River because some parts carry brown and yellow earth. Ella likes learning about rivers and her favourite is the Nile (6,671 km), which is important in Egypt's history. Both rivers helped ancient civilizations develop and are still important today; in China the Yellow River is called the "mother river", and Ella says we should protect them by all means.

Section B 1b · Reaching New Heights

Section B 1b 主题：珠穆朗玛峰 · 攀登精神

To most people, Mount Qomolangma is one of the greatest wonders of the world.

对大多数人来说，珠穆朗玛峰是世界上最伟大的奇迹之一。

词汇to most people 对大多数人来说；Mount Qomolangma 珠穆朗玛峰；wonder 奇迹 语法one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。

On the highest place on earth, you will feel like you are able to touch the sky.

在地球上最高的地方，你会感觉自己仿佛能够触摸到天空。

词汇on earth 在地球上；feel like 感觉像；touch 触摸 语法the highest 是最高级；feel like + 从句。

But only the best climbers reach the top, and that is only if they survive the terrible conditions first.

但只有最优秀的登山者才能到达顶峰，而且只有他们首先挺过恶劣的环境才行。

词汇reach the top 到达顶峰；survive 挺过；condition 条件；环境 语法the best 最高级；only if 引导条件状语从句。

On the mountain, temperatures can fall to -30 degrees.

在这座山上，气温能降到零下 30 度。

词汇temperature 气温；fall to 降到；degree 度 语法can + 动词原形表示可能；fall to。

Climbers must also deal with thin air, high cliffs, and changeable weather.

登山者还必须应对稀薄的空气、高耸的悬崖和多变的天气。

词汇deal with 应对；thin air 稀薄的空气；cliff 悬崖；changeable 多变的 语法must + 动词原形；三者并列作宾语。

Before 1960, no one was able to reach the top of Qomolangma from the northern side.

1960 年以前，没有人能从北侧登上珠穆朗玛峰的顶峰。

词汇be able to 能够；northern 北部的；side 侧面 语法be able to do sth.；from the northern side 作状语。

Mountain climbers even called that way the "Death Road", but a Chinese climbing team was determined to make the climb.

登山者甚至把那条路称为“死亡之路”，但一支中国登山队决心进行这次攀登。

词汇climber 登山者；death 死亡；be determined to 决心做 语法call A B；be determined to do sth.；but 转折。

On 24 May 1960, climbers Liu Lianman, Wang Fuzhou, Qu Yinhua, and Gong Bu arrived at the Second Step.

1960 年 5 月 24 日，登山队员刘连满、王富洲、屈银华和贡布到达了“第二台阶”。

词汇arrive at 到达；the Second Step 第二台阶 语法arrive at + 地点；人名并列作同位语。

It was 8,600 metres above sea level.

它海拔 8600 米。

词汇above sea level 海拔 语法above sea level 表示海拔高度。

People said it was so high that even birds could not reach it.

人们说它太高了，连鸟都飞不上去。

词汇so...that... 如此……以至于…… 语法so...that... 引导结果状语从句。

Liu allowed his teammates to step on his shoulders.

刘连满让队友们踩在他的肩膀上。

词汇allow sb. to do 允许某人做；teammate 队友；shoulder 肩膀 语法allow sb. to do sth.；step on。

Bit by bit, the climbers pulled themselves up, and they made it past the Second Step.

登山队员们一点一点地往上爬，成功通过了“第二台阶”。

词汇bit by bit 一点一点地；pull oneself up 爬上去；make it 成功 语法pull oneself up 反身代词；make it past...。

At 4:20 a.m. on 25 May, the Chinese national flag flew above the world's highest mountain for the first time.

5 月 25 日凌晨 4 点 20 分，中国国旗第一次在世界最高峰上空飘扬。

词汇national flag 国旗；fly 飘扬；for the first time 第一次 语法the world's highest 最高级；for the first time 作状语。

In 1975, another Chinese team climbed to the top of Qomolangma to do research.

1975 年，另一支中国登山队登上珠穆朗玛峰顶峰进行考察。

词汇research 研究；考察 语法to do research 作目的状语。

This time, they brought a ladder to the Second Step.

这一次，他们带了一架梯子到“第二台阶”。

词汇ladder 梯子；bring 带来 语法bring sth. to...。

Until 2007, almost all climbers from the northern side used this "Chinese Ladder" to reach the top much faster.

直到 2007 年，几乎所有从北侧攀登的登山者都使用这架“中国梯”，以便更快地到达顶峰。

词汇almost 几乎；use 使用；much faster 快得多 语法to reach 作目的状语；much 修饰比较级。

In 2020, a third team returned to Qomolangma to measure its height—8,848.86 metres.

2020 年，第三支队伍重返珠穆朗玛峰，测量它的高度——8848.86 米。

词汇return 返回；measure 测量；height 高度 语法to measure 作目的状语；破折号后补充数据。

Climbing Qomolangma is dangerous.

攀登珠穆朗玛峰是危险的。

词汇climbing 攀登；dangerous 危险的 语法动名词 Climbing 作主语。

Some climbers reach the top successfully, but many fail.

一些登山者成功登顶，但许多人失败了。

词汇successfully 成功地；fail 失败 语法but 转折。

Why do people still risk their lives to climb the highest mountain in the world?

为什么人们仍然冒着生命危险去攀登世界最高峰呢？

词汇risk one's life 冒生命危险；still 仍然 语法why 引导特殊疑问句；risk one's life to do sth.。

It is because human curiosity and ambition cannot be stopped.

这是因为人类的好奇心和雄心壮志无法被阻挡。

词汇curiosity 好奇心；ambition 雄心；stop 阻挡 语法It is because... 引导表语从句；cannot be stopped 被动语态。

Or, as the English explorer George Mallory put it, it is simply "because it's there"!

或者，正如英国探险家乔治·马洛里所说，仅仅是“因为它在那里”！

词汇explorer 探险家；simply 仅仅 语法as...put it 表示“正如……所说”；because 引导表语从句。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

文章介绍攀登珠穆朗玛峰。它是世界最伟大的奇迹之一，但只有最优秀的登山者才能登顶，而且要先挺过零下 30 度、稀薄空气、高悬崖和多变天气。1960 年以前没人从北侧登顶，登山者称那里为“死亡之路”，但中国登山队决心攀登。1960 年 5 月 24 日，刘连满、王富洲、屈银华、贡布到达海拔 8600 米的“第二台阶”，刘连满让队友踩他的肩膀，5 月 25 日凌晨中国国旗第一次飘扬在世界最高峰。1975 年登山队带去“中国梯”，到 2007 年几乎所有北侧登山者都用它；2020 年第三支队伍测得高度 8848.86 米。人们为什么冒险？因为好奇心与雄心无法被阻挡，或者如马洛里所说：“因为它在那里。”

English Summary

The article introduces climbing Mount Qomolangma. It is one of the world's greatest wonders, but only the best climbers reach the top—and only if they survive temperatures of -30°C, thin air, high cliffs and changeable weather. Before 1960 no one had reached the top from the northern side, and climbers called it the "Death Road", but a Chinese team was determined to try. On 24 May 1960 Liu Lianman, Wang Fuzhou, Qu Yinhua and Gong Bu reached the Second Step at 8,600 metres; Liu let his teammates step on his shoulders, and at 4:20 a.m. on 25 May the Chinese flag flew above the world's highest mountain for the first time. In 1975 another team brought a ladder, the "Chinese Ladder", used by almost all northern climbers until 2007; in 2020 a third team measured the height at 8,848.86 metres. People take the risk because curiosity and ambition cannot be stopped—or, as Mallory put it, "because it's there".

Section B 3a · The Great Barrier Reef

Section B 3a 主题：大堡礁 · 自然奇观广告

Do you love the ocean?

你喜欢海洋吗？

词汇ocean 海洋 语法一般现在时一般疑问句。

Do you want to visit one of the natural wonders of the world?

你想参观世界自然奇观之一吗？

词汇natural 自然的；wonder 奇观 语法one of the + 复数名词；want to do sth.。

If so, visit the Great Barrier Reef!

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是一则介绍大堡礁的广告。开头用问句吸引读者：你爱海洋吗？想参观世界自然奇观吗？大堡礁位于澳大利亚东北海岸，是最值得参观的神奇地方之一，也是世界上最大的珊瑚礁，占地 34.5 万平方公里，包括 900 多个岛屿和白色沙滩。它也是充满生机的：可以游泳、潜水、浮潜，观赏珊瑚、植物和成群的彩色鱼；潜得更深还能看到大鱼、海龟和鲨鱼。对热爱海洋的人，参观大堡礁是一生的梦想。

English Summary

This is an advertisement for the Great Barrier Reef. It opens with questions to attract readers: do you love the ocean, do you want to visit a natural wonder? Located on the northeastern coast of Australia, the reef is one of the most amazing places to visit and the biggest coral reef in the world, covering 345,000 square kilometres with over 900 islands and white sand beaches. It is also alive: visitors can swim, dive or snorkel and see coral, plants and schools of colourful fish; diving deeper, they might see large fish, turtles and sharks. For ocean lovers, visiting the reef is the dream of a lifetime.
