Section A · 3a: Peter and Yaming's Free Time

Section A · 3a 主题：兴趣爱好 · 书法与吉他

Peter: Hey Yaming!

嗨，Yaming！

词汇hey 嗨（招呼用语） 语法Hey 是随意的招呼语，后面可加对方名字。

Peter: What are you up to?

你在忙什么？

词汇be up to 正在忙…… 语法What are you up to? 是口语中询问对方“在做什么”的常见表达。

Yaming: Hi Peter.

嗨，Peter。

词汇hi 嗨 语法Hi 是常用的问候语。

Yaming: I'm doing calligraphy.

我在练书法。

词汇calligraphy 书法 语法现在进行时 be + doing，表示正在进行的动作。

Peter: It's beautiful!

太美了！

词汇beautiful 美丽的 语法It's = It is；It's + 形容词，对事物作出评价。

Peter: What are you writing?

你在写什么？

词汇write 写 语法现在进行时的特殊疑问句：疑问词 + be + 主语 + doing?。

Yaming: It's a poem by Li Bai.

是李白的一首诗。

词汇poem 诗；by 由……创作的 语法It's = It is；by Li Bai 表示“由李白创作的”。

Yaming: My grandmother taught me how to write it.

是我奶奶教我写的。

词汇grandmother 奶奶；teach 教 语法teach sb. how to do sth. 教某人如何做某事；teach 的过去式是 taught。

Peter: What's this character?

这个字是什么？

词汇character 汉字；字符 语法What's = What is；character 在此指“汉字”。

Yaming: It's yue.

是“月”字。

词汇yue 月（汉语拼音） 语法It's = It is；用拼音直接说明汉字的读音。

Yaming: It means "moon".

它的意思是“月亮”。

词汇mean 意思是 语法It means ... 表示“它的意思是……”；mean 后接名词。

Peter: It's nice!

真不错！

词汇nice 好的；不错的 语法It's + 形容词，对事物作出评价。

Peter: I like watching you write like that.

我喜欢看你那样写字。

词汇like doing sth. 喜欢做某事；watch sb. do sth. 看某人做某事 语法like doing sth.；watch sb. do sth. 中 do 用动词原形。

Peter: But it looks difficult.

但它看起来很难。

词汇look 看起来；difficult 困难的 语法look 是系动词，后接形容词作表语；but 表转折。

Yaming: Well, the first step is always the hardest.

嗯，万事开头难。

词汇first step 第一步；hardest 最难的 语法the + 形容词最高级表示“最……的”。

Yaming: When I started to do calligraphy, I spent hours practising single strokes.

我刚学书法时，花了好几个小时练习单个笔画。

词汇start to do sth. 开始做某事；spend 花费；practise 练习；stroke 笔画 语法when 引导时间状语从句；spend + 时间 + doing sth. 花时间做某事。

Peter: I know how that feels!

我知道那是什么感觉！

词汇know 知道；feel 感觉 语法how that feels 是宾语从句，作 know 的宾语。

Peter: My dad's teaching me to play the guitar.

我爸爸正在教我弹吉他。

词汇teach sb. to do sth. 教某人做某事；guitar 吉他 语法My dad's = My dad is；现在进行时表示正在进行的动作。

Peter: It's difficult, but he always encourages me not to give up.

这很难，但他总是鼓励我不要放弃。

词汇encourage 鼓励；give up 放弃 语法encourage sb. to do sth. 的否定形式是 encourage sb. not to do sth.。

Yaming: Exactly.

正是如此。

词汇exactly 完全正确；正是 语法Exactly. 用于赞同对方的说法。

Yaming: Now I do calligraphy to relax.

现在我练书法是为了放松。

词汇relax 放松 语法to relax 是不定式作目的状语。

Yaming: I even like how the ink smells!

我甚至喜欢墨汁的气味！

词汇even 甚至；ink 墨汁；smell 闻起来 语法how the ink smells 是宾语从句；smell 是系动词。

Yaming: Why did you choose to play the guitar?

你为什么选择弹吉他？

词汇choose 选择 语法Why + did + 主语 + 动词原形? 询问原因，用一般过去时。

Peter: I think playing the guitar is cool.

我觉得弹吉他比较酷。

词汇think 认为；cool 酷的 语法I think + 宾语从句；playing the guitar 是动名词短语作从句主语。

Peter: Will you teach me to write a poem?

你愿意教我写诗吗？

词汇will 愿意；teach sb. to do sth. 教某人做某事 语法Will you ...? 用于请求或询问对方意愿。

Peter: I can teach you to play a song in return.

作为回报，我可以教你弹一首歌。

词汇in return 作为回报 语法can + 动词原形；in return 是固定短语，表示“作为回报”。

Yaming: It's a deal!

一言为定！

词汇deal 交易；协议 语法It's a deal! 是口语表达，意为“成交、一言为定”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P4 Section A · 3a 中 Peter 和 Yaming 的对话（3a 要求：读对话，圈出两人空闲时间的活动）。Peter 见到 Yaming，问他在忙什么；Yaming 说自己正在练书法，写的是李白的诗，是奶奶教他写的。Peter 对“月”字很感兴趣，觉得书法很美，但看起来很难。Yaming 说万事开头难，自己刚开始练书法时花了很多时间练习单个笔画。Peter 深有同感，说爸爸正在教他弹吉他，虽然难，但爸爸总是鼓励他不要放弃。Yaming 说现在练书法是为了放松，他甚至喜欢墨汁的气味，并问 Peter 为什么选择弹吉他；Peter 觉得弹吉他很酷，还想请 Yaming 教他写诗，作为回报他教 Yaming 弹一首歌。Yaming 欣然同意：“一言为定！”

English Summary

This is the conversation between Peter and Yaming in Section A · 3a on page 4. In 3a, students read the conversation and circle Peter's and Yaming's free-time activities. Peter greets Yaming and asks what he is up to. Yaming says he is doing calligraphy; he is writing a poem by Li Bai, and his grandmother taught him how to write it. Peter asks about the character, and Yaming explains that it is yue, meaning "moon". Peter thinks it is nice and likes watching Yaming write, but he thinks it looks difficult. Yaming says the first step is always the hardest, and when he started calligraphy he spent hours practising single strokes. Peter knows how that feels: his dad is teaching him to play the guitar, which is difficult, but his dad always encourages him not to give up. Yaming says he now does calligraphy to relax and even likes how the ink smells; he asks why Peter chose to play the guitar, and Peter says he thinks playing the guitar is cool. Peter offers to teach Yaming to play a song if Yaming teaches him to write a poem, and Yaming agrees: "It's a deal!"

语法点 Grammar

What are you up to?

be up to 表示“正在忙什么”，是口语中常见的问候表达。

I'm doing calligraphy.

现在进行时 be + doing 表示正在进行的动作。

What are you writing?

现在进行时的特殊疑问句：疑问词 + be + 主语 + doing?。

It's a poem by Li Bai.

It's = It is；by 表示“由（某人）创作的”。

My grandmother taught me how to write it.

teach sb. how to do sth. 教某人如何做某事；teach 的过去式是 taught。

What's this character?

What's = What is；character 这里指“汉字”。

It's yue. It means "moon".

It means ... 表示“……的意思是”；mean 后接名词。

It's nice!

It's + 形容词，对事物作出评价。

I like watching you write like that.

like doing sth. 喜欢做某事；watch sb. do sth. 看某人做某事。

But it looks difficult.

look 是系动词，后接形容词作表语。

Well, the first step is always the hardest.

the + 形容词最高级表示“最……的”；这句话意为“万事开头难”。

When I started to do calligraphy, I spent hours practising single strokes.

when 引导时间状语从句；spend + 时间 + doing sth. 花时间做某事。

I know how that feels!

how that feels 是宾语从句，表示“那是什么感觉”。

My dad's teaching me to play the guitar.

My dad's = My dad is，现在进行时；teach sb. to do sth. 教某人做某事。

It's difficult, but he always encourages me not to give up.

encourage sb. to do sth. 鼓励某人做某事，否定式为 encourage sb. not to do sth.。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

Well, the first step is always the hardest.

嗯，万事开头难。

the + 形容词最高级 the hardest 表示“最难的”；这句话是英语中常见的鼓励用语。

My dad's teaching me to play the guitar.

我爸爸正在教我弹吉他。

My dad's 是 My dad is 的缩写，构成现在进行时；teach sb. to do sth. 表示“教某人做某事”。

It's difficult, but he always encourages me not to give up.

这很难，但他总是鼓励我不要放弃。

encourage sb. to do sth. 的否定形式是 encourage sb. not to do sth.；give up 表示“放弃”。

Section B 1b · Hobbies That Are Good for You

Section B 1b 主题：爱好与成长 · 事实与观点

When work or school is over, what do you do to pass the time?

工作或学习结束后，你会做什么来打发时间？

词汇be over 结束；pass the time 打发时间 语法when 引导时间状语从句；to pass the time 表示目的。

Do you spend time on hobbies you love?

你会把时间花在你热爱的爱好上吗？

词汇spend time on 把时间花在……上；hobby 爱好 语法spend time on sth. 表示“花时间在……上”；you love 是定语从句修饰 hobbies。

Reading or listening to music can help us to calm down after a busy day.

阅读或听音乐能帮助我们在忙碌一天后平静下来。

词汇calm down 平静下来 语法动名词短语作主语；help sb. (to) do sth. 帮助某人做某事。

Physical hobbies, such as playing tennis or doing yoga, can help us to keep fit.

身体类爱好，比如打网球或做瑜伽，能帮助我们保持健康。

词汇physical 身体的；such as 例如；keep fit 保持健康 语法such as 用于举例；keep fit 表示“保持健康”。

And some hobbies are creative.

有些爱好是有创造性的。

词汇creative 有创造性的 语法and 连接上文；creative 作表语。

For example, people sometimes paint pictures to remember a beautiful landscape or object, or reduce stress.

例如，人们有时画画来记住美丽的风景或物品，或者减轻压力。

词汇for example 例如；landscape 风景；object 物品；reduce stress 减轻压力 语法to do 不定式表示目的；or 连接两个动词。

Luca Bruno likes to be creative in his free time.

Luca Bruno 喜欢在空闲时间发挥创造力。

词汇be creative 有创造力；in one's free time 在某人空闲时间 语法like to do sth. 喜欢做某事。

But he doesn't make art or write stories.

但他并不搞美术，也不写故事。

词汇make art 搞美术；创作艺术 语法否定句中用 or 连接并列成分；but 表转折。

Instead, this Italian teenager finds it fun to make mobile phone apps.

相反，这个意大利青少年觉得制作手机应用程序很有趣。

词汇instead 相反；Italian 意大利的；teenager 青少年；app 应用程序 语法find it + 形容词 + to do sth. 中 it 是形式宾语。

He thinks that apps can be a great way to help people.

他认为应用程序可以是帮助人们的好方法。

词汇a way to do sth. 做某事的方法 语法thinks that ... 后接宾语从句；a way to do sth. 表示“做某事的方法”。

He started programming when he was ten, after he read about famous businessmen who started out as programmers.

他十岁开始编程，那是在他读到一些起初是程序员的著名商人的故事之后。

词汇program 编程；programmer 程序员；start out 起初 语法when 引导时间状语从句；who 引导定语从句修饰 businessmen。

His latest app allows people around the world to find study partners for learning Italian.

他最新的应用程序让世界各地的人找到学习意大利语的学习伙伴。

词汇latest 最新的；allow 允许；study partner 学习伙伴 语法allow sb. to do sth. 允许某人做某事；for learning 表示用途。

They can learn Italian and make friends at the same time!

他们可以学意大利语，同时交朋友！

词汇at the same time 同时 语法and 连接两个并列动作；at the same time 表示“同时”。

"It gives me such a sense of achievement to see people using the app," says Luca.

“看到人们使用这个应用程序，我很有成就感，”Luca 说。

词汇sense of achievement 成就感；see sb. doing sth. 看见某人正在做某事 语法It gives sb. sth. 双宾语结构；see sb. doing sth. 表示“看见某人正在做某事”。

"I believe I'm helping people around the world to learn Italian!"

“我相信我正在帮助世界各地的人学习意大利语！”

词汇believe 相信；help sb. to do sth. 帮助某人做某事 语法I believe + 宾语从句；现在进行时表示正在进行的动作。

Some people prefer not to make things.

有些人更喜欢不去制作东西。

词汇prefer to do sth. 更喜欢做某事 语法prefer to do sth. 的否定形式是 prefer not to do sth.。

They prefer to collect them!

他们更喜欢收集东西！

词汇collect 收集 语法them 指代上文的 things。

In India, Maya Badal doesn't collect coins or stamps.

在印度，Maya Badal 不收集硬币和邮票。

词汇coin 硬币；stamp 邮票 语法否定句中用 or；in India 表示地点。

Instead, this teenage girl collects postcards!

相反，这个十几岁的女孩收集明信片！

词汇postcard 明信片 语法instead 表示“相反”；this teenage girl 是主语的同位语。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P6 Section B 1b 的文章《对你有益的爱好》。文章说，工作或学习结束后，我们可以用爱好来打发时间：阅读或听音乐能帮助我们在忙碌一天后平静下来；运动类爱好（如打网球、做瑜伽）能帮我们保持健康；还有一些爱好是创造性的，比如有人画画来记住美丽的风景或物品，或减轻压力。接着文章介绍两个青少年的例子：意大利少年 Luca Bruno 喜欢创新，他不搞美术、不写故事，而是觉得做手机应用程序很有趣；他十岁开始编程，最新的应用程序能帮世界各地的人找到学习意大利语的学习伙伴，让大家学语言、交朋友两不误。他说看到人们使用这个应用程序很有成就感，相信自己在帮助世界各地的人学意大利语。最后文章提到，有些人更喜欢收集而不是制作，比如印度的 Maya Badal 不收集硬币和邮票，而是收集明信片。

English Summary

This is the article "Hobbies That Are Good for You" in Section B 1b on page 6. It says that when work or school is over, we can pass the time with hobbies: reading or listening to music can help us calm down after a busy day; physical hobbies such as playing tennis or doing yoga can help us keep fit; and some hobbies are creative — for example, people paint pictures to remember a beautiful landscape or object, or to reduce stress. The article then gives two teenagers' examples. Luca Bruno, an Italian teenager, likes to be creative in his free time, but he doesn't make art or write stories; instead, he finds it fun to make mobile phone apps. He started programming when he was ten, after reading about famous businessmen who started out as programmers, and his latest app allows people around the world to find study partners for learning Italian so that they can learn Italian and make friends at the same time. He says seeing people use the app gives him a sense of achievement and that he believes he is helping people around the world learn Italian. Finally, the article says some people prefer to collect things rather than make them: in India, Maya Badal doesn't collect coins or stamps; instead, this teenage girl collects postcards!

They keep you fit, and they can even lower stress and improve your memory and concentration.

keep sb. + 形容词；and 连接并列谓语。

Some scientists believe that making music can lower our blood pressure and make our body stronger.

believe that ... 后接宾语从句；make our body stronger 中 stronger 作宾语补足语。

This makes it harder for us to fall sick.

make it + 形容词 + for sb. to do sth. 中 it 是形式宾语。

So, if you think that playing the guitar is a waste of time, remember that this hobby is actually a great way to stay healthy!

if 引导条件状语从句；think that ... 和 remember that ... 后接宾语从句。

Hobbies are also good for your emotional well-being.

be good for 表示“对……有好处”。

Creative hobbies like painting or writing can help you deal with negative emotions by giving you a way to express how you feel.

by doing sth. 表示方式；how you feel 是宾语从句。

In one study, after just 45 minutes of making art, most of the participants reported feeling more confident in themselves!

after 引导时间状语；report doing sth. 表示“报告做了某事”。

However, while hobbies have many benefits, it is important to find the right balance.

while 表示“虽然”；it is important to do sth. 句型。

Playing computer games or chatting online may be fun, but such hobbies usually involve a lot of screen time.

动名词短语作主语；involve + 名词表示“包含、涉及”。

That could cause problems such as eye strain or poor sleep.

cause 表示“引起”；such as 用于举例。

Why not choose an outdoor hobby instead?

Why not do sth.? 用于提出建议；instead 表示“代替、而”。

Hanging out with friends reduces loneliness and can be a good way to build relationships.

动名词短语作主语；a way to do sth. 表示“做某事的方法”。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

This makes it harder for us to fall sick.

这让我们更不容易生病。

make it + 形容词 + for sb. to do sth. 中 it 是形式宾语，真正宾语是 to fall sick；fall sick 表示“生病”。

So, if you think that playing the guitar is a waste of time, remember that this hobby is actually a great way to stay healthy!

所以，如果你认为弹吉他是浪费时间，请记住这项爱好其实是保持健康的好方法！

if 引导条件状语从句；a waste of time 表示“浪费时间”；a great way to do sth. 表示“做某事的好方法”。

Hobbies are also good for your emotional well-being.

爱好对你的情绪健康也有好处。

be good for 表示“对……有好处”；emotional well-being 是固定表达，意为“情绪健康”。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看 P81 的文章标题 Work Hard, Play Hard，猜一猜文章会讲什么。
  • 先浏览全文，找出文章的大致结构（身体、情绪、平衡）。

读中

  • 边读边画出表示“好处”和“问题”的关键词。
  • 找出文中表示建议的句子（Why not ...?）。

读后

  • 用表格整理爱好的好处和需要注意的问题。
  • 结合文章，写 3—5 句话说明自己会怎样平衡学习和爱好。

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面 4 篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What is Yaming doing when Peter sees him?

  1. Playing the guitar.
  2. Reading a poem.
  3. Doing calligraphy.
  4. Writing an email.
答案与解析

C 原文：Yaming: I'm doing calligraphy. 可知 Peter 见到 Yaming 时，Yaming 正在练书法。

2. Who taught Yaming how to write the poem?

  1. His grandmother.
  2. His father.
  3. His teacher.
  4. Peter.
答案与解析

A 原文：My grandmother taught me how to write it. 可知是 Yaming 的奶奶教他写这首诗的。

3. What does Luca Bruno like to make in his free time?

  1. Games for children.
  2. Mobile phone apps.
  3. Music videos.
  4. Shopping apps.
答案与解析

B 原文：Instead, this Italian teenager finds it fun to make mobile phone apps. 可知 Luca 喜欢制作手机应用程序。

4. What does Luca's latest app help people do?

  1. Learn English.
  2. Make new friends only.
  3. Find famous businessmen.
  4. Find study partners for learning Italian.
答案与解析

D 原文：His latest app allows people around the world to find study partners for learning Italian. 可知这个应用程序帮人们找到学习意大利语的学习伙伴。

5. What is Shuyin's favourite free-time activity?

  1. Horse riding.
  2. Painting.
  3. Writing poems.
  4. Reading.
答案与解析

A 原文：But my favourite free-time activity is horse riding. 可知 Shuyin 最喜欢的空闲活动是骑马。

6. What does Shuyin bring Nini for a snack?

  1. Some apples.
  2. Some grass.
  3. Some carrots.
  4. Some bread.
答案与解析

C 原文：especially when I bring her some carrots for a snack! 可知 Shuyin 给 Nini 带胡萝卜当零食。

7. According to the Reading Plus article, what can physical hobbies do?

  1. Make us feel lonely.
  2. Waste our time.
  3. Cause eye strain.
  4. Keep us fit and lower stress.
答案与解析

D 原文：They keep you fit, and they can even lower stress and improve your memory and concentration. 可知身体类爱好能让人保持健康、减轻压力。

8. What problem can playing computer games or chatting online cause?

  1. Too much outdoor time.
  2. Eye strain or poor sleep.
  3. Making more friends.
  4. Better memory and concentration.
答案与解析

B 原文：That could cause problems such as eye strain or poor sleep. 可知这类爱好可能导致眼睛疲劳或睡眠不好。
