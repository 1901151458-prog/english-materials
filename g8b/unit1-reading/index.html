Section A 3a · Calligraphy and Guitar

Section A 3a 主题：爱好 · 对话

Peter: Hey Yaming! What are you up to?

嘿，亚明！你在忙什么呢？

词汇be up to 忙于；从事 语法What are you up to? 是口语中询问“你在做什么”的表达。

Yaming: Hi Peter. I'm doing calligraphy.

嗨，彼得。我正在写书法。

词汇calligraphy 书法 语法现在进行时 am doing 表示正在进行的动作。

Peter: It's beautiful! What are you writing?

真漂亮！你在写什么？

词汇beautiful 漂亮的；write 写 语法What are you writing? 现在进行时的特殊疑问句。

Yaming: It's a poem by Li Bai. My grandmother taught me how to write it.

是李白的一首诗。我奶奶教我写的。

词汇poem 诗；by 由……创作；teach sb. how to do 教某人如何做 语法teach sb. how to do sth.；by Li Bai 表示作者。

Peter: What's this character?

这个字是什么？

词汇character 汉字；字符 语法What's...? 询问身份/内容。

Yaming: It's yue. It means "moon".

是“月”字。意思是“月亮”。

词汇mean 意思是；moon 月亮 语法It means... 表示“它的意思是……”。

Peter: It's nice! I like watching you write like that. But it looks difficult.

很不错！我喜欢看你那样写字。但这看起来很难。

词汇like doing 喜欢做；watch sb. do 看某人做；difficult 困难的 语法like doing sth.；watch sb. do sth.；look + adj.。

Yaming: Well, the first step is always the hardest. When I started to do calligraphy, I spent hours practising single strokes.

嗯，万事开头难。我刚开始写书法的时候，花了好几个小时练习单笔划。

词汇the first step 第一步；hardest 最难的；spend time doing 花时间做；stroke 笔画 语法the first step is the hardest；when 引导时间状语从句；spend time doing。

Peter: I know how that feels! My dad's teaching me to play the guitar. It's difficult, but he always encourages me not to give up.

我懂那种感觉！我爸爸在教我弹吉他。很难，但他总是鼓励我不要放弃。

词汇feel 感觉；teach sb. to do 教某人做；encourage 鼓励；give up 放弃 语法how that feels 是宾语从句；encourage sb. not to do sth.；give up。

Yaming: Exactly. Now I do calligraphy to relax. I even like how the ink smells! Why did you choose to play the guitar?

确实如此。现在我写书法是为了放松。我甚至喜欢墨水的味道！你为什么选择弹吉他？

词汇exactly 确实；relax 放松；ink 墨水；smell 闻起来；choose 选择 语法to relax 作目的状语；how the ink smells 是宾语从句；why 引导特殊疑问句。

Peter: I think playing the guitar is cool. Will you teach me to write a poem? I can teach you to play a song in return.

我觉得弹吉他酷。你能教我写诗吗？作为回报，我可以教你弹一首歌。

词汇cool 酷的；in return 作为回报；teach sb. to do 教某人做 语法I think 后接宾语从句；play the guitar；in return 作状语。

Yaming: It's a deal!

就这么定了！

词汇deal 交易；约定 语法It's a deal! 是口语中表示“成交/一言为定”的表达。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这段对话围绕 Peter 和 Yaming 的业余爱好展开。Peter 看到 Yaming 在写书法，称赞她写得好，并询问她写的内容；Yaming 说这是李白的诗，是奶奶教她写的，还解释了“月”字的意思。Yaming 说万事开头难，自己初学时花很多时间练单笔划；Peter 也有同感，因为爸爸正在教他弹吉他，虽然难，但爸爸鼓励他不要放弃。Yaming 写书法是为了放松，Peter 觉得弹吉他酷；两人约定互相教对方写诗和弹歌。

English Summary

The conversation is about Peter's and Yaming's hobbies. Peter sees Yaming doing calligraphy and asks what she is writing; Yaming says it is a poem by Li Bai, taught by her grandmother, and explains the character "yue". Yaming says the first step is always the hardest and that she spent hours practising single strokes. Peter feels the same because his dad is teaching him guitar; it is difficult, but his dad encourages him not to give up. Yaming does calligraphy to relax, and Peter thinks playing the guitar is cool. They agree to teach each other to write a poem and play a song.

Section B 1b · Hobbies That Are Good for You

Section B 1b 主题：有益爱好 · 事实与观点

When work or school is over, what do you do to pass the time?

当工作或学校的学习结束后，你会做些什么来打发时间？

词汇be over 结束；pass the time 打发时间 语法when 引导时间状语从句；to pass 作目的状语。

Do you spend time on hobbies you love?

你会把时间花在自己喜爱的爱好上吗？

词汇spend time on 在……上花时间；hobby 爱好 语法spend time on sth.；you love 是省略关系词的定语从句。

Reading or listening to music can help us to calm down after a busy day.

阅读或听音乐能帮助我们在忙碌的一天后平静下来。

词汇reading 阅读；calm down 平静下来；busy 忙碌的 语法动名词 Reading / listening 作主语；help sb. to do sth.。

Physical hobbies, such as playing tennis or doing yoga, can help us to keep fit.

体育类爱好，比如打网球或做瑜伽，能帮助我们保持健康。

词汇physical 身体的；tennis 网球；yoga 瑜伽；keep fit 保持健康 语法such as 举例；help sb. to do sth.；keep fit。

And some hobbies are creative.

还有一些爱好是富有创造性的。

词汇creative 有创造性的 语法主系表结构。

For example, people sometimes paint pictures to remember a beautiful landscape or object, or reduce stress.

例如，人们有时会画画，以此来记住美丽的风景或事物，或者缓解压力。

词汇for example 例如；paint 画；landscape 风景；reduce stress 缓解压力 语法to remember...or reduce... 作目的状语；or 连接并列目的。

Luca Bruno likes to be creative in his free time. But he doesn't make art or write stories.

卢卡·布鲁诺喜欢在空闲时间进行创造性活动。但他不进行艺术创作或写故事。

词汇be creative 有创造性；free time 空闲时间；make art 创作艺术 语法like to do sth.；but 转折；doesn't 否定。

Instead, this Italian teenager finds it fun to make mobile phone apps.

相反，这位意大利少年觉得制作手机应用程序很有趣。

词汇instead 相反；Italian 意大利的；teenager 青少年；app 应用程序 语法find it + adj. + to do sth.；it 是形式宾语。

He thinks that apps can be a great way to help people.

他认为应用程序可以成为帮助人们的好方式。

词汇think 认为；a great way to do 做……的好方式 语法that 引导宾语从句；a way to do sth.。

He started programming when he was ten, after he read about famous businessmen who started out as programmers.

他十岁时就开始编程了，那是在他了解到一些著名商人最初是程序员之后。

词汇programming 编程；famous 著名的；businessman 商人；start out 开始 语法when/after 引导时间状语从句；who started out... 是定语从句。

His latest app allows people around the world to find study partners for learning Italian.

他最新的应用程序能让世界各地的人找到学习意大利语的学习伙伴。

词汇latest 最新的；allow 允许；study partner 学习伙伴 语法allow sb. to do sth.；for learning 作目的/用途。

They can learn Italian and make friends at the same time!

他们可以同时学习意大利语和交朋友！

词汇at the same time 同时 语法can do；and 连接并列谓语。

"It gives me such a sense of achievement to see people using the app," says Luca.

卢卡说：“看到人们使用这个应用程序，我有一种很强的成就感。”

词汇sense of achievement 成就感；see sb. doing 看到某人正在做 语法It gives sb. sth. to do sth.；see sb. doing sth.。

"I believe I'm helping people around the world to learn Italian!"

“我相信我正在帮助世界各地的人学习意大利语！”

词汇believe 相信；help sb. to do 帮助某人做 语法I believe 后接宾语从句；help sb. to do sth.。

Some people prefer not to make things. They prefer to collect them!

有些人更喜欢收集东西，而不是制作东西！

词汇prefer 更喜欢；collect 收集 语法prefer to do rather than do 的省略表达。

In India, Maya Badal doesn't collect coins or stamps. Instead, this teenage girl collects postcards!

在印度，玛雅·巴达尔不收集硬币或邮票。相反，这个十几岁的女孩收集明信片！

词汇India 印度；coin 硬币；stamp 邮票；postcard 明信片 语法doesn't collect；instead 表示转折。

"I know some people think postcards are rather old-fashioned, but I think they're a good way to learn about different places," Maya says.

玛雅说：“我知道有些人认为明信片相当过时，但我认为它们是了解不同地方的好方式。”

词汇old-fashioned 过时的；learn about 了解 语法some people think 后接宾语从句；but 转折。

"I think it's interesting to see the beautiful buildings and landscapes in foreign countries."

“我觉得欣赏外国美丽的建筑和风景很有趣。”

词汇building 建筑；landscape 风景；foreign 外国的 语法I think 后接宾语从句；it is + adj. + to do sth.。

Maya's first postcard shows the Mogao Caves in Dunhuang.

玛雅的第一张明信片展示的是敦煌莫高窟。

词汇Mogao Caves 莫高窟；show 展示 语法一般现在时；show sth.。

Her dad sent it to her when she was five, and she liked it so much that she put it up on her bedroom wall!

她五岁时，爸爸送给了她这张明信片，她非常喜欢，就把它贴在了卧室的墙上！

词汇send sth. to sb. 把某物寄给某人；put up 张贴；so...that... 如此……以至于…… 语法send sth. to sb.；so...that... 引导结果状语从句。

Now there are over one hundred postcards from thirty-two countries on Maya's walls.

现在，玛雅的墙上有来自 32 个国家的一百多张明信片。

词汇over 超过；country 国家 语法There be 句型；from... 作后置定语。

She dreams of travelling to these places in the future!

她梦想着未来能去这些地方旅行！

词汇dream of 梦想；travel 旅行；in the future 未来 语法dream of doing sth.；in the future 作时间状语。

中文大意

文章介绍有益的爱好。工作或学习结束后，阅读、听音乐能让人平静，体育类爱好能保持健康，创造性爱好如画画能记住风景或缓解压力。文章举了两个青少年的例子：意大利少年 Luca 喜欢做手机应用程序，十岁开始编程，他的应用帮助世界各地的人找学习意大利语的伙伴，他从中获得成就感；印度女孩 Maya 喜欢收集明信片，第一张是爸爸送的敦煌莫高窟明信片，现在墙上有来自 32 个国家的一百多张，她梦想去这些地方旅行。

Luca, an Italian teenager, enjoys making mobile phone apps; he started programming at ten, and his app helps people around the world find partners to learn Italian, giving him a sense of achievement. Section B 3a · My Favourite Free-time Activity

Section B 3a 主题：骑马 · 论坛帖子

Jason: Hi, everyone! I'd like to take up a new free-time activity, and I'm hoping to get some ideas.

大家好！我想培养一项新的业余活动，希望能得到一些想法。

词汇take up 开始从事；free-time activity 业余活动；hope 希望 语法would like to do；hope to do；and 连接并列谓语。

Jason: What do you do? Why do you like it? Please tell me more!

你们都做些什么？为什么喜欢它？请多跟我讲讲！

词汇why 为什么；tell 告诉 语法特殊疑问句；祈使句 Please tell me more!。

Shuyin: I have many free-time activities, like painting, writing poems, and reading.

我有很多业余活动，比如绘画、写诗和阅读。

词汇painting 绘画；poem 诗；reading 阅读 语法like 举例；动名词并列作宾语。

Shuyin: But my favourite free-time activity is horse riding.

但我最喜欢的业余活动是骑马。

词汇favourite 最喜欢的；horse riding 骑马 语法my favourite... is... 主系表结构。

Shuyin: When I was six, I visited my grandfather's farm, and he took me to ride a horse.

我六岁的时候，去了爷爷的农场，他带我去骑马。

词汇farm 农场；take sb. to do 带某人做 语法when 引导时间状语从句；take sb. to do sth.。

Shuyin: It was such an amazing experience!

那是一次非常棒的经历！

词汇such 如此；amazing 令人惊叹的；experience 经历 语法such a/an + adj. + 名词；感叹句。

Shuyin: Now I do it at his farm every weekend with my favourite horse, Nini.

现在我每个周末都会去他的农场，和我最喜欢的马妮妮一起骑马。

词汇every weekend 每个周末；favourite 最喜欢的 语法一般现在时表示习惯；with... 作伴随状语。

Shuyin: I love horse riding because I enjoy being in nature.

我喜欢骑马，因为我喜欢置身于大自然中。

词汇enjoy doing 喜欢做；nature 大自然 语法because 引导原因状语从句；enjoy + 动名词。

Shuyin: Horse riding also helps me stay fit.

骑马也有助于我保持健康。

词汇help sb. do 帮助某人做；stay fit 保持健康 语法help sb. do sth.；stay fit。

Shuyin: But most importantly, it lets me spend time with Nini!

但最重要的是，它能让我和妮妮共度时光！

词汇most importantly 最重要的是；let sb. do 让某人做；spend time with 和……共度时光 语法let sb. do sth.；spend time with sb.。

Shuyin: She's my best friend.

她是我最好的朋友。

词汇best friend 最好的朋友 语法主系表结构。

Shuyin: She's happy to see me whenever I visit, especially when I bring her some carrots for a snack!

每次我去看她，她都很高兴，尤其是当我给她带些胡萝卜当零食的时候！

词汇whenever 无论何时；especially 尤其；bring 带来；snack 零食 语法whenever 引导时间状语从句；when 引导时间状语从句。

Shuyin: I'm lucky to have her in my life.

我的生活中有她，真的很幸运。

词汇lucky 幸运的 语法be lucky to do sth.；have sb. in one's life。

这是一个论坛帖子。Jason 想培养一项新的业余活动，向大家征求建议；Shuyin 分享了自己的经历。她有绘画、写诗、阅读等爱好，但最喜欢骑马。六岁时她去爷爷的农场，爷爷带她骑马，那是很棒的经历；现在她每个周末都和最喜欢的马 Nini 一起骑马。她喜欢骑马是因为能亲近自然、保持健康，最重要的是能和 Nini 共度时光；Nini 是她最好的朋友。

This is a forum post. Jason wants to take up a new free-time activity and asks for ideas. Shuyin shares her experience: she has many hobbies such as painting, writing poems and reading, but her favourite is horse riding.
