Section A · 3a: Talking About a Book

Section A · 3a 主题：读书分享 · 奇幻文学

Peter: What have you read recently, Fu Xing?

你最近读了什么书，Fu Xing？

词汇recently 最近；read 读 语法现在完成时：have + 过去分词，询问最近读过的书。

Fu Xing: I've just finished Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll.

我刚读完 Lewis Carroll 的《爱丽丝漫游奇境记》。

词汇finish 完成；by 由……所著 语法现在完成时 have + 过去分词；just 表示“刚刚”。

Fu Xing: It's a fantasy classic about a girl who gets lost in a magical world.

这是一部关于一个女孩迷失在魔法世界的奇幻经典。

词汇fantasy 奇幻；classic 经典作品；magical 神奇的 语法who gets lost in a magical world 是定语从句，修饰 girl。

Fu Xing: Have you ever read it, Peter?

你读过它吗，Peter？

词汇ever 曾经 语法现在完成时的一般疑问句；ever 常用于疑问句，表示“曾经”。

Peter: I've never read it, but I've watched the movie.

我从没读过它，但我看过电影。

词汇never 从未；movie 电影 语法never 用于现在完成时表示“从未”；but 表转折。

Fu Xing: You should read the book too!

你也应该读读这本书！

词汇should 应该 语法should + 动词原形表示建议。

Fu Xing: I've always preferred the book to the movie.

我一直觉得书比电影更好。

词汇prefer 更喜欢 语法prefer A to B 表示“比起 B 更喜欢 A”。

Peter: I will!

我会的！

词汇will 将会 语法I will. 用于回应建议，表示“我会的”。

Peter: What's your favourite part of the book?

你最喜欢书中的哪一部分？

词汇favourite part 最喜欢的部分 语法What's + 形容词性物主代词 + favourite part 询问最喜欢的部分。

Fu Xing: Do you remember when Alice finds a tiny door?

你还记得爱丽丝发现一扇小门的时候吗？

词汇remember 记得；tiny 极小的；door 门 语法remember + 宾语从句；when 引导时间状语从句。

Fu Xing: She drinks from a bottle and grows smaller ...

她喝下瓶子里的东西，变小了……

词汇drink 喝；bottle 瓶子；grow smaller 变小 语法一般现在时叙述故事情节；grow + 形容词表示“变得……”。

Peter: I remember!

我记得！

词汇remember 记得 语法简短的肯定回答，表示记得对方说的事情。

Peter: Then she realizes she's forgotten the key on a table, but she's grown too small to reach it.

然后她意识到自己把钥匙忘在桌子上了，可她已经变得太小，够不到了。

词汇realize 意识到；forget 忘记；key 钥匙；reach 够到 语法realize 后接宾语从句；too ... to ... 表示“太……而不能……”。

Fu Xing: Yes, then she eats a magic cake and grows bigger!

对，然后她吃了一块魔法蛋糕，又变大了！

词汇magic 有魔力的；cake 蛋糕；grow bigger 变大 语法eat 的第三人称单数是 eats；and 连接两个并列谓语。

Peter: I guess you really love this book!

我猜你真的很喜欢这本书！

词汇guess 猜 语法I guess + 宾语从句，表示推测。

Fu Xing: Yes.

是的。

词汇yes 是的 语法Yes. 是对上文的肯定回答。

Fu Xing: I've always loved reading fantasy books.

我一直喜欢读奇幻书。

词汇always 一直；fantasy books 奇幻书 语法现在完成时表示从过去持续到现在的状态；love doing sth. 喜欢做某事。

Fu Xing: And this story is about growing up.

而且这个故事讲的是成长。

词汇grow up 成长 语法be about 表示“关于”；growing up 是动名词短语作介词宾语。

Fu Xing: I find it interesting.

我觉得它很有趣。

词汇find 觉得；interesting 有趣的 语法find + 宾语 + 形容词，表示“觉得……是……的”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P64 Section A · 3a 中 Peter 和 Fu Xing 的对话（3a 要求：读对话，弄清谁读过这本书、谁看过电影）。Peter 问 Fu Xing 最近读了什么；Fu Xing 说她刚读完 Lewis Carroll 的《爱丽丝漫游奇境记》，这是一部关于一个女孩迷失在魔法世界的奇幻经典，并问 Peter 读过没有。Peter 说没读过但看过电影；Fu Xing 建议他也读读这本书，说自己总是更喜欢书而不是电影。Peter 答应会读，并问 Fu Xing 最喜欢书中的哪一部分。Fu Xing 问他是否记得爱丽丝发现一扇小门、喝下瓶子里的水变小的地方；Peter 说记得，然后她发现钥匙忘在桌上，可她已经变得太小够不到了。Fu Xing 说对，然后她吃了一块魔法蛋糕又变大了。Peter 猜 Fu Xing 真的很喜欢这本书；Fu Xing 说对，她一直喜欢读奇幻书，这个故事讲的是成长，她觉得很有趣。

English Summary

This is the conversation between Peter and Fu Xing in Section A · 3a on page 64; in 3a, students read the conversation to find out who has read the book and who has watched the movie. Peter asks Fu Xing what she has read recently. Fu Xing says she has just finished Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll — a fantasy classic about a girl who gets lost in a magical world — and asks Peter whether he has ever read it. Peter says he has never read it but has watched the movie. Fu Xing tells him he should read the book too, because she has always preferred the book to the movie. Peter agrees, and asks what her favourite part of the book is. Fu Xing asks whether he remembers when Alice finds a tiny door, drinks from a bottle and grows smaller; Peter remembers, and says that then she realizes she has forgotten the key on a table but has grown too small to reach it. Fu Xing adds that Alice then eats a magic cake and grows bigger. Peter guesses that she really loves the book, and Fu Xing says she has always loved reading fantasy books; the story is about growing up, and she finds it interesting.

What have you read recently, Fu Xing?

现在完成时：have/has + 过去分词，询问最近读过的书。

I've just finished Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll.

现在完成时 have + 过去分词；just 表示“刚刚”；by 表示“由……所著”。

It's a fantasy classic about a girl who gets lost in a magical world.

who gets lost in a magical world 是定语从句，修饰 girl。

Have you ever read it, Peter?

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I've always preferred the book to the movie.

我一直觉得书比电影更好。

prefer A to B 表示“比起 B 更喜欢 A”，to 是介词，后接名词或动名词。

Do you remember when Alice finds a tiny door?

你还记得爱丽丝发现一扇小门的时候吗？

remember 后可接宾语从句；when 引导时间状语从句，用一般现在时叙述故事。

Then she realizes she's forgotten the key on a table, but she's grown too small to reach it.

然后她意识到自己把钥匙忘在桌子上了，可她已经变得太小，够不到了。

realize 后接宾语从句，从句用现在完成时；too ... to ... 表示“太……而不能……”。

Section B 1b · The Secret Garden

Section B 1b 主题：经典阅读 · 成长与友谊

In this story, Mary Lennox, a spoiled and selfish 10-year-old girl goes to live with her uncle, Mr Craven, after her parents pass away.

故事中，被宠坏、自私的 10 岁女孩 Mary Lennox 在父母去世后，去和叔叔 Mr Craven 一起生活。

词汇spoiled 被宠坏的；selfish 自私的；uncle 叔叔；pass away 去世 语法after 引导时间状语从句；go to live with 表示“去和……一起生活”。

Her uncle has a huge house, but he is seldom there.

她叔叔有一栋大房子，但他很少在那里。

词汇huge 巨大的；seldom 很少 语法has 表示“有”；seldom 是频度副词；but 表转折。

Mary spends a lot of time alone and bored.

Mary 常常独自一人，感到无聊。

词汇spend 花费；alone 独自；bored 感到无聊的 语法spend + 时间；alone 与 bored 描述状态。

At night she often hears crying coming from somewhere.

夜里她常常听到不知从哪里传来的哭声。

词汇crying 哭声；somewhere 某处 语法hear sth. doing sth. 表示“听到……正在做某事”。

One day, Mary hears about a beautiful garden.

一天，Mary 听说了一座美丽的花园。

词汇hear about 听说；garden 花园 语法hear about 表示“听说”。

It belonged to her uncle's wife.

它属于她叔叔的妻子。

词汇belong to 属于 语法belong to 表示“属于”，通常不用进行时。

After she died, the garden became a painful memory for him, so he locked it up.

她去世后，这座花园成了他痛苦的回忆，于是他把花园锁了起来。

词汇painful 痛苦的；memory 回忆；lock up 锁起来 语法after 引导时间状语从句；so 表示结果。

No one has seen it for ten years.

十年间没有人见过它。

词汇no one 没有人 语法现在完成时 has seen；for ten years 表示持续时间。

One morning, Mary finds an old key.

一天早上，Mary 找到一把旧钥匙。

词汇key 钥匙 语法一般现在时叙述故事情节；find 的第三人称单数为 finds。

When it unlocks a half-hidden door, she discovers the secret garden inside!

当钥匙打开一扇半掩的门时，她发现了里面的秘密花园！

词汇unlock 打开（锁）；half-hidden 半掩的；discover 发现 语法when 引导时间状语从句；unlock 表示“打开（锁）”。

Unfortunately, nobody has taken care of the plants, and therefore, much of it is in a bad state.

不幸的是，没有人照料这些植物，因此花园的大部分都状态很差。

词汇unfortunately 不幸的是；take care of 照顾；therefore 因此 语法现在完成时 has taken care of；therefore 表示“因此”。

Later, Mary tells her secret to a new friend, Dickon.

后来，Mary 把自己的秘密告诉新朋友 Dickon。

词汇later 后来；secret 秘密 语法tell sb. sth. 告诉某人某事。

The young boy knows a lot about gardening, and they work together to make the garden beautiful again.

这个男孩很懂园艺，他们一起努力让花园重新变美。

词汇know a lot about 对……了解很多；gardening 园艺 语法know a lot about 表示“对……了解很多”；make + 宾语 + 形容词。

One night, Mary hears someone crying again and discovers Colin, her cousin.

一天晚上，Mary 又听到有人哭泣，发现了她的表弟 Colin。

词汇someone 某人；cousin 表兄弟姐妹 语法hear sb. doing sth. 听到某人正在做某事。

He is unhappy and weak because he has lived alone in a hidden room since his mother's death.

他孤独、虚弱，因为母亲去世后他一直独自住在隐蔽的房间里。

词汇unhappy 不快乐的；weak 虚弱的；hidden 隐蔽的 语法because 引导原因状语从句；现在完成时 has lived；since 表示“自从”。

He has not walked for years because he thinks he has an illness.

他多年没有走路，因为他以为自己有病。

词汇illness 疾病 语法现在完成时 has not walked；because 引导原因状语从句。

Mary and Dickon tell him stories about the garden and take him secretly to see it.

Mary 和 Dickon 给他讲花园的故事，还偷偷带他去看花园。

词汇secretly 秘密地 语法tell sb. stories 给某人讲故事；take sb. to do sth. 带某人去做某事。

Over time, Colin grows stronger.

渐渐地，Colin 变得强壮起来。

词汇over time 随着时间的推移；stronger 更强壮的 语法grow + 形容词比较级表示“变得……”。

The fresh air and beautiful garden are good for him, and he proves that he is not ill when he gets to his feet for the first time in years!

新鲜空气和美丽的花园对他很有好处，他多年后第一次站起来，证明自己并没有病！

词汇fresh air 新鲜空气；prove 证明；get to one's feet 站起来 语法be good for 表示“对……有好处”；when 引导时间状语从句。

The three friends spend time together in the garden and grow happier day by day.

三个朋友在花园里一起度过时光，一天天变得更快乐。

词汇day by day 一天天地；happier 更快乐的 语法spend time together 一起度过时光；grow + 比较级表示“变得……”。

In the end, Mary's uncle returns home.

最后，Mary 的叔叔回家了。

词汇in the end 最后；return home 回家 语法in the end 表示“最后”；return home 表示“回家”。

To his great surprise, he sees Colin walking and running, and he discovers that the secret garden has become as alive and beautiful as it ever was!

令他大为惊讶的是，他看到 Colin 在走、在跑，并发现秘密花园变得像从前一样生机勃勃、美丽！

词汇to one's surprise 令某人惊讶的是；alive 有生气的 语法to one's surprise 表示“令某人惊讶的是”；as + 形容词 + as 表示“和……一样”。

中英文全文大意 Summary

中文大意

这是教材 P66 Section B 1b 中《秘密花园》的故事梗概（改编自 Frances Hodgson Burnett 的同名小说）。故事中，被宠坏、自私的 10 岁女孩 Mary Lennox 在父母去世后去和叔叔 Mr Craven 一起生活。叔叔有一栋大房子，但他很少在那里，Mary 常常独自一人、感到无聊；夜里她常听到不知从哪里传来的哭声。一天，Mary 听说有一座美丽的花园，那是叔叔妻子的花园；她去世后，花园成了叔叔痛苦的回忆，于是他把花园锁了起来，十年没人见过它。一天早上，Mary 找到一把旧钥匙；她用钥匙打开一扇半掩的门，发现了里面的秘密花园！不幸的是，没人照料植物，所以花园大部分地方状态很差。后来，Mary 把秘密告诉新朋友 Dickon；这个男孩很懂园艺，他们一起努力让花园重新变美。一天晚上，Mary 又听到哭声，发现了表弟 Colin。他孤独、虚弱，因为母亲去世后他一直独自住在隐蔽的房间里；他多年没有走路，因为他以为自己有病。Mary 和 Dickon 给他讲花园的故事，还偷偷带他去看花园。渐渐地，Colin 变得强壮了；新鲜空气和美丽的花园对他很有好处，他多年后第一次站起来，证明自己并没有病。三个朋友在花园里一起度过时光，一天天变得更快乐。最后，Mary 的叔叔回家，惊讶地看到 Colin 在走、在跑，并发现秘密花园变得像从前一样生机勃勃、美丽！

English Summary

This is the story summary of The Secret Garden in Section B 1b on page 66 (adapted from Frances Hodgson Burnett's novel). In the story, Mary Lennox, a spoiled and selfish 10-year-old girl, goes to live with her uncle, Mr Craven, after her parents pass away. Her uncle has a huge house but is seldom there, and Mary spends a lot of time alone and bored; at night she often hears crying coming from somewhere. One day Mary hears about a beautiful garden that belonged to her uncle's wife. After she died, the garden became a painful memory for him, so he locked it up, and no one has seen it for ten years. One morning Mary finds an old key; when it unlocks a half-hidden door, she discovers the secret garden inside. Unfortunately, nobody has taken care of the plants, so much of it is in a bad state. Later, Mary tells her secret to a new friend, Dickon; the young boy knows a lot about gardening, and they work together to make the garden beautiful again. One night Mary hears someone crying again and discovers Colin, her cousin, who is unhappy and weak because he has lived alone in a hidden room since his mother's death. He has not walked for years because he thinks he has an illness. Mary and Dickon tell him stories about the garden and take him secretly to see it. Over time Colin grows stronger; the fresh air and beautiful garden are good for him, and he proves that he is not ill when he gets to his feet for the first time in years. The three friends spend time together in the garden and grow happier day by day. In the end Mary's uncle returns home; to his great surprise, he sees Colin walking and running, and discovers that the secret garden has become as alive and beautiful as it ever was!

中文大意

这是教材 P91–P92 两页 Reading Plus 的剧本《The Boy and the Elf》（改编自 Selma Lagerlöf 的《尼尔斯骑鹅旅行记》）。故事开头，瑞典男孩 Nils 站在书桌旁，父母准备出门，叮嘱他要学习、不要欺负农场里的动物。Nils 说自己很久没欺负动物了；父亲说他昨天拽了猫尾巴，母亲说他追母鸡，父亲还说刚抓到他朝奶牛扔鞋。Nils 笑着答应做个好孩子；父母摇摇头走了，Nils 叹气坐下，抱怨读书无聊，希望能发生点什么事。这时一只小精灵悄悄从前门进来。Nils 说自己从没见过精灵，要抓住它。被抓住的精灵尖叫着让他放手，说会给他一枚旧硬币；Nils 问还有什么，精灵说有银勺子和一枚大金币；Nils 说也许可以放它走，精灵道谢，但 Nils 又说其实不想放，叫它过来。只听砰的一声巨响，Nils 摔倒了；等他起来，精灵不见了，而 Nils 变小了——精灵把他变成了另一个精灵！Nils 问精灵去哪儿了、为什么书桌变大了。他走到外面，看到猫、母鸡和奶牛，它们现在都比他大。Nils 说动物们也变大了；猫说不是它们变大，而是 Nils 变小了；母鸡说他现在比它们还小；奶牛说 Nils 一直对它们不好，现在要付出代价。Nils 道歉，并请求帮忙找到精灵，因为他需要精灵把他变回男孩。猫喊“抓住他！”，动物们便追赶 Nils。

English Summary

This is the play The Boy and the Elf in the Reading Plus on pages 91–92 (adapted from Selma Lagerlöf's The Wonderful Adventures of Nils). At the beginning, Nils, a young Swedish boy, is standing next to a desk while his parents are by the door, ready to leave. They tell him to study and not to be mean to the farm animals; Nils says he hasn't been mean to them in ages, but his father mentions that he pulled the cat's tail yesterday, his mother says he ran after the hens, and his father says they have just caught him throwing shoes at the cow. Nils promises to be good; his parents shake their heads and leave, and Nils sighs and sits down, complaining that reading is boring and wishing something would happen. An elf enters quietly through the front door, and Nils decides to catch it. The elf screams and begs to be let go, offering an old coin; when Nils asks what else it has, the elf offers a silver spoon and a big gold coin. Nils says he could let it go, and the elf thanks him, but Nils changes his mind and tells it to come here. There is a loud BANG and Nils falls over; when he gets up, the elf has disappeared and Nils has become smaller — the elf has turned him into another elf! Nils wonders where the elf has gone and why the desk has got bigger. He walks outside and sees a cat, a hen and a cow, all now bigger than him. The cat says they haven't got bigger — Nils has got smaller; the hen says he is smaller than them now; and the cow says Nils has been mean to them and is now going to pay the price. Nils apologises and asks them to help him find the elf, because he needs the elf to turn him back into a boy. The cat shouts "Get him!", and the animals chase after Nils.

语法点 Grammar

Make sure you study, and don't be mean to the farm animals.

祈使句；make sure + 从句表示“确保……”；don't 开头的否定祈使句。

I haven't been mean to the animals in ages.

现在完成时 have not been；in ages 表示“很久以来”。

Yesterday you pulled the cat's tail.

一般过去时；the cat's tail 是名词所有格。

And we've just caught you throwing shoes at the cow.

现在完成时 have caught；catch sb. doing sth. 表示“抓到某人正在做某事”。

[Laughing.] Okay. I promise to be good!

promise to do sth. 承诺做某事；方括号内是舞台提示。

Reading is so boring!

动名词 reading 作主语；so + 形容词表示程度。

I wish something would happen.

wish + 宾语从句，从句用 would + 动词原形表示虚拟语气。

An elf! I've never seen one of those before ... I'm going to catch it!

现在完成时 have never seen；be going to 表示打算。

Let me go! I'll give you an old coin.

let sb. do sth. 让某人做某事；I'll = I will。

I'm not sure ... What else have you got?

现在完成时特殊疑问句 What else have you got?。

Okay ... I could let you go.

could + 动词原形表示委婉的说法；let sb. do sth.。

When he gets up, the elf has disappeared, and Nils has become smaller.

when 引导时间状语从句；现在完成时 has disappeared / has become。

The elf has turned him into another elf!

现在完成时 has turned；turn A into B 表示“把 A 变成 B”。

Where has that elf gone? And why has the desk got bigger?

现在完成时的特殊疑问句；get + 形容词比较级表示变化。

You've been mean to us, Nils. And now you're going to pay the price.

现在完成时 You've been；be going to 表示将要。

难句解析 Difficult sentences

I wish something would happen.

我希望发生点什么事。

wish 后接宾语从句时，从句常用 would + 动词原形，表示对将来的愿望。

When he gets up, the elf has disappeared, and Nils has become smaller.

等他站起来时，精灵已经不见了，Nils 也变小了。

when 引导时间状语从句；has disappeared / has become 是现在完成时，表示动作已完成。

You've been mean to us, Nils. And now you're going to pay the price.

Nils，你一直对我们不好。现在你要付出代价了。

现在完成时 You've been 表示从过去持续到现在的状态；be going to 表示将要发生。

阅读指引 Reading guide

读前

  • 看 P91 的剧本标题和人物，猜一猜故事内容。
  • 先快速浏览 P91–P92 两页剧本，找出出场的角色。

读中

  • 边读边画出舞台提示（方括号内容），弄清人物的动作和场景。
  • 注意 Nils 的态度变化（得意 → 变小 → 道歉）。

读后

  • 分角色朗读剧本，注意语气和表情。
  • 续写或表演：接下来 Nils 会怎样？

练习 · 8 题

阅读上面 4 篇文章，选择最佳答案；先独立完成，再点开“答案与解析”核对，并回原文定位依据。

1. What book has Fu Xing just finished?

  1. Alice's Adventures in Wonderland.
  2. The Secret Garden.
  3. The Old Man and the Sea.
  4. The Wonderful Adventures of Nils.
答案与解析

A 原文：I've just finished Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll. 可知 Fu Xing 刚读完《爱丽丝漫游奇境记》。

2. What does Fu Xing prefer?

  1. The movie.
  2. Both the book and the movie.
  3. The book.
  4. Neither of them.
答案与解析

C 原文：I've always preferred the book to the movie. 可知 Fu Xing 更喜欢书。

3. Why did Mary's uncle lock up the garden?

  1. Because it was too big.
  2. Because it was a painful memory.
  3. Because it was full of animals.
  4. Because it was far away.
答案与解析

B 原文：After she died, the garden became a painful memory for him, so he locked it up. 可知花园是叔叔痛苦的回忆，所以他把它锁了起来。

4. What is the name of Mary's cousin?

  1. Dickon.
  2. Mr Craven.
  3. Nils.
  4. Colin.
答案与解析

D 原文：One night, Mary hears someone crying again and discovers Colin, her cousin. 可知 Mary 的表弟叫 Colin。

5. When was The Old Man and the Sea written?

  1. In 1953.
  2. In 1932.
  3. In 1952.
  4. In 1962.
答案与解析

C 原文：It was written in 1952 by the American writer Ernest Hemingway. 可知这本书写于 1952 年。

6. What does the fisherman catch after months without fish?

  1. A huge fish.
  2. A shark.
  3. A boat.
  4. A bird.
答案与解析

A 原文：One day he catches a huge fish ... 可知渔夫捕到了一条大鱼。

7. Why did Nils become smaller?

  1. He fell over.
  2. He read a book.
  3. He ran after the hens.
  4. The elf turned him into another elf.
答案与解析

D 原文：the elf has disappeared, and Nils has become smaller. The elf has turned him into another elf! 可知是小精灵把 Nils 变成了精灵。

8. Why do the animals chase after Nils?

  1. He gave them food.
  2. He has been mean to them.
  3. He helped the elf.
  4. He wants to play with them.
答案与解析

B 原文：You've been mean to us, Nils. And now you're going to pay the price. ... [The animals chase after Nils.] 可知因为 Nils 一直对动物们不好。
