\section{过去进行时：构成}
过去进行时由「\textbf{was / were + 动词 -ing}」构成，表示在\textbf{过去某一时刻或某一段时间正在进行的动作}。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item What \underline{was Kate doing} at the time of the earthquake? --- She \underline{was doing} her homework.
\item What \underline{were your family doing} when the typhoon hit? --- My children and I \underline{were reading} together.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{关键：}这一特定的过去时间除有上下文暗示，一般用\textbf{时间状语}来表示（at that time / at eight last night 等）。主语单数用 was，复数用 were。

\section{过去进行时：用法与背景}
过去进行时还可用来为\textbf{一个或一系列动作的发生提供背景}。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item Everyone \underline{was enjoying} the beautiful weather that morning. Some people \underline{were swimming} in the sea, and some \underline{were relaxing} on the beach.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{教材提示：}持续时间长的动作一般用过去进行时，持续时间短的常用一般过去时。如果主句和从句都用过去进行时，则表示两个动作都持续了一段时间。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item David \underline{wrote} a letter last night.（写完了）
\item David \underline{was writing} a letter last night.（不一定写完）
\end{itemize}

\section{现在完成时：构成}
现在完成时由「\textbf{助动词 have / has + 过去分词}」构成。主语第三人称单数用 has，其余用 have。
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{6.4cm}p{6.4cm}}
\hline
\textbf{肯定式} & \textbf{否定式}\\
\hline
I / You have finished. & I / You haven't finished.\\
He / She / It has finished. & He / She / It hasn't finished.\\
We / You / They have finished. & We / You / They haven't finished.\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item She \underline{has been} a volunteer since 2022.
\item They \underline{have} never \underline{seen} snow before.
\end{itemize}

\section{现在完成时：四种用法}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{5.2cm}p{8.0cm}}
\hline
\textbf{用法} & \textbf{例句}\\
\hline
过去动作对\textbf{现在}造成影响或结果 & I can't find my key. I \underline{have lost} it.\\
从过去\textbf{持续到现在} & She \underline{has lived} here since she was born.\\
与 already / never / ever / just / before / yet 连用 & I have \underline{already} borrowed the book. / Have you \underline{ever} heard of it?\\
have been to 去过（已回）／have gone to 去了（未回） & Helen \underline{has been} to Xi'an twice. / She \underline{has gone} to Xi'an.\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\textbf{教材注释：}非延续性动词一般不能与表示持续时间的状语连用；但在\textbf{否定结构}中，可用非延续性动词的现在完成时表示持续。\\
\quad I \underline{haven't bought} any food since last week.

\section{现在完成时 vs 一般过去时}
现在完成时强调\textbf{现在的情况}，所以\textbf{不能}与表示过去的时间状语连用（yesterday、last night、three weeks ago、in 1990 等）；一般过去时只表示过去的动作或状态，可以和这些状语连用。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item Fu Xing \underline{has worked} at a nursing home since last year.（她到现在还在那里工作）
\item Fu Xing \underline{worked} at a nursing home last year.（不涉及现在）
\end{itemize}
\textbf{做题口诀：}看到 yesterday / last ... / ... ago / in 1990，一律用一般过去时。
