01

并列连词

and / but / or / so / both ... and ...

并列连词用来连接并列的单词、短语、从句或句子。常见的有 and、but、for、or、both ... and ...、not only ... but also ...。

连词关系例句
and并列／递进The Nile and the Yellow River helped ancient civilizations.
but转折You can fool some people, but you cannot fool all of them.
or选择／否则To be, or not to be. / Hurry up, or you'll be late.
so结果He studied hard, so he passed.
not only ... but also递进Thank you for being not only my teacher but also my friend.
02

从属连词

引导从句，不能单独成句

从属连词用来引导复合句中从句。常见的有 when、while、as、before、after、until、if、because、although、though、than、so that、so ... that、unless、as soon as 等。

He started programming when he was 10.

他 10 岁时开始编程。

You are braver than you believe.

你比你相信的更勇敢。

Please give me a call as soon as you arrive.

你一到就给我打电话。

If you run after two hares, you will catch neither.

脚踩两只船，两头落空。

03

状语从句七类

时间 / 地点 / 原因 / 条件 / 结果 / 目的 / 让步

类型引导连词
时间before、after、when、while、as、as soon as、since、not ... until、whenever
地点where、wherever
原因because、as、since
条件if、unless
结果so ... that
目的so、so that
让步although、even though

教材注释：在时间状语从句和条件状语从句中，通常用一般现在时表示一般将来时。
If it doesn't rain tomorrow, we'll go for a picnic.

04

两个高频陷阱

不能同时使用的连词

× 错误

Although you were busy, but you helped me.

Because he is clever, so I admire him.

✓ 正确

Although you were busy, you helped me.

He is clever, so I admire him.

规则：though / although 与 but 不可同时使用；because 与 so 也不可同时使用。二者只能留一个。
