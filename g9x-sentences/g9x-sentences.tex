\section{简单句的「并列」陷阱}
简单句可以带\textbf{并列主语}或\textbf{并列谓语}，这类句子\textbf{仍是简单句}。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item Tom \underline{and} Jerry are good friends.（并列主语）
\item Some students \underline{walk or ride} bicycles to school.（并列谓语）
\end{itemize}
\textbf{关键区别：}and 连接的是词/短语 → 简单句；and 连接的是两个主谓结构 → 并列句。

\section{并列句的连词}
and（并列递进）、so（结果）、but（转折）、or（选择/否则）。
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item They are sweet rice balls, \underline{and} we eat them during the Lantern Festival.
\item There are many boat races around the world, \underline{but} this one is special.
\end{itemize}

\section{复合句的从句}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item I wonder \underline{if} the two water festivals are similar.（宾语从句）
\item What do you want to be \underline{when} you grow up?（时间状语从句）
\item Do you have everything \underline{that} you need?（定语从句）
\end{itemize}
