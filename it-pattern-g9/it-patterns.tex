\section{句型结构：It 是形式主语}
It is + adj. + (for / of sb.) + to do sth.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item It is important \underline{for us to learn English}.（真正主语：to learn English）
\item It is kind \underline{of you to help me}.（真正主语：to help me）
\item It is interesting \underline{to read this book}.（没有 for/of sb.）
\end{itemize}
\textbf{三种形式：}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item It is + adj. + to do sth.
\item It is + adj. + for sb. + to do sth.
\item It is + adj. + of sb. + to do sth.
\end{enumerate}

\section{for 还是 of}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{4.6cm}p{4.2cm}p{6.8cm}}
\hline
\textbf{结构} & \textbf{形容词描述} & \textbf{常见形容词}\\
\hline
It is + adj. + for sb. + to do & 描述“做这件事”的性质 & important, necessary, difficult, easy, hard, useful, possible, dangerous, interesting\\
It is + adj. + of sb. + to do & 描述“某人的品质” & kind, nice, polite, brave, clever, wise, honest, generous, thoughtful, foolish, wrong\\
\hline
\end{tabular}

口诀：形容词夸“人”用 of，形容词说“事”用 for。

\section{同义句转换}
To do ... is adj. (for/of sb.) → It is adj. (for/of sb.) to do ...
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item To learn English is important for us. → It is important for us to learn English.
\item To help others is kind of him. → It is kind of him to help others.
\item To save the child was brave of him. → It was brave of him to save the child.
\end{itemize}
注意时态：原句 was，改写后仍用 It was。

\section{五个高频易错点}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item for / of 混用：× important of us → ✓ important for us。
\item 漏掉 to：× kind of you help me → ✓ kind of you to help me。
\item 用 doing 代替 to do：× important for us learning → ✓ important for us to learn。
\item 形式主语误用 that / this：× That is important ... → ✓ It is important ...
\item 时态不一致：To save ... was brave → It was brave ...（不是 It is）。
\end{enumerate}
