01

句型结构：It 是形式主语

It is + adj. + (for / of sb.) + to do sth.

这个句型里，it 没有实际意思，只占主语位置，叫形式主语；真正的主语是后面的不定式 to do sth.。

It is important for us to learn English.

对我们来说，学好英语很重要。

真正主语：to learn English；for us 表示“对谁而言”。

It is kind of you to help me.

你帮我，真是太善良了。

真正主语：to help me；of you 表示“谁具有这种品质”。

It is interesting to read this book.

读这本书很有趣。

没有 for/of sb. 时，直接 It is + adj. + to do。

结构公式：It is + 形容词 + for / of + 某人 + to do sth.
注意：to 不能丢，do 不能用 doing / 原形替代。

三种常见形式

  1. It is + adj. + to do sth.：It is important to learn English.
  2. It is + adj. + for sb. + to do sth.：It is important for us to learn English.
  3. It is + adj. + of sb. + to do sth.：It is kind of you to help me.
02

for 还是 of？看形容词描述谁

形容词描述“事情”用 for；描述“人的品质”用 of。

结构形容词描述常见形容词例句
It is + adj. + for sb. + to do 描述“做这件事”的性质 important, necessary, difficult, easy, hard, useful, possible, dangerous, interesting It is important for us to protect the environment.
It is + adj. + of sb. + to do 描述“某人的品质” kind, nice, polite, brave, clever, wise, honest, generous, thoughtful, foolish, wrong It is kind of you to help me.

× 错误

It is important of us to learn English.

It is kind for you to help me.

It is important for us learn English.

✓ 正确

It is important for us to learn English.

It is kind of you to help me.

It is important for us to learn English.

判断口诀：形容词夸“人”（kind / brave / clever）用 of；形容词说“事”（important / easy / necessary）用 for。

03

形容词分类表：一眼判断

先记高频词，再在句子里验证。

for 类：描述事情

  • important / necessary
  • difficult / easy / hard
  • useful / helpful / possible
  • dangerous / safe / interesting

It is necessary for us to take notes.

of 类：描述人的品质

  • kind / nice / polite
  • brave / clever / wise
  • honest / generous / thoughtful
  • foolish / selfish / wrong

It is wise of you to make a plan.

有些形容词两边都能用？要看句意。例如 It is good for you to exercise.（锻炼对你有好处）与 It is good of you to help.（你帮忙真好）含义不同，不能只背单词。

04

同义句转换：To do ... is adj. → It is adj. ... to do

把不定式从主语位置挪到句末，用 it 占位。

To learn English is important for us.
→ It is important for us to learn English.

不定式 to learn English 是真正主语；用 it 作形式主语后，句子更自然。

To help others is kind of him.
→ It is kind of him to help others.

kind 描述他的品质；改写后保持 of him。

转换三步

  1. 用 It is 开头（时态跟原句）；
  2. 保留原形容词和 for/of sb.；
  3. 把不定式 to do 放到句末。

时态：原句是 was 时，改写后也要用 It was。例如：To save the child was brave of him. → It was brave of him to save the child.

05

五个高频易错点

这些地方最容易在选择题、语法填空和写作中丢分。

1

for / of 混用

× It is important of us to learn English.
✓ It is important for us to learn English.

2

漏掉 to

× It is kind of you help me.
✓ It is kind of you to help me.

3

用 doing 代替 to do

× It is important for us learning English.
✓ It is important for us to learn English.

4

形式主语误用 that / this

× That is important for us to learn English.
✓ It is important for us to learn English.

5

时态不一致

× To save the child was brave of him. → It is brave of him to save the child.
✓ It was brave of him to save the child.

拓展：不要与 It takes sb. some time to do sth. 混淆。那个句型里 it 也是形式主语，但谓语是 takes，不是 is。

06

Unit 2 Inspiring People：把句型用进话题

励志人物、品质与行动，是 Unit 2 写作和口语的常用语境。

描述人物品质

of sb.

  • It is brave of him to face difficulties.
  • It is kind of the volunteer to help the old man.
  • It is generous of the man to donate money.

常考：brave / kind / generous / clever / wise + of sb.

描述事情意义

for sb.

  • It is important for us to learn from inspiring people.
  • It is necessary for young people to set goals.
  • It is helpful for us to read his story.

常考：important / necessary / helpful / possible + for sb.

写作提示：介绍一位激励你的人时，可以写：It is brave of him to ... / It is important for us to learn from him. 一个 of、一个 for，句型就丰富了。
