01

反身代词（Reflexive Pronouns）

表达「……自己；本身」，由 -self / -selves 构成

反身代词是英语中表达「……自己；本身」含义的一类代词，是在第一人称、第二人称形容词性物主代词或第三人称人称代词宾格形式的词尾加 -self 或 -selves 组成。

表1　反身代词（教材原表）
数第一人称第二人称第三人称
单数myselfyourselfhimself / herself / itself
复数ourselvesyourselvesthemselves
表2　反身代词的用法（教材原表）
句法作用例句
动词或介词的宾语Be careful when you're using the knife! Don't cut yourself.
In teaching others, we teach ourselves.
I'll be away for a week. You and your sister need to take care of yourselves.
Don't be too hard on yourself!
主语或宾语的同位语I cooked the dinner myself.
You had better ask the dentist herself.
表语Just be yourself.
Mary hasn't been quite herself recently.

教材提示：反身代词用作主语或宾语的同位语时，一般用于强调，常译为「本人；亲自」。常见后接反身代词作宾语的动词或动词短语有 cut、hurt、protect、teach、enjoy、help、take care of、look after 等。

Help yourself to some fruit.

随便吃点水果吧。

help oneself 固定搭配。

The children enjoyed themselves at the party.

孩子们在聚会上玩得很开心。

enjoy oneself ＝ have a good time。

02

情态动词（Modal Verbs）

表示提议或建议：should / could

情态动词可以用来表示提议或建议。教材重点讲了两个：should 和 could。

1. should

情态动词 should 作「应该；应当；可以」讲，用以表达职责和义务、提出劝告，而且表述的是自己的主观看法，其后接动词原形。

When you have the flu, you should wear a mask when you're outside.

患流感时，你出门应戴上口罩。

should + 动词原形。

If you did something wrong, you should say sorry.

如果你做错了什么，你应该道歉。

提出劝告。

should 的否定式是 should not / shouldn't，意思是「不应当；不该」。

You shouldn't eat so much next time.

下次你可不要吃这么多了。

We shouldn't judge a book by its cover.

我们不应该以貌取人。

2. could

情态动词 could 也用于提出建议，比 can 语气更为委婉。

A: What should I take with me to the party?
B: You could take some fruit or flowers.

A：我去参加聚会应该带点什么呢？　B：你可以带些水果或鲜花。

If you want to say sorry, you could call him.

如果你想说道歉，你可以给他打电话。

03

过去进行时（Past Continuous Tense）

was / were + 动词-ing，表示过去某时正在进行

1. 构成和基本用法

过去进行时由「was / were + 动词 -ing」构成，表示在过去某一时刻或某一段时间正在进行的动作。这一特定的过去时间除有上下文暗示，一般用时间状语来表示。

A: What was Kate doing at the time of the earthquake?
B: She was doing her homework.

地震发生时凯特在做什么？她在做作业。

A: What were your family doing when the typhoon hit?
B: My children and I were reading together.

台风来袭时你们一家在做什么？我和孩子们在读书。

过去进行时还可用来为一个或一系列动作的发生提供背景。

Everyone was enjoying the beautiful weather that morning. Some people were swimming in the sea, and some were relaxing on the beach.

那天早上，大家都在享受美好的天气。有人在海里游泳，有人在沙滩上放松。

过去进行时既可用在主句中，也可用在从句中，还可以同时用在主句和从句中。

We were visiting our relatives when the strong winds started.

强风刮起时我们正在走亲戚。

I was doing some chores while my mother was preparing lunch.

妈妈在做午饭时我在做家务。

教材提示：持续时间的长的动作一般用过去进行时，持续时间的短的常用一般过去时。如果主句和从句都用过去进行时，则表示主句和从句中的动作都持续了一段时间。

表3　过去进行时的肯定式、否定式、疑问式和简略回答（教材原表）
肯定式 / 否定式
I / He / She / It was working.
We / You / They were working.		I / He / She / It was not working.
We / You / They were not working.
疑问式和简略回答
Was I working?
Yes, you were. / No, you were not (weren't).		Were you working?
Yes, I was. / No, I was not (wasn't).
Was he / she / it working?
Yes, he / she / it was.
No, he / she / it was not (wasn't).		Were we / you / they working?
Yes, you / we / they were.
No, you / we / they were not (weren't).

2. 一般过去时与过去进行时用法的比较

一般过去时表示在过去某个时间发生过的动作或存在的状态；过去进行时则表示在过去某一时刻或某一段时间正在进行的动作。

一般过去时（写完了）

David wrote a letter to his friend last night.

大卫昨晚给他的朋友写了封信。

过去进行时（信不一定写完）

David was writing a letter to his friend last night.

大卫昨晚一直在给朋友写信。

04

现在完成时（Present Perfect Tense）

have / has + 过去分词，连接过去与现在

1. 构成和基本用法

现在完成时由「助动词 have / has + 过去分词」构成。以动词 finish 为例：

表4　现在完成时的肯定式、否定式、疑问式和简略回答（教材原表）
肯定式否定式
I / You have finished.
He / She / It has finished.
We / You / They have finished.		I / You have not (haven't) finished.
He / She / It has not (hasn't) finished.
We / You / They have not (haven't) finished.
疑问式简略回答
Have I / you finished?
Has he / she / it finished?
Have we / you / they finished?		Yes, you / I have. / Yes, he / she / it has. / Yes, you / we / they have.
No, you / I have not (haven't). / No, he / she / it has not (hasn't). / No, you / we / they have not (haven't).
表5　现在完成时的用法（教材原表）
用法例句
表示过去发生或已经完成的动作对现在造成的影响或结果A: Have you chosen a book for your report yet?
B: Yes, I've picked The Final Problem.（我不必再做选择了。）
A: Have you ever read The Three-Body Problem?
B: No, I haven't.（我对该书不了解。）
表示某一动作或状态从过去某一时间开始并一直持续到现在，可以和表示延续的时间状语连用She's been a volunteer since 2022.
Mary has lived with her uncle since her parents died.
I have been busy these days. I haven't had a day off for three months.
How long has he worked at the museum?
现在完成时可以 already、never、ever、just、before、yet 等连用I have already borrowed the book from the library.
He has never seen snow before.
Have you ever heard of Oliver Twist?
I've just finished Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll.
have (has) been 表示「曾经到过某地」，have (has) gone 表示「已经去某地了」Helen has been to Xi'an once. She wants to go there again to learn more about the long history of this city.
Helen has gone to Xi'an. She'll be back in a week.

教材注释：非延续性动词一般不能与表示持续时间的状语连用，但在否定结构中，可用非延续性动词的现在完成时表示持续的动作或状态。

I haven't bought any food since last week. The fridge is empty now.
Where has Tom gone? We haven't seen him the whole morning.

2. 现在完成时和一般过去时的区别

现在完成时表示过去发生的某一动作对现在造成的影响或结果，强调现在的情况，所以它不能和表示过去的时间状语连用，如 yesterday、last night、three weeks ago、in 1990 等。而一般过去时只表示过去的动作或状态，和现在不发生联系，它可以和上述表示过去的时间状语连用。

现在完成时（与她现在的状况有关）

Fu Xing has worked at a nursing home since last year.

付兴从去年开始就在一家养老院工作。（她现在已经还在那里工作。）

一般过去时（不涉及现在）

Fu Xing worked at a nursing home last year.

付兴去年在一家养老院工作。（不涉及她是否还在那里工作。）

05

动词不定式（Infinitives）

to + 动词原形，可作宾语、目的状语、宾语补足语

1. 动词不定式的构成

动词不定式的基本形式是「to + 动词原形」。动词不定式没有人称和数的变化，在句子中不能作谓语。动词不定式仍保留动词的语义特点，可以有自己的宾语和状语。

2. 动词不定式的用法

动词不定式具有名词、形容词和副词的特征，因此可以作主语、宾语、宾语补足语、定语和状语等。教材重点呈现作宾语、目的状语和宾语补足语三种用法。

表6　动词不定式的句法作用（教材原表）
句法作用例句
作宾语Do you want to visit the Great Wall?
I like to play badminton in my free time.
On Saturday, James decided to make fried chicken.
作目的状语What do you do to pass the time?
Allen ran to the living room to get the fire extinguisher.
Do not stand up to reach for food across the table.
作宾语补足语My instructor encouraged me to get back up whenever I fell.
My parents told me not to push myself too hard.
Doctor Cooper asked him to be more careful in the future.

3. 动词不定式的否定式

动词不定式的否定形式由「not + 动词不定式」构成。

I would prefer not to talk about it.

我宁愿不谈这件事。

"Try not to make any noise when you eat," his mother said.

他妈妈说：「吃饭时尽量不要发出声音。」

4. 「疑问词 + 不定式」结构

动词不定式可以和 what、which、how、where、when 等连用。

He didn't know what to say to Ella.

他不知道该对埃拉说什么。

She asked me how to use the washing machine.

她问我怎么用洗衣机。

The tour guide will tell you where to go next.

导游会告诉你接下来去哪里。

5. 不带 to 的动词不定式

有一些动词后用作宾语补足语的不定式通常不带 to，主要有两类：感官动词（see、hear、watch、feel、notice 等）和使役动词（let、make、have 等）。

The referee saw Matt push the other player.

裁判看到马特推了另一个球员。

He heard someone walk down the stairway.

他听到有人走下楼梯。

If you have any questions, please let me know.

如果你有任何问题，请让我知道。

Hobbies can make you feel good about yourself.

爱好能让你对自己感觉良好。

06

连词（Conjunctions）

并列连词 vs 从属连词

用来连接单词、短语或句子的虚词叫连词。连词在句中不能单独作句子成分，一般不重读。按其性质，连词可分为两类：并列连词和从属连词。

1. 并列连词

并列连词是指用以连接并列的单词、短语、从句或句子的连词。常见的并列连词有 and、but、for、or、both ... and ...、not only ... but also ... 等。

You can fool some of the people all of the time, and all of the people some of the time, but you cannot fool all of the people all of the time.

你可以一直欺骗一部分人，也可以在一段时间欺骗所有人，但你不可能一直欺骗所有人。

To be, or not to be, that is the question.

生存还是毁灭，这是个问题。

The Nile and the Yellow River helped ancient civilizations to develop.

Thank you so much for being not only my teacher but also my friend.

2. 从属连词

从属连词是指用以引导复合句中从句的连词。常见的从属连词有 when、while、as、before、after、until、if、because、although、though、than、so that、so ... that、unless、as soon as 等。

He started programming when he was 10.

他 10 岁时开始编程。

If you run after two hares, you will catch neither.

脚踩两只船，两头落空。

You are braver than you believe, stronger than you seem, and smarter than you think.

你比你相信的更勇敢，比你看起来更坚强，比你想象的更聪明。

Please give me a call as soon as you arrive in Beijing.

你一到北京就给我打电话。

教材注释：在同一个句子中，though（although）与 but 不可同时使用，because 与 so 也不可同时使用。

× Although you were so busy, but you took the time to help me.（应删去 but，或删去 although）
× Because Zhuge Liang is so clever and has a plan for everything, so I admire him.（应删去 because，或删去 so）

07

状语从句（Adverbial Clauses）

时间、地点、原因、条件、结果、目的、让步七类

在复合句中，用作状语的从句叫状语从句。根据所表达的意思，状语从句可分为时间、地点、原因、条件、结果、目的和让步等类型。

表7　不同类型的状语从句（教材原表）
类型引导连词例句
时间before、after、when、while、as、as soon as、since、not ... until、whenever 等As the front door opened, James heard the sound of Allen singing.
You won't know if you can do it until you try.
I like to write about my feelings in my diary whenever I feel bad or sad.
地点where、wherever 等Keep the dictionary where you can see it.
I bring a book wherever I go.
原因because、as、since 等Alice can't reach the key on the table because she's too small.
Chinese people love the ginkgo tree, as it is a symbol of hope and long life.
Since he has returned, you could now ask for his help.
条件if、unless 等If you don't want to forget your resolutions, write them down.
Nothing is impossible unless you think it is.
结果so ... thatI was so hungry that I ate a big bowl of noodles and a big plate of dumplings.
目的so、so that 等We'll sit near the front so we can hear the speaker better.
You can write to your parents so that they'll know how you feel.
让步although、even though 等Although my guitar is old, it means a lot to me.
Tom felt proud of their team, even though they lost.

教材注释：在时间状语从句和条件状语从句中，通常用一般现在时表示一般将来时。

Please say hello for me when you see her next week.
If it doesn't rain tomorrow, we'll go for a picnic.
